# genre - Instrumental

## curiosities
**"Mood Music" for Older Audiences** : Instrumental albums were a common category stocked in early record stores and general retailers. These records were often marketed as "moon music" or mood music, specifically targeting older demographics. "Plenty of those instrumental albums that were supposed to be good for moon music for older people." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jD1dWxNLvfE ← instrumental
**Chart Popularity in 1962 (1962)** : In 1962, instrumentals were highly popular on the charts, with "vocal-less hits" such as "Stranger on the Shore," "The Stripper," and "Telstar" all reaching number one.
