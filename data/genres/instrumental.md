**Definition - Lacks Full Verse and Chorus** : For the purpose of charting and historical analysis, a song is generally classified as an instrumental if it lacks a full verse and chorus, even if it contains a few spoken words or vocalizations. Chart historian Joel Whitburn's books are considered the authoritative source, or "Bible," for these classifications.
