# genre - Euro Disco

## curiosities
**Italian and German Hub** : Primarily headquartered in Italy and Germany, this scene significantly influenced late 1980s British pop artists such as Rick Astley and Kylie Minogue. "Another offshoot was Euro Disco, which was headquartered mostly in Italy and Germany. That scene later influenced a lot of late 80s British pop like Rick Astley and Connie Menog." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1KF5MwJVYng ← euro-disco
**Boney M's Pioneering Act** : Frank Farian scored a string of hits in the 1970s with his Euro Disco act, Boney M. This group became incredibly popular across Europe and in the UK, defining a distinctive sound and aesthetic for the genre. **Decline and Fizzling on Charts** : Boney M's British and European hit streak began to "tail off as disco fizzled on the charts" in the late 1970s. The decline was marked by songs like "Gotta Go Home" just missing the UK top 10 in late 1979, signaling the end of their era as major hitmakers. "As for Bony M's British and European hit streak, it finally began to tail off as disco fizzled on the charts." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Limited US Appeal (1981)** : In 1981, pure, campy Euro disco only occasionally managed to achieve significant popularity in America, even two years after disco's general peak. This limited appeal prevented many artists, such as Boney M, from securing bigger hits in the US market, despite the genre's catchy elements.
