# genre - Post Disco

## curiosities
**Emergence from Post Punk** : Post Disco began to emerge from the "post punk folks" as the stigma of dancing in clubs dissipated after the decline of mainstream disco. It incorporated significant pop and R&B elements, reflecting a new direction for dance music. "Some of this new dance momentum peeled off into something called Post Disco." ← Ongoing History of New Music > The Post-Punk Explosion Part 4： Alt-Dance | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0FYq5MBdHw4&list=PLM8bxV0E1Gse5C3n1u-mkVSTZLL5AJICG
**Pop and R&B Elements** : This genre, a direct successor to disco in the post-punk era, was characterized by its incorporation of pop and R&B elements. Artists like Madonna, Prince, and Michael Jackson (with "Thriller") were associated with this sound, which was distinct from the earlier, more monotonous disco. **Evolution from Disco** : After the 1970s, disco did not die but rather evolved into various "captivating dance floor idioms," forming the foundation for later genres like freestyle. "disco didn't really die after the 70s.
