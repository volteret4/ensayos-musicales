**Early Example - Kiss's "Beth" (1976)** : Kiss's "Beth," which peaked at number 7 in the fall of 1976, is identified as "one of the earliest power ballads." Sung by drummer Peter Criss, it was a very soft ballad built around piano and strings, with minimal electric instruments, foreshadowing the trend of hard rock bands softening their sound for chart success. **Ultimate 80s Power Ballad - Motley Crüe's "Home Sweet Home"** : Motley Crüe's "Home Sweet Home" is arguably the ultimate power ballad of the 1980s, despite its poor Hot 100 performance (peaking at number 89). It was MTV's biggest video for much of 1985 and 1986, consistently topping requests on "Dial MTV," but was considered "too soft for rock fans and too day-class A for pop fans." **Prominence in 80s Soundtracks** : The power ballad genre was a significant feature of 80s movie soundtracks, capable of generating massive hits like Phil Collins' "Against All Odds (Take a Look at Me Now)" even when the associated film or soundtrack did not achieve blockbuster status.
