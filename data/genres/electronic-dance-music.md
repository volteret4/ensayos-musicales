# genre - Electronic Dance Music

## curiosities
**Love Parade Disaster (Duisburg, Germany, July 24, 2010)** : The annual free Love Parade electronic dance music festival in Duisburg, Germany, on July 24, 2010, resulted in 21 deaths and over 700 injuries. The single, narrow tunnel entrance to the festival grounds became dangerously overcrowded; despite police attempts to seal it off and instruct people to turn back, continuous pushing from behind caused an immense crush, with autopsies revealing all victims had suffered broken ribs from the pressure. **Time Warp Festival Tragedy (Buenos Aires, 2016)** : At the Time Warp Festival in Buenos Aires in 2016, a significant number of people died on the first night due to "bad drugs," with four others hospitalized in critical condition from poisoning. The severity of the incident led to the immediate cancellation of the festival's second day. "Most people died on the first night from bad drugs." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pgchw2h87aw ← electronic-dance-music
**Time Warp Organizers Arrested, Festivals Banned** : Following the drug-related deaths at the Time Warp Festival in Buenos Aires, the festival organizers were arrested and charged with facilitating a location where individuals could purchase illegal drugs. As a direct consequence of this tragedy, Argentina subsequently banned electronic music festivals. "The festival organizers were arrested and charged with facilitating a place where people could purchase drugs. **First Album of the Year Win (2014)** : Electronic Dance Music (EDM) secured its first Album of the Year Grammy in 2014 with Daft Punk's "Random Access Memories." The album was also noted as the "robotic French duo's most retro sounding LP," suggesting the Academy might have favored a less cutting-edge representation of the genre. **Revival of Number One Debuts** : After a period of dormancy following the "American Idol" wave, the number one debut was revived by the growth of digital music, particularly in the age of electronic dance music. Digital hits in this genre were able to "blow up big time" through downloads and later streaming, often before accumulating a significant radio audience.
