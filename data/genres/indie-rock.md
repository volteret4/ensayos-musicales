**Female Success at Alternative Rock Peak (Early 1990s)** : Performers from a prior era of Indy Rock found renewed and often their biggest career successes by forming new bands at the peak of alternative rock. This trend included artists like Juliana Hatfield and Tanya Donelly, whose new projects topped the Modern Rock chart. **Hybridization in Early 2000s Pop** : In the early 2000s, the sound of new-vogue garage bands, including indie rock elements, began to bleed into pop music, exemplified by the Yeah Yeah Yeahs' "Maps" and its influence on Kelly Clarkson's "Since U Been Gone."
