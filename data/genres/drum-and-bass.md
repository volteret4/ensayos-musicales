# genre - Drum and bass

## curiosities
**Influence on USS Song** : A drum and bass beat was used in the demo of USS's song "Pasturella Pestus," providing the musical foundation for Ash's long-held lyrical part. "That went over a little drum and bass beat and the part I could never get out of my head like it was a brilliant part." ← Ongoing History of New Music > USS In Their Own Words： Part 2 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N-3eddy4J7g&list=PLM8bxV0E1Gse5C3n1u-mkVSTZLL5AJICG
**Influence on Outcast (Late 1990s)** : British dance music known as Drum and Bass, which was prominent in UK clubs during the second half of the 1990s, directly influenced Outcast's 2000 single "B.O.B." The duo attempted to synthesize its "frenetic 155 beats per minute" rhythms into their rap sound. "Running at a frenetic 155 beats per minute, the song was Outcast's attempt to synthesize the rhythms of the British dance music known as Drum and Bass, which had been blasting in UK clubs throughout the second half of the 1990s." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Shake It Like a Polaroid Picture Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ee9d1ba84fb8f044632
