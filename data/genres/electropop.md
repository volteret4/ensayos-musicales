**Fusion with Top 40 (2010)** : By 2010, Electronic Dance Music (EDM), which had been steadily growing since the late 1990s and 2000s, had fully integrated with centrist top 40 music. This fusion resulted in a potent form of electropop characterized by prominent bass drops, auto-tuned vocals, and mid-song crescendos.
