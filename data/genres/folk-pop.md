# genre - Folk Pop

## curiosities
**Laurel Canyon as a Root for 70s Rock** : The "roots of 70s rock" are identified in the "folk can pop music that was made in Laurel Canyon in Southern California." This area fostered the development of singer-songwriters and harmony-driven sounds that became central to the decade's musical landscape. **Watered-Down Sound of the 1950s** : In the 1950s, much of commercial folk music, or folk pop, was characterized as "watered down," though it was immensely popular, with acts like The Weavers achieving mainstream success despite this perception.
