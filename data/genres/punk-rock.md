# genre - punk rock

## curiosities
**The Cramps' Counter-Narrative** : While often associated with the punk movement, The Cramps publicly stated they did not identify with it. They argued that punk "disavowed the origins of rock and roll and wanted to destroy everything," whereas The Cramps embraced and celebrated rock's historical roots as "mythomaniacs." "Ellos decían no sentirse identificados con el movimiento, ya que el punk renego a los orígenes del rock and roll y quería acabar con todo, en cambio de Crams eran unos mitómanos." ← Amor, Terror y Psychobilly： La Historia de The Cramps | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBVTzVQDQeQ
**Fusion in Southern Indie Rock** : The "economy of punk" was a foundational element in the development of "jangly rock" in the Southern United States during the 1980s. This raw, efficient approach to songwriting and performance was fused with country rock influences to create an indie music sound that defined the decade and influenced bands like R.E.M. **Early Uncommerciality** : Initially, punk rock was defining itself in the mid-70s as music for a "blank generation" that eschewed the commerciality of earlier rock waves. Its origins at venues like CBGB were associated with uncommercial, "bitterly unapproachable music" not intended for pop charts.
