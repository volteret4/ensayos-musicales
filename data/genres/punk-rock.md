# genre - punk rock

## curiosities
**The Cramps' Counter-Narrative** : While often associated with the punk movement, The Cramps publicly stated they did not identify with it. They argued that punk "disavowed the origins of rock and roll and wanted to destroy everything," whereas The Cramps embraced and celebrated rock's historical roots as "mythomaniacs." "Ellos decían no sentirse identificados con el movimiento, ya que el punk renego a los orígenes del rock and roll y quería acabar con todo, en cambio de Crams eran unos mitómanos." ← Amor, Terror y Psychobilly： La Historia de The Cramps | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBVTzVQDQeQ
**Fusion in Southern Indie Rock** : The "economy of punk" was a foundational element in the development of "jangly rock" in the Southern United States during the 1980s. This raw, efficient approach to songwriting and performance was fused with country rock influences to create an indie music sound that defined the decade and influenced bands like R.E.M. "jangly rock that fused the economy of punk with the country rock of Graham Parsons into an indie music that defined the 80s." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > A Little Love and Some Tenderness Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f6f64fe6d21276e433e
**Rise in Early 90s** : Alternative rock was on the rise in the early 90s, with bands like Nirvana topping the charts, influencing the music landscape around the time of the 1992 Grammys, though the Academy chose to go "fully pre-rock" that year.
