# genre - New Romantic

## curiosities
**David Bowie's Influence (1980)** : The New Romantic movement of the early 1980s was significantly jumpstarted by David Bowie's surreal yet fashionable clown character in his groundbreaking 1980 music video for a song from his "Scary Monsters" album. This visual style, featuring elements like extras from the Blitz Club, became highly influential. **Descendant of Glam** : The New Romantic movement is identified as a direct descendant of glam, particularly in its emphasis on wild, flamboyant, and eccentric fashion being as crucial as the music. It formed a clear lineage from David Bowie and Roxy Music of the 1970s to the music of the early 1980s, enabling bands like Duran Duran, Japan, Ultravox, Adam Ant, Spandau Ballet, Visage, Soft Cell, and The Human League. "the first obvious descendants of Glam was the New Romantic movement. **Synth-Based Offshoot of Technopop** : New Romantic was a specific offshoot of synth-based music, originating in British clubs like Billies and The Blitz Club. This movement was instrumental in breeding groups such as Spandau Ballet, Visage, and Culture Club, characterized by their unique style and electronic sounds. "One such offshoot was known as New Romantic, which had its roots in a couple of British clubs dedicated to new music. One was called Billies, and the other was the Blitz Club. This was the breeding site of groups like Spandovalet, Visage, and Culture Club." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5PJRCC6EwUY ← new-romantic
**Emergence in the 1980s** : The "Let's Dance" album and song by David Bowie in 1983 were described as "glossy, new romantic new wave," aligning with the sound of contemporary bands like Duran Duran and Culture Club. This suggests New Romanticism as a distinct style within the broader New Wave movement characterized by sophistication and danceability.
