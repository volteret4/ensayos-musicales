While clean electric guitar sounds were acceptable in earlier decades, the desire for heavier and louder music necessitated the adoption of distortion and fuzz. "Modern Rock. **Drought for Female Soloists** : From 1996 to 2013, the modern rock chart (later Alternative Songs) experienced a 17-year drought where no female soloist topped the chart. Tracy Bonham's "Mother Mother" in 1996 was the last, until Lord's "Royals" in late summer 2013. **"Broification" of Rock Radio** : During the three years of Lilith Fair's original run (1997-1999), the "broification of rock radio" accelerated. By 1999, alt-rock stations were more likely to play bands like Limp Bizkit, Korn, Godsmack, Kid Rock, and Creed, whose music was characterized by "party bro lyrics," making the genre less hospitable to female artists.
