**Nile Rogers' Early Musical Interest** : In his late teens and early twenties, Nile Rogers, who identified as a "hippie," harbored a significant interest in avant-garde music, an inclination influenced by his stepfather's musical tastes. This early exposure to unconventional music broadened his artistic horizons beyond traditional soul. "Rogers was not a soul man, but a hippie.
