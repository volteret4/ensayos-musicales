**Commercial Potential Stretch** : In retrospect, it was considered "a stretch to believe that noise rock and post-hardcore were going to become platinum-selling genres," even at the peak 90s alternative rock moment, despite bands like The Melvins and The Jesus Lizard being signed to major labels. **Kurt Cobain's Influence** : Noise rock bands like Flipper, The Melvins, Scratch Acid, and The Jesus Lizard were among Kurt Cobain's favorite groups, highlighting their influence on Nirvana's artistic direction.
