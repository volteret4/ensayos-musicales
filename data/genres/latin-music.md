**Universe of Sounds, Styles, and Rhythms** : Latin music is described not merely as a genre, but as an entire universe of sounds, styles, and rhythms, developed over centuries and across generations by Afro-Caribbean and Iberian descendants. It encompasses a vast array of best-selling styles such as salsa, merengue, bachata, Mexican Ranchera, Bossa Nova, Samba, and Son. "But Latin music is more than a genre. **Influence on Mainstream English Pop** : Latin music has significantly shaped mainstream English-language pop through various elements, including the brass instrumentation characteristic of mariachi groups and the irresistible rhythm of the piano clave, which is derived from Latin jazz and salsa. These elements are often taken for granted in popular music. **Limited Success on U.S. Flagship Charts** : Historically, the success of Spanish-language music has been quite limited on mainstream U.S. flagship charts like the Hot 100 Songs Chart or the Billboard 200 album chart. Many songs considered legendary within the Latin music world have never even appeared on these charts, highlighting an "overwhelming Anglophone tendency" in U.S. pop. "On hit parade, we tend to focus on all genre charts like the Hot 100 Songs Chart, or the Billboard 200 album chart. **Latin Boomita, Version 1.0 (1999-2000)** : This period, kicked off by Ricky Martin's success in 1999, saw a surge of Latin stars on the American charts. By 2000, however, it began to show strain as nearly all the hits, despite their Latin rhythms and instrumentation, were in English and often produced by non-Latin collaborators aiming for Anglophone crossover. Critics and many longtime fans regarded this wave as "inauthentic and watered down." **Cultural Integration in South Texas** : In the Rio Grande Valley of Texas, "La Macarena" was seen as an "added spice" that "fit in well with our culture" of Latin music heritage and line dancing traditions. It provided an alternative to country western line dances, embraced by Latinos. "La Macarena was just like an added spice.
