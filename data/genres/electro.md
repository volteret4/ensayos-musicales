# genre - Electro

## curiosities
**Belgium Origin and Chemical Brothers Influence** : A new electro sound emerged in Belgium, which found a very important base in The Chemical Brothers' pioneering work in blending electronic and rock elements. This sound gave rise to artists like Soulwax and 2manydjs. **Dance Sound in New York and Chicago** : Electro was one of the various types of dance music that emerged from clubs in New York and Chicago, intriguing artists like New Order. It contributed to the evolving electronic sound that captivated the alternative dance scene. **Pop Chart Trend in Early 2010s** : The pop sound of the early 2010s prominently featured electro elements, along with Euro dance and EDM, influencing artists like Rihanna to shift her musical direction towards "happy and up-tempo records." **Relationship to Freestyle** : Electro is considered the "closest cousin and direct forebear" to freestyle, representing a culmination of various dance music forms that preceded it. "All of these forms of dance music eventually culminated in the short-lived genre that is freestyle's closest cousin and direct forebear. **Roots and Definition** : Electro is defined as a type of techno-pop that has its roots in the synth-based innovations of groups such as Kraftwerk, whose 1981 album "Computer World" is regarded as a "proto-electro" work.
