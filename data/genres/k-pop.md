# genre - K-pop

## curiosities
**DMZ Propaganda Countermeasure** : In South Korea, K-pop songs were blasted to the north from speakers set up along the south side of the demilitarized zone (DMZ). This was done to drown out propaganda announcements coming southward from North Korea, whose speakers were too weak to compete. The practice stopped in 2018 when it created too much tension and threatened hostilities.
