# genre - Singer-songwriter

## curiosities
**ABBA's Non-Affiliation** : ABBA was not considered part of the singer-songwriter movement that was active in the mid-1970s, further distinguishing their musical style. "they were not part of the singer songwriter movement." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 56 - Talk Shows of the 1970s | https://www.ftr70.com/
**Ben Howard's Style** : Ben Howard is described as a typical "singer with a guitar" in the singer-songwriter tradition, who has achieved considerable success in the UK, with a style comparable to artists like Bon Iver (referred to as Curnivar net in the transcript). "es el típico chico un tanto cantador con la guitarra." ← Music Radar Clan > LANZAMIENTOS JUNIO 2018 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AvdFC5Q0nKk&list=PLBFijugu0fhAAIR-Ibh59aaU8VWg-SkLc
**Part of Alt Rock Umbrella** : Singer-songwriters whose style didn't conform to the mainstream view of rock were among the "new fresh, strange and fascinating sounds" discovered under the umbrella of Alternative Rock in the 1990s. "Things like Goth, Industrial, Shoegays, Lofi, even singer songwriters that didn't fit the mainstream's view of Rock." ← Ongoing History of New Music > The 90s Part 6： The Punk Revival | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0QtMI00T5jw&list=PLM8bxV0E1Gse5C3n1u-mkVSTZLL5AJICG
**New Wave of Performers (Mid-1980s)** : The mid-1980s saw a resurgence of folk-inspired performers, including a new wave of singer-songwriters, who revitalized the genre after its peak decades earlier. This movement was notable for the emergence of strong and commercially successful female voices. "In the second half of the 1980s, more than a decade after the folk and singer-songwriter movements had peaked, a new wave of folk-inspired performers came to the fore." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Building a Herstory Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Stereotype of Soul-Bearing Diary Lyrics** : The music of the 1990s, particularly by women, was often stereotyped as "soul-bearing diary lyrics singer-songwriter." However, the chart-topping success and varied musical styles of women like the "90s sisters in song" rendered this narrow stereotype moot. "the music of this period was far more varied than the stereotype of the soul-bearing diary lyrics singer-songwriter." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Building a Herstory Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Distinct from Yacht Rock** : The singer-songwriter genre is largely considered distinct from Yacht Rock, with few crossovers. Songs like James Taylor's "Your Smiling Face" were excluded from the Yachtsky scale due to this classification. "Same thing, same thing with a lot of singer songwriter stuff." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Yacht Or Nyacht | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
