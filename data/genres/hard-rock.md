**AC/DC Rule Origin** : The hard rock band AC/DC lent its name to "The ACDC Rule," which states that the initial chart performance of a recording reflects the artist's last hit. This rule was coined because AC/DC's first and, for a long time, only number one album was the one released after their biggest seller, "Back in Black," illustrating how prior success influences subsequent chart entries. "I named it after ACDC because the hard rock bands first and for a long time only number one album was the LP after their biggest seller back in black." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Toppermost of the Poppermost Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef60c30a1408dc29358
