**AC/DC Rule Origin** : The hard rock band AC/DC lent its name to "The ACDC Rule," which states that the initial chart performance of a recording reflects the artist's last hit. This rule was coined because AC/DC's first and, for a long time, only number one album was the one released after their biggest seller, "Back in Black," illustrating how prior success influences subsequent chart entries.
