# genre - West Coast

## curiosities
**Influence on Kendrick Lamar** : The West Coast sound was a significant and early influence on Kendrick Lamar. From a very young age, his rapping style demonstrated maturity and drew heavily from this tradition, alongside inspirations from artists like NAS, Eminem, and Tupac. **Early Attempt at Classification** : "West Coast," often used as a single word, was another term applied to the smooth music of the 1970s before the advent of "Yacht Rock." Its lack of distinct imagery and poor searchability made it less effective for defining the genre in the "Google age." "Some of these other terms that we've seen applied to it, it's like West Coast, all one word.
