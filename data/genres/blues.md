# genre - blues

## curiosities
**Source of Floating Lyrics** : Carl Perkins' "Matchbox" incorporates "floating lyrics" found in various traditional blues songs, such as the verse "I'm a poor boy and I'm a long way from home" from the song "Poor Boy, Long Way From Home." This demonstrates the influence of traditional blues on early rockabilly and rock and roll. "Instead he uses floating lyrics that one can find in all sorts of other blues songs." ← Episode 51： ＂Matchbox＂ by Carl Perkins | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Nrjr0AGd3U
**Roots in Early "Hillbilly" Music** : The foundational influence of blues music is evident in the earliest recordings labeled as "hillbilly" records, such as Jimmie Rodgers' "blue yodels," which were inherently blues songs, sometimes featuring collaborations with prominent blues figures like Louis Armstrong. This highlights how initial industry categorizations imposed artificial divides between closely related musical forms. "the earliest hillbilly records are blues records right and yet there's this divide." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Can’t Tell Me Nothin’ | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Janis Joplin's Early Ambitions** : Janis Joplin's initial aspiration in San Francisco in 1963 was to make it as a blues singer, performing in coffee houses, highlighting the genre's foundational importance to her artistic identity. "She went out to San Francisco for the first time to try to make it as a blues singer, playing in the coffee houses." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Posthumous Hits Edition, Live from Seattle | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**CBGB's Original Intent** : Blues was one of the genres CBGB was originally named for, signifying Hilly Crystal's initial vision for the venue before it became famous for punk rock.
