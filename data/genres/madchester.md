**Coining of the Term "Madchester" and "Rave On"** : The term "Madchester" originated from two individuals from Newcastle who were creating videos for the Happy Mondays' "Rave On" EP. While searching for a title for tracks about all-night warehouse parties, a band associate (referred to as "Balsaw, the buddy on the album") jokingly suggested "Rave On." The press then reported on a "song about all night parties that they call raves," leading to the widespread adoption of "raves" for these events and "Madchester" for the scene, initially born from a casual remark. "And Balsaw, the buddy on the album, picks a buddy on the album up and went, oh, it's called Rave On. Just for a bit of fun. And then the next day the press was like, oh, this song about all night parties, that the call raves." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZStbEe4Gkk ← madchester ← madchester
