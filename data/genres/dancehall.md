# genre - Dancehall

## curiosities
**Influence on Jungle Music** : Dancehall, referred to as "dexhol" in the transcript, was a significant musical influence on the jungle genre. Its rhythmic and melodic characteristics, alongside ragga, contributed to jungle's distinct sound and deep bass lines. **Modernization and Commercial Rise (1990s)** : By the 1990s, dancehall music had been modernized for the hip-hop era and was rising alongside rap as a significant commercial proposition. Reggae fusion artists like Shabba Ranks were appearing on soundtracks, and Jamaican-American vocalists like Shaggy were breaking at pop radio, demonstrating its growing influence and fusion with American hip-hop.
