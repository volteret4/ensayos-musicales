**Emergence of "Beefs" (1984)** : The rap world saw the emergence of "beefs" or "Dis Records" in late 1984, with releases like Roxanne Shante's "Roxanne, Roxanne" and The Real Roxanne's "Roxanne's Revenge." While the "biggest beef" of the year was a media-concocted rivalry in pop, these rap dis tracks indicated a developing aspect of hip-hop culture. **Sampling of Sting and The Police** : The hip hop community, with its large sampling budgets, found Sting and The Police's "groove oriented" music a coveted source for samples and interpolations. This began before Puff Daddy's "I'll Be Missing You" and continued with artists like Spice One, Missy Elliott (for 702), Talib Kweli, Tupac, and Black Eyed Peas. "The police in particular were a very grove oriented group thanks to Sting's prominent baselines and Andy Summers' hypnotic guitar riffs.
