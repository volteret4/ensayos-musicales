**The Cramps' Abhorrence (Mid-1970s)** : In the mid-1970s, Poison Ivy and Lux Interior "abhorred" the progressive rock and singer-songwriter music that filled mainstream record stores, prompting them to seek out rarer, more primitive sounds in second-hand bins.
