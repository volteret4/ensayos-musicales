**KISS's Theatricality as Inspiration** : The American glam metal band KISS, known for dominating stages in the mid-1970s with their full makeup and never revealing their faces, deeply impressed Nile Rogers. This onstage gimmick inspired him to develop Sheik's own "faceless" band concept, seeking to combine theatrical anonymity with sophisticated artistic performance.
