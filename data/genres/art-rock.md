**Roxy Music's Influence on Sheik's Image** : British art rockers Roxy Music, especially their intricate musicianship and glam style personified by lead singer Brian Ferry, had a profound impact on Nile Rogers during a London club gig in the early 1970s. Their innovative use of glamorous models on album covers instead of band members also significantly inspired Rogers' vision for Sheik's distinctive aesthetic.
