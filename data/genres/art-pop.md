# genre - art pop

## curiosities
**Bowie's Consistent Style** : Art pop was a genre that characterized parts of David Bowie's discography, contributing to the 70s feel of his 2002 album *Heathen*. "No wonder then that the album felt somewhat like a 70s record, very art rock, very art pop, along with some of the dystopianism and dread heard on records like Ziggy Stardust and Aladdin St. Diamond Dogs and Station to Station." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yq2A_o9-ulM ← art-pop
