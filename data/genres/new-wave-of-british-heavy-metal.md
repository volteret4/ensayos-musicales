# genre - New Wave of British Heavy Metal

## curiosities
**Key Bands of the Movement** : This movement, active in the late 1970s and early 1980s, encompassed edgy bands such as Judas Priest, Iron Maiden, and initially, Def Leppard, before Mut Lang transformed Def Leppard's sound. **Origin of "New Wave of New Wave" Term** : The term "new wave of new wave" used for the early 1990s revival was reportedly poached by British music journalists from this earlier movement, which began around the turn of the 1980s. "which they seem to have poached from an earlier movement called the new wave of British heavy metal, which began around the turn of the 1980s." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9CNNsqXiL6U ← new-wave-of-british-heavy-metal
**Late 70s Early 80s Movement** : This movement encompassed metal bands such as Def Leppard, Judas Priest, and Iron Maiden, with Def Leppard identified as its biggest-selling band. The widespread use of the UK's Union Jack flag in Def Leppard's merchandise is a vestige of this movement. "The biggest selling band of the late 70s early 80s movement that was then called the New Wave of British Heavy Metal." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The You Give Rock a Bad Name Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Movement Definition** : This term was coined to describe a group of metal bands, including Judas Priest, Iron Maiden, and Def Leppard. The "new wave" designation referred to the next generation of bands in the genre, not specifically to the synthesizer-driven or aesthetic characteristics of pop new wave music. "They literally came up with a group of metal bands that were called the New Wave of British Heavy Metal. But back when that term was coined, they didn't mean new wave like synthesizers and Kaja Gugu hair or anything like that. They sort of meant new wave as in just the next wave of bands like Judas Priest and Iron Maiden and Deaf Leopard." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Music Trivia - Rock & Roll Hall of Fame Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
