## curiosities
**Nancy Spungin's Punk Hangout** : Max's Kansas City, another iconic New York punk venue, was a place where Nancy Spungin hung out, engaging with the punk scene and sometimes dealing drugs for bands. **Nile Rogers' Musical Development Hub** : This legendary Manhattan club served as a pivotal venue for Nile Rogers' musical development in the late 1960s and early 1970s. He frequented it, observed The Velvet Underground multiple times, and even performed there with his first band, New World Rising. Rogers likened its significance to his own career to that of Hamburg's Kaiser Keller for The Beatles, describing it as the place where he "honed his chops."
