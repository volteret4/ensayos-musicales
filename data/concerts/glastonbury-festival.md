# concert - Glastonbury Festival

## curiosities
**Radiohead's Legendary Set (June 28, 1997)** : On June 28, 1997, Radiohead performed a set at Glastonbury Festival that some maintain was "the best show of all time ever." Despite initial technical problems with stage monitors and a spotlight blinding Thom Yorke, the band overcame these issues, and Yorke's request to light the crowd created a moment where "the magic set in," solidifying the band's legend following the release of their album *OK Computer*. **Barry Gibb's Solo Performance (2017)** : Just shy of his 71st birthday in 2017, Barry Gibb performed a full set for thousands at England's Glastonbury Festival. With the help of instrumentalists and singers, he recreated the Bee Gees' hits, singing heartily and in falsetto, demonstrating his continued commitment to the band's legacy.
