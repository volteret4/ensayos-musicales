## curiosities
**Clash Induction and Failed Reunion Prospect (March 10, 2003)** : The induction ceremony for The Clash into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in New York on March 10, 2003, was seen as "the best ever prospect for a clash reunion." There were strong indications that the band would perform up to three songs, but Joe Strummer's death in December 2002 meant he never attended the ceremony. **Creedence Clearwater Revival Induction (1993)** : Creedence Clearwater Revival was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1993, which was notable as it was their very first year of eligibility. The induction occurred three years after Tom Fogerty's death, and was marked by palpable tension as John Fogerty refused to perform with former bandmates Stew Cook and Doug Clifford, although he did acknowledge them verbally.
