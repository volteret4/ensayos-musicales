**Traffic Chaos and Mass Migration (1969)** : By August 13, 1969, tens of thousands of cars were stuck on surrounding roads, unable to advance, as people attempted to access a venue without direct road entry. Frustrated and paralyzed, thousands abandoned their vehicles mid-road and chose to walk, in some cases up to 20 kilometers, to reach the festival. An estimated 180,000 of the nearly half a million attendees left their cars behind.
