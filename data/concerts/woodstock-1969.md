# concert - Woodstock (1969)

## curiosities
**Peaceful Festival** : The producers, Michael Lang and Artie Kornfeld, were considered fortunate that Woodstock was largely a peaceful festival, especially when compared to the chaotic and problematic Ozark Music Festival of 1974. "You know when I think about it all I just think about too how lucky Michael Lang and Arty Cornfeld were that Woodstock was largely a peaceful festival." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 46 - The Chaos and Music of the Ozark Music Festival of 1974 | https://www.ftr70.com/
**Career Launcher for Santana and Sly & The Family Stone** : The 1969 Woodstock mega-concert was instrumental in launching the careers of Carlos Santana and his band, introducing their fusion of Latin rhythms and furious guitar rock to a wider audience. It also solidified Sly and the Family Stone's reputation as fierce live performers. "As we discussed in our hip parade episode about the hit makers spawned by the Woodstock festival, the 1969 mega concert was instrumental in launching the career of Carlos Santana and introducing his fusion of Latin rhythms and furious guitar rock." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Spirit of ’71, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Influence on Subsequent Releases** : The *Woodstock* movie and its soundtrack, released in mid-1970, prompted Epic Records to reissue Sly and the Family Stone's 1969 track "I Want to Take You Higher" as a single, which subsequently made the Top 40. "First, they took advantage of the mid 1970 release of the Woodstock movie and its soundtrack by issuing the 1969 family stone track I Want to Take You Higher as a single." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Spirit of ’71, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Festival Dates and Location (August 15-18, 1969)** : The Woodstock Festival took place in upstate New York from August 15th through the 18th, 1969. The 50th anniversary of this historic event was commemorated in August 2019. "Back in 1969 from August 15th through the 18th, the Woodstock Festival took place in upstate New York." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Nostalgic for Number Ones | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Hot 100 Chart Performance of Acts (Week Ending August 16, 1969)** : During the week of the festival, no Woodstock act managed to enter the top 10 of the Billboard Hot 100. Blood, Sweat & Tears held the highest-ranking song, "Spinning Wheel," at number 11. Other notable acts on the chart that week included Credence Clearwater Revival, The Who, and Sly and the Family Stone. "Interestingly, no Woodstock act that week was in the top 10." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Nostalgic for Number Ones | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Top LPs Chart Performance of Acts (Week Ending August 16, 1969)** : Crosby, Stills & Nash had the highest-ranking album among Woodstock performers on the Billboard Top LPs chart during the festival week, with their self-titled debut album positioned at number 7, one spot away from its eventual peak of number 6. The Jimi Hendrix Experience's "Smash Hits" was at number 14, Joe Cocker's "With a Little Help from My Friends" was at number 49, and Canned Heat's "Hallelujah" was at number 59. "Their self-titled debut album was at number seven that week, one position away from its eventual peak of number six." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Nostalgic for Number Ones | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Impact on Careers** : It has been widely asserted that the original Woodstock Festival played a pivotal role in making or significantly advancing the careers of several performing artists. This claim is being examined through the analysis of chart performance in the months and years following the event.
