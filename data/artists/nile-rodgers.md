## curiosities
**Mid-90s Overdose and Eight Flatlines** : In the mid-1990s, renowned producer and musician Nile Rodgers was a severe abuser of drugs and alcohol, often partying with Robert Downey Jr. A particularly intense period of cocaine and alcohol use led him to the hospital, where he flatlined eight times. He was legally dead, and doctors were completing paperwork when he unexpectedly started breathing on his own. "He ended up in hospital where he flatlined eight times. He'd actually been legally dead, and the doctors were filling up the paperwork.
