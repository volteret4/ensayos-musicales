## albums
**Can't Slow Down (1983)** : This album, described as a "Yuppie Soul Blockbuster," won Album of the Year at the 1985 Grammy Awards, despite strong competition from albums like Prince's "Purple Rain" and Bruce Springsteen's "Born in the U.S.A." It was a commercial "smash," equaling or exceeding the sales of its fellow nominees and generating many "culture-blanketing hits." Richie's music was seen by the Academy's elders as the "kind of smooth shaman they found legible."
