## curiosities
**Euro Disco Influence** : His late 80s British pop sound was influenced by the Euro Disco scene, which was predominantly based in Italy and Germany. "That scene later influenced a lot of late 80s British pop like Rick Astley and Connie Menog." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1KF5MwJVYng ← rick-astley
**Inadvertent Rickrolling Icon** : Rick Astley became the unintentional face of the "Rickrolling" internet meme, discovering it when a friend Rickrolled him. He later participated in the phenomenon by performing the song live from a Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade float in 2008, a moment dubbed a "live Rick roll." "Rick Astley himself helped it along by unexpectedly emerging from a Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade float to sing the song. A live Rick roll." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bferkEWvJg0 ← rick-astley
**SAW Production's Disco Debt** : Rick Astley's success, particularly with "Never Gonna Give You Up," was notable because the production by Stock Aitken Waterman clearly owed a debt to vintage 70s club music, with synthesized string arrangements unabashedly recalling classic disco, marking a shift from the trend of hiding disco connections in pop music. "What was perhaps most notable about Rick Asley's success in particular was the production's obvious debt to vintage 70s club music." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The Queen of Disco Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**1987 Pop Chart Topper** : In 1987, Rick Astley was a highly successful, "unironic pop chart topper" in both the UK and the US. His cover of "When I Fall In Love" was a strong contender for the UK Christmas number one but was ultimately beaten by the Pet Shop Boys. "in 87 the froggy voiced crooner was an unironic pop chart topper in both the UK and the US." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Christmas is All Around Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Stock Aitken Waterman Collaboration** : Rick Astley scored a slew of hits with the British production team Stock Aitken Waterman starting in the mid-1980s.
