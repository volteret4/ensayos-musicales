## albums
**Empire (1990)** : Queensrÿche's "Empire" album debuted on the album chart in September 1990, the same week as Garth Brooks' "No Fences." It was legitimately huge and went platinum, eventually being certified for a million in sales by spring 1991 and sitting in the top 10 on the pop albums chart, illustrating the chart bias against country albums at the time, which would later be corrected by SoundScan. "The same week, Garth Brooks' No Fences debuted on the album chart in September 1990, so did the latest album by Meville Band, Queens Rider. I'm not picking on Queens Rider. Their Empire album was legitimately huge and deserved to go platinum. And it did. By the spring of 1991, Queens Rider's album was certified for a million in sales and it was sitting in the top 10 on the pop albums chart." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Friends in Low Places, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Adoption of the "Rock Dot" Umlaut** : Queensrÿche is listed as a band that adopted the umlaut (or "rock dot") in their name for aesthetic reasons, contributing to the visual style often associated with metal bands.
