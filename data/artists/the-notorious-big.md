**Juicy (1994)** : Biggie's second single ever and the first from his debut album "Ready to Die," this track mythologized his rags-to-riches story growing up in Brooklyn. It reached number one on Billboard's Hot Rap Singles chart and number 27 on the Hot 100 in November 1994, making it one of only two singles in this ranking to crack the pop top 30.
