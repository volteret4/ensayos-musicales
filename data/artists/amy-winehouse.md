**Relapses and Death (2011)** : Despite going to rehab several times, including a two-week stay in late January 2008, Amy Winehouse experienced a major relapse after a brief period of sobriety, which led to her death by alcohol intoxication on July 23rd, 2011. She had been sober for almost two weeks but began drinking again due to boredom, telling her doctor this three days before her death. The coroner's report stated she spent her last hours drinking vodka alone in her bedroom, watching YouTube videos of herself, leading to her stopping breathing, slipping into a coma, and dying with a blood alcohol level 20% above what is usually fatal. Her security guard found her lying face down in her bed at 2:30 AM and she had not moved by 3 PM the following afternoon when paramedics were called.
