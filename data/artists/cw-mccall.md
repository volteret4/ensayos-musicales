## songs
**Convoy (1975)** : Co-written with Chip Davis, this single was released in November 1975. It became a time capsule of trucker life in the mid-70s, featuring CB slang and telling a story of protest against the 55 mph speed limit, toll roads, and the difficulties faced by the trucking industry. The narrator, Rubberduck, leads a growing group of vehicles. The song hit number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in January 1976 and held the number one spot on the country chart for six weeks. Rolling Stone ranked it number 98 on its list of the top 100 country songs of all time, and it inspired a 1978 movie. "Convoy is a time capsule of trucker life in the mid-70s." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 31 - The Spirit of '76 - Pop Music on America's Bicentennial | https://seventies.libsyn.com/ep-31-the-spirit-of-76-pop-music-on-americas-bicentennial
**Convoy (1976)** : This novelty single became a number one hit on the Billboard Hot 100 in January 1976, also topping the country charts and reaching number one in Great Britain, Australia, and Canada. The song, written by Bill Freese with music by Chip Davis, is a story about a trucker named Rubber Duck who gathers a large convoy of trucks via CB radio to travel across the country, eluding helicopters and the National Guard. While its creation was inspired by the 1973 oil crisis and truckers' resistance to the 55 mph speed limit, Bill Freese maintained it was purely entertainment.
