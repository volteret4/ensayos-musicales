## curiosities
**Confederate Flag Use** : The band prominently used the Confederate flag in their imagery. Their lead guitar player, Stanley Knight, had a huge Confederate flag displayed on his guitar, and the drum risers during their performances were covered with a bunch of these flags. "Black Oak Arkansas's lead guitar player Stanley Knight had a huge Confederate flag on his guitar and the band covered the drum risers with a bunch of these flags." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 3 - Nostalgia, Race, and Rebels in 70s Southern Rock | https://seventies.libsyn.com/03-bittersweet-home-alabama
**Drum Samples for Beastie Boys** : The drums from a song by the Southern rock band Black Oak Arkansas were sampled in the Beastie Boys' track "Shake Your Rump." "and even some drums from a song by a southern rock band called Black Oak Arkansas." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l1jPP1FhGo8 ← black-oak-arkansas
**Rebellious Image and Criminal Activity** : The band not only glorified the image of the rebellious "hell raiser" but actively lived it. Two of their founding members stole public address equipment from their high school for their band, and subsequently engaged in a "little crime spree." They would have faced prison time if they had not been minors at the time.
