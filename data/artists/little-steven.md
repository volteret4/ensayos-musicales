## curiosities
**Sun City Mission Success (Later Statement)** : Years after the release of "Sun City," Little Steven pointed out that the song achieved its fundamental mission. It successfully made it "socially unacceptable for artists to perform in Sun City," thus achieving its activist goal despite modest chart performance.
