## albums
**Jesus Christ Superstar (1970)** : This concept album for the rock musical gained word-of-mouth popularity in late 1970, eventually topping the album chart in early 1971. The album's success provided the "leveling up" necessary for the title track single, performed by Murray Head, to finally become a top 40 hit after multiple failed attempts.
