**You and Your Hand (2007)** : Issued as a single in January 2007 from the album "I'm Not Dead," this song features a provocative title and is described as Pink's quintessential "kiss-off" anthem to "no good men." It's a blunt dressing-down of a would-be suitor in a nightclub attempting to buy her a drink. Co-written and produced by Max Martin, its fiendishly catchy melody, with his famous "pleasurable melodic math," makes it surprisingly uplifting for its blunt subject matter.
