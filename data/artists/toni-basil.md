## songs
**Mickey (1982)** : This song became a hit largely fueled by MTV, reaching number one on the Hot 100 in late 1982. Toni Basil, who was a singer, choreographer, and video artist, wrote the song specifically to accompany her video clip featuring a drill team of cheerleaders. This early appearance predated her later fame as an MTV star and showcased her involvement in avant-garde visual music.
