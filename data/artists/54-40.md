**Band Name Origin – American History, James Polk's "54-40 or Fight" Manifest Destiny Slogan (1840s)** : The name 54-40 came from Brad Merritt's stream-of-consciousness naming session in which he generated ideas including "Bridge View Dogs" (after their Surrey rehearsal space area) and "Safeway Rebels." He landed on 54-40, the slogan of US presidential candidate James Polk, who in the 1840s argued that America should take the entire Oregon Territory up to latitude 54°40' North — which included what is now British Columbia, at the time shared with Britain. The two founders had both moved around extensively (Merritt in the US, Osborne across Canada) and had met in high school in Tsawwassen, literally on the US border. "I wanted something which was big and yet small at the same time." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GI4aaHX9Izo ← 54-40

**Loss of Control Over Venue Branding** : The band eventually lost the battle against corporate branding in venues.
