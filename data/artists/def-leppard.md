**Pour Some Sugar On Me (1987)** : The initial idea for this song emerged in 1986 when Joe Elliott, alone in the studio, began playing an acoustic guitar and singing a hook. Producer Mutt Lange heard it and insisted it be developed into a song for the *Hysteria* album, despite the band having already worked for over two years. Lange quickly programmed a drum machine, added bass and chords, and Elliott sang the chorus for a "crappy demo." Phil Collen later contributed an iconic guitar riff for the verse. The song was the quickest to produce, taking only ten days, and evolved from an initially slower vocal melody (compared to The Beatles' "Help!") to a doubled-up, mid-tempo dance track popular in clubs, including among strippers. Released in Summer 1987, the single hit number two the following summer. Joe Elliott's lyrics, which he described as "nonsensical but it's got a fanatic power to it," were partly inspired by the Run-DMC and Aerosmith collaboration on "Walk This Way." Elliott also considered it "singer songwriter stuff" akin to Neil Sedaka, Carol King, or Burt Bacharach, aiming for a more mature sound.
