## curiosities
**Performance at The Troubadour** : Kris Kristofferson was observed performing at The Troubadour, which routinely featured artists playing multiple shows throughout the week, including two shows Tuesday through Thursday and three shows on Friday and Saturday. "And you know, I saw Chris Christopherson here." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 41 - The Impact of The Troubadour on 1970s Popular Culture | https://seventies.libsyn.com/ep-41-the-impact-of-the-troubadour-on-1970s-popular-culture
**Country Music as White Man's Soul Music (1991)** : Chris Christofferson stated in 1991 that country music was "white man's soul music," emphasizing its themes of drinking, cheating, and sex, which he noted were not typically discussed in pop music. This viewpoint, however, is considered problematic as it inherently excludes black people from participating in country music, either as performers or fans. "country is white man's soul music. It was about real things like drinking, cheating, sex, things they didn't talk about in pop music." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 53 - More than Charley Pride - African Americans in 70s Country | https://www.ftr70.com/
