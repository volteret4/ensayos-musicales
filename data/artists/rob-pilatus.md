**Post-Scandal Struggles and Death** : After the commercial failure of the "Rob and Fab" album, Rob Pilatus spent the remainder of the 1990s in and out of rehab, deeply affected emotionally by the rapid boom and bust cycle of fame. He attempted suicide in the early 1990s, and despite Frank Farian bailing him out of jail in 1997 and attempting a comeback, Pilatus was found dead on April 2, 1998, in a Frankfurt hotel room, due to an overdose of prescription drugs and alcohol.
