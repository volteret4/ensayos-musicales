# artist - Robert Christgau

## curiosities
**Praise for Fleetwood Mac's *Rumours* (1977)** : In a year-end review for the *Village Voice* in 1977, amidst the rise of punk, Christgau lauded Fleetwood Mac's album *Rumours*, stating that "rock and roll is supposed to be about pleasure as well as all the heavy stuff and I'm glad that in this year of the punk Fleetwood Mac is here to remind us of that."
