**Murder of the Universe (2017)** : This album, released recently in 2017, is the second installment of a projected five albums the band aimed to release within that year. It has left listeners deeply disconcerted, raising more questions than answers. The album is conceptual, divided into three seemingly independent stories, a format that was popular in the late 1960s but has since declined. Musically, it is described as incredibly complex and well-worked, featuring three distinct styles: epic progressive rock for the first part, epic heavy metal for the central part, and 70s psychedelic rock for the third part, all linked by an interesting musical thread despite the lyrical disconnections. A significant critique is the use of extensive spoken word narration by a man and a woman, which is said to detract from the music.
