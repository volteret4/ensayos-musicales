**Cannabis Resin Record Experiment** : Slightly Stupid experimented with pressing a record using cannabis resin, with the conceptual idea of listening to it and then melting it down to smoke. This experiment was highly impractical, costing $6,000 worth of resin for a single record and yielding very poor sound quality on a turntable. Only two copies made of bubble hash resin were ever produced, ultimately leading the band to smoke the resin and release the track "Dabbington" on regular vinyl.
