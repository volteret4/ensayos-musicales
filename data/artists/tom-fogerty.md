## curiosities
**Death (1990)** : Tom Fogerty passed away in 1990, three years before Creedence Clearwater Revival was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame. His brother, John Fogerty, acknowledged him in his acceptance speech at the induction ceremony.
