**Death at London Hilton (January 15, 2018)** : Dolores O'Riordan died in room 2005 at the London Hilton on Park Lane in Mayfair on January 15, 2018. She had flown from New York to London to work on a solo project titled "Dark" and discuss a new Cranberries album. At 1:12 am, she left an enthusiastic voicemail for a friend about the recording progress, and at 2:00 am, she called her mother, with things appearing fine. She was later found submerged in her bathtub, with numerous empty mini-bar alcohol bottles, an empty champagne bottle, and prescription pills in the room. Toxicology reports indicated that drugs were not an issue, but her blood alcohol content at the time of death was 0.33, more than four times the legal limit for drunk driving. Her death was ruled as accidental drowning due to alcohol toxicity. She was 46. On February 24, 2016, she was fined $6,600 Canadian, with the judge noting it would be unfair to be harsher given her mental illness.
