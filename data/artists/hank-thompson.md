## songs
**The Wild Side of Life (1952)** : This song was a country number one hit in 1952, spending 15 weeks at the top of Billboard's country best-sellers chart. It is described as a cynical lament about women who abandon traditional roles for nightlife, featuring the infamous line where Thompson sings with "mock naivete" about not knowing "God made honky tonk angels." The song's provocative lyrics prompted a direct response from Kitty Wells with her answer record. ## curiosities
**Honky Tonk Singer and Rockabilly Precursor** : Hank Thompson was a honky tonk singer who performed a "paired down version" of Bob Wills' western swing style. His music incorporated the rhythms and instrumentation of western swing but emphasized vocals and non-instrumental solos. Thompson was one of several artists whose music served as a precursor to rockabilly. He was most successful with mid-tempo ballads, such as "The Wild Side of Life." ← Episode 43： ＂I Gotta Know＂ by Wanda Jackson | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gEInec4z65Q
**Mentor to Wanda Jackson** : Thompson discovered Wanda Jackson when she was 14 during a TV audition. Impressed by her voice, he invited her to perform with his band, even though she was inexperienced. He couldn't take her on tour due to her schooling but mentored her on stage whenever he played in Oklahoma, teaching her how to perform with other musicians. He also invited her to appear on his local TV show, which eventually led to her own TV show.
