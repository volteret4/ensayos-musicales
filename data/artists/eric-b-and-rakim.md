## songs
**Friends (1989)** : Eric B. and Rakim were featured on Jody Watley's 1989 top 10 hit "Friends." Rakim contributed two long rap stretches, which were given clear, prominent showcase in the mix, with hip-hop lead producer Andre Simone practically clearing space for Rakim to rap and Eric B. to scratch. **My Melody (1986)** : This B-side classic by rap duo Eric B & Rakim was ironically titled "My Melody" because Rakim rapped "Check out My Melody" over an unsparing beat with scarcely any actual melody at all. This exemplified the "purism" prevalent in hip-hop's first decade, where male rappers largely avoided overt melodicism. **Paid in Full** : British DJ duo Coldcut produced this "now-legendary" remix, adding film dialogue and samples from James Brown and Israeli singer Ofra Haza. Original DJ Eric B reportedly hated it, but the remix transformed "Paid in Full" into a cross-cultural hit, reaching number three on Billboard's Club Play Chart in 1988 and becoming their biggest hit outside Billboard's rap chart. ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Invented the Remix Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
