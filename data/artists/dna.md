## songs
**Tom's Diner (1990)** : This song, credited to DNA featuring Suzanne Vega, was an "improbably excellent and somewhat novel" pop house jam. The British DJ duo DNA remixed Vega's a capella track into a hit that reached the top five on the pop chart and top 10 on the R&B chart by late 1990.
