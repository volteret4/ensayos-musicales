**Uptown Funk (2014)** : Co-written with Bruno Mars, this song faced a barrage of plagiarism lawsuits from artists such as The Gap Band, The Sequence, Kalash, Zap, and Victoria. The legal challenges resulted in the songwriting credits and royalties being divided among Ronson, Mars, and ten other parties, none of whom participated in the song's actual recording. **Uptown Funk (2014)** : A mega-blockbuster hit officially credited to Mark Ronson featuring Bruno Mars. Ronson had previously produced three tracks on Mars's "Unorthodox Jukebox" album, including "Locked Out of Heaven," and the duo had vowed to collaborate again. The song's creation was an extensive process, taking months and spanning studios in six cities, involving three horn sections (including Mars's band The Hooligans and Ronson's collaborators The Dap-Kings), over 100 takes, and famously featured Philip Lawrence singing the bass line. Released in November 2014, it spent 14 weeks at number one on the Hot 100 in early 2015 and won the Grammy for Record of the Year. "Uptown Funk was credited officially to Mark Ronson, the British born American-raised producer who had already won Grammys for Helming Hits by Amy Winehouse and Adele." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Lenny on Mars Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f54d1ba84fb8f0463bf
**Uptown Funk (2015)** : This song featuring Bruno Mars was the Billboard year-end number one for 2015 and the biggest hit for both men. Released in late 2014, it reached number one in January 2015 and stayed there for 14 weeks, guaranteeing its year-end title. Its "co-sure confidence" arrived as charts shifted towards streaming music and a hip hop era of bravado, defeating hits like Wiz Khalifa's "See You Again" and Fetty Wap's "Trap Queen."
