**Uptown Funk (2014)** : A mega-blockbuster hit officially credited to Mark Ronson featuring Bruno Mars. Ronson had previously produced three tracks on Mars's "Unorthodox Jukebox" album, including "Locked Out of Heaven," and the duo had vowed to collaborate again. The song's creation was an extensive process, taking months and spanning studios in six cities, involving three horn sections (including Mars's band The Hooligans and Ronson's collaborators The Dap-Kings), over 100 takes, and famously featured Philip Lawrence singing the bass line. Released in November 2014, it spent 14 weeks at number one on the Hot 100 in early 2015 and won the Grammy for Record of the Year.
