# artist - Jermaine Dupri

## songs
**What's Going On (2001)** : Jermaine Dupri produced the 2001 all-star cover of Marvin Gays' "What's Going On." This single, executive produced by Bonne, featured mega-stars like Britney Spears and Christina Aguilera and was initially for AIDS research, later shifted to benefit the Red Cross after 9-11.
