## songs
**Hair (1969)** : This family band from New England, who later inspired TV's "The Partridge Family," recorded a cover of the title song from "Hair" for a TV variety show. Its popularity led to its release as a single, which rose to number 2 on the Hot 100 by mid-May, swept up in "Hair Mania" alongside The Fifth Dimension's number one hit. "The Cow Sills cover proved so popular that they issued it as a single and got swept up into Hair Mania. By mid-May, with the fifth dimension's Aquarius Let The Sunshine In still at number 1, the Cow Sills Hair rose to number 2." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Lullaby of Broadway Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
