**Gangnam Style (2012)** : This song marked America's first major K-pop hit, showcasing YouTube's significant hit-making prowess. Its video featured Psy performing his comical "horsey riding dance," which served as a precursor to dances like those associated with "Old Town Road." The video made history by becoming the first YouTube video to surpass one billion views globally. This viral success spurred over 5 million downloads in America, which in turn generated terrestrial radio airplay, leading to its peak at 12th on Billboard's weekly radio songs chart—a remarkable achievement given that 98% of the lyrics were Korean. It was America's top-selling download for more than a month, peaking at number two on the Hot 100 for seven weeks in the fall of 2012, primarily due to limited radio spins, just missing the integration of YouTube data into Billboard's charts. **Gangnam Style (2012)** : This song was a pop phenomenon likened to Billy Ray Cyrus's "Achy Breaky Heart," complete with a "horsey riding maneuver" dance that resembled a country line dance. It sold over five million copies in America, becoming the biggest K-pop track in US history.
