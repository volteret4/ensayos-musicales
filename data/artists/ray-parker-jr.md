**Ghostbusters (1984)** : This title song from the movie "Ghostbusters" became a number one hit. Tasked with writing a song for a sci-fi comedy starring Bill Murray and Dan Aykroyd, Parker Jr. creatively turned the challenging word "Ghostbusters" into a call-and-response chant. The song faced a plagiarism dispute with Huey Lewis and The News over similarities to "I Want a New Drug," which was settled out of court. Forty years later, it remains an indelible comic film theme, appearing in sequels and enjoying perennial play every Halloween. It was also one of five number one hits nominated for the Best Original Song Oscar in 1984. **Creative Songwriting Solution** : When asked to write the theme for the movie "Ghostbusters," Ray Parker Jr. faced the challenge of few words rhyming with "Ghostbusters." He cleverly solved this by transforming the word into an iconic call-and-response chant. "Parker realized there weren't too many words that rhymed with Ghostbusters.
