# artist - Ray Parker Jr.

## genres
- Technopop-Ballad

## songs
**Ghostbusters Theme Song (1984)** : Ray Parker Jr. was enlisted to create the iconic theme song for the "Ghostbusters" film after an earlier, less effective theme was used in promotional pleas to Canadian movie chains. His version became synonymous with the 1980s movie starring Bill Murray, Harold Ramis, Ernie Hudson, and Dan Aykroyd. "So they got Ray Parker Jr. to do a new theme." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HoBx6s3JmVQ ← ray-parker-jr
**I Just Can't Get Over Loving You (1984)** : This "lush Technopop-Ballad" was a top 20 hit, released a few months earlier than Wang Chung's "Dance Hall Days." Its sound demonstrated a cross-genre appeal, hinting at the increasing blend of styles in 1984 pop music.
