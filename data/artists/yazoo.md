**Situation (1982)** : This 1982 classic brought synth pop to the dance floor, becoming a number one club hit despite only reaching number 73 on the US pop chart. It combined a "acetic British chill" with Alison Moyet's "soulful American sounding vocals," famously including her distinctive laugh. "New Wave duo Yazoo...brought synth pop to the dance floor with their 1982 classic Situation. ## curiosities
**Difficult Recording of Second Album** : The second Yazoo album was recorded under strained circumstances, with Vince Clarke and Alison Moyet literally not speaking to each other. Clarke would work on the music, leave the studio, and then Moyet would come in to record vocals, with messages about retakes left through the recording team.
