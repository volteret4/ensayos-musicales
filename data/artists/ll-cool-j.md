**Bigger and Deffer (1987)** : Recorded in the closing weeks of 1986 and released in spring 1987, this album expanded LL's collaborators, working with DJ Pooh, who helped maintain his street credibility. It reached number three on the album chart by summer 1987, matching Run DMC's peak the year before, and presented his biggest pop crossover move to date. It achieved double platinum status and eventually sold three million copies.
