**All I Want for Christmas Is You (1994)** : Co-written and co-produced by Mariah Carey and Walter Afanasieff, this song was recorded in the summer of 1994. Sony Music released a video in October 1994 but opted not to issue it as a retail single, which was a common business strategy in the 1990s to encourage album sales. Consequently, it only appeared on Billboard's Radio Songs chart, peaking at number 12. Despite not being an active single, it debuted on the Hot 100 at number 83 in the 1999-2000 holiday season after a rule change allowing album cuts to chart. In 2003, it debuted on Billboard's Hot Digital Tracks chart, rising into the top 10 as the highest-ranking digital track not a current single, marking its first top 10 appearance on any Billboard chart. In 2019, its 25th anniversary year, it became the number one song in America, making it the highest-charting holiday hit of the 21st century and the highest since 1958.
