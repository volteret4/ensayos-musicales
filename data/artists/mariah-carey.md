## songs
**All I Want for Christmas Is You (1994)** : Originally released in 1994, this song is considered the all-time modern holiday song champion, generating an estimated $70 million USD in royalties despite being played only two months a year. It is one of the best-selling singles of all time, not just Christmas singles, and has been covered numerous times by artists across various genres. "Mariah Carey's All I Want for Christmas is You, which originally came out in 1994." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nYPGQiDL5bw ← mariah-carey
**All I Want for Christmas Is You (1994)** : Mariah Carey's Christmas classic, recorded in 1994, was not released as a single at the time but only as an album cut, a deliberate decision by Carey and Sony Music. Consequently, it was ineligible for the Hot 100 in 1994 and did not chart. Its eventual topping of the Hot 100 a quarter-century later is attributed more to Billboard metrics catching up with its inherent popularity rather than a traditional second chance hit narrative, as the song was always popular. "Here's the thing about Carey's hit. It's not an archetypal second chance hit. **Fantasy (1995)** : This song became Mariah Carey's ninth number one hit, out of a career total of 19, in September 1995. It was historic for several reasons, being one of the first sample-based pop songs to top the Hot 100 and, in its remix featuring Ol' Dirty Bastard, it helped establish the rap and R&B duet. Most notably, Fantasy was the first song by a female artist and one of the first songs overall to debut at number one on the Hot 100, which had never happened before 1995 for a female artist. Unlike Michael Jackson's "You Are Not Alone," "Fantasy" turned out to be a massive and well-loved hit, spending eight weeks at number one through the fall of 1995 and setting up an even bigger follow-up hit. "Fantasy was historic for several reasons. It was one of the first sample based pop songs to top the hot 100." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Toppermost of the Poppermost Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef60c30a1408dc29358
