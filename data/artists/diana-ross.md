**Recording "Love Hangover"** : When producer Hal Davis presented Diana Ross with "Love Hangover," she initially disliked the music, though she liked the lyrics. Davis then transformed the studio into a club atmosphere with red and strobe lights to help Ross feel the mood, resulting in her genuine laughter being captured and left in the final recording. **Barry Gordy's Attraction and Group Signing** : Motown staff widely believed that The Primettes (later The Supremes) were signed primarily because of Barry Gordy's attraction to Diane Ross, rather than the group's inherent talent, despite their initial lack of success. "Motown had already signed one girl group, a group called The Primates, who had been renamed and who had so far released two singles. But the Supremes, as they were renamed, wouldn't become successful for several years, and were generally regarded as a joke among the Motown staff, who thought, not entirely without reason, that they had been signed more because Barry Gordy was attracted to Diane Ross, one of the members of the group, than because of any talent they had." ← Episode 93： ＂Please Mr. Postman＂ by the Marvelettes | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPg8Wx30-uM
**Successful Disco Pivot (1970s)** : After leaving The Supremes in 1970 for a solo career, Diana Ross successfully adapted to the disco sound, scoring chart toppers such as "Love Hangover."
