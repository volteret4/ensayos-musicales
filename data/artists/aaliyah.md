## albums
**One in a Million (1996)** : This album, largely shaped by Timbaland and Missy Elliott's production, went double platinum. It featured the hit singles "If Your Girl Only Knew" and "One in a Million," solidifying Aaliyah's new sound and establishing Timbaland and Missy Elliott as in-demand hitmakers. ## songs
**Are You That Somebody? (1998)** : Recorded in a single night for the "Dr. Dolittle" film soundtrack, this became Aaliyah's most acclaimed single and a definitive showcase for Timbaland's beatmaking genius. The track was built from stuttering guitar licks, beatboxing, a halting rap by Timbaland, and a sample of a baby cooing, included as an homage to Aaliyah, whom Timbaland affectionately called "Baby Girl." Despite its futuristic and complex sound, Aaliyah's "fluttery start-stop vocals" tied it together. It reached number 4 on Hot 100 Airplay and spent eight weeks at number 1 on the R&B Airplay chart, riding the charts for nearly eight months and remaining one of her most enduring and played tracks. "Are you that somebody went on to become Alias most acclaimed single and the ultimate showcase for Tim Beland's beatmaking genius." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Flip It and Reverse It Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
