## songs
**Another Way to Die (2008)** : Alicia Keys supplied the vocals for "Another Way to Die," a duet with Jack White for the 2008 James Bond film "Quantum of Solace." This marked the first duet in the history of Bond themes. The video was shot in Toronto while Keys was promoting her film "The Secret Life of Bees." "Keys supplied the vocals." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K0pgJ7XAlpo ← alicia-keys
**Fallen (2001)** : This number one hit by Alicia Keys was one of the culturally relevant and commercially successful songs that lost Record of the Year to U2's "Walk On" at the 2002 Grammys. It was noted for having a "much longer legacy and much stronger Spotify streams" than the winning song. "Walk On beat a pair of number one hits, fallen by Alicia Keys." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > And the Grammy Goes to… Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eef154465cd600c4ddc
**My Boo (2004)** : This duet with Usher contributed to his spending "more than half the year on top of the Hot 100" in 2004, a period of peak imperial status. "he spent more than half the year on top of the Hot 100, including his Alicia Keys duet My Boone." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Material Girl in an Imperial World Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f03154465cd600c5379
