## songs
**Licensed to Kill (1989)** : Gladys Knight was brought in to sing the title theme for the 1989 James Bond film "Licensed to Kill." The song, co-written by Narada Michael Walden, had a very 80s sound and production, becoming a hit in Europe and reaching number 79 in Canada, though it did not chart in America. "Gladys Knight was brought into sing and the song was a hit but only in Europe." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K0pgJ7XAlpo ← gladys-knight
**That's What Friends Are For (1985)** : Gladys Knight, described as "still an R&B hitman," was invited by Dionne Warwick to contribute vocals to this benefit single for AIDS research. She joined Warwick, Stevie Wonder, and Elton John in creating a "super friends of pop" collaboration. "Warwick proposed that they bring in a couple more vocalists. Her first choice was Gladys Knight, who was still an R&B hitman." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Killing Me Softly Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

**Motown Sound with Southern Flavor** : Signed with her group The Pips to Motown in the late 1960s, Gladys Knight was noted for fusing the polished Motown sound with a grittier, more southern-flavored R&B style. "Gladys Knight, who was signed with her group The Pips to Motown in the late 60s, they fused that polished motor town sound with a grittier, more southern flavored R&B." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I'm Your Whitney Tonight Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
