## songs
**Blurred Lines (2013)** : Co-written with Robin Thicke, this song led to a lawsuit from Marvin Gaye's estate, accusing them of copying the "feel and sound" of Gaye's "Got to Give It Up." Williams and Thicke admitted to deliberately trying to copy the song's groove and feel as an homage. Despite expert testimony on musical differences, a jury awarded the Gaye estate over $8 million, a ruling that caused outrage in the music community and set a precedent for "feel"-based lawsuits. **Happy (2014)** : This "unicorn of a single" was written for the kids' movie "Despicable Me 2," nominated for an Oscar, and served as the soundtrack to a 24-hour music video. It's a throwback slice of retro soul that topped the Hot 100 for ten weeks. Pharrell Williams took sole credit for writing, producing, and performing the song, making it a "self-powered smash" that was inescapable in 2014. It defeated Katy Perry's "Dark Horse" and Iggy Azalea's "Fancy" for the year-end title.
