**Murder at Chelsea Hotel (October 12, 1978)** : Nancy Spungen, girlfriend of Sid Vicious, was found dead from a single stab wound to her stomach in room 100 of the Chelsea Hotel in New York on October 12, 1978. Nancy had a troubled life, diagnosed with schizophrenia at 15 and supporting herself through prostitution from age 17 before becoming a groupie to bands like The Ramones, New York Dolls, and Aerosmith. She became inseparable from Sid Vicious for 19 months, and they both were heroin and Tuinal addicts. On the night of October 11, 1978, several people visited their room, and Sid allegedly took up to 52 Tuinal tablets, completely incapacitating him. At around 2:30 am on October 12th, Nancy asked Rockets Redglare, their drug connection and Sid's bodyguard, for Dilaudid. Later, around 7:30 am, female moans were heard, and at 10 am, Sid called the front desk. Nancy was found stabbed and bleeding to death, in a weird angle under the bathroom sink. Sid initially confessed but was likely too incapacitated. The theory later emerged that Rockets Redglare, who died in 2007, was the murderer, allegedly stabbing Nancy with Sid's Bowie knife during an argument over stolen cash, a confession he reportedly made while drinking at CBGB. "Nancy Spungin, girlfriend of ex-sex Bristol Sidvicious, at the Chelsea Hotel on October 12, 1978." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zM3gu_nbw-k ← nancy-spungen ← nancy-spungen
**Unsolved Mystery - Suspects and Theories** : While Sid Vicious was charged with her murder, many dispute his guilt, suggesting a New York actor and drug dealer named Rockets Red Glare (Michael Mora) as a primary alternative suspect. A theory posits that Rockets brought drugs to the hotel around 1:30 AM on October 12, stayed until 5 AM while Sid was unconscious, stole a large amount of money (up to $20,000), and then murdered Nancy, whose autopsy indicated she died between 5 and 9 AM. "The favorite suspect by most is a New York actor and drug dealer who went by the name Rockets Red Glare. He was a hangar on with Sid and Nancy, often supplying them with drugs. At around 1.30 in the morning of October the 12th, Nancy called Rockets and asked him to bring some drugs. He arrived somewhere after 3 and stayed until about 5. Remember Sid is apparently completely unconscious at this point. The autopsy on Nancy suggested that she died between 5 and 9 am. The police report also says that a large amount of money was missing from the room, maybe as much as $20,000. Rockets was spotted at the hotel at around 4.
