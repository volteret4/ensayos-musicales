## songs
**The Middle (2001)** : Released in Fall 2001 as the lead single from "Bleed America," "The Middle" was emo's true radio breakthrough, expertly disguising its misfit sympathies beneath a shiny pop exterior. The music video depicted an introvert at a party who finds an equally shy girl, reinforcing the relatable lyrics: "just try your best, try everything you can. Be yourself, it doesn't matter if it's good enough." The song made emo broadly relatable and musically irresistible, debuting on the modern rock chart in November 2001, hitting number one in April 2002 after nearly six months, and astonishingly reaching number five on the pop chart by June. "The middle made emo broadly relatable and musically irresistible." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Write Sins, Not Tragedies, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Work (2004)** : In 2004, Jimmy Eat World scored another significant hit with the anthemic song "Work," which reached number one on the modern rock chart.
