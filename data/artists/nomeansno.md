**Van Theft & Polish Mafia (Poland)** : While on tour in Poland one Christmas, NoMeansNo had their rented van stolen after a show in Warsaw, containing about $10,000 worth of merchandise. With local police being unhelpful and the Dutch rental company concerned about insurance coverage outside Holland, a fan connected them with the Polish Mafia. Band member Rob Wright met two heavily armed men in a church basement and negotiated the van's return for 2000 German marks. The next day, the van and all its contents were recovered from a barn outside Warsaw. However, the Warsaw police later demanded another 2000 German marks for operating outside legal channels. "The next day the van was given the name of a village about 100 kilometers outside of Warsaw and told to wait in the town square." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DgN0vBIugLc ← nomeansno
