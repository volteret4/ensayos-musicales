## curiosities
**Huge Influence on Soul Music** : Billy Ward and The Dominoes were a massive influence on the development of soul music. Their lead singers, Clyde McFatter and Jackie Wilson, sang in a style heavily indebted to the church music that the Isley Brothers had also performed, making them a natural inspiration for the Isley Brothers' transition from gospel to doo-wop. "Billy Ward and the Dominoes were a huge influence on the music that became soal, with hit records like Have Mercy Baby." ← Episode 81： ＂Shout＂ by the Isley Brothers | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USJDERCABjk
**Biggest Black Vocal Group** : The Dominoes were recognized as the biggest black vocal group of the early 1950s. "They were the biggest black vocal group of the early 50s." ← Episode Sixty-Four： ＂Reet Petite＂ by Jackie Wilson | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYU-H4NvnmE
**Chart Decline Post-McPhatter** : After Clyde McPhatter's departure, The Dominoes began to experience less success in the charts, having no hits at all in 1954. "Their records weren't telling as well as they had been when Clyde McFatter was the group lead singer, and in 1954 they had no hits at all." ← Episode Sixty-Four： ＂Reet Petite＂ by Jackie Wilson | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYU-H4NvnmE
**Discipline from Ward** : Billy Ward taught Jackie Wilson breath control and provided him with much-needed discipline, becoming a father figure. "Ward taught in breath control and became something of a father figure, giving him some discipline for the first time in his life." ← Episode Sixty-Four： ＂Reet Petite＂ by Jackie Wilson | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYU-H4NvnmE
**Focus on Live Performances** : The group's strategy was not solely to succeed as recording artists but also as live performers, securing a lucrative two-year residency in Las Vegas. "The group weren't just looking to have success as recording artists, but as live performers." ← Episode Sixty-Four： ＂Reet Petite＂ by Jackie Wilson | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYU-H4NvnmE
**Jackie Wilson's Departure** : Jackie Wilson decided to leave the group in 1957 because it became apparent that Billy Ward's ambitions extended no further than continuing to play Las Vegas indefinitely, earning vast amounts of money without hard work or travel, while Wilson desired more from his career. "It had become a pament that Ward had no bigger ambitions than to keep playing Las Vegas forever, and keep making vast amounts of money without having to travel or work especially hard." ← Episode Sixty-Four： ＂Reet Petite＂ by Jackie Wilson | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYU-H4NvnmE
**Leader Billy Ward as Vocal Coach** : The group was led by Billy Ward, who served as a vocal coach and was not their lead singer. "They were led by Billy Ward, a vocal coach who was not their lead singer." ← Episode Sixty-Four： ＂Reet Petite＂ by Jackie Wilson | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYU-H4NvnmE
**Lucrative Las Vegas Residency** : The Dominoes secured a two-year residency in Las Vegas, supporting Louis Prima and Keely Smith, earning a massive $5,000 a week, though most of this went to Ward. "They got a two year residency in Las Vegas, supporting Louis Prima in Keely Smith. The group were getting $5,000 a week, at massive amount of money in those days." ← Episode Sixty-Four： ＂Reet Petite＂ by Jackie Wilson | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYU-H4NvnmE
