## albums
**Sports (1983)** : This LP was a massive success for Huey Lewis and The News, a San Francisco band that had been active since 1979. By the time "If This Is It" cracked the Hot 100 in the summer of 1984, "Sports" had topped the Billboard album chart and sold 5 million copies. The album's success was greatly aided by a string of "high-concept, comical and plot-heavy videos" that transformed the band's career. "For their LP sports, the Telegenic Huey Lewis and his band recorded a string of high-concept, comical and plot-heavy videos.
