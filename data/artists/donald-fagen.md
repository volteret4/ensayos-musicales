## albums
**The Nightfly (1982)** : This solo debut album was released in the closing weeks of 1982 and was nominated for Album of the Year at the Grammys, where it lost to Toto's *Toto IV*. It represents a key work from a Steely Dan member fitting the Yacht Rock aesthetic.
