## albums
**Brave New World (1969)** : This album featured a collection of psychedelic rock songs, notably including "space cowboys." It was one of two albums released by the band in 1969. ## songs
**Abracadabra (1982)** : This song by the Steve Miller Band was listed as the number 9 Billboard Top 10 hit for the year 1982. "number nine Abracadabra Steve Miller band." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 55 - Heartland Rock is about More than the Heartland | https://www.ftr70.com/
**Jackson-Kent Blues (1970)** : Steve Miller wrote this song expressing outrage and frustration over the Jackson State and Kent State shootings. The lyrics criticize politicians for being unresponsive and preoccupied with voters while students are killed, and lament the "silent majority" glued to television screens, suggesting government agencies like the CIA and FBI are coming "unglued." "Looking for my congressman to make it well known, but the politicians already won't answer his telephone, making it his office while they're shooting kids down at home worried about the voters, but he won't be worried long." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 43 - Music and The Kent State Massacre of May 4, 1970 | https://seventies.libsyn.com/ep-43-music-and-the-kent-state-massacre-of-may-4-1970
