## albums
**Someone's Ugly Daughter (1995)** : This grunge album was secretly recorded by Mariah Carey under the band name Chick in 1995, during a period when she felt burned out on pop music. Carey wrote, produced, sang backup vocals, and directed the videos for the project, which is described as good and typical of angsty female-fronted rock from the 1990s.
