## songs
**You Ain't Seen Nothing Yet (1974)** : As a member of Bachman-Turner Overdrive, Randy Bachman recorded this 1974 number one hit. The song, featuring a stuttering chorus hook, was originally intended only for his brother, who had a speech impediment, and was not meant to be a national hit.
