**BBC Junior Studio Engineer (1942)** : In 1942, Daphne Oram began her career as a junior studio engineer at the BBC. Her initial responsibility was to ensure uninterrupted music broadcasts during Nazi bombing raids by seamlessly switching to recorded music if live transmissions were disrupted, serving as a morale booster and a psychological tactic against the enemy. Sin embargo, en 1959, Oram abandonó el proyecto que había fundado, ya que estaba más interesada en desarrollar su propia técnica musical y enfoque creativo, lo que la llevó a fundar su propio estudio.
