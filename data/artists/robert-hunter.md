## curiosities
**Co-writer for Bob Dylan** : Robert Hunter, the longtime lyricist for the Grateful Dead and a friend of Bob Dylan, was one of the very few co-writers Dylan ever composed with. Hunter contributed to the songwriting on Dylan's 2009 album "Together Through Life," marking a rare collaboration in Dylan's largely solitary songwriting career.
