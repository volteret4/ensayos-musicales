# artist - Ashlee Simpson

## curiosities
**Saturday Night Live Pre-recorded Track Malfunction** : Ashlee Simpson's career was significantly impacted by a highly publicized "melt down" during her performance on Saturday Night Live. Her pre-recorded audio tracks malfunctioned on live television, "exposing the charade" of her lip-syncing and effectively bringing her mainstream run to an end due to the subsequent public backlash over her lack of authentic live performance.
