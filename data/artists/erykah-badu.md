**Neo-Soul Standard (Turn of the Millennium)** : Erykah Badu was cited as an example of neo-soul, a style of "adult R&B" that was typical around the turn of the millennium. "It was an adult R&B record, which around the turn of the millennium typically meant so-called neo-soul, such as D'Angelo, Erika Badu or Jill Scott." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > A Little Love and Some Tenderness Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**R&B Newcomer and Diversifier** : Erykah Badu, an R&B newcomer enjoying her "first wave of critical acclaim," helped diversify Lilith Fair in Year Two (1998). Her inclusion was part of the festival's effort to broaden its musical scope. She also returned for the 2010 Lilith Fair revival. "Other artists who helped diversify Lilith in Year 2 were R&B newcomer Erica Badu, who was enjoying her first wave of critical acclaim." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Building a Herstory Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
