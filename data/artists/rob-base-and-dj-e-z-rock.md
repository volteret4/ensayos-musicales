## curiosities
**Charted on Hot Rap Singles (1989)** : Rob Base and DJ E-Z Rock appeared at number six on Billboard's first-ever Hot Rap Singles chart in March 1989, positioned just below Millie Vanille. This placement indicates their status as significant acts in the early days of dedicated rap charts.
