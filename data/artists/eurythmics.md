## songs
**Here Comes the Rain Again (1983)** : This "ghostly number 4 hit" exemplified the British New Wave and Synthpop movements during the second British invasion. Its unique sound contributed to the UK's redefinition of the "cutting edge of cool" in America, fueled by MTV. "Here they are with their ghostly number 4 hit, Here Comes the Rain Again." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > What’s 1984 Got to Do with It Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef5154465cd600c4fa3
**Sweet Dreams (Are Made of This) (1983)** : This R&B in Synthpop Clothing hit is one of the most iconic songs of the second British invasion, with estimates suggesting it has been covered, sampled, or interpolated around 130 times. Its music video, featuring Annie Lennox in drag, Dave Stewart playing a computer keyboard, and cows in a conference room, was considered "MTV Gold." The song took 17 weeks to climb the Hot 100, waiting out The Police's "Every Breath You Take" for four weeks at number two before taking the number one spot the first week of September 1983. "Sweet dreams are made of this. One of the most iconic hits of the second British invasion was R&B in Synthpop Clothing, whose sample.com estimates that sweet dreams has been covered, sampled, or interpolated, around 130 times." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The British Are Charting Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f7a154465cd600c7ae7
