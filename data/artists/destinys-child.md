**The Writing's On the Wall (1999)** : This album served as the group's second release, successfully obliterating any notion of a "sophomore slump" and selling 8 million copies in America alone. It built upon the legacy of earlier 1990s girl groups like En Vogue and TLC, but critics, such as John Perellis of The New York Times, noted the group's subtle sonic advances, particularly Beyoncé's voice and the dynamic way melodies interacted with rhythm tracks. The album made good on the group's long-standing effort to fuse Beyoncé's potent R&B singing with hip hop rhythms. "The Writing's On the Wall finally made good on this bespoke formula." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say My Name, Say My Name, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
