## songs
**Fireflies (2009)** : In 2009, this debut single by Christian electro-pop artist Adam Young, known as Owl City, reached number one on the Hot 100 in America. Unlike the Arctic Monkeys, the introverted Adam Young actively curated his Myspace profile, using it to develop a fanbase that ultimately led to his signing with major label Universal Republic. **Fireflies (2009)** : Owl City's first and only major solo hit, this song was a Number One smash in 2009. It is remembered as a higher-charting and better-recalled hit than "Good Time." **Good Time (2012)** : A duet with Carly Rae Jepsen, this song was a Top 10 hit in September 2012. It held statistical importance for both artists, guaranteeing that they could not fairly be referred to as one-hit wonders by long-time chart columnist Paul Grine. His manager highlighted that "people feel like they know Owl City, like they've got a direct connection to him, because of how he approaches his connection with them online." This illustrates the growing importance of direct artist-fan connection through social media.
