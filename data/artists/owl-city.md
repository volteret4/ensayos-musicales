## songs
**Fireflies (2009)** : In 2009, this debut single by Christian electro-pop artist Adam Young, known as Owl City, reached number one on the Hot 100 in America. Unlike the Arctic Monkeys, the introverted Adam Young actively curated his Myspace profile, using it to develop a fanbase that ultimately led to his signing with major label Universal Republic. **Future of Music Business (2009)** : Owl City was seen by his label, Universal Republic, as "representing the future of our business." His manager highlighted that "people feel like they know Owl City, like they've got a direct connection to him, because of how he approaches his connection with them online." This illustrates the growing importance of direct artist-fan connection through social media.
