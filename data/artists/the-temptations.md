## concerts
- **Live Aid (1985)** : Two members of The Temptations, David Ruffin and Eddie Kendrick, performed at the Live Aid concert in 1985. Notably, they performed alongside Darryl Hall and John Oates, rather than with Phil Collins. "two of the temptations David Ruffin and Eddie Kendrick did indeed perform at Live Aid, but with Darryl Hall and John Oats, not Phil Collins."

**War (1970)** : This controversial song, written by Motown Legends Norman Whitfield and Barrett Strong during the height of the Vietnam War, was first recorded by The Temptations. However, Motown's Barry Gordy, fearing it would politicize the group's image, refused to issue it as a single for them, only allowing its release as a 45 if another Motown artist recorded it, leading to Edwin Starr's famous version. "Fearful of politicizing the image of Motown's flagship vocal group, Barry Gordy refused to issue war as a Temptations single, and he would only put it out as a 45 at all if someone else at Motown would record it." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Tramps Like Us, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

**Live Performances with Hall and Oates (1985)** : David Ruffin and Eddie Kendricks of The Temptations performed a medley of Motown classics, including "My Girl," with Hall and Oates at New York's legendary Apollo Theatre in May 1985. This performance was reprised at Live Aid in July 1985. A single of this live mashup brought Ruffin and Kendricks back to the Top 40 for the first time in a decade. "after repreasing that set with them at Live Aid in July, the duo took full advantage of their clout to issue not only a Darryl Hall and John Oats live at the Apollo LP, but also a single with Ruffin and Kendrick's, bringing the two temps back to the top 40 for the first time in a decade." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Rock ’n Soul, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
