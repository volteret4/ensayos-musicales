**Love Will Keep Us Together (1975)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1975. It is considered "somewhat important to the development of what later became known as Yacht Rock," riding an early example of the "Dubie Bounce." The song was crafted by California studio musicians who had previously played with The Beach Boys, led by Captain Daryl Dragon, known for his seafaring hat.
