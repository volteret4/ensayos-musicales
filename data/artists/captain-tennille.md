**The Way I Want to Touch You (1974)** : Captain and Tennille released this song three times in 1974, first self-released and then on two different labels, hoping it would be their breakthrough hit. Despite generating buzz and leading to their signing with A&M Records, it initially failed to achieve any chart action. Following the massive success of "Love Will Keep Us Together" in 1975, A&M reissued the single, which then became a smash, reaching number four in the fall of 1975 and going gold. ## curiosities
**Captain Daryl Dragon's Stage Persona** : Captain Daryl Dragon, the leader of Captain & Tennille, performed on stage wearing a "seafaring hat," contributing to the duo's distinctive image. "led by a man, Captain Darryl Dragun, who performed on stage in a seafaring hat." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hits of the Year Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce81
**1976 Chart Dominance** : In 1976, the Billboard charts were dominated by artists like Captain & Tennille, whose mainstream pop sound contrasted sharply with the eclectic tastes of emerging bands like The B-52s. This environment contributed to the B-52s' decision to not listen to top 40 radio. "the billboard charts were dominated by the likes of the captain and teniel" ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Deadbeat Club Edition, Part One | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Nautical Theming** : Daryl Dragon, known as "the Captain," performed on stage in a seafaring hat, a notable nautical theme that connects to the later, though literal, internet interpretation of "Yacht Rock." "Dragon and Taneel made their bones in the early 70s as keyboardists for the Beach Boys, and their breakthrough smash featured a slap-happy keyboard sound the Yacht Rock founders would later call the Dubie Bounders." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > What a Fool Believes, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**"Dubie Bounders" Keyboard Sound** : Their breakthrough smash, "Love Will Keep Us Together," featured a distinct slap-happy keyboard sound that the Yacht Rock founders would later coin as the "Dubie Bounders."
