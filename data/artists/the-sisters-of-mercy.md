**More (1990)** : This lead single from the album *Vision Thing* was co-written and produced by Jim Steinman and Andrew Eldritch. Described as "Goth Dunn Steinman, with a cinematic sound and just a hint of Broadway," it topped the modern rock chart for five weeks at the end of 1990, holding off hits by Morrissey and The Happy Mondays. This marked Jim Steinman's first number one on the alt-rock chart. "More was Goth Dunn Steinman, with a cinematic sound and just a hint of Broadway." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Turn Around, Bright Eyes, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**This Corrosion (1987)** : Written by Andrew Eldritch as "Goth style dance rock with big hooks and a towering sound," this song was produced by Jim Steinman, who secured a budget for a 40-person chorus. It became the Sisters of Mercy's biggest British hit to date, reaching the top 10 in the UK in 1987. It also performed well on alternative and college stations in the US, and would likely have been a US hit if a modern rock chart had existed then.
