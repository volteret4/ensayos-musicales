## curiosities
**Late 1980s Success** : Northern Pikes was among the new acts signed throughout the late 1980s who achieved hits and solid-selling albums. This success was incentivized by the serious prospect of radio airplay, coverage by MuchMusic, and funding assistance, reflecting a growing investment in Canadian talent by the recorded music industry. "Throughout the late 1980s, new acts were signed and had hits in solid-selling albums, Blue Rodeo, The Box, Pursuit of Happiness, Northern Pikes, National Velvet, Alana Miles, 5440, Cowboy Junkeys." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6PGgNCaW4xo ← northern-pikes
**Brian Potvin's Talent Scouting and Songwriting** : Upon the Pikes' retirement, guitarist Brian Potvin transitioned into talent scouting for Polygram and Universal Records, working with artists such as Finger Eleven and Tegan and Sara. He also freelances as a songwriter, contributing material to other bands and to the Discovery Channel, including the series "Survivor Man." "When the Pikes retired, he went to work scouting talent for Polygram, and later Universal Records. This had him work with everyone from finger 11 to Tegan and Sarah. He also freelances as a songwriter.
