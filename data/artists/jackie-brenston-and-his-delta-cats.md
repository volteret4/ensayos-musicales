## songs
**Rocket 88 (1951)** : This song, inspired by the Oldsmobile Rocket 88 car, is frequently cited by many as the first ever rock and roll song. It was recorded in early March 1951 in Memphis under unusual circumstances. **Rocket 88 (1951)** : Released under this name, the song features a fuzzed-up guitar sound played by Willie Kitsert, resulting from a damaged guitar amplifier where the speaker cone had burst. The band, led by Ike Turner, improvised a repair with wadded newspaper, creating a distinctive sound that some consider the birth of rock and roll due to its early use of guitar distortion. ## curiosities
**Buzz-Inducing Guitar Amp Recording** : In early March 1951, while heading to Memphis to record, the band had a flat tire. During the unloading of their car to access the spare, a guitar amplifier fell and dislodged its speaker cone. Lacking funds for a new amp or repairs, they improvised by stuffing newspaper around the cone, which created a distinctive "buzz" that became a defining characteristic of their song "Rocket 88."
