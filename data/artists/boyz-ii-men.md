## songs
**End of the Road (1992)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1992, becoming a "monster" hit. It spent an unprecedented 13 weeks at number one, setting a new record for the longest run atop the Hot 100 to that date and becoming the biggest number one since Elvis Presley's "Hound Dog/Don't Be Cruel" in 1956. This "three-hunky plea for a woman's affections," written by Babyface, defeated Sir Mix-A-Lot's "Baby Got Back" and Kris Kross's "Jump." "13 weeks at number one. The longest run in Hot 100 History to that date." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hits of the Year Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce81

**One Sweet Day (1995)** : This duet with Mariah Carey dropped in November 1995 and achieved unprecedented chart success. It not only debuted at number one but also remained at the top of the Hot 100 for a then-record 16 weeks, solidifying its place in chart history. "When her duet with R&B vocal troupe Bois to Men one sweet day dropped in November, it not only debuted at number 1, it stayed on top for a then record 16 weeks." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Toppermost of the Poppermost Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef60c30a1408dc29358
