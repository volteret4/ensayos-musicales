**Formation and Naming** : Boyz II Men, a vocal quartet, formed at a Philadelphia high school in the late 1980s and deliberately named themselves after the 1988 New Edition song "Boys to Men." "Boys to men, a vocal quartet that formed at a Philadelphia high school in the late 80s, not only named themselves after the 1988 new edition song Boys to Men." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Want It That Way Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Discovery and Management by Michael Bivins** : The group was discovered by New Edition member Michael Bivins, who also launched the hip-hop forward spin-off group Bell Biv DeVoe in 1990. Bivins became their manager and facilitated their signing to Motown, the same label that nurtured the Jackson 5. "They were discovered by new edition member Michael Bivens, who in 1990 had launched a new edition spin-off group, the more hip-hop forward Belle Biv de Vaux." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Want It That Way Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Vocal Prowess and Influence** : Boyz II Men's singles were renowned vocal showcases, establishing new standards for harmony singing and vocal arranging. Their "vocal fireworks" were explicitly cited by several future late 90s boy band members as an aspiration. "The bigger reason Boys to Men became important to boy band history however was what they inspired. Their singles were vocal showcases, setting new benchmarks for harmony singing and vocal arranging." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Want It That Way Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Transcendence of "Boy Band" Label** : While initially known for dancing and stylish outfits, as their hits grew, Boyz II Men evolved into the premier soul group of their generation, quickly transcending the boy band category, much like the inherent progression suggested by their name. "Boys to Men simply weren't boys for long.
