**Super Bowl LIV Half-time Show Performance (2020)** : Shakira, a Colombian superstar, led off the first-ever Super Bowl half-time show devoted exclusively to music by Latin American performers. The spectacle was a proud celebration of Latin heritage and featured a wealth of hits. "Led off by Colombian superstar Shakira and headlined by Puerto Rican American singer, dancer, and actress Jennifer Lopez, the spectacle was a proud celebration of Latin heritage." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > La Vida Loca Edición | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Precedent for Reissued Albums** : Shakira had previous CDs reissued in special edition form, a practice that preceded Rihanna's "Good Girl Gone Bad Reloaded," though Rihanna's reissue set an unprecedented level of success for such reissues. "previous CDs by Usher and Shakira had been similarly reissued" ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Only Girl in the World Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Pre-Crossover Sound** : Before her widely recognized "crossover," Shakira was performing "very different" music, described as "very kind of almost basic, but catchy alt rock in the late 90s." Her early sound was essentially American rock music sung in Spanish. "She was doing like very kind of almost basic, but catchy alt rock in the late 90s." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Hits Don’t Lie | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Early Tour Experience** : On her very first tour, Shakira performed short sets, playing for only about 40 minutes because she didn't have many songs in her repertoire at that time. "I actually saw her on that very first tour. She only played for like 40 minutes because she didn't have it many songs." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Hits Don’t Lie | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Crossover Transformation** : Her crossover period marked a "big pivot" in her career, characterized by "huge stage setups" and elaborate "dancing" performances, a stark contrast to her earlier alt-rock style. This transformation was an intentional move to reach Anglo audiences, incorporating more Latin and even Arabic elements into her music, reflecting her Lebanese heritage. "So when she came out with these huge stage setups and, the dancing co-brother she had on stage, that was just like, what is going on here?" ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Hits Don’t Lie | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Latin Pop Boom Figure** : Shakira rose to fame during the "Latin pop boom" around 20 years prior to the discussion. "the Latin boom of 20 years ago, the one that made Ricky Martin famous, was kind of a half step the moment when JLo and Shakira became famous as well because all of those hits were in English." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Wesley on Whitney | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Super Bowl Half-Time Show Performance** : Shakira performed at a Super Bowl Half-Time Show alongside Jennifer Lopez, an event that served as a powerful reminder of Latin crossover music. "this year's JLo and Shakira Half-Time Show at the Super Bowl reminded us all of the power of Latin crossover." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Wesley on Whitney | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
