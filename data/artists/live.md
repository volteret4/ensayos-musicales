**Lightning Crashes (1994)** : Released in 1994, this song topped the Modern Rock Chart by 1995 and achieved high rotation at Top 40 radio. Its popularity was crucial in pushing Live's album "Throwing Copper" to the number one spot on the Billboard album chart. ## curiosities
**Rise to Number One** : Hailing from York, Pennsylvania, Live was considered a mid-tier band with only modest radio hits a year before their breakthrough. Their classification as "alternative rock" on charts, radio, and in record stores was a key factor in their rapid ascent to the top band in America in the mid-90s.
