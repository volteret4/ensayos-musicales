Despite the band members being "photographed wearing modest hippie suits and ties," their music was explicitly described as "pure bubblegum," highlighting the commercialization of the "hippie" aesthetic. **Concocted Meet-Up Band** : The Ohio Express was described as a "concocted meetup band" that lacked a permanent lineup, with its members often spawned from the same musicians as the 1910 Fruitgum Company. Despite being photographed in "modest hippie suits and ties," their music was characterized as "pure bubblegum," reflecting the trend of mainstream acts co-opting hippie aesthetics.
