## songs
**Boombastic (1995)** : This 1995 number three smash single by Jamaican American vocalist Shaggy successfully crossed reggae toasting with American hip hop and pure pop. His success, along with other reggae and dancehall artists breaking into mainstream pop radio, had a "knock on effect" on homegrown American rappers, leading to further fusion milestones in sing-rap.
