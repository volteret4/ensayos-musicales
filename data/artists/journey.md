**Las Vegas Residency Trend** : Journey joined a growing number of rock bands who began accepting offers for Las Vegas residencies, following the path of pop stars who had successfully redefined Vegas entertainment. This trend allowed artists to have fans come to them, rather than enduring the exhaustive nature of traditional touring.
