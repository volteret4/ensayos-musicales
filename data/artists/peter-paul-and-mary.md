**Leaving on a Jet Plane (1969)** : This song, written by John Denver, was recorded by Peter, Paul and Mary for their 1967 LP 'Album 1700' but released as a single in 1969. The lyrics, initially about lover's regret, gained resonance as a parable for soldiers leaving for the Vietnam War. It became their first and only number one hit on the Hot 100 in December 1969.
