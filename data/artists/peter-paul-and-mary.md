**Best-Selling 60s Folk Group** : Peter, Paul and Mary were the best-selling group of 60s folk, comprising New York folk soloists Peter Yarrow, Paul Stookey, and Mary Travers. They joined forces under manager Albert Grossman to revive the mixed-gender sound of The Weavers and capitalize on the burgeoning folk scene.
