## curiosities
**Use of Devil's Triad** : Wagner, a highly influential classical composer, is noted for his use of the "devil's tri tone" (flattened fifth) in his works. His inclusion in this list highlights that this particular dissonant interval was a recognized compositional element within the classical tradition, long before its later controversial associations. **"Bridal Chorus" in "Ms. Jackson"** : Outcast incorporated a recurring piano line from opera composer Richard Wagner's famed "Bridal Chorus," also known as "The Wedding March," into their hit song "Ms. Jackson." This was a clever touch, as the song's theme revolved around a man's refusal to settle down, contrasting with the traditional marriage anthem.
