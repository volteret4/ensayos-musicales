**Gangsta's Paradise (1995)** : This song featuring L.V. was the Billboard year-end number one for 1995, becoming an "instant hip-hop classic" upon its summer release. It was built from an interpolation of Stevie Wonder's deep cut "Pastime Paradise." Despite spending only two weeks at number one, it generated massive sales, going triple platinum in under six months. The song endured well beyond its movie origin, "Dangerous Minds," a film about an unlikely teacher, mirroring the theme of another year-end number one. **Initial Opposition to "Amish Paradise"** : Coolio was initially very protective of his reputation and sounded "genuinely pissed" at "Weird Al" Yankovic for deflating his street cred with "Amish Paradise". He stated, "I ain't with that. I didn't give it any sanction. I think that my song was too serious. It ain't like it was beat it." However, Al recorded the song thinking Coolio's management had signed off, and the "beef" was squashed by 1996.
