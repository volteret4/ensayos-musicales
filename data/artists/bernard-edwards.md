**Brooklyn Background and R&B Roots** : Raised in Brooklyn, Bernard Edwards was born just weeks apart from Nile Rogers. **Distinctive "Chucking" Bass Approach** : Edwards was recognized for his unique "distinctive, fleet-fingered, chucking approach on the bass." This patented bass style became a signature element of Sheik's sound, with Nile Rogers believing that "nobody but Edwards could play" the intricate bass part of "Everybody Dance." **Musical Director for New York City (1973)** : In 1973, Edwards secured a pivotal gig as the musical director for the R&B vocal troupe New York City, which had a number 17 hit with "I'm Doing Fine Now." This role required both Edwards and Nile Rogers, as backing musicians, to master contemporary R&B styles like Philadelphia Soul.
