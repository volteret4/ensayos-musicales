**Amazing Grace (1972)** : This ambitious multimedia project, recorded live over two nights in January 1972 at the New Temple Missionary Baptist Church in Los Angeles, was a critical and commercial triumph. Selling a million copies initially and later certified double platinum by the RIAA, it remains Franklin's top-selling album and the best-selling live gospel LP of all time.
