**Teddy Bear (1976)** : This unbelievably maudlin and tear-jerking story song entered the Top 40 charts, depicting a little boy who uses his deceased father's CB radio to communicate with truckers. It was one of many copycat songs that emerged during the CB radio craze, inspired by CW McCall's "Convoy."
