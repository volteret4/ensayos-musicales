## songs
**One Night in Bangkok (1984)** : Murray Head's only other notable hit, which came a decade and a half after "Superstar" finally cracked the top 40. This later success came long after the "Jesus Christ Superstar" album itself had become a household name, showcasing that Head did not become a consistent household name from his first hit. **One Night in Bangkok (1984)** : The lead single from the musical "Chess," a collaboration with ABBA co-leaders Benny Anderson and Bjorn Olveus. This song reached number three in America, making it one of the three biggest US hits Benny and Bjorn ever scored. Its chorus featured "very Abba-esque" masked vocals. **Superstar (1970)** : This title track from the rock musical "Jesus Christ Superstar" required three attempts to become a hit. Initially, Murray Head's single reached number 74 in early 1970. After the musical's concept album gained word-of-mouth popularity and topped the album chart, the single was re-released, peaking at number 60, and finally, on its third try, cracked the top 40 to reach number 14 in May 1971. ## curiosities
**Judas in Jesus Christ Superstar** : Murray Head, a theatre actor who had just acted in the West End production of "Hair," was hired to play Judas on the "Jesus Christ Superstar" concept album, released in 1970.
