**Missing You (1984)** : This song became John Waite's first solo number one hit in the USA, holding the top spot for a solitary week. Legend suggests it was written about MTV VJ Nina Blackwood, though Waite later confessed it was "about her and a bunch of other women." The song, noted for its "unreliable narrator" claiming not to miss the subject, was a cathartic and profoundly relatable expression from Waite's soul. It remains his moment of greatness, a radio perennial, and his biggest streaming song.
