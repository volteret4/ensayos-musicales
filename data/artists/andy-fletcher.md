**Devotional Tour Breakdown and Replacement (1993)** : During the recording of "Songs of Faith and Devotion," Andy Fletcher exhibited signs of severe depression and was sent home from sessions. On the Devotional Tour, he was depressed, homesick, and hypochondriac, suffering a nervous breakdown that led to a month-long hospitalization, causing him to miss the entire second leg of the tour. He was replaced by keyboardist Darryl Bata Montey for 39 shows.
