## albums
**Supernatural (1999)** : This star-packed album by Carlos Santana was his debut for Arista Records and was overseen by Arista mogul Clive Davis. Already in the works months before the Latin pop explosion of 1999, the breakthrough success of artists like Ricky Martin and Jennifer Lopez further enhanced its commercial prospects. The album was certified 15 times platinum and won Album of the Year at the Grammy Awards. ## songs
**Maria Maria (2000)** : This was Carlos Santana's second number one hit, following "Smooth," and featured an even more pronounced Latin flavor. "Maria Maria" spent an additional 10 weeks at the top of the Hot 100, contributing to Santana's blockbuster success in the wake of the 1999 Latin pop explosion. **Oye Como Va (1970)** : Carlos Santana, a Mexican American guitarist, transformed Tito Puente's original "Oye Como Va" into an electric rock jam in 1970. Santana's version achieved significant chart success, reaching number 13 on the Hot 100 in 1971, cementing its place in the Latin crossover canon. **Smooth (1999)** : Co-written and sung by Matchbox Twenty singer Rob Thomas, "Smooth" was the lead single from Carlos Santana's album *Supernatural*. Featuring a cha-cha beat and prominent horns, the song topped the Hot 100 in October 1999 and remained at number one for a dozen weeks into 2000. It later won Record and Song of the Year at the Grammy Awards.
