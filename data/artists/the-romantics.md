**What I Like About You (1980)** : Co-written by Wally Palmer, Mike Skill, and Jimmy Marinos and sung by drummer Marinos (unusual for the frontman), this "rave up" with elements of Chuck Berry and The Yardbirds was a favorite in their live set. Debuting on the Hot 100 in February 1980, it took five weeks to reach number 49 before falling off the chart three weeks later. It is their all-time perennial, played nearly 42,000 times on radio (roughly twice "Talking in Your Sleep") and streamed over 30 million times in the last 12 months (nearly double "Talking in Your Sleep"). It represents a clear example of a lower-charting hit eclipsing a higher-charting successor and closing their live sets. ## curiosities
**Delayed Commercial Success and Licensing** : "What I Like About You" was an initial commercial disappointment, not breaking the Top 40 upon its January 1980 release. Its massive success came a year later, driven by heavy rotation of its video on MTV. This led to the song being extensively licensed for various uses, from the Los Angeles Dodgers to Barbie dolls, SeaWorld, and numerous movies and TV shows, generating substantial revenue for the band. "It wasn't until a video started getting played on this new thing called MTV a year later that it began to have any real impact." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hstJ8M2laho ← the-romantics ← the-romantics
**Image and Genre Perception** : The Romantics, a Detroit-based band, were known for dressing in red leather. Initially, they perceived themselves as more aligned with punk rock or new wave. However, their music ultimately leaned heavily into the Powerpop sound, demonstrating the genre's pervasive influence at the turn of the decade.
