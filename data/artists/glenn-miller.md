## curiosities
**First Gold Record Award Recipient (1941)** : The Glenn Miller Orchestra received the first gold record award in 1941 for one of their million-plus selling records. This recognition initiated a lasting trend within the music industry of presenting awards for sales milestones. **Disappearance During World War II (December 1942)** : Big Band leader Glenn Miller went missing in December 1942 when his military plane reportedly went down over the English Channel during a mission in World War II. Despite extensive efforts, no wreckage or remains were ever found, though wartime conditions and severe weather were cited as contributing factors. "No one has ever heard from Big Band leader Glenn Miller after his military plane evidently went down somewhere over the English Channel while on a mission during World War II." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sCwgvHg86Oc ← glenn-miller
**First Big Band Experience (Mid-1930s)** : Glenn Miller gained his first experience in putting together a big band when he was hired by British bandleader Ray Noble. Due to musicians' union restrictions, Noble's own band could not travel to the US, so he employed Miller to assemble a new American band.
