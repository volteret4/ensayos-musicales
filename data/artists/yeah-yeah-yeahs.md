**New York Formation and Midwest Influences** : Singer Karen O. and drummer Brian Chase met at college in Ohio in the late 1990s. They later moved to New York, where they met photographer and guitarist Nick Zinner. Their initial band, Unitarred, evolved its sound to incorporate the "trashy, punky Garage Rock" Karen O. heard in Ohio, drawing influences from Detroit (140 miles up I-90) and Cleveland (35 miles east on Highway 10), near Akron (home of The Pretenders and The Black Keys). "Singer Karen O. met drummer Brian Chase at College in Ohio in the late 1990s." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UpWz3X33vSY ← ye-eyes

**Karen O as a Walking Billboard** : Karen O, the highly charismatic front person of the Yeah Yeah Yeahs, became a willing "walking billboard" for New York designer Christian Joy. As the band gained popularity and Karen O became a photographed figure, the increased visibility of Christian Joy's designs led to a surge in orders, allowing the designer to leave her day job and pursue fashion full-time. "The Ye-E-S featuring senior Karen O who is dressed by an up-and-coming New York designer named Christian Joy.
