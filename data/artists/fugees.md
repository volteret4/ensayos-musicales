**Killing Me Softly (1996)** : This hip-hop-fueled cover of Roberta Flack's 1970s classic, performed by Lauryn Hill, was the Fugees' second radio single from *The Score*. However, it was made unavailable at retail, a strategy that "did wonders for the Fuji's album sales" by compelling listeners to purchase *The Score*.
