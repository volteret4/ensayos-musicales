## curiosities
**Success Preceding Solo Debut** : The Fugees were a successful rap troupe whose achievements provided the context for Lauryn Hill's "celebrated solo debut" in the fall of 1998. Her first single, "Doo Wop (That Thing)," debuted at number one, building on the foundation of the group's prior success.
