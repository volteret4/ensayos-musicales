**Back in Black (1980)** : Released six months after Bon Scott's death, this album served as a tribute to him and became the band's biggest LP ever. Despite peaking at number 4 on the Billboard album chart in December 1980, it has spent nearly 450 weeks on the chart and was certified for sales of 22 million copies in 2016. After counting streaming into tallies, it was re-certified in 2019 for 25 million total sales plus streams, continuing to sell well into 2021.
