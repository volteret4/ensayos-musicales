## songs
**Get In The Ring (1991)** : Featured on Guns N' Roses' 1991 album "Use Your Illusion," this song was a direct challenge from Axl Rose to journalists. After "years of tangling with journalists who dissected his lyrics for racism and homophobia," Rose "literally dared those scribes by name to meet him in person for a public brawl."
