# artist - Tommy James and the Shondells

## songs
**Crimson and Clover (1969)** : This song topped the Hot 100 in the early weeks of 1969, following Marvin Gaye's "I Heard It Through the Grapevine," just as "Proud Mary" was scaling the charts. **Mony Mony (1968)** : The original version of this song, released in 1968, is a possible precursor for the audience chant that later became widely associated with Billy Idol's live version. Some theories suggest the tradition of audience participation extends back to its play in New York clubs between 1968-1970.
