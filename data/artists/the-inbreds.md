# artist - The Inbreds

## curiosities
**Founder of The Enclave and Todd Sullivan's Mentor** : Tom Zutat founded The Enclave, a short-lived boutique record label with significant funding, and was the mentor to Todd Sullivan, who signed Sloan to Geffen. Zutat's ambition was to make Sloan a major hit to prove Geffen wrong, but The Enclave ceased operations within six months.
