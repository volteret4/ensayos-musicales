## songs
**Dirt Road Anthem (2010)** : This was the significant cover of "Dirt Road Anthem," where Jason Aldean "vocalized the whole song himself," both singing the chorus and rapping the verses. Released as the third single from his "My Kind of Party" album, it successfully topped Billboard's Hot Country Songs Chart in July 2011. ## curiosities
**On Stage During Route 91 Harvest Festival Shooting (2017)** : Jason Aldean was performing on stage at the Route 91 Harvest Festival in Las Vegas at approximately 10:00 PM on October 1, 2017, when Stephen Paddock began firing into the crowd from the Mandalay Bay Hotel. The shooting started at 10:05 PM and continued for ten minutes, resulting in the deadliest mass shooting by a lone gunman in American history. "At around 10, Jason L. Dean took the stage." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xid8CZf4oYo ← jason-aldean
**Pioneer in Genre Mixing** : Jason Aldean was among the artists who, decades prior to Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road," were mixing country music with R&B and Rap, contributing to a cross-genre tradition. "everyone from Ray Charles and the Rappen Duke to Bubba Sparks and Jason Aldine were mixing country music with R&B and Rapp." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Ride ’til I Can’t No More Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce88
**Collaborations with Country Rappers** : Jason Aldean is mentioned as a country mainstay who collaborated with artists like Colt Ford, further demonstrating the cross-pollination of genres. "He dropped bars alongside everyone from Country Mainstays, Jake Owen and Jason Aldin to his white country rap forebear, Bubba Sparks." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Ride ’til I Can’t No More Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce88
**Pop Crossover with Rap (2011)** : Jason Aldean achieved a pop crossover on the charts with his song "Dirt Road Anthem," which included both himself and Ludacris rapping. This occurred a dozen years before he gained infamy for his controversial number one hit, "Try That in a Small Town." "Jason Aldin crossed over on the pop charts with a song in which both he and Luda were rapping." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Ride ’til I Can’t No More Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Bro Country Era Chart Topper** : Jason Aldean was cited as an artist producing "testosterone-fueled hits" during the "Bro Country Era" of the early 2010s. His music featured "cookie-cutter lyrics about trucks, boozy lake parties, and pretty women in cutoff shorts," which became interchangeable with other artists of the genre. "Whether the chart toppers were by Jason Aldean or Jake Owen, or by the so-called King of the Bros. Luke Bryan. These testosterone-fueled hits were becoming interchangeable." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Taylor’s Version of Country, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Use of Trap Beats in Country** : Jason Aldean is among the artists mentioned who started using trap beats on country records, contributing to the shift in what is considered "normal" within the genre and challenging previous notions of what production styles were permissible. "suddenly a whole bunch of artists like Jason Aldin Morgan Wallen Luke Bryan started using trap beats on country records full Florida Georgia line and now we consider that completely normal." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Can’t Tell Me Nothin’ | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
