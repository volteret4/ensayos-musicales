## curiosities
**On Stage During Route 91 Harvest Festival Shooting (2017)** : Jason Aldean was performing on stage at the Route 91 Harvest Festival in Las Vegas at approximately 10:00 PM on October 1, 2017, when Stephen Paddock began firing into the crowd from the Mandalay Bay Hotel. The shooting started at 10:05 PM and continued for ten minutes, resulting in the deadliest mass shooting by a lone gunman in American history. "At around 10, Jason L. Dean took the stage." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xid8CZf4oYo ← jason-aldean
**Pioneer in Genre Mixing** : Jason Aldean was among the artists who, decades prior to Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road," were mixing country music with R&B and Rap, contributing to a cross-genre tradition. "everyone from Ray Charles and the Rappen Duke to Bubba Sparks and Jason Aldine were mixing country music with R&B and Rapp." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Ride ’til I Can’t No More Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce88
**Collaborations with Country Rappers** : Jason Aldean is mentioned as a country mainstay who collaborated with artists like Colt Ford, further demonstrating the cross-pollination of genres. "He dropped bars alongside everyone from Country Mainstays, Jake Owen and Jason Aldin to his white country rap forebear, Bubba Sparks." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Ride ’til I Can’t No More Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce88
