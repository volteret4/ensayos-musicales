## curiosities
**Hugh Whitaker Gasoline, Firebombing, and Axe Attack on Former Business Partner** : After the House Martins broke up, drummer Hugh Whitaker invested his life savings with a partner named James Hughitt in a car dealership. By 1993, Hughitt's mismanagement had left Whitaker completely broke. Whitaker responded by pouring gasoline into Hughitt's mailbox at least twice; when this produced no results, he firebombed his house; when that also failed, he attacked him with an axe, hacking him up significantly. Whitaker served a six-year sentence for assault. He now plays in local bands in Leeds and stays away from the car business. Meanwhile, another House Martins alumnus, guitarist Quentin Cook, renamed himself Fatboy Slim and sold a billion records. "He went a little crazy." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMVGuHjJp9U ← the-house-martins
