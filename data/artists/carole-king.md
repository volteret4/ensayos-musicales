**Child Prodigy and Relative Pitch** : From a very early age, Carole King displayed exceptional musical talent. As a child, her father would use her talent as a party trick: she would turn to the wall while he played notes on the piano, and she would call out which ones he was playing. While her father claimed she had perfect pitch, she actually possessed relative pitch, meaning she could identify all other notes once given a starting note, which her father always provided as middle C.
