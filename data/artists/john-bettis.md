# artist - John Bettis

## songs
**Human Nature (1983)** : John Bettis was commissioned by Quincy Jones to write the lyrics for Michael Jackson's "Human Nature" after Jones heard Steve Porcaro's instrumental demo for the "Thriller" album. **Songwriter of "Slow Hand" and Other Hits** : John Bettis, a prolific songwriter, co-wrote "Slow Hand" and is credited with numerous other hits, including The Carpenters' "Top of the World," Michael Jackson's "Human Nature," and Madonna's "Crazy For You."
