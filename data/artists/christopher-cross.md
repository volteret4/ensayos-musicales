# artist - Christopher Cross

## genres
- Yacht Rock

## albums
**Christopher Cross (1979)** : This self-titled debut album was a major success, going "quintuple platinum" and spending "more than two years on Billboard's top LP chart." It swept the top four Grammys in 1981, including Best New Artist for Cross himself. Despite its popularity, its sweep became "exhibits A and B of the recording academies out of touchness" among rock critics in the 1980s. However, later generations' "rehabilitation of both Yacht Rock as music" makes the win "seem somewhat less egregious now."
