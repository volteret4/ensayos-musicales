## albums
**Curtis Blow (1980)** : This album was one of the earliest rap albums to achieve gold certification, doing so more than four years before Run DMC's debut album reached the same milestone. It set an early commercial precedent for rap albums. **The Breaks (1980)** : This song was an early rap hit that, like Sugar Hill Gang's "Rapper's Delight" and Afrika Bambaataa's "Planet Rock," topped out at number four on Billboard's R&B chart. This pattern indicated a persistent "ceiling" for rap hits on black radio during its nascent years. **King Holiday (1986)** : Curtis Blow co-wrote and produced "King Holiday," the awareness-building celebration for the first Martin Luther King Day national holiday. His involvement as a rapper and producer positioned him as a key figure in this R&B and hip-hop charity single. ## curiosities
**Genuine Musical Genius** : Curtis Blow is recognized as one of the "genuine stars, genuine musical geniuses" of the early rap and hip-hop scene, whose contributions were not always taken seriously by the wider music market. "It didn't matter that there were genuine stars, genuine musical geniuses. One DMC, Curtis Blow, Africa Babata, Grandmaster Flash, LL Cool J, the scene and the sounds were just not taken seriously." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l1jPP1FhGo8 ← curtis-blow
**Management by Russell Simmons** : Curtis Blow's career was managed by artist-manager Russell Simmons, who also steered the careers of Run DMC, Houdini, and The Fearless Four. This management highlights Simmons' significant role in nurturing early rap talent. "Russell Simmons, an artist-manager who, in addition to his brother's group, steered the careers of such rap acts as Curtis Blow, Houdini, and The Fearless Four." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The Def Jams Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
