## songs
**Can't Nobody Hold Me Down (1997)** : Puff Daddy and Ma$e's 1997 hit, constructed from two prior hits and borrowing its title from Matthew Wilder's 1984 hit "Break My Stride." The song also prominently sampled Grandmaster Flash & The Furious Five's 1982 single "The Message." This "hip hop Frankenstein's monster" spent six weeks at number one, surpassing the chart performance of both original songs. **I'll Be Missing You (1997)** : This song, released by Sean Combs (aka Puff Daddy), was the winner of the 1997 Billboard Song of the Summer. It was "built out of" The Police's "Every Breath You Take" and functioned as an elegy for Combs' deceased friend, rapper Biggie Smalls (aka The Notorious B.I.G.). Neither the song nor Combs's career is noted as having aged particularly well. **Roxanne 97, Puff Daddy Remix (1997)** : As Puff Daddy, Sean Combs was invited by Sting to remix The Police's first hit, "Roxanne," for a greatest hits compilation. This "quirky result" featured DJ scratching, samples from UTFO's "Roxanne Roxanne," and rap bars from Praz of The Fugees, with Sting appearing in the music video. The track was officially credited to Sting and The Police and reached number 20 on the R&B chart and number five on the Hot Rap Singles chart. ## curiosities
**Gen Y Pop Preference** : Puff Daddy was cited as an example of the pop artists that Generation Y was into, highlighting the diverse range of mainstream pop and hip-hop acts that appealed to this demographic in the late 90s. "It was all spice girls and backstreet boys and puff daddy and on vogue." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=amHre9ZZFkU ← puff-daddy
**High Video Budgets** : Puff Daddy, along with other prominent hip-hop artists, readily spent millions on music videos. These significant expenditures were part of hip-hop's emphasis on displaying wealth, status, and identity, using videos as a branding tool beyond just selling singles or albums. "MC Hammer, Buster Rhymes, and Puff Daddy had no trouble spending a couple of million on videos." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7IDbqdIw3WU ← puff-daddy
**Music Videos Directed by Director X** : Puff Daddy is included in the list of artists whose music videos have been directed by Director X. "I have directed videos for Alicia Keys, Puff Daddy." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QBCEmZ4Jheo ← puff-daddy
**Highest Earner of the Year (2016)** : Puff Daddy was the top earner on the Forbes list for the year, taking in approximately 130 million euros (over 100 million dollars). This significant income was largely attributed to a massive tour commemorating the 25th anniversary of his first album, Bad Boy. Despite being a very important figure in the industry for his production work, he did not typically rank so high in previous years, but the tour propelled him to the top.
