# artist - Zager and Evans

## songs
**In the Year 2525 (1969)** : This apocalyptic song achieved number one status on the Billboard charts for six consecutive weeks, and also topped the charts in Canada, the UK, West Germany, Ireland, and New Zealand. It sold millions of copies and was notable for being recorded in a studio situated in the middle of a Texas cow pasture. **In the Year 2525 (1969)** : This song was the winner of the 1969 Billboard Song of the Summer. It is described as a "dauer dystopian summer screed" and was a total curio by a one-hit wonder duo. After dominating the summer of 1969, a period that included the moon landing and Woodstock, the duo never appeared on the Hot 100 chart again.
