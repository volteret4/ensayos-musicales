## curiosities
**Assault by Jack White** : In December 2003, Jason Stollsteimer was allegedly assaulted by Jack White at The Magic Stick club in Detroit during a record release party. White, who had previously produced an album for The Von Bondies and invited them to open for The White Stripes, reportedly became enraged after Stollsteimer brushed him off, leading to White allegedly punching him multiple times and spitting on him.
