## songs
**Sail (2011)** : This early 2010s electro rock track became one of the longest-running hits in Hot 100 history. After an initial 20-week chart run peaking at number 82 in 2011 and early 2012, it fell off the chart for a year. Numerous media placements, including a History Channel trailer, a BMW ad, a Brad Pitt movie, and a TV dance performance, brought it back to the Hot 100 in 2013, where it reached number 17 and remained on the chart for another 59 weeks, crossing over from alternative rock to pop formats.
