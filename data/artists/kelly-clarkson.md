**Since U Been Gone (2004)** : This song, released in December 2004 as the lead single from Clarkson's album "Breakaway," ascended to number 2 on the Hot 100 by April 2005. Co-written and produced by Max Martin and Dr. Luke, it was inspired by the Yeah Yeah Yeahs' "Maps," mimicking its chugging guitar and G major chord but building to a triumphant pop chorus. Originally offered to Pink and Hillary Duff (who couldn't hit the high notes), it found its perfect match in Clarkson. Though initially deemed "too pop" for rock radio, it won instant acclaim from tastemakers for its eclecticism and was hailed as "pop's Trojan Horse," smuggling indie rock production elements into a big pop hit. It spent significantly longer on the chart than the number 1 song that blocked it, 50 Cent's "Candy Shop." "Since you've been gone, was pop's Trojan Horse, smuggling indie rock production elements into a big pop hit." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The Silver Medalists Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
