# artist - Gary Jules

## songs
**Mad World (2001)** : As vocalist, Gary Jules collaborated with composer Michael Andrews on a spooky, glacial-paced cover of Tears for Fears' 1982 single "Mad World." Recorded for the "Donnie Darko" soundtrack, his frail voice provided a haunting punctuation to the arrangement, perfectly complementing the song's dark lyrics. **Mad World (2003)** : This cover, performed by Gary Jules with Michael Andrews, was initially issued on the official soundtrack and DVD of the movie *Donnie Darko*. It later took on a life of its own, released as a single on December 15, 2003, and immediately soared to the top of the British charts, becoming the Christmas number one that year.
