## curiosities
**90s Alt-Rock Discovery** : Lush is listed as one of many bands from the 1990s that older listeners (e.g., in their mid-40s) who loved grunge as teenagers might discover as "new to them" music today. This suggests they were part of the broader alternative rock scene that went unnoticed by some at the time. ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m7MbV-eCLyE ← lush
**Female-Led Band Dynamics** : Lush, a London-based band composed of two men and two women, was notable for its "sexual equality," with the women, Miki Berenyi and Emma Anderson, largely "in charge" and responsible for writing "pretty much all the songs." This balance of female vocals with male guitar aggression was a notable aspect of the shoegaze scene. "Mickey Burrani and Emma Anderson, they were out front and wrote pretty much all the songs." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vEHdoUlGurY ← lush ← lush
