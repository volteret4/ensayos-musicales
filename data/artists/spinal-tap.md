## curiosities
**Adoption of the "Rock Dot" Umlaut** : Spinal Tap, a fictional band, is included in the list of groups that used the umlaut (or "rock dot") in their name, highlighting the widespread adoption of this stylistic choice in rock and metal music. "There's Motley Crew, Queen's Reich, Spinal Tap, Green Jelly, Motorhead, Maximal Park, Moxie Frouze, and tons of others." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPok3MemDmE ← spinal-tap
**Fictional Band from "This Is Spinal Tap" (1984)** : Spinal Tap is a fictional British metal band chronicled in the 1984 comedy film "This Is Spinal Tap," which helped pioneer the mockumentary format. The film satirized essential heavy metal truths, including the music's appeal being largely confined to young men, as discussed in a scene where band members David St. Hubbins (Michael McKean) and Nigel Tufnell (Christopher Guest) are interviewed.
