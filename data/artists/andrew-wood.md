## curiosities
**Tribute Concept for Shangri-La Dee Da (1980)** : Andrew Wood, from the Seattle band Mother Love Bone, died of an overdose in 1980. Stone Temple Pilots' "Shangri-La Dee Da" album was originally intended as a double album, partly as a tribute to Wood, complete with a documentary film and a coffee table book, though the concept was scaled back by the record company. His passing led to the band's hiatus and inspired the formation of the tribute project, Temple of the Dog.
