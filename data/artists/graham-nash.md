## curiosities
**Manchester Origin** : Graham Nash, from Crosby, Stills & Nash, is cited as another prominent musician from Manchester. "the guy out of Crosby Cills and Nash, you know, great, Nash is so much." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_Scyr2czh8 ← graham-nash
**Response to "Ohio" Release** : Graham Nash played a key role in assembling Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young in Los Angeles to record "Ohio" after Neil Young wrote it. He later stated that when the song was released, listeners recognized that the band was speaking for them, indicating the song resonated deeply with the public. "Graham Nash later said in speaking for ourselves Listeners recognized that we were speaking for them too." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 5 - Anti-Nixon Songs of the 70s | http://www.ftr70.com
**Relationship with Joni Mitchell** : Graham Nash was dating Joni Mitchell at the time of the Woodstock Festival in August 1969. He reportedly provided her with details about the festival, which she used to write her famous song "Woodstock" as she did not attend herself. "She was dating Graham Nash at the time." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Ladies of the Canyon and the Rhythm Nation | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
