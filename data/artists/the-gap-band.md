## songs
**Oops Up Side Your Head (1979)** : The Tulsa, Oklahoma family funk trio The Gap Band's 1979 R&B hit. Its sound was so strikingly similar to the refrain "uptown, funk you up" in Mark Ronson and Bruno Mars's "Uptown Funk" that The Gap Band successfully sued and eventually settled for a belated songwriting credit, highlighting issues of musical interpolation and copyright. "The Gap Band, whose 1979 R&B hit, oops, up, side your head. **Uptown Funk (2014)** : Bruno Mars echoed Rick James and The Gap Band on this smash hit, which he created with Mark Ronson. This exemplified Mars's tendency to draw from distinct genres for his chart-topping songs.
