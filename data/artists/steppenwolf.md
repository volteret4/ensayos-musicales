# artist - Steppenwolf

## songs
**Born to Be Wild (1968)** : Written by Mars Bonfire in 1967, this song contains the phrase "heavy metal" but is not considered the origin of the heavy metal music genre's name. The lyric refers to the "noise made by big, loud, heavy motorcycles" (specifically Harley Davidson's), not a type of music, and the sound of what is now known as heavy metal music did not exist at the time the song was written. **Born to Be Wild (1968)** : Although originally a number two hit in 1968, this anthem by Steppenwolf is now most strongly associated with the 1969 biker film *Easy Rider*. Its inclusion in the film's soundtrack, which repurposed pre-existing hits, contributed to the film's and soundtrack's cultural impact and commercial success.
