## songs
**Ice Ice Baby (1990)** : This song features an interpolation, or recreation, of Queen and David Bowie's "Under Pressure," rather than a direct sample. **Ice Ice Baby (1990)** : Vanilla Ice's 1990 hit that rocketed to number one on the Hot 100, famously incorporating the iconic baseline from Queen and David Bowie's 1981 duet "Under Pressure." **Ice Ice Baby (1990)** : This "chart topping smash" propelled Vanilla Ice to fame in the early 1990s. However, its "hokey pop friendly production, sampling of Queen and David Bowie, and appropriative flow" drew offense from the rap group Third Bass, leading to their satirical response "Pop Goes the Weasel." **Ice Ice Baby (1990)** : A song that controversially sampled or "stole" the "immortal baseline" from Queen and David Bowie's 1981 hit "Under Pressure." This usage became a notable point of contention and discussion regarding musical appropriation.
