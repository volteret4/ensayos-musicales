## albums
**To the Extreme (1990)** : This album shot to number one on the album chart and stayed there for 16 weeks, shipping 4 million copies "out of the gate" and eventually going Septuple Platinum. It was the album that knocked MC Hammer's *Please Hammer Don't Hurt 'Em* out of the number one spot in the fall of 1990. Its success was largely due to SBK Records pulling the "Ice Ice Baby" single from stores once it was poised to top the Hot 100, forcing album purchases.
