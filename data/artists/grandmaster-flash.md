**Pioneers of Rap/Hip Hop** : Grandmaster Flash was a key figure who emerged from the nascent rap and hip hop scene on the streets of New York, where DJs developed techniques of "turntableism" by chopping and manipulating records to create new sounds for MCs to rhyme over. **Student of Breaks** : As a pioneering figure in hip hop, Grandmaster Flash dedicated three years of his teenage life to immersing himself in music, specifically focusing on understanding and manipulating "breaks" in funk and soul records. He developed a meticulous approach, noticing that the breaks could sometimes be physically seen in the vinyl grooves. To mark these sections, he would use a grease pencil or crayon directly on the records. "Grandmaster Flash said that he devoted three years of his life as a teenager just immersing himself in the music and trying to figure out what to do with these breaks." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 6 - The 70s Roots of Hip Hop | https://seventies.libsyn.com/ep-6-the-70s-roots-of-hip-hop ← grandmaster-flash
