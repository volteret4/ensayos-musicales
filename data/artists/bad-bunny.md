## albums
**Debite de Armas Photos (2025)** : This album is a comprehensive exploration of Puerto Rican identity and music, incorporating a "smorgasbord" of sounds from salsa to bombé and plena. It is emerging as a possible favorite at the Grammys and is tied to a forthcoming Super Bowl halftime show. "It's an all-encompassing Puerto Rican exploration of everything from salsa to all manner of Latin music, and it's a wonderful lesson." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Slate’s Music Club 2025 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Un Verano Sin Ti (2022)** : This album was the top Billboard 200 title of 2022, spending more weeks on top of the chart than any other album that year. It made history as the first all-Spanish language number one album of the year in America, though its success was considered predictable due to its consistent chart performance. "Like I could have told you months ago, the top Billboard 200 title of 2022 was going to be Bad Bunny's Un Verano Sinti, because that album spent more weeks on top of the chart than anything this year." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hits of the Year Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
