# artist - David Jones

## curiosities
**Inspired by Tommy Steele (Age 9)** : A nine-year-old David Jones (who would later change his name to Bowie) was inspired to become a rock and roll star after seeing Tommy Steele perform on tour. Steele's "cocknified impression of an American singing style" is identified as a root for Bowie's own London take on Elvis. "On Steele's subsequent tour, a nine-year-old kid called David Jones, who would later change his name to Bowie, went to see him and came away inspired to become a rock and roll star." ← Episode 48： ＂Rock With the Caveman＂ by Tommy Steele | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=scDLm48u2yc
