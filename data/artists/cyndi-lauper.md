## albums
**She's So Unusual (1983)** : This album was a "stellar" nominee for Album of the Year at the 1985 Grammy Awards, which recognized music from 1984 and late 1983. It ultimately lost to Lionel Richie's "Can't Slow Down." "among the stellar album of the year nominees were, Prince's Purple Rain, Cindy Loppers She's So Unusual, Bruce Springsteen's Born in the USA, and Tina Turner's Private Dancer." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > And the Grammy Goes to… Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef0154465cd600c4e3a
**She's So Unusual (1983)** : Cyndi Lauper's solo debut LP was released in the second half of 1983, just weeks apart from Madonna's self-titled debut album. By the end of 1984, this album had made history by spawning four top five hits, making Lauper the first woman to achieve this feat from a single album. "Released their solo debut LPs, she's so unusual and Madonna, respectively, just weeks apart in the second half of 1983." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > What’s 1984 Got to Do with It Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef5154465cd600c4fa3

## curiosities
**"I Want My MTV" Campaign** : Cyndi Lauper was one of the prominent artists who appeared in promos for the "I Want My MTV" campaign. This highly effective campaign encouraged people to call their local cable companies to demand that they carry the new channel, significantly expanding MTV's reach and power across the US.
