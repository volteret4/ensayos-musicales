## genres

## songs
**All Time High (1983)** : Rita Coolidge performed "All Time High" as the title theme for the 1983 James Bond film. This song exemplified the "very mature adult contemporary" style that characterized many of the Bond themes during Roger Moore's tenure, which were often perceived as more "joky" than earlier films.
