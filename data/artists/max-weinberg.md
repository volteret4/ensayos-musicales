## curiosities
**Drummer on Bat Out of Hell** : Max Weinberg, the drummer from Bruce Springsteen's E Street Band, was hired by Jim Steinman for *Bat Out of Hell*. He brought an epic sound to the album, with Steinman praising his "emotionalism" and playing style, which was likened to a "classical rubata" where the conductor controls the flow. **Steinman Collaborator** : Max Weinberg was a frequent collaborator with Jim Steinman, having played drums on the *Bad Out of Hell* sessions, "Total Eclipse of the Heart" (Bonnie Tyler), and "Making Love Out of Nothing at All" (Air Supply).
