**Needles and Pins (1963)** : Jackie DeShannon recorded this song in early 1963, backed by members of The Wrecking Crew. It reached the lower reaches of the US Hot 100 but made number one in Canada. She insists she co-wrote the song with Sonny Bono and Jack Nitzsche, though she was uncredited. ## curiosities
**Opening Act for The Beatles (1964)** : Jackie De Shannon was one of the opening acts for The Beatles' concert at Maple Leaf Gardens in Toronto on September 6, 1964. She, along with the other support acts, was booed by the audience who were impatient and only wanted to see The Beatles.
