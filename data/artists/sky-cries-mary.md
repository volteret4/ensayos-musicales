**Possible First Musical Netcast (Two Weeks Before Stones, 1994)** : Two weeks prior to The Rolling Stones' November 1994 netcast, Seattle band Sky Cries Mary partnered with the company Starwave to deliver a live performance that many consider to be the first netcast. This event also coincided with the band's creation of their own CD-ROM, titled "Coconut Soup and other advertisers." **First Professional Internet-Only Concert (1994)** : On November 10, 1994, this Seattle group made history by performing what is believed to be the first-ever Internet-only concert by a professional band. Despite the technical challenges, which resulted in a "messy and glitchy" broadcast with poor audio, the event was lauded as a significant "technological triumph" and a pioneering step for webcasts and streaming.
