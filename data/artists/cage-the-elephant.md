## songs
**Night Runnin' (2019)** : This song from the "Social Cues" album was an unfinished track that Caged the Elephant had for years. They admired Beck's songwriting and sent him music files from their Nashville studio to his place in LA via services like Dropbox. Beck returned the song with new verses within 24 hours, completing it. "It had hung around the band for years without completion and that was starting to get annoying." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjQBaX_3oL4 ← caged-the-elephant ← caged-the-elephant

## curiosities
**Planned Woodstock 50 Performance** : Cage the Elephant was part of the original lineup of 80 artists slated to perform at the Woodstock 50 festival in August 2019, which aimed to commemorate the 50th anniversary of the legendary Woodstock event. The festival was ultimately canceled due to a host of unresolved issues. "80 artists had originally signed up, including Santana, Dead & Company, The Killers, Jay-Z, Imagine Dragons, Cage the Elf and Miley Cyrus, Vampire Weekend, and more." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uEe_HLUay5M ← cage-the-elephant ← cage-the-elephant
