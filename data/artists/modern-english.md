**I Melt With You (1983)** : This British new wave song peaked on the Hot 100 in 1983 at No. 78. Despite its low chart performance, it is a staple of classic hits radio, with 74,000 plays on US terrestrial radio in the last 12 months (2021 and 2022). It was streamed nearly 31 million times in the same period. It was considered an early MTV classic, but its edgy sound and its label (Sire Records) being known more for hip acts like The Ramones or Talking Heads than for pop hits (before promoting Madonna) may have contributed to its low chart position. The public was not ready for it in 1983 but has come around to it over the last four decades.
