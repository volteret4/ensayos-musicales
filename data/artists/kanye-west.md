**Pioneer of a New Hip Hop Image and Sound** : Kanye West emerged as an artist challenging the dominant gangster rap aesthetic of the late 90s and early 2000s, which was exemplified by artists like 50 Cent, Jay-Z, and Eminem with their gold chains and tough attitudes.
