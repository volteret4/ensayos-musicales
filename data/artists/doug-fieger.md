**Relationship with Sharona Alperin** : Doug Fieger and Sharona Alperin toured together for a couple of years, during which time Sharona described their relationship as "magical." However, she eventually ended the romantic relationship when she was 21 due to Fieger's possessiveness, though they remained friends until his death in 2012.
