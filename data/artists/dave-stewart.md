## songs
**Don't Come Around Here No More (1985)** : Dave Stewart of Eurythmics co-wrote and produced this psychedelic digital rock track for Tom Petty. The song, featuring electric sitar and cellos, became a number 13 pop and number two rock hit in spring 1985, and its trippy music video became Petty's most beloved MTV hit.
