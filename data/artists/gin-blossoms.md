**Till I Hear It From You (1995)** : "Till I Hear It From You" by the Gin Blossoms was one of two legitimate hits from the soundtrack of the 1995 movie *Empire Records*. The film, featuring Liv Tyler and Renée Zellweger, was loaded with tracks from the 80s and 90s, though only 16 were released on the soundtrack CD. ## curiosities
**Post-Nirvana Sound** : This Arizona power-pop band channeled the jangle of R.E.M. through the brooding sound of Nirvana to achieve chart success on the modern rock chart. They were also cited as a jangle rock band that broke through and showed the alt-pop form was key to the post-grunge universe. "Other bands who channeled the Djangle of REM through the brooding of Nirvana to top the modern rock chart included Arizona Powerpop band, The Gin Blossoms." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > All Apologies Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
