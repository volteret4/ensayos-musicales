## songs
**Sex and Candy (1997)** : Released in 1997, this song achieved significant commercial success, peaking at number two in Canada and number eight on the Billboard Hot 100. Its popularity made it a prominent track on top 40 radio, and it was later covered with some success by Maroon 5. **Sex and Candy (1998)** : This hit set a then-record on the modern rock chart by spending an improbable 15 weeks at number one. Despite its massive success, the band faced difficulties in following it up. **John Wasneyac's Life in Canada** : John Wasneyac, the singer and leader of Marcy Playground, has resided in Canada for an extended period, is married with four sons, and is a partner in a recording studio in Vancouver, which he reportedly acquired using the financial proceeds from the hit song "Sex and Candy." **Tour Routine** : John Wozniak described a typical tour bus schedule involving playing a gig, getting on the bus to sleep, waking up in the next town due to a driver, having a little free time for personal tasks or writing music, then sound check, dinner, interviews, and finally the next gig before repeating the cycle. The only saving grace mentioned were the minutes spent performing. "It's like you play a gig. You're done with the gig. You go to the van. You go wherever, hang out in the van. I mean, the bus. I'm sorry, I'm talking about a bus here. You jump on the bus, you sleep, and you wake up in the next town because there's a driver that drives you." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DgN0vBIugLc ← marcy-playground
**"Alt Rock One Hit" Example** : Marcy Playground became an example of the "Alt Rock One Hit" phenomenon of the late 90s, where bands could ride one massive radio hit to a platinum album but then struggled to follow up and faced oblivion.
