## curiosities
**Founding Member and Keyboardist** : Tony Banks was a teenage schoolmate of Peter Gabriel and Mike Rutherford, forming the band Garden Wall, which later became Genesis. He was a virtuosic keyboardist, making key contributions to Genesis's sound, including a classic keyboard solo on the bridge of "That's All." "Tony Banks, a virtuosic keyboardist, Mike Rutherford, a strong bassist who doubled on rhythm guitar and Phil Collins and a depth drummer who sang harmonies and even took an occasional lead vocal without question, the star of Genesis in its early 70s incarnation was lead singer and flamboyant frontman Peter Gabriel." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Invisible Miracle Sledgehammer Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Mixed Feelings on Collins's Solo Success** : Banks, as a bandmate of Phil Collins, expressed mixed feelings about Collins's immense solo success, jokingly stating, "he was our friend, and we wanted him to be successful, just not that successful." However, he also acknowledged that "Phil became so big, but I think the two careers helped each other," recognizing the symbiotic relationship between Collins's solo career and Genesis's increasing popularity.
