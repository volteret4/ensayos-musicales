**Whipping Post (1969)** : This song appeared on the band's debut studio album in 1969, but it was the extended live version, recorded in 1970 and released on "Live at Fillmore East" in 1971, that gained significant fame. The studio version ran for five minutes, while the live version extended to 22 minutes. The new FM stations of the early 1970s, particularly late-night DJs, would play the entire live version. It is characterized as a blues song depicting betrayal, pain, and suffering. "Whipping Post, that's from a live at Philmore East recorded in 1970, released in 1971.
