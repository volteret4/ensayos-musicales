# artist - Finneas O'Connell

## songs
**No Time to Die (2020)** : Finneas O'Connell co-wrote and recorded the theme song for "No Time to Die" with his sister, Billie Eilish. They composed and recorded the track over three days on a tour bus in late 2019, with a full 70-piece orchestra added in England. His collaboration with Eilish resulted in her becoming the youngest ever Bond theme artist and the song winning a Grammy. "She and Brother Finneas wrote and recorded it over three days on a tour bus in late 2019." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K0pgJ7XAlpo ← finneas-oconnell
**What Was I Made For? (2023)** : Co-written by Finneas and his sister Billie Eilish for the *Barbie* soundtrack, this song received the Best Original Song Academy Award in 2024. Its acclaim stems from its beauty, its thematic relevance to the film, and its profound exploration of art and purpose, drawing comparisons to Simon & Garfunkel's impact in *The Graduate*. "What was I made for?
