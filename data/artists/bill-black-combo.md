**Instrumental Hits and Opening for The Beatles (1960s)** : Formed by Bill Black after his permanent departure from Elvis's band, Bill Black's Combo achieved a series of instrumental hits over several years, including eight Top 40 singles. Their popularity earned them the prestigious role of opening act for The Beatles' first US tour.
