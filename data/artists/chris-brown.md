**Altercation with Rihanna (2009)** : A disturbing altercation between Chris Brown and his then-girlfriend Rihanna occurred just before the 2009 Grammy Awards, approximately six months after "Disturbia" became a number one hit. This incident significantly altered the career trajectories of both artists. "About six months after Disturbia gave Rihanna her fourth career number one, a disturbing altercation between her and Brown, just before the 2009 Grammy Awards would change both of their trajectories." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Only Girl in the World Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Assault on Rihanna (February 2009)** : The night before the Grammy Awards, after attending Clive Davis's famed annual pre-Grammies party, Brown assaulted Rihanna following her confrontation about a text message from another woman on his phone. He choked her when she attempted to call the police, then fled the scene. Brown later turned himself in, was charged with assault, and sentenced to probation and community service. Years later, Rihanna forgave him and even recorded music with him again.
