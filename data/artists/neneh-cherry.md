**Global Melodic Rap Pioneer (1989)** : Swedish-born Neneh Cherry's "Buffalo Stance" was a groundbreaking number three pop hit in 1989. She blended elements of club, electro, and freestyle, both rapping and singing in American and British accents. The single demonstrated how global rap had become and that it could embrace melody while still sounding "street," reaching number 16 on Billboard's rap songs chart, a strong showing for a woman rapper at the time.
