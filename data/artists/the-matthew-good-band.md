**Underdogs (1997)** : Released on October 7, 1997. The band initially aimed to work with producer Gil Norton, who was unavailable due to commitments with Foo Fighters, leading him to recommend Warren Lives. The record deal with Private Music (a BMG subsidiary) unexpectedly folded on the day recording was scheduled to commence. Matthew Good, having secured a payout for two records through legal maneuvering, self-funded the album, with Warren Lives ultimately producing it. Despite initial skepticism from A&M/Universal, where an A&R executive bet it wouldn't sell more than 30,000 copies, the album's sales rapidly escalated from around 20,000 to over 100,000 records within months following the release of the "Operations" music video. ## curiosities
**Breakup in 2001** : The Matthew Good Band dissolved around 2001, an outcome of pervasive internal dysfunction, Matthew's illness, and an unwillingness to tour the `Audio of Being` record. The band members had reached a point where communication broke down, and individuals asserted their indispensability, leading Matthew, who usually tried to "placate" others, to finally end the group. "And that was the end of the Matthew Good Band." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--g0gugy3ZE ← matthew-good-band ← matthew-good-band
**Dave's Disregard for Band Work** : During the writing phase for `Audio of Being` in Whistler, Dave (likely Dave Genn) arrived late because he was busy working with "Hollywood" (presumably Holly McNarland) and in "Ireland." Matthew Good expressed frustration, believing Dave should have prioritized band commitments given their success (a near Triple Platinum album). "Dave came up for a little bit. But unfortunately, he was a little late because he was busy with Hollywood and Ireland working with Hollywood." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--g0gugy3ZE ← matthew-good-band ← matthew-good-band
**Dysfunctional Tour and Matthew's 30th Birthday** : The tour supporting `Audio of Being` was marked by extreme dysfunction, with band members refusing to speak to one another. Matthew's 30th birthday occurred on this tour in "Gogory," where he avoided his bandmates by leaving a celebratory cake in his hotel room and going to the Corral Center. He also sustained a back injury in a car accident during the tour. "The whole tour was ridiculous." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--g0gugy3ZE ← matthew-good-band ← matthew-good-band

**Internal Dysfunction and Political Dynamics** : The Matthew Good Band suffered from significant internal dysfunction, described as "very political and weird," particularly during the creation of `Audio of Being`. This included members vying for publishing rights by injecting ideas and a general lack of communication during tours, ultimately contributing to the band's dissolution. "It might have been even more successful had the Matthew Good band been functioning properly. There was a lot of internal dysfunction." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--g0gugy3ZE ← matthew-good-band ← matthew-good-band
**Publishing Rights Conflicts** : During rehearsals for `Audio of Being`, band members deliberately tried to inject their ideas into songs, not purely for creative reasons, but "because they know that if they do, then they can make a case for publishing." This self-serving behavior significantly "hampered" the entire creative and recording process. "People are trying to inject ideas because they know that if they do, then they can make a case for publishing." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--g0gugy3ZE ← matthew-good-band ← matthew-good-band
**Top Billing Misperception** : The band was publicly perceived as primarily "Matthew Good," giving him top billing despite his personal preference against it. This public misperception was a factor in the band's identity and internal dynamics. "The Matthew Good Band was a group that gave Matt top billing, something that he never wanted. This all came as the result of a public misperception." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--g0gugy3ZE ← matthew-good-band ← matthew-good-band
**Band Name Evolution (Matthew Good Band vs. MGB)** : The band initially used the acronym "MGB" on the first 5,000 copies of their debut album, "Last of the Ghetto Astronauts." However, public demand at radio (for "Alabama Motel Room") and retail led people to consistently request "Matthew Good Band." Faced with this popularity, the decision was made to officially adopt Matthew Good's name for the band, partly because early members were not fully committed and often had other academic pursuits. This event, however, was later described as "the beginning of the end" due to escalating ego issues. "The decision was made that that's what we would call it." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-s0gwXY_CZQ ← the-matthew-good-band ← the-matthew-good-band

**The Edgefest Panda Incident (1999)** : During the Matthew Good Band's performance on the main stage at Edgefest in Wilson Park, Barrie, in 1999, Universal Music marketing representative Iber Hamilton, dressed in a giant panda costume, attempted a stage dive during the song "Everything's Automatic." From the 10-12 foot high stage, he launched into the crowd, which unexpectedly parted "like the Red Sea." The panda landed face down on the ground 12 feet below, suffering a bleeding nose (his head was protected by a football helmet within the costume) and was subsequently kicked by some members of the audience. Matthew Good, performing with his microphone back by the monitor lines, did not immediately see what happened but later found Hamilton in the back with a bloody face. This incident later inspired the artwork for the "Loser Anthem" EP. "The crowd parts like the Red Sea. Everybody moves. Panda, bam! Face down on the ground, 12 feet below the stage." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-s0gwXY_CZQ ← the-matthew-good-band ← the-matthew-good-band
