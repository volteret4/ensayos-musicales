**You Keep Me Hangin' On (1987)** : Kim Wilde, daughter of 50s British teen idol Marty Wilde, became an 80s pop star in her own right by collaborating with her brother and producer, Ricky Wilde. Together, they remade The Supremes' "You Keep Me Hangin' On" as high-energy dance pop. When Wilde's cover reached number one in June 1987, it reset the record for the longest gap (21 years) between an original number one and a number one remake. ## curiosities
**Charity Gig at 43,000 Feet (2013)** : On March 10, 2013, Kim Wilde, along with Tony Hadley of Spandau Ballet, performed a charity gig aboard a British Airways 767 at an altitude of 43,000 feet. This event pushed the boundary of high-altitude concerts, reaching near the operational ceiling of current passenger jets.
