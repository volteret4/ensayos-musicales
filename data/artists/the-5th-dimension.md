## songs
**Aquarius/Let the Sunshine In (1969)** : This song spent six weeks on top of the Hot 100, which was more than any other hit that year. Despite its commanding run, it did not take the year-end top spot, representing an instance where a song with the longest run at number one was "seemingly robbed" of the year-end crown. "26 out of 65 times, a song other than the one with most weeks at number one has taken the crown." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hits of the Year Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Aquarius/Let the Sunshine In (1969)** : This medley by the Fifth Dimension, composed of "Aquarius" from "Hair's" opening number and "The Flesh Failures (Let The Sunshine In)" from its end, debuted on the Hot 100 in early March 1969, reached number one by mid-April, and became one of only two songs from stage musicals (after Louis Armstrong's "Hello Dolly") to top the Hot 100. "The fifth dimension's Aquarius slash Let The Sunshine In debuted on the Hot 100 in early March 1969. As their single rose on the Hot 100, the Hair original cast album soared into the top 20, then the top 10. By mid-April, the fifth dimension's Aquarius reached number 1, by the way becoming one of only two songs that originated in stage musicals ever to top the Hot 100 after Louis Armstrong's Hello Dolly." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Lullaby of Broadway Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

**Discovery of Hair Musical** : While touring New York in 1968, Billy Davis Jr. of the Fifth Dimension met the producer of "Hair" and, after inviting him to their show, was invited to see "Hair." The group was so taken by the opening number, "Aquarius," that they agreed to record it. "While touring New York in 1968, Billy Davis met the producer of Hair and invited him to see the fifth dimension's New York show. In return, the producer invited the group to see Hair." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Lullaby of Broadway Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Medley Creation** : The Fifth Dimension's producer suggested grafting "Aquarius," which felt like half a song and ended abruptly, onto "The Flesh Failures (Let The Sunshine In)," a rousing gospel-flavored sing-along from the very end of the musical "Hair," to create their chart-topping medley. "The only catch was that Aquarius felt like half a song. In the musical, it is a couple of minutes long and ends rather abruptly. So, their producer suggested that they graft Aquarius onto a song from the very end of the musical, arousing gospel-flavored sing-along titled The Flesh Failures, with the parenthetical subtitle Let The Sunshine In." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Lullaby of Broadway Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
