## albums
**KooKoo (1981)** : The debut solo album by the Blondie singer, produced and co-written by Nile Rogers and Bernard Edwards. It was described as a "cutting-edge fusion of funk, rock, and dance music," though perhaps "too cutting-edge" for its time. The album peaked at number 25, produced no Top 40 hits, and left the charts within three months. Its "freaky" album cover, an image by H.R. Giger depicting Harry's face pierced with swords, was suggested to have hindered its commercial performance.
