The guitarist was Ronnie Wood, who six years later would replace Mick Taylor in the Rolling Stones. The singer was a gravel-voiced former grave digger named Rod Stewart. The name changed from Small Faces to simply the Faces — because the two tall new members made the "small" descriptor irrelevant. "The new guitarist was Ronnie Wood.
