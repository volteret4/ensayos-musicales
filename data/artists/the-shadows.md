**Name Change** : The band known as "Cliff Richard and The Drifters" eventually changed its name to "Cliff Richard and The Shadows." This change occurred by Cliff Richard's 19th birthday, at which point none of the original members of The Drifters remained in the group. "By Cliff's 19th birthday, the only thing left of the original drifters was the name, and soon that would change too, as Cliff Richard and the drifters became Cliff Richard and the shadows." ← Episode 70： ＂Move It＂ by Cliff Richard and the Drifters | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoEWP94f6S4
**Origin of "The Shadows' Walk"** : While performing Cliff Richard's cover of "Willie and the Hand Jive" live in 1959, The Shadows developed a synchronized dance. They started moving in unison to visually enliven the song. This performance was so well-received by audiences that they began applying these dance steps to every song, which ultimately evolved into their famous, or infamous, signature stage movement known as "The Shadows' Walk." "The audience reaction was so great that they started doing it on every song, the famous or infamous Shadows' work had developed." ← REUPLOAD Episode 71： ＂Willie and the Hand Jive＂ by Johnny Otis | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ez6BCUlI6Rg
**Origin of Name** : Formed by Harry Webb, Terry Smart, and Norman Midham, the group initially considered calling themselves "The Planet" after the launch of Sputnik. However, after researching the etymology of "planet" and discovering it came from the Greek for "wanderer" or "Drifter," they adopted the name "The Drifters," unaware of the existence of an American group with the identical name. "Inspired by the recent launch of Sputnik, they thought of calling themselves the planet, but they decided that wasn't quite right, and looked up the etymology of planet and found it came from the Greek for wanderer or Drifter, and so they became the Drifters, unaware there was an American group of the same name." ← Episode 70： ＂Move It＂ by Cliff Richard and the Drifters | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoEWP94f6S4
**Original Lineup Evolution** : The initial formation included Harry Webb, Terry Smart, and Norman Midham. Ian Sanwell later joined as the lead guitarist. The lineup then evolved to include Ian Sanwell on bass, Terry Smart, Hank Marvin, and Bruce Welch. Eventually, only the name "The Drifters" remained from the original configuration before the band was rebranded as "The Shadows."
