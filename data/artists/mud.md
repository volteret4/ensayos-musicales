## songs
**Lonely This Christmas (1974)** : This song was recorded by the glam rock band Mud in December 1974, a year after the famous Slade vs. Wizzard Christmas chart battle. It was a 50s holiday pastiche and an homage to the sound of Elvis Presley, and it soared to number one on the UK charts, demonstrating the ongoing impact of the Christmas chart competition.
