## albums
**Days of Future Past (1967)** : Faced with a lack of hits, The Moody Blues were invited by their label in 1967 to record a classical rock hybrid album to showcase stereophonic sound. They produced an original song cycle titled "Days of Future Past," which included "Tuesday Afternoon" and the centerpiece, "Nights in White Satin." ## songs
**Go Now (1964)** : This "beat combo style single" was a Top 10 hit for The Moody Blues during the early years of the British Invasion, but its sound gave little indication of the band's future musical direction. It stands as one of their three American Top 10 hits across different decades. **Go Now (Influence)** : "Go Now" by The Moody Blues is mentioned as one of many songs from the 1960s onwards that utilized the descending bass line technique pioneered by Dave Van Ronk in his "House of the Rising Sun" arrangement. **Nights in White Satin (1967)** : The centerpiece of their 1967 LP "Days of Future Past," this nearly six-minute ballad with a poetry interlude was initially too long for pop radio. A severely edited three-minute version issued as a single in early 1968 flopped on the pop chart at number 103, but the song remained popular on progressive FM rock radio. In 1972, Decker Records issued a more judicious four-and-a-half-minute edit. By then, radio programmers and record buyers were accustomed to progressive and psychedelic pop sounds, leading "White Satin" to blow up on Top 40 radio and reach number two in November 1972, effectively defining The Moody Blues' career. ## curiosities
**Early Use of Electronic Drums** : Graham Edge of The Moody Blues is noted in historical records for his early use of electronic drums. He collaborated with a professor at Cessax University in this pioneering endeavor. "As far as history records, the first electronic drums were used by Graham Edge of the Moody Blues when he collaborated with a professor at Cessax University." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WL3zEWJJNuw ← the-moody-blues ← the-moody-blues
**Following Sgt. Pepper's Lead** : The Moody Blues were among the bands that got into the act of creating concept albums after the release of The Beatles' *Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band*. "That opened the floodgates, the moody blues, the who, Frank Zappa, the kinks, and a bunch of other bands got into the act." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dm5N6yDRxXQ ← the-moody-blues
**Three Decades of Top 10 American Hits** : The Moody Blues achieved the rare distinction of having three Top 10 American hits in three different decades: "Go Now" in the 1960s, "Nights in White Satin" in the 1970s, and "Your Wildest Dreams" in the 1980s.
