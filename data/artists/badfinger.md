## songs
**Baby Blue (1972)** : This song was a number 14 hit in 1972 and was memorably used in the 2013 series finale of AMC's *Breaking Bad*. Following its airing, digital sales jumped from under 200 to 5,000 copies in hours, and streams increased by over 9,000% by 2013. It reached number 32 on Billboard's Digital Songs chart but did not return to the Hot 100.
