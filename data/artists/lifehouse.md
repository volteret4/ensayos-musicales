## songs
**Hanging by a Moment (2001)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 2001, despite never reaching number one on the weekly Hot 100 chart. It is cited as the "ultimate example of what Billboard critics would later call mini van rock"—unhip, Top 40-friendly post-grunge that acted as "glue for radio at the turn of the millennium." The Malibu band secured the title through its sheer persistence and radio friendliness, staying on the Hot 100 for over a year. ## curiosities
**2000 Chart Presence** : Lifehouse (referred to as "Lifhouse") appeared on the charts in 2000, representing the perception that many rock acts at the time only delivered a small number of strong songs across their albums, contributing to a sense of rock's decline.
