## curiosities
**Career Trajectory - Teenage Idol to Executive Producer** : Sean Cassidy's star began to fade after his third album, leading him to transition into acting. Following his acting career, he became a TV writer and producer. Today, he holds the position of executive producer for the TV show "New Amsterdam." This career pivot suggests he might have learned from his brother David's experiences regarding the transient nature of teenage idol fame. "When he did see that his star was starting to fade after his third album He turned his attention to acting after that he became a TV writer and a producer Today he is an executive producer of a TV show called New Amsterdam." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 30 - The Sweet Sound of 70s Bubblegum Music | https://seventies.libsyn.com/030-bubblegum ← sean-cassidy
**Concert Hysteria** : Shaun Cassidy's concerts generated an "incredible" level of excitement and hysteria among fans, as observed by Al Wallace, the chief of security at The Plaza in Detroit, who likened it to the frenzy for Diana Ross or Tony Orlando. "Al Wallace who was the chief of the security force at the plaza in Detroit And this was his observation a few hours after a Sean Cassidy concert He said, I have never, I mean never in my life seen anything like this And I have handled security on everything from presidential motorcades to Diana Ross and Tony Orlando The excitement the kid created was incredible." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 30 - The Sweet Sound of 70s Bubblegum Music | https://seventies.libsyn.com/030-bubblegum ← shaun-cassidy
**Critical Reception** : Critics like Scott Kane of the *Atlanta Constitution* severely criticized Shaun Cassidy, describing his voice as "bland and colorless" and his music, specifically "Da Doo Ron Ron," as "excruciating." Kane's negative reviews provoked a flood of outraged letters from Cassidy's fans. **TV Star and Pop Music Star Image** : Shaun Cassidy successfully combined a TV star image with a pop music star image, following in the footsteps of Ricky Nelson and his brother David Cassidy. He starred alongside Parker Stevenson and Pamela Sue Anderson in the ABC series *The Hardy Boys and Nancy Drew Mysteries* from 1977 to 1979.
