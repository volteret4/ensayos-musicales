**Old Town Road (2018)** : This song was created by rapper Montero Hill (Lil Nas X), who, being "desperate" and seeing it as his "last shot," purchased a remix of Nine Inch Nails' "34 Ghosts 4" from Dutch beatmaker YoungKio. He wrote lyrics over it in about a day and released it independently on December 3, 2018. The song exploded thanks to YouTube and TikTok, especially after Billy Ray Cyrus got involved. It hit number one worldwide and spent a record 19 weeks at the top of the Billboard Hot 100, making Lil Nas X an international superstar. Due to commercial use of the Creative Commons-licensed "34 Ghosts 4," Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross received official songwriting credits and shared the song's chart records. "The song was Old Town Road. **Old Town Road (2018)** : Released in December 2018, this song quickly became established across social media as the official soundtrack for the "Yeehaw Agenda." Its initial official music video was composed entirely of Red Dead Redemption 2 game footage. TikTok users found the song especially effective for short cowboy-themed videos, particularly keying into the moment after the opening chorus when the trap beat drops and the lyric "I got the horses in the back" is sung. This track changed the landscape of TikTok, becoming the first hit song plus meme incubated on the platform, following examples like I Love Friday's "Mia Khalifa" which took off as a meme but never charted. The song later scaled charts, tangled with Nashville, and set all-time Hot 100 records.
