## albums
**Letter from Elena (1964)** : This minor hit, which bubbled under the Hot 100 at number 103, was a spoken word single released by Casey Kasem to capitalize on the peak of Beatlemania. The track features Kasem reading a letter from a fan named Elena about meeting The Beatles, set to the instrumental melody of The Beatles' hit "And I Love Her" from the *Hard Day's Night Soundtrack*. This early experience with fan letters would later inspire the "Long-Distance Dedication" segment on American Top 40.
