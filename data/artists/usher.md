## curiosities
**Album Sales (2004)** : Usher's album sales in 2004 reached approximately 7 million copies, matching the previous year's top sellers. His commercial success was notable as the music industry grappled with evolving distribution models and declining physical sales. **Grammy Nominations and Wins (2005)** : Usher was nominated eight times at the 2005 Grammys and won three prizes, but all were in R&B genre categories. He controversially lost Record of the Year for his smash hit "Yeah!" to Ray Charles and Nora Jones, an outcome deemed a "pinnacle of avoiding cultural relevance."
