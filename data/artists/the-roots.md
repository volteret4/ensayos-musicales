**Rap Career Choice in 90s/00s** : The Roots, despite being musically adept enough to be a pure instrumental combo like The Meters or The J.B.'s in a previous generation, pursued a career in rap due to the commercial imperatives of the 1990s and 2000s. "a musically adept troop like the roots would become a pure instrumental combo like the meters or the J.B.s in a prior generation. But given the commercial imperatives of the 90s and aughts, it made more sense for the roots to pursue a career in rap." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Insert Lyrics Here Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Debut Album (1994-1997)** : The Roots released a major label debut album between 1994 and 1997, a period of significant hip-hop debuts. "These are all artists who had debut major label albums during that time besides Biggie, Nas, Outcast, Method Man, The Roots, Ray Kwan, Olderity Bastard, GZ, Ghostface, Killa, and Missy Elliott." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Posthumous Hits Edition, Live from Seattle | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
