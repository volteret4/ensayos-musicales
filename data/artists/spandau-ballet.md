**Pioneers of New Romanticism** : Spandau Ballet emerged from the vibrant British club scene of Billies and The Blitz Club, which served as breeding grounds for groups associated with the New Romantic movement. This offshoot of synth-based music was characterized by its distinctive style and electronic sound. "One such offshoot was known as New Romantic, which had its roots in a couple of British clubs dedicated to new music. One was called Billies, and the other was the Blitz Club.
