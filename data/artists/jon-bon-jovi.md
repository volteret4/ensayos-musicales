## albums
**Young Guns 2 Soundtrack (1990)** : In 1990, John Bon Jovi issued his first solo album, which was entirely devoted to the soundtrack for the Western movie sequel "Young Guns 2." This venture mirrored Prince's single-artist "Batman Soundtrack" and came during an "imperial high point" in his career. ## songs
**Blaze of Glory (1990)** : The title track from John Bon Jovi's solo album for the "Young Guns 2" soundtrack, this song topped the Hot 100 in the summer of 1990. Its success was further recognized with an Oscar nomination.
