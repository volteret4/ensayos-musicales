## albums
**I Love Rock 'n Roll (1981)** : This debut album, also titled "I Love Rock 'n Roll," featured a re-recording of Joan Jett's cover of The Arrows' song. The album peaked at number 2 on the album chart, behind The Go-Go's "Beauty and the Beat," despite the single topping the Hot 100. **I Love Rock 'n' Roll (1982)** : Joan Jett & the Blackhearts' signature song, a powerful reboot of The Arrows' 1975 barnburner that had previously failed to chart in either the UK or America. Their version spent seven weeks at number one on the Hot 100, establishing it as a massive hit.
