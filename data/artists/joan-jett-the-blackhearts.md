**I Love Rock 'n' Roll (1982)** : Joan Jett & the Blackhearts' signature song, a powerful reboot of The Arrows' 1975 barnburner that had previously failed to chart in either the UK or America. Their version spent seven weeks at number one on the Hot 100, establishing it as a massive hit.
