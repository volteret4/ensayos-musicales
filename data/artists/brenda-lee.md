Brenda Lee's rocking around the Christmas tree." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B2g4QSREzpw ← brenda-lee
**Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree (1958)** : Recorded and released as a single in 1958 when Brenda Mae Tarplay (Brenda Lee) was 13 years old, the song didn't chart until 1960, reaching number 14 after she became a teen idol. Due to prevailing trends against holiday music charting well, it couldn't crack the Top 10 even at the height of her fame. In the streaming era, it returned to the Hot 100 every Christmas starting in 2013, steadily gaining strength. In 2023, after four years of peaking at number two behind Mariah Carey, and aided by a new music video featuring the 78-year-old Brenda lip syncing to her 13-year-old vocals, it finally topped the Hot 100. This set multiple chart records: the longest climb to number one (65 years), Lee as the oldest chart topper ever (78), and the longest gap between number one hits (63 years). "Brenda Lee's rockin' around the Christmas tree is an inspiring comeback story." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Second-Chance Hits Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef18e6dd12efb02c42e

**Voice Polish Comparison (1975)** : Critic Robert Hull, in his assessment of Olivia Newton-John's singing, included Brenda Lee as an example of a vocalist with more "polish." **Oldest Chart Topper Ever (2023)** : In 2023, at 78 years old, Brenda Lee became the oldest chart topper ever when "Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree" reached number one, surpassing Louis Armstrong's record of 62 with "Hello Dolly." **Longest Climb to Number One (2023)** : Brenda Lee's "Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree" achieved the longest climb to number one in chart history in 2023, taking 65 years from its initial recording and release in 1958. "The longest climb to number one in chart history at 65 years." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Second-Chance Hits Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef18e6dd12efb02c42e
**Longest Gap Between Number One Hits (2023)** : In 2023, Brenda Lee set the record for the longest gap between number one hits at 63 years, with "Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree" reaching the top spot after her previous number one, "I Want to Be Wanted," in 1960.
