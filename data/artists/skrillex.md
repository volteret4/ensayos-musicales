## songs
**Scary Monsters and Nice Sprites (2010)** : This "de facto instrumental" and "bone-rattling bass dropper" made Skrillex's name. It was a top 5 dance single and even managed to reach number 69 on the Hot 100 in 2011. The song exemplified a new sound blending EDM and Tropical House with pleading romantic lyrics. "The big heroes weren't dudes with guitars that were people with laptops, Swedish house mafia, David Guetta, dead mouse, of itchy, Calvin Harris, Skrillex, tens of thousands of people attended their gigs and countries all over the world." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Y6nHw5xx88 ← skrillex
