**To Sir with Love (1967)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1967, taken from the soundtrack of the film of the same name, in which singer Lulu also starred alongside Sidney Poitier. It originally appeared as a B-side but gained popularity because DJs preferred its "wistful side" over the A-side. It topped the Hot 100 for five weeks, beating out "The Letter" and "Ode to Billie Joe." **To Sir with Love (1967)** : This British superstar's only American Top 10 hit was a massive chart topper in the fall of 1967, and Billboard ranked it as the number one song of the year. It was the title song of the 1967 movie of the same name, in which Lulu also co-starred. Despite her ecstatic recording of the track, her label, Epic Records, initially doubted its potential, releasing it as the B-side to her cover of Neil Diamond's "The Boat That I Row." However, DJs preferred the flip side, leading it to command the Hot 100 for five weeks by October 1967. "To serve with love British superstar Lulu's only American top 10 hit was a huge one. ## curiosities
**"To Sir, with Love" Movie (1967)** : Lulu co-starred in the 1967 movie "To Sir, with Love" alongside Sidney Poitier. The film was set in a British inner-city school and served as the context for the song of the same name. **"To Sir with Love" Songwriting (1967)** : Lulu disliked the initial song candidates for the movie's theme, so she suggested her friend, songwriter Mark London, who penned the melody in five minutes. Don Black wrote the lyrics the following day, leading to the creation of "To Sir with Love." "So Lulu proposed her friend songwriter Mark London for the job and he penned the melody to the song in five minutes." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The B-Sides Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
