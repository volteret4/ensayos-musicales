**Heat Waves (2022)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 2022. Recorded and first released in 2020 at the start of the pandemic, its "moodiness aligns with this year of vibes," characterizing ethereal tracks that scaled the charts. It spent 91 weeks on the Hot 100, breaking The Weeknd's record for most time on the chart.
