**Pioneer of Guitar Effects** : Jet Beck was a pioneer in introducing new sounds and techniques to the electric guitar. He introduced reverberation, the hammer-on technique, deep distortions, and innovative microphone placements that created "contamination" with speakers and amplifiers, revolutionizing guitar playing.
