## curiosities
**Involved in Marc Bolan's Death (September 16, 1977)** : Gloria Jones was Mark Bolan's girlfriend and was driving the Mini Cooper when it crashed into a tree in southwest London on September 16, 1977, resulting in Bolan's death. "his girlfriend Gloria Jones drove their mini-cooper into a tree in southwest London." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=blDNqVFheAw ← gloria-jones ← gloria-jones
**Re-recording after Northern Soul Popularity (1973)** : After British club DJ Richard Seerling began playing her original B-side "Tainted Love" to great success in Northern Soul clubs, Gloria Jones re-recorded the song in 1973, but this new version also failed to gain traction.
