**Standard Setter for Rap Eloquence** : By the late 1980s, Rakim was widely regarded as the "standard setter for rap eloquence" and the "king of hip hop flow." His prominent rap segments on Jody Watley's "Friends" (1989) showcased this talent and helped establish a new template for pop-rap crossover.
