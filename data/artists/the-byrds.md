**Formation at The Troubadour** : The Byrds were formed when Roger McGuinn, initially a traditional folky, began playing Beatles songs at The Troubadour. Not long after, he returned with Gene Clark, and history was made when David Crosby sang, leading to an impromptu harmonizing session among the three at the club. **Influence on Space Oddity Sound** : The Byrds were pioneers of psychedelic folk and folk rock whose "trippy grooves" echoed in David Bowie's "Space Oddity." This sound, combined with British Folk and Bee Gees-style storytelling, marked Bowie's move away from Mod Rock towards a new phase.
