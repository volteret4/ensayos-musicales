## albums
**Eyes That See in the Dark (1983)** : Barry Gibb was asked by country megastar Kenny Rogers to produce his 1983 album. Impressed by Gibb's prior country success (with Conway Twitty) and his work with Barbara Streisand, Rogers enlisted Barry, Robin, and Maurice Gibb to write and produce the entirety of the album, which included the smash hit "Islands in the Stream." **Guilty (1980)** : Barry Gibb produced Barbara Streisand's 1980 album "Guilty," which was described as "a Bee Gees album in Barbara Streisand clothing." He was photographed on the cover with Streisand and, though not formally credited as a Bee Gee, was presented as her artistic equal. All nine tracks were written or co-written by Barry Gibb, with his team of Albe Galuton and Carl Richardson producing, and more than half the songs included writing credits by Robin or Maurice Gibb. ## curiosities
**Uncredited Backing Vocalist and Producer** : Barry Gibb wrote Samantha Sang's 1978 hit "Emotion" and sang such prominent backup vocals on the chorus that he "basically hijacks the recording," making it almost a duet, yet he received no co-credit. He also produced and performed on many hits for his brother Andy Gibb, similarly disappearing into the fine print. "Gibb basically hijacks the recording from Samantha Sang.
