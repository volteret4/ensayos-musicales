**Fatal Boating Accident in Cozumel (December 2000)** : On December 18, 2000, while scuba diving with her sons and dive master Ivan Diaz off Cozumel's Chunkanab reef, a national marine park restricted to power boats, Kirsty MacColl was struck and killed instantly by the propellers of a 31-foot motorboat named the "Percolito." The boat, powered by 315 horsepower engines, roared through the restricted area as MacColl surfaced, and she managed to push her 15-year-old son Jamie out of the way just before impact, sustaining severe head and chest injuries that virtually severed her body. "Just after 2pm, just as Kirstie came to the surface after the first of two planned dives, a 31-foot motorboat called the Percolito came roaring through this restricted area with its 315 horsepower engines.
