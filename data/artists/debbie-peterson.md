## songs
**Be With You (1989)** : This song was co-written and sung by Peterson and chosen as the third single from *Everything* by the band members to provide a showcase for someone other than Susanna Hoffs. However, it stalled at number 30 on the Hot 100, which undermined the band's push for a more egalitarian spotlight. ## curiosities
**Drummer for The Bangles and Collaborator** : Debbie Peterson was the drummer for The Bangles, a band that transitioned from the alternative Paisley Underground scene to a hugely successful Top 40 act. After The Bangles' breakup, she went on to play with notable artists like Cindy Lauper, Tom Petty, and Elvis Costello. "Their drummer was Debbie Peterson. Since The Bangles break up, Debbie has played with Cindy Lopper and Tom Petty and Elvis Costello, just to name a few." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9GHpC0kCgHY ← debbie-peterson
