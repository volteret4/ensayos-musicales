# artist - Cameron Crowe

## curiosities
**1977 Article on Little River Band** : In 1977, he authored an article about Little River Band, describing them as having "melodic lead guitarists and for sophisticated singer songwriters" who "mastered an effortless commerciality." During his interview with the band, lead guitarist David Briggs asked him to avoid comparing them to CSNY, while Crowe personally felt their musical spirit was closer to The Beatles. **Director of "Singles"** : Cameron Crowe directed the 1992 romantic comedy "Singles," which was his second film and the follow-up to "Say Anything." Set in Seattle's Grunge scene, the movie famously featured cameos by members of Soundgarden, Alice in Chains, and Pearl Jam. "Singles, a romantic comedy from the director Cameron Crowe. **Prescient Filming** : Crowe filmed "Singles" six months before Soundgarden, Alice in Chains, and Pearl Jam achieved widespread fame, and even before Pearl Jam released their debut album. This made the movie uncannily prescient in capturing the burgeoning Seattle music wave.
