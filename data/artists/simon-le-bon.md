## songs
**Do They Know It's Christmas? (1984)** : Simon Le Bon was one of the initial British pop stars contacted by Bob Geldof for Band-Aid's "Do They Know It's Christmas?" He shared his verses with Sting in the song's solo-laden first half and delivered a "smooth" solo that was noted as a memorable "firework" moment.
