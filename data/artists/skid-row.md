## albums
**Skid Row (1989)** : Released in 1989, this self-titled debut album generated a pair of top 10 hits: "I Remember You" (number 6) and "18 & Life" (number 4). Despite riding the album chart for a year and a half and selling 5 million copies, the album never rose higher than number 6. **Slave to the Grind (1991)** : This album debuted at number one on the Billboard 200, despite generating no top 10 hits on any chart and selling less than half as many copies as its predecessor. Its chart-topping success was attributed to a combination of the ACDC rule (a massive, gradually acquired fanbase showing up all at once) and the more accurate SoundScan data.
