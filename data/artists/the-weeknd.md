## songs
**Blinding Lights (2020)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 2020. Abel Tesfaye, known as The Weeknd, a Torontonian, drew heavily on 80s musical influences for this "mega smash." Its victory was largely due to its "epic longevity," spending a record 90 weeks on the Hot 100. Its presence was so prolonged that it was also the number three song of 2021. While its overall chart longevity record was broken, it still holds records for most time in the Top 5 (43 weeks) and Top 10 (57 weeks).
