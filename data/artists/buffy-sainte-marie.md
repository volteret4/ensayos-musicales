**Early Online Collaboration Pioneer** : For her album "Coincidence and Likely Stories," Buffy St. Marie, living in Hawaii, collaborated with Chris Berkett in London by sending MIDI files of her keyboard parts via Compuserve (a dial-in modem service before the World Wide Web). This pioneering method allowed the album to be "born on the web." "So Roger managed to get her MIDI files onto the Compuserve and I managed to work out and download them via Compuserve into my system in London." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjQBaX_3oL4 ← buffy-st-marie ← buffy-st-marie
