## albums
**Young Americans (1975)** : A then-unknown Luther Vandross collaborated on David Bowie's 1975 album 'Young Americans'. He not only sang backup vocals but also arranged the vocals on the title track, leading his chorus to sing a lyrical reference to The Beatles' "A Day in the Life" in the middle of the song.
