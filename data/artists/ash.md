**Bus Accident on I-5 (2002)** : On August 16, 2002, while Ash was part of Moby's Area Two Tour and traveling to Detroit, their bus tipped over on Interstate 5 in Oregon. The incident occurred after the driver swerved to avoid large tire shreds in the middle of the road. All band and crew members, who were sleeping in their bunks at the time, were thrown from their positions, with drummer Rick McMurray sustaining broken ribs, which was thankfully the worst of the injuries. "Ash was a part of Moby's area two tour and they were on their way to Detroit on the night of August 16th, 2002 when their bus tipped over on interstate five in Oregon." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DgN0vBIugLc ← ash
