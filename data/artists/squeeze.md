## albums
**East Side Story (1981)** : This album by Squeeze was produced by Elvis Costello, who was returning a favor to Squeeze frontman Glenn Tilbrook after Tilbrook provided counterpoint vocals on Costello's "From a Whisper to a Scream." This collaboration highlights the reciprocal relationships between artists in the early 1980s new wave scene. ## songs
**Tempted (1981)** : This song, released in 1981, became Squeeze's biggest hit. It features keyboardist Paul Carrack on lead vocals, who was only in the band for about a year. The track has been widely covered by artists like Sting and Joe Cocker, and has been used extensively in TV commercials for brands such as Heineken and Burger King, as well as in video games like Grand Theft Auto. ## curiosities
**New Wave Powerpop** : Squeeze is included in the list of bands associated with the new wave form of Powerpop. Their work exemplifies the genre's ability to evolve and integrate into the broader alternative rock landscape of the late 1970s and early 1980s. **Paul Carrack's Short Tenure and Future Role** : Paul Carrack, who sang lead on Squeeze's biggest hit "Tempted," was only a member of the band for approximately one year and did not stay much longer. He later became the permanent lead singer for Mike + The Mechanics.
