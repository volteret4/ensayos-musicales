**Come Away With Me (2002)** : Released on Blue Note Records, this was Nora Jones' "juggernaut debut album" that came out in February 2002. Before its official release, actress Sandra Bullock had an advanced copy and sought to include music from it in her movie "Two Weeks Notice." The initial recording session for the album with producer Craig Street involved recording 21 songs in a week and mixing them immediately, but the album was rejected due to perceived issues with the mixes. Ultimately, three "amazing takes" from this session were used on the final record. She was eager to avoid being "pigeonholed" into a specific sound like a piano trio and deliberately explored new genres, such as bluegrass, which influenced her second album to be "a little more country." Don Was compared her artistic evolution to Miles Davis, noting how she, like Miles, always sounded like herself but kept it interesting by "changing the textures."
