**I Will Survive (1979)** : Co-written by Freddie Perrin, this immortal disco classic became a number one hit in late winter 1979, marking a comeback for Gloria Gaynor. Initially underestimated by her label, Polydor, it was placed on the B-side of the late 1978 single "Substitute." The song was unusual for the time, even among disco songs, for its lack of background vocalists and its relentlessly wordy nature. Disco and radio programmers discovered the "garrulous anthem" on the B-side, and it debuted on the Hot 100 just before Christmas 1978. It took three months to reach the top, spent three weeks at number one, and went platinum. For Gaynor, who had undergone spinal surgery after a non-stage collapse, it was a personal declaration of independence and an anthem for survival. "I will survive co-written by veteran songwriter Freddie Perrin... was unusual for the time, even among disco songs, for featuring no background vocalists and being so relentlessly wordy." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The B-Sides Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
