**Influence on 1980s Snare Drum Sound** : Phil Collins' distinctive drum sound profoundly influenced recording producers and engineers in the 1980s, marking a significant departure from earlier close-miking techniques. His sound, characterized as big, fat, boomy, and powerful, inspired the adoption of recording acoustic drums in large rooms with hard surfaces to incorporate natural echo and long decay, rather than seeking dead quiet environments. "The biggest influence on the new 80s snare drum sound was Phil Collins." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TsiYLGGSJ2U ← phil-collins
