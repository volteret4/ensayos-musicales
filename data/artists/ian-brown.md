## curiosities
**British Airways Air Rage and Imprisonment (1998)** : On February 13, 1998, following an MTV event in Paris, Ian Brown boarded British Airways flight 1611 from Charles de Gaulle to Manchester. Once airborne, he reportedly argued with a flight attendant, allegedly threatening to cut her hands off, and then caused a disturbance near the cockpit by banging on the door and swearing at the crew during the plane's descent. Upon landing, he was arrested and charged with endangering the safety of an aircraft. Despite his defense, a judge with ties to the airline industry sentenced him to four months in Strangeways Prison in Manchester to "set an example," and he is now permanently banned from flying British Airways. "The message to the public, he said, must be that anyone who behaves in this way will lose their liberty." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eGBnwPWRGo ← ian-brown
