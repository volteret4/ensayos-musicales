**Venus (1986)** : This British dance pop trio regularly covered "Venus" in their live sets before deciding to transform it into a high-energy club song. They enlisted British hitmaking production team Stock Aitken Waterman, resulting in a smash that hit the top 10 in their British homeland and number one in America. It was their first U.S. chart-topper for Stock Aitken Waterman and remains Bananarama's only number one U.S. hit, a staple of 80s classic hits radio.
