## albums
**All Over the Place (1984)** : The Bangles' 1984 debut album is noted as a "great sounding record." Before their mainstream success as a Top 40 band, they were part of the alternative scene known as The Paisley Underground. "Their debut album from 1984 is still a great sounding record." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9GHpC0kCgHY ← the-bangles

## songs
**Walk Like an Egyptian (1987)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1987, a "catchy, kooky" phenomenon paired with a memorable dance. It topped the Hot 100 for four weeks from late 1986 into early 1987, and no other song surpassed its longevity for the remainder of 1987. The Bangles are recognized as the only all-female rock band to achieve a year-end Hot 100 number one. "Hitchie, Fattish, and paired with a Kookie Dance, The Bangles' biggest hit was a phenomenon, topping the hot 104 weeks at the end of 1986 into early 1987." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hits of the Year Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce81

## curiosities
**Early Booking by Kevin Lyman** : The Bangles were among the bands booked by Kevin Lyman, the future founder of the Warped Tour, for university performances at California State Polytechnic University in Pomona when they were starting their career in the early 1980s. "What he would basically do is hire bands like Bad Religion and the Bangles when those groups were just starting out in the book at the university." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7jfQaUV6Bq4 ← the-bangles
**Transition from Alt-Psych to Pop** : The Bangles began as a popular four-piece, all-female band in the US West Coast's Paisley Underground scene around 1982, initially playing alt-psych. They later transitioned into a successful pure pop band, similar to The Go-Go's. "But in the beginning, they were very alt-psych. They were called the bangles." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LgJ7ww2AgF4 ← the-bangles
**Only All-Female Rock Band to Score Year-End #1 (1987)** : The Bangles are the "only all-female rock band to score a year and hot 100 number one" with "Walk Like an Egyptian," marking a significant milestone in music history. "Incidentally, The Bangles are the only all-female rock band to score a year and hot 100 number one." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hits of the Year Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce81
