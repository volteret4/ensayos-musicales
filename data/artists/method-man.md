According to Steven Tallte's New York Times magazine cover story, Method Man's label, Def Jam, specifically recorded and timed the single's release to "blow up" during the summer, strategically pairing the Wu Tang Clan rapper with the "Queen of Hip Hop Soul." **The Riddler (1995)** : This deep cut was included on the *Batman Forever* soundtrack, but it was not featured in the film itself. This exemplifies the trend of "inspired by" soundtracks in the mid-90s, where artists recorded tracks knowing they might not make the movie.
