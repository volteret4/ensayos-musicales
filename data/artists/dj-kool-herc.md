**The Merry Go Round Technique** : DJ Kool Herk pioneered the "merry go round" technique, which involved using two turntables to extend the "break" sections of funk and soul records. He observed that dancers (B-boys) would wait for specific parts of records to perform their special moves. By extending these instrumental breaks, he allowed dancers more time to "do their thing" and showcase their best moves, which became known as breakdancing in the 1980s. He discussed this experiment in a 2005 interview with Terry Gross on NPR's Fresh Air.
