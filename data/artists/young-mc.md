**Bust a Move (1989)** : Written by Marvin Young (Young MC), this loquacious story song reached number seven in the fall of 1989. It was a friendlier, catchier, and more melodic track that, like Tone Loc's "Wild Thing," represented a fun-loving party rap approach. The song's lyrics tell a tongue-twisting tale about smarties, parties, and a best friend's wedding. ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Oh. My. God.
