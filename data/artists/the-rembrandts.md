The Rembrandts recorded a version that was long enough to function as a standalone radio single, distinguishing it from many TV themes. **I'll Be There for You (1995)** : This song served as the theme to the hit NBC sitcom *Friends* in 1994 and 1995. Despite being "the most played song at US Radio" for eight weeks in the summer of 1995 and a "would-be chart-topper," it initially did not appear on the Hot 100 because The Rembrandts, compelled by their label but wishing to maintain an alternative rock image, insisted it only be available as an album cut. Later, in the fall of 1995, it was belatedly issued as the B-side to their follow-up track "This House Is Not A Home" on a cassette single, finally appearing on the Hot 100 and reaching number 17, driven by buyers wanting to own the *Friends* theme.
