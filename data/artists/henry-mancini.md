## albums
**The Music from Peter Gunn (1959)** : Composed by Henry Mancini, this smash TV soundtrack took home the Album of the Year prize at the very first Grammy Awards in 1959. It subsequently became Billboard's top-selling LP of 1959, highlighting an early instance where Grammy voters, despite their stated intentions, rewarded mass-popular music. **The Music from Peter Gunn (1959)** : This soundtrack album for the TV detective show "Peter Gunn" was initially underestimated by RCA Records, which only printed 8,000 copies, questioning demand for instrumental TV music. Despite this, it went on to top Billboard's Best Selling LPs chart for 10 weeks in 1959, establishing a new TV-to-pop-charts model for instrumental themes. "Henry Mancini's soundtrack to the TV detective show Peter Gunn. The album underestimated by RCA records which only printed 8,000 copies at first, wondering who would want to buy an LP of instrumental TV music, went on to top Billboard's Best Selling LP's for 10 Weeks in 1959." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > A Deal with the TV God Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
