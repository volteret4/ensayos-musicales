**Theme from Mission: Impossible (1968)** : Lalo Schifrin's classic instrumental theme for the CBS espionage show "Mission: Impossible" cracked the Hot 100, peaking at number 41 in 1968, just missing the top 40. Unlike Henry Mancini's "Peter Gunn" theme, which was a huge album seller but not a single, Schifrin's theme achieved chart recognition as a single. "Lalo Schifrin's classic theme for the CBS espionage show Mission Impossible, which sounded cool even away from the two. Unlike Henry Mancini's theme from Peter Gunn, which sold truckloads of albums but didn't chart as a single, Schifrin's banger of the theme actually cracked the hot 100, just missing the top 40 at number 41 in 1968." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > A Deal with the TV God Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
