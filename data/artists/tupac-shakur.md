**California Love (1996)** : This song was initially only available on Tupac's early 1996 album, "All Eyes On Me." It was eventually issued as a single in June 1996, included as Track 2 on the "How Do You Want It?" maxi-single. The "Double A-side" single sold rapidly, driven by demand from casual fans who had waited half a year to buy "California Love" on its own. While the single topped the Hot 100, "California Love" was technically listed as a B-side because "How Do You Want It?" had stronger airplay. It is considered Tupac's greatest hit.
