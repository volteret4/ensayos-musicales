**The Christmas Song (Merry Christmas to You / Chestnuts Roasting on an Open Fire) (1946)** : Written by jazz and pop crooner Mel Tormé, Cole and his trio recorded this song in 1946, notably layering on strings for the first time. It became a smash, reaching number three on both Billboard's pop and R&B charts, and returned year after year. The success of this song changed the direction of Cole's career, leading him to re-record it multiple times with arrangers like Nelson Riddle, singing on increasingly elaborate orchestral arrangements. ## curiosities
**Capitol Records Era** : Nat King Cole was a significant artist, referred to as a "crew member," at Capitol Records during the 1940s. The label in this period was largely dedicated to traditional music forms and standards, predating its significant involvement with rock and roll.
