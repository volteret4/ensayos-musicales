# artist - Ja Rule

## curiosities
**Fyre Festival Co-Founder and Promoter** : Ja Rule, in collaboration with Billy McFarland, conceived the idea for the Fyre Festival. This event was promoted as an ultra-exclusive, multi-day concert on a private Bahamian island, promising luxury experiences and top artists to attendees who paid up to $250,000 for VIP packages. "Together with rapper Ja Rule, he conceived the idea of an ultra-exclusive multi-day concert event on a private island in the Bahamas." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GYUuTbOvyak ← ja-rule
**Precedent for Self-Succession in Featured Roles** : Ja Rule was one of two acts (Puff Daddy) who succeeded themselves in featured roles on the Hot 100. This broader category of chart success included Outcast, who were the seventh act to achieve self-succession. "When we include two other acts who succeeded themselves in featured roles, Puff Daddy and Ja Rule, Outcast were the seventh act to achieve this feat." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Shake It Like a Polaroid Picture Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ee9d1ba84fb8f044632
