## songs
**Ain't It Funny (2001)** : Following the controversial success of "I'm Real," Ja Rule went back into the studio with Jennifer Lopez in the fall of 2001 to reimagine "Ain't It Funny" from her album "J-Lo." This resulted in another entirely new song credited as a remix. Billboard, making a concession to Sony Music, grandfathered this track in, allowing its two distinct versions to be merged for chart tracking. It reached number one in March 2002, becoming the final hit to benefit from this controversial remix merging strategy. **I'm Real (2001)** : Born Jeffrey Atkins in Queens, New York, Ja Rule was on a roll in 2000 and 2001 with a string of hits on Murder Incorporated, often featuring female singers and rappers, consistent with his "thug and love" image. Jennifer Lopez's producer Corey Rooney commissioned Ja Rule and the Murder Inc. team to completely remake Lopez's song "I'm Real." This resulted in a track that shared almost nothing with the original beyond the title, including different tempo, melody, and lyrics. The Murder Remix dominated airplay, leading Billboard to credit the song as Jennifer Lopez featuring Ja Rule when it reached number one and spent five weeks at the top. ## curiosities
**Fyre Festival Co-Founder and Promoter** : Ja Rule, in collaboration with Billy McFarland, conceived the idea for the Fyre Festival. This event was promoted as an ultra-exclusive, multi-day concert on a private Bahamian island, promising luxury experiences and top artists to attendees who paid up to $250,000 for VIP packages. "Together with rapper Ja Rule, he conceived the idea of an ultra-exclusive multi-day concert event on a private island in the Bahamas." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GYUuTbOvyak ← ja-rule
