## songs
**Warm Leatherette (1978)** : Released in November 1978 under the name The Normal, this song was recorded by Daniel Miller in his bedroom using a Korg 700S synthesizer and a 4-track reel-to-reel machine, despite his lack of traditional musical experience. It became a hit, selling 30,000 copies after Rough Trade record shop in Notting Hill agreed to stock a test pressing, intriguing many young musicians with its minimalist, futuristic, and attitude-filled sound. **Warm Leatherette (1978)** : This seven-inch single, released by Daniel Miller's one-man synth band project The Normal, sold 30,000 copies. The profits from this single were reinvested into Mute Records, which Miller had founded to release his own music. "He had a one-man synth band project called The Normal. ## curiosities
**Bedroom Punk Aesthetic with Synthesizers** : Daniel Miller, aspiring to be a punk but unable to play guitar, bought a Korg 700S and a 4-track reel-to-reel machine from his film editor savings. He recorded "Warm Leatherette" in his bedroom, creating a minimalist, futuristic sound with attitude, which sold 30,000 copies. This demonstrated that musical experience wasn't necessary for creating impactful electronic music, embodying a punk sensibility of DIY attitude over traditional ability. "He started experimenting with it in his bedroom. Recorded two songs, found some money to make a test pressing and convinced the rough-trade record shop in Notting Hill to stock it." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5PJRCC6EwUY ← the-normal-daniel-miller
**Minimalist Sound as a Contrast** : The minimalist sound of Daniel Miller's "Warm Leatherette" served as a benchmark against which the later, more sonically advanced and textured Technopop songs from 1980-81 were compared, highlighting the rapid technological progress in a short period. "Remember what warm leatherettes sounded like? Now compare that minimalism to something like this, which came out less than two years ago." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5PJRCC6EwUY ← the-normal-daniel-miller
**Bedroom Recording Pioneer** : Daniel Miller, the sole member of The Normal, composed and recorded the single "Warm Leatherette" in his bedroom in 1978, showcasing the accessibility of electronic music production. "Daniel Miller, the solitary member of a band known as The Normal from 1978 with Warm Leatherette, a single that became a substantial UK hit that year, and a song that was composed and recorded in Daniel's bedroom." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9uTORLOm5Yg ← the-normal
**Founding Mute Records** : Using the profits from "Warm Leatherette," Daniel Miller established his own independent record label, Mute, dedicated exclusively to guitar-less music, which later introduced artists like Depeche Mode, Erasure, and Yazoo. "He took the profits and founded his own independent record label devoted entirely to guitar less music. He called the label Mute." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9uTORLOm5Yg ← the-normal
**Funding Mute Records Expansion** : The money generated from the sale of 30,000 copies of The Normal's seven-inch single, "Warm Leatherette," was reinvested by Daniel Miller into Mute Records, which eventually led to his collaboration with Depeche Mode. "Daniel took the money from that single Warm Leatherette by The Normal and plowed it back into mute, which eventually saw him cross paths with Depeche Mode." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sCtQqVBtCaI ← the-normal
**Independent Sales Success** : After recording "Warm Leatherette" in his bedroom, Miller sold 30,000 copies by placing it for sale on consignment in independent record stores. "And just by putting it for sale on consignment and independent record stores, Daniel sold 30,000 copies." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9uTORLOm5Yg ← the-normal
**Influenced by Bowie and Kraftwerk** : Miller was influenced by the electronic music of David Bowie and Kraftwerk, combining the concept of stylish, futuristic, machine-generated music with punk rock's DIY aesthetic. "Like a number of young British musicians, Miller had been exposed to craft work and the electronic musings of David Bowie." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9uTORLOm5Yg ← the-normal
