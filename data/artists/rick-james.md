**Super Freak (1981)** : This classic funk jam reached number 16 on the Hot 100 in 1981 and number 3 on Billboard's R&B chart. Its potential was arguably hindered by radio formatting in the early 80s, where pop stations underplayed or underreported records by Black artists due to the disco backlash. However, it was prominently sampled by MC Hammer on his 1990 smash "U Can't Touch This." Currently, "Super Freak" garners 25,000 radio spins and 55 million streams in the last year, vastly surpassing his other hits like "Cold Blooded."
