## curiosities
**Founders of The Batcave Club** : Members of Specimen founded The Batcave club in Soho, London, in July 1982, which became the central headquarters for the burgeoning goth scene, characterized by its theatrical, performance art-like approach and specific aesthetic. "it was called the Batcave. And it was founded by members of a group called Specimen." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XaLsZFLrMnI ← specimen
**Radical First Single Release** : Specimen's first single was uniquely released on video, a seriously radical move for 1982, demonstrating their theatrical and performance art-like approach to music. "For example, their first single wasn't released on record or cassette. They issued it on video, a seriously radical thing to do for 1982." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XaLsZFLrMnI ← specimen
**Spreading the Goth Gospel** : Specimen, through their Batcave club, played a significant role in spreading the goth culture, with Batcave nights franchising to other UK clubs and even to New York, establishing a consistent look, feel, and sound. "The Batcave people did a lot, probably more than anyone, to spread the gospel of Goth in those early days. And again, the heart of it all was specimen." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XaLsZFLrMnI ← specimen
**Founding The Batcave Club** : Speseman, described as a highly ambitious and theatrical group focused on innovative live performances, founded The Batcave nightclub in London in July 1982. "The Batcave was founded by a group called Speseman, a very theatrical group obsessed with pushing the envelope of the live performance." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gd9sO7c3N1s ← speseman
**Video-Only Single Release** : Their first single was released exclusively on video, not on traditional record or tape, an unusual and "pretty wild" approach for the early 1980s. "For example, their first single wasn't released on record or tape. It was only released on video, which is pretty wild for the early 1980s." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gd9sO7c3N1s ← speseman
