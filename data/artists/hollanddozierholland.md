## songs
**Locking Up My Heart** : This was the first single released with a Holland-Dozier-Holland writing credit, written and produced for The Marvelettes after Motown requested a new single for their album "The Marvelous Marvelettes." The song was a comparative flop for the group and contributed to their "downward slump." ## curiosities
**Followed Bowie Bonds Model** : The Motown songwriting team of Holland-Dozier-Holland followed David Bowie's innovative financial model, utilizing intellectual property to secure financial instruments after the success of the Bowie Bonds. This demonstrated the broader impact of Bowie's pioneering business acumen in the music industry. "And once the Bowie bonds sold, others followed, including the Motown songwriting team of Holland-Doseyour Holland, as well as a number of other professional songwriters." ← Ongoing History of New Music > Remembering David Bowie： Part 2 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2FyAZGUI_mk&list=PLM8bxV0E1Gse5C3n1u-mkVSTZLL5AJICG
**Lamont Dozier's Death** : Lamont Dozier, who was one-third of the highly influential Motown songwriting team Holland-Dozier-Holland, died on August 8th at the age of 81. "That very same day at Lamont Dozier, one-third of the great Motown songwriting team, Holland Dozier Holland, died at the age of 81." ← Ongoing History of New Music > In Memoriam of Those Lost in 2022 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdPeii-8Ysw&list=PLM8bxV0E1Gse5C3n1u-mkVSTZLL5AJICG
**Autobiography Title** : The autobiography of Brian and Eddie Holland is named after "Come and Get These Memories," despite the team having co-written many bigger and more obvious hits. This highlights the significance of that song as the beginning of the Motown sound. "Brian and Eddie Holland's autobiography is named after this, rather than after any of the bigger and more obvious hits they would like to co-write." ← Episode 111： ＂Heat Wave＂ by Martha and the Vandellas | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AAiUJhSKjzs
**Division of Labor** : Within the Holland, Dozier and Holland team, Brian Holland and Lamont Dozier typically took charge of the musical aspects, including riffs, hooks, melodies, chord changes, and studio work with drummers, keyboardists, and bass players. Eddie Holland primarily concentrated on crafting lyrics, focusing on meaning and "repeat formation." "Holland Dozier and Holland, really Brian Holland and the Montdozier, who took charge of the musical side of things with Eddie concentrating on the lyrics, would make their own." ← Episode 111： ＂Heat Wave＂ by Martha and the Vandellas | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AAiUJhSKjzs
**Motown Domination** : The team of Holland, Dozier and Holland were pivotal in Motown's domination of the American charts throughout the rest of the decade, following the release of their defining "Motown sound" record for Martha and the Van Dellers. They transitioned from minor players to the most important team for the label's commercial success. "it began a domination of the American charts that would last the rest of the decade, a domination caused in large part by the team of Holland, Dozier and Holland." ← Episode 111： ＂Heat Wave＂ by Martha and the Vandellas | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AAiUJhSKjzs
