**Hound Dog (1953)** : This song was a million-selling R&B number one hit in 1953, years before Elvis Presley's cover version. Written for Thornton by legendary songwriters Jerry Lieber and Mike Stolar, "Hound Dog" is characterized as a "feisty rant by a woman telling a gigolo to hit the road." Its immense popularity led to an answer record from Rufus Thomas titled "Bear Cat." ## curiosities
**Annoyed by Lieber's Direction** : Big Mama Thornton was reportedly annoyed at Jerry Lieber for instructing her on how to sing "Hound Dog," one of the very first songs written by Lieber and Stoller. This incident highlights Lieber's characteristic approach to directing lead vocalists.
