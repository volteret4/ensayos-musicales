# artist - Salt-N-Pepa

## songs
**Push It (1988)** : This sexually-frank hit was certified gold in the spring of 1988. Despite its blockbuster sales, reaching platinum status by the end of the year and being the only gold record on the Hot 100 at one point when vinyl 45s sales were slumping, it only peaked at number 19. **Whatta Man (1994)** : This 1994 song featured girl group En Vogue. It is a notable example of the "featured hook singer" model where women take both the lead rapper position (Salt-N-Pepa) and the featured singer role (En Vogue). "For example, in 1994 on What A Man, women take both parts with Salt and Peppa in the lead rapper position and girl group and Vogue as the featured singer." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Feat. ## curiosities
**Guest Star on Martin** : The hip hop group appeared as guest stars on the TV show *Martin*, which was known for featuring "hip hop royalty" in its episodes. "Cammyos and guest stars on the show included Snoop Dogg Christopher Kid Reed, Salt and Peppa, Biggie Smalls, and Outcast." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6O5TCTO-go4 ← salt-and-peppa
**Platinum Level Rappers** : Salt-N-Pepa are identified as among the "platinum level women rappers" who demonstrated the strength of female hip hop throughout the 1990s. Their success highlighted a "glaring omission" in Lilith Fair's initial lineup, which was light on R&B and nearly devoid of hip hop. "Queen Latifa, Salt and Peppin, Debrat, Lisa left eye Lopez, there was a wealth of platinum level women rappers in the 90s." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Building a Herstory Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
