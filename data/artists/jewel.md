**Foolish Games (1995)** : This song was a deep cut on the *Batman Forever* soundtrack and was not included in the film. Its presence highlights the common practice in the mid-90s of padding soundtracks with tracks not actually featured in the movie, often to leverage movie hype for artist visibility. "There were deep cuts on the soundtrack like Method Man's The Ridler or Jewels Foolish Games that weren't in the films at all." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hooked to the Silver Screen Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

**Foolish Games (1995)** : Following the strategy used for "You Were Meant for Me," Atlantic and Jewel re-recorded this deep cut. **Unsuccessful Country Crossover (2000s)** : Folk-pop singer Jewel attempted multiple crossovers to the country charts in the 2000s, but her efforts never reached higher than number 13 on Billboard's Hot Country Songs. She serves as an example of a "pop carpetbagger" who struggled to gain acceptance and significant chart success in the country format. "Folkpop singer Jewel got no higher than number 13 on Billboard's Hot Country songs with her many crossover attempts." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > A Little Love and Some Tenderness Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Beneficiary of Morissette's Success** : Jewel was among the intrepid women rockers who found increased access to alt rock and pop radio following Alanis Morissette's significant triumph. "But it also opened up both alt rock and even pop radio to another wave of intrepid women rockers, including Fiona Apple." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > All Apologies Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Shift from Alt-Rock to Pop Megastar (1996)** : Jewel, an Alaskan folk pop singer-songwriter, initially saw her breakthrough hit "Who Will Save Your Soul" gain traction at alt-rock stations (peaking at number 14). However, her greater and more enduring success came at pop stations, leading to her becoming a "pop megastar" with her second single "You Were Meant for Me" and her album *Pieces of You* selling 12 million copies.
