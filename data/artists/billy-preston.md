**The "Fifth Beatle"** : Billy Preston was one of the few individuals referred to as the "Fifth Beatle," alongside producer George Martin, drummer Pete Best, and manager Brian Epstein. He earned this distinction by being the only other musician to be credited on a record label alongside The Beatles, playing keyboards during their "Let It Be" sessions in 1969 and on "Abbey Road." His manager, Joyce Moore, described his contribution as "sprinkl[ing] the glue and the glitter" that made everything "just felt good" when he joined the band.
