## albums
**Chariots of Fire (1981)** : Vangelis's score for the 1981 film *Chariots of Fire* broke a drought of soundtracks topping the Billboard album chart, achieving number one status on both the album and singles charts in May 1982. The score was a blend of old Hollywood energy, as it accompanied an Oscar-winning picture, and new technology, being composed largely on synthesizers, which set a precedent for 80s movie music.
