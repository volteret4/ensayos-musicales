**Overlooked Philadelphia Artist** : Darryl Hall mentioned The Stylistics by name in his Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction speech as an example of a Philadelphia artist overlooked by the institution, advocating for more Philadelphia artists to be recognized. "What happened to the stylistics and the delphinics?" ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Rock ’n Soul, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Influence on Barry Gibb's Falsetto** : Barry Gibb specifically cited the Philly Soul sound of The Stylistics, particularly the exceptionally high falsetto of their lead singer Russell Tompkins Jr., as a key model for his new vocal approach. "Barry Gibb specifically cited the Philly Soul sound of the stylistics whose lead singer Russell Tompkins Jr sang in an especially high falsetto, a key model for Barry's new vocal approach." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Nights on Broadway Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
