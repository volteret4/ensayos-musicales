**Impact on Sexual Identity** : Mark Bowen's glittery, satiny, and somewhat sexually ambiguous public persona on TV, especially given his height of five foot four, resonated deeply in early 1970s Britain, where homosexuality had only recently been decriminalized (1967 Sexual Offences Act) but public displays remained illegal. For some, his appearance pushed boundaries, while for others, it offered a liberating affirmation of their own non-binary feelings. "Now, for some, a pretty glittery, satiny, five foot four Mark Bowen on TV was really close to that line about doing something gay in public. For others, though, it was not just a breath of fresh air. It was also like, hey, that's me. I feel like that. And it's okay to be that way." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=blDNqVFheAw ← mark-bowen ← mark-bowen
