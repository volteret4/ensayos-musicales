**Joining The Velvet Underground** : Moe Tucker joined The Velvet Underground at the age of 19, immediately picking up the sticks for the "weirdo New York band." Her decision to pursue music was driven by the appeal of playing in the band over her previous job as a keypunch operator at IBM. **Unconventional Drumming Style** : Moe Tucker, the original drummer for The Velvet Underground, developed a distinctive and unconventional drumming style. She played standing up, primarily used mallets instead of sticks, and notably played a bass drum positioned on its side, employing marching band drums or a timpani. Her approach focused on keeping time and providing deep rhythmic backing, which was visually striking and subtle yet mesmerizing on recordings. "Mot wasn't exactly what you would call a conventional rock drummer. First of all, she played standing up. She played mostly with mallets, not sticks. There was a snare drum and a couple of tom-toms, but no symbols. And most striking of all, her bass drum was on its side." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9GHpC0kCgHY ← maureen-moe-tucker
**Breaking Gender Barriers – First Female Rock Drummer (Retrospectively)** : When Maureen Tucker joined The Velvet Underground in 1965, there were no known women drummers in rock music; playing drums was widely considered a "man's job." She therefore broke significant gender barriers, though this pioneering role was largely unnoticed at the time, possibly due to her unconventional drumming setup. Only in retrospect has she received due recognition as a musical pioneer. "Mo broke down some serious gender barriers But oddly enough no one really took any notice at the time probably because she wasn't setting upper drums properly anyway So only in retrospect that Mo got her due as a musical pioneer." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gsSTJWN6cUo ← maureen-tucker
**Inspiration for Style – Nigerian Drum Virtuoso** : Maureen Tucker stated that her distinctive drumming style and setup, which included playing while standing and using felt mallets, were inspired by a Nigerian drum virtuoso. "Mo says her style and setup was inspired by a Nigerian drum virtuoso." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gsSTJWN6cUo ← maureen-tucker
**Joining The Velvet Underground – Unique Circumstances** : Maureen Tucker was the last member to join The Velvet Underground. She was drafted into the band after their initial drummer quit and relocated to Nepal, where he later died of malnutrition. **Unorthodox Drumming Style – Standing and Mallets** : Maureen Tucker, often known as Mo, had an "unorthodox" drumming style and setup. Instead of sitting behind a conventional drum kit, she stood up to play. She used felt mallets instead of traditional sticks and laid her bass drum on its side to play it with mallets, similar to a marching bass drum. She also "hardly used symbols." "Most style was On orthodox instead of sitting down behind a conventional kit she stood up and played everything with felt mallets instead of sticks." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gsSTJWN6cUo ← maureen-tucker
**Radical Female Drummer Style** : Mo Tucker was noted as a radical female drummer. Instead of sitting behind a standard drum kit, she played standing up, with bass drums turned on their sides, and used mallets instead of traditional drumsticks to strike everything. "Yeah, a woman drummer. That was radical and she played like a radical." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6oUkMOkEr9U ← mo-tucker
