## songs
**Groovy Train (1990)** : This song reached number 6 in the UK and number 15 on the US Modern Rock chart, almost making the US Top 40 by peaking on the Hot 100 at number 41. Its title itself encapsulates the Madchester movement. "And Liverpool sextet The Farm who took Groovy Train. That song title alone really says it all about the Madchester movement." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Champagne Supernova Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Misattribution as Manchester Band** : Although The Farm was actually from Liverpool, they were often "lumped in with Manchester" during the peak of the Madchester scene due to the prevailing sound and cultural influence. "The farm were actually from Liverpool, but they got lumped in with Manchester." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZStbEe4Gkk ← the-farm ← the-farm
