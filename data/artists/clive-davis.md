# artist - Clive Davis

## curiosities
**Rejection of "Bat Out of Hell"** : As the legendary head of CBS Records, Clive Davis flatly rejected Meat Loaf's *Bat Out of Hell* album, famously telling songwriter Jim Steinman, "clearly you have no idea how to write a rock song." This decision by Davis highlighted his skepticism towards the album's unconventional rock opera format.
