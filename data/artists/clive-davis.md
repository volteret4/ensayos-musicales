**Strategic Influence on Millie Vanille's US Album** : Davis, as a hands-on executive, insisted on Millie Vanille swapping out some tracks for the American version of their album. He also proposed they cover The Isley Brothers' "It's Your Thing" and provided a song by Diane Warren. His promotional machine was credited with making Millie Vanille more successful in America than anywhere else globally.
