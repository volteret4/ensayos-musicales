## albums
**I Love My Lady (1981)** : A jazz-inflected album recorded with producers Edwards and Rogers in 1981. It was controversially shelved by Columbia Records, which opted instead to release a Mathis greatest hits album. Tracks from this project would not officially surface until the 2010s. "after recording an entire album, the jazz-inflicted I Love My Lady, the LP was shelved by Columbia Records in favor of a Mathis greatest hits album." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > These Are the Good Times, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Johnny's Greatest Hits (1958)** : This LP, released in 1958, compiled his early singles, including "Chances Are" and "It's Not For Me To Say," and topped the album chart. It is widely credited with inventing the concept of a greatest hits album and set a longevity record, spending 490 weeks (about 10 years) on the Billboard album chart, a record later surpassed by Pink Floyd's "Dark Side of the Moon." "Johnny's greatest hits not only topped the album chart in 1958, the first such compilation to do so, it went on to spend 490 weeks, about 10 years on the Billboard album chart." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Mighty Real Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Pioneer of Pre-Recorded Cassettes** : Johnny Mathis was among the first artists to have pre-recorded cassettes released. These releases began appearing in 1965, marking an early adoption of the compact cassette format by record labels.
