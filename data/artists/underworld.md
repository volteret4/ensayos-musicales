**Born Slippy .NUXX (1995)** : A man from Manchester was threatened with jail time for repeatedly playing Underworld's "Born Slippy" at 3 AM. **Born Slippy .NUXX (1995)** : This pounding electronic dance music track, played with rock, featured fragmented lyrics by vocalist Carl Hyde depicting an alcoholic. Its recurring, echoing keyboard riff behaved like a rock song's guitar riff, creating a "spine tingling hook." A number two UK chart hit, it was the biggest song on the "Trainspotting" soundtrack and was seen as a "working class elegy for passing youth." Carl Hyde acknowledged its loose affiliation with Britpop but felt its performance at Reading Festival signaled "the passing of something and the coming of something else." "Born Slippy by the Welsh band Underworld was pounding electronic dance music played with rock the fragmented lyrics by vocalist Carl Hyde depict an addict in this case an alcoholic drowning in logger logger logger." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Champagne Supernova Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

**Trainspotting Soundtrack Contribution (1996)** : Underworld contributed to the first *Trainspotting* soundtrack in 1996. This soundtrack gained widespread acclaim and is often listed among the best movie soundtracks of all time, known for its mix of old and new rock and electronic music that captured the film's desperate yet uplifting tone. "New Order, Pulp, Blur, Underworld, Elastica, Lou Reed." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zIkjd1OjCXM ← underworld
**Britpop's Swan Song** : "Born Slippy" by Underworld was identified as the song that marked the end of Britpop, alluding to its past while neutralizing the movement, and pointing towards the future of British music in harder club rhythms. **Coachella Performance (2023)** : The band was included on the 2023 Coachella bill, performing "Born Slippy" to a crowd, signifying their lasting legacy a quarter-century after Britpop's rise and fall. "a quarter century after the rise and fall of Britpop, it is perhaps appropriate that the band that sang Britpop's swan song is the one left standing today, delivering the anthem born slippy to a crowd in a tent in India, California, underworld were living a more celebratory version of Park Life." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Champagne Supernova Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Late 90s Electronica Wave (Late 1990s)** : Underworld was a prominent example of a "DJ Driven Act" that characterized the late 1990s wave of Electronica and Dancerock. U2's "Pop" album, with its deeper dive into sampling and synthesizers, was theoretically "on trend" with such artists during this period.
