**Iris (1998)** : Recorded for the soundtrack to the Hollywood blockbuster "City of Angels," this "even more love-lorn ballad" spent five weeks at number one on the modern rock chart. It also set a record with 18 weeks atop the Hot 100 airplay chart, making it the most played song at radio—including many adult contemporary stations—for over four months. **Iris (1998)** : This power ballad was written for the *City of Angels* soundtrack after the movie was completed. Inspired by Nicholas Cage's fallen angel character's romantic travails (though no character named Iris exists in the film), it became the year's most ubiquitous radio hit, spending a record 18 weeks at number one on Hot 100 Airplay. Like "Uninvited," it was not released as a retail single, which propelled the soundtrack to quintuple platinum sales, making it more profitable than the movie. **Iris (1998)** : This song, from the "City of Angels" soundtrack, dominated radio playlists for half of 1998, becoming the most played song at radio for 18 weeks in the summer and fall, an even longer run than No Doubt's "Don't Speak." It is unofficially considered one of the biggest singles of the entire 1998 year and would have been a number one hit if the rule change had occurred a few months earlier, leaving it in "Chart History limbo."
