## albums
**The Rolling Stones Songbook (1965)** : This album, released in 1965, was a collection of orchestral covers of Rolling Stones songs, including an interpretation of "The Last Time." These arrangements were often "watered down" versions, designed for easy listening fans who might be offended by rock and roll music. A five-note progression from their version of "The Last Time" later became the basis for The Verve's "Bittersweet Symphony."
