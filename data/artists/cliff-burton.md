**Ace of Spades Bunk Selection (September 27, 1986)** : The events leading to Cliff Burton's death began with the band drawing cards to decide bunk assignments on the tour bus. Cliff drew the Ace of Spades, giving him first choice, and he chose the bunk Kirk Hammett had been using, making the incident a tragic twist of fate. **Fatal Tour Bus Accident (September 27, 1986)** : Cliff Burton died on September 27, 1986, in Sweden during Metallica's tour for "Master of Puppets." After drawing the Ace of Spades to choose his bunk on the bus, he slept in Kirk Hammett's usual spot. The bus hit black ice around 7 a.m., causing the driver to lose control, resulting in the bus leaving the road and rolling onto its side.
