**Popcorn (1972)** : This composition, originally created to demonstrate the technical capabilities of the Moog synthesizer, was famously covered by Moog player Stan Free and his group Hot Butter in 1972. Their instrumental version achieved widespread international success, reaching number one across Europe and number nine in America.
