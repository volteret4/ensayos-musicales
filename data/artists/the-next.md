## songs
**Too Close (1998)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1998, a "sleeper hit" in the spring. The R&B trio from Minneapolis crafted its groove by slowing down and replaying Kurtis Blow's 1980 hit "Christmas Rappin'," which imparted an "old school feel" to the track. The song, an ode to men grinding too close on the dance floor, spent five weeks at number one and remained on the Hot 100 for over a year, enabling it to defeat Brandy and Monica's 13-week topper "The Boy Is Mine" for the year-end title.
