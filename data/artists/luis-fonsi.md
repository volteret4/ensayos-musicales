## songs
**Despacito (2017)** : This song, featuring Daddy Yankee, was the winner of the 2017 Billboard Song of the Summer and is recognized as the "biggest Latin pop crossover hit of all time." Its music video, filled with "Puerto Rican beaches, street parties and sweaty nightclubs," has been viewed 8 billion times, making it one of the most-watched YouTube videos in history (second only to "Baby Shark"). Although originally released and topping the Latin songs chart in winter, a Justin Bieber remix propelled it to number one on the Hot 100 just after Memorial Day, fulfilling its "destiny as a hot weather classic." The Borequena jam's title, which "implores you to move slowly," is seen as "always good summer advice." "What's crazy about Desbasito by Luis Fonsey and Daddy Yankee is it was released and first topped the Latin songs chart in the winter. **Despacito (2017)** : While on tour in Bogota in March 2017, Justin Bieber heard "Despacito" and, despite not speaking Spanish, contacted Fonsi's management to jump on a remix. Fonsi agreed, recalling the success of "Macarena" with a partial English remix. Bieber harmonized mostly in phonetic Spanish, with only a snippet of English at the beginning, but this was enough for the song to "positively explode in the Anglo-Pop world." The remix shot from number 48 to number 9 on the Hot 100 the week it dropped, and three weeks later, it was number 1. "Despacito" spent a staggering 16 weeks at number one, tying the all-time record set by Mariah Carey and Boyz II Men, becoming the first primarily Spanish Hot 100 chart-topper since "Macarena" 21 years prior. ## curiosities
**Self-Awareness Post-Success** : During the immense chart rampage of "Despacito" in 2017, Luis Fonsi was noted for his "wonderfully self-deprecating and self-aware" demeanor in interviews, understanding the unique moment he was experiencing without letting it get ahead of him. He has also expressed no intention of trying to replicate the success of "Despacito," preferring to stick to writing pop ballads on guitars, which he identifies as his core strength. "Every time I saw interviews with Fonsey, he was wonderfully self-deprecating and self-aware and realized he had a tiger by the tail and he knew it was a moment and he was not letting it get ahead of him." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Hits Don’t Lie | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
