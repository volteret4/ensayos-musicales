## curiosities
**Transition from Hokum Blues to Gospel** : Initially known as Georgia Tom, he recorded Hokum songs with Tampa Red's Hocum Drug Band in 1928, but later, as Thomas Dorsey, he became renowned for writing much of the core gospel music repertoire after experiencing a religious conversion while on stage. "Georgia Tom, who as Thomas Dorsey would later be best known as the writer of Much of the Core repertoire of gospel music." ← Episode 54： Keep A Knockin | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oA4Y_K9E5Y4
