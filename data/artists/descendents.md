## albums
**Milo Goes to College (1982)** : This 1982 record packed 15 aggressive songs into just 22 minutes. It was notable for its early use of melody and harmonies, which at the time was considered hugely radical and unconventional for American punk music. Their distinctiveness stemmed from incorporating more melody into their music, a strategy that helped them stand out from the crowd.
