**Heaven Is a Place on Earth (1987)** : This lead-off single from Carlisle's second solo album, "Heaven on Earth," reached number 1 on the charts almost exactly one year after Bon Jovi's "You Give Love a Bad Name." Structurally, it was considered a "carbon copy" of Bon Jovi's hit, demonstrating the pervasive influence of the pop metal formula in the late 80s, despite veteran songwriter Rick Knowles not pilfering the melody.
