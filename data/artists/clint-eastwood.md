**Directorial Debut "Play Misty for Me" (1971)** : Clint Eastwood, coming off his 1971 film "Dirty Harry," launched his directing career with "Play Misty for Me." He personally selected Roberta Flack's "The First Time Ever I Saw Your Face" to soundtrack a romantic montage in the film, paying $2,000 for the rights, which proved crucial to Flack's breakthrough.
