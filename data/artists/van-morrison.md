## songs
**Brown Eyed Girl (1967)** : This song was a number 10 hit in 1967 and is currently Van Morrison's most played hit. It garnered 37,000 radio spins in a year and a staggering 126 million streams, far surpassing both "Domino" and "Moon Dance" in modern popularity. "Brown Eye Girl. **Domino (1970)** : This song was Van Morrison's highest charting US hit of the 1970s, peaking at number 9 in early 1971. It still receives some play, with 2,600 radio spins in the last year and 11 million streams on streaming services. **Moon Dance (1970)** : This track, released in 1970, was only issued as a single in 1977 and subsequently peaked at number 92 on the Hot 100. Despite its low chart position, it has accumulated 3,800 radio spins in the last year and 27 million streams, significantly outperforming "Domino" in modern consumption.
