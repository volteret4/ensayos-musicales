## songs
**A Taste of Honey (1965)** : Herb Alpert's "Brassy ring-a-ding-dinging cover of the pop standard" became a number seven hit in 1965 and subsequently won the 1966 Record of the Year prize at the Grammys. His mid-60s rise and this win gave the Grammys "another way to seem with it." **This Guy's in Love With You (1968)** : This song was the winner of the 1968 Billboard Song of the Summer. It is described as a "pleasant, but placid" ballad. ## curiosities
**Connection to "Never Say Never Again" Theme (1983)** : Herb Alpert, co-founder of A&M Records, is connected to the "Never Say Never Again" theme through his marriage to Lani Hall, who sang the song. This familial tie links a major record label figure to one of the less-remembered Bond themes. "She was married to her Belpert, the A and A and M records." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K0pgJ7XAlpo ← herb-alpert
**Encounter with Gino Vannelli** : Herb Alpert, at A&M Records, was directly approached by Gino Vannelli, who audaciously bypassed security to plead for a chance in music. Alpert responded by giving Vannelli the opportunity he sought. "Herb Alpert turned around and said, hey, okay, what is it? And he said, just give me a chance. And he did.
