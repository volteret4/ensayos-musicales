# artist - Judy Collins

## songs
**Both Sides Now (1968)** : Collins' "achingly beautiful cover" of Joni Mitchell's song leapt into the Top 20 on the Hot 100 the week before Thanksgiving Day 1968, on its way to a Top 10 peak just before Christmas. It was famously used in the "Mad Men" series finale as "the dialogue in Don Draper's head expressed in song."
