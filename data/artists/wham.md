**Careless Whisper (1984)** : This was Wham!'s second US number one hit, featuring a notable saxophone line. The song is representative of the "Sophisti-pop" sound that emerged in the mid-80s, blending soul, cocktail jazz, and lavish production. "Think of the saxophone line on WAMS 2nd US number one, Carolus Whisper." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The British Are Charting Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f7a154465cd600c7ae7
**Careless Whisper (1985)** : In America, this song was credited to Wham! featuring George Michael, though it was a George Michael solo single internationally. It features an iconic sax riff, indelible melody, and soulful vocal from George Michael, and crossed over to Billboard's R&B chart where it cracked the top 10. The song defeated Madonna's "Like a Virgin," Foreigner's "I Want to Know What Love Is," and another Wham! hit, "Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go," for the 1985 title. "Credit to WAM in America, but really a George Michael solo single around the world, careless whisper has only grown in stature since George Michael's untimely passing in 2016." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hits of the Year Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce81
**Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go (1984)** : This song was another hit by Wham! that was defeated by their own "Careless Whisper" for the 1985 year-end title. **Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go (1984)** : In 1984, determined to break into the US market, Wham! leader George Michael employed the "old British invasion trick of selling America back to the Americans." This song was a "shameless send up of 50s sock hop and 60s Motown" and reached number one on the Hot 100 by November 1984. "Wake me up before you go go was a shameless send up of 50s sock hop and 60s Motown. **Early Alternative Classification** : Wham! was initially classified as an alternative band, even though their new wave style had significantly reduced punk influences. **George Michael's Former Band** : Wham! is mentioned as the group George Michael departed from before releasing his solo debut album, "Faith," which won Album of the Year in 1989. "Or George Michaels solo debut away from Wham, The Blockbuster Faith winner for 1989." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > And the Grammy Goes to… Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef0154465cd600c4e3a
**Global Success (1980s)** : Wham! was a pop duo that achieved global success, topping charts on both sides of the Atlantic and touring China. They were known for fusing British new wave with blue-eyed soul. "WAM had conquered the world, topping the charts on both sides of the Atlantic, touring China, and fusing British new wave with blue-eyed soul on hits like Wake Me Up before you go-go, careless whisper, and I'm your man." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say a Little Prayer Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eec154465cd600c4d67
**Breakup and Farewell Concert (1986)** : The duo officially broke up in the summer of 1986 after a farewell concert at Wembley Stadium, leading George Michael to launch his solo career. "In the summer of 1986, WAM officially broke up following a farewell Wembley Stadium concert, and George Michael began work on his solo debut." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say a Little Prayer Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eec154465cd600c4d67
**Selling America Back to Americans** : In 1984, Wham! leader George Michael consciously used the tactic of "selling America back to the Americans" to achieve US chart success. Their song "Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go" was a "shameless send up of 50s sock hop and 60s Motown."
