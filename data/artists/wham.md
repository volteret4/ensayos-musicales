**Last Christmas (1984)** : Recorded in 1984 by George Michael and Andrew Ridgley, this single was called a "high watermark of mid-80s British synth pop song craft" by Laura Snaps of the Guardian. Despite its current popularity, it sounded nothing like Wham!'s earlier hits and its original chart performance was unusual: it didn't chart in the US in 1984 (issued only as a B-side), and in the UK, as a double A-side with "Everything She Wants," it peaked at number two behind Band-Aid's "Do They Know It's Christmas?".
