## songs
**Tik Tok (2009)** : This smash hit topped the Hot 100 in the closing days of 2009 and went on to become the top hit of 2010. Written and produced by Lucas Gottwald (Dr. Luke), the song's lyrics quite literally centered on getting blackout drunk. **TiK ToK (2009)** : Kesha's debut single "TiK ToK" surged to number one post-Christmas 2009, selling 610,000 downloads in a single week. "Post Christmas 2009, new pop star and kinder rapper Kesha saw her debut single TikTok surge to number 1 after selling 610,000 downloads in a single week." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Smells Like Christmas Spirit, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**TiK ToK (2010)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 2010, spending nine weeks at number one at the start of the year. Kesha, described as electronic dance pop's "Ultimate Party Girl," opened her career with this bang. The song, which has "no relation to the video service that now dominates social media," secures the year-end title over hits from Lady Antebellum, Train, Katy Perry, and Usher. **Till the World Ends (2011)** : Kesha contributed new vocals to a remix of Britney Spears' hit "Till the World Ends" in 2011. This remix helped revive the flagging single, propelling it to a new peak of number three on the charts.
