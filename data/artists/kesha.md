## songs
**TiK ToK (2010)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 2010, spending nine weeks at number one at the start of the year. Kesha, described as electronic dance pop's "Ultimate Party Girl," opened her career with this bang. The song, which has "no relation to the video service that now dominates social media," secures the year-end title over hits from Lady Antebellum, Train, Katy Perry, and Usher.
