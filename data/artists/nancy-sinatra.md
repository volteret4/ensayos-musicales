**These Boots Are Made for Walkin' (1966)** : Nancy Sinatra's 1966 hit was featured in one of the most famous Scopatone films. Scopatones were video jukeboxes, weighing 1,500 pounds and capable of playing three dozen 16-millimeter films, most of which were in color and shot exclusively for the machine. **These Boots Are Made for Walkin' (1966)** : This song was part of the loud music bombardment directed at the Branch Davidian cult compound in Waco, Texas, in 1993. **These Boots Are Made for Walkin' (1966)** : This "immortal kiss-off record" hit number one on the Hot 100 for the first time for Nancy Sinatra when she was 25 years old. It was a "killer team up" with songwriter-producer Lee Hazlewood and remains a memorable 60s hit. **You Only Live Twice (1967)** : Nancy Sinatra, daughter of legendary singer Frank Sinatra, sang the title theme for the 1967 James Bond film "You Only Live Twice." While a different version of the song became a hit single, her rendition was the one featured in the movie and heard by cinema audiences.
