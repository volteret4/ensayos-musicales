## curiosities
**Cautious Approach to Coming Out** : Neil Tennant, lead singer of Pet Shop Boys, was notably "more cautious" about his sexuality than some contemporaries. He remained quiet about his personal life for many years, despite gay themes appearing in the band's music from the start, such as in "It's a Sin" and "Rent."
