**The Past Is Still Alive (2024)** : This album, by the New Orleans band, marked a breakthrough for them after over 15 years, bringing Alynda Segarra's long-standing work into sharp focus. It functions as an "audio memoir," narrating Segarra's youth as a "kid at risk" who traveled across America, and through these memories, it reflects on America's past, portraying it as both beautiful and dangerously threatening. The record is noted for its "handmade and intimate quality," easing off the gas in the right places, making it endlessly listenable and moving, without the heavy-handedness of earlier works. ## curiosities
**Band Longevity and Evolution** : Hurray for the Riff Raff has been active for over 15 years, with *The Past Is Still Alive* marking a significant evolution in their sound and songwriting from earlier, blues-influenced, repetitive songs that reflected Alynda Segarra's past as a "train hopper." **Alynda Segarra's Youth and Songwriting** : Alynda Segarra's early life as a "hobo" and "train hopper" who rode the rails significantly influenced the repetitive, "train wheels" sound of her early blues-influenced songs. On *The Past Is Still Alive*, she evolved to tell stories with more structure, offering an "intersectional queer vision of Bohemian life." "Alinda was someone who rode the rails they were kind of a hobo early on they were a train hopper and that was a sound she'd built but on these songs she's figured out how to do it. So I'm going to start with a structure and tell stories so exquisitely and offering us this intersectional queer vision of Bohemian life." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Slate Music Club 2024 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
