## albums
**Jesus Christ Superstar (1970)** : This work by Andrew Lloyd Webber was referenced by Green Day as an influence while they were structuring and making sense of their material for *American Idiot*. "An Andrew Lloyd Webber's Jesus Christ superstar to see if there's anything there that can help us make sense of all this." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1KyNY3zv6uc ← andrew-lloyd-webber
**Jesus Christ Superstar (1970)** : This concept album for the rock musical gained word-of-mouth popularity in late 1970, eventually topping the album chart in early 1971. The album's success provided the "leveling up" necessary for the title track single, performed by Murray Head, to finally become a top 40 hit after multiple failed attempts. "Then, later in 1970, the musical's concept album became a word of mouth hit and the LP finally topped the album chart in early 71." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Second-Chance Hits Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Co-Creator of Jesus Christ Superstar** : Andrew Lloyd Webber co-created the musical *Jesus Christ Superstar* with Tim Rice. They initially released it as an album in 1970 because they could not secure funding for a stage production at the time. "Andrew Lloyd Webber and Tim Rice could not get the money for a stage production." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 32 - Take Me to Church - Religion in 70s Popular Music | https://seventies.libsyn.com/ep-32-take-me-to-church-religion-in-70s-popular-music ← andrew-lloyd-webber
**Songwriting Partnership with Tim Rice** : Andrew Lloyd Webber was the former songwriting partner of Tim Rice, the lyricist who later collaborated with Elton John on "The Lion King" soundtrack. "Tim Rice, former songwriting partner of Andrew Lloyd Webber, and the lyricist behind a string of smash theatrical musicals..." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The Imperial Elton and George Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**"Evita" Co-Creator** : Andrew Lloyd Webber was the co-creator of the original theatrical musical "Evita" and reunited with Tim Rice to pen the new song "You Must Love Me" expressly for the 1996 film adaptation starring Madonna. "The original theatrical musical was of course the handiwork of UK superstar producer playwrights Andrew Lloyd Weber and Tim Rice." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The Veronica Electronica Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**UK Superstar Producer Playwright** : Andrew Lloyd Webber was one half of the UK superstar producer playwright duo responsible for the original theatrical musical *Evita*. He reunited with Tim Rice to pen "You Must Love Me" expressly for the movie and for Madonna, winning an Oscar for Best Original Song. "The original theatrical musical was, of course, the handiwork of UK superstar producer playwrights Andrew Lloyd Webber and Tim Rice." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Madonna - The Veronica Electronica Edition (Encore) | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Collaboration with Tim Rice** : Andrew Lloyd Webber, a British composer and librettist, collaborated with Tim Rice on musicals such as "Jesus Christ Superstar" and "Evita," initially releasing them as concept albums when theatrical productions were difficult to secure. "British composers and librettists Andrew Lloyd Webber and Tim Rice, both in their early 20s, couldn't get it on stage." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Lullaby of Broadway Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Panned Jesus Christ Superstar Broadway Version** : Andrew Lloyd Webber himself panned the 1971 Broadway version of "Jesus Christ Superstar," which critics also criticized and which won no Tony Awards, indicating its theatrical debut did not live up to the success of its concept album. "Critics and Andrew Lloyd Webber himself panned the 1971 Broadway version, and it won No Tony Awards." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Lullaby of Broadway Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Creator of Mega Musicals** : Andrew Lloyd Webber is recognized as the creator of transatlantic mega-musicals like "Cats" and "The Phantom of the Opera," which generated songs that became part of the showtunes canon and captivated Broadway audiences. "Spectacles like cats, lemisarab and the phantom of the opera generated songs that entered the showtunes canon and made Broadway audiences swoon." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Lullaby of Broadway Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
