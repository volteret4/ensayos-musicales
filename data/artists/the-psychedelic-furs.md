## songs
**The Ghost in You (1984)** : This song, released in 1984, was found to have melodic similarities to The Strokes' song "Eternal Sunday" from their 2020 album "The New Abnormal." As a result, Richard Butler and Tom Butler of The Psychedelic Furs received songwriting credits on "Eternal Sunday." "There's another song on here called Eternal Sunday, which sounded a little too much like The Ghost In You from The Psychedelic Furs, which is why Richard and Tom Butler of the Furs get a songwriting credit on that." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jEfofyobGiI ← the-psychedelic-furs

## curiosities
**12-inch Remix Business** : The Psychedelic Furs also got into the 12-inch remix business, aligning with a broader trend in the 1980s driven by the success of tracks like New Order's "Blue Monday." This format was crucial for bridging indie music with dance culture. "And so was Depeche Mode and The Cure and The Human League and Simple Minds and The Cult and Killing Joke and Erasure and Psychedelic Furs and Madness. They all got into the 12-inch business." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OiqdZfxdBP0 ← psychedelic-furs
**Influence on "On a Friday" (1986)** : Psychedelic Furs was noted as one of the 80s influences audible on "On a Friday's" 1986 self-issued five-track cassette. "You too, water boys, psychedelic furs, the Smiths." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t-G22K0vLnM ← psychedelic-furs ← psychedelic-furs
**Unrealized Album Project** : David Botrell and Bob Lannwaw were slated to work on the next Psychedelic Furs record. However, the project did not proceed because the band had no songs written for rehearsal, and Lannwaw, having just spent a year and a half with an artist who wrote as they went, declined to continue. "Dan was slated to do the next psychedelic furs record and I went down with him to rehearse at John Giblin's rehearsal studio in Surrey" ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vWvYe1a-2Aw ← psychedelic-furs ← psychedelic-furs
**Collaboration with Steve Lillywhite** : The Psychedelic Furs were among the many notable bands and artists with whom British producer Steve Lillywhite worked on albums. "Steve Lilley White, the British producer who worked on albums from You Too, The Psychedelic Furs, Peter Gabriel Simple Minds, Talking Heads, Morrissey, Rolling Stones, Dave Matthews, BDI, The Killer, Suzing the Banshees, and dozens more." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hjProJd_JrM ← the-psychedelic-furs
**John Hughes Film Exposure (1980s)** : The Psychedelic Furs were among the bands whose music was heavily mined for the soundtracks of John Hughes' 1980s films. These carefully curated soundtracks played a significant role in exposing numerous North American audiences to various alternative rock acts, particularly British performers. "So many people learned of bands like The Specials and Spandovalet and Billy Idle and General Public and Wang Chung and Yellow and New Order and the Psychedelic Furs and the Smiths and Echo and the Bunyman, Susanne Vagan in excess the Jesus and Mary chain, Flesh for Luluo and so many others from those soundtrack albums." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zIkjd1OjCXM ← the-psychedelic-furs
