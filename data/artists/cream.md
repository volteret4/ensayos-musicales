**Sunshine of Your Love (1968)** : This hit single from "Disraeli Gears" achieved success by entering the Top 40 in early 1968, and later re-charting that summer to reach number five in August 1968. The song is notable for Jack Bruce's high tenor vocals, Eric Clapton's distinctive guitar riffs, and Ginger Baker's double bass drum work, offering "another glimpse of what is to come for heavy metal." Its structure, built around a repeated main riff, foreshadowed a common technique in heavy metal. Jack Bruce's lead vocal style on this track is also suggested as an influence on Robert Plant.
