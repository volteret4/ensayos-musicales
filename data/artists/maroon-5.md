## curiosities
**Pop Dominance in 2010s** : Maroon 5 contributed to the resurgence of pop music that swamped rock by 2011 or 2012, highlighting the genre's increasing popularity alongside hip hop and EDM during the 2010s. "And you can make the argument that it certainly did swamp by not only a rise in pop music, you know, Maroon 5, Justin Bieber, Rihanna, Pink Kitty Perry, Carly Recepcion, Adele, but also by the increasing strength of hip hop and the big beats and bass lines of EDM." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Y6nHw5xx88 ← maroon-5
**Produced by Mutt Lange** : Maroon 5 is among the artists who have had their music produced by Mutt Lange, a producer recognized for his significant influence on modern pop sound. "Their producer, Mut Lang, is at least as influential on the sound of modern pop as Max Martin is, having produced everyone from Shania Twain to Maroon 5." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The You Give Rock a Bad Name Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

**Example of Modern Electro-Pop** : Maroon 5's chart-topping hits were cited as examples of the modern electro-pop sound prevalent in the early 2010s, which was largely electronic rather than acoustic. Their success contributed to the production style Taylor Swift's evolving music began to demand, with many of these hits produced or co-written by Max Martin. **Adam Levine's Branding Strategy** : Despite Adam Levine's individual notoriety, he largely records under the Maroon 5 brand, even for songs that prominently feature him or incorporate elements like rappers and fewer guitars, prioritizing the established band identity for radio play.
