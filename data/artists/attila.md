**Attila (1970)** : This was the one LP released by the proto-metal band Attila, consisting of Billy Joel on keyboards and his former Hassles bandmate John Small on drums. The album cover famously featured the duo posed in a meat locker wearing medieval armor, with hair comparable to Led Zeppelin's Robert Plant. The album was not commercially successful, and the band broke up within a year.
