## albums
**Philosophy of the World (1969)** : Released in 1969, this album by The Shaggs is considered one of the great cult records of the last 50 years. It is described as "weird" and "hard to listen to," often guaranteed to clear a party. Despite its unusual qualities, Kurt Cobain ranked it as number five on his top 50 albums of all time. "The result was a record called Philosophy of the World... That is one of the great cult records from the last 50 years. It is weird, it is hard to listen to, and guaranteed to chase everybody out of a party at the end of the night." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gxbuajGKbko ← the-shaggs
**Philosophy of the World (1969)** : This album was recorded in less than a day, featuring 12 songs by the trio of sisters from New Hampshire. Despite being considered "not that good" by conventional standards, it earned legendary status and was praised for the sincerity and authenticity of its heartfelt performances. Its initial reception for being "so bad" paradoxically made it legendary, leading to its re-evaluation and widespread availability. ## curiosities
**Cult Status and Musician Endorsements** : Despite or because of their unconventional sound, The Shaggs are frequently name-checked by famous musicians who cite their recordings as favorite garage rock albums. They are paradoxically also described as "quite possibly the worst group to ever walk into a recording studio." "The Shags are often named checked by famous musicians, asked to name their favorite garage rock recordings of all time. They are also paradoxically quite possibly the worst group to ever walk into a recording studio." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gxbuajGKbko ← the-shaggs
**Father's Managerial Vision** : Their father, Austin Wiggins, served as their manager and producer. Despite being financially poor, he purchased instruments for his daughters and paid for their lessons. He drove them to Massachusetts to record, believing he needed to capture their sound "while they were still at their peak," despite what others might perceive as their lack of musical talent. "They were managed and produced by their father Austin Wiggins, and I'm putting air quotes around managed and produced. Despite being dirt poor, he bought his children instruments and paid for lessons. And then he drove everybody down to Massachusetts in order to get his girls on tape while they were still at their peak." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gxbuajGKbko ← the-shaggs
**Kurt Cobain's High Regard** : Despite the album's peculiar nature, Kurt Cobain notably ranked *Philosophy of the World* as number five on his personal list of the top 50 albums of all time. "Kurt Cobain ranked this album as number five on his top 50 albums of all time." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gxbuajGKbko ← the-shaggs
