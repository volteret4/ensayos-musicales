## songs
**Barracuda (1977)** : Ann Wilson, the lead singer of Heart, wrote this hit song (which reached number 11 on the Hot 100 in 1977) as a direct diss against the band's former label, Mushroom Records. The label had scandalously tried to market Ann and her sister Nancy Wilson as an "incestuous lesbian couple." ## curiosities
**View on 70s Rock Subgenres** : Anne Wilson described the proliferation of subgenres in 1970s rock as significant "musical inbreeding" due to the constant shifting of personnel and mutual influences within bands. She characterized this fragmentation as a "big jam," reflecting a dynamic and interconnected musical landscape.
