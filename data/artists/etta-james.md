**At Last (1961)** : Originally written in 1941 and first recorded by Glenn Miller and his orchestra, Etta James reinvented this song two decades later on her 1961 album, "elongating and eroticizing" it into a "satisfied sigh." It reached number two on Billboard's R&B chart but only number 47 on the Hot 100, possibly due to being "too earthy" for mainstream white audiences in the 60s. It has since become synonymous with Etta James and grown in popularity since her death a decade ago. In the last year, it received 1,200 radio spins and a remarkable 42 million streams, with lifetime Spotify streams of 340 million, more than double any other Etta James hit. "but only number 47 on the hot 100." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > At Last, My Legacy Has Come Along Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Johnny Otis's Proud Discovery** : Etta James was a musical artist whom Johnny Otis was most proud of discovering. Coincidentally, Otis died three days before James, leading her to overshadow him in obituaries, an outcome he would have desired. "He died three days before the discovery of whom he was most proud, ethy James, and she overshadowed him in the obituaries, as he would have wanted." ← REUPLOAD Episode 71： ＂Willie and the Hand Jive＂ by Johnny Otis | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ez6BCUlI6Rg
