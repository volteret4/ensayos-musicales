**Home Sweet Home (1985)** : Arguably the ultimate power ballad of the 1980s, and the biggest video on MTV for much of 1985 and 1986, consistently generating the most requests on "Dial MTV," the channel's daily call-in request show. Despite its immense video popularity, it peaked at a "mystifying" number 89 on the Hot 100 and was off the chart in a month and a half. Even on album rock radio, it peaked at a modest number 38, perceived as "too soft for rock fans and too day-class A for pop fans." It was famously featured in the Gen X nostalgia movie "Hot Tub Time Machine." "Arguably the ultimate power ballad of the 1980s, also famous that a quarter century later, the Gen X nostalgia movie Hot Tub Time Machine would close with a hilarious homage to it." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The You Give Rock a Bad Name Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Smoking in the Boys Room (1985)** : An "undeniable, pop-friendly cover" of Brownsville Station's 1974 number three hit, featured on Motley Crüe's "Theatre of Pain" album. Despite its popularity, it could not replicate the original's top five or top ten peak, maxing out at number 16 on the Hot 100 in September 1985.
