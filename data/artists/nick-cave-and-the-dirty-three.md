# artist - Nick Cave and the Dirty Three

## albums
**Songs in the Key of X (1996)** : This 1996 soundtrack album, based on "The X Files" program, includes a hidden track in its pre-gap area. Hints in the liner notes encouraged listeners to explore the disc. The track, "Time, Jism, Transyntum, Ednund, Rurentum," is found by rewinding 9 minutes from Track 1 and is followed by a cover of "The X Files" theme. "In 1996, there were songs in the key of X.
