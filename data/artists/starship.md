**We Built This City (1985)** : "We Built This City" is a 1985 smash hit by Starship, whose original version is "already cited in many music polls as one of the worst hits of all time." This song was famously covered by Mark Hoyle (Ladbaby) in 2018 as "We Built This City on Sausage Rolls" for a novelty Christmas number one campaign. "Yes, this is a cover of We Built This City, the 1985 Starship Smash, with new lyrics and a new title." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Christmas is All Around Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**We Built This City (1985)** : This smash hit is explicitly stated to bear only a "passing resemblance to Bruce," differentiating it from the more direct Springsteen imitations and "schlockiest Springsteenia" of the era, such as the KBC Band's output. ## curiosities
**Rebooted 60s Band** : Starship is described as a "rebooted 60s band" for whom superstar American songwriter Diane Warren had scored number one hits in the late 1980s. This highlights Warren's ability to contribute to the success of established acts. "Warren was herself on a hot streak, having scored number one's just in the last two years for rebooted 60s band Starship." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
