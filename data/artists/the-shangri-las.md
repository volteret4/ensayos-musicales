**Teen Drama Specialization** : The Shangri-La's' unique appeal lay in their ability to amplify teen girl drama, often incorporating spoken-word interludes into their songs. "Their special sauce was turning on the teen girl drama, complete with spoken word interludes." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be My Baby-Baby-Baby Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Origin** : Described as a quartet of tough-talking, gum-cracking, street-gossiping girls from Queens, New York, led by the "tough, but yearning" Mary Weiss. "Arguably, the last major Girl Group to break before the mid-60s were New York's Shangri-La's, a quartet of tough-talking, gum-cracking, street gossiping Queens girls led by the tough, but yearning Mary Weiss." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be My Baby-Baby-Baby Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
