**Here, My Dear (1978)** : This 1978 double LP was recorded by Marvin Gaye to fulfill a divorce settlement with his ex-wife, Anna Gordy, sister of Motown founder Berry Gordy. As part of a nasty two-year court battle, Gaye agreed to pay Anna the royalties from his next album. The album's spiteful title reflects its content, with all tracks focusing on the failure of the marriage from Marvin's perspective. **Let's Get It On (1973)** : Released in 1973, this album featured liner notes written by Marvin Gaye himself, which included a quote from T.S. Eliot: "birth and copulation and death." While undoubtedly about sex, the album is also described as being as "socially and politically aware" as his earlier "What's Going On." Sociology professor Michael Eric Dyson praised it as a brave album for a black man to create such erotic content, directly countering the black exploitation films of the era by celebrating black sexuality, eroticism, and love. It reflects a part of the sexual revolution that viewed sexual freedom as beautiful, natural, and potentially a "gift from God."
