## curiosities
**Bobby Graham's Uncredited Drumming** : Bobby Graham, a session drummer for The Kinks, is "now best known" for performing all the drumming that was credited to Dave Clark on records by The Dave Clark Five, including hits like "Bits and Pieces." "Graham is now best known for having done all the drumming credited to Dave Clark on records by the Dave Clark 5, such as bits and pieces." ← Episode 119： ＂You Really Got Me＂ by the Kinks | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_XNJ6Qwmh_c
**First Kinks Tour Companions** : The Kinks embarked on their "first big tour" sharing the bottom of the bill with The Dave Clark Five and The Hollies, who were positioned higher up. This tour marked Mick Avory's first live performances with The Kinks.
