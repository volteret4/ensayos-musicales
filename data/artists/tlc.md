## albums
**CrazySexyCool (1994)** : This sophomore album, released in late 1994, became the top-selling girl group album of all time, certified diamond for 10 million in sales. Its title was a "cheeky portmanteau of adjectives describing the three TLC ladies," and its singles dominated radio in 1994 and 1995. "Crazy, sexy, cool. It became their fastest seller to date, debuting at number one and generating two Hot 100 number one songs, "No Scrubs" and "Unpretty."
