**Popularizing Folk as Pop Music (1958)** : This San Francisco threesome of "clean cut 20-somethings" played an apolitical, "Erzatz collegiate folk" style, scoring a string of number one albums and hit singles starting in 1958. Despite being regarded as "sellouts" by hardcore folkies, they played an instrumental role in popularizing folk as pop music, with Dylan later claiming to be a fan.
