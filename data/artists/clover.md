# artist - Clover

## curiosities
**Backed Elvis Costello** : Members of this California band provided the backing for Elvis Costello on his 1977 debut album, *My Aim Is True*, as he had not yet formed his own backing band. "He was backed by members of the California band Clover, who would regroup in the 80s as the chart topping band Huey Lewis and The Newt." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Angry Young Men Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f780c30a1408dc2baf3
**Evolution into Huey Lewis and the News** : After recording with Elvis Costello, Clover returned to the United States and subsequently changed their name, becoming the well-known band Huey Lewis and the News. This means Costello's first backing band was essentially Huey Lewis and the News, without Huey himself on vocals for those sessions. "Once the recording sessions wrapped up, Clover went back to the States and changed their name. You may have heard of them. Huey Lewis in the news." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vKRKu7O_5HA ← clover ← clover
**Post-Breakup Member Destinies** : After Clover broke up in 1978, its members pursued various paths. One individual joined The Doobie Brothers before eventually quitting music to become a chiropractor. Another member achieved recognition by writing the song "867-5309/Jenny" for Tommy Tutone. Additionally, two members, along with Clover's singer (who had merely hung around during the Elvis Costello sessions), went on to form the successful band Huey Lewis and the News. "Clover broke up in 1978. One guy joined the Dubie Brothers before quitting music to become a chiropractor, and another guy wrote the song 867-5309-Geni for Tommy Tutone." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=itK5fEqnA5E ← clover ← clover
**Pseudonym for American Clover** : The Sham Rocks was a pseudonym adopted by the American band Clover when they were working in the UK. This was done to avoid legal trouble, as there was already an existing British band named Clover. Under this name, they famously backed Elvis Costello on his debut album, My Aim Is True, although they remained uncredited. "This is because there was already a British band called Clover, so the American Clover tried to use the name, the Sham Rocks, to avoid getting in trouble." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=itK5fEqnA5E ← the-sham-rocks ← the-sham-rocks
**Pseudonym for American Clover** : The Sham Rocks was the name under which the American band Clover performed as Elvis Costello's backing band for his debut album, "My Aim Is True," while they were living in London.
