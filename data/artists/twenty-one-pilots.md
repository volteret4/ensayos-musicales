## albums
**21 Pilots (2009)** : The band's first self-produced album, released on December 29, 2009, featured the lineup of Tyler Joseph, Nick Thomas, and Chris Salah. Despite being a low-key, home-recorded indie release, it sold approximately 115,000 copies. "The first 21 pilots album was a self-produced thing released on December 29, 2009." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sAxFvktx-A ← 21-pilots
**Blurryface (2015)** : This fourth record, released in 2015, marked the band's major breakthrough and began a more mystical and complicated phase for the group. It explored themes of doubt, insecurity, anxiety, and religion through the character Blurryface. The album sold close to 2 million copies and produced six singles. "They blew up with the blurry face album in 2015." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sAxFvktx-A ← 21-pilots
**Blurryface (2015)** : Highly collectible special vinyl editions of the *Blurryface* album, each uniquely produced with an inkblot design so that "no two are the same." These editions are highly sought after by fans and can trade for hundreds of dollars. "But fans really treasure the inkblot special vinyl editions." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sAxFvktx-A ← 21-pilots
**Blurryface (2015)** : An instrumental version of the *Blurryface* album that mysteriously appeared in the UK. It's very hard to find." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sAxFvktx-A ← 21-pilots
**Blurryface (2015)** : A very rare and distinctive limited edition cassette version of the *Blurryface* album, notable for its bright red color. "When the album was released, it came out in the usual ways, but there was also a limited edition cassette that was very, very red." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sAxFvktx-A ← 21-pilots
**Blurryface (2015)** : This album was identified as the biggest rock album of the 2010s, based on various metrics including sales and streams. It spent 234 weeks on the charts, held the number one position for nine weeks, and all its songs combined have been streamed 3.5 billion times. "The biggest rock album of the 2010s was Bluriface from 21 Pilots." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=72Lei7uug8c ← twenty-one-pilots
**Blurryface (2015)** : This album contributed significantly to Twenty One Pilots becoming one of the top streaming artists in 2016, the year streaming generated more revenue for the American recorded music business than all other forms of music distribution combined. "If we go back to that year, we'll see that one of the top streaming artists of 2016 was these guys, thanks to their blurry face album." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZzIqIydZTlA ← twenty-one-pilots
**No Fun Intended (2008)** : This was a solo record made by Tyler Joseph in his parents' basement when he was about 17 or 18, still in his senior year of high school. Nick Thomas, a future 21 Pilots bassist, contributed some guitars to the project. **Regional At Best (2011)** : Released on July 8, 2011, this album was largely Tyler Joseph's project with minimal guest appearances, coming out shortly after Nick Thomas left and Josh Dunn joined. Tyler referred to it as a " glorified mixtape," and while initially distributed to fans, copies were later discontinued, deleted, and wiped from existence due to a record deal. "This project was pretty much Tyler's baby with the exception of a couple of small guest appearances." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sAxFvktx-A ← 21-pilots
**Regional At Best & 21 Pilots (2011)** : A hard copy edition released through the Tinderbox label that featured both the *Regional At Best* album and the band's first self-titled album. This package was specifically created with the purpose of attracting the attention of record labels. "Plus, there was also a second hard copy edition through a label called Tinderbox. **Trench (2018)** : Released in 2018, this album expanded the band's intricate mythology, exploring themes of doubt, insecurity, anxiety, faith, and suicide. It details a fictional world built around the city of Dema and introduces characters like the chief bishop Niko and the rebel group, the Banditos. "The first single from Trench was jumpsuit." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sAxFvktx-A ← 21-pilots

## curiosities
**Band Hiatus (2017-2018)** : After completing two world tours in support of *Blurryface*, the band entered a period of hiatus starting July 6, 2017, which lasted almost a year. This break allowed Tyler Joseph and Josh Dunn to concentrate on creating new music without any interruptions or distractions. "After a couple of world tours in support of blurry face, the band went dark, on July 6, 2017, so Tyler and Josh could focus on music without any interruption or distraction." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sAxFvktx-A ← 21-pilots
**Band Name Origin ("All My Sons")** : The band's name is derived from Arthur Miller's 1947 play, "All My Sons," which is based on a true story. The play tells of an Ohio man who knowingly approved defective aircraft engines for use in World War II, leading to the deaths of 21 pilots. "This play is called All My Sons from 1947. And it's based on a true story about a man also from Ohio who conspired with the army to approve faulty and defective aircraft engines for use in combat in World War II. And as a result of this bad equipment, 21 pilots died." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sAxFvktx-A ← 21-pilots
**Band Name Spelling Choice** : Tyler Joseph intentionally omits the hyphen between "20" and "1" in the band's name, prioritizing its visual design and how it appears in text or can be adapted into a logo or other graphic element. "But for Tyler, it's all about design. All about how it looks in text and how it can be adapted into a logo or other graphic design." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sAxFvktx-A ← 21-pilots
