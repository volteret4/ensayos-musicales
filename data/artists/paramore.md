**Early Career and 360 Deal** : Paramore, originating from a tiny town in Tennessee, signed a 360 deal with Fueled by Ramen, which had a deal with Atlantic Records (part of Warner Music Group). This deal provided them with new gear, a new van, and financial support for singer Hayley Williams' mother to continue Hayley's schooling while the band toured as she was still in high school. "Paramore is from a tiny town in Tennessee.
