**Fancy (2014)** : This was Iggy Azalea's first Hot 100 hit, dominating the summer of 2014. Its success was partly due to an irresistible 80s-esque chorus hook, which was expertly deployed by English singer-songwriter Charlie XCX. "Iggy Ezelia...Her first ever hot 100 hit, Fancy, dominated the summer of 2014, thanks in part to an irresistible 80s-esque chorus hook, which was expertly deployed by English singer songwriter Charlotte Atchison, aka Charlie XCX." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Rolling in God’s Royal Uptown Road Edition. | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Fancy (2014)** : Featuring Charli XCX, this song by the Australian rapper had a long run at number one in 2014 and was predicted to, and did, take the Song of the Summer title. While successful, its cultural reception was mixed, with some critics complaining about it, and its aging is debated a decade later, particularly regarding the artist's persona.
