## curiosities
**Co-founder of Detroit Techno** : Kevin Saunderson (transcribed as Kevin Sanderson) is one of the celebrated "Holy Trinity" who pioneered techno in Detroit in the mid-1980s. "Aunque el tecno en la acción de Detroit ha mediado los 80 de la mano de Juan Atkins, Derrick Meill y Kevin Sanderson, la celebre santísima trinidad..." ← Cómo Jeff Mills transformó el techno para siempre | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=omE3zOiZAXs
**Creative Evolution of Techno** : Kevin Saunderson is identified as the great creative force who elevated techno to a second level. He took Juan Atkins's initial idea and developed it to a superior creative standard, playing a pivotal role in the evolution of early techno into its second generation.
