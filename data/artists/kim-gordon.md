## curiosities
**Dog Named Sid Barrett** : Kim Gordon was a devoted admirer of Pink Floyd's early musical output. Her admiration was such that she named her dog after Syd Barrett, one of the founding members of Pink Floyd. "Kim Gordon, was such a fan of Pink Floyd's early work that she named her dog Sid Barrett." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HoBx6s3JmVQ ← kim-gordon
**Early Life and Education** : Born in Rochester, New York, and raised in Los Angeles, Kim Gordon formed her first band during her time as a student at York University in Toronto. "She was born in Rochester, New York, raised in Los Angeles, but she formed her first band while she was a student at York University in Toronto." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eo32mAZd8DI ← kim-gordon
**Female Pioneer Inspiration** : Kim Gordon of Sonic Youth is cited as an inspiration for the Riot Grrrl movement, representing a legacy of female pioneers in punk and post-punk. "Taking inspiration from the female pioneers of punk and post-punk, it would be Patty Smith and Susie Sue and Kim Gordon of Sonic Youth, Chrissy Hine, Oni DeFranco, and so on." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zZM9ajwYr9E ← kim-gordon ← kim-gordon
**Influence and Mentorship** : Kim Gordon became a significant inspiration and influence for many young women in music, particularly within the indie scene. Her impact was such that a young Courtney Love from San Francisco wrote to her asking her to produce Hole's debut record. "So many young women were inspired and influenced by her, including a 20-something young woman from San Francisco who wrote Kim a letter saying, look, I love what you do. I have a new band. Will you produce our debut record for us?" ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eo32mAZd8DI ← kim-gordon
**Mother of Coco Haley Gordon Moore** : Kim Gordon is the mother of Coco Haley Gordon Moore. "Coco Haley Gordon Moore, son of Thurston Moore, and Kim Gordon of Sonic Youth." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U40hOYy_94E ← kim-gordon ← kim-gordon
**Guest Vocalist with Nirvana** : Kim Gordon of Sonic Youth was among the four women guest vocalists who joined Nirvana for their Rock and Roll Hall of Fame performance in 2014, and again at the 2025 Fire Aid Benefit. This collaboration was seen as honoring Kurt Cobain's feminist ideals. "For their Rock Hall performance, original members Chris Novicellich and Dave Grohl and later Nirvana member Pat Smir were joined on stage for their four song set by four woman guest vocalists, Joan Jett, Kim Gordon of Sonic Youth, St Vincent, and for the finale of the set, Lord, who sang the final Nirvana Modern Rock number one of Kurt Cobain's lifetime, All Apologies." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > All Apologies Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
