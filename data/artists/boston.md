## albums
**Boston (1976)** : Boston's debut album, released in 1976, included a very long essay on its back cover. This essay served as a de facto press release, detailing the band and the record's creation, designed to attract potential buyers browsing in record stores by providing compelling information about the new artist. **Boston (1976)** : This self-titled debut album, released in 1976, never surpassed number three on the LP chart but became the band's all-time blockbuster, certified by the RIAA for sales of 17 million copies. It featured the iconic hit "More Than a Feeling" and had an extraordinary chart run of 137 weeks on the Billboard album chart, surpassing the combined total of their other five studio LPs. **Don't Look Back (1978)** : Released in 1978, this album became the new number one album in America, notably "evicting" the popular *Grease* soundtrack from the top spot. Its title song reached number 8 on American Top 40, characterized by Boston's signature combination of power chords and Brad Delp's high-pitched vocals. Although certified septuple platinum for 7 million sales, it sold 10 million fewer copies than their debut LP. "The brand new No. 1 album in America this week is Don't Look Back.
