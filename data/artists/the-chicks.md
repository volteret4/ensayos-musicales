## curiosities
**The "Dixie Chick Theory" for Decline of Protest Songs** : In March 2003, at a concert in London, Natalie Maines, the lead singer, stated that the band was ashamed that President George Bush was from Texas, in response to the invasion of Iraq. Within a week, country radio stations banned their music, and nearly 20 years later, many people have not forgiven Maines for her comments, contributing to a theory about why protest songs have ceased to be hits. **Grammy Competition - 2000** : The Chicks, then known as The Dixie Chicks, were described as a "Diamond-selling country trio" and competed against Santana for Grammy awards in 2000. **Collaborated with Beyoncé at CMA Awards (2016)** : The country trio The Chicks joined Beyoncé for her performance of the "Texas celebrating country and western indebted" song "Daddy Lessons" at the 2016 CMA Awards. This performance was a notable moment in the conversation surrounding the history of the black cowboy and the cross-pollination of genres.
