**Formation and Early Management** : The group was formed in 1994 after London managers held auditions to find a girl group to compete with British boy bands, using Eternal's "Stay" as the audition song. After some member changes, Victoria Adams, Melanie Brown, Emma Bunton, Melanie Chisholm, and Geri Halliwell were chosen. Initially called "Touch," they briefly adopted "Spice" before settling on "Spice Girls" due to another rapper named Spice. "At first they were called Touch. Then, after working on a song called Sugar and Spice, they went with Spice as a moniker. Eventually, after learning that there already was a rapper called Spice, they would settle on the Spice Girls." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be My Baby-Baby-Baby Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eed8e6dd12efb02c340
**Ditching Original Management and Signing with Virgin** : By 1995, the group ditched their original management team, feeling their ideas weren't being heard. They retained their songwriters, found new producers, and secured Simon Fuller as their manager. They signed with Virgin Records, which notably agreed to let them present themselves as five equal personalities with no single lead singer. "By 1995, the future Spice Girls had ditched the management team who had auditioned them out of concern that they weren't listening to the ladies' ideas." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be My Baby-Baby-Baby Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eed8e6dd12efb02c340
