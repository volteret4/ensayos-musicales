## songs
**Cruise Remix (2013)** : In 2013, a decade after collaborating with country star Tim McGraw, rapper Nelly teamed up with Nashville duo Florida Georgia Line for a remix of their country smash "Cruise." Florida Georgia Line announced this hip-hop remix through their social media channels, leading to its explosion on the Hot 100, where it reached the top five on the pop chart months after having already topped the Country Chart. **Hot in Here (2002)** : This song was the winner of the 2002 Billboard Song of the Summer. Produced by The Neptunes (Pharrell Williams and Chad Hugo), it was created by taking an old Chuck Brown go-go record and turning up the heat "high enough to make you 'take off all your clothes'." The St. Louis rapper, Nelly, spelled "here" with two R's in a "Dirty South Style," and the song was number one for 7 weeks from June to September. ## curiosities
**Blanked at Major Categories (General)** : Nelly was listed among prominent rap/hip-hop artists who have been "blanked" in major general field Grammy categories. "In addition to B, J, Kanye and Kendrick, major category nominees that have been blanked include MC Hammer, Coolio, TLC, Nelly, Eminem, Ludicrous, Lil Wayne and Missy Elliott." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > And the Grammy Goes to… Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eef154465cd600c4ddc
**Precedent for Self-Replacement at Number One** : Nelly was one of the four acts (Elvis Presley, The Beatles, and Boys to Men) that Outcast followed in replacing themselves at number one on the Hot 100. Including acts who succeeded themselves in featured roles, Nelly was among the seven acts to achieve this feat.
