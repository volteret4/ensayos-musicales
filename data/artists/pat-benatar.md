**Crimes of Passion (1980)** : This 1980 follow-up LP also took months to peak, but by the end of 1980, Benatar had scored her first ever top 10 hit with the "classic" "Hit Me with Your Best Shot." The album was "deep with hits" including "Hell is For Children," "Treat Me Right," and "You Better Run," whose video was famously the second ever played on MTV in 1981. It rose to number two on the album chart by early 1981 and held that position for a month, riding the top 10 for about six months. However, it never reached number one, peaking behind John Lennon and Yoko Ono's posthumous album *Double Fantasy*. It is certified quadruple platinum (4 million sales). "Crimes of Passion was deep with hits, including Hell is For Children, Treat Me Right, and You Better Run. **Precious Time (1981)** : Released just six months after *Crimes of Passion* peaked, this album unexpectedly became Pat Benatar's only number one LP, a feat she herself admitted was a "fluke" and "not her best work." Although a video was produced for the title track, it was not promoted to radio. The album did produce top 40 hits with "Fire and Ice" and "Promises in the Dark." Benatar explicitly attributed its number one success to "the strength of Crimes of Passion," stating *Crimes of Passion* "should have gone to number 1." It achieved double platinum status (2 million sales), which was half the sales of its predecessor. "On the other hand, when she finally did score a number 1 LP just 6 months later, even she admitted it was a fluke. ## curiosities
**"I Want My MTV" Campaign** : Pat Benatar was one of the prominent artists who appeared in promos for the "I Want My MTV" campaign. This highly effective campaign encouraged people to call their local cable companies to demand that they carry the new channel, significantly expanding MTV's reach and power across the US. "Promos like this began to run constantly on MTV featuring the police, Pat Benatar, Pete Townsend, Cindy Loper, Billy Eidle, and even David Bowie." ← Ongoing History of New Music > The Rise and Fall and Future of the Music Video - Part 1 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqd6IbUJ7tg&list=PLM8bxV0E1Gse5C3n1u-mkVSTZLL5AJICG ← pat-benatar ← pat-benatar
