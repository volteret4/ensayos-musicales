## curiosities
**"I Want My MTV" Campaign** : Pat Benatar was one of the prominent artists who appeared in promos for the "I Want My MTV" campaign. This highly effective campaign encouraged people to call their local cable companies to demand that they carry the new channel, significantly expanding MTV's reach and power across the US. "Promos like this began to run constantly on MTV featuring the police, Pat Benatar, Pete Townsend, Cindy Loper, Billy Eidle, and even David Bowie." ← Ongoing History of New Music > The Rise and Fall and Future of the Music Video - Part 1 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqd6IbUJ7tg&list=PLM8bxV0E1Gse5C3n1u-mkVSTZLL5AJICG ← pat-benatar ← pat-benatar
**Enduring Marriage and Rock and Roll Hall of Fame Induction** : Pat Benatar married her band's guitarist, Neil Geraldo, in 1982; he was her second husband. The couple has remained together since their marriage and were jointly inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2022. "He was her second husband and they married in 1982. **Rock Credibility and AOR Radio** : Pat Benatar is presented as a "rocker gal" who established herself on Album-oriented Rock (AOR) radio before moving towards poppier songs. Crucially, unlike Madonna, Benatar "remained a fixture on rock radio" and successfully made Billboard's rock tracks charts, serving as a contrast to how female artists with dance-pop elements were treated by the rock format. "But unlike Madonna, who was never played by rock stations, Benatar remained a fixture on rock radio." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Raise Your Glass Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f7364fe6d21276e441d
**Reputation as Album Rock Leader** : Pat Benatar spent years establishing herself as the "leading woman on album rock radio" before her 1984 crossover success with "Love is a Battlefield." This background highlighted her established rock credentials prior to experimenting with dance-oriented sounds.
