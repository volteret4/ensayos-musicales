## curiosities
**Times Square Soundtrack Contribution (1980)** : Robin Gibb of the Bee Gees contributed to the soundtrack for the 1980 movie *Time Square*. His inclusion was attributed to his role as one of the film's producers, despite the soundtrack largely featuring emerging punk rock and new wave artists. "Yeah, Robin Gibb of the Bee Gees.
