## albums
**Pinetops Boogie Woogie (1928)** : Released in 1928, this record is cited as the first "real" Boogie record. The rhythm heard in this song, even a decade before the Glenn Miller Orchestra's "In the Mood," is noted as not being significantly different, demonstrating the shuffle rhythm that largely characterized early Boogie Woogie. ## curiosities
**First Musician Shot – Chicago Dance Hall** : Pinedop Smith, a Boogie-Woogie piano player from Chicago, was the first musician known to have been shot. In 1929, just as he was about to enter a recording session, he was fatally shot during a fight at a dance hall. It is speculated that he might not have been the intended victim of the shooting.
