## songs
**Call Me Maybe (2012)** : This song was the winner of the 2012 Billboard Song of the Summer, identified as the "ideal song of the summer." It is a "bop with a struddy beat," featuring "lilleting electronic strings that suggest summer languor," and is "built for clubbing or lazing by the pool." Recorded and released in 2011 in Canada, it gained traction in America in 2012 through word of mouth and social sharing. As it peaked during the summer, numerous homemade videos for the song emerged, predating TikTok. Its sing-along chorus introduced a new catchphrase into the lexicon, and Jepsen performed it at the 2015 Capital Summertime Ball in London. "Here's my number call me maybe. How old school? **Call Me Maybe (2012)** : This "Irresistible Confection" was boosted by Justin Bieber on Twitter and exploded in America following the release of its video on YouTube. The song debuted on the Hot 100 in March 2012 and reached number one by June. Its video helped establish it as not only 2012's song of the summer but also one of the most socially shared songs of the year. It inspired countless fan videos that recreated the song and its universal hand gesture for a romantic phone call, institutionalizing the concept of a hit song as a "Hit Me" challenge before the mainstreaming of Instagram, Vine, and TikTok.
