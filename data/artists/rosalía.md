## albums
**Lux (2025)** : Described as a religious album not made for religious people, Lux features lyrics in 14 languages, each dedicated to a different female saint. The album was created with the London Symphony Orchestra, but its scope is too broad to be categorized purely as classical. It involved years of work and embeds stories of artists, saints, and mystics, alongside relationship songs and "saucy bits of trauma." "It's got lyrics in 14 languages, each corresponding to a different female saint." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Slate’s Music Club 2025 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Previous Album** : Rosalía's album prior to "Lux" was perceived as a deliberate attempt to achieve pop success on conventional pop terms. This contrasts sharply with the direction taken on "Lux," which is described as a 180-degree turn in artistic ambition. "The last album felt very much like a bid for pop success on pop terms." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Slate’s Music Club 2025 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Accusation of Cultural Appropriation** : Rosalía has been accused by certain sectors of the music industry, music critics, and classical/conservative flamenco practitioners of cultural appropriation. This criticism stems from her Catalan origin, lack of Andalusian background, and non-Roma (gitana) ethnicity, suggesting she is appropriating a culture that does not belong to her. "han atacado Rosalía diciendo que hace una especie de apropiación cultural porque ya es de Cataluña, no tiene nada que ver con Andalucía, no es gitana, no tiene nada que ver con los gitanos, ni con la etnia gitana, y el parecer está haciendo una propia acción cultural de una cultura que no le pertenece." ← Music Radar Clan > ROSALIA. Por qué la apropiación cultural es un timo. | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PubYV9sbFQw&list=PLBFijugu0fhAAIR-Ibh59aaU8VWg-SkLc ← rosalía ← rosalía
**Adopted Trap Codes from El Raval** : Rosalía, who frequented the same circles in El Raval as the early trap artists, began to adopt some of their codes and integrate them into her own musical proposals. This indicates the strong influence of the trap scene on her work. "Personas que habitaban estos mismos círculos del rabal, como Rosalía, empezaron a adoptar alguno de esos códigos para integrarlos en sus propuestas." ← La salvaje historia del TRAP ESPAÑOL (2013-2026) | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hBhiU5jj6s
**Music Videos Directed by Director X** : Rosalía is included in the list of artists for whom Director X has directed music videos. "I have directed videos for Alicia Keys, Puff Daddy, Cisco, Destiny's Child, Drake, Justin Bieber, Two Chains, Rosalie." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QBCEmZ4Jheo ← rosalía
**Popularized Ratchet Aesthetic** : Rosalía, along with Bad Gyal, further popularized the "ratchet" aesthetic, which was initially put into vogue by La Zowi. "La Zoe puso de moda todo el roguito del rachet que popularizaría más tarde, Rosalía o Batguial." ← La salvaje historia del TRAP ESPAÑOL (2013-2026) | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hBhiU5jj6s
**Academic Approach to Artistry** : Rosalía is characterized as an "academic artist" who likely pursued "Lux" out of boredom with previous work, aiming for something highly ambitious. She reportedly used Duolingo during the album's creation, reflecting the multi-lingual effort. "I think she just might have gotten a little bored and wanted to do something super ambitious, clearly, because she was out here on Duolingo." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Slate’s Music Club 2025 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Collaboration with London Symphony Orchestra** : Rosalía collaborated with the London Symphony Orchestra, which backed her up on "virtually every track" of her album "Lux." This orchestral involvement highlights the album's ambitious classical elements. "This of course is Rosalia's album that she did with the London Symphony Orchestra." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Slate’s Music Club 2025 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**American Pop Producers on "Lux"** : Despite its diverse and non-traditional approach, the credits for "Lux" include American pop producers, indicating a blend of influences and production styles. "If you dig into the credits of that album too, it's still, you know, there's actually American pop producers mixed in there as well." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Slate’s Music Club 2025 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
