## curiosities
**Accusation of Cultural Appropriation** : Rosalía has been accused by certain sectors of the music industry, music critics, and classical/conservative flamenco practitioners of cultural appropriation. This criticism stems from her Catalan origin, lack of Andalusian background, and non-Roma (gitana) ethnicity, suggesting she is appropriating a culture that does not belong to her. "han atacado Rosalía diciendo que hace una especie de apropiación cultural porque ya es de Cataluña, no tiene nada que ver con Andalucía, no es gitana, no tiene nada que ver con los gitanos, ni con la etnia gitana, y el parecer está haciendo una propia acción cultural de una cultura que no le pertenece." ← Music Radar Clan > ROSALIA. Por qué la apropiación cultural es un timo. | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PubYV9sbFQw&list=PLBFijugu0fhAAIR-Ibh59aaU8VWg-SkLc ← rosalía ← rosalía
**Adopted Trap Codes from El Raval** : Rosalía, who frequented the same circles in El Raval as the early trap artists, began to adopt some of their codes and integrate them into her own musical proposals. This indicates the strong influence of the trap scene on her work. "Personas que habitaban estos mismos círculos del rabal, como Rosalía, empezaron a adoptar alguno de esos códigos para integrarlos en sus propuestas." ← La salvaje historia del TRAP ESPAÑOL (2013-2026) | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hBhiU5jj6s
**Music Videos Directed by Director X** : Rosalía is included in the list of artists for whom Director X has directed music videos. "I have directed videos for Alicia Keys, Puff Daddy, Cisco, Destiny's Child, Drake, Justin Bieber, Two Chains, Rosalie." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QBCEmZ4Jheo ← rosalía
**Popularized Ratchet Aesthetic** : Rosalía, along with Bad Gyal, further popularized the "ratchet" aesthetic, which was initially put into vogue by La Zowi. "La Zoe puso de moda todo el roguito del rachet que popularizaría más tarde, Rosalía o Batguial." ← La salvaje historia del TRAP ESPAÑOL (2013-2026) | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hBhiU5jj6s
