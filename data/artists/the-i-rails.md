# artist - The I-Rails

## members
- Chris O'Connor

## curiosities
**Predecessor to Primitive Radio Gods** : The I-Rails was a Southern California band from which Primitive Radio Gods frontman Chris O'Connor evolved. Despite releasing four albums between 1985 and 1991, the band achieved no significant success and eventually broke up. "They evolved out of a Southern California band called the I-Rails, a group that amounted to exactly nothing over four albums between 1985 and 1991." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SOAL0GODN3M ← the-i-rails ← the-i-rails
