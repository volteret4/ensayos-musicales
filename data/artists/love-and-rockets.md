**So Alive (1989)** : The lead single from their self-titled fourth album, this song was a "slinky homage" to the sound of 70s glam rockers T. Rex. It topped the Modern Rock chart in June 1989, staying there for over a month. Improbably, it crossed over to the pop charts, peaking on the Hot 100 at number three by August 1989. This performance was better by far than any band of Love and Rockets' generation had achieved on the US pop charts to date, surpassing Depeche Mode, Siouxsie, New Order, or The Cure.
