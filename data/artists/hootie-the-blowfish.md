## albums
**Cracked Rear View (1994)** : This album was packed with songs the band had recorded for their independent EPs, including all three future hit singles. Its production was handled by Don Gehman, known for his work with John Mellencamp and R.E.M. Initially, expectations were modest, with Atlantic Records hoping it would sell as well as The J-Hawks' album. Despite internal skepticism and a desire by Atlantic's head of A&R to shelve it as "irreleasable," it was issued in July 1994. After initial slow sales outside the South, it was almost dropped, but a performance on the *Late Show with David Letterman* dramatically boosted its profile. It went gold by mid-December 1994, platinum by February 1995, rose to number one on the Billboard 200 in May 1995 (its 44th week), and went quadruple platinum. By the end of 1995, it was Billboard's number one album of the year with 12 million certified sales, outperforming records by Garth Brooks, Boyz II Men, TLC, and Pearl Jam. As of 1996, it had sold 16 million copies, and with streaming factored in by the late 2010s, it was recertified at 21 times platinum, making it the ninth highest certified album in chart history according to the RIAA. **No US Airplay Royalties for Cover Songs** : As an American artist playing a cover on American radio, Hootie & the Blowfish did not receive radio airplay royalties for "I Go Blind." These royalties are typically paid only to the composers and publishers of the original song. Their only benefit came from mechanical royalties (sales) of the "Friends" soundtrack or the cassette single.
