**Brothers in Arms (1985)** : Released in 1985, *Brothers in Arms* is widely recognized as the first AAD album. The recording process was analog, but the mastering and mixing were done digitally, and the physical format was digital. Its status as the "first" is controversial due to the progressive integration of digital effects into many analog recordings, blurring the exact line of transition. **Brothers in Arms (1985)** : This album, Dire Straits' fifth studio effort, was recorded at Air Studios on the island of Montserrat. It became a global number one smash, topping charts around the world, including in America, and sold more than 30 million copies. It featured guest harmony vocals from Sting on the track "Money for Nothing." **Money for Nothing (1985)** : This song was famously written by Mark Knopfler after he observed workmen at a New York appliance store watching MTV and making snide remarks about musicians. While Dire Straits were recording it at Air Studios in Montserrat, Sting, who was vacationing on the island, was invited to add harmony vocals. His contribution, particularly a riff on the "I want my MTV" slogan (sung to the tune of The Police's "Don't Stand So Close To Me"), earned him a co-songwriting and publishing credit, generating significant income. The song, along with the "Brothers in Arms" album, became a global number one hit. "Famously, Knopfler wrote money for nothing. After observing some workmen at a New York appliance store watching MTV and making snide remarks about how easy it must be to be a musician." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > If You Love Sting, Set Him Free Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef30c30a1408dc2922b
