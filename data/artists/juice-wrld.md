## songs
**Lucid Dreams (2018)** : In 2018, the late SoundCloud rapper Juice Wrld scored his biggest hit ever with this "heavily emo song rap smash." "Lucid Dreams" topped the R&B/Hip Hop chart, reached number two on the Hot 100, and became one of Spotify's most played songs with nearly 3 billion streams. The song is built out of a sample of Sting's 1993 track "Shape of My Heart," a sample Sting joked would "put his grandkids through college."
