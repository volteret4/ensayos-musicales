## songs
**Driver's License (2021)** : Olivia Rodrigo's debut solo single, a breakup song released in 2021. Listeners speculated its lyrics contained hints about a love triangle involving other Disney actors, creating a "perfect storm for TikTok" due to the mystery and catchy nature of the song. Within three days, it topped all streaming charts, soon reaching the Billboard Hot 100, and became the fastest song ever to hit 100 million listens on Spotify, largely due to its viral success on TikTok.
