## songs
**Driver's License (2021)** : Olivia Rodrigo's debut solo single, a breakup song released in 2021. Listeners speculated its lyrics contained hints about a love triangle involving other Disney actors, creating a "perfect storm for TikTok" due to the mystery and catchy nature of the song. Within three days, it topped all streaming charts, soon reaching the Billboard Hot 100, and became the fastest song ever to hit 100 million listens on Spotify, largely due to its viral success on TikTok. "Everyone was like, I'm crying about these teens that I know nothing about. It debuted at number one on the Hot 100 in 2023, fell out, and then remarkably returned to number one nine weeks later, demonstrating its enduring hit status.
