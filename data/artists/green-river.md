## curiosities
**Coined "Pure Grunge"** : Mark Arm, a member of Green River and a coworker of Bruce Pavitt at the Musak Corporation, is credited with writing a letter to a local entertainment weekly in the early 1980s, describing a fictional band as "Pure Grunge." This is considered the first recorded instance of anyone in the area referring to the new Seattle sound with the "G-word." "In the early 1980s, he wrote a letter to one of the local entertainment weeklies.
