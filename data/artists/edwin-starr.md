# artist - Edwin Starr

## songs
**War (1970)** : Edwin Starr took on this controversial anti-war song, originally written by Norman Whitfield and Barrett Strong, after Motown's Barry Gordy prevented The Temptations from releasing it as a single. It subsequently became Starr's signature song and a massive hit, reaching number one in the summer of 1970.
