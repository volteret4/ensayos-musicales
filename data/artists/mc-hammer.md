## albums
**Please Hammer Don't Hurt 'Em (1990)** : This album was a "chart monster" for MC Hammer, reaching number 1 on the Billboard album chart and staying there for 21 weeks. It was the fastest album to be certified 10 times Platinum (Diamond) by April 1991, achieving this milestone faster than *Born in the U.S.A.*, *Purple Rain*, or *Hysteria*. Its sales in its first year surpassed those of any album by Madonna, Whitney Houston, or Janet Jackson to that point. It achieved its massive success despite "U Can't Touch This" being hampered by a unique single release strategy and having no number 1 singles on the album, with only three of its four singles reaching the top 40. The album's success marked it as the first hip-hop album to reach this level, and it remains the third best-selling single-disc rap album of all time. **U Can't Touch This (1990)** : This song was MC Hammer's first pop crossover hit and became ubiquitous, regarded by many as the "song of the summer" in 1990. It heavily sampled Rick James' 1981 R&B hit "Super Freak" and entered the "jock jam and party starter pantheon" instantly upon its spring 1990 release. Despite its widespread popularity and ubiquity, it only peaked at number 8 on the Billboard Hot 100 on June 16, 1990, and didn't even make the top five, an underperformance due to Capital Records' deliberate decision to release it only on a niche 12-inch vinyl format. This strategy aimed to compel consumers to buy the full *Please Hammer Don't Hurt 'Em* album.
