**Early Career Collaboration with Barry Manilow** : Bette Midler was a friend of Barry Manilow, who played piano for her in the early 1970s, including performances at gay bath houses. This collaboration was part of Manilow's foundational career experiences. "Even though Manolo had famously broken his career in the early 70s, playing piano behind his friend Bet Middler at Gay Bath Houses, Manolo didn't finally come out until the mid 2010s, after he wed his longtime partner Gary Keefe following the legalization of same-sex marriage." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Mighty Real Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
