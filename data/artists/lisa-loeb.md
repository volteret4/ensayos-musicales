**Stay (I Missed You) (1994)** : This conversational ballad was contributed by Lisa Loeb, who was unsigned at the time, to the "Reality Bites" soundtrack after actor Ethan Hawke discovered her. Released as a single, it reached number seven on the modern rock chart and impressively summited the Hot 100 in August 1994, making it the only song loosely termed 90s Alt Rock to top the big pop chart.
