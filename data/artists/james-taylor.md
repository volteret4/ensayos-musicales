## curiosities
**Mellow Rock Trend (Early 1970s)** : James Taylor was cited as an example of artists popular in the early 1970s whose music contributed to the "Mellow" trend, as fans deserted the harder, more soulful sounds of the 60s for a softer approach. "The word everyone was using was Mellow, James Taylor, Bread, Olivia Newton John, Paul Simon, the California Rock of the Eagles, a new look Fleetwood Mac." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYi3r0G-ne4 ← james-taylor
**Soft Rock Influence on Garth Brooks** : James Taylor, a soft rock singer-songwriter, was a significant musical inspiration for Garth Brooks, who grew up listening to more rock than country. Brooks and his collaborators consciously translated the sound of "old James Taylor records" into the wistful small-town narratives found in Brooks' country hits like "Unanswered Prayers." "Growing up in Yukon, Oklahoma, Garth Brooks, by his own admission, listened more to rock than country music. In particular, he was inspired by the soft rock, profored by singer songwriters like James Taylor and Dan Fogelberg." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Friends in Low Places, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Laurel Canyon Scene** : James Taylor was one of the fellow songwriters befriended by Carol King in Laurel Canyon, a Hollywood Hills neighborhood that was a hotbed of 60s rock counterculture and an instigator of the singer-songwriter movement. "Their King befriended fellow songwriters, Joni Mitchell. And James Taylor." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Spirit of ’71, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Contribution to Tapestry** : James Taylor played and sang backup on Carol King's follow-up album to *Writer*, which would become the highly successful *Tapestry*. "Both Mitchell and Taylor would wind up playing and singing back up on King's follow-up to her well-reviewed but poor-selling writer album." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Spirit of ’71, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Singer-Songwriter Market Opener** : James Taylor's albums released since 1970, alongside those of Joni Mitchell, helped to open the market for solo singer-songwriters. He made his reputation in the world of confessional folk. "But the chart breakthrough of albums from Joni Mitchell and James Taylor since 1970 had opened the market to solo singer songwriters." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Spirit of ’71, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Influence on New Country Artists** : James Taylor was cited by new country artists like Garth Brooks at the turn of the 1990s as a significant rock influence for their music, alongside The Eagles and Billy Joel, bridging the gap with traditional country inspirations like Hank Williams. "New artists like Brooks cited 70s rock acts like the Eagles, James Taylor and yes Joel as inspirations for their music as much as they cited Hank Williams." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Still Billy Joel to Me Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
