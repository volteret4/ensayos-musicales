## albums
**...And Then There Was X (1999)** : DMX's third album was released on December 21, 1999, exactly one year after his previous strategic Christmas week release. It knocked Celine Dion from her album chart position and became his top-selling album, featuring hits like "What's My Name" and "Party Up." "It knocked Selene Dion from her album chart, Perch, and became his top seller, packed with his biggest hits, including What's My Name, and Party Up." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Smells Like Christmas Spirit, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Flesh of My Flesh, Blood of My Blood (1998)** : This second album was recorded and released under a million-dollar bonus challenge from the president of Def Jam, with just a few weeks to spare before the end of 1998, specifically on December 22nd, three days before Christmas. The album's cover photo, showing the rapper shirtless and drenched in blood, was decidedly un-Christmasy. Despite industry skepticism about such a late release, it debuted with sales of 670,000 copies, one of the top 10 biggest weeks of the SoundScan era, and codified a new industry practice for hip-hop album drops aimed at post-Christmas fans. "Flesh of my flesh, blood of my blood, debuted sales of 670,000 copies, one of the 10 biggest weeks of the sound scan era to date." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Smells Like Christmas Spirit, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**It's Dark and Hell Is Hot (1998)** : DMX's debut album, released in May 1998, debuted on top of the Billboard album chart, an impressive feat for a rap newcomer. It was still lodged in the top 30 when his second album was issued later that year.
