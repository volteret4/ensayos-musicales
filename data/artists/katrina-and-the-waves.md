# artist - Katrina and The Waves

## songs
**Going Down to Liverpool** : This song was originally by the British-American band Katrina and the Waves. It was later covered by The Bangles as their second single from "All Over The Place." **Walking on Sunshine (1985)** : The horn section featured on this popular song was performed by musicians known as The Rumor Brass. This same group also contributed to The Clash's "Revolution Rock" from their "London Calling" album.
