**Keyboardist for The Doors** : Ray Manzarek is identified as the keyboardist for the iconic band The Doors. **Keyboard Solo on "Light My Fire" (1967)** : Ray Manzarek's "long keyboard freak out" was a prominent part of the nearly seven-minute original album version of "Light My Fire." This section was cut out by Elektra Records to create a shorter radio-friendly single version.
