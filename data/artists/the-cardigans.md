## songs
**Lovefool (1996)** : This song served as The Cardigans' American breakthrough, reaching number nine on the Modern Rock chart. More significantly, it spent eight weeks as the number two most played song on pop stations and nearly a year on the Hot 100 Airplay chart, much longer than its four-month run on the Modern Rock chart. **Pop Crossover Success (1996)** : The Cardigans, a Swedish indie rock band fronted by Nina Persson, experienced their American breakthrough with "Lovefool" from their third album. While it charted on Modern Rock, its major success came on pop stations, where it spent eight weeks as the #2 most played song and nearly a year on the Hot 100 Airplay chart. This exemplified the trend of women-led acts achieving pop smash hits as alt-rock radio pivoted away.
