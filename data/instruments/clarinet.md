## curiosities
**Role in IMS's "Hello World" (N/A)** : The classical composition "Hello World," created and notated entirely by the IMS computer program from the University of Melaga, was specifically written for clarinet, violin, and piano. This demonstrates an early instance of a computer program composing for specific acoustic instruments without human musical input. "It was a classical composition for clarinet, violin, and piano." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QFtS8fl553Y ← clarinet
**Traditional Trag Jazz Instrument** : The clarinet is a traditional instrument used in Trag Jazz, a revival of Dixieland Jazz. Trag Jazz music was explicitly made with clarinets, among other brass and rhythm instruments. "That meant music made with trumpets, the trombone, the clarinet, the banjo, upright bass and drums." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zNIVqKqAlnk ← clarinet
**Mr. Acker Bilk's Signature Instrument** : A "mellow clarinet diddy" was characteristic of Mr. Acker Bilk's music, notably featured in his 1962 Hot 100 number one hit, "Stranger on the Shore." "a mellow clarinet diddy by a Somerset man who called himself Mr. Acker-Bilk was the Hot 100's number one song of 1962." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The British Are Charting Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Nile Rogers' Early Musical Education** : During his childhood, Nile Rogers learned to play the clarinet in school. This experience with wind instruments, along with the flute, provided him with the ability to read music, a skill he later credited with helping him secure gigs. He even initially attempted to transpose clarinet chords onto his first guitar. "Learning both the flute and the clarinet in school before trying to transpose clarinet chords onto his first guitar." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > These Are the Good Times, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
