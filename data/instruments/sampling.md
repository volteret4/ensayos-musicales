## curiosities
**New Art Form (Late 1980s)** : Sampling emerged as a new art form around 1988, significantly contributing to the mashing up of hip hop and dance music. Its impact was famously demonstrated by MARRS' number one hit "Pump Up the Volume." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kFLMkvJAaUY ← sampling
**Satire of Sampling in Hip Hop (1991)** : Third Bass's 1991 hit "Pop Goes the Weasel" served as a "satire of sampling and pop rap," specifically targeting Vanilla Ice. The song itself was "built around samples of Peter Gabriel and The Hooh," demonstrating a meta-commentary on the practice that could also achieve chart success.
