# instrument - Glockenspiel

## curiosities
**Used by The Killers in Springsteen-influenced Song** : The glockenspiel was a notable instrument, specifically its "flourishes," used by The Killers in an unnamed song from their 2006 album "Sam's Town" that was heavily influenced by Bruce Springsteen's "Born to Run." "There are the guitar licks, the instrumentation, especially the Glockenshmeel Flourishes, the big descending musical accents, the big production." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2EerEOuP52I ← glockenspiel
**Phil Spector's "Wall of Sound" Inclusion** : Phil Spector incorporated glockenspiel into his famous "Wall of Sound" arrangements for his Christmas records, contributing to their distinctive ringing, chiming sound. This influence was directly applied by Bruce Springsteen in the arrangement of his song "Born to Run." "Springsteen also admired the ringing chiming sound of Specter's Christmas records, which mixed zylophone and glockenspiel into the wall of sound." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Tramps Like Us, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
