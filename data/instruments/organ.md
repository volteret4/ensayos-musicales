# instrument - Organ

## curiosities
**Alternative to Pianos for Large Groups** : Early Hammond organs were marketed to churches as an alternative to pianos, which could be too quiet for large choirs and congregations. The organ's ability to produce sustained, powerful sound made it suitable for such environments. "The first organs were marketed to churches as an alternative to giant expensive pipe organs, or pianos, which could be too quiet for large choirs and congregations." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y0HW8LQKvJw ← organ
**Traditional Keyboard in Rock** : The organ was listed as a traditional keyboard instrument frequently employed in rock music, forming part of the conventional instrumentation for many decades. "For the longest time, the sounds of rock were made with voice guitars, bass, drums and keyboards like piano and organ." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5PJRCC6EwUY ← organ
**Key Element in "House of the Rising Sun"** : Alan Price's "bold organ" playing was a critical component of The Animals' 1964 number one hit, "House of the Rising Sun," which critic Dave Marsh noted for connecting the ancient tune to a "live wire."
