# instrument - Farfisa organ

## curiosities
**Signature Garage Rock Sound** : The Farfisa organ, characterized as a "thin sounding organ," was a common and distinctive instrument frequently used in Garage Rock bands. Its unique timbre contributed to the genre's basic and raw sound. "It tires, bass, drums, vocals and maybe the odd bit of keyboard, often with a thin sounding organ called a farfisa." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UpWz3X33vSY ← farfisa-organ
**Signature New Wave Sound** : The Farfisa organ played a significant role in the sound of new wave music, notably used by Kate Pierson of The B-52s to emulate the 60s acts they loved. Its distinctive sound also spread throughout post-punk, appearing in the first two albums by Elvis Costello.
