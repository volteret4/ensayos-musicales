# instrument - piano

## curiosities
**Elvis Presley's Unexpected Skill (1956)** : During the Million Dollar Quartet session, Elvis Presley played the piano "perfectly decent[ly]" for the first part of the session, despite claiming not to be able to play the instrument. His casual noodling initially prompted Jerry Lee Lewis to try and displace him. "For the first part of the session, Elvis is on the piano, and while he claimed to not be able to play, he actually does a perfectly decent job." ← Episode 51： ＂Matchbox＂ by Carl Perkins | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Nrjr0AGd3U
**Jerry Lee Lewis's Boogie Style (1956)** : Jerry Lee Lewis, known as a Louisiana piano player influenced by Moon Mulligan, immediately launched into a boogie piano part upon hearing Carl Perkins sing the lines for "Matchbox." This spontaneous contribution significantly shaped the song's sound, making it sound "exactly like a Jerry Lee Lewis record" until the guitar solo. "And so when Karl Perkins started singing the lines for Matchbox Blues, Jerry Lee immediately started playing a boogie piano part." ← Episode 51： ＂Matchbox＂ by Carl Perkins | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Nrjr0AGd3U
