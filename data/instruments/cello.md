# instrument - Cello

## curiosities
**Mary Guibert's Classical Training** : Mary Guibert, Jeff Buckley's mother, was a classically trained musician who played cello. "Mom was a classically trained musician playing piano and cello." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MmXDbXI6Sqk ← cello
**Usage in "Don't Come Around Here No More" (1985)** : Cellos were among the instruments used in Tom Petty's psychedelic digital rock track "Don't Come Around Here No More," adding to its distinctive texture. "A psychedelic digital rock track, co-written and produced by Dave Stewart of Eurythmics, punctuated by electric satar and cellos, and featuring a trippy, Allison Wonderland music video that became Petty's most beloved MTV hit." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - Le Petty Prince Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Pizzicato Style in Emo Pop** : The cello was notably used in Panic! at the Disco's signature hit "I Write Sins Not Tragedies." Its "baroque instrumentation" included a cello played "pizzicato style," contributing to the song's unique sound that blended classical elements with crashing punk choruses. "equally broke instrumentation built around a cello played pizzicato style" ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Write Sins, Not Tragedies, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Emotional Impact in Raspberry Beret** : The cello lines in Prince's song "Raspberry Beret" took on a particularly mournful quality when heard after the artist's death, contributing to the emotional weight of his passing. "The cellos on the otherwise whimsical raspberry bray, blaring from my stereo that afternoon, had never sounded so mournful." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Posthumous Hits Edition, Live from Seattle | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
