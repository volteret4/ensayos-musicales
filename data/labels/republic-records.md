# label - Republic Records

## curiosities
**Three Doors Down's Signing** : Three Doors Down secured a deal with Republic Records after a performance at CBGB caught their attention. This led to the release of their multi-platinum debut album, "The Better Life." **Limited Single Pressing** : Republic Records employed a strategy of pressing very limited quantities of CD singles, such as only 70,000 copies for Chumbawamba's "Tubthumping." This created artificial scarcity, allowing the song to climb charts based on airplay while driving album sales.
