# label - American Recordings

## curiosities
**Major Label for Outsider Artist** : American Recordings, founded by Rick Rubin, provided major label distribution for some of Wesley Willis's material. This achievement represents a rare instance of an outsider musician gaining significant mainstream industry support. **Rick Rubin's New Label (1990s)** : Founded by Rick Rubin, initially named Def American, after his falling out with Def Jam. This label began signing largely rock acts such as Danzig, The Black Crowes, and The Jesus and Mary Chain. "Rubin started a new label, Deaf American, later renamed American recordings.
