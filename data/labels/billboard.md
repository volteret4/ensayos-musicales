**Billboard Hot 100 Singles Chart** : The Billboard Hot 100 singles chart is a primary industry benchmark, where a song's entry into the Top 40 is frequently used as the starkest definition of a "hit." **Historical Chart Compilation** : In earlier periods, the Billboard Hot 100 was compiled using a combination of radio airplay data and sales figures for 45 RPM, seven-inch singles, which determined the original "one hit wonders." "Now, in the old days, the chart was put together using a combination of radio, airplay and the sales of 45 RPM, seven, and singles." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d_uDHds2XGQ ← billboard ← billboard
**Influence of Pop Fan Consumption** : The listening habits of pop music fans, who often listen to the same songs multiple times, can significantly skew streaming charts, which in turn impacts the positions of songs on the Billboard Hot 100. "pop fans tend to be heavy consumers of music in the sense that they'll listen to the same songs 10, 20, 30 times in a row. And that skews where songs end up on the streaming charts, which in turn impacts Billboard Hot 100." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d_uDHds2XGQ ← billboard ← billboard

**Song of the Summer Chart Establishment** : Billboard magazine "belatedly built a bespoke chart" specifically to determine one definitive annual Song of the Summer winner. This "chart Bible" later retroactively declared an official Song of Summer for every year since the Hot 100 was founded in 1958.
