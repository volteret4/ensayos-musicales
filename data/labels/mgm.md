# label - MGM

## curiosities
**Blackboard Jungle Release (1955)** : MGM released the film *Blackboard Jungle* in 1955. This movie was significant for featuring Bill Haley and the Comets' "Rock Around the Clock" in its opening titles, marking the first time a rock and roll song was used in a Hollywood feature film. "In 1955, MGM released Blackboard Jungle, a film about an interracial intercity school." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zIkjd1OjCXM ← mgm
**Genie C. Riley's Move for Autonomy** : Genie C. Riley moved to MGM Records after breaking her contract with Shelby Singleton's Plantation label, seeking to gain more control over her career and personal image. "Eventually, Riley broke her contract with Singleton and went to MGM to try to assert some control over her own career and her life." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 23 - Women of Country Music in the 70s | https://seventies.libsyn.com/023-country-women-72820-2-45-pm
**Capitalizing on Beatlemania with "My Bonnie"** : After The Beatles' explosion in America, the MGM label took advantage of the demand by digging the single "My Bonnie" out of their vaults and releasing it. This track featured the Beatles as a backing group for Tony Sheridan from 1961, illustrating how any product associated with the band would sell during Beatlemania. "After the Beatles' explosion in America, the MGM label dug the single out of their vaults and threw it on the market." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The Fab Four Sweep Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
