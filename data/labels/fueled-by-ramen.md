# label - Fueled by Ramen

## curiosities
**Paramore's Original Label** : Fueled by Ramen was the original label to sign Paramore, which subsequently struck a 360 deal with Atlantic Records, owned by the Warner Music Group. "They were originally signed to a label called Fueled by Ramen, who struck a deal with Atlantic Records, which is owned by the Warner Music Group, which is one of the major labels." ← Ongoing History of New Music > The Rock Snob | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=__7iEl3lH8g&list=PLM8bxV0E1Gse5C3n1u-mkVSTZLL5AJICG
**Pivotal in Emo's Rise** : Co-founded by future Interscope president John Janick and Less Than Jake drummer Vinny Fiorello, the Florida-based label Fueled By Ramen played a crucial role in taking emo "from underground to underground in the 2000s." The label's strategy helped emo merge into pop punk and gain commercial prominence, notably by signing bands like Fall Out Boy. "the label Ferella and Janik co-founded would wind up helping to take emo from underground to underground in the 2000s, as emo merged into pop punk." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Write Sins, Not Tragedies, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Biggest Hit with "Good Girls Go Bad" (2009)** : In 2009, even amidst the rise of EDM and Lady Gaga, Fueled by Ramen, alongside Decadence, achieved their biggest hit with the dance pop song "Good Girls Go Bad" by Cobra Starship, which featured Leighton Meester and reached number seven on the Hot 100. "the decadence and fueled by ramen labels scored their biggest hit with the dance pop song good girls go bad." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Write Sins, Not Tragedies, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
