# label - Hansa

## curiosities
**Bowie and Iggy Pop Recording Studio in Berlin** : Hansa Studio, specifically Studio 2, was the recording location for David Bowie and Iggy Pop's projects during their time in Berlin. Located about 500 yards from Potsdamer Platz, near the Berlin Wall, it was where Bowie produced Iggy's "The Idiot" and recorded his own albums "Low" and "Heroes." **Millie Vanille's European Label** : The German label Hansa released Millie Vanille's debut album, "All Or Nothing," in November 1988 in Europe. This label played a role in the initial European success of the group before their American breakthrough.
