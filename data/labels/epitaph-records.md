# label - Epitaph records

## curiosities
**Punk Rock Indie Success** : Based in Orange County, California, Epitaph Records is a punk rock label funded by the band Bad Religion, who were dissatisfied with the major label system. After initial difficulties, the label achieved massive success with The Offspring's album *Smash*, which became the biggest-selling independent record in modern history with 15 million worldwide sales. "Epitaph was funded by a band that was unsatisfied with the major label system. **The Offspring's Independent Success** : Epitaph Records was described as a "celebrated punk indie label" where The Offspring recorded their album "Smash," which went platinum and became the best-selling indie album of all time, notably without major label distribution for its lead single. **The Offspring's Indie Success** : The Offspring signed to this "celebrated independent punk label" and released their 1992 LP *Ignition*, which saw strong sales in the SoCal scene. In 1994, while still on Epitaph, The Offspring scored a major national radio hit with "Come Out and Play." **Best-Selling Independently Released Album** : The Offspring's *Smash* (1994), released on Epitaph, remains the best-selling independently released album of all time, with 6 million US sales and 11 million global sales.
