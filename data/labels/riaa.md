**1970s Certification Standards** : In the 1970s, the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA) had specific certification standards: a million-selling single was certified Gold, and a two-million-selling single was certified Platinum. Official multi-platinum awards were not yet in existence, as they were introduced in the 1980s. Unofficially, Sheik's "Le Freak," with six million US sales, was considered the first-ever "triple platinum" single since the introduction of platinum awards.
