**"Mother" of the Grunge Movement** : Geffen Records is described as the "absolute mother" of the grunge movement, playing an immensely significant role in shaping the sound of American rock. **Cross-Pollination with Sonic Youth** : The signing of Sonic Youth to Geffen Records was highly influential. Sonic Youth's sound permeated the grunge albums being made at Geffen, and conversely, Sonic Youth absorbed fresh ideas from the younger grunge bands also working there, which helped to update their sound significantly during the 1990s. **First Major Label Internet Distribution (1994)** : In 1994, Geffen Records made history by issuing Aerosmith's B-side "Head First" for free on Compuserve. This was the first instance of a major label distributing a song over the internet, serving as an early "beta test" for digital music dissemination, despite the slow dial-up speeds of the time.
