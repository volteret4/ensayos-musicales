# label - London Records

## curiosities
**Bronski Beat's Quick Signing (Early 1980s)** : London Records signed Bronski Beat after the band had performed only nine gigs. This rapid acquisition indicates the immediate impact and distinctive appeal the synth rock trio had on the music industry in the early 1980s. "It took them all of nine gigs to get a record deal with London Records." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hswtuenFwvM ← london-records
**Former US Subsidiary for The Rolling Stones** : London Records served as the US subsidiary for Decca Records and was responsible for issuing The Rolling Stones' albums in the United States prior to the band establishing their own label, Rolling Stones Records.
