# label - Sub Pop Records

## curiosities
**Financial State in 1989** : In 1989, when Nirvana recorded their debut album "Bleach," Sub Pop Records out of Seattle was described as "perpetually broke." "The band was a completely non-entity at the time, recording for the perpetually broke subpop records out of Seattle." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tbmssCZ05ro ← sub-pop-records
**Nirvana's First Single Release (1988)** : Sub Pop Records was the label responsible for issuing Nirvana's very first single in October 1988, a release that is now identifiable by unique dead wax inscriptions. "issued on subpop records in October of 1988" ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IIJySSo276k ← sub-pop-records
**Singles Club (1989)** : In 1989, Sub Pop Records ran a "Singles Club" that mailed subscribers a random 7-inch single every month. The very first release through this club was Nirvana's "Bleach" single, mailed out on November 1, 1989, with 1,000 copies hand-numbered in red pen on the white center label, becoming highly valuable collectibles. "This was a club where the label mailed you a seven inch single every month, a random seven inch single." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tbmssCZ05ro ← sub-pop-records
**Founding and Early Signings** : Co-founded by Bruce Pavitt, Sub Pop Records is a legendary Seattle label that signed Soundgarden to their inaugural recording contract. Soundgarden's 1987 debut single, "Hunted Down," notably served as the label's telephone hold music for several years. "Subpop wound up signing Soundgarden to their first recording contract." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > All Apologies Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef5154465cd600c4fb2
**Codifying Seattle Sound** : Sub Pop released Soundgarden's first two EPs, "Screaming Life" and "FOP," which were instrumental in codifying the distinct "Seattle sound." "Subpop released Soundgarden's first two EPs, Screaming Life and Thop, which helped codify the Seattle sound." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > All Apologies Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef5154465cd600c4fb2
**Defining Grunge** : After signing Green River, Sub Pop co-founder Bruce Pavitt described their second EP in a label catalog as "ultra-lucy Grunge that destroyed the morals of a generation," a quote that significantly contributed to the adoption and stickiness of the "grunge" genre name. "Bruce Pavitt described their second EP in a label catalog as, quote, ultra-lucy Grunge that destroyed the morals of a generation. Unquote, the genre name stuck." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > All Apologies Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef5154465cd600c4fb2
