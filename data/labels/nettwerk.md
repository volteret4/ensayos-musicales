# label - Nettwerk

## curiosities
**North American Release (2000)** : Nettwerk, an indie label based out of Vancouver, released an album in North America on November 7th, 2000, which had initially been released in the UK on July 10th, 2000. Despite initial mixed reviews, this album eventually sold over 100 million copies and helped kickstart alt-rock in the 2000s. **Sarah McLachlan's Canadian Label and Management** : Terry McBride was the CEO of Sarah McLachlan's Canadian label, Nettwerk, and also served as her manager. He played a key role in the early discussions and challenges that led to the creation of Lilith Fair.
