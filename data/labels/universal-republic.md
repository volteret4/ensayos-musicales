**Signed Owl City (2009)** : Universal Republic signed Christian electro-pop artist Adam Young, known as Owl City, in 2009. The label viewed Owl City as "representing the future of our business," particularly due to Young's active curation of his Myspace profile, which fostered a direct connection with his fanbase online.
