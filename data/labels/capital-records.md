**Extensive Promotion of Smile** : Capital Records initially provided strong backing for Brian Wilson and the Smile project, even hiring Derek Taylor, former press chief for The Beatles, to promote Wilson as a musical genius. The label actively advertised Smile, predicting over a million sales by January 1967 and printing more than 400,000 album covers in anticipation. **Major Label Status and Johnny Otis Signing (1957)** : Capital Records was recognized as one of the biggest record labels in the United States. Johnny Otis secured a contract with Capital in 1957, ensuring him major label distribution for the first time in many years, largely due to the advocacy of comedian Mickey Katz. **Bonnie Raitt Signing (Late 1980s)** : Bonnie Raitt signed with Capital Records in the late 1980s after getting sober. This move preceded the release of her highly successful and Grammy-winning album "Nick of Time," which Capital helped promote to adult contemporary radio. **Pioneering Album-Sales Strategy (1990)** : Capital Records implemented a pivotal decision in 1990 by releasing MC Hammer's "U Can't Touch This" only on a niche 12-inch vinyl format. This made the song eligible for the Hot 100 but significantly reduced consumer purchases of the single, compelling the "overwhelming majority" to buy Hammer's *Please Hammer Don't Hurt 'Em* album instead. This kicked off a decade-long industry experiment to change business economics by influencing album purchases. **US-Specific Beatles Releases (1960s)** : Capital Records in America handled The Beatles' releases differently from the UK, sometimes issuing album tracks as singles (e.g., "Nowhere Man" from *Rubber Soul*) and creating US-only compilations like *Yesterday and Today*. **Initial Hesitation with The Beatles** : Capital Records in the US initially passed on releasing The Beatles' singles throughout 1962 and most of 1963, only getting "with the program" when "Beagle Mania" in England became undeniable and US fans were independently requesting "I Want to Hold Your Hand" on the radio.
