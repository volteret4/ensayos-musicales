**Innovator of Double-Sided Discs (1910)** : In 1910, Columbia introduced and demonstrated its "Double Disc Records," which featured music on both sides, offering two different selections for a price slightly above that of a single-sided record. These records were playable on various machines, including Columbia Graphophones and Victor Talking Machines, and were promoted as providing double value. "The purpose of this record is to demonstrate the Columbia Double Disc Records." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jD1dWxNLvfE ← columbia

**Major Label Destination for Johnny Cash** : Columbia Records was the major label that Johnny Cash eventually signed with, spending almost his entire career there after leaving Sun Records. His move was influenced by a desire to follow Elvis Presley's path to a major label amidst personal and professional turmoil. **Vinyl Production Prioritization** : Columbia Records, facing high international demand for vinyl production, strategically delayed the release of Depeche Mode's "Where Is The Revolution" vinyl single. This decision was made to ensure that sufficient units of the *Spirit* album could be manufactured and released first, with the single's vinyl scheduled for April 28th after album production was secured. **Beyoncé's "I Am...
