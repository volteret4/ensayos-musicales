**"Magic Trick" Chart Strategy** : Sony Music pioneered a "magic trick" strategy in the mid-1990s to achieve number one debuts, particularly with Michael Jackson's "Scream" and "You Are Not Alone," and Mariah Carey's "Fantasy." This involved pre-hyping songs to radio for an extended period (up to two months) before releasing them as retail singles, thus exploiting Billboard's rule that songs were ineligible until for sale. Upon release, massive first-week sales combined with pre-existing strong airplay guaranteed instant number one positions. "Sony Music, Michael Jackson's label, had been promoting screen to pop radio stations for about a month when the single arrived in stores." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Toppermost of the Poppermost Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef60c30a1408dc29358
