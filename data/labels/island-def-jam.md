# label - Island Def Jam

## curiosities
**Worldwide Record Deal for Sum 41** : Following a bidding war among various record companies, Sum 41 ultimately signed a worldwide record deal with Island Def Jam, while strategically retaining Aquarius Records for their Canadian releases. "we signed a record deal for the world with Ion Def Jam." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6L4xKBFEYkE ← island-def-jam
**Lady Gaga's First Label Deal** : In 2006, acclaimed music executive L.A. Reed signed Lady Gaga (then Stephanie Germanotta) to Island Def Jam, which was a leading label group in the industry. However, Gaga was dropped by the label just three months later because they were "unsure of what to do with her" creatively and commercially. ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Gaga is Born Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
