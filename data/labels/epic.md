# label - Epic

## curiosities
**Audioslave Co-Release (First Album) – Partnership with Interscope** : For Audioslave's debut album, Epic Records, which was Rage Against the Machine's label, collaborated with Interscope Records (Chris Cornell's label) for a joint release. This arrangement was part of a complex compromise to accommodate both artists' existing contracts. "The first album would be released by both Epic, Rage Against the Machine's label, and Interscope, Chris's label." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lip0bZX7hBE ← epic
**Audioslave Release (Third Album for Rest of World) – Continued Involvement** : Epic Records continued its involvement with Audioslave for their third album, handling its release for the rest of the world, while Interscope managed the US release. This demonstrated an ongoing, albeit split, relationship with the supergroup. "then the third album would be Interscope for the US and Epic for the rest of the world." ← https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lip0bZX7hBE ← epic
**"King of Pop" Mandate (1991)** : Epic Records, Michael Jackson's label, insisted that media refer to Jackson as the "King of Pop" for the first time ever, which journalists and fans largely adopted. "Jackson's label, Epic, insisted the media refer to him for the first time ever as the King of Pop, and most journalists and fans dutifully obliged." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Smells Like Christmas Spirit, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
