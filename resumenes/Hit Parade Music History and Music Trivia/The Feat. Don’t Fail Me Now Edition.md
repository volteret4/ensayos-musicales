## albums

## songs
**Nothing's Gonna Change My Love For You (1987)** : This was a gentle top-20 ballad that marked Glenn Medeiros's breakthrough in 1987. It established his image before he tried to pump up his sound with more up-tempo material. "He had broken through in 1987, with a gentle top-20 ballad called Nothing's Gonna Change My Love For You."
**She Ain't Worth It (1990)** : This song, released in May 1990 and reaching number one on the Billboard Hot 100 by July, was a one-off pairing between Glenn Medeiros and Bobby Brown. It is notable for being the first chart-topping Sing and Rap, two-act pairing in Billboard history and the formal debut of the word "Featuring" on a Hot 100 number one hit. Bobby Brown's rap was a last-minute addition, comprising a quick eight-bar bridge he wrote on the spot, lasting only 20 seconds in the middle of the song, with a small snatch at the start. "The song was called She Ain't Worthy, and it was a one-off pairing between two young men who had never met before the recording session and would never work together again."

## curiosities

**Pioneering Genre Crossover Credit** : The formal credit for Billy Preston on "Get Back" was prophetic from a music business perspective, as it involved a Black R&B performer supporting a White rock group. This model of genre crossover would become a dominant trend in the age of hip hop, making the credit "apt." "But, from a music business perspective, what made the Get Back credit prophetic and apt was that it involved a black R&B performer supporting a white rock group, the very model of genre crossover that would take over the industry in the age of hip hop."
