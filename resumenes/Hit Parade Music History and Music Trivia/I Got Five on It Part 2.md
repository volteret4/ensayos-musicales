# artist - James Brown

## genres
Rhythm and Blues
Rock and Roll
Soul
Funk

## curiosities
**"The One" Concept and Funk Music** : James Brown developed the legendary concept of "the one," which became the bedrock of funk music. He emphasized the first beat of the bar, a shift from previous rhythm and blues, rock and roll, and soul music that stressed the second and fourth beats. To him, "the one" was not just a rhythmic measure but a profound, "cosmic concept" and a "mode for living." "James Brown's legendary concept of the one. Previously with rhythm and blues, rock and roll and soul music, the emphasis had been on the second and fourth beats of the bar. James Brown did was to stress the first beat. This became the bedrock of funk music, the rhythm of the one. To the godfather of soul, the one wasn't just a way to measure rhythm in an exceptionally funky pop song. It was a cosmic concept to James. Getting on the one was a mode for living, a way of life."
**"Godfather of Soul" Nickname** : Commonly referred to as the "godfather of soul." "To the godfather of soul, the one wasn't just a way to measure rhythm in an exceptionally funky pop song."

**The Great War Against the Single (1990s)** : This "decade-long experiment" by the music business in the 1990s involved eliminating retail singles to compel consumers to purchase full-length albums just to acquire one hit song. This tactic caused many 90s hitmakers to underperform on or disappear from the Hot 100, led to significant consumer backlash, and is arguably a factor in the rise of Napster. "I called it the Great War against the Single. A decade-long experiment by the music business to eliminate retail singles and compel a full-length album purchase from MC Hammer to Alanis Morissette, the Rembrandt's to No Doubt, Chambawamba to Sugar Ray, a slew of 90s hitmakers underperformed on or disappeared altogether from the Hot 100, because you couldn't buy their songs, only their albums. It was a tactic that led to consumer backlash and, arguably, Napster."
