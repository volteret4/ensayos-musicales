**Last Christmas (1984)** : Recorded in 1984 by George Michael and Andrew Ridgley, this single was called a "high watermark of mid-80s British synth pop song craft" by Laura Snaps of the Guardian. It is one of the few songs from the late 20th century to enter the holiday music canon and has been covered by many artists, including Ariana Grande. Despite its current popularity, it sounded nothing like Wham!'s earlier hits and its original chart performance was unusual: it didn't chart in the US in 1984 (issued only as a B-side), and in the UK, as a double A-side with "Everything She Wants," it peaked at number two behind Band-Aid's "Do They Know It's Christmas?". However, at the end of 2020, it finally reached number one in the UK. On streaming services, it is Wham!'s all-time biggest hit due to its omnipresence on pre-programmed Christmas playlists, with lifetime streams 40-60% higher than "GoGo" or "Whisper" and plays in 2020 dwarfing "GoGo" 2-1. Radio spins in 2020 (40,000 nationwide) were concentrated in November and December (around 20,000 plays a month in those two months), making it seem omnipresent despite having fewer annual spins than "Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go" or "Careless Whisper" when averaged throughout the year. "Was recorded in 1984 by the duo of George Michael and Andrew Ridgley, then known as Wam."
**Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go (1984)** : This "jitterbugging" single was the lead single from Wham!'s second album, "Make It Big," and gave the duo their first number one hit on both sides of the Atlantic. It remains one of their most popular year-round radio staples. According to MRC data in 2020, it was spun by DJs more than 60,000 times over 12 months, averaging about 5,000 times a month nationwide. Its lifetime streams on Spotify are significantly less than "Last Christmas." "it gave Wam their first number one on both sides of the Atlanta."
**Careless Whisper (1984)** : This "sax-drenched soul-ballad" was released directly after "Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go" and is another reliable "golden oldie" for the band. It was considered a George Michael solo single in the UK but was credited to "Wham! featuring George Michael" in America. In 2020, it was spun by DJs more than 52,000 times over 12 months, making it bigger on American radio than "Last Christmas." Its lifetime streams on Spotify are significantly less than "Last Christmas." "Considered a George Michael solo single in the UK, it was credited in America to Wam featuring George Michael."
**Everything She Wants (1984)** : This slow-burning R&B lament was issued as a "double A-side" single with "Last Christmas" just before the 1984 holiday season in the UK. The single got stuck at number two on the British charts, behind Band-Aid's "Do They Know It's Christmas?". "It was issued in a so-called double A side single just before the 84 holiday season, paired with the slow-burning R&B lament everything she wants."

## albums
**Make It Big (1984)** : This was Wham!'s second album. The lead single from this album, "Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go," was instrumental in making Wham! a massive success. "That album was called Make It Big, and this single did just that to Wam."

## curiosities

"So much so that they've created a game out of trying to avoid it?"
