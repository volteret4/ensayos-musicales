It entered the Billboard album chart in mid-September and quickly reached the Top 10 in under two months, an exceptionally fast ascent for a new group without prior recorded history. More than half of the album's material had been performed at Woodstock, and it peaked at number four on the Top LPs chart by November 1969. "The release of Santana's self-titled debut album two weeks later could not have been better timed. The Santana LP entered the Billboard album chart in mid-September and flew up the chart, reaching the top 10 in under two months remarkably fast for a brand new group with no prior recorded history."
**Abraxas (1970) - First Number One Album** : This album became Santana's first number one album on the chart, taking over the top spot twelve weeks after the *Woodstock* LP finished its run. *Abraxas* also produced the top five hit "Black Magic Woman." "A Braxis took over, becoming Santana's first number one album and spawning the top five hit Black Magic Woman."

**Carlos Santana's Early Life and Formation (1967)** : Carlos Santana, a Mexican-American musical prodigy born to a violin-playing mariachi father from Autlán de Navarro, had been performing in San Francisco for several years before forming the band Santana in 1967 at the age of 22. "Carlos Santana, leader of and guitarist for the band that bore his name, was all of 22 years old when he took to the stage at Woodstock. The Mexican-American musical prodigy, son of a violin-playing mariachi father from Uthlan Denavaro, had been gigging around San Francisco for several years before forming Santana in 1967."
**Signed by Bill Graham (1969)** : Santana secured a signing with Columbia Records in early 1969, a move that occurred at the direct urging of the renowned manager and concert promoter, Bill Graham. "They were signed to Columbia Records in early 1969 at the behest of legendary manager and concert promoter Bill Graham."
**Woodstock Condition by Bill Graham** : Bill Graham, who was consulted by the Woodstock organizers for festival planning assistance, agreed to help only on the specific condition that the then-unknown band Santana be granted a performance slot. "It was Graham, who had been asked by the Woodstock organizers for help planning the festival, who agreed to help only on the condition that the then-unknown Santana be allowed to play."
**Least Recorded Material at Woodstock** : At the time of their Woodstock performance, Santana possessed the least amount of recorded material among all performers, having only recently finished their self-titled debut album which was not scheduled for release until two weeks after the festival. "In essence, unless you counted the solo John Sebastian or the recristened band Mountain, Santana had the least recorded material, basically none, of any performer at Woodstock."
**Low Payment for Woodstock Performance (1969)** : Santana was among the lowest-paid acts on the Woodstock bill, receiving a modest $750 for their performance, a sum that proved to be an extraordinary value for the organizers given the band's subsequent explosive popularity. "Accordingly, Santana were among the lowest paid acts on the bill, earning just $750 for the gig."
**Carlos Santana's Mescaline Experience at Woodstock** : On the afternoon of Saturday, August 16, 1969, Carlos Santana performed at Woodstock while under the influence of a powerful dose of mescaline, which he had received from Jerry Garcia of the Grateful Dead just hours earlier. Santana recounted asking himself for help "to stay in tune and on time." "Carlos Santana was contending with a powerful dose of mescaline that his friend, Jerry Garcia of the Grateful Dead, had given him just a couple of hours before. Quote, I was really, really on it, you know? Carlos recounted in an interview last month with The New York Times. I asked myself over and over, just helped me stay in tune and on time."
**Woodstock Performance as Formal Debut** : Santana's fierce and uncompromising performance at Woodstock, characterized by their psychedelic, intricate, and enveloping Afro-Caribbean polyrhythms, was widely regarded as the formal debut of one of rock history's greatest guitarists to a mass audience. "Simply put, the audience at Woodstock was witnessing the formal debut of one of the greatest guitarists in rock history."
**Pre-Woodstock Film Chart Success** : Santana's significant chart success, including their debut album reaching the Top 10 and their first single "Evil Ways" becoming a Top 10 hit, occurred notably *before* the release of the *Woodstock* film and its soundtrack album, which later expanded their music to a nationwide audience. "What was remarkable about all this chart activity for Santana was it was still ahead of the release of the Woodstock film and its soundtrack album, which would spread the music of the festival to a nationwide audience."
**Multiple Number One Albums** : Beyond *Abraxas*, Santana achieved the number one position on the album chart three more times between the 1970s and the 2000s, accumulating more than a dozen other top 40 hits during this period. "The band returned to number one on the album chart three more times between the 70s and the 2000s, and they scored more than a dozen other top 40 hits."
**Global Album Sales** : Across all its various lineups and incarnations, Carlos Santana's self-titled band has collectively sold more than 100 million albums worldwide. "Globally, all incarnations of Carlos Santana's self-titled band have sold more than 100 million albums."
**Greatest Fame from Woodstock** : Santana is recognized as indisputably holding the top position among performers who ascended to the greatest fame as a direct consequence of their legendary performance at Woodstock, America's most celebrated music festival. "This is why Santana, rightfully and indisputably, holds the top spot on the list of performers who rose to the greatest fame as a result of Woodstock, America's most celebrated music festival."
**Refusal to Perform at Woodstock '99** : Carlos Santana, then in his early 50s, made the decision not to perform at the "infamous, mostly disastrous" Woodstock '99 festival, which was held on a military base in upstate New York. "But Carlos Santana, by then in his early 50s, elected not to perform."
