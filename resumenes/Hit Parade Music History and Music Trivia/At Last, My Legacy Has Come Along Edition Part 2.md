# curiosity

**The Legacy Hit Concept** : A legacy hit is a song that underperformed on the charts in its day, missing the top 10, the top 40 or even the entire Hot 100, but is now one of the first songs associated with an artist. For this countdown, songs had to peak below the top 25 on the Hot 100 and reach a modern threshold of radio or streaming ubiquity, which excluded songs like "Sweet Caroline" and "Thong Song." "a song that underperformed on the charts back in the day, missing the top 10, top 40, or even the entire Hot 100"
**Cameron Crowe's Double Impact** : Two legacy hits were turned around by Cameron Crowe films: "In Your Eyes" in Say Anything (1989) and "Tiny Dancer" in Almost Famous (2000). "another movie directed by Cameron Crowe."
**MTV Launch Timing** : "Once in a Lifetime" charted about six months before MTV launched. After MTV, Talking Heads became an easier sell to record buyers and radio programmers. "after MTV launched, Talking Heads were a much easier sell to record buyers and radio programmers."
**Joel Whitburn's Top Pop Singles** : This reference book of chart hits lists Etta James' top Billboard pop charters, and "At Last" is not among them. "In Joel Whippern's reference book of chart hits Top Pop Singles"
**Luminate Data and Wedding Songs** : Luminate supplied the radio and streaming data used for the rankings. "At Last" has been the centerpiece of more weddings than even Luminate could count, alongside wedding staples "I Wanna Dance With Somebody" and "Shout." "it has been the centerpiece of more weddings than even Luminate could count."
**80s Nostalgia Comparisons** : "Talking in Your Sleep" is part of the 80s new wave nostalgia pantheon, though its numbers are not as strong as "I Melt With You" or "Jessie's Girl." "Talking in your sleep has entered the pantheon of Nostalgia 80s new wave."
