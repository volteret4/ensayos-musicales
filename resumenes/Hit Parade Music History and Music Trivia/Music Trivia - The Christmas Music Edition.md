# artist - Max Martin

## member of
Sharon team

## curiosities
**First American Top 40 Hit - Beautiful Life (1995)** : Max Martin achieved his first American Top 40 hit with Ace of Base's single "Beautiful Life" in 1995. This marked the beginning of his prolific career in pop music production and songwriting. "Beautiful life was the first American top 40 hit for producer, songwriter, and pop mastermind Max Martin."
**Prolific Hitmaker** : After his initial success with "Beautiful Life," Max Martin went on to produce and write dozens more hit songs for various artists. His consistent output solidified his reputation as a pop mastermind. "He would go on to score dozens more."
**No Interviews (Present Day)** : Max Martin has maintained an air of mystery by not giving interviews in the present day. This decision has contributed to the intrigue surrounding his work and creative process. "Max Martin never gives interviews anymore."
**Evolving Public Perception** : Over 20 years, the public's perception of Max Martin has evolved from viewing him as merely talented and a "worthy gamble" to recognizing his excellence as "inevitable." He is now considered one of the all-time greats, rivaling figures like Lennon McCartney for the number of number-one hits on the Hot 100. "at the time there was a sense of him as... As very talented as as kind of like a worthy gamble and now the perception is that his excellence is inevitable."
**Cultural Misunderstanding in Songwriting** : A significant part of the "alchemy" of Max Martin's early songwriting success, particularly with "Baby One More Time," stemmed from cultural misunderstandings due to his still-developing English proficiency. He didn't anticipate the interpretation of "hit me baby one more time" and refused to change it, leading to a memorable and "electric lyric." "so much of it works because Max Martin's English was still a bit of a work in progress and he did not foresee how people would interpret the phrase hit me baby one more time."
**R&B vs. Maximalist Pop Vision** : Initially, Max Martin considered "Baby One More Time" to be an R&B song. However, it was ultimately shaped into a "maximalist pop" track, a genre that proved to be a good fit for Britney Spears, who herself wanted to sing like an R&B diva. "this is not really an R&B song we know you think it's an R&B song but what it is is this kind of version of maximalist pop."

## curiosities
**Early Career Agency** : Contrary to the stereotype of being a creation of middle-aged male record executives, Britney Spears had significant agency in her early career, driving her portrayal in media and the rollout of her songs. She displayed her creative vision, even at a young age. "Britney contrary to stereotype had more agency and drove more of how she was portrayed in the media and how this song was rolled out."
**"Baby One More Time" Video Vision** : At just 16 years old, Britney Spears conceived the entire video treatment for "...Baby One More Time," including the Catholic school girl uniforms, the outfit, and the attitude. She famously overruled director Nigel Dick's initial idea for the video. "the video treatment was her idea the outfit was her idea the attitude all came from her."
**Critiques as "Problematic" (2018 perspective)** : As an adult revisiting the "Baby One More Time" era, it's interesting to note how some adults in 2018 retrospectively viewed the song and video as "problematic," a perspective not shared by many children who consumed the culture at the time. "I really did not understand why adults thought anything about it was to use a 2018 word problematic."
**Interviews for "Britney Spears Wanted to Be a Star"** : For the *Entertainment Weekly* article "Britney Spears Wanted to Be a Star," people interviewed included Nigel Dick, who directed the "...Baby One More Time" video, and Barry Wise, who was the president of her label at the time. "everyone from Nigel Dick who directed the baby one more time video to Barry Wise who was the president of her label back then."
**Creative Vision and Marketplace Connection** : Many people, particularly men, who worked with Britney Spears insisted that she was the architect of her career, possessing a creative vision, understanding the marketplace, and knowing how to connect with her fans, traits often exclusively credited to men. Jolivia Rollingstone stated that if record companies could have created more Britneys, they would have tried. "Britney is the architect of her career and that she has not been given the credit that she is due for."
**Mandy Moore Comparison** : Mandy Moore was cited as an example of an artist record companies attempted to mold into "another Britney," but without the same success, underscoring Britney's unique appeal and agency. "if the record company could have created more than one Britney they would have done it and they tried and Mandy Moore is an actress."

# curiosity

**Swedish Craftsmen Influence on Pop** : Swedish producers, songwriters, and artists have consistently dominated music charts for decades, starting as far back as ABBA. Their unique approach, sometimes influenced by a developing grasp of English, has led to memorable and successful pop songs. "Swedish craftsmen Swedish artists producers songwriters who have you know dominated our charts over the decades going all the way back to Abba."
**UK Christmas Number One Tradition** : For nearly 50 years, the UK has had a unique tradition of obsessively following and even betting on which song will be number one on the British charts during Christmas week. This "peculiar tradition" is institutionalized and creates a "crazy contest" annually. "every year the British obsessively talk about bet on literally their bookies who take bets on this on what will be number one on the UK charts the week of Christmas."
**Nielsen Company Digital Download Tracking** : The Nielsen company began tracking digital downloads in 2003, providing data on the most downloaded songs in the US. This data was used to compile a list of most downloaded holiday songs as of November 2016. "Nielsen company which began tracking digital downloads in two thousand three."
**_Frozen_ Soundtrack Success** : The soundtrack for the movie *Frozen* achieved immense commercial success, spending many weeks at number one on the regular Billboard 200 album chart, not just children's charts. This widespread popularity translated into significant digital sales for its songs. "_frozen_ spent so many weeks at number one on the regular album chart not just like you know a kitty album chart but like the regular Billboard 200 album chart."
**Love Actually and the Christmas Number One** : The 2003 film *Love Actually* features a subplot about an aging rock star, played by Bill Nighy, attempting to score a UK Christmas number one with a rebranded cover of "Love Is All Around" called "Christmas Is All Around."
