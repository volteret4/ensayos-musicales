# artist - Madonna

## albums
**The Immaculate Collection (1990) - First Greatest Hits CD** : This compilation, also referred to as "Madge's first greatest hit CD," was released in 1990. It notably excluded her number one hit "Who's That Girl," despite that song's chart success. "Madge's first greatest hit CD."
