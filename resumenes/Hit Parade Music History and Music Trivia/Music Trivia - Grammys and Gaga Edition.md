# genre - Hip Hop
## curiosities
**Grammy Historical Bias** : The National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences (NARAS) has a "checkered history" with hip hop, particularly in the frontline Grammy categories such as Album of the Year, Record of the Year, and Song of the Year. This bias is attributed to the generally older demographic of Grammy voters and a perceived problem with "hip hop recording values," such as reliance on samples and less emphasis on traditional instrumental chops. "The Grammys, Narus, again, the National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences, has a checkered history with hip hop, particularly in the front line categories."
**Album of the Year Scarcity** : Hip hop albums almost never take the Album of the Year Grammy prize, which is typically the last and most prominent award of the night. In Grammy history, only two albums have achieved this: Lauren Hill's "The Miseducation of Lauren Hill" (1999) and Outkast's "Speakerboxxx/The Love Below" (2004). Even in these cases, the albums were noted to have significant R&B "bones" alongside their hip hop elements. "Literally two albums in Grammy history have taken the album of the year prize."

# curiosity
**UK Christmas Number One Tradition** : There is a "great British tradition" of "random left field novelty cheesy," or "NAF," songs taking the UK number one crown at Christmas. LadBaby's "We Built This City on Sausage Roll" winning in 2018 is highlighted as a recent example of this phenomenon. "it's part of the tradition, the great British tradition of random left field novelty cheesy to use a British term, NAF songs, taking the UK number one crown at Christmas."
**Grammy Rule Change - Nominee Expansion (2019)** : For the 2019 Grammy Awards, the number of nominees in the frontline categories (Record of the Year, Song of the Year, Album of the Year) was increased from five to eight. This significant change, unprecedented since the Grammys launched in the 1950s, was implemented as a "diversity move" to try and get more types of records, such as country, hip hop, and R&B, into each category. "There are now eight nominees in each of these categories, record of the year, song of the year, album of the year. That is new."
**Grammy Voter Demographic** : Grammy voters are generally perceived as being considerably older. This demographic is believed to contribute to a bias against certain musical styles, particularly hip hop, that often rely on samples or do not feature a significant amount of traditional instrumental chops. "The Grammy voter, picture somebody considerably older than you or me, has a problem with hip hop recording values, if you will."
**Best New Artist Grammy Category Issues** : The Best New Artist Grammy category is described as having a "checkered history" and being "funny" due to its inconsistent eligibility rules. It has notably awarded artists who later faded into "terrible ignominy," such as Starland Vocal Band and Milli Vanilli, while frequently excluding deserving artists like Lady Gaga, Whitney Houston, and Cardi B for "random technicalities," or sometimes including artists who had already established careers for years. "Best New Artist is such a funny category. The history of that award is so checkered, so many of one and then gone on to, terrible ignominy from Starland Vocal Band to Millie Vanilley."
