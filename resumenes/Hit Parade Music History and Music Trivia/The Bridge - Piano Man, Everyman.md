# artist - Billy Joel

## albums
**The Bridge (1986) - Double Platinum Despite Critically Weakest 80s Album** : This album is widely considered Billy Joel's weakest release of the 1980s, an assessment Joel himself shares. Despite this critical reception, "The Bridge" achieved double platinum sales and generated three songs that reached the Top 40 charts. Its release marked a significant point, leading into the final phase of Joel's pop music recording career before his eventual retirement from permanent recording. "The Bridge is widely regarded as Billy Joel's weakest 1980s album, including by Joel himself."

## songs
**The Bridge (1986) - Final Track of Album** : This song is prominently featured as the final track on Billy Joel's 1986 album, also titled "The Bridge." "The final track on Billy Joel's 1986 album The Bridge."

## curiosities
**Retirement from Pop Music Recording** : Following the release of "The Bridge" in 1986, Billy Joel entered the concluding stage of his career focused on recording pop music. He only went on to record two more studio albums within the pop genre before making a permanent departure from recording. "In retrospect, The Bridge served as a bridge into the final phase of Billy Joel's career."
