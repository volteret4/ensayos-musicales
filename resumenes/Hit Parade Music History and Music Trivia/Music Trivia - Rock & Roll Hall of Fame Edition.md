# genre - New Wave of British Heavy Metal
## curiosities
**Movement Definition** : This term was coined to describe a group of metal bands, including Judas Priest, Iron Maiden, and Def Leppard. The "new wave" designation referred to the next generation of bands in the genre, not specifically to the synthesizer-driven or aesthetic characteristics of pop new wave music. "They literally came up with a group of metal bands that were called the New Wave of British Heavy Metal. But back when that term was coined, they didn't mean new wave like synthesizers and Kaja Gugu hair or anything like that. They sort of meant new wave as in just the next wave of bands like Judas Priest and Iron Maiden and Deaf Leopard."
