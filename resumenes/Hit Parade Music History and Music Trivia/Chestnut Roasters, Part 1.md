# artist - Bing Crosby

## genres
Pop

## songs
**Swinging on a Star (1944)** : Spent a total of 10 cumulative weeks at number one on Billboard's pop singles charts (Best Selling Retail Records and Most Played Jukebox Records) in 1944, which was the most of any chart topper that year. It became a standard thanks in large part to its prominent inclusion in the film *Going My Way*. The tune itself won the Best Original Song prize at the 1944 Academy Awards and probably contributed to Crosby's own Oscar win that night for Best Actor. "Swinging on a star has become a standard. Thanks in large part to its prominent inclusion in the film Going My Way, the Crosby Starring Best Picture Academy Award winner for 1944."
**White Christmas (1942)** : This song, written by Irving Berlin for Crosby's 1942 film *Holiday Inn*, won the Best Song prize at the Oscars. The 1942 recording has reportedly sold more than 50 million copies worldwide. When including Crosby's later re-recordings of the song, such as the 1947 version commonly heard on radio today, Bing's "White Christmas" has sold upwards of 100 million copies, making it the best-selling single period of all time as per the Guinness Book of World Records. "Bing Crosby's White Christmas, a song Irving Berlin wrote for Crosby's 1942 film Holiday Inn."

## curiosities
**Oscar Wins (1944)** : At the 1944 Academy Awards, "Swinging on a Star" won Best Original Song (from his film *Going My Way*, which also won Best Picture), and this likely contributed to Crosby's win for Best Actor. "At that same Oscar ceremony, the tune itself won the best original song prize, and it probably contributed to Crosby's own Oscar win that night for best actor."
**Best-Selling Single of All Time** : His song "White Christmas," when including all versions and re-recordings, has sold upwards of 100 million copies, making it the biggest seller of any recording, more than four decades after his passing. "This is the best selling single period of all time."
