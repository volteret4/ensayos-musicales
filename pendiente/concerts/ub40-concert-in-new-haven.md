# concert - UB40 Concert in New Haven

## curiosities
**Brett Kavanaugh Bar Fight (September 1985)** : A UB40 concert in New Haven in September 1985 became a focal point of news when it was reported that Brett Kavanaugh, then a Supreme Court nominee, was involved in a bar fight following the event with his friend Chris Dudley. This period of UB40's history was notable for being a "nether period" where "Red Red Wine" had been out for years, but the band wasn't yet American chart toppers.
