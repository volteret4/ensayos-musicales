# concert - Source Awards 1995

## curiosities
**Andre 3000's "The South Got Something to Say" Speech (1995)** : At the 1995 Source Awards, Andre 3000 of Outkast concluded his acceptance speech before a New York audience by declaring, "the South got something to say." This pivotal moment is credited with spurring the relocation of rap's geographical center from the East and West Coasts to the South, with Atlanta becoming its capital.
