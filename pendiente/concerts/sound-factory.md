# concert - Sound Factory

## curiosities
**Site of KRS-One vs. PM Dawn Confrontation** : In January 1992, the Sound Factory in New York City hosted a multi-act concert where KRS-One and Boogie Down Productions interrupted PM Dawn's set, pushing the duo off stage.
