# concert - Rock and Roll Hall of Fame 2010 Induction Ceremony

## curiosities
**Genesis Induction and Gabriel's Absence** : Genesis was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2010, an induction that included early members Peter Gabriel and Steve Hackett. However, Peter Gabriel declined to attend the ceremony, and the band did not perform.
