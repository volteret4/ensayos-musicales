## curiosities
**Pearl Jam's "Jeremy" Sweep** : Pearl Jam's harrowing cinematic video for "Jeremy," depicting an abused child, became an MTV sensation and "would sweep the MTV video music awards the following year" after its 1991 release. **Kanye West's Interruption of Taylor Swift (2009)** : Kanye West's infamous interruption of Taylor Swift at the MTV Video Music Awards was referenced in 2015 when he briefly climbed the stage at the Grammys, threatening to protest Beck's Album of the Year win, recalling the earlier incident. "Kanye West climbed to the stage, threatening to do to Beck what he'd done six years earlier to Taylor Swift at the MTV Video Music Awards." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > And the Grammy Goes to… Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef0154465cd600c4e3a
**Madonna's Pivotal Performance (September 1984)** : At the first MTV Video Music Awards in September 1984, Madonna delivered the "most talked about performance," debuting "Like a Virgin" while famously appearing on stage in a disheveled bridal gown. This event was crucial in her ascent to becoming "80s pop's new Imperial Queen."
