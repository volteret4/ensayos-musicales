## curiosities
**John Bryan's Residency (1990s)** : Musician John Bryan held a weekly Friday night musical residency at LA Club Largo, serving as a "nexus" for the Los Angeles music scene and attracting rising artists like Amy Mann, Michael Penn, Fiona Apple, and Elliot Smith, who were regulars and often sat in on stage.
