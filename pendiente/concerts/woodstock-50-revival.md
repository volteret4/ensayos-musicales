# concert - Woodstock 50 Revival

## curiosities
**Cancellation (2019)** : The festival organizers officially called off plans for the Woodstock 50 Revival. Its lineup had been announced in March 2019, and it was slated to feature a diverse mix of newer acts alongside several artists who had also performed at the original Woodstock in 1969.
