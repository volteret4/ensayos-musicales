# concert - Apollo Theatre (New York)

## curiosities
**Prestigious Hall and Oates Gig** : In May 1985, Darryl Hall and John Oates performed a very prestigious gig at New York's legendary Apollo Theatre. This was their first performance with The Temptations' David Ruffin and Eddie Kendricks, a collaboration later reprised at Live Aid and released as a live LP and single, marking a significant moment in their career and for Motown legends.
