# concert - Harlem Square Club

## curiosities
**Legendary Live Performance** : In January 1963, Sam Cook delivered a gritty and enraptured performance for a predominantly black crowd at the Harlem Square Club in Miami. This show, captured on a recording posthumously released in 1985, is widely considered Cook's best live album, demonstrating his unique ability to command an audience and "code switch" musically.
