# concert - Elton John show at Madison Square Garden (Thanksgiving 1974)

## curiosities
**John Lennon's Last Live Appearance** : As a fulfillment of a bet with Elton John, John Lennon made a surprise appearance at Elton's show at Madison Square Garden on Thanksgiving in 1974. This performance tragically marked Lennon's last live appearance, occurring one year before he began a long recording sabbatical and six years before his assassination.
