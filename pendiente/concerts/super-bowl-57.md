## curiosities
**Rihanna's Halftime Performance and Pregnancy Revelation (2023)** : Rihanna served as the halftime performer for Super Bowl 57 in 2023, delivering what was described as a "quintessential Rihanna performance"—regal, steely, and effortlessly cool, commanding the stage (which appeared to float) without aggressive dance moves. Her outfit notably revealed that she was pregnant with her second child, a "milestone for a woman performer on the halftime stage" and an implied message that new music would only come when she was ready.
