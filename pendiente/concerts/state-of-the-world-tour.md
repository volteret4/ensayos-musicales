# concert - State of the World Tour

## curiosities
**Social Commentary Through Performance (Recent) - 30 Years After Rhythm Nation** : Janet Jackson's nearly two-year "State of the World Tour" across America, Canada, and Japan was explicitly named to reflect its social commentary. Concerts began with a montage of news and media clips, drawing attention to societal issues 30 years after her "Rhythm Nation" album, and featured a performance of the song "State of the World."
