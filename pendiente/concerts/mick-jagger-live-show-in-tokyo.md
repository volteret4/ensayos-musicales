# concert - Mick Jagger live show in Tokyo

## curiosities
**Tina Turner's Guest Performance of "Brown Sugar"** : At a live Mick Jagger show in Tokyo "a couple of decades" after its release, Tina Turner joined Jagger on stage to perform "Brown Sugar." This performance was highlighted as a testament to Turner's fearless nature and sense of humor, given the song's controversial lyrics.
