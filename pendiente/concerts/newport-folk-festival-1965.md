# concert - Newport Folk Festival (1965)

## curiosities
**Bob Dylan's "Going Electric" Controversy** : At the 1965 Newport Folk Festival, Bob Dylan famously performed his song "Maggie's Farm" "plugged in and extra loud," electrifying his set. This performance antagonized the Newport audience, leading to boos, and became a legendary moment in music history, later depicted in the film "A Complete Unknown."
