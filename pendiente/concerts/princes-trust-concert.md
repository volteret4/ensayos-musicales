# concert - Princes Trust Concert

## curiosities
**Robin and Barry Gibb's Final Performance (May 2006)** : This concert, a nationally televised event from the Tower of London in May 2006, featured Robin and Barry Gibb sharing a "final final moment" on stage. They performed for Prince Charles and his sons Harry and William, marking one of only two times the remaining Gibb brothers performed together after Maurice's death.
