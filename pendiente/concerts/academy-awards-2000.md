# concert - Academy Awards (2000)

## curiosities
**Amy Mann's Performance and Loss** : Amy Mann performed her Oscar-nominated song "Save Me" live at the 2000 Academy Awards ceremony. Despite the nomination and performance, she ultimately lost the Best Original Song award to Phil Collins' "You'll Be In My Heart" from the film "Tarzan."
