# concert - Madison Square Garden (1971)

## curiosities
**Rick Nelson Concert Incident (1971)** : In the fall of 1971, Madison Square Garden hosted a nostalgic multi-act concert featuring classic rock and rollers like Chuck Berry, Bo Diddley, Bobby Rydell, and Rick Nelson. Rick Nelson and his band received a hostile reception from the "hippie crowd," who reportedly booed him, causing Nelson to storm off stage and later write the song "Garden Party" about the experience.
