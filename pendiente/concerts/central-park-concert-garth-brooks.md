# concert - Central Park Concert (Garth Brooks)

## curiosities
**Historic Free Show (August 7, 1997)** : Garth Brooks and his band, Fulfilled Dream, performed a free show at New York's Central Park on August 7, 1997, which was televised live on HBO. Brooks sought to dispel the myth of his rural-only audience and knew a major NYC show would attract fans nationwide. **Record-Breaking Attendance (1997)** : The concert featured the largest stage ever seen in Central Park's North Meadow. While New York City expected 300,000 attendees, crowd estimates on the night were a minimum of 750,000, with a later fire department estimate putting the figure at just under a million. It was generally agreed to be the park's largest concert ever, reportedly outdrawing previous mega-concerts by Paul Simon, Simon & Garfunkel, and Diana Ross. **Guest Performance by Billy Joel (1997)** : Native New Yorker Billy Joel was a special guest at the concert. He performed some of his own songs, including "New York State of Mind," and also joined Brooks on piano and vocals for Brooks' hit "Ain't Goin' Down ('Til the Sun Comes Up)."
