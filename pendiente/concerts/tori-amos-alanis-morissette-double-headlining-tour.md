# concert - Tori Amos / Alanis Morissette double headlining tour

## curiosities
**Alternative to Lilith Fair** : Instead of joining Lilith Fair, Tori Amos mounted her own "double headlining tour" with Alanis Morissette. This tour was presented as a different kind of female-led concert experience, with Amos explicitly critiquing Lilith Fair's format.
