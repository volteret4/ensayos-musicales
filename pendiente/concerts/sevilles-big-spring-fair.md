# concert - Seville's Big Spring Fair

## curiosities
**"Macarena" Debut (1993)** : Los Del Rio debuted "Macarena" at Seville's Big Spring Fair in 1993. The fair, characterized by tents for parties, food, and music performances, served as the initial proving ground for the song, which rapidly gained popularity among attendees by the fair's end.
