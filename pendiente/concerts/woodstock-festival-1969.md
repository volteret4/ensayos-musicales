# concert - Woodstock Festival (1969)

## curiosities
**Artist Breakthrough Potential** : In contrast to modern festivals where established artists headline, Woodstock 1969 had the unique capacity to launch an artist's career and "put an artist on the map." "when we think about festivals now, it's so rare for a festival to put an artist on the map." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Ladies of the Canyon and the Rhythm Nation | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Performer Payment Hierarchy** : The Woodstock Festival featured a distinct hierarchy among its performing artists, which was reflected in their payment. While headliners like The Who or Blood, Sweat & Tears received higher compensation, newer acts such as Sha Na Na were paid significantly less. "there was absolutely a hierarchy of bands at Woodstock as I point out in the episode, you can even if you want to Google it, folks. You can even find out how much everybody at Woodstock was paid." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Ladies of the Canyon and the Rhythm Nation | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Traffic Congestion in Bethel, NY** : The Woodstock Festival, held in Bethel, New York, was plagued by "terrible traffic" and general disorganization throughout the weekend in August 1969. Concerns about this traffic prevented some artists, like Joni Mitchell, from attending. "the traffic was terrible and everything was a mess in Bethel, New York that weekend." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Ladies of the Canyon and the Rhythm Nation | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Temporary "Second Biggest City" with No Violence** : For two nights, the Woodstock Festival site in Bethel, New York, temporarily became "the second biggest city in New York," remarkable for its complete lack of violence despite the massive gathering. "like about two nights ago, that place up there was the second biggest city in New York. And it had no violence." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Ladies of the Canyon and the Rhythm Nation | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**"Aquarian Exposition" and Utopian Experience** : Woodstock 1969 was officially subtitled "An Aquarian Exposition," reflecting its character as an "improbable" and "truly utopian experience." Many positive memories from the festival highlight its unique, peaceful atmosphere. "It was a truly utopian experience or to use the subtitle they gave the concert. It was an aquarium experience." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Ladies of the Canyon and the Rhythm Nation | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Restored Film Screening** : A restored print of the Woodstock film was screened at the Metropolitan Museum of Art as part of its "Met Friday's series" on September 13th, concurrent with an exhibition on rock instruments. "they're even showing a restored print of the Woodstock film." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Ladies of the Canyon and the Rhythm Nation | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
