# concert - 53rd annual Grammy Awards (2011)

## curiosities
**Mumford & Sons' Big Break** : Held in Los Angeles on February 13th, 2011, this event provided Mumford & Sons with their "big break in America." Their live performance, introduced by David Letterman and including backing Bob Dylan, led to their album "Sign No More" soaring into the Top 10 and going platinum, despite not winning any Grammys that night. **Bob Dylan's Performance of "Maggie's Farm"** : One of the ironies of the 2011 Grammys performance was that Bob Dylan performed his 60s electric blues jam "Maggie's Farm," the same song he infamously performed at the 1965 Newport Folk Festival. This showcase helped a new generation of 21st-century folk acts, like Mumford & Sons, scale the charts.
