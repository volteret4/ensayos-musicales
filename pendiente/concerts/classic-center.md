# concert - Classic Center

## curiosities
**The B-52's 34th Anniversary Live Recording (2011)** : The B-52's recorded a live show at the Classic Center in Athens, Georgia, in 2011 to commemorate their 34 years as a band. The performance was later released on DVD and Blu-ray.
