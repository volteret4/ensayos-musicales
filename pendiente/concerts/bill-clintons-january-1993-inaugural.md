# concert - Bill Clinton's January 1993 Inaugural

## curiosities
**Presidential Request for Reunion** : US presidential candidate Bill Clinton, who had used Christine McVie's song "Don't Stop" as his campaign theme, requested that the classic Fleetwood Mac lineup reform for one night at his January 1993 inaugural to perform the song. Lindsey Buckingham even agreed to appear for this "command performance."
