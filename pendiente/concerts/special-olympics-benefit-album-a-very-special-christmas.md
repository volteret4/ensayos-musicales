# concert - Special Olympics Benefit Album (A Very Special Christmas)

## curiosities
**Run DMC's Unique Contribution (1987)** : Run DMC was the only rap act invited to record a track for this multi-platinum benefit album, contributing "Christmas in Hollis" as the only new composition specifically written for it, unlike other performers who recorded traditional carols or covers.
