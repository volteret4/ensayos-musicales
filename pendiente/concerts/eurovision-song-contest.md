# concert - Eurovision Song Contest

## curiosities
**Olivia Newton-John's 1974 Participation** : In 1974, Olivia Newton-John represented Britain in the international song competition, but she was reportedly forced to sing "Boom Bang" while wearing a "very matronly light blue dress" that resembled a nightgown. She later commented that "no dress could have saved that horrific song" and ultimately lost to Abba's "Waterloo." **Germany's 1987 Entry featuring Rob Pilatus** : Germany's 1987 entry in the Eurovision Song Contest was the song "Let The Sun Into Your Heart" by the band Wind. Rob Pilatus, later of Millie Vanille, appeared as a backing vocalist in at least one performance video for the song, which placed second in the competition. **"Love Is Blue" Performance in 1967** : The vocal rendition of the French song that Paul Mauriat later covered as "Love Is Blue" secured fourth place in the 1967 Eurovision Song Contest. Mauriat's subsequent instrumental cover became a "stealth smash," spending five weeks at number one on the Hot 100 in 1968. "Love Is Blue, a harpsichord-inflected instrumental cover of a French song that, in its vocal version, had placed fourth in 1967's Eurovision Song Contest." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Insert Lyrics Here Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**ABBA's 1974 Victory** : The annual music competition held in Brighton, UK, in 1974, where ABBA represented Sweden and not only "took the crown for their country" but went on to become "the most beloved, most famous Eurovision winner ever."
