# concert - Live Aid (July 1985)

## curiosities
**George Michael's Global Showcase** : At the star-studded Live Aid concert in July 1985 at Wembley Stadium, Elton John brought George Michael on stage to sing with him, performing Elton's 1974 hit "Don't Let the Sun Go Down on Me." John notably ceded the lead vocal spotlight to Michael, providing a major global showcase and a "seal of approval" from the prior decade's top British male star to his 80s heir apparent.
