**Annual Gathering of Music Academics (since 2002)** : The Pop Conference is an annual gathering founded in 2002 by academics and critics Eric Weisbard and Ann Powers, originally sponsored by the Experience Music Project (now MoPOP). It is a weekend-long music festival in the tradition of Woodstock and Coachella, but focused on panel discussions and presentations of scholarly papers, with the motto "less rock, more talk."
