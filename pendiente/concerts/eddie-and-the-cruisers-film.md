# concert - Eddie and the Cruisers (Film)

## curiosities
**Box Office Flop to Cable TV Hit (1983-1984)** : The 1983 film "Eddie and the Cruisers," which aimed to sell its fictional story of a disappeared rock star, was a box office flop, pulled from theaters after just three weeks. However, it debuted on cable TV in 1984, becoming a word-of-mouth hit with huge ratings on HBO and significantly changing the trajectory of its soundtrack's songs.
