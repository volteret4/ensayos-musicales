# concert - The Bottom Line

## curiosities
**Pivotal Multi-Night Stand (Summer 1975)** : Bruce Springsteen's multi-night stand at The Bottom Line in New York City in the summer of 1975 proved pivotal, serving as a career-breaking moment comparable to Jimi Hendrix at Monterey Pop or Elton John at the Troubadour. The shows became "the talk of the town," attracting fans, critics, and industry insiders who packed the tiny club, with one early show even simulcast on New York AOR station WNEW-FM. **Inspiration for *Bat Out of Hell*** : Jim Steinman and Meat Loaf attended Springsteen's Bottom Line shows, which deeply inspired Steinman and informed his collaboration with Meat Loaf on their debut album, *Bat Out of Hell*. "Springsteen's bottom line show inspired Steinman and deeply informed his collaboration with Meetloaf, the debut album Bat Out of Hell." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Tramps Like Us, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Bruce Springsteen Performance (1975)** : Bruce Springsteen played at this New York Club in 1975, just before the release of his breakthrough album *Born to Run*, a performance that deeply impressed Jim Steinman and inspired him to create *Bat Out of Hell* as an album that sounded like a movie.
