# concert - The Girls Just Wanna Weekend

## curiosities
**Brandi Carlile's Multi-Day Concerts** : Brandi Carlile, a Grammy-winning alternative country and Americana artist, mounted a pair of annual concerts called "The Girls Just Wanna Weekend" in 2018 and 2019. These were multi-day concerts, not a touring festival, and included former Lilith performers like Sheryl Crow, The Indigo Girls, and Shawn Colvin. "Carlyle's was not a touring festival.
