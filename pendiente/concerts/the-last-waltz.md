# concert - The Last Waltz

## curiosities
**Guest Superstars** : The Band's farewell concert, "The Last Waltz," recorded on Thanksgiving Day 1976, featured more than a dozen guest superstars. The lineup included notable artists such as Van Morrison, The Staple Singers, Joni Mitchell, Neil Young, and Bob Dylan. **All-Star Sing-Along Climax** : Unlike George Harrison's "Concert for Bangladesh," "The Last Waltz" culminated with an all-star sing-along. This performance of Bob Dylan's "I Shall Be Released" featured the gathered artists around microphones, displaying "sheepish smiles on the faces of the coke-addled, post-hippy rock stars," hinting at the final form of celebrity mega-events.
