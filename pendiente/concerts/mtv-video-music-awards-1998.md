## curiosities
**Video of the Year for Ray of Light** : At the 1998 MTV Video Music Awards, Madonna and video director Jonas Åkerlund were presented with the Video of the Year award for "Ray of Light." Geri Halliwell, then 27, presented the "Moon Man" trophy to the 40-year-old Madonna, whom she described as "My Idol Growing Up."
