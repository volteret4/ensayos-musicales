# concert - Live Aid (1985)

## curiosities
**Phil Collins' Dual Venue Performance** : Phil Collins earned the distinction of being the "most omnipresent performer" at the 1985 Live Aid concert, playing live at both the London and Philadelphia venues. **The Temptations Members' Separate Appearance** : David Ruffin and Eddie Kendrick, two members of The Temptations, performed at Live Aid. However, they appeared on stage with Darryl Hall and John Oates, not as part of Phil Collins' various collaborations that day.
