# concert - Royal Command Performance

## curiosities
**The Beatles' Performance for the Queen (November 1963)** : The Beatles performed at the Royal Command Performance in November 1963, an event where they famously concluded with "Twist and Shout." John Lennon memorably introduced the song with a request for the "people in the cheaper seats [to] clap your hands" and the "rest of you if you just rightly [rattle] your jewelry," showcasing their witty irreverence.
