# concert - Chernobyl benefit

## curiosities
**John Denver's Performance (1987)** : John Denver toured Russia in 1987 to perform at a Chernobyl benefit concert. This was part of his advocacy efforts and his willingness to perform in communist countries that other Western artists were avoiding.
