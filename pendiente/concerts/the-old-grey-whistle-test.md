# concert - The Old Grey Whistle Test

## curiosities
**UK Television Boost for Bat Out of Hell** : This UK televised music show played a 9-minute long video of Meat Loaf performing the title track from *Bat Out of Hell*. Its immense popularity with the audience led to it being played again the following week, significantly contributing to the album's entry and sustained presence on the British album chart.
