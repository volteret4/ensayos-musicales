# concert - Altamont Speedway Concert

## curiosities
**Ill-Fated and Violent Event (December 1969)** : Held in December 1969, this concert, headlined by The Rolling Stones, was described as "ill-fated" and "violent." Its tragic events had a profound impact on the band, with the transcript noting they were "reeling from ultimate" in its aftermath.
