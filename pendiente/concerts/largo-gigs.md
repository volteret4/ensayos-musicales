# concert - Largo gigs

## curiosities
**Amy Mann and Paul Thomas Anderson Connection** : John Bryan's weekly Largo gigs in Los Angeles were a regular haunt for Amy Mann, and it was through these performances that young filmmaker Paul Thomas Anderson, a denizen of these events, first encountered and became a fan of her music.
