# concert - 2019 MTV Video Music Awards

## curiosities
**Missy Elliott's Video Vanguard Award** : Missy Elliott received MTV's Video Vanguard Award, their highest commemorative honor, at the 2019 VMAs, with a video package celebrating her history with Pharrell Williams and Timbaland as musical visionaries from Virginia Beach. **"Truth Hurts" Rising to #1** : Lizzo performed her rising single "Truth Hurts" at the 2019 VMAs, a song that shared the bravado and sexual agency characteristic of Missy Elliott's music. The week after the awards, "Truth Hurts" rose to number one on the Hot 100.
