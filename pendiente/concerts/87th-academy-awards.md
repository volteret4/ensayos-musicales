# concert - 87th Academy Awards

## curiosities
**Lady Gaga's Julie Andrews Tribute (February 2015)** : In February 2015, Lady Gaga performed a tribute to Julie Andrews and the songs of "The Sound of Music" at the 87th Academy Awards, commemorating the film's 50th anniversary. Dressed in a traditional ball gown, Gaga sang four songs from the musical to widespread admiration from the audience in the Dolby Theatre and millions on social media, receiving an embrace and thanks from Julie Andrews herself immediately after.
