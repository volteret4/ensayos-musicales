# concert - Apollo Theater

## curiosities
**Nile Rogers' Groove Development** : The Apollo Theater in Harlem provided Nile Rogers with a transformative gig in its house band. On his debut night, he backed Screamin' Jay Hawkins, but more significantly, the experienced "old timers" in the band taught him how to truly play funk and soul using R&B notation. This critical instruction instilled in him the "groove" that had been absent from his classical and jazz training, fundamentally shaping his future musical direction.
