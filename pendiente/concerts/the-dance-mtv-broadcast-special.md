# concert - The Dance (MTV broadcast special)

## curiosities
**Revisiting Old Glories** : In May 1997, Fleetwood Mac was persuaded to perform for an MTV broadcast special titled "The Dance," where they revisited their older hits. The resulting live album was a blockbuster, topping the Billboard album chart and going quintuple platinum.
