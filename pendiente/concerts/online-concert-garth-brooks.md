# concert - Online Concert (Garth Brooks)

## curiosities
**Innovative Pandemic Performance (2020)** : During the COVID-19 lockdown, Garth Brooks performed an online concert that was uniquely broadcast to 300 drive-in theaters across North America. This approach was hailed by industry observers as "one of the most ingenious ways to circumvent the limits on live performance" during a challenging year for the music industry.
