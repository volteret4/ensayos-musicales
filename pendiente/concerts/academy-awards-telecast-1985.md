# concert - Academy Awards Telecast 1985

## curiosities
**Infamous Performance of "Against All Odds"** : The producers of the Academy Awards made an infamous decision not to invite Phil Collins to perform his Oscar-nominated hit "Against All Odds." Instead, actress-choreographer Anne Reineking was asked to record and lip-sync Collins's song while dancing, a performance widely panned by critics.
