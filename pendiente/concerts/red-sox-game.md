## curiosities
**Origin of "Sweet Caroline" Sing-along** : The practice of mass group sing-alongs to Neil Diamond's "Sweet Caroline," which includes the improvised triple "so good, so good, so good," is legendary to have started at a Red Sox game in Boston's Fenway Park in 1997. This tradition has since spread to stadiums worldwide and ensures the song's enduring legacy.
