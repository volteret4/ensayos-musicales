# concert - Hella Mega Festival

## curiosities
**2021 Tour Lineup** : In 2021, after concerts were able to resume following the first wave of the COVID-19 pandemic, the Hella Mega Festival featured a notable lineup including Green Day, Fallout Boy, and Weezer. While Green Day and Fallout Boy had not yet shared the stage during the tour, it was scheduled to continue through the following July.
