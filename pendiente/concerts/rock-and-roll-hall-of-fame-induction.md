# concert - Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction

## curiosities
**Aretha Franklin's Induction (January 3, 1987)** : On January 3, 1987, Aretha Franklin became the first woman ever inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, in only its second year of existence. The ceremony took place at the Waldorf Astoria in New York City, with Keith Richards giving her induction speech, though Franklin herself was not present. **Absence of Aretha Franklin (1987)** : Aretha Franklin did not attend her own induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame. Her absence was attributed partly to her dislike of leaving Detroit and, more significantly, to her being busy as a "current hitmaker," distinguishing her from most other inductees who were past their prime. **Acoustic Performance Lead-off** : In 1998, when Fleetwood Mac was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, they performed an acoustic set. The set was notably led off by Christine McVie's classic 1976 hit "Say You Love Me," rather than any of the bigger hits from the "Rumours" album, highlighting McVie's significant contribution. **Prince's Triumphant Performance (2004)** : Prince delivered a triumphant performance at the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony in 2004, which included a blazing guitar solo during a George Harrison Beatles song. He later praised Tom Petty for his role in the performance.
