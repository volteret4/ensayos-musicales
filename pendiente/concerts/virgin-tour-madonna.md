# concert - Virgin Tour (Madonna)

## curiosities
**Beastie Boys as Opening Act (1985)** : In 1985, Madonna, then America's biggest pop star, invited the Beastie Boys to open dates on her "Virgin Tour." Although the audience of Madonna fans did not appreciate the Beastie Boys' "loud antics," this tour was a crucial "lucky break" that provided the trio with valuable promotion and MTV exposure.
