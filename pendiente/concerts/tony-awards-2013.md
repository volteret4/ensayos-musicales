# concert - Tony Awards (2013)

## curiosities
**Kinky Boots' Success and Cyndi Lauper's Win** : The musical "Kinky Boots" premiered on Broadway in April 2013, coinciding with that year's Tony Awards where it secured six prizes, including Best Musical and Best Score, the latter awarded solely to Cyndi Lauper. This made Lauper the first ever unaccompanied solo female composer to win the prize.
