# concert - Monterey Pop Festival (1967)

## curiosities
**Promotion via San Francisco (Be Sure to Wear Flowers in Your Hair)** : The song "San Francisco (Be Sure to Wear Flowers in Your Hair)" was specifically commissioned by producer Lou Adler and written by John Phillips to promote the Monterey Pop Festival, which they were organizing in 1967. This demonstrates an early example of a song functioning as a direct advertisement for a major music event.
