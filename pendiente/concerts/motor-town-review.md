# concert - Motor Town Review

## curiosities
**Live Multi-Act Tour (1962)** : Barry Gordy launched the Motor Town Review in 1962 as a live, multi-act concert tour that traveled across the country, following the tradition of the Chitlin Circuit. "Barry Gordy had launched a live, multi-act concert tour he called the Motor Town Review." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Everybody Say YEAH! Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Deep South Performances (1962)** : The tour sent Motown's artists, including Little Stevie Wonder, into the deep south, where "resistance to the visiting black performers by white residents was fierce." "Gordy even sent his stable of artists into the deep south, where resistance to the visiting black performers by white residents was fierce." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Everybody Say YEAH! Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**"Fingertips" Origin (1962)** : It was during a performance at Chicago's Regal Theatre in June 1962, as part of the Motor Town Review, that Little Stevie Wonder's improvised, extended rendition of "Fingertips" was recorded, a performance that Barry Gordy "stumbled across" and decided to release. "Recorded at Chicago's Regal Theatre in June 1962, it was supposed to be a set closing rendition of the Jazz Soul of Little Stevie Track fingertips." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Everybody Say YEAH!
