# concert - Super Bowl LIV Half-time Show

## curiosities
**First All-Latin American Performers (2020)** : The Super Bowl LIV half-time show in 2020 was a culturally historic event, being the first ever devoted exclusively to music by Latin American performers. Led by Shakira and headlined by Jennifer Lopez, the spectacle proudly celebrated Latin heritage, with JLo notably waving a Puerto Rican flag during her performance.
