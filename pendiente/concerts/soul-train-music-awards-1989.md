# concert - Soul Train Music Awards (1989)

## curiosities
**Whitney Houston Booing Incident** : During the 1989 Soul Train Music Awards, at the peak of her popularity, Whitney Houston was booed by a significant portion of the audience when she was nominated. This moment, captured on film, became known as the "Zapruder film of Whitney Houston lore" and is a subject of discussion in documentaries about her career.
