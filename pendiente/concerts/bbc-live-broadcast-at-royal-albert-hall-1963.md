# concert - BBC live broadcast at Royal Albert Hall (1963)

## curiosities
**Meeting Jane Asher** : In April 1963, The Beatles performed at a BBC live broadcast at the Royal Albert Hall, where they met 17-year-old London actress Jane Asher. This meeting initiated Paul McCartney's five-year courtship with Asher and his subsequent close relationship with her family, which benefited Peter and Gordon.
