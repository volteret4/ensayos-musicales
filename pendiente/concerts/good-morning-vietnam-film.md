# concert - Good Morning Vietnam (Film)

## curiosities
**Revival of "What A Wonderful World" (1987)** : The 1987 Robin Williams military comedy film "Good Morning Vietnam" prominently featured Louis Armstrong's "What A Wonderful World," reviving the song's popularity more than 16 years after Armstrong's death and leading to its reissue and first-time entry into the US Hot 100.
