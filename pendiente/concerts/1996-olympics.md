## curiosities
**"Macarena" Dance Appearance** : The dance craze associated with Los del Río's "Macarena" was so pervasive by the summer of 1996 that it made appearances at major global events, including the 1996 Olympics. This highlights the song's widespread cultural impact beyond just music charts.
