# concert - Late Night with David Letterman

## curiosities
**R.E.M.'s National TV Debut** : R.E.M. made their national television debut on NBC's "Late Night with David Letterman" on October 6, 1983. During their appearance, they performed a new, unnamed song that would later become "South Central Rain" and were introduced by Letterman, who mentioned their album "Murmur" was named one of the five best released so far in 1983 by the Los Angeles Times.
