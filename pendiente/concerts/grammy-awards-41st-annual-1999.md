# concert - Grammy Awards (41st annual, 1999)

## curiosities
**Madonna's First Recording Grammy** : At the 41st annual Grammy Awards in February 1999, Madonna took home her first Grammy ever for a recording (she had previously only won for a concert video), accepting the Best Pop Album prize for "Ray of Light" and expressing her thrill, sharing the stage with William Orbit.
