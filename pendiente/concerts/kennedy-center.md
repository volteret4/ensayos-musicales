# concert - Kennedy Center

## curiosities
**Neverland Workshop (1977)** : In the spring of 1977, the Kennedy Center in Washington, D.C., hosted a work-in-progress workshop of "Neverland," the stage show Jim Steinman had originally intended *Bat Out of Hell* to be, featuring Ellen Foley singing lead on songs like "Heaven Can Wait." "Jim Steinman actually staged Neverland, the show that Bad Out of Hell was originally supposed to be.
