# concert - Club MTV tour

## curiosities
**MTV's First Package Tour (1989)** : Launched by MTV's dance music show, Club MTV, this was the channel's first package tour. Millie Vanille was invited to join, signifying their rise to "superstar level opportunities." Other acts on the bill included Tone Loc, Information Society, and Paula Abdul. "Before the summer was over, they had been invited out on the road by MTV. **Site of Infamous Milli Vanilli Incident** : The Club MTV tour, intended as a high point in Milli Vanilli's career, became the location where an infamous incident involving the group transpired. **Millie Vanilley Lip-Sync Glitch (July 21, 1989)** : On July 21, 1989, at a Club MTV Tour date in Bristol, Connecticut, Millie Vanilley experienced a public lip-syncing glitch when their backing track got stuck in a loop. Despite the moment being captured on tape and becoming damning evidence, the live audience was unfazed at the time.
