# concert - Coach Shella Music Festival (2014)

## curiosities
**Outcast Reunion Headlining Performance** : As a highlight of their brief 2014 reunion tour, Outcast headlined the "blockbuster" Coach Shella Music Festival in Indio, California. During their performance, Big Boi echoed Andre 3000's sentiment from the 2004 Grammys by shouting out to "the fans who truly remembered Outcast from back in the day," while playing their 1993 single "Players Ball."
