# concert - Soul Train Awards

## curiosities
**Meeting of Whitney Houston and Bobby Brown (1989)** : Whitney Houston and Bobby Brown first met at the Soul Train Awards in April 1989. This particular evening was notable as Brown's LA and Babyface-composed song "Every Little Step" held the number one R&B spot in America.
