# concert - Ghost (Film)

## curiosities
**"Unchained Melody" as Cultural Phenomenon (1990)** : The 1990 romantic drama "Ghost," a top summer blockbuster, prominently featured The Righteous Brothers' "Unchained Melody." This feature sent the song back up the charts and firmly cemented its association with the early 90s for post-Boomer generations, transforming it into a cultural phenomenon.
