## curiosities
**Performance Circuit for Black Acts and Audiences** : In 1956, rock and roll acts primarily played the Chitlin' Circuit, which was dedicated to black acts performing for black audiences, highlighting the segregated nature of the music industry at the time. "rock and roll acts played the Chipplin circuit, and that was black acts for black audiences." ← Episode 40： ＂Drugstore Rock 'n' Roll＂ by Janis Martin | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0i2p9TelN0
**Tour Stops for Black Artists** : The Chitlin' Circuit was a derogatory but commonly used term referring to a network of clubs and theaters that served as regular tour destinations for nearly all major black artists of the era. Lucky Millinder's band, among others, occasionally toured this circuit. "the rather derogatory name for the clubs and theatres that were regular tour stops for almost all major black artists at the time." ← Episode 7： ＂Good Rockin' Tonight＂ by Wynonie Harris | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgqSPoYRUCM
**Black-Friendly Performance Venues** : The Motor Town Review, launched by Barry Gordy, traveled across the country in the "great tradition" of the so-called Chitlin Circuit, which comprised black-friendly performance venues.
