# concert - Eurovision Song Contest (1974)

## curiosities
**Olivia Newton-John's Participation** : Olivia Newton-John represented the UK at the 1974 Eurovision Song Contest, performing "Long Live Love" and placing fourth. The contest was notably won by ABBA for Sweden with "Waterloo."
