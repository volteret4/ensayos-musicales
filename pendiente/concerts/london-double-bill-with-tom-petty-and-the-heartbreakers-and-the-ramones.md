# concert - London Double Bill with Tom Petty and The Heartbreakers and The Ramones

## curiosities
**Glenn Tilbrook's Experience (1977)** : Glenn Tilbrook of Squeeze recalled seeing Tom Petty and The Heartbreakers on a double bill with The Ramones in London in 1977, describing the experience as "absolutely fantastic" due to the punk energy combined with musical craft and driving energy of both bands.
