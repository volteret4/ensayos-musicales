**Headline-Grabbing Performances** : Held in Brooklyn, New York, the awards ceremony was notable for its headline-grabbing performances, particularly a controversial pairing of Robin Thicke and Miley Cyrus. Thicke performed "Blurred Lines," while Cyrus performed "We Can't Stop," accompanied by her infamous attempt at twerking.
