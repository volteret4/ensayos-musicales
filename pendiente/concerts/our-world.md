# concert - Our World

## curiosities
**First Global Live Satellite Broadcast (1967)** : "Our World" was the first-ever global live televised satellite broadcast, watched by some 600 million people worldwide in 1967. The Beatles performed John Lennon's "All You Need Is Love" during this broadcast, strategically using it as a platform to create and market a hit song.
