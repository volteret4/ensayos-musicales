# concert - Fire Aid Benefit

## curiosities
**Nirvana Performance with Female Vocalists (2025)** : The acclaimed Rock Hall performances of Nirvana with female vocalists were repeated 11 years later, in 2025, at the Fire Aid Benefit in Los Angeles. This continued to honor the band's departed leader's gender-fluid and feminist ideals.
