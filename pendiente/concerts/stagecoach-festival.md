# concert - Stagecoach Festival

## curiosities
**Lil Nas X Performance (2019)** : Lil Nas X appeared at the Stagecoach Festival in 2019, performing "Old Town Road" in his cowboy outfit. This was part of his strategy to keep the song cross-cultural and viral, alongside appearances at sporting events, awards shows, and even an elementary school.
