## curiosities
**Early Buzz for "Everybody Dance"** : This local New York club played a crucial role in generating early excitement for Sheik's first recording, "Everybody Dance." Before its official release on vinyl, the club's DJ began spinning a lacquer test pressing, and the crowd's enthusiastic shouts repeatedly demanded more. When Nile Rogers visited and revealed his involvement, he was treated "like a king." "local New York club The Night Owl began spinning a lacquer test pressing.
