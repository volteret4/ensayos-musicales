# concert - Adelphi Ballroom

## curiosities
**Hall & Oates' First Meeting (1967)** : The Adelphi Ballroom in West Philly was the improbable location where Darryl Hall and John Oates first met in 1967. They were both scheduled to perform with their respective bands (The Temp Tones and The Masters) at a multi-act radio showcase when a gang fight erupted backstage, leading them to meet while seeking safety.
