# concert - Grammy Awards (41st Annual, February 1999)

## curiosities
**Madonna's First Grammy for a Recording** : In February 1999, at the 41st Annual Grammy Awards, Madonna received her first-ever Grammy for a recording, having only previously won for a concert video. She was visibly thrilled while accepting the Best Pop Album prize for "Ray of Light," and brought her "shy British collaborator," William Orbit, on stage with her.
