# concert - Soul Train Music Awards

## curiosities
**Whitney Houston Booing Incident (1989)** : At the 1989 Soul Train Music Awards, Whitney Houston was booed by the audience simply for being nominated, despite being at her pop chart peak. This incident unexpectedly galvanized Houston, leading to a significant career comeback and greater success, particularly with Black audiences.
