# concert - Wembley Arena

## curiosities
**George Michael's Cover to Cover Tour Final Show (March 23, 1991)** : The final performance of George Michael's "Cover to Cover" tour took place at Wembley Arena in London on March 23, 1991, featuring a surprise guest appearance by Elton John.
