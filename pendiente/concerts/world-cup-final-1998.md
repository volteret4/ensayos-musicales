# concert - World Cup Final 1998

## curiosities
**Ricky Martin's "The Cup of Life" Performance** : Ricky Martin performed "La Copa de la Vida" (The Cup of Life), the official theme song of the 1998 World Cup, in France's National Stadium at the World Cup Final on July 12, 1998. This performance was broadcast globally, reaching over a billion viewers and providing Martin with a massive international platform.
