# concert - CMA Awards

## curiosities
**Beyoncé and The Chicks Performance (2016)** : The 2016 CMA Awards featured a performance by Beyoncé, who was joined by country trio The Chicks, to perform her Texas-celebrating country and western-indebted song "Daddy Lessons." This performance sparked cultural debate and contributed to the percolating conversation around the history of the black cowboy, which the #yeehawagenda later amplified.
