**Greatest All-Star Performance (March 2004)** : The March 2004 Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony is widely considered to have featured the greatest all-star performance in its three-decade history. This performance was of George Harrison's "While My Guitar Gently Weeps," led by Tom Petty and featuring a fiery, three-minute guitar solo by Prince. **Inductees and Eligibility (March 2004)** : The March 2004 ceremony saw Prince inducted in his first year of eligibility, 25 years after his first recording. Other inductees like Jackson Browne, The Dells, Bob Seger, Traffic, and ZZ Top had been eligible for a decade or more before their belated induction, while George Harrison was inducted posthumously for his post-Beatles work, two years after his death.
