# concert - Source Awards

## curiosities
**East Coast vs. West Coast Feud (1995)** : The 1995 Source Awards in New York City became a flashpoint for the escalating East Coast versus West Coast rap feud, serving as the event where tensions reached a peak. The New York audience repeatedly booed West Coast artists, leading to fiery acceptance speeches, most notably by Suge Knight, who dissed Puff Daddy and invited Tupac Shakur to join Death Row Records. **Outcast's Booing and "The South Got Something To Say" (1995)** : Outcast, despite winning the Best New Rap Group award at the 1995 Source Awards, were booed by the New York audience, who were caught up in the East Coast-West Coast rap tensions. In response, Andre Benjamin delivered a profoundly prophetic speech, concluding with the iconic line, "The South Got Something To Say," which became a rallying cry and cemented Southern rap's future influence.
