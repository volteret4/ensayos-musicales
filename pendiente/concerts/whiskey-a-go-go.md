# concert - Whiskey-A-Go-Go

## curiosities
**Otis Redding's Advocacy for Black Artists (1966)** : During a 1966 performance at the Whiskey-A-Go-Go in Hollywood, Otis Redding paused his "brilliant tense, kind of hard driving set of hits" to ask the audience, "Do you see how hard we have to work to eat? Do you see how hard we have to work?" This moment highlighted his concern for the conditions of black musicians.
