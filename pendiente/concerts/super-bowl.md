# concert - Super Bowl

## curiosities
**Whitney Houston's Legendary Anthem (1991)** : Whitney Houston delivered a globally televised and now legendary performance of "The Star-Spangled Banner" at Super Bowl XXV in early 1991. The performance, though pre-recorded, gained outsized popularity, often attributed to its timing coinciding with the January 1991 launch of the first Persian Gulf War, a moment that resonated with American pride and jingoism. **Whitney Houston's 1991 Performance of The Star Spangled Banner** : Whitney Houston's recording for the 1991 Super Bowl became a legendary rendition of the national anthem. It returned to the Hot 100 after the 9/11 attacks in 2001, peaking at number six, and was her last top 10 hit. The version lasted 165 seconds and was famously recorded in 4/4 time, an alteration from the original 3/4 waltz time. **Alicia Keys' 2013 Performance of The Star Spangled Banner** : Alicia Keys holds the record for the longest version of "The Star Spangled Banner" performed at the Super Bowl, with her 2013 piano rendition clocking in at 155 seconds. This is notably shorter than Whitney Houston's version (165 seconds), despite Houston's version having an added beat per measure.
