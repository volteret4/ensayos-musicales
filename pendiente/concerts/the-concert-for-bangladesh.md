# concert - The Concert for Bangladesh

## curiosities
**Eric Clapton's Shared Grammy Credit (1973)** : Eric Clapton shared credit for the 1973 Album of the Year Grammy for "The Concert for Bangladesh," which was a George Harrison project. This was one of the few instances where Clapton received Grammy recognition for his output prior to his "Unplugged" sweep in 1993. **Groundbreaking Benefit Event (1971)** : Organized by George Harrison at Madison Square Garden in New York in August 1971, "The Concert for Bangladesh" was the first large-scale benefit concert of its kind, essentially "inventing the form nearly a decade and a half before livin." It brought together a half dozen superstars and generated funds for Bangladeshi refugees, ultimately raising some $12 million through the concert, a documentary film, and a triple album. **Fundraising Impact (1971-1973)** : Over a year and a half after the concert, its offshoot projects, including a documentary film (a box office hit in 1972) and a triple album (Billboard number two, 1973 Grammy Album of the Year), continued to generate funds for Bangladeshi refugees. It raised a quarter million dollars in the first year and ultimately accumulated some $12 million. **Grammy Outlier (1973)** : Winning Album of the Year at the 1973 Grammys, "The Concert for Bangladesh" was considered an odd choice, especially for the 1970s, which favored "consensus monocultural Grammy wins" for pop blockbusters. It was a modest seller, lacked big radio hits, and had already been off the charts for months by Grammy Night, contrasting sharply with other million-selling, chart-topping winners of the decade. The Recording Academy was seen as rewarding "Harrison's good intentions and his cavalcade of stars." **Seminal Cause-Supporting Event (1971)** : Organized by George Harrison in the latter half of 1971, the Concert for Bangladesh was a "seminal event in the history of cause supporting music." It spawned a triple LP which won the Grammy for Album of the Year. **Shared Grammy for Album of the Year (1973)** : Bob Dylan shared in the 1973 Album of the Year prize for George Harrison and Friends' "Concert for Bangladesh." This marked one of Dylan's early Grammy wins, though not as a solo artist for his own material, highlighting his participation in a significant charity event.
