# concert - The Concert for Bangladesh

## curiosities
**Eric Clapton's Shared Grammy Credit (1973)** : Eric Clapton shared credit for the 1973 Album of the Year Grammy for "The Concert for Bangladesh," which was a George Harrison project. This was one of the few instances where Clapton received Grammy recognition for his output prior to his "Unplugged" sweep in 1993.
