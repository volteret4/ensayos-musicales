# concert - Giant Stadium

## curiosities
**The Cure and Love and Rockets Performance (Summer 1989)** : In the summer of 1989, The Cure and Love and Rockets toured together and played at New Jersey's Giant Stadium. This performance was seen as matching Depeche Mode's earlier feat at the Rose Bowl.
