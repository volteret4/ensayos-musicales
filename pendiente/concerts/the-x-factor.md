## curiosities
**Talent Competition Show** : "The X Factor" is a TV talent competition show where Simon Cowell and Nicole Scherzinger famously formed the boy band One Direction on its British version. Cowell later attempted the same gambit on the American edition, forming the girl group Fifth Harmony in 2012 from solo vocalists.
