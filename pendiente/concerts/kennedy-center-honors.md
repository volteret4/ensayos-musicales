**Aretha Franklin's Performance for Carole King (2015)** : In 2015, Aretha Franklin performed at the Kennedy Center Honors to celebrate honorary Carole King. Franklin's performance of "(You Make Me Feel Like) A Natural Woman," where she sat at the piano then rose and dropped her fur coat, became a "quintessential Aritha Franklin moment," moving both King and President Obama. "It happened in 2015 at the Kennedy Center Honors, while Obama was still President. It was not actually an honorary that night. She'd already received the Kennedy Center Honors back in 1994 under Bill Clinton's presidency.
