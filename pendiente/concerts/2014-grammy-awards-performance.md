# concert - 2014 Grammy Awards Performance

## curiosities
**Medley Performance (Staples Center)** : At the 2014 Grammy Awards held at the Staples Center, Duffbunk, Ferel Williams, Nile Rodgers, and special guest Stevie Wonder performed a medley that included "Get Lucky," Stevie Wonder's "Another Star," and a few bars of Chic's "Le Freak."
