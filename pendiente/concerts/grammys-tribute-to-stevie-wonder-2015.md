# concert - Grammys tribute to Stevie Wonder (2015)

## curiosities
**Celebration of an Icon** : In 2015, the National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences organized a televised special, the "Grammys tribute to Stevie Wonder", to honor one of their most decorated artists, featuring performances by contemporary superstars like Farell Williams, Annie Lennox, Ariana Grande, Lady Gaga, and Beyoncé Knowles.
