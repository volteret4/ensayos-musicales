# concert - Sphere (Las Vegas)

## curiosities
**First Band Residency (2023-2024)** : U2 earned the distinction of being the "first band to perform" in the new, immersive Las Vegas concert venue known as Sphere. Their 40-concert residency, which extended through early March 2024, incorporated cutting-edge technologies like "video projections on the venue's massive wrap-around screen, beam forming and wave field synthesis audio technologies," marking yet "another technical first for U2."
