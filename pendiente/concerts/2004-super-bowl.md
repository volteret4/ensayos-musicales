## curiosities
**Inspiration for YouTube** : The "Nipplegate controversy" involving Justin Timberlake and Janet Jackson at the 2004 Super Bowl served as the initial inspiration for former PayPal employees to create YouTube. The widespread difficulty in finding playable video of the incident online after it was scrubbed from broadcast TV highlighted the need for an easy video-sharing platform.
