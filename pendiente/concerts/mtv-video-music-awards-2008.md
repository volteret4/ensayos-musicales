# concert - MTV Video Music Awards (2008)

## curiosities
**Taylor Swift's Red Carpet Appearance** : In September 2008, Taylor Swift was invited by Big Machine Records to work the red carpet at the MTV Video Music Awards, marking a notable interaction between the youth-oriented channel and a country star. At the event, Swift interviewed then-bigger stars Katy Perry and Miley Cyrus, all three being nominated for Best New Artist. This moment is seen as ironic given Swift's later feud with Perry.
