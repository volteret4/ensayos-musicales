# concert - March on Washington (1963)

## curiosities
**Peter, Paul and Mary's Performance** : On August 28, 1963, Peter, Paul and Mary performed their cover of Bob Dylan's "Blowin' in the Wind" live at the March on Washington. The single was still in the top five at the time, and this exposure significantly benefited Dylan's own "Free Wheelin'" LP.
