# concert - Amnesty International's Conspiracy of Hope Tour

## curiosities
**Co-headlined by Socially Conscious Stars (1986)** : In 1986, Sting co-headlined this tour alongside other socially conscious stars such as U2 and Peter Gabriel. For the tour, Sting also briefly reunited The Police for some dates.
