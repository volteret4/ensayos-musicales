# concert - Copacabana Nightclub

## curiosities
**Career Redemption** : In July 1964, Sam Cook triumphantly returned to the Copacabana Nightclub, known as the "Copa," more than six years after a previous performance there had "bombed." His rapturously received set at this iconic venue was later issued as the live album "Live At The Copa," marking a final career triumph and closing a significant chapter for him.
