# concert - Budokan Arena

## curiosities
**Chic Performance and Bernard Edwards' Last Gig (1996)** : In April 1996, Nile Rodgers was invited to perform at the legendary Budokan Arena in Tokyo, bringing along Chic members, including Bernard Edwards, who played his legendary basslines despite being gravely ill, making it his final performance.
