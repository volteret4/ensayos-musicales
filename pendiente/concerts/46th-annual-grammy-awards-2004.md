# concert - 46th Annual Grammy Awards (2004)

## curiosities
**Outcast's Album of the Year Win** : At the 46th annual Grammy Awards in Los Angeles, Andre Benjamin and Antoine Patton (Outcast) achieved a career pinnacle by winning the Grammy for Album of the Year for *Speaker Box, Slash, The Love Below*. This made them only the second hip-hop act ever to receive the National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences' top prize, following Lauren Hill five years prior.
