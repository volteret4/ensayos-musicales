# genre - Caribbean style Euro Disco

## curiosities
**Christmas Chart Success (1970s)** : The German-Jamaican vocal group Boney M transformed Harry Belafonte's gentle Calypso "Mary's Boy Child" into a Caribbean-style Euro disco track. This reinterpretation helped the song shoot to number one in December 1978, where it stayed for the entire month, demonstrating the genre's appeal for Christmas hits.
