**Path to Country Radio Acceptability (2000s-2010s)** : The trajectory from Bubba Sparxxx to Colt Ford to Jason Aldean illustrated that a particular type of rap became acceptable in country music. This acceptance was facilitated when Hick Hop increasingly distanced itself "from actual rap production and black co-creators like Timbaland," making it easier to register on country radio.
