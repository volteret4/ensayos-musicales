# genre - Orchestral Pop

## curiosities
**"What A Wonderful World" as Ballad** : Louis Armstrong's "What A Wonderful World" is described as an "orchestral pop ballad about the wonders of existence," marking a stylistic departure as Armstrong provided only vocals, without his signature trumpet.
