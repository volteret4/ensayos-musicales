# genre - Celtic Folk Punk

## curiosities
**Unexpected Christmas Ballad Success (1987)** : The Pogues, known as a "boozy, rollicking Celtic folk punk act," were not typically associated with balladry. However, their collaboration with Kirsty MacColl on the Christmas ballad "Fairytale of New York" in 1987 became their best-known song and one of the most beloved British songs of the last three decades, peaking at number two for Christmas.
