# genre - Club Music

## curiosities
**Synergy with Digital Tools** : Club music, along with electronic music, found a synergy with digital tools due to the technical precision these tools offer. **Cyndi Lauper's Rejuvenation** : Cyndi Lauper embraced and delved deeper into club music in the late 2000s, leading to significant chart success with her 2008 album "Bring You To The Brink," which produced two number one singles on Billboard's Club Play Chart. This demonstrated her versatility and appeal in the genre.
