**Targeting Multiple Audiences (1999)** : Latin house tracks, like Jennifer Lopez's "Waiting For Tonight," were strategically aimed at diverse audiences, including top 40 radio, Spanish-language stations, and club DJs with specialized remixes, showcasing an early form of multi-format promotional blitz.
