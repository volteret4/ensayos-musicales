# genre - Smooth Jazz

## curiosities
**Joe Jackson's Radio Success** : In the summer of 1984, at the height of synth pop, Joe Jackson successfully managed to get smooth jazz on the radio with his single "You Can't Get What You Want (Till You Know What You Want)." This achievement showcased his genre-bending capabilities and commercial appeal.
