# genre - Metal Grunge

## curiosities
**Green Jellÿ's Unique Novelty** : The metal grunge hybrid band Green Jellÿ was unique as the only hard rock act to truly attempt a pure novelty single. Their thrashy fairy tale "Three Little Pigs" became a top 20 single in early 1993, selling a half-million copies with little radio airplay.
