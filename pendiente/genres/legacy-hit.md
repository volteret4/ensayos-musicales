# genre - Legacy Hit

## curiosities
**Definition and Criteria** : A legacy hit is a song that originally underperformed on the charts (missing the top 10, top 40, or even the entire Hot 100) but has since become one of the first songs associated with an artist. For the purpose of this countdown, qualifying songs peaked below the Top 25 on the Hot 100 (most below the Top 40) and achieved a certain modern threshold of radio airplay or streaming ubiquity.
