# genre - Rootsy folk rock

## curiosities
**John Mellencamp's Imperial Capital** : During his imperial peak, John Mellencamp utilized his "cultural capital" to record "The Lonesome Jubilee" (1987), incorporating folk and country instruments, and successfully placed "rootsy folk rock" like "Paper in Fire" on pop radio, which was otherwise dominated by hair metal and dance pop.
