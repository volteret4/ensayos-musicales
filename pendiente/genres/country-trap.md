# genre - Country Trap

## curiosities
**Merging Country Tropes with Rap** : Country Trap emerged as a distinct sound by the mid-2010s, characterized by the cross-pollination of hip-hop and country music. Pioneered by artists like Young Thug, it intentionally blended country tropes and twang into rap jams, paving the way for hits like "Old Town Road."
