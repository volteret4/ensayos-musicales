# genre - Girl Group

## curiosities
**Lyrical Innovation by Gerry Goffin** : With "Will You Love Me Tomorrow," Gerry Goffin found a lyrical formula that would define girl groups from that point on, introducing a kind of "female adolescent emotion" that had previously been absent from pop music. The lyrics from a woman's perspective worrying about a one-night stand resonated with millions.
