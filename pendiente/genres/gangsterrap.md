# genre - Gangsterrap

## curiosities
**Early 90s Era** : Gangsterrap was a prominent genre in the early 90s. Ace of Base's "Beautiful Life" acted as a musical bridge, connecting this era with the later rise of boy bands and "bling bling" culture in the late 90s.
