# genre - Bro Country

## curiosities
**Era Defined by Interchangeable Hits** : The "Bro Country Era" in the early 2010s was characterized by "testosterone-fueled hits" from artists like Jason Aldean, Jake Owen, and the "King of the Bros," Luke Bryan. These songs featured "cookie-cutter lyrics about trucks, boozy lake parties, and pretty women in cutoff shorts," making them sound "interchangeable" and contributing to a male-dominated country radio landscape.
