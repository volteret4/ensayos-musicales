# genre - Rocksteady

## curiosities
**Bridge Genre (Mid to Late 1960s)** : Rocksteady was the predominant Jamaican music of the mid to late 1960s, serving as a bridge between the more frenetic, up-tempo Ska of the early 60s and the smokeier, groovier, more "woke," and ultimately more durable Reggae genre. It is characterized by a steady, gentle, mid-tempo pulse.
