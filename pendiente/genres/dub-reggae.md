# genre - Dub Reggae

## curiosities
**Joe Jackson's Exploration** : Joe Jackson explored Dub Reggae more deeply than ever before on his late 1980 album "Beat Crazy." The title track showcased this stylistic direction, demonstrating Jackson's willingness to experiment with genres far removed from his initial punk-influenced new wave sound.
