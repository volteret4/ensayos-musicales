## curiosities
**Chuck Brown's Pioneering Role** : Chuck Brown, leader of The Soul Searchers, is identified as a pioneer of Washington, D.C. GoGo music. His band's 1974 jam "Ashley's Roach clip" featured a drum break that became hugely influential in rap history.
