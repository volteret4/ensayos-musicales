# genre - 60s girl group Pop

## curiosities
**Influence on Springsteen and Ramones** : This genre was a shared influence between Bruce Springsteen and Joey Ramone, with Ramone particularly fond of it, leading The Ramones to cover The Ronettes and influencing Springsteen's songwriting for the group.
