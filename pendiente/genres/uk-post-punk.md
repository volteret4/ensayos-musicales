# genre - UK Post-punk

## curiosities
**Golden Age on US Charts (Late 80s/Early 90s)** : The late 1980s and early 1990s marked the peak popularity for UK Post-punk in America, especially on alternative rock radio stations, before the "watershed peetered out." **Slow US Emergence** : UK post-punk bands experienced a slow and gradual emergence as US hitmakers, often taking a decade or more to achieve significant chart success. This contrasts with their earlier breakthroughs on British charts in the late 1970s and early 1980s.
