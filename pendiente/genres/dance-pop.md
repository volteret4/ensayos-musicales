**Kraftwerk's Early Contributions** : Kraftwerk's albums "Trans-Europe Express" and "The Man-Machine" produced foundational dance pop tracks like "Trans-Europe Express," "Showroom Dummies," "The Model," and "The Robots." These tracks featured repetitive patterns, simple melodies, and mechanical grooves, making electronic music universally danceable.
