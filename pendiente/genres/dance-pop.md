# genre - Dance Pop

## curiosities
**Billy Ocean's Style** : Billy Ocean's "Get Out of My Dreams, Get Into My Car" was described as "Clattery Dance Pop with Husky Soul Vocals," demonstrating the energetic and kitschy elements of the genre as produced by Mut Lang. **Kraftwerk's Early Contributions** : Kraftwerk's albums "Trans-Europe Express" and "The Man-Machine" produced foundational dance pop tracks like "Trans-Europe Express," "Showroom Dummies," "The Model," and "The Robots." These tracks featured repetitive patterns, simple melodies, and mechanical grooves, making electronic music universally danceable. **Novelty Hits** : The late 1990s saw a wave of novelty dance pop hits, such as Aqua's "Barbie Girl" and Lou Bega's "Mambo No. 5," which, despite being "gleefully junky," achieved significant commercial success, often compelling album purchases due to the withholding of singles.
