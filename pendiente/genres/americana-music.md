## curiosities
**Revival by O Brother, Where Art Thou? Soundtrack (2002)** : The soundtrack to *O Brother, Where Art Thou?* reached number one in early 2002 and was credited with doing for Americana music what the *Deliverance* soundtrack had previously done for Bluegrass in the early 1970s, bringing the genre to wider prominence.
