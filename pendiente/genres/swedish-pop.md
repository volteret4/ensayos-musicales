# genre - Swedish Pop

## curiosities
**1994 Chart Coup (1994)** : In 1994, Ace of Base's success with "The Sign" represented a "coup" for Swedish pop, dominating Billboard's year-end charts at a time when grunge and gangsta rap were at their peak. This conquest "set the stage for the takeover of Western Pop by Swedish songwriters, led by Max Martin later in the decade." **English Factor in Songwriting** : The history of Swedish pop shows that a less-than-perfect grasp of idiomatic English by songwriters can lead to "happy accidents" in lyrics, resulting in unique and memorable pop refrains. Examples include ABBA's "All That She Wants Is Another Baby" and Roxette's "Hello You Fool I Love You Come and Join the Joy Ride."
