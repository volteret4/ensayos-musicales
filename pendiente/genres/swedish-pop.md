# genre - Swedish Pop

## curiosities
**1994 Chart Coup (1994)** : In 1994, Ace of Base's success with "The Sign" represented a "coup" for Swedish pop, dominating Billboard's year-end charts at a time when grunge and gangsta rap were at their peak. This conquest "set the stage for the takeover of Western Pop by Swedish songwriters, led by Max Martin later in the decade."
