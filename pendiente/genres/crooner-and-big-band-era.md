# genre - Crooner and Big Band Era

## curiosities
**Simultaneous Charting of Multiple Versions (Pre-Rock)** : During the Crooner and Big Band era, prior to the rise of rock and roll, it was a common occurrence for a popular song to appear on the charts in several different recordings at virtually the same time, as seen with "To Each His Own" in 1946.
