# genre - Rock and Soul

## curiosities
**Hall & Oates' Signature Blend** : This genre, a seamless blend of black and white sounds, was championed by Darryl Hall and John Oates, who believed it had universal appeal and could effortlessly cross over across the radio dial. Their success validated this concept, making it a defining sound of the 80s and beyond.
