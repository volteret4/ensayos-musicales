# genre - Jazz Pop

## curiosities
**Remade for Christmas Number One (2001)** : The jazz-pop bolero "Something Stupid," originally by Frank and Nancy Sinatra, was remade as a duet by Robbie Williams and Nicole Kidman. This remake took the UK Christmas number one title in 2001, demonstrating the enduring appeal of classic song structures when reinterpreted by contemporary artists for the holiday season.
