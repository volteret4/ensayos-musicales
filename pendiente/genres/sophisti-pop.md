# genre - Sophisti-pop

## curiosities
**Evolution of New Wave Synth Pop** : By 1984 and 1985, New Wave Synth Pop began morphing into Sophisti-pop, a new blend characterized by soul, cocktail jazz, and lavish production, exemplified by songs like Wham!'s "Careless Whisper" and Sade's "Smooth Operator."
