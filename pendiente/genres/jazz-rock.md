# genre - Jazz Rock

## curiosities
**Early Attempt at Classification** : "Jazz Rock" was one of the terms people tried to use in the 1970s to define the "smooth music" that would later be categorized as Yacht Rock. The term, along with others like "Revlon Rock" and "West Coast," failed to effectively capture the essence of the genre. "I go back and read articles from the 70s and there's all sorts of terms that people are trying to throw onto this music from jazz rock to like Revlon Rock, some other stuff that's just nuts and just doesn't work." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Yacht Or Nyacht | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
