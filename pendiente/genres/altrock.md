# genre - Altrock

## curiosities
**"Broification" Trend** : By the summer of 1999, if one listened to an Altrock station, they were "much more likely to hear Aguilera's rockers limp biscuit... than anything by a Lilith performer." This reflected the accelerating "broification" of rock radio during Lilith Fair's original run, where bands like Limp Bizkit, Korn, Godsmack, Kid Rock, and Creed dominated.
