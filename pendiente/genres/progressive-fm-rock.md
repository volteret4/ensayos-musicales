# genre - Progressive FM Rock

## curiosities
**Sustained Airplay for "Nights in White Satin"** : In the early days of progressive FM rock radio, The Moody Blues' "Nights in White Satin" never truly faded away, even after its initial pop single release flopped. This sustained airplay allowed for a successful reissue years later as audiences and programmers became accustomed to progressive and psychedelic sounds.
