# genre - Fusion Funk

## curiosities
**Last Exit's Genre** : Last Exit, formed by Sting and Jerry Richardson, played a style of "jazzy fusion funk," a departure from the conservative repertoire of their previous band, the Newcastle Big Band, as they sought to play more modern rock-era jazz. "So Last Exit played a kind of jazzy fusion funk.
