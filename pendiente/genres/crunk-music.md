# genre - Crunk Music

## curiosities
**Origin of Snap Music** : Crunk Music is the genre from which Snap Music, an Atlanta-based hip-hop subgenre, is an offshoot. Snap Music took the essence of rap, reducing it to simpler, digital elements suitable for ringtones. "An Atlanta-based offshoot of Crunk Music, Snap was rap reduced to its digital essence, an 808-thump, a bit of high-hat, some snapping, of course, and chanting vocals, the simpler, the better." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Ride ’til I Can’t No More Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce88
