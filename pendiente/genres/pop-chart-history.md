**1971 as a Pivotal Year** : The year 1971 is described as an "embarrassment of riches" with several artists releasing acclaimed LPs, some serving as a "last will and testament." Its singles charts were "pretty stellar," making it "one of the most varied, widest, ranging, and even experimental years for hits ever." The year saw the 1960s "arguably finally came to artistic fruition in the early 70s," fueled by "revolutionary consciousness" and "amazing music." Over the last decade, 1971 has emerged as a "favorite focus of music fans, critics, even filmmakers," with consensus coalescing around it as a pivotal year.
