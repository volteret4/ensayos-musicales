# genre - Wall of Sound

## curiosities
**Influence on Wizzard's Music** : The "wall of sound" production technique, famously associated with Phil Spector, was a significant influence on the glam rock band Wizzard. Their hits blended the 70s crunch of glam with the brassy pop and wall of sound of older singles, making them particularly well-suited for recording Christmas songs with a classic feel. **Phil Spector's Signature Production** : The "Wall of Sound" is the signature production technique famously developed by legendary producer and songwriter Phil Spector, known for its dense, orchestral, and often reverberant texture that created a cinematic depth in pop music.
