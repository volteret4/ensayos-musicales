# genre - Electro-funk

## curiosities
**Dominant in Early Memphis Clubs** : In the early Memphis club scene, electro-funk from artists like Nucleus or Africa Bambaataa was the primary music for dancing before the emergence of New York hip-hop and the fusion efforts of DJ Spanish Fly. **Mainstreaming by Ronson and Mars** : Mark Ronson and Bruno Mars were instrumental in reintroducing and mainstreaming the electro-funk sound for pop audiences through their mega-hit "Uptown Funk." This particular sound had largely only charted within R&B in its heyday, especially in the wake of the early 80s disco backlash, making its mainstream pop success with "Uptown Funk" a notable achievement. **Ready for the World Mistaken for Prince** : Flint, Michigan's Ready for the World topped the Hot 100 in 1985 with an electro-funk jam, "Oh Sheila," that many pop fans "mistook for Prince," highlighting the powerful influence of Prince and the Revolution's imperial phase.
