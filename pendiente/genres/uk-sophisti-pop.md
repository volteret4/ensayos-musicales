# genre - UK Sophisti-Pop

## curiosities
**Rise in Mid-1980s** : In 1984 and 1985, the "smoothest music on the charts" often originated from England, without a direct connection to the West Coast studio scene. This "jazzy cocktail pop" was its own breed, as smooth as peak Yacht Rock, but closer in origin to New Romantic and New Wave movements. "This was the moment for UK's Sepist Pop, like Shade.
