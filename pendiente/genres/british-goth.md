# genre - British Goth

## curiosities
**First Modern Rock Number 1 Song (1988)** : Siouxsie and the Banshees, a British Goth band fronted by Siouxsie Sioux, had the first Modern Rock number 1 song with "Peekaboo" when the chart launched in September 1988. This was notable as a goth-tinged song topping a Billboard chart.
