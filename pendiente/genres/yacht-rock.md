# genre - Yacht Rock

## curiosities
**Firefall as Yacht Rock (1977)** : Firefall's music, particularly during their Austin City Limits appearance in Season 2 (1977), is retrospectively categorized as "Yacht Rock" by contemporary observers, indicating a style distinct from traditional country music and showcasing Austin City Limits' early expansion beyond its roots. "Firefall is not country. I think today the kids would call it Yacht Rock, I think." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 49 - Austin City Limits and the 70s Country Music Revolution | http://ftr70.com
**1980s Grammy Controversy and Later Rehabilitation** : The Grammy wins for Christopher Cross's self-titled debut album (1981) and Toto's "Toto IV" (1983) were highly contentious in the 1980s, serving as "exhibits A and B of the recording academies out of touchness" according to rock critics. However, in "later generations, the rehabilitation of both Yacht Rock as music and Africa in particular among millennials and Gen Z make these wins seem somewhat less egregious now." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > And the Grammy Goes to… Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef0154465cd600c4e3a
