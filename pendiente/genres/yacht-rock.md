**Invented Genre (2000s)** : The genre name "Yacht Rock" was coined by four L.A.-based actors and writers in the 2000s for a comical video series. It describes a brand of real-life late 1970s and early 1980s smooth music that they imagined being played on high-end watercraft, and the invented genre subsequently "took the internet by storm." **Origin and Definition (1976-1984)** : Yacht Rock, a term coined by a 2005 Web TV series, describes a genre of "really smooth music" that dominated radio airwaves from approximately 1976 to 1984. It offered a definitive name to a type of music that critics and listeners had struggled to classify, replacing terms like jazz rock, Revlon Rock, West Coast, Soft Rock, or Retail Rock. "From 1976 to 1984, the radio airwaves were dominated by really smooth music, also known as Yacht Rock." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Yacht Or Nyacht | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Viral Spread and Cultural Impact** : The "Yacht Rock" web series, launched in 2005, became an early viral internet phenomenon on YouTube due to its strong storylines, characters, and cliffhanger endings, winning the Channel 101 competition. The term itself gained traction because it was evocative, Google-friendly, and provided a clear visual style (captain's hats, Hawaiian shirts) that made it accessible for tribute bands and fans to adopt. "I think the term caught on because it defined a kind of music that people had been struggling to define ever since it was being produced." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Yacht Or Nyacht | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Evolution of Sound** : Early Yacht Rock music tended to sound more like Boz Scaggs, while later Yacht Rock, especially after 1979-1980, was heavily influenced by the sound of Michael McDonald, the "What a Fool Believes" riff, and the production of Quincy Jones and Rod Temperton. This later phase often featured the "doobie bounce" and a distinct "yacht soul strain." "Yeah, earlier, yeah, rock tends to sound more like buzz, skags than Michael McDonald, like the first couple years and to up before what a full belief comes out." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Yacht Or Nyacht | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Misinterpretations and Visuals** : The literal interpretation of "Yacht Rock" led some to believe the music had to be about boats or seafaring, mistakenly including artists like Jimmy Buffett. The web series' portrayal of characters like Coco Goldstein, dressed in captain's hats and Hawaiian shirts, became an accessible and cheap "fashion template" for tribute bands and audiences, further popularizing the visual stereotype. "It was much easier to put on a captain's hat and listen to Brandy by looking glass and go, I'm doing Yacht Rock." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Yacht Or Nyacht | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Artistic Value and Permission to Enjoy** : Initially dismissed by traditional rock critics who valued "rebellious spirit," "high energy," and a "raw" sound, Yacht Rock's comedic portrayal in the web series shifted perception, giving people "permission" to openly enjoy the "very well crafted music." Many who approached it ironically eventually developed a genuine appreciation for its quality. "And making a comedy show out of it not only starts to change the perception around what this music is, but it's also at the same time sort of giving people permission." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Yacht Or Nyacht | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**R&B and Jazz Roots (Yacht Soul)** : The core of Yacht Rock, particularly the "yacht soul strain," is deeply rooted in Black R&B and jazz. Many foundational "white Yacht Rock guys" like Kenny Loggins, Michael McDonald, Steely Dan, and Toto grew up listening to these genres, and the same session musicians often played on both "white pop records" and R&B records by Black artists around 1979-1980, making it true "crossover music." "First of all, all the guys, all the white Yacht Rock guys that you first think of, Kenny Loggins, Michael McDonald, Steely Dan, all growing up, listen to Black R&B, Black jazz." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Yacht Or Nyacht | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

**Decline and End of Chart Dominance (Mid-1980s)** : The chart dominance of Yacht Rock began to wane after *Thriller* in late 1982, which shifted pop towards more kinetic forms. While some yacht rock players contributed to later hits, the movement's chart command quietly ended by 1986, when it still "didn't have a real life name."
