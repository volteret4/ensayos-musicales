# genre - New Traditionalism

## curiosities
**Mid-80s Emergence** : "New traditionalism" was a term coined in the mid-1980s specifically to distinguish this brand of country music from the then-disdained country-pop sound of the early 80s, which was associated with the "Urban Cowboy" era. Artists like Ricky Skaggs, Keith Whitley, and Dwight Yoakam led this movement, returning to the genre's roots. "George Strait's brand of country came to be called new traditionalism. **Late 80s Flourishing** : New traditionalism flourished in the late 1980s because there was little upside for Nashville artists in attempting to blend country with straight-ahead pop. The pop mainstream had largely rejected country product, making a return to roots-oriented music a more viable strategy.
