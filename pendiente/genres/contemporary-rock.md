## curiosities
**Rise in Movie Soundtracks (1967)** : Contemporary rock began to feature prominently in movie soundtracks starting with *The Graduate* in 1967, which initiated a wave of more modern music genres, including R&B, pop, funk, and disco, on the silver screen.
