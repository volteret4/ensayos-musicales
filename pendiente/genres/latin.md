## curiosities
**Influence on Joe Jackson** : Joe Jackson absorbed Latin sounds and met mambo and salsa in Latin clubs during his time in New York City in the early 1980s, particularly when Hector LaVoe was dominant. These influences significantly informed his songwriting and recording for his "Night and Day" album. "In Latin clubs, Jackson heard salsa and met Engay at a time when Hector LaVoe was dominant.
