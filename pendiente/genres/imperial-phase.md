# genre - Imperial Phase

## curiosities
**Definition and Origins** : The "Imperial Phase" is a 21st-century term describing a phenomenon in popular music fandom that dates back to its beginning. It characterizes a moment when an artist seemingly "has the secret of pop" and can achieve a hit with almost any release. **Identifying Characteristics** : An Imperial Phase can be identified by streaks of number one or top 10 hits, as well as "improbable hits" (success with unexpected sounds). A streak of top ten hits is a "sure sign," and the emergence of imitators further confirms an artist's elite status within this phase. "Remember I said one sure sign of an Imperial phase is a streak of top ten hits? **Conditions for Matching Madonna's Streak** : The current streaming era, where superstars commonly place many album cuts on lower chart rungs, means that the conditions may "never exist again" for any artist, even Taylor Swift, to match Madonna's record of 16 consecutive Top 5 singles.
