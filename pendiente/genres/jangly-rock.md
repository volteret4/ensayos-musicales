# genre - Jangly Rock

## curiosities
**Southern Incubation (1980s)** : Jangly rock emerged from college towns in the Southern United States, such as Athens, Georgia, during the 1980s. This genre fused the raw economy of punk with the country rock influences of Graham Parsons, creating an indie music sound that defined the decade and profoundly influenced bands like R.E.M. and Hootie & the Blowfish. **Mainstreaming in the Mid-1990s** : What began as cutting-edge and postmodern jangly rock from R.E.M. in the 1980s became "centrist rock and even pop" in the mid-1990s, largely due to bands like Hootie & the Blowfish. This "mainstreaming and mellowing of Djangly Americana" created a hospitable environment on the airwaves for a new generation of college-friendly bands.
