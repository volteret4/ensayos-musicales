## curiosities
**Pop Chart History - Past Success and Modern Challenges** : Broadway cast albums like "Hair" reached number one on the Billboard 200 in 1969. In the 21st century, while none have repeated this feat, albums such as "Dear Evan Hansen," "The Book of Mormon," and "Hamilton" have charted well, with "Dear Evan Hansen" debuting at number eight, the highest 21st-century debut.
