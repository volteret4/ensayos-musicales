# genre - Hippie Bubblegum

## curiosities
**Peak Boomer Decade Category** : Hippie bubblegum was identified as one of the whole categories of hits from the 1960s, a "peak boomer decade," as depicted and resurrected by shows like "Mad Men." **Late 60s Singles Charts** : In the late 1960s, the singles charts were often dominated by "frilly, sometimes forgettable pop music" that fit into the "hippie bubblegum" category, exemplified by catchy songs from acts like The Turtles and The Monkees. "On the R&B side, the hits from Soul Music's leading lights were often legendary, from Queen of Soul Aretha Franklin, to the late Otis Redding, to Motown Legend Marvin Gaye, while on the pop side, the singles that scaled the charts often fit into that hippie bubblegum category I identified in the first part of our show." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Singing Nuns and Green Tambourines Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
