# genre - Acapella

## curiosities
**Improbable Christmas Number One (1983)** : The acapella group The Flying Pickets achieved an improbable UK Christmas number one in 1983 with their "all vocal, hymn-like cover" of Yazoo's synth-pop classic "Only You." This showcased how unexpected genres and styles could capture the public's imagination during the holiday competition.
