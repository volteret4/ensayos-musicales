# genre - Country-politan

## curiosities
**Country Music Association's Initial Goal** : The Country Music Association, formed in 1958, aimed to promote country music, specifically the "country-politan" style, which was a smoother version incorporating strings instead of traditional banjos and fiddles. This approach was driven by the desire to make country music more broadly appealing and profitable, attracting pop and rock fans. "Not the Fiddles and Banjo's country, but the country-polletin, the smoother version with the strings instead of the twang." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 40 - Honestly Loving Olivia Newton-John | https://seventies.libsyn.com/ep-40-honestly-loving-olivia-newton-john
**Intentional Crossover Sound (1970s)** : The "country-politan" sound, also known as country-pop, featured big backing orchestral arrangements and was intentionally designed in the 1970s to increase country music's radio airplay and record sales. This strategy proved effective and created more exposure for artists. "the so-called country-politin sound, the country-pop sound with the big backing orchestral arrangements was intentional." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 23 - Women of Country Music in the 70s | https://seventies.libsyn.com/023-country-women-72820-2-45-pm
**Pioneering Sound** : Owen Bradley is recognized as the pioneer of the country-politan sound, which was notably made famous by Patsy Cline.
