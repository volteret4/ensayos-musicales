**The Integrated Musical** : The rise of the "integrated musical," exemplified by "Oklahoma!" in the 1940s, marked a significant shift where songs, plot, character development, and dance all worked together towards an "operatic ideal." "Another one was the rise of what musical theater scholars call the integrated musical. So the integrated musical means a show that the songs support the plot and they develop the character and the dance also supports the plot and the characters. Everything's working together. "in the 80s and 90s there are people were kind of the moaning the demise of the Broadway musical that it was its best days were behind it.
