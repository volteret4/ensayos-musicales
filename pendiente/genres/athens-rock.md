# genre - Athens Rock

## curiosities
**R.E.M. as the Face of the Scene (1983-1985)** : During the mid-1980s (1983-1985), R.E.M. was recognized as the leading band and "the face" of the Athens Rock music scene, while their predecessors, The B-52s, were mostly out of the public eye.
