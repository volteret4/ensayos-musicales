# genre - Hippie Hits by Trendhoppers / Bubblegum Pop

## curiosities
**Co-option by Mainstream** : The phenomenon of "hippie hits by trendhoppers" illustrates how popular "fad[s] or fashion[s]" can be "co-opted by the mainstream." This led to the creation of "toothless" or "child's play" versions of psychedelia and "pure bubblegum" pop from groups like The Lemon Pipers, 1910 Fruitgum Company, and Ohio Express. **Boomer Affection** : While "hippie bubblegum pop" of the late 1960s is generally not well-remembered by 21st-century audiences and has evolved into styles like "electronic club music," baby boomers who were teens, tweens, or younger during that era "express genuine affection for their era of bubblegum." This highlights a generational divide in musical memory and appreciation.
