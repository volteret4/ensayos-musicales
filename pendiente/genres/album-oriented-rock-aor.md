# genre - Album-Oriented Rock (AOR)

## curiosities
**Dominance in 70s FM Radio** : AOR was a prevalent genre that dominated FM Radio during the first half of the 1970s, recognized for its lengthy musical suites, often 15-20 minutes long, performed by elaborate rock ensembles featuring multiple guitar and keyboard solos, jazzy instrumental breakdowns, and string arrangements.
