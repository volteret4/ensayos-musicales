# genre - Southern Hip Hop

## curiosities
**Growth and Influence (2000s)** : Outcast significantly contributed to changing not just the sound but the breadth of hip hop, including geographically, helping Atlanta become "Hip Hop's center of gravity" by the 21st century. Their expansive vision affirmed that hip hop, particularly from the South, would become the lingua franca of 21st-century pop. **Early 90s Centers (Houston, Miami)** : In the early 1990s, Southern rap was predominantly associated with cities like Houston, Texas, home to groups like The Ghetto Boys and Underground Kings (UGK), and Miami, which developed its own subgenre called Miami Bass Music, popularized by acts like The Two Live Crew. "At the time, Southern rap was more tied to cities like Houston, Texas, home of the ghetto boys. Or the Underground Kings, better known as UGK. Or the City of Miami, which had developed its own Southern hip hop subgenre called Miami Bass Music." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Shake It Like a Polaroid Picture Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Post-95 Source Awards Takeover (Late 90s)** : The "takeover of Southern Rap was in full effect" by the late 1990s, spreading beyond Atlanta to other cities. New Orleans rappers like Master P and Juvenile, through labels such as No Limit and Cash Money, achieved quadruple platinum sales levels in 1998 and 1999, affirming the immense potency and widespread success of Southern Hip Hop.
