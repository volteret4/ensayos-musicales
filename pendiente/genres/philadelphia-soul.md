# genre - Philadelphia Soul

## curiosities
**Origins and Key Producers** : Philadelphia Soul was a distinctive R&B style that gained widespread recognition in the 1970s. It was notably made famous by influential Philly producers such as Kenny Gamble, Leon Huff, and Tom Bell. This style was embraced by groups like New York City, for whom Bernard Edwards and Nile Rogers honed their R&B playing skills as backing musicians.
