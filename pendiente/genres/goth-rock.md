# genre - Goth Rock

## curiosities
**Evolution from Punk** : The sound of Gothic rock was anticipated by bands like Siouxsie and the Banshees, who layered tribal drumming and sinister vocals over punk energy. "The London group, fronted by Susan Janet Ballion, aka Susie Sue, had the energy of punk, but from the start layered in tribal drumming and sinister vocals that would anticipate the sound of Gothic rock." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Lost and Lonely Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Defining Elements** : Intensive bass playing (like Peter Hook's for Joy Division) and gloomy vocals (like Ian Curtis's baritone) became core elements of goth rock, emerging around 1978-1979 on British rock singles. "Both of these sounds, intensive bass and gloomy vocals would become core elements of post punk and Goth Rock." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Lost and Lonely Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Commercialization** : Goth music gradually became more commercial in the early 1980s, with albums like Siouxsie and the Banshees' "Kaleidoscope" and "Juju," and The Cure's "Pornography" reaching the UK top 10. "Goth music was gradually becoming more commercial." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Lost and Lonely Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Blueprint for Subculture** : Bauhaus's single "Bela Lugosi's Dead" (1979) served as a skeletal blueprint for the entire Goth subculture. "Their single Bella Legosi's dead in the late summer of 1979 would serve as a skeletal blueprint for the entire Goth subculture." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Lost and Lonely Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**"Moap rock" or "Sad Bastard Music"** : Goth rock is one of the terms used for the subgenre of "melodic melancholia" that broke through in the US, alongside "moap rock" and "sad bastard music." "Today on Hit Parade, we chart the breakthrough of the subgenre that's been called everything from moap rock to goth rock to sad bastard music." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Lost and Lonely Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**The Cure's Evolution** : The Cure's Robert Smith initially turned away from the heaviest, darkest goth rock in the early 1980s for career and mental health reasons, but returned to writing dark, massive-sounding goth rock with "Disintegration" in 1989. "Robert Smith have turned away from the heaviest, darkest goth rock for the sake of The Cure's career and his own mental health." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Lost and Lonely Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Visual Influence** : Goth rock, along with UK post-punk, has had a significant visual influence, with bands like The Cure, Siouxsie, and Bauhaus credited with inspiring the aesthetic of stores like Hot Topic. "Countless bands adopted UK post-punk and gothrock attributes." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Lost and Lonely Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
