# genre - Comedy

## curiosities
**Early Grammy Category** : Comedy music was one of the established Grammy award categories from the inception of the awards in 1959. "Jazz, classical, pop, country, Broadway, movie soundtracks, even comedy and children's music had Grammy award categories." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > And the Grammy Goes to… Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef0154465cd600c4e3a
**Album of the Year Winners (1961, 1963)** : Comedy albums achieved significant Grammy recognition in the early 1960s, with Bob Newhart's "The Button-Down Mind of Bob Newhart" winning Album of the Year in 1961 and Vaughn Meader's "The First Family" winning in 1963. These were the first and last times that comedy LPs secured the top Grammy prize, reflecting an era when "comedy LP's were big business." "No matter what was selling, comedy albums took home the big prize in both 1961 and 1963, the first and last times that's ever happened in an era when comedy LP's were big business." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > And the Grammy Goes to… Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef0154465cd600c4e3a
