# genre - High Energy

## curiosities
**Characteristics and Impact** : At the turn of the 1980s, High Energy (Hi-NRG) was characterized as "synthesized disco" reduced to its densest form, with relentless tempos, staccato rhythms, and pulsating baselines, described as feeling "like dance music as a drug." Along with Italo disco, it became part of the broader 80s Post-Disco culture.
