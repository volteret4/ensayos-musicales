# genre - Doo-Wop

## curiosities
**Isley Brothers' Genre Shift** : The Isley Brothers transitioned into singing doo-wop songs at Ronald's insistence after the death of Vernon, influenced by their favorite act, Billy Ward and The Dominoes. Their first record with Richie Barrett, "Don't Be Jellis," was described as a standard doo-wop ballad. **Cross-Racial Charting (1950s)** : Doo-wop classics such as "Earth Angel" and "Why Do Fools Fall in Love" experienced black-fronted original versions charting alongside or slightly behind covers by white vocalists, illustrating a significant aspect of early cross-racial interpretation in popular music. "the duop classic, Earth Angel, was first recorded by black quartet The Penguins in 1954... **Influence on Madonna's "Cherish" (1989)** : Madonna's song "Cherish" incorporated elements of Doo-wop, alongside 60s Sunshine Pop and girl group energy, as a "sequel to True Blue."
