# genre - New Wave Rock

## curiosities
**Influence on Pop Transition (late 1970s - early 1980s)** : New Wave Rock, alongside disco, influenced the sound of pop music in the late 1970s and early 1980s. Olivia Newton-John's single "Deeper Than the Night" (1979) leaned a bit toward this genre, and her 1982 single "Heart Attack" was described as a "New Wave Bop," marking her full transition into contemporary trends.
