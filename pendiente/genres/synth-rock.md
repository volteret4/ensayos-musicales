**Genesis's 1980s Sound** : Genesis is now largely remembered as a quintessential synth rock band of the 1980s, marking a significant evolution from their earlier progressive rock sound. This shift became increasingly evident as Phil Collins, Tony Banks, and Mike Rutherford embraced more poppy, synth-laden arrangements, exemplified by albums like "Abacab."
