# genre - Americana Folk

## curiosities
**Grammy Success with Robert Plant and Alison Krauss (2009)** : The Americana Folk genre received significant Grammy recognition in 2009 when Robert Plant and Alison Krauss's album "Raising Sand" swept Album of the Year and Record of the Year. This marked a departure from the historical lack of Grammy recognition for Plant's band, Led Zeppelin.
