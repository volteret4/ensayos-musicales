# genre - Socially Conscious Music

## curiosities
**Resurgence in Late 1980s Pop and Rock** : In the late 1980s, socially conscious music became not only viable but fashionable across rock and R&B, championed by artists like Peter Gabriel, Sting, and U2, and even scaling the charts with songs such as Suzanne Vega's "Luca" about child abuse. This trend dovetailed with a rise in Afro-centrism in black popular entertainment and on college campuses, inspiring artists like Janet Jackson to embed message-driven content into commercial pop.
