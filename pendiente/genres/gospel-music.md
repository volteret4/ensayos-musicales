# genre - Gospel Music

## curiosities
**Community's Open Secret** : The gospel music community had a more relaxed attitude towards homosexuality and bisexuality compared to other parts of the church, allowing relationships like that between Sister Rosetta Tharp and Marie Knight to be an "open secret." "their relationship was regarded as an open secret within the gospel music community, which had a rather more relaxed attitude to homosexuality and bisexuality than the rest of the church." ← Episode 5： ＂This Train＂ by Sister Rosetta Tharpe | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=II-hDnwUgT8
**Origin and Creator** : Modern black gospel music largely originated around the same time as big bands and was primarily created by Thomas Dorsey around 1930. He integrated popular music idioms, such as blues and jazz, with traditional Christian hymns, making it distinct from earlier forms of religious singing. "gospel music, as we talk about it now, was largely the creation of one man, Thomas Dorsey." ← Episode 5： ＂This Train＂ by Sister Rosetta Tharpe | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=II-hDnwUgT8
**Ceiling for Stars** : Sam Cooke perceived that being a star in gospel music, as good as it was, had a "ceiling" in terms of broader reach and financial opportunity compared to pop music. "As good as it was being a star in gospel music, he could see it had a ceiling." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Don’t Know Much About History, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
