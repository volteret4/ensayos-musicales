# genre - Electronic Dance Music (EDM)

## curiosities
**Emergence as Centrist Pop Term (Late 2000s)** : In the late 2000s, Electronic Dance Music, or EDM, caught on as a new catch-all term and became a "centrist pop term in its own right." Michelangelo Matos points out that EDM appealed to scholars and critics because it lacked the specific stylistic connotations of terms like house or techno, and it avoided the negative associations of "Electronica" with the "late 90s major label Gold Rush."
