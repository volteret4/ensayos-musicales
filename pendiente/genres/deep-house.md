## curiosities
**Experimentation (Mid-2010s)** : In the mid-2010s, some Deep House producers began experimenting with more aggressive melodies. They achieved this while maintaining the deep and danceable groove characteristic of House music, essentially increasing the intensity without sacrificing the fundamental rhythm.
