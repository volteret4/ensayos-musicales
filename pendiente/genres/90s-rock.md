# genre - 90s rock

## curiosities
**Favorable to Women Performers (1990s)** : The 90s rock genre and the decade overall were "very good to women performers," with many of the biggest acts on radio and Billboard charts being women. This period was perceived as a consummation of female success in popular music, even if some women performers weren't part of Lilith Fair.
