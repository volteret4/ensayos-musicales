**Emergence from Yacht Rock** : Yacht Soul is described as an R&B offshoot of Yacht Rock that became pivotal to Quincy Jones's career by the 1980s. It notably employed many of the same nexus of California session musicians favored by smooth bands like Steely Dan, Toto, and The Doobie Brothers. "Yacht Soul. **Emergence and Definition (1979-1980)** : The "yacht soul strain" emerged around 1979-1980, primarily driven by the collaboration between Quincy Jones, with his "extremely soulful, jazzy sound," and Rod Temperton, with his "extreme pop mentality." This period also saw David Foster and J. Graden writing for R&B artists like Earth, Wind & Fire, creating crossover music using the same session musicians as white pop records.
