**Legacy of Social Content and Protest** : Soul music has a rich history of integrating social content and protest while remaining radio-friendly, as exemplified by legends like Stevie Wonder and Marvin Gaye. This legacy inspired artists like Janet Jackson, who believed there was a contemporary space for such message-driven pop within the New Jack Swing era, blending romantic and socially conscious songs under one umbrella.
