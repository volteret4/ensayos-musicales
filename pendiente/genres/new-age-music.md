# genre - New Age Music

## curiosities
**Public Perception (Pre-Yani)** : Before artists like Yani achieved mainstream success through PBS, new age music was largely dismissed by the general public as background music, often associated with activities like "hot stone massages, or being put on hold." The genre lacked a superstar figure, making Yani's breakthrough all the more unexpected.
