# genre - Shoegaze rock

## curiosities
**Precursor to Britpop** : A wave of Shoegaze rock, along with UK indie and Madchester, eventually coalesced into Britpop. "a wave of UK indie, madchester, and shugaz rock eventually coalesced into Britpop through the hits of Swade, Alastica, pulp, and of course, Blur and Oasis." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Champagne Supernova Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Lush as Leading Lights** : Years before Britpop, Lush, co-led by Emma Anderson and Miki Berenyi, were leading lights of the Shoegaze movement, with their music described as "chiming and dense." "Years before Britpop, they were leading lights of the Shugaz movement.
