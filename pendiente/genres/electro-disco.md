# genre - Electro Disco

## curiosities
**Modern Summer Song Sound** : Electro disco is presented as a sound that inherently fits the modern concept of a summer song, particularly with a "throwback" aesthetic. Sabrina Carpenter's "Espresso," with its "throwback electro disco sound," is highlighted as an example of a track "built for beaches, barbecues, and cars booming down the street."
