# genre - New Wave Dance Music

## curiosities
**Disco's Evolution in the 1980s** : In the early 1980s, prominent artists like Michael Jackson and Madonna were instrumental in transforming and retooling disco. Their efforts led to the emergence of "new wave dance music," a significant development that highlighted disco's adaptability and enduring influence rather than its supposed demise.
