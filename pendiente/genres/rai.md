# genre - Rai

## curiosities
**Algerian Folk Music Collaborated with Sting** : Rai is an Arabic folk music genre from Algeria. Sting collaborated with Algerian Rai singer Cheb Mami on the song "Desert Rose" for his 1999 album "Brand New Day," specifically co-writing the track to showcase Mami's vocals.
