# genre - Pre-Rockers / Non-Rockers

## curiosities
**Continuity of Traditional Pop** : This genre encompasses artists from the "old guard" like Frank Sinatra and Dean Martin, as well as "post-rock hitmakers" such as Wayne Newton, Tom Jones, and Engelbert Humperdinck. These artists continued to achieve chart success throughout the 1960s with music that sounded "like rock had never happened," showcasing a continuity of traditional pop sounds amidst the rise of rock and roll.
