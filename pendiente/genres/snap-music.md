# genre - Snap Music

## curiosities
**Atlanta-Based Crunk Offshoot** : Snap Music is a hip-hop subgenre that originated in Atlanta as an offshoot of Crunk Music. It is characterized by its reduced digital essence, featuring elements like an 808-thump, high-hats, snapping sounds, and simple chanting vocals, with the guiding principle that "the simpler, the better." "Lafay Taffy was a prototypical example of the hip hop subgenre as Snap Music. An Atlanta-based offshoot of Crunk Music, Snap was rap reduced to its digital essence, an 808-thump, a bit of high-hat, some snapping, of course, and chanting vocals, the simpler, the better." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Ride ’til I Can’t No More Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce88
**Colloquially Known as Ringtone Rap** : Snap Music was also commonly referred to as "Ringtone Rap" because its crisp and simple sonic structure made it ideal for reproduction by the primitive, chirpy mobile phones prevalent around 2005. The simplicity allowed rap hooks to be easily customized for caller identification.
