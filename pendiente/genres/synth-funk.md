# genre - Synth Funk

## curiosities
**Aretha Franklin's 80s Comeback Sound** : Luther Vandross introduced "a new brand of synthesized funk" into Aretha Franklin's music with her 1982 album *Jump to It*, playing a crucial role in her 1980s comeback and modernizing her sound.
