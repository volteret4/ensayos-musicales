# genre - Proto-grunge

## curiosities
**Elvis Costello's "Blood and Chocolate"** : Elvis Costello's 1986 album "Blood and Chocolate," characterized by its bruising and raucous sound, recorded loud and near live in the studio with few takes, was later tagged by critics as a kind of "proto-grunge" even before the term itself had been coined. This highlights the album's aggressive sound and its unintentional foresight.
