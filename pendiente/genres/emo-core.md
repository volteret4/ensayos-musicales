# genre - Emo core

## curiosities
**Origin of the Term** : The term "emotional hardcore" or "Emo core" was coined to describe the music of early bands like Rites of Spring. This genre emerged when artists, notably Guy Picciotto, combined the open-hearted, sentimental lyrics exemplified by Ian MacKaye of Minor Threat with a more angular and jaggedly melodic punk sound.
