# genre - Early Jazz

## curiosities
**Homage in The Sting Soundtrack (1973)** : Early Jazz music was honored in Marvin Hamlisch's soundtrack for the 1973 film *The Sting*, which served as an homage to both Ragtime and Early Jazz. The soundtrack's chart-topping success contributed to a resurgence of interest in these historical genres.
