# genre - Rock, Alternative, or Hard Rock (2010s)

## curiosities
**Covers as a Chart Strategy** : In the 2010s, amidst the crowding out of traditional guitar music on radio by hip hop and electronic dance music, rock, alternative, and hard rock bands consistently produced hit covers of existing songs, utilizing them as a "sure-fire way" to reach the top of the charts.
