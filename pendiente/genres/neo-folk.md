# genre - Neo Folk

## curiosities
**Vanguard Movement (Mid-1980s)** : Suzanne Vega represented the vanguard of neo-folk music in the mid-1980s. This genre combined acoustic instrumentation with contemporary new wave rock influences and occasional synthesizers, offering a fresh sound that was not "acetic" despite its folk roots. "Vega represented the vanguard of what folk music sounded like at this moment in pop. **Dominance of Female Voices (Mid-1980s)** : While the neo-folk wave of the mid-1980s included male performers like Billy Bragg and Peter Case, the "strongest voices and notably the most commercially successful ones tended to be the Wim." This indicated a significant female presence and impact within the genre. **Prominent on First Modern Rock Chart (1988)** : Neo Folk artists, including 10,000 Maniacs, Tracy Chapman, and Joan Armatrading, were heavily represented on Billboard's inaugural Modern Rock Tracks chart when it launched in September 1988. This highlighted the genre's significant crossover appeal within the alternative music landscape. "Moreover, that first Modern Rock chart wasn't all postpunk music.
