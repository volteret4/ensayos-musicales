# genre - Rumba Flamenca

## curiosities
**Los Del Rio's Signature Style** : Los Del Rio's music is characterized as Rumba Flamenca, based on the acoustic flamenco guitar and known for its very upbeat and celebratory nature. This traditional Spanish sound was the foundation from which "Macarena" originated. "They make very upbeat, very traditionally Spanish sounding music.
