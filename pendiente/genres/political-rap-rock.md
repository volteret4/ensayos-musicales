# genre - Political Rap Rock

## curiosities
**Rage Against the Machine's UK Success** : The genre "Political Rap Rock" is primarily associated with Rage Against the Machine, an American band whose 1993 song "Killing In The Name" surprisingly topped the UK Christmas chart in 2009. While the band was relegated mostly to alternative rock radio in their home country, they had scored some minor top 40 hits in England, making their Christmas number one campaign less improbable but still a significant feat for the genre.
