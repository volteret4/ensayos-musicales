## curiosities
**Pioneering Film Format** : The 1984 comedy film "This Is Spinal Tap," directed by Rob Reiner and starring Christopher Guest, Michael McKean, and Harry Sheerer, helped pioneer the mockumentary format. It offered a straight-faced but hilarious chronicle of the fictional British metal band Spinal Tap.
