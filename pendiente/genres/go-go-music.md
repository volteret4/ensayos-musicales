# genre - Go Go music

## curiosities
**Influence on Club Nouveau's "Lean On Me"** : Washington D.C.'s Go Go music style contributed the beat to Club Nouveau's successful R&B cover of "Lean On Me," which reached number one on the Crossover 30 chart in its inaugural week.
