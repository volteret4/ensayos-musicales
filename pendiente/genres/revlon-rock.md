# genre - Revlon Rock

## curiosities
**Early Attempt at Classification** : "Revlon Rock" was an unsuccessful term people used in the 1970s to describe the smooth music that would eventually be named Yacht Rock. It's noted as one of several "nuts" and ineffective terms.
