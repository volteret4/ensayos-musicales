# genre - Boy band

## curiosities
**Early Television Appearances (1960s)** : In the 1960s, boy bands like The Monkees frequently appeared on live television shows to promote their music. As their popularity grew and touring schedules became demanding, these bands often resorted to pre-recording lip-synced performances on sound stages for TV broadcasts, fulfilling audience demand despite a lack of artistic narrative in many such early videos. **Origins and Evolution** : The history of the boy band predates Backstreet Boys and *NSYNC, with versions existing before those performers were born, tracing back to the 1950s, 60s, and 70s. The modern boy band, as an assembled group of usually five young men with crafted material, showmanship, and dancing, took shape by the 1980s. "Boy bands are at least as old as rock and roll itself." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Want It That Way Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Characteristics and Fandom** : Boy bands are characterized by kinetic dancing, fashionable dressing, preening for cameras and teen magazines, and singing. Their members are crush-worthy objects who invite fans to choose favorites and learn videocoreography. They often carry themselves with the profiles of rock stars. The only consistent commonality among boy bands is "rabid fandom by a young fan base," which is "mostly determined by the girls." "The only thing boy bands have in common is rabid fandom by a young fan base." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Want It That Way Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Lack of Firm Definition** : There is no firm definition for a boy band, with some groups dancing and others not, some relying on outside songwriters while others wrote their own material, and some being assembled by industry architects while others formed organically. "There are no firm borders that can sign one act to boy band status and keep another group out." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Want It That Way Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Murky Term History** : Pop scholars date the term "boy band" to the 1980s, possibly from an early interview with manager Lou Perlman, but it likely arose organically and the phenomenon existed long before the term was coined. "The very term boy band has a murky history, pop scholars dated to only the 1980s based on an early interview with notorious boy band manager, Lou Perlman." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Want It That Way Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Roots in Black Culture** : Like rock and roll itself, the boy band phenomenon is "at its core rooted in black culture." This aspect is often belittled or erased, as white vocal groups emulating or covering black performers frequently achieved more widespread popularity. "It's fine to enjoy boy bands of all kinds, but keep in mind, like rock and roll itself, the boy band is at its core rooted in black culture, and it's easy for that archetype to be belittled or erased." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Want It That Way Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**"Wilderness Period" in Early 90s** : After the fall of New Kids on the Block, the early 90s entered a "wilderness period" for boy bands, coinciding with the rise of Grunge and Gangsta Rap, until the late 90s saw a resurgence.
