# genre - Miami Bass Music

## curiosities
**Platinum Success and Crossover (Late 80s - Early 90s)** : This "bodacious style" of Southern hip hop was first propelled to platinum status in the late 1980s by the group The Two Live Crew. By the early 1990s, the Miami Bass sound was successfully crossing over onto the pop charts, influencing non-Miami acts like 95 South and Tag Team, whose 1993 singles featured Miami Bass-style production.
