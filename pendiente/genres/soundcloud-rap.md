# genre - Soundcloud rap

## curiosities
**Distinct Vocal Style** : Soundcloud rap is characterized by a "whiny" vocal style, exemplified by artists like Juice WRLD, which differentiates it from earlier sing-rapping styles that more closely approximated traditional Hip Hop flow. "The vocal wine of Juice World, whose real name was Geraud Higgins, was typical of the Soundcloud rap song, even though it was even further removed from the roots of rap." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say My Name, Say My Name, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Gen Z Artists** : This subgenre is particularly associated with a wave of "Gen Z artists" who adopted its distinctive vocal characteristics, which continued into the 2020s, heard in hits like Lil Nas X's "Montero (Call Me By Your Name)." "Before this wave of Gen Z artists, most Singrappers had a cadence that approximated Hip Hop flow.
