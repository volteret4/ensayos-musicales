# artist - Nadia Komenic

## curiosities
**Red Rocks Performance with John Tesh** : Olympic gymnast Nadia Komenic performed routines on stage to John Tesh's music during his "Live at Red Rocks" concert special. Her involvement was part of Tesh's strategy to create a grand spectacle and enhance the production value of the show.
