# artist - Curtis Stigers

## genres
- Smooth Jazz

## songs
**What's So Funny About Peace, Love, and Understanding (1992)** : This song, originally written by Nick Lowe for his band Brinsley Schwarz and made famous by Elvis Costello in 1978, was covered by smooth jazz vocalist Curtis Stigers for "The Bodyguard" soundtrack in 1992. Buried in the back half of the album, this cover ultimately led to Nick Lowe becoming a millionaire due to the soundtrack's tens of millions of sales worldwide.
