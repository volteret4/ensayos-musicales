## songs
**Theme from A Summer Place (1960)** : This quintessential easy listening instrumental was the Billboard year-end number one for 1960, spending nine weeks at number one. Despite its title, it is not the version featured in the 1959 movie "A Summer Place" starring Sandra Dee and Troy Donahue. It became a staple on Light FM radio and defeated hits like The Everly Brothers' "Cathy's Clown" and Brenda Lee's "I'm Sorry" for the year-end title.
