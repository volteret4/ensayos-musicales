## songs
**Theme from A Summer Place (1960)** : This quintessential easy listening instrumental was the Billboard year-end number one for 1960, spending nine weeks at number one. Despite its title, it is not the version featured in the 1959 movie "A Summer Place" starring Sandra Dee and Troy Donahue. It became a staple on Light FM radio and defeated hits like The Everly Brothers' "Cathy's Clown" and Brenda Lee's "I'm Sorry" for the year-end title. **Theme from A Summer Place (1960)** : This instrumental was the top single of 1960, maintaining the number one position for nine weeks and becoming arguably "the dreamiest number one of the period." Originating from a teen drama starring Troy Donahue and Sandra Dee, it was paradoxically perceived as "old people music for young people" due to its gentle sound and was famously parodied in a 1990s episode of "The Simpsons."
