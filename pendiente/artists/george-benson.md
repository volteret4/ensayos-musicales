## albums
**Breezin' (1976)** : This LP reached number one and received multiple Grammys, including an award for its engineering. Its title track virtually "codified 70s lounge pop" and served as an important antecedent for the polished direction of West Coast-derived pop. "6 months after EWF's Smash LP topped the chart, Jazz and R&B guitarist George Benson was at number one with Breezen. **Give Me the Night (1980)** : This collaborative album between George Benson and Quincy Jones is regarded as the quintessential example of the "Quincy sound." All Music praised it, noting that "Benson's voice caught beautifully in the rich floating sound had never before been put to such versatile use." The album reached number three on the album chart and attained platinum certification. "The album they wound up making together might be the prime example of the Quincy sound. It was called Give Me the Night. All music says of the Give Me the Night LP quote, Quincy Jones is the master catalyst and his regular team is in control. **Give Me the Night (1980)** : This 1980 album, produced by Quincy Jones, saw George Benson "go full-yacht" and returned him to the top five on the album chart. Jones rebooted Benson's career by employing an "army of session players" including Lee Ritenour, Greg Phillinganes, George Duke, Paulinho da Costa, David Foster, and Michael McDonald. ## curiosities
**Guitar Prodigy to R&B Frontman** : George Benson emerged as a guitar prodigy, evolving from a respected jazz fusion player in the 1960s into a prominent R&B frontman in the 1970s. He was recognized as a progenitor of the Yacht Rock genre due to his signature jazzy instrumentals and smooth vocal style. "George Benson, a guitar prodigy, had evolved from an acclaimed jazz fusion player in the 60s to an R&B frontman in the 70s. We talked about Benson in our Yacht Rock episode of Hit Parade as one of the progenitors of that genre thanks to his jazzy instrumentals and smooth vocals." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Wanna Rock with Q. **Career Reboot by Quincy Jones** : In 1980, Quincy Jones "rebooted" George Benson's career with the album *Give Me the Night*, by surrounding him with a renowned team of session players. Benson's earlier work was described as "proto-Yacht Soul," and this album fully embraced the yacht sound.
