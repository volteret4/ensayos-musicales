## songs
**Go Away Little Girl (1963)** : Steve Lawrence's only Hot 100 number one hit, topping the chart for two weeks in January 1963. The song, co-written by Carole King and Gerry Goffin, is described as sexist, demeaning, and sex-negative, with a lyric accusing a young woman of being a temptress just for existing and a cloying melody. **Go Away Little Girl (1963)** : Steve Lawrence's only number one hit, which he took to the top of the charts in January 1963. This song, written by Carol King and Jerry Goffin, was later covered by Donnie Osmond, making it the first song to top the Hot 100 twice.
