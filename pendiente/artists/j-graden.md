# artist - J. Graden

## curiosities
**Collaboration with David Foster** : J. Graden, referred to as a "Journeyman," co-produced and co-wrote "Morning" by Al Jarreau with David Foster. He also collaborated with David Foster in writing for R&B artists like Earth, Wind & Fire around 1979-1980, contributing to the "yacht soul" strain. "Produced by Journeyman J.
