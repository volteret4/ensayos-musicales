# artist - Tevin Campbell

## songs
**Tomorrow (1990)** : Teen singer Tevin Campbell was featured on this single from Quincy Jones's "Back on the Block" album, which became the third consecutive track from the LP to hit number one on the R&B chart in June 1990.
