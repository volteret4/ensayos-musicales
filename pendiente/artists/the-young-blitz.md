## genres

## songs
**Get Together (1967)** : This song by New York folk rockers The Young Blitz initially peaked at number 62 when first released in 1967. Two years later, in 1969, it gained momentum after DJ Dan Ingram used it in a promo and it was subsequently picked up for a public service announcement by the National Conference of Christians and Jews, leading to its reissue as a single and a climb to number five.
