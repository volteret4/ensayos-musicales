## songs
**The Real Roxanne (1985)** : In response to Roxanne Shanté's diss track, UTFO recruited their own female rapper, Adelaide Martinez (aka The Real Roxanne), to record a single under the same title. This track reached number 44 on the R&B chart and charted simultaneously with UTFO's original and Shanté's response in late 1985. **Roxanne Roxanne (1984)** : Recorded by this Brooklyn quartet, this track was initially released in 1984 as the B-side to their debut single. It gained more attention than its A-side due to its "shady tall tale about a fly girl who's all stuck up and won't respond to the men's amorous advances." It became a favorite with breakers and cracked the top 10 on Billboard's R&B chart in March 1985, a remarkable achievement for a new group on an independent label.
