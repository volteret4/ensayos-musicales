# artist - Julie Covington

## albums
**Evita (1976)** : Julie Covington, a stage actress and singer, fronted the initial concept album for "Evita," the musical by Andrew Lloyd Webber and Tim Rice, which featured the ballad "Don't Cry for Me Argentina" that topped the UK singles chart in early 1977.
