## songs
**Too Many Times (1986)** : In a collaboration with country star Earl Thomas Conley, Anita Pointer performed this duet, which soared to number two on the Hot Country singles chart in November 1986. This marked the first time a "Pointer" name had appeared on the country chart since "Fairytale" twelve years prior. ## curiosities
**"Country People" Self-Identification** : Anita Pointer, a member of the Pointer Sisters, stated, "We were country people," reflecting their upbringing in Arkansas where country music was prevalent in their home, on the radio, and in church. "We were country people. Anita Pointer said." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 53 - More than Charley Pride - African Americans in 70s Country | https://www.ftr70.com/
**Inspiration for "Fairy Tale"** : Anita Pointer's personal heartbreak over a married DJ inspired her and her sister Bonnie to write the country song "Fairy Tale," marking their entry into the country music industry. "Anita was dating a DJ and he neglected to tell her that he was married. Thus, their love that she thought they had was a fairy tale." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 53 - More than Charley Pride - African Americans in 70s Country | https://www.ftr70.com/
**Daughter's Death and Her Own Illness/Death (2003, 2022)** : Anita Pointer lost her daughter, Jada, to cancer in 2003, and subsequently began missing gigs due to her own illness before ultimately succumbing to cancer herself on the last day of 2022 at age 74.
