## curiosities
**Origin from Joey D and the Starlighters** : The instrumentalists from the 1964 lineup of Joey D and the Starlighters eventually departed to form their own group, The Young Rascals. This new band went on to achieve a series of hit records between 1965 and 1970. "The young rascals, a group who had a series of hits from 1965 to 1970, started out as the instrumentalists in the 1964 lineup of Joey Dean the Starlighters, before breaking out to become their own band." ← Episode 91： ＂The Twist＂ by Chubby Checker | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LzsS8EXdsm8
**Cited Influence on "Leave a Tender Moment Alone"** : Both fans and Billy Joel himself initially cited The Rascals as one of the many influences for his 1984 mid-tempo ballad "Leave a Tender Moment Alone."
