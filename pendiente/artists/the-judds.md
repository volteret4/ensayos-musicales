**Country Megastars, Pop Obscurity** : The Judds were megastars within the country music world, achieving eight consecutive country number ones starting with "Mama, He's Crazy" in 1984. However, in the pop world, none of their albums managed to crack the Top 50, illustrating the genre's "wilderness period" where country music existed in its own distinct sphere. "In that world, the Juts were megastars.
