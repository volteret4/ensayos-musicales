# artist - Roxanne Hoyle

## curiosities
**2018 UK Christmas Number One Campaign Recipient** : Roxanne Hoyle, known as LadbabyMum, was the intended recipient of a "unique holiday present" from her husband, Mark Hoyle (Ladbaby): a UK Christmas number one. She featured in her husband's 2018 novelty song "We Built This City on Sausage Rolls," which aimed to revive the quirky spirit of the British Christmas chart. "In it, he announced he was going to try to get his wife Roxanne Hoyle, aka LadbabyMum, mother of their 18-month-old son, a unique holiday present.
