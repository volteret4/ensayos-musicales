## curiosities

**Originator of Chopped & Screwed** : DJ e-Screw is a legendary figure who originated the Chopped & Screwed G-Hop scene in Houston during the 1990s. His celebrated "screwtapes" were influential and collected by musicians like Robert Disarro. "Originada por el legendario DJ e-Screw, esta técnica consistía en ralentizar trásticamente los debates, repetir fragmentos de canciones y rapear de forma restaurada, casi son ámbola, hasta lograr un efecto inmótico y casi narcótico." ← Witch House： Cuando internet inventó su género maldito | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SLlzXA0eyu0
**Chop 'N' Screw Pioneer** : DJ Escriu cultivated a cult scene in Houston in the early 90s by experimenting with radically slowing down music to "narcotic levels." This sound, known as Chop 'N' Screw, transformed G-Funk into a dragged-out, immersive experience. "Relentizar la música hasta a niveles narcóticos." ← ¿Qué pasó con el PHONK？ (La historia real) | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ehfWyUHFFT8
**Creation of Chopped and Screwed Sound** : DJ Screw's experimentation involved repeating long phrases with pauses and stretching beats until the songs seemed to "melt," giving the music a new, dreamlike and narcotic meaning. This unique manipulation of tempo and pitch became known as "Chopped and Screwed," with "Chop" referring to the cuts, repetitions, and jumps in phrases, and "Screw" to the dragged, deep tempo. **Death and Enduring Legacy (2000)** : Although initially a local phenomenon, DJ Screw's legacy expanded significantly after his death in 2000 due to an overdose of "lean." His pioneering work ultimately influenced artists across the United States and beyond, even impacting culturally distant genres like vaporwave. **Early Experiments with Hip Hop (Early 1990s)** : DJ Screw, a pivotal figure in the Houston, Texas music scene, began experimenting with his hip hop records in the early 1990s. He developed a technique of mixing two copies of the same record simultaneously at a significantly slower tempo, typically around 60-70 BPMs, resulting in a unique, languid sound. "Empezó a experimentar con sus discos de Hic Hop, al mezclar dos copias del mismo disco en paralelo a un tiempo más lento del habitual, alrededor de los 60-70 MPMs." ← ¿Por qué el Slowed & Reverb y los Speed Up están rompiendo YT y TikTok？ | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WWra85DsCo
**Legendary Mixtapes and Local Scene Growth** : His now-legendary mixtapes on cassette began circulating, with people sharing and re-recording them. This dense, heavy sound quickly spread throughout Houston, fostering a vibrant local scene comprising rappers, producers, and a distinctive aesthetic characterized by gold chains, vintage cars, and even its own associated substance, "lean" or "purple drunk"—a mixture of cough syrup with codeine and soda—which amplified the hypnotic and narcotic effect of the slowed mixes.
