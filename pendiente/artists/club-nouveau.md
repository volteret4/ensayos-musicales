## songs
**Lean On Me (1987)** : The Sacramento, California R&B dance troupe's radical reinterpretation of Bill Withers' classic blended the vibe of the then-new R&B subgenre New Jack Swing and the Washington D.C. funk style Go-go. Despite its arrangement being very dated to the mid-80s, Club Nouveau took "Lean On Me" back to number one in March 1987, making the song the fifth double chart topper in Hot 100 history.
