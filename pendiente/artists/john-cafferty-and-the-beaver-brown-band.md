# artist - John Cafferty and the Beaver Brown Band

## genres
- Bar Band Rock

## albums
**Eddie and the Cruisers Soundtrack (1983)** : This soundtrack was recorded by John Cafferty and the Beaver Brown Band but was initially billed under the pseudonym "Eddie and the Cruisers" to align with the film's fictional story. The album cracked the Top 10 and went triple platinum after the movie gained popularity on cable TV. ## songs
**On the Dark Side (1983)** : Originally a 1983 single from the flop movie soundtrack *Eddie and the Cruisers*, this song, which sounded "an awful lot like Springsteen and the East Street band," was revived by radio stations and belatedly became a top ten hit in the fall of 1984 due to Bruce Springsteen's imperial phase. "John Cafferty and the Beaver Brown bands on the dark side became a top ten hit belatedly in the fall of 1984." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Material Girl in an Imperial World Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f03154465cd600c5379
**On the Dark Side (1983)** : Originally released in the fall of 1983 on the "Eddie and the Cruisers" soundtrack and credited to "Eddie and the Cruisers," this single peaked at number 64 on the Hot 100 before quickly disappearing. In 1984, after the movie became a word-of-mouth hit on HBO and coincided with the resurgence of bar band rock, "On the Dark Side" was reissued, this time credited to John Cafferty and the Beaver Brown Band. It re-entered the Hot 100 and soared to number 7 by October. "In the late summer of 84, on the dark side was reissued, this time credited to John Cafferty and the Beaver Brown Band. **Tender Years (1983)** : A ballad from the "Eddie and the Cruisers" LP, "Tender Years" had briefly charted in 1983. Following the movie's success on cable TV, it returned to the Hot 100 in 1985 and reached number 31.
