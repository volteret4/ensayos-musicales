## members

## curiosities
**Parody Band Connection to Oasis** : Neil Innes, perhaps best known for playing a faux John Lennon in the TV Beatles' parody band The Rutles, had his song "How Sweet to Be an Idiot" cited as an obvious antecedent to Oasis's "Whatever," leading to a co-writing credit. This suggests even some of Oasis's Beatles allusions were second-hand.
