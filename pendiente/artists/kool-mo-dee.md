# artist - Kool Mo Dee

## genres
- New York Street Rap
- R&B

## songs
**Wild Wild West (1988)** : A number four R&B hit in 1988, its accompanying video was rich with cowboy imagery. However, the song itself was primarily a "straight up New York Street Rapp record," with its tales of gunplay arguably referencing showdowns on the west side of Manhattan. ## curiosities
**Featured on Will Smith's "Wild Wild West" (1999)** : Kool Mo Dee appeared as a featured artist on Will Smith's 1999 single "Wild Wild West," reprising his 1988 hook from his own song of the same title, despite the two tracks having no direct relation.
