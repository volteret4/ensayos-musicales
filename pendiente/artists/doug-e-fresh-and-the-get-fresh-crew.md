# artist - Doug E. Fresh and The Get Fresh Crew

## songs
**La Di Dadi (1985)** : The flip side of Doug E. Fresh and The Get Fresh Crew's single, this track received almost as much black radio airplay as "The Show." On it, Slick Rick crooned the melody and lyrics from A Taste of Honey's 1981 hit, "Sukiyaki," further infusing recognizable melodies into hip-hop and demonstrating a deliberate melodic crossover. **The Show (1985)** : On the A-side of this storied two-sided hit single, British-born rapper Slick Rick interpolated a few bars from The Beatles. This marked an early and notable instance of incorporating famous melodies from other genres into hip-hop, pushing the boundaries of what rap could include.
