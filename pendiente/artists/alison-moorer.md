## curiosities
**Duet with Kid Rock on "Picture"** : Alison Moorer, a country singer, collaborated with rap rocker Kid Rock on a duet version of his song "Picture," which reached number 21 on the country charts. This duet format was a common "gambit" for pop artists attempting country crossover, allowing them to gain some traction within the genre.
