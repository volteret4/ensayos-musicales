**New Generation of Women Performers (1989)** : The Indigo Girls, described as an Atlanta folk duo composed of Amy Ray and Emily Saliers, were part of a new generation of women performers broken by the Modern Rock chart in 1989. Their success indicated the chart's role in fostering female talent. "By 1989, the Modern Rock chart was breaking a new generation of women performers, including Atlanta folk duo Amy Ray and Emily Sayles, better known as The Indigo Girl." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Building a Herstory Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Road Warriors** : The Indigo Girls are described as "Road Warriors," highlighting their extensive touring experience. They appeared at various Lilith Fair dates and returned for both the 2010 Lilith Fair revival and Brandi Carlile's "The Girls Just Wanna Weekend" concerts.
