## songs
**Al Yoop** : A novelty record put together by Kim Fowley and Gary Paxton. It became a hit, leading to the formation of a touring band for The Hollywood Argales that included Derry Weaver and Marshall Leib. Gaynell Hodge played piano and Sandy Nelson played drums on the record. **Ali Oop (1960)** : This piano boogie about a caveman from the funny pages spent one week at number one in July of 1960. It was a goofy homage to the comic strip character, originally written by Dallas Frazier and re-recorded by Gary Paxton as a "beatnik proto-hippy jam" under this new fake band name. **Alley Oop (1960)** : This was a chart-topping novelty hit in 1960, described as a "lurching party record about a caveman named Alley Oop." The band was a fictional, one-off Los Angeles group. ## curiosities
**Fake Band Naming** : The group was a "fake band" named by Gary Paxton after an L.A. recording studio located on the corner of Hollywood Boulevard and Argyle Avenue. "He called his new act The Hollywood Arguiles, a fake band that Paxton named after an L.A. recording studio on the corner of Hollywood Boulevard and Arguile Avenue." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Singing Nuns and Green Tambourines Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Influence on David Bowie** : David Bowie's 1971 glam rock ballad "Life on Mars" features the line "look at those cavemen go," which was borrowed directly from The Hollywood Argyles' "Ali Oop." "If you've ever heard David Bowie's 1971 glam rock ballad life on Mars, his line about look at those cavemen go is borrowed from the Hollywood Arguiles Ali Oop as they say great artists steal." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Singing Nuns and Green Tambourines Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
