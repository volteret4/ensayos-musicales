**Openly Gay Member of Pentatonix** : Scott Hoying is one of two openly gay members of the acapella singing troupe Pentatonix, which topped the album charts in 2015 with their self-titled fourth album. This success represents a significant achievement for openly queer artists in the mid-2010s.
