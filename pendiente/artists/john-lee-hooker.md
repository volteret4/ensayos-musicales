## curiosities
**"One Chord Feel" Style** : John Lee Hooker is cited as an artist known for his "one chord feel" style, where the groove takes precedence and harmonic changes are minimized, a characteristic also observed in Roy Brown's original recording of "Good Rockin' Tonight." "The song ends up having some of the one chord feel that people like John Lee Hookahad, where the groove is all, and harmonic change is thrown out of the window." ← Episode 7： ＂Good Rockin' Tonight＂ by Wynonie Harris | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgqSPoYRUCM
