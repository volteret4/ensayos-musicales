# artist - patch-up boys

## curiosities
**Non-Rappers with Rap Hits (1980s)** : The "patch-up boys" were identified as artists from the 1980s who, despite not being primarily rappers, successfully incorporated rapping into their music to achieve major hits. This trend was also seen with Blondie during the same decade.
