# artist - Baha Men

## songs
**Who Let the Dogs Out (2000)** : This song, described as a "brain fungus," was a Top 40 hit in 2000 and was included in VH1's countdown of greatest one-hit wonders. "the Baja Men's 2000 brain fungus who let the dogs out." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > One and Done, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**You All Dat (2001)** : This single was the Baha Men's follow-up to their 2000 hit "Who Let the Dogs Out." Despite the success of its predecessor, "You All Dat" only managed to reach number 94 on the charts. "even they were able to follow their 2000 top 40 hit about unleashed canines with a 2001 single called you all death.
