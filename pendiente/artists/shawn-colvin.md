**Sunny Came Home (1997)** : This song was a Grammy winner in early 1998, taking home both Record of the Year and Song of the Year at the 1998 Grammy Awards. In the summer of 1997, the single was pressed in limited retail quantities specifically so it could appear on the Hot 100 while it was already the most played song at US Radio. It spent a month as the top airplay track and was Colvin's only Hot 100 hit. ## curiosities
**Early Career and Lilith Fair Involvement** : Shawn Colvin had sung with Suzanne Vega in the 1980s and scored her first modern rock hits in the early 1990s. She was on the bill for Lilith Fair and later was a guest at Brandi Carlile's "The Girls Just Wanna Weekend" concerts, connecting generations of female artists.
