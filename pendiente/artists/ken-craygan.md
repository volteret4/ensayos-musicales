# artist - Ken Craygan

## curiosities
**Co-founder of We Are the World (1985)** : Ken Craygan was part of the original team behind "We Are the World" in 1985. Eight years later, in 2010, he decided to mount a new version of the single, largely motivated by its 25th anniversary, which became "We Are the World 25 for Haiti."
