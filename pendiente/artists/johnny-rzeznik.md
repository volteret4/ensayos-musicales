## curiosities
**Inspiration for "Iris"** : Johnny Rzeznik, lead singer and songwriter for The Goo Goo Dolls, was inspired by the romantic travails of Nicholas Cage's fallen angel character in *City of Angels* when writing the power ballad "Iris" for the film's soundtrack. He liked the name "Iris" despite no such character existing in the movie. "The Doll's lead singer and songwriter, Johnny Resnick, was inspired by the film's plot, specifically the romantic travails of the Nicholas Cage fallen Angel character.
