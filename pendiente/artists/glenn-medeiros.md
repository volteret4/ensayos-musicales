**She Ain't Worth It (1990)** : This song, released in May 1990 and reaching number one on the Billboard Hot 100 by July, was a one-off pairing between Glenn Medeiros and Bobby Brown. It is notable for being the first chart-topping Sing and Rap, two-act pairing in Billboard history and the formal debut of the word "Featuring" on a Hot 100 number one hit. Bobby Brown's rap was a last-minute addition, comprising a quick eight-bar bridge he wrote on the spot, lasting only 20 seconds in the middle of the song, with a small snatch at the start. "The song was called She Ain't Worthy, and it was a one-off pairing between two young men who had never met before the recording session and would never work together again." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Feat.
