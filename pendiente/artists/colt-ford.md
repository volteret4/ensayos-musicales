## curiosities
**Evolution to Hitmaker** : Described as an "irrepressible striver," Colt Ford consistently worked to transition from being merely a hip-hop novelty act to a legitimate hitmaker. He frequently collaborated with various artists, including country mainstays like Jake Owen and Jason Aldean, as well as his "white country rap forebear," Bubba Sparxxx.
