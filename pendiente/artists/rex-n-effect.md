**New Jack Swing (1989)** : This song was recorded by Rex N Effect, a protégé group of Teddy Riley co-led by his brother Kell Riley. The title explicitly references the subgenre Teddy Riley had effectively owned by 1989. **Rump Shaker (1992)** : This rap single, infused with the pop and R&B swagger of New Jack Swing, became a blockbuster hit for Rex N Effect, peaking at number two on the Hot 100 in 1992. The song's video, shot in Virginia Beach, featured "lascivious images of bikini-clad women." It also marked the songwriting debut of 19-year-old Pharrell Williams, who wrote a verse deputized by Teddy Riley.
