## curiosities
**Broken by Major Labels via PBS** : Charlotte Church was among the new artists whose careers were significantly boosted when major record labels started investing in PBS pledge specials. These specials became a strategic platform for labels to introduce and establish new talent in the mainstream, capitalizing on the proven effectiveness of the pledge drive model.
