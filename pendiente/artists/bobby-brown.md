## albums
**Bobby (1992)** : This album, Brown's first new LP since the chart-dominating "Don't Be Cruel," was highly anticipated and debuted impressively at number two on the Billboard 200 album chart. It quickly spawned a number two pop, number one R&B smash produced by LA and Babyface, and also featured a cameo by Whitney Houston on a duet. **Don't Be Cruel (1988)** : Released in the summer of 1988, this album expertly blended multiple styles of late 80s black pop, establishing Brown as a major R&B-to-pop crossover megastar. At the end of 1989, Billboard named it the Number One Pop and R&B album of the year, having generated five top ten hits and achieving Sex Tuple Platinum certification. **Early Career with New Edition (1983-1986)** : Bobby Brown began his career as a member of New Edition, a Boston-based boy band often viewed as the 1980s bridge between the Jackson 5 and New Kids on the Block. During his time with the group from 1983 to 1986, they scored a dozen R&B hits, including eight top tens and three number ones, enjoying popularity with both white and especially black audiences.
