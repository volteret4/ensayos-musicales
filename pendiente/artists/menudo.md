## songs
**Hold Me** : Released during Ricky Martin's tenure with the group, "Hold Me" was one of Menudo's few English-language singles and their only single to ever reach the Hot 100, peaking at a modest number 62. The lead vocals on this track were sung by Robbie Rosa, who would later co-write several hits for Ricky Martin.
