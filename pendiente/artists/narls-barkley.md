## songs
**Crazy (2006)** : This number two hit by Narls Barkley, the duo formed by Celo Green and Brian Dangermouse Burton, was one of the "biggest and most acclaimed singles of the Aughts." Similar to Outcast's "Hey Y'all," it "straddled multiple post-hip-hop genres all at once," showcasing its genre-blending appeal.
