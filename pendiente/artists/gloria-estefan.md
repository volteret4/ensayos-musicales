## albums
**Mi Tierra** : Gloria Estefan's all-Spanish album *Mi Tierra* represented a return to her Cuban roots. Similar to Linda Ronstadt's *Canciones de Mi Padre*, this album achieved platinum status and became one of her top sellers, even though its tracks did not receive crossover airplay on Top 40 radio.
