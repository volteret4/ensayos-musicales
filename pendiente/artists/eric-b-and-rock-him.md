**Paid in Full (1987)** : This legendary "Seven Minutes of Madness mix" by British DJ duo Coldcut made "Paid in Full" a global track. Coldcut's version incorporated film dialogue, needle drops of James Brown, Israeli singer Ofra Haza, and other cuts by Eric B. and Rock him, while emphasizing the original's irresistible beat—a sample of The Soul Searchers' "Ashley's Roach clip." This remix reached number three on Billboard's Club Play Chart, becoming Eric B. and Rock him's biggest hit on any Billboard chart outside of the rap songs chart, and was a chart smash in England, Germany, and about a dozen other countries. Eric B. reportedly disliked the remix, while Rock him loved it.
