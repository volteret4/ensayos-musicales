**Somebody That I Used to Know (2012)** : Featuring Kimbra, this song is considered arguably the greatest year-end winner by a one-hit-wonder in Hot 100 history. It was remarkably original and "left field," with nothing else on the radio sounding like it at the time. It won Record of the Year at the 2013 Grammys, an award presented and endorsed by Prince.
