**Eyes Wide Open (2012)** : This song was the immediate follow-up to Gotye's massive hit "Somebody That I Used to Know." It only spent one week on the regular Hot 100, peaking at number 96, and "got nowhere near a year end Billboard chart." After this release, Gotye "never returned to the Hot 100," illustrating that year-end number ones often lack "coattails." **Somebody That I Used to Know (2011)** : This aching ballad was the only major hit by Belgian-born Australian singer-songwriter Gotye, eventually becoming America's number one song for all of 2012 and a Grammy winner for Record of the Year. YouTube played a major role in its success as a "sleeper hit." The original video, featuring Gotye and Kimbra in an empty room enacting a breakup with body paint, became a word-of-mouth hit even more than the song itself, leading to numerous YouTube covers and a rare "uber cover" by the TV show "Glee" while the song was still rising on the charts. It began an eight-week run atop the Hot 100 by spring 2012, and its video was watched over a billion times, ranking among the top 100 clips in YouTube history. "Somebody that I used to know was the only major hit by Belgian born Australian singer songwriter Goethe. But it wound up being America's number one song for all of 2012, an eventual Grammy winner for Record of the Year, and again, YouTube played a major role in making it a sleeper hit." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Twerking and Chatrouletting Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
