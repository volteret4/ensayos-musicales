# artist - Mason Ramsey

## curiosities
**The Yodeling Kid, Old Town Road Collaborator** : Mason Ramsey, known as "the yodeling kid" and country music's online sensation, added his "kitty friendly verses" to one of Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road" remixes. He also joined Lil Nas X, BTS, Diplo, and Billy Ray Cyrus for the performance of "Old Town Road" at the 2020 Grammy Awards.
