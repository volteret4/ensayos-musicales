# artist - Mason Ramsey

## curiosities
**The Yodeling Kid, Old Town Road Collaborator** : Mason Ramsey, known as "the yodeling kid" and country music's online sensation, added his "kitty friendly verses" to one of Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road" remixes. He also joined Lil Nas X, BTS, Diplo, and Billy Ray Cyrus for the performance of "Old Town Road" at the 2020 Grammy Awards. **"Yodeling Kid" on "Old Town Road" Remix (2019)** : Mason Ramsey, known as the "Yodeling Kid" after going viral on the internet, was featured at 12 years old on the second remix of Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road." His presence was a "savvy fan service" due to the song's "huge pre-pubescent fanbase," helping the song maintain its number one position against chart competitors.
