**Ashley's Roach clip (1974)** : This 1974 funk instrumental, led by Washington, DC GoGo music pioneer Chuck Brown, was not a big chart hit on its own. However, its drum break became a formative beat in rap history, sampled on dozens of hip hop classics and anchoring several big hits by other artists.
