**Baby, Come to Me (1982)** : This fiery duet with James Ingram was initially released as a single in early 1982 and flopped, peaking at number 73. Later that year, it was revived by its inclusion in the soap opera "General Hospital." The TV exposure propelled Austin and Ingram's song to number one, making it a quintessential "second chance hit" with only a five-month gap between its two chart runs. "In its first run, baby come to me peaked at number 73.
