# artist - The Real Roxanne

## members
- Adelaide Martinez

## songs
**The Real Roxanne (1985)** : Adelaide Martinez, adopting the moniker "The Real Roxanne," was recruited by UTFO to record this single as their counter-response in the Roxanne Wars. The song managed to reach number 44 on the R&B chart and, at one point in late 1985, charted simultaneously with UTFO's original and Roxanne Shanté's response.
