## albums
**Verbatim (2024)** : This album by the Palestinian Canadian musician is considered a "perfect an alt pop album" that offers solace, hope, and beauty. Nemahsis's voice is described as very raspy and clear, possessing a wide range, and she experiments with various styles including guitar pop, new wave, and folk songs. Although she wrote it as a non-political album, it became politicized due to her identity and the fact that she was abandoned by her label after October 7th, leading her to work as an independent artist since then.
