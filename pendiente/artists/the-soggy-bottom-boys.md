# artist - The Soggy Bottom Boys

## songs
**Man of Constant Sorrow (2000)** : This song, a cover of a traditional American folk song, anchored the plot and soundtrack of the Coen Brothers' film *O Brother Where Art Thou*. It was credited to the fictional band The Soggy Bottom Boys, with lead vocals lip-dubbed by star George Clooney. ## curiosities
**Fictional Band** : The Soggy Bottom Boys were a fictional band, lip-dubbed by actor George Clooney in the Coen Brothers' 2000 satirical comedy film, *O Brother Where Art Thou*. Despite being a non-existent group, their credited performance anchored the film's plot and soundtrack.
