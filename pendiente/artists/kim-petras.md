## songs
**Unholy (2022)** : Kim Petras, a trans woman born in Cologne, Germany, and based in Los Angeles, teamed up with Sam Smith for the 2022 single "Unholy." This song, which celebrated "transgressive love and sexual freedom," became a number one hit and marked the first number one by a trans artist, alongside a gender non-binary artist.
