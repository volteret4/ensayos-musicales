# artist - Ellis Hall

## curiosities
**Vocalist for Kenny G** : Ellis Hall was the singer featured on Kenny G's cover of the Junior Walker hit "What Does It Take to Win Your Love," which was the first single from Kenny G's 1986 album "Duotones" and reached number 15 on the black singles chart.
