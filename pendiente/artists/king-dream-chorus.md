## songs
**King Holiday (1986)** : Released in early 1986, this song was an awareness-building celebration for the first year Martin Luther King Day was observed as a national holiday in America. Produced and co-written by rapper Curtis Blow, the King Dream Chorus featured a lineup of R&B singers and hip-hop stars. The song reached number 30 on Billboard's Hot Black Singles chart, largely propelled by the star attraction of Whitney Houston, who had become a centerpiece of the project after not being popular enough for USA for Africa in early 1985.
