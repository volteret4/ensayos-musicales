# artist - Rory Dodd

## curiosities
**Frequent Steinman Collaborator** : Rory Dodd was a frequent collaborator with Jim Steinman and sang lead vocals on the track "Rock and Roll Dreams Come Through" from Steinman's 1981 album *Bad for Good*, which became a US Top 40 hit. "one track, rock and roll dreams come through, sung by frequent Steinman collaborator Rory Dodd, actually cracked the US top 40, reaching number 32 on the Hot 100 in the summer of 1981." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Turn Around, Bright Eyes, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Falsetto Counterpoint Vocalist** : Rory Dodd provided the prominent falsetto counterpoint on Bonnie Tyler's "Total Eclipse of the Heart," so much so that it was "practically a duet." He also performed the demo for Air Supply's "Making Love Out of Nothing at All" and provided backing vocals for Celine Dion's "It's All Coming Back to Me Now."
