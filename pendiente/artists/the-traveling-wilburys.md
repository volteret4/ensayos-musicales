**Volume One (1988)** : This debut album from the supergroup was recorded quickly after their accidental formation. It generated a string of major rock radio hits and even minor pop radio hits, proving highly successful commercially. **Accidental Supergroup Formation (1988)** : The Traveling Wilburys formed more or less by accident in early 1988 when Jeff Lynne was at George Harrison's house to record a B-side for Harrison's "Cloud 9" single "This Is Love." Bob Dylan, Tom Petty, and Roy Orbison were also present, leading to an impromptu five-man jam session that yielded "Handle with Care." The song was deemed too good for a B-side, and thus the supergroup was born. **Cornpone Pseudonyms** : The Traveling Wilburys, comprising five famous rock legends, chose to bill themselves as a gang of traveling musician half-brothers using cornpone pseudonyms. George Harrison was Nelson Wilbury, Roy Orbison was Lefty Wilbury, Jeff Lynne was Otis Wilbury, Tom Petty was Charlie T. Wilbury Jr., and Bob Dylan adopted the name Lucky Wilbury. The idea for these pseudonyms may have originated with Bob Dylan himself.
