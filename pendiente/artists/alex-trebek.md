# artist - Alex Trebek

## curiosities
**Host of Syndicated Jeopardy!** : Alex Trebek became the host of the new syndicated form of the game show "Jeopardy!". "Weird Al" Yankovic's single "I Lost on Jeopardy" was released just a couple of months before the show's return with Trebek as host.
