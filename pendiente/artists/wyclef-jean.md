# artist - Wyclef Jean

## curiosities
**Target of Lauryn Hill's "Lost Ones" (1998)** : Wyclef Jean was the perceived target of Lauryn Hill's track "Lost Ones" from her 1998 solo debut, "The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill." The song was "widely heard as a diss against her former bandmate from the Fugees," marking a significant public expression of personal and professional independence.
