## albums
**The Carnival (1997)** : Wyclef Jean's debut solo album led off with "We Trying to Stay Alive," a rap interpolation of the Bee Gees' "Stayin' Alive." This track made the top three of Billboard's rap chart, and while Barry Gibb expressed not being a fan of sampling, he and his brothers were compensated for the hit. ## curiosities
**Target of Lauryn Hill's "Lost Ones" (1998)** : Wyclef Jean was the perceived target of Lauryn Hill's track "Lost Ones" from her 1998 solo debut, "The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill." The song was "widely heard as a diss against her former bandmate from the Fugees," marking a significant public expression of personal and professional independence. **Producer and Rapper on No No No Part 2 (1998)** : Wyclef Jean both produced and rapped on Destiny's Child's first hit, "No No No Part 2," making it easy for some to assume he was the prime mover behind its success during the peak Fugees era. His involvement highlighted the interconnections between prominent artists and groups in the fusing R&B and hip-hop scene.
