# artist - Jessica Hopper

## curiosities
**Acclaimed Critic and Writer** : Jessica Hopper is an acclaimed critic with a career spanning over two decades, contributing to numerous publications including Rolling Stone, The New York Times Magazine, GQ, The Guardian, L, and Book Forum. She was also a longtime contributor to the Chicago Reader, a columnist for the village voice, Chicago Tribune, and Punk Planet, and served as the music consultant for this American life for eight years. **Research Methods for Lilith Fair Piece** : For her oral history on Lilith Fair, Jessica Hopper conducted interviews with numerous audience members. This research revealed that a key factor in the festival's success was its ability to provide a safe space, primarily for women and young women, who often felt unsafe in traditional concert environments.
