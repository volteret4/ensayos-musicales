# artist - John Hurley

## curiosities
**Co-writer of "Son of a Preacher Man"** : John Hurley co-wrote the song "Son of a Preacher Man" with Ronnie Wilkins. The song was specifically intended for Aretha Franklin but was rejected by her before becoming a signature hit for Dusty Springfield.
