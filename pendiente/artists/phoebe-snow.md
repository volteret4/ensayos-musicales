# artist - Phoebe Snow

## curiosities
**Influence on "Just the Way You Are"** : Phoebe Snow visited Billy Joel in the studio with Linda Ronstadt and played a crucial role in convincing him to include "Just the Way You Are" on "The Stranger" album, a song that Joel and his band initially considered omitting.
