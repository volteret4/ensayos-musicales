# artist - Billy Vera and The Beatles

## songs
**At This Moment (1981)** : This song, credited to Billy Vera and The Beatles, had an initial three-week run on the chart in 1981, peaking at number 79. It was dramatically revived in 1986 when the TV sitcom "Family Ties" used it as a love theme for Michael J. Fox's character, Alex P. Keaton, propelling the song all the way to number one.
