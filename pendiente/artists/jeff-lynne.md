## curiosities
**Emulating The Beatles' Production (1970s)** : Jeff Lynne, known for his work with Electric Light Orchestra, spent the 1970s emulating the production approach of his heroes, The Beatles. This production style influenced his later work with George Harrison and The Traveling Wilburys. **Formation of The Traveling Wilburys (1988)** : Jeff Lynne was present at George Harrison's house in early 1988 during a jam session with Harrison, Bob Dylan, Roy Orbison, and Tom Petty. This session led to the creation of "Handle with Care" and the unexpected formation of The Traveling Wilburys supergroup.
