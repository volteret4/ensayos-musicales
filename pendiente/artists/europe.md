## songs
**The Final Countdown (1986)** : An "absurdly catchy" song released in the spring of 1986 from the band's third album, months before Bon Jovi's "Slippery When Wet." Despite MTV playing its video in low rotation for months, radio stations initially avoided their "moose-scandinavian pop metal." However, following Bon Jovi's breakthrough, it debuted on the Hot 100 and reached the top 10 by spring 1987. In England, it went all the way to number one, becoming the first metal song to top the UK charts ever. Its "deathless keyboard riff" is noted for its enduring presence in stadiums and as ringtones.
