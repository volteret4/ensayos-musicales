# artist - Johnny Rivers

## songs
**Secret Agent Man (1966)** : This song was Johnny Rivers' theme for CBS's American broadcast of the British import "Danger Man." It successfully functioned as a standalone pop hit, independent of the show, reaching number three on the Hot 100 in 1966. Its success demonstrated that a theme song could thrive on radio if it had a distinct personality.
