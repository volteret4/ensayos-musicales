# artist - The Wellingtons

## songs
**The Ballad of Davy Crockett (1955)** : This vocal group performed the version of "The Ballad of Davy Crockett" that was actually heard on the "Disneyland" TV miniseries. Bill Hayes later recorded a chart-topping cover, but The Wellingtons' version was the one broadcast on television.
