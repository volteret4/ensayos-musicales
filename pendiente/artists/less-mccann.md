## curiosities
**Discovery of Roberta Flack** : Renowned jazz pianist and vocalist Less McCann was instrumental in Roberta Flack's career breakthrough. He discovered her while she was performing in D.C. clubs and was so impressed by her voice that he arranged her audition with Atlantic Records.
