# artist - Lee Dorsey

## songs
**Yes We Can (1970)** : This song, written by Allen Toussaint, was originally recorded by New Orleans R&B singer Lee Dorsey in 1970, becoming a minor hit (number 46 on Hot Soul Singles) before being transformed into a major success by The Pointer Sisters.
