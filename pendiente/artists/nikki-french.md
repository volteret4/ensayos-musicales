# artist - Nikki French

## songs
**Total Eclipse of the Heart (1995)** : This high-energy dance cover of Bonnie Tyler's hit, produced by Mike Stock and Matt Aitken, was a smash around the world in 1994 and 1995. In America, French's "throbbing take" on the song peaked at number 2 in June 1995, giving songwriter Jim Steinman another major hit and reestablishing "Total Eclipse" as his most successful composition.
