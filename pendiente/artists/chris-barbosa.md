## curiosities
**Producer of "Let the Music Play"** : Teenage Puerto Rican producer Chris Barbosa composed Shannon's "Let the Music Play" in his Bronx home studio, originally titling it "Fire and Ice." His track, utilizing a Roland TR-808 and synth sounds, was instrumental in defining freestyle's sound and becoming its first pop chart breakthrough.
