## curiosities
**Dream Contemporary Lilith Lineup** : In a 2015 Vulture article, the trio of sisters known as Haim "spitballed a pretty credible contemporary Lilith lineup." Their dream tour included major artists like Taylor Swift (whom they were friends with and opened for) and Lord, as well as modern indie rock acts Savages, Chvrches, Jenny Lewis, and Florence + The Machine.
