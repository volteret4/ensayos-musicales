# artist - Clean Bandit

## songs
**Rockabuy (2016)** : "Rockabuy" by Clean Bandit was the UK Christmas number one for 2016. Described as "perfectly ordinary, catchy, but not special," its chart run from mid-November all the way through mid-January exemplified the new era of streaming dominance. The song's sustained popularity meant its number one position didn't even pause for Christmas, indicating a shift away from traditional, intentionally timed holiday hits. ## curiosities
**UK Christmas Number One (2016) - Streaming Dominance** : Clean Bandit, a British group, achieved the UK Christmas number one in 2016 with their single "Rockabuy." This marked a shift in the nature of the Christmas number one, as the song's chart dominance, lasting from mid-November through mid-January, was due to the increasing influence of streaming, which meant existing hits were more likely to stay at number one rather than be unseated by novelty or charity singles.
