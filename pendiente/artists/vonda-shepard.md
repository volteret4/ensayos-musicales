**Searchin' My Soul (1997)** : A five-year-old single by Vonda Shepard that was chosen as the theme song for Fox's legal satire *Ally McBeal* in 1997. This exposure launched her into stardom, with the song charting in half a dozen countries and reaching number 22 on the American adult contemporary chart, more than half a decade after its initial recording.
