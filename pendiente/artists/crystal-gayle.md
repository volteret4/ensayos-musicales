# artist - Crystal Gayle

## songs
**You and I (1983)** : This duet with country veteran Crystal Gayle and Eddie Rabbit reached number seven on the Hot 100 in February 1983. It was Eddie Rabbit's final pop crossover hit and helped bring the "Urban Cowboy" era of country-pop to a close.
