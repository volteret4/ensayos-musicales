## curiosities
**Neo-Soul Standard (Turn of the Millennium)** : Jill Scott was cited as an example of neo-soul, a style of "adult R&B" that was typical around the turn of the millennium. "It was an adult R&B record, which around the turn of the millennium typically meant so-called neo-soul, such as D'Angelo, Erika Badu or Jill Scott." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > A Little Love and Some Tenderness Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Duet with Darius Rucker** : Darius Rucker included a duet with Jill Scott on his solo R&B album "Back to Then," which was eventually released on Hidden Beach, Scott's neo-soul imprint.
