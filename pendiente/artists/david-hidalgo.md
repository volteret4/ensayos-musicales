## curiosities
**Accordion on Bob Dylan's "Together Through Life"** : David Hidalgo, a member of Los Lobos, contributed accordion to Bob Dylan's 2009 album "Together Through Life." His addition of the instrument was a main distinguishing feature of the album's sound, contributing to its unique sonic position between the jaunty "Love and Theft" and the morose "Time Out of Mind."
