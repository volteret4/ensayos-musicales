## curiosities
**Formation and Sound** : Amy Mann formed this post-punk group, contributing her unique bass playing and vocal style to its angular, arty sound. Mann was one of only three female singers featured on the 1981 Boston punk compilation. "She soon formed a post-punk group called Young Snakes, to which she brought the unique sound of her bass playing and her voice." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be the One to Walk in the Sun, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Creative Differences** : Mann grew dissatisfied with the band's angular, arty sound, stating that the other two members "objected to any love songs or any songs that had melody" and preferred "lyrics that didn't mean much." This indicated her personal preference for more melodic and lyrically meaningful songwriting, which led to her departure. "But Mann tired of the band's angular arty sound.
