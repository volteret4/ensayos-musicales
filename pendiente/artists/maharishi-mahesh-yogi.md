# artist - Maharishi Mahesh Yogi

## curiosities
**Target of "Sexy Sadie"** : The Maharishi Mahesh Yogi was the spiritual leader with whom The Beatles had recently broken ties due to a "sexual transgression." He was the intended target of John Lennon's song "Sexy Sadie," which Lennon originally wanted to name "Maharishi." "Sexy Sadie was a kiss off to the Maharishi Mahesh Yogi.
