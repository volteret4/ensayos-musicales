**Pun-Based Hit** : The song title "Judy in Disguise with Glasses" was a direct riff on The Beatles' "Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds," and the entire number one hit was built around this pun. "By the way, that song title, Judy in disguise with glasses, was just a riff on the Beatles song title, Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds, from Sargent Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club band.
