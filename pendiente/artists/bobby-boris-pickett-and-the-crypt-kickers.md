# artist - Bobby Boris Pickett and the Crypt Kickers

## songs
**Monster Mash (1962)** : This creepy comedy classic first topped the Hot 100 in October 1962, coinciding with Halloween. It experienced two subsequent chart revivals: a brief reentry at number 91 in 1970 after a label reissue, and a more significant return in 1973, peaking at number 10 in August, far from Halloween, driven by regional radio play.
