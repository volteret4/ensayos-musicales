## curiosities
**Lyricist for "Cracked Rear View" Album Title** : The title of Hootie & the Blowfish's debut album, *Cracked Rear View*, was derived from a poignant lyric in a 1987 song by John Hiatt. While the band sometimes joked it was a "perial butt joke," it was actually a metaphor about a car's literal rear-view mirror, underscoring a deeper meaning behind the seemingly dubious title.
