**This Is America (2018)** : This song, by Donald Glover (also known as Childish Gambino), is described as an "indictment of American culture" and was a sensation upon its release. It debuted at number one on the Hot 100 a week after its "disturbing, inflammatory and much-memed video" premiered, the same night Glover debuted the song on Saturday Night Live. The video significantly fueled its number one debut more than the song itself. It went on to win the Grammy for Song and Record of the Year in 2019, noted for its blend of Afrobeats, hip hop, and withering satire, making it a unique Hot 100 chart-topper. As of 2025, it clocks in at just 11 radio spins a week, and its lifetime Spotify plays are lower than five other Childish Gambino hits.
