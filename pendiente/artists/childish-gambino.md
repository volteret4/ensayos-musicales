# artist - Childish Gambino

## curiosities
**2018 Grammy Nomination** : Childish Gambino (Donald Glover) was nominated for Album of the Year at the 2018 Grammys, a category ultimately won by Bruno Mars's "24K Magic." Mars acknowledged Gambino, alongside other nominees, for his "top shelf artistry and music."
