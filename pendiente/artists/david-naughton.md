## songs
**Making It (1979)** : Actor and "one-hit wonder" David Naughton took the 1979 theme to ABC's disco-focused show "Making It" to number five on the charts. Despite the show lasting only two months, the song peaked months after the show had gone off the air, illustrating that a TV show didn't necessarily need high ratings for its theme song to succeed.
