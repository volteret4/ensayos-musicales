## curiosities
**Early CBGB Performer** : Con Fulham, a Country and Bluegrass artist, was one of the first musicians to perform at CBGB as early as 1973, according to CBGB founder Hilly Crystal. This illustrates the club's original intention to host country, bluegrass, and blues music.
