# artist - Maureen McGovern

## songs
**Different Worlds (1979)** : Maureen McGovern performed this theme song for Donna Pescau's TV show "Angie." It reached number 18 on the Pop Chart and number one on the Adult Contemporary Chart. The show "Angie" itself lasted for two seasons. **The Morning After (1973)** : This song not only topped the charts but also won the Academy Award for Best Original Song at the 1973 Academy Awards. It was a song that Barbara Streisand had previously turned down.
