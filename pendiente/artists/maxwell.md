## songs
**Where Is The Love (2010)** : R&B chart-topper Maxwell "lured" Roberta Flack out on stage to duet with him at the 2010 Grammy Awards on this song, which Flack had made famous with Donnie Hathaway back in 1972. This performance honored the original duet and showcased its enduring appeal.
