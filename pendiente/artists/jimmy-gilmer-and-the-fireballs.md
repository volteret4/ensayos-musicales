## songs
**Sugar Shack (1963)** : This "corny, catchy, crazily popular trifle" was the Billboard year-end number one for 1963, fronted by Chicago-born Jimmy Gilmer with his New Mexico-based band. Billboard controversially categorized it as an R&B record, allowing it to chart on both the pop and soul charts despite the all-white band and its "hokey" groove. The song, a story about a guy flirting with a coffee shop waitress, spent five weeks at number one.
