## curiosities
**Limited Chart Success** : As the middle child of the Jackson family, La Toya Jackson recorded nearly a dozen albums but largely struggled to achieve significant hitmaker status. Her chart performance was modest, with over half of her R&B hits failing to reach the top 40 on that chart, and her pop chart peak never surpassing number 54.
