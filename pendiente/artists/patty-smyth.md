## songs
**Sometimes Love Just Ain't Enough (1992)** : This duet with Don Henley became Patty Smyth's biggest hit, released in 1992. It was credited directly to Patty Smyth with Don Henley, marking a successful period in her solo career after the breakup of Scandal.
