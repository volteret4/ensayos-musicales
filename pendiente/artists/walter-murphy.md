**A Fifth of Beethoven (1976)** : This 1976 smash hit was a disco arrangement of Ludwig van Beethoven's Fifth Symphony. Recorded by New York-based composer Walter Murphy, it reached number one on the Hot 100 in the fall of 1976. Coincidentally, Murphy dubbed his backing players "The Big Apple Band," leading to a name conflict with Nile Rogers and Bernard Edwards' unrelated band, which also used that name.
