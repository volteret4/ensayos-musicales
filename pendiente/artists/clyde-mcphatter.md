## curiosities
**Featured in Rock and Roll Film (1957)** : Clyde McFatter was featured in the 1957 Alan Fried film, "Mr. Rock and Roll," alongside other rock and roll and R&B stars. This film also notably included Lionel Hampton, the only jazz musician in the lineup, highlighting the cross-genre recognition of pioneers. "You can see him in the 1957 Alan Fried film, Mr. Rock and Roll, playing his vibraphone, as the only jazz musician in a film which otherwise features Little Richard, Clyde McFatter, and other rock and Darwin-based stars at the time." ← Episode 1： ＂Flying Home＂, Benny Goodman, Charlie Christian, and Lionel Hampton | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RmfeJYl84Qs
**Tour Headliner (1957)** : Clyde McPhatter headlined an otherwise all-Black tour that The Crickets were booked on, due to the mistaken belief by promoters that The Crickets were a Black doo-wop group. "The group even got booked on an otherwise all Black tour, headlined by Clyde McFatter and Otis Rush, booked by people who hadn't realised they were white." ← Episode 74： ＂It Doesn't Matter Any More＂ by Buddy Holly | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6unib5T2mDQ
