# artist - Staff Sergeant Barry Sadler

## genres

## songs
**Ballad of the Green Berets (1966)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1966 and was recorded by Staff Sergeant Barry Sadler, an army special forces member moonlighting as a singer. It resonated with Americans who were wary of the direction of 1966 popular culture and still supported the Vietnam War. Stereo Gum's Tom Bryan called it the "worst number one he's ever written about," and its success felt counter-cultural given other popular songs of the year like "96 Tears" and "When a Man Loves a Woman." **Ballad of the Green Berets (1966)** : This song, described as "not a great song" but a "legitimately big hit," spent five weeks at number one on the Hot 100, the most of any song in 1966, and sold millions. It was later claimed by Billboard as the top song of 1966 after the magazine re-ranked the year, correcting an earlier claim that "California Dreamin'" held the top spot.
