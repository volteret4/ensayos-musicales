# artist - Art Fleming

## curiosities
**Original Jeopardy! Host** : Art Fleming was the host of the original NBC version of the game show "Jeopardy!" He made a cameo appearance in "Weird Al" Yankovic's "I Lost on Jeopardy" music video.
