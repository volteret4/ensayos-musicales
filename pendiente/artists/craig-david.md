## curiosities
**Absence of British Acts on US Charts (2002)** : In April 2002, one week after a former top 10 hit by R&B and UK garage singer Craig David dropped off the Hot 100, the American Pop Chart was devoid of British acts for the first time in 38 years, pre-dating The Beatles' British Invasion.
