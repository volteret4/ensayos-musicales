# artist - LA & Babyface

## members
- Antonio Reed
- Kenneth Edmonds

## curiosities
**Rising Writer-Producers (1988-1989)** : Antonio Reed (LA Reed) and Kenneth Edmonds (Babyface) swiftly established themselves as highly successful writer-producers after their vocal group The Deele's 1988 hit "Two Occasions." They were recognized for developing a distinctive "smooth but kinetic" pop and R&B sound, which led them to produce smash hits for various artists including Karen White, Paula Abdul, and After Seven in 1988 and 1989. **Collaboration with Bobby Brown (1988-1992)** : The duo co-wrote and produced half the tracks on Bobby Brown's successful "Don't Be Cruel" album, including all of its hit singles that were not "My Prerogative." They also composed his number one R&B song "Every Little Step" and later produced a number two pop, number one R&B smash from Brown's 1992 album, "Bobby." **Collaboration with Whitney Houston (1990)** : In 1990, Clive Davis approached LA Reed and Babyface to produce tracks for Whitney Houston's third album. They ultimately produced three tracks for her album "I'm Your Baby Tonight," with the title track heralded as introducing Houston's new sound for the 90s. **Waiting to Exhale Soundtrack Production (1995)** : Kenneth "Babyface" Edmonds was the primary creative force behind the multi-platinum "Waiting to Exhale" soundtrack LP, serving as its producer and almost entirely writing its content. This album showcased Whitney Houston alongside other leading female R&B artists.
