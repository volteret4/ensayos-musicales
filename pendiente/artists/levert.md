# artist - Levert

## curiosities
**Top Five R&B Hit (1989)** : Levert had a top five hit on the R&B chart in March 1989, during the same week that Millie Vanille was also charting at number three on the R&B chart with "Girl You Know It's True." This context highlights the strong competition in the R&B genre at the time. **Temporary Crossover Success** : Levert is presented as a good example of a crossover artist who "came once" to mainstream pop success but did not maintain that presence, embodying the idea of an artist "just visiting" the pop charts.
