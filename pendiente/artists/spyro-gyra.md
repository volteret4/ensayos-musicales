**Morning Dance (1979)** : This easy listening instrumental by the jazz fusion band Spyro Gyra reached number 24 on the Hot 100 in the summer of 1979, but more significantly, it topped Billboard's Adult Contemporary chart. It was identified as "the most influential instrumental at the end of the 70s," foreshadowing the subsequent wave of mass appeal instrumental pop, known as smooth jazz.
