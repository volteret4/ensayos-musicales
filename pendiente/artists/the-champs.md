# artist - The Champs

## songs
**Tequila (1958)** : This number one hit from early 1958, produced by the L.A. Session Group The Champs, was originally issued as a B-side. It has since evolved into a ubiquitous party staple and is frequently associated with the 1985 film "Pee-wee's Big Adventure."
