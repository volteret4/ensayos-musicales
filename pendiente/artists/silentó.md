## songs
**Watch Me (Whip/Nae Nae) (2015)** : In 2015, 17-year-old Atlanta rapper Ricky Hawk, known as Silentó, released this track along with its signature dance. He dropped the song on multiple platforms including SoundCloud, YouTube, Instagram, and Vine. Although only his YouTube views counted for the Hot 100, these views were sufficient to propel "Watch Me" to number three by July 2015, underscoring YouTube's power in driving viral dance hits.
