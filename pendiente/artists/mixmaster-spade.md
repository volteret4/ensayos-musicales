# artist - Mixmaster Spade

## curiosities
**Genius of Love Adaptation (1988)** : In 1988, West Coast MC Mixmaster Spade sing-rapped his lines over the melody of "Genius of Love" while simultaneously throwing a diss at Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde. This adaptation showed the melody creeping back into the song's interpretations after its earlier conversion to pure rap, reflecting evolving approaches to melodic rap.
