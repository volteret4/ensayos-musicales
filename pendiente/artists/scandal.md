## songs
**The Warrior (1984)** : This song was Scandal's sole top 10 hit, reaching number seven in the summer of 1984. It was notably credited as "Scandal featuring Patty Smyth," a billing that reflected internal band strife and was intended to prepare the marketplace for lead singer Patty Smyth's eventual solo career.
