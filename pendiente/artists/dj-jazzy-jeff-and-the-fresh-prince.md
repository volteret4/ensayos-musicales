## songs
**Parents Just Don't Understand (1988)** : This novelty hit took the inaugural Best Rap Performance Grammy in 1989, which was the first year the category was introduced. However, the award was not televised, leading Jeff and Fresh Prince Will Smith to boycott the ceremony in protest. **Summer Time (1991)** : This hip hop track, essentially a meta-song about summer songs, was strategically written and recorded by the commercially savvy Will Smith (The Fresh Prince) to chart in the summer and pay homage to Black Summer culture. The strategy proved successful, as "Summer Time" hit number four in August 1991, becoming their only top 10 hit together, although the "official" Song of the Summer concept was not yet established. ## curiosities
**Boycott of 1989 Grammys** : DJ Jazzy Jeff and Fresh Prince (Will Smith) led a boycott of the 1989 Grammy ceremony because their Best Rap Performance award for "Parents Just Don't Understand" was not televised. This event marked an inauspicious start for rap's presence at the Grammys.
