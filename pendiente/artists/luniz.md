## songs
**I Got 5 on It (1995)** : This song was a top 10 hit in 1995, notable for its memorable hook. It was recently revived by the Jordan Peele movie "Us." According to "Hit Parade" rules, Luniz are considered a one-hit wonder because this was their only top 40 hit on either the Hot 100 or the R&B chart.
