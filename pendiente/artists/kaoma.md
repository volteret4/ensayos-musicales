## songs
**Lambada** : The French Brazilian group Kaoma recorded "Lambada," a dance and song that became a global phenomenon at the turn of the 1990s. Sung entirely in Portuguese and fueled by a "forbidden dance" that predated YouTube as a dance craze, "Lambada" topped charts in over a dozen countries and was a sizable US club hit, though only a modest pop hit.
