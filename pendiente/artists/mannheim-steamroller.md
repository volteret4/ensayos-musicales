## curiosities
**American Gramophone Label Origin** : Chip Davis founded American Gramophone in 1974 as an Omaha-based independent label, initially to release his own instrumental music project, Manheim Steamroller, a synthesizer classical fusion venture. The label later also released C. W. McCall's material. "In 1974, Davis founded American Gramophone, an Omaha-based indie label that he launched mostly to release his own instrumental music, a synthesizer classical fusion project that he called Manheim Steam Roller." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I’d Like to Teach the World to Buy Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f1264fe6d21276e0cc5
**Launch Through C.W. McCall's Success** : The unexpected chart success of C. W. McCall, which originated from an ad campaign for sliced bread, played a crucial role in launching the musical career of Chip Davis. Davis, who created the music for McCall, ultimately became far better known for his Manheim Steamroller project. "But the most interesting footnote of the CW McCall trendlet was how it helped launch the musical career of Chip Davis, who ultimately became better known for his other project." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I’d Like to Teach the World to Buy Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f1264fe6d21276e0cc5
**Funding from CW McCall Royalties** : Chip Davis, the mastermind behind Manheim Steamroller, utilized the royalty earnings from his CW McCall project to finance his personal musical venture, enabling him to resign from his previous occupation as a jingle writer. "As for Chip Davis, the composer, he was able to quit his day job as a jingle writer and use his CW McCall royalty money to fund a musical endeavor of his own." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Decoder Ring - We Got Ourselves a Convoy | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Shared Band Members with CW McCall** : The musicians who performed as the C. W. McCall band were the exact same individuals who comprised Manheim Steamroller. They would change costumes, wearing blue jeans with tailcoats for Manheim Steamroller performances and then switching to blue jean jackets for their country band persona, believing the audience never realized they were the same group. "In fact, the CW McCall band and Manheim Steam Roller were the same people. We used to wear blue jeans with tails coats for the Manheim Steam Roller Part and then we'd take off the tails coats and put on a blue jean jacket and then we were a country band." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Decoder Ring - We Got Ourselves a Convoy | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Composer's Initial Disinterest in Country** : Chip Davis, despite achieving success with the country music character CW McCall, initially had no inclination towards the genre. He once made a statement that he would never live in Nebraska nor write country music. "At one point in time, I made a statement that I said there's two things I'll probably never do in my life.
