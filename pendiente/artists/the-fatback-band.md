**King Tim III (1979)** : This track, featuring personality jock King Tim III, was an early rap experiment from the funk group. It was seen as an extension of the call-and-response music the band had been making for years, similar to Joe Bataan's approach, and predated "Rapper's Delight" as a self-consciously rap song. **King Tim III (Personality Jock) (1979)** : This 1979 single by the New Jersey funk group is regarded as an "almost accidental progenitor of rap." While Fatback was primarily a funk act, this track featured fundamental rap hallmarks like hand-clapping invitations, name-spelling, and self-aggrandizing boasts, delivered almost as a novelty by a funk band. ## curiosities
**Accidental Rap Progenitors** : The Fatback Band, a funk group active since 1973, released "King Tim III" in 1979, which, despite featuring rap elements, was presented almost as a novelty by a funk band. This track emerged when hip-hop was primarily a live performance medium, making the band an "almost accidental progenitor of rap" by committing an MC to vinyl.
