## curiosities
**Early Billy Joel Band** : The Hassles were a Long Island band that signed a recording contract, and Billy Joel played with them after being in The Echos. Their sound evolved from British invasion rock to a form of psychedelic R&B, showcasing Joel's early genre exploration.
