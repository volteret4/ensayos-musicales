# artist - The Three Tenors

## members
- Jose Carreras
- Luciano Pavarotti
- Placido Domingo

## concerts
- **PBS Pledge Drive (March 1991)** : The Three Tenors made their "biggest mark" during the March 1991 PBS Pledge Drive, where a repackaged version of their ABC-aired concert special was broadcast. This airing "defied all expectations," generating a significant amount of money for PBS and propelling the tenors into household name status, serving as an inspiration for other artists like Yani and John Tesh. "But the three tenors made their biggest mark during another major week-long event. The March PBS Pledge Drive."
- **World Cup in Italy (1990)** : The Three Tenors—Jose Carreras, Placido Domingo, and Luciano Pavarotti—performed their first concert together in 1990 as part of the World Cup festivities in Italy. "Jose Carreras, Placido Domingo, and Luciano Pavarotti performed their first concert together in 1990 as part of the World Cup in Italy."

## curiosities
**Repackaged Concert for PBS** : The Three Tenors' concert special was not originally produced for PBS; it had been previously aired by ABC the year before its highly successful run during the March 1991 PBS Pledge Drive. Its repackaging for public television proved to be an unexpected success. "The three-tenor's concert special wasn't made for PBS.
