# artist - Billy Steinberg & Tom Kelly

## genres
- Pop

## songs
**Eternal Flame (1989)** : Co-written with Susanna Hoffs, this gentle ballad became a number one hit for The Bangles, although its focus on Hoffs's lead vocal became a source of internal tension within the band. **I Drove All Night (1989)** : This vintage torch ballad was written by Steinberg and Kelly and given to Cindy Lopper, becoming a top 10 hit for her. It was originally intended for Roy Orbison and was also covered by Celine Dion. **In Your Room (1989)** : Co-written with Susanna Hoffs, this song was a rocking, slightly psychedelic hit for The Bangles, reaching number 5 on both the Hot 100 and the Modern Rock Chart. **Like a Virgin (1984)** : This song, written by Steinberg and Kelly, became a number one hit for Madonna, showcasing their ability to write "arch and clever" pop songs. **True Colors (1986)** : Written by Steinberg and Kelly, this song was offered to Cindy Lopper. They initially pictured it as gospel-flavored, similar to "Bridge Over Troubled Water," but Lopper's unique arrangement redefined it.
