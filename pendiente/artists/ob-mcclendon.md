# artist - O.B. McClendon

## curiosities
**Pioneer Black Country Artist** : O.B. McClendon was one of only two black artists, alongside Charley Pride, to reach the Top 40 of the country charts prior to The Pointer Sisters' success with "Fairy Tale."
