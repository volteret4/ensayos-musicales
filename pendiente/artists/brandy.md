## songs
**The Boy Is Mine (1998)** : This duet with Monica was the winner of the 1998 Billboard Song of the Summer and was the most dominant Song of the Summer of the 90s, spending 13 weeks at number one between June and August 1998. The song "maxed out on Sass" and, thanks to producer Rodney Jerkins, "lilted and fluttered like a butterfly circling over a hammock." "With 13 big weeks at number one, all of them racked up between June and August of 1998, the most dominant song of the summer of the 90s was Brandy and Monica's The Boy Is Mine." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Song(s) of the Summer Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef50c30a1408dc292e4
**The Boy Is Mine (1998)** : This duet with Monica commanded the summer of 1998 by spending 13 weeks at number one on the Hot 100. However, it ultimately "couldn't seal the deal at year end" and did not become the year's top song. "Or Brandy and Monica's The Boy Is Mine, which commanded the summer of 1998 with 13 big weeks at number one and couldn't seal the deal at year end." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hits of the Year Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Sittin' Up in My Room (1995)** : Brandy's song was one of three singles from the *Waiting to Exhale* soundtrack that simultaneously charted in the top 10 in mid-February 1996, a record for soundtracks at the time, not seen since *Saturday Night Fever*. All songs on the soundtrack were written by Kenneth Babyface Edmonds. "Brandy's sitting up in my room were all in the top ten simultaneously." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hooked to the Silver Screen Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Peak of Teen R&B** : Brandy is mentioned as part of the "peak of teen R&B" in 1998 on the music charts. "It wasn't just a good time in 1998 for R&B on the charts. It was the peak of teen R&B. Pink, Destiny's Child, the early years, or Brandy and Monica. Or Alia." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Raise Your Glass Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f7364fe6d21276e441d
**Atlantic Records R&B Hitmaker (circa 2000)** : Brandy was one of the contemporary R&B hitmakers with whom Atlantic Records was scoring successfully on the charts around the year 2000. Her success theoretically positioned Atlantic as an ideal label to release Darius Rucker's solo R&B project. "They had been scoring on the charts with such hitmakers as Brandy, Alia, and Timberland." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > A Little Love and Some Tenderness Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Timbaland's "Wilderness Period" (2004-2006)** : During Timbaland's two-year "wilderness period" where his tracks largely missed the top 10 or 40, he kept busy working on albums for artists like Brandy, LL Cool J, and Bubba Sparxxx. "Tim kept himself busy working on albums for artists ranging from Brandy to LL Cool J to Bubba Sparks." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Flip It and Reverse It Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Waiting to Exhale Soundtrack Contributor (1995)** : Brandy, described as a "Teen Ingenue" and a multi-hyphenate singer and actress (also starring in the TV sitcom "Moesha"), contributed Babyface-penned hits to the multi-platinum "Waiting to Exhale" soundtrack. "and was Teen Ongenu Brandy, a multi-hyphenate singer and actress who was starring in the new TV sitcom Moesha." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I'm Your Whitney Tonight Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Collaboration with Rodney Jerkins** : Brandy was one of the artists produced by R&B hitmaker Rodney Jerkins, who also produced and co-wrote Jennifer Lopez's debut single "If You Had My Love." This connection highlights the shared production talents across various mainstream pop and R&B artists of the era. "The song could just as easily have been recorded by the other artists Jerkins was producing at the time, such as Brandy, Monica, or Destiny's Child." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > La Vida Loca Edición | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
