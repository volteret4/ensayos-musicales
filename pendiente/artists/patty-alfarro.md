# artist - Patty Alfarro

## curiosities
**Vocalist for Basside Boys Remix** : Patty Alfarro was recruited by Carlos De Arza to record the female vocals for the Basside Boys' "Macarena" remix. She copied a guide track he had prepared and was specifically instructed to "just be that girl," embodying the character of Macarena in the English verses. "I said, Patty, can you come here and just be this girl? So Patty said sure.
