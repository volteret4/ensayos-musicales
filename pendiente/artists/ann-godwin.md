# artist - Ann Godwin

## curiosities
**Lyricist for "Let the Music Play"** : Ann Godwin was the ghostwriter for the lyrics of Shannon's "Let the Music Play," which explored themes of club culture, dancing as foreplay, and the blend of revelation and concealment on the dance floor.
