## songs
**Stranger on the Shore (1962)** : This sultry clarinet instrumental was the Billboard year-end number one for 1962, a time when instrumentals were popular on the charts. It was a "product of its time" and became the first song by a British artist to top the Hot 100, preceding The Beatles' chart dominance. ## curiosities
**First British Artist to Top Hot 100 (1962)** : Bernard Stanley Bilk, a native of Somerset, England, was the first Brit to reach number one on the Hot 100 with "Stranger on the Shore." This occurred before The Beatles' emergence on the charts, marking a significant moment for British artists in the US.
