## songs
**Baby, I Love Your Way / Free Bird Medley (1988)** : Florida freestyle group Will to Power's bubbly 1988 medley, which combined Peter Frampton's "Baby, I Love Your Way" and Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Free Bird" (corrected from "Leonard Skinner's Freeberg"). This medley reached number one, exemplifying the continued success of straight covers at the turn of the 80s into the 90s.
