## songs
**Baby, I Love Your Way / Free Bird Medley (1988)** : Florida freestyle group Will to Power's bubbly 1988 medley, which combined Peter Frampton's "Baby, I Love Your Way" and Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Free Bird" (corrected from "Leonard Skinner's Freeberg"). This medley reached number one, exemplifying the continued success of straight covers at the turn of the 80s into the 90s. **Baby, I Love Your Way / Free Bird Medley (1988)** : A unique mashup conceived by Bob Rosenberg, blending Peter Frampton's "Baby I Love Your Way" and Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Free Bird" into a freestyle ballad. Susie Carr sang the "Baby I Love Your Way" portion, while Rosenberg himself took on "Free Bird," resulting in an ungainly but successful medley that reached number 1.
