# artist - Mirwais Ahmadzaï

## curiosities
**Producer for Madonna's Music Album** : Mirwais Ahmadzaï, a French progressive electronic musician, served as the producer for Madonna's 2000 album "Music." He helped Madonna expand upon the "Ray of Light" formula with a different electronic sensibility.
