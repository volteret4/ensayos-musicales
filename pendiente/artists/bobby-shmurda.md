## songs
**Hot Boy (2014)** : This hit by Jamaican-American rapper Akeel Pollard, known as Bobby Shmurda, began as a full-length homemade video. The video depicted the 19-year-old Shmurda drinking, smoking, and performing his single on a Brooklyn street with friends. A key moment in the video, where Shmurda removes his cap, turns his back to the camera, and performs a side-to-side hip swivel with back-and-arm twists, was isolated by a Vine user in 2014 and dubbed "the Shmoney Dance." This Vine clip was watched over three million times in two months, during which Shmurda signed with Epic Records.
