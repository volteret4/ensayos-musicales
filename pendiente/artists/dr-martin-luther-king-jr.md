# artist - Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.

## curiosities
**Honor for Aretha Franklin (1968)** : Dr. Martin Luther King Jr., a friend of Aretha Franklin, honored her with a Southern Christian Leadership Conference Award in 1968, two months before his death. This recognition underscored her status as a black icon and a symbol of pride and confidence for African Americans during the civil rights movement.
