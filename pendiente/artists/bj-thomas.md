**Raindrops Keep Fallin' on My Head (1969)** : This song, part of the "Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid" soundtrack, was the number one song on the Billboard Hot 100 for a month, starting the week ending January 3rd, 1970. It won an Oscar for Best Original Song. Radio stations initially struggled to categorize it due to its unique sound compared to other popular music, and Ray Stevens declined to record it. BJ Thomas recorded the song while recovering from laryngitis, not believing he was at his best, but Burt Bacharach loved the performance. It features in the movie during a scene where Paul Newman, as Butch Cassidy, rides a bike with Catherine Ross. "Rain Drops Keep Falling On My Head by BJ Thomas... It is part of the soundtrack to Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 28 - Soundtracks of the 70s | https://seventies.libsyn.com/028-soundtracks-of-the-70s-12221-5-45-pm

## curiosities
**Recording During Laryngitis** : BJ Thomas recorded "Raindrops Keep Falling On My Head" while recovering from laryngitis, feeling he was not performing at his best. However, composer Burt Bacharach approved of his take. "Thomas was recovering from Lair and Gytus when he recorded the song. And so he didn't think that he was at his best, but Burke Bacchairac loved it." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 28 - Soundtracks of the 70s | https://seventies.libsyn.com/028-soundtracks-of-the-70s-12221-5-45-pm
**1970s Crossover Success** : BJ Thomas, a moderately twangy singer-songwriter, was successful in working both sides of the street in the 1970s, securing number one hits on both the Hot 100 and Hot Country Singles charts. This highlighted the permeable boundaries between pop and country music during that era. "All of these were number one hits on both the hot 100 and hot country singles." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Friends in Low Places, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
