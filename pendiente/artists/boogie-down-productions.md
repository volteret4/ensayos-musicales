# artist - Boogie Down Productions

## members
- KRS-One

## songs
**The Bridge is Over (1987)** : Considered by rap historians to be the highlight of the Bridge Wars, this 1987 BDP single is described as a "bouncy piano-based boom-bap jam." It gained significant influence, being "sampled by dozens of other rap tracks" in the future. Despite its "pop-friendly allusion" through interpolation of Billy Joel's "It's Still Rock and Roll to Me" and high praise from connoisseurs, the song did not make the national charts. **South Bronx** : This response record was recorded by Boogie Down Productions (BDP) after their leader, KRS-One, took offense at "The Bridge" suggesting Queens birthed rap. "South Bronx" directly challenged this claim, igniting "a string of disc records that came to be called The Bridge Wars."
