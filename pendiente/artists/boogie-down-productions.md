**Criminal Minded (1987)** : This debut album by New York's Boogie Down Productions, led by KRS-One, is frequently cited as a progenitor of gangster rap, alongside Schoolly D's 1985 debut and early singles by Ice-T. ## songs
**The Bridge is Over (1987)** : Considered by rap historians to be the highlight of the Bridge Wars, this 1987 BDP single is described as a "bouncy piano-based boom-bap jam." It gained significant influence, being "sampled by dozens of other rap tracks" in the future. Despite its "pop-friendly allusion" through interpolation of Billy Joel's "It's Still Rock and Roll to Me" and high praise from connoisseurs, the song did not make the national charts. **South Bronx** : This response record was recorded by Boogie Down Productions (BDP) after their leader, KRS-One, took offense at "The Bridge" suggesting Queens birthed rap. "South Bronx" directly challenged this claim, igniting "a string of disc records that came to be called The Bridge Wars." ## curiosities
**Charter Artists on Hot Rap Songs Chart (1989)** : Boogie Down Productions became "charter artists" on Billboard's Hot Rap Songs chart when it launched in 1989, indicating their consistent success and prominence in the genre as it gained dedicated chart recognition.
