# artist - Ava Max

## songs
**Sweet But Psycho (2018)** : This song by American artist Ava Max emerged as a "dark horse" contender for the UK Christmas number one in 2018. It was in a tight, neck-and-neck competition with Ariana Grande's "Thank U, Next" and LadBaby's "We Built This City on Sausage Roll," with all three songs within a few hundred streams and sales of each other.
