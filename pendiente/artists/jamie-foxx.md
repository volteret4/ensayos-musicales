# artist - Jamie Foxx

## albums
**Unpredictable (2005)** : Released on December 27, 2005, one week after Mary J. Blige's "The Breakthrough," this was Jamie Foxx's first new album after a decade focused on his acting career. It featured guest appearances from Mary J. Blige and several rap superstars including Common, Kanye West, Snoop Dogg, The Game, and Ludacris. The album followed "The Breakthrough" into the number one spot in January 2006, and the two discs alternated holding the Billboard 200's top position for more than a month.
