## curiosities
**Side Project for Laughs (mid-1990s)** : Ming Tea was formed in the mid-1990s as a side project by Susanna Hoffs, songwriter/guitarist Matthew Sweet, and comedian Mike Myers, purely for enjoyment. It was conceived as a faux British 60s band. "Susanna Hoffs and her friend Songwriter and Guitarist Matthew Sweet formed a side project just for laughs with comedian Mike Myers, a faux British 60s band they called Ming-T." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be the One to Walk in the Sun, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Inspiration for Austin Powers Character (mid-1990s)** : The Ming Tea project was instrumental in inspiring Mike Myers to create his iconic time-traveling British spy character, Austin Powers, leading to the hit film series.
