# artist - Rebbie Jackson

## genres
- Pop
- R&B

## songs
**Centipede (1984)** : This song, written and produced by her brother Michael Jackson, was Rebbie Jackson's first of the Jackson sisters to score a top 40 hit. It performed respectably in the fall of 1984, reaching number four on Billboard's R&B chart and number 24 on the Hot 100, though it was her only pop hit. ## curiosities
**Eldest Jackson Sibling** : Rebbie Jackson was the eldest sibling of the Jackson family, and the first of the three Jackson sisters to score a top 40 hit. She continued to have a handful of top 40 R&B hits throughout the rest of the 1980s.
