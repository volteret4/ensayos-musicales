# artist - Ludacris

## genres

## songs
**Stand Up (2003)** : This number one hit by fellow Atlanta recording artist Ludacris was "knocked out" of the top spot on the Hot 100 by Outcast's "Hey Y'all" in late 2003. The event highlighted the stark contrast between Ludacris's contemporary Atlanta hip hop sound and Andre 3000's departure from the genre.
