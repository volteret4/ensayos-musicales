## curiosities
**Creedence Clearwater Revisited (1993)** : Doug Clifford, along with Stew Cook, formed and toured as "Creedence Clearwater Revisited" starting in 1993. This controversial move took place after the original band's breakup and amidst John Fogerty's legal struggles over the use of the Creedence name.
