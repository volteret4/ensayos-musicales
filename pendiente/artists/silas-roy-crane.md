# artist - Silas Roy Crane

## curiosities
**Mentor and Label Co-founder** : Silas Roy Crane was Sam Cook's former mentor and an ongoing advisor from The Soul Stirrers. He was honored as one of the namesakes for SAR Records, which stood for Sam, Alex, and Roy, alongside Sam Cook and J.W. Alexander. "It was named after Sam Cook, J.W.
