# artist - The Oak Ridge Boys

## genres
- Country

## songs
**Elvira (1981)** : The Oak Ridge Boys' cover of Dallas Frazier's "Elvira" reached number five on the Hot 100 in the summer of 1981. This hit showcased a kitschy pop sensibility, sharing space in the top 10 with Juice Newton's "Queen of Hearts," and exemplified songs crossing from country to pop in the wake of "Urban Cowboy." "Like the Oak Ridge Boys cover of Dallas Frazier's Elvira had a kitschy pop sensibility.
