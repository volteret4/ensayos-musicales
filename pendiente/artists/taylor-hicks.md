# artist - Taylor Hicks

## songs
**Do I Make You Proud (2006)** : As the winner of "American Idol" season five in 2006, Taylor Hicks's coronation song, "Do I Make You Proud," debuted at number one on the Hot 100. He was the last "American Idol" finalist to score an instant Hot 100 number one before the show's cultural relevance began to wane. ## curiosities
**Last American Idol Instant #1 Debut** : Taylor Hicks was the last "American Idol" finalist to score an instant Hot 100 number one with his coronation song "Do I Make You Proud" in 2006. His achievement marked the end of the half-decade "Idol-driven chart toppers" as the show's cultural relevance began to slightly decline.
