## songs
**Banned in the USA (1990)** : Luther Campbell, under the one-off nom de rap Luke, scored a top 20 hit in 1990 with "Banned in the USA," which sampled Bruce Springsteen. The credit read "Luke featuring the two live crew," a strange moniker given that Luke was a member of the Two Live Crew, similar to the Wham! featuring George Michael anomaly.
