# artist - Glen Medeiros

## songs
**She Ain't Worth It** : This song, featuring Bobby Brown, reached number one and is noted as an early instance of a "featuring" credit being used, although not for a special guest artist. Instead, it was part of a "weird contractual arrangement" to highlight an artist already associated with the release.
