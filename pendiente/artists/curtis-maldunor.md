# artist - Curtis Maldunor

## genres

## songs
**Sephiron** : An early 70s British folk song whose "familiar sounding lyrics" were borrowed and formed the backbone of Madonna's title track, "Ray of Light." The duo were ultimately given songwriting credit on Madonna's track.
