# artist - Tim McGraw

## genres
- Country

## albums
**Not a Moment Too Soon (1994)** : McGraw's debut album spent two weeks (a fortnight) at number one on the Billboard 200 and achieved quadruple platinum status within a year. It debuted at number one, preventing Reba McEntire's "Read My Mind" from reaching the top spot. ## curiosities
**Vocals on Nelly's "Over and Over" (2004)** : Tim McGraw provided "soulful high-loan some vocals" for Nelly's acclaimed 2004 single "Over and Over." This collaboration marked a significant cross-genre effort, though the song was not promoted to country stations. "rapper Nellie's acclaimed single Over and Over featured soulful high-loan some vocals from Nashville Megastar Tim McGraw." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Ride ’til I Can’t No More Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Top Country Artist and Power Couple** : Tim McGraw is identified as one of the top artists in country music and, with his wife Faith Hill, forms "Nashville's ultimate recording power couple." This performance recreated a moment from Swift's earliest career, honoring her roots and connection to the country music community.
