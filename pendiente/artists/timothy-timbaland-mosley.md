# artist - Timothy "Timbaland" Mosley

## curiosities
**Production for Bubba Sparxxx (2001)** : As part of a black production team including Organized Noise, Timbaland supplied the "thumping beats" for Bubba Sparxxx's debut album "Dark Days Bright Nights." He also produced the video for Bubba's song "Ugly."
