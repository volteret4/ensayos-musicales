## songs
**Forget You (2010)** : A "comically foul-mouthed retro Motown-style pastiche" penned by The Smeezingtons for CeeLo Green, famously known on radio as "Forget You" (original title "Fuck You"). This song became a number two Hot 100 smash, further demonstrating The Smeezingtons' stylistic versatility.
