## curiosities
**Dayton, Ohio Funk Pioneer** : Roger Troutman, along with his band Zapp, originated from Dayton, Ohio, a city known as the "birthplace of funk." He was a recognizable figure in the local community, often seen in everyday places like Kroger. His contributions, alongside other Dayton funk bands, helped define the sound of the 70s and 80s. "For example, Roger Troutman and Zap, he was definitely from Dayton.
