# artist - Barack Obama

## curiosities
**Association with "Yes We Can Can" (2008)** : During his 2008 presidential campaign, Barack Obama's rallies frequently featured The Pointer Sisters' 1973 hit "Yes We Can Can," which became totemic for anticipating his campaign slogan.
