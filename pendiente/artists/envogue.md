## songs
**Yesterday (1992)** : The R&B girl group Envogue released their cover of "Yesterday" in 1992 as an album cut. Due to the chart rules at the time, it was not allowed on the Hot 100, but it did receive radio airplay, reaching number 73 on Billboard's pop radio chart and number 29 on the R&B radio chart.
