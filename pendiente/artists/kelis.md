## songs
**Caught Out There (1999)** : This song, written by Pharrell Williams and Chad Hugo as a showcase for Kelis (Colise Rogers), was an unconventional R&B track that incorporated the swagger of a hip-hop beef record and the raw crunch of alternative rock, serving as a diatribe against cheating men. Kelis, despite possessing a sweet singing voice, "literally shouts the chorus." It became a top 10 R&B hit in 1999 and a top 10 pop hit in half a dozen countries worldwide, marking a significant artistic victory for both Kelis and The Neptunes. ## curiosities
**Fierce Persona (1999)** : Kelis, born Colise Rogers, projected an "out of the box" fierceness as a young New Yorker fresh out of high school. This persona was showcased in her song "Caught Out There," which was written by The Neptunes specifically for her. "A young New Yorker, just out of high school, named Colise Rogers, who recorded simply as Colise, and out of the box, Colise projected fierceness." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Flip It and Reverse It Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Timbaland's Admiration (Late 1990s)** : While The Neptunes were recording "Caught Out There" with Kelis in Master Sound Studios in Virginia Beach, Timbaland and Missy Elliott were working next door. Timbaland recalled hearing the track for the first time and expressing his strong admiration for the beat, wishing he had made it himself. "And I was working in the studio A and then they had a studio B. And I walked to the back door, and I said, Missy, you got to hit it. And I was like, oh man, I wish I had made that beat. That was the beat." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Flip It and Reverse It Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
