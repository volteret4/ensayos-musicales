## curiosities
**Career Trajectory** : Tommy Roe quit his job as an electrician in the early 1960s to pursue pop songwriting and performing, ultimately scoring six top 10 hits in the 60s, including two number ones. "Tommy Row, who quit his job as an electrician in the early 60s to write and perform pop songs, is best known when he is remembered for Dizzy, his 1969 smash." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Singing Nuns and Green Tambourines Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Adaptability to Pop Trends** : Roe was writing and singing bubblegum hits before the genre even had a name, and later, when pop became trippy in the late 60s, he successfully adapted to the "new hippie bubblegum sound" and returned to number one. "So Tommy Row was writing and singing bubblegum hits before that mini genre had a name. He kept scoring hits through the mid 60s, like sweet pea and hooray for hazel. Then when pop got trippy in the late 60s, Row, still in his 20s, adapted to the new hippie bubblegum sound, and went right back to number one." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Singing Nuns and Green Tambourines Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**60s Hitmaker** : Tommy Roe was a serious hitmaker in the 1960s, scoring eight Top 40 hits since 1962, with "Dizzy" being his fifth Top 10 hit.
