# artist - 702

## genres
- hip hop influenced R&B

## songs
**Steelo (1996)** : This hit by the R&B girl group 702 was produced by Missy Elliott, who created its underlying beat by transforming The Police's 1980 song "Voices Inside My Head." "Steelo" reached number 12 on the R&B chart and became a top 40 pop hit in 1996.
