## songs
**Rock Me Tonight (1985)** : This hit by slow jam king Freddie Jackson topped the R&B chart back-to-back with Whitney Houston's "You Give Good Love" in 1985. His success demonstrated that the top R&B artist of the year wasn't necessarily a pop crossover star like Michael Jackson, highlighting a distinct R&B trend.
