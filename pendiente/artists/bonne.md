# artist - Bonne

## songs
**What's Going On (2001)** : Bonne served as executive producer for the 2001 all-star cover of Marvin Gays' "What's Going On." The single, produced by Jermaine Dupri, boasted numerous mega-stars and was originally intended for AIDS research, later benefiting the Red Cross after the 9-11 attacks.
