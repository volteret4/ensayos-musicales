# artist - Martin Quittenden

## songs
**Maggie May (1971)** : Co-written by Martin Quittenden and Rod Stewart, this song became a number one hit for Stewart in 1971. It was based on one of Stewart's early sexual exploits and featured a mandolin solo, becoming a defining track for his "cheeky Rod persona."
