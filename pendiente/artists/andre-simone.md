# artist - Andre Simone

## curiosities
**Producer of Jody Watley's "Friends"** : Andre Simone was the hip-hop lead producer for Jody Watley's 1989 hit "Friends." He intentionally cleared space in the mix to give prominent showcase to Rakim's rap and Eric B.'s scratching, thereby setting a new template for pop-rap tracks.
