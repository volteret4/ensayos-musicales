## curiosities
**Self-Proclaimed and Billboard-Certified "No Longer One-Hit Wonder"** : Lewis Capaldi jokingly called himself a one-hit wonder when "Someone You Loved" topped the charts. However, with "Before You Go" reaching the Top 10 in 2020, Billboard magazine published an article in August 2020 explicitly declaring him "no longer a one hit wonder," recognizing his second successful US hit.
