## albums
**Hooked on Classics (1981)** : This album, a medley of well-known classical pieces including "Flight of the Bumblebee," "Rhapsody in Blue," and "The Marriage of Figaro," was unexpectedly issued by the London's Royal Philharmonic Orchestra. It achieved significant commercial success, reaching number ten on the Hot 100, demonstrating that even respected classical ensembles participated in the medley craze. ## songs
**Hooked on Classics (1982)** : This track, released in early 1982, smashed together snippets of well-known classical pieces such as "Flight of the Bumblebee," "Rhapsody in Blue," and "The Marriage of Figaro." It was built upon a relentless "Stars on 45" like clap beat and reached number 10 on the Hot 100, reflecting a fleeting fad rather than an enduring instrumental pop form.
