# artist - Epic Records

## curiosities
**Stalling for Time** : Epic Records, the label for Sly and the Family Stone, actively stalled for time during Sly Stone's period of deep addiction and inactivity, during which he did not produce new studio albums for over two years. This included reissuing old tracks and releasing a *Greatest Hits* compilation with a novel marketing strategy.
