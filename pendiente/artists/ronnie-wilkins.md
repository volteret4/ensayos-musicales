# artist - Ronnie Wilkins

## curiosities
**Co-writer of "Son of a Preacher Man"** : Ronnie Wilkins co-wrote the song "Son of a Preacher Man" with John Hurley. The song was specifically intended for Aretha Franklin but was rejected by her, later becoming a signature hit for Dusty Springfield.
