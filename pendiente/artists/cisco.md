# artist - Cisco

## genres

## songs
**Incomplete (2000)** : This ballad topped the Hot 100 in August of 2000, becoming Cisco's number one hit. It was released as a CD single, with "Thong Song" included as a bonus track or B-side. It achieved respectable radio airplay, especially on R&B radio, and stellar sales, with hundreds of thousands buying the single, many to obtain the "Thong Song." In the last year, "Incomplete" was played on the radio about 2,700 times and streamed a little over 12 million times. **Thong Song (2000)** : This R&B track reached number 3 on the Hot 100 in May of 2000. It was never issued as a physical retail single, charting based solely on considerable radio airplay. Nowadays, it is significantly more popular than his number one hit "Incomplete," generating over 6,000 radio spins and nearly 30 million streams in the last year, effectively doubling "Incomplete"'s numbers.
