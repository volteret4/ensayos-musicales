## curiosities
**Major Pop Act & Chart Rival (1989)** : Paula Abdul was one of the biggest new pop acts of 1989, and notably, she was the only artist to score as many number one hits that year as Millie Vanille. She also performed on the inaugural Club MTV package tour, marking a significant presence in the late 80s pop scene. "And Paula Abdul, one of the biggest new pop acts of the year and the only artist scoring as many number ones that year as Millie Vanille." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Choreographic Lineage** : Paula Abdul is recognized for choreographing some of Janet Jackson's influential dance moves. This choreography has been passed on through generations of pop acts, potentially echoing in videos like Normani's "Motivation." **Attempt to Match Michael Jackson's Record** : Paula Abdul was among several stars who attempted to pull five number one hits from a single album after Michael Jackson's "Bad" accomplished it in 1987. She managed to achieve a "forceum" (four) of number ones from her respective albums but did not reach five.
