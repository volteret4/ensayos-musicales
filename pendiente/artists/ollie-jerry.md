## songs
**Breakin'... There's No Stopping Us (1984)** : This song, performed by electro-soul duo Ollie and Jerry, reached number nine on the charts in August 1984. It was generated from the film "Breakin'," which was one of two teen exploitation dance movies released by Canon Films that year, further popularizing breakdancing culture.
