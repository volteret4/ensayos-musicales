**Hide and Seek** : This moody electronic a cappella single soundtracked *The O.C.*'s season 2 finale, helping to break Imogen Heap's career. It became a gold-selling digital download and later spawned a Hot 100 number one hit when Jason Derulo prominently sampled it for his 2009 smash "Whatcha Say."
