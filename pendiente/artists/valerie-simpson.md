# artist - Valerie Simpson

## songs
**Ain't No Mountain High Enough (1970)** : Valerie Simpson co-wrote the radical reimagining of this song for Diana Ross's first post-Supremes solo single in 1970, featuring Ross speaking more lyrics than singing. Her talents contributed to making this single a majestic smash. "Her remake of Marvin Gaye and Tammy Turell's Ain't No Mountain High enough was a radical reimagining by songwriters Nick Ashford and Valerie Simpson with Ross speaking more of the lyrics than she sang." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bad Moon on the Rise Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Stuff Like That (1978)** : Valerie Simpson contributed to the lyrics and melodic hooks for this Quincy Jones track, performing vocal work alongside Nick Ashford and Chaka Khan. This collaboration resulted in an R&B chart-topper and a Top 40 pop hit for Jones.
