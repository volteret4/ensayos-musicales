# artist - Julian Temple

## curiosities
**Director of "Learning to Fly" Music Video (1991)** : Julian Temple directed the music video for Tom Petty and The Heartbreakers' "Learning to Fly." The video, starring Johnny Depp and Faye Dunaway, satirized the music video era and the teen rock idols of the early 90s pop scene.
