# artist - American Football

## songs
**Never Meant** : A soundfont version of "Never Meant" by American Football, titled "Never Mint 64" and created by Canalos Gerjou, was the first soundfont song to go viral. This track served as an inspiration for other creators in the soundfont community, marking a significant moment in the genre's popularization. "La primera canción volverse viral fue Deber Mint 64, una versión de Neber Mint de la banda M a Medican Football." ← El fenómeno de Soundfont Album.
