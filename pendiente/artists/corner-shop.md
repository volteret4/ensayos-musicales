## curiosities
**Protest Against Morrissey (1992)** : The band Corner Shop gained early recognition in the music press in 1992 when they publicly burned a poster of Morrissey in front of EMI's headquarters. This act was a protest against some of Morrissey's then-recent solo songs, to which they took "understandable exception."
