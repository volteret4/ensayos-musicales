## curiosities
**Name Conflict with Walter Murphy** : The Big Apple Band, formed by Nile Rogers and Bernard Edwards, was forced to change its name after New York-based composer Walter Murphy coincidentally used "The Big Apple Band" as the name for his backing players on his 1976 number one hit "A Fifth of Beethoven." This conflict led Rogers and Edwards to adopt the name "Sheik" for their band.
