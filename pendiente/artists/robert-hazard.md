## songs
**Escalator of Life (1983)** : This was Robert Hazard's only appearance on the Hot 100, peaking at number 58 in mid-1983. He was characterized as a "would-be hitmaker who never quite hit," an American artist attempting to emulate a British new wave sound. **Girls Just Want to Have Fun** : Hazard wrote and demoed this song, originally an "ode to girls and their frivolity" from a male perspective, described as "quirky, horny, and kind of sexist." His version depicted a boy warned by his parents about "good time girls," before it was famously transformed by Cyndi Lauper.
