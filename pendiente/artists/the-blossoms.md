## songs
**He's a Rebel (1962)** : This song was recorded by The Blossoms, featuring Darleen Love's powerful lead vocals, but it was controversially credited to The Crystals by producer Phil Spector. Spector, desperate to release a version before Vicky Carr's, used The Blossoms but printed The Crystals' name on the single, which subsequently spent two weeks at number one on the Hot 100 in November 1962. "Desperate to get a version of this surefire hit into stores before a competing version by singer Vicky Carr, Phil Spector recorded the Blossoms singing it, featuring Darleen Love's powerhouse vocals. **Uncredited Backup and Lead Vocals** : This LA trio frequently sang backup on numerous hit records but never broke into the Top 40 under their own name. They operated under the erratic direction of producer Phil Spector, who viewed them, including lead singer Darleen Love, as essentially interchangeable performers. "This LA trio, who sang backup on dozens of hit records, not only never broke into the top 40, at least under their own name, they suffered under the whims of the renowned genius and infamously, erasible producer Phil Spector." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be My Baby-Baby-Baby Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Producer's Manipulation** : Phil Spector, while impressed with lead singer Darleen Love, orchestrated for The Blossoms to record "He's a Rebel" but credited it to The Crystals because the latter group could not attend the studio session in time. "Spector was mainly impressed with the Blossoms lead singer, the legendary Darleen Love. **Session Work Recognition** : After Darleen Wright joined in 1957, The Blossoms' material improved significantly, leading them to record singles for various labels and gain recognition for session work, backing artists such as Sam Cooke.
