## curiosities
**"Pet Sounds" Co-writer (1966)** : Tony Asher co-wrote most of the *Pet Sounds* album with Brian Wilson, including the lyrics for "God Only Knows." He noted the difficulty of using the word "God" in a pop song during that era.
