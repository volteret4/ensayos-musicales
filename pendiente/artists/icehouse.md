## songs
**Electric Blue (1987)** : This catchy synth pop track was co-written by John Oates and Icehouse singer Iva Davies. John Oates admitted it "could easily have been a Hall and Oates" song. Released as an Icehouse single in 1987, it reached number 7 on the Hot 100 by May 1988, becoming a Top 10 hit during Hall and Oates' hiatus.
