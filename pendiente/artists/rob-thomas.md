**Smooth (1999)** : Rob Thomas, the singer for Matchbox Twenty, co-wrote and sang on "Smooth," a collaboration with Carlos Santana. This song was the lead single from Santana's *Supernatural* album, featuring a cha-cha beat and prominent horns. "Smooth" topped the Hot 100 in October 1999 for a dozen weeks and later won Record and Song of the Year at the Grammy Awards.
