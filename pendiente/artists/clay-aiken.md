# artist - Clay Aiken

## songs
**This Is The Night (2003)** : As the runner-up of the second season of "American Idol," Clay Aiken's debut single, "This Is The Night," went from nowhere on the Hot 100 to instant number one status in June 2003. This was largely due to his devoted fanbase, the "Claymates," who bought nearly 400,000 copies in its first week, compensating for the song's "near total lack of radio spins." This marked the reopening of the Hot 100 to number one debuts after a five-year hiatus caused by a chart policy change. **The Claymates Fandom** : Clay Aiken's "army of fans," who called themselves the "Claymates," were determined to make him a chart topper. Their collective buying power of nearly 400,000 copies of "This Is The Night" in its first week was enough to propel the song to number one, despite its lack of radio airplay.
