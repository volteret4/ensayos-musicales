# artist - Clay Aiken

## albums
**Measure Of A Man (2003)** : Clay Aiken's debut album, "Measure Of A Man," hit number one in 2003, selling with "blockbuster sales" to his "army of claimates." Aiken was the runner-up on American Idol that year, and he would later come out as gay in 2008, a revelation that "broke his female fan's hearts." ## songs
**This Is The Night (2003)** : As the runner-up of the second season of "American Idol," Clay Aiken's debut single, "This Is The Night," went from nowhere on the Hot 100 to instant number one status in June 2003. This was largely due to his devoted fanbase, the "Claymates," who bought nearly 400,000 copies in its first week, compensating for the song's "near total lack of radio spins." This marked the reopening of the Hot 100 to number one debuts after a five-year hiatus caused by a chart policy change.
