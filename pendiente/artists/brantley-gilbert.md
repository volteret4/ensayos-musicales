# artist - Brantley Gilbert

## songs
**Dirt Road Anthem (2008)** : Brantley Gilbert originally sang the "sweet, melodic" chorus for Colt Ford's "Dirt Road Anthem" in 2008, a part that was "squarely suited for country radio." "A honky-tongue rap whose chorus sung by Brantley Gilbert was sweet, melodic, and squarely suited for country radio." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Ride ’til I Can’t No More Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Dirt Road Anthem (2010)** : Gilbert rerecorded "Dirt Road Anthem" with Colt Ford listed as the featured act. This version was "very similar to Ford's," with slightly longer song portions, and while it didn't chart, it provided "a template for how the song could retain Ford's rap while going fully country."
