# artist - Ferel Williams

## concerts
- **2014 Grammy Awards Performance** : Ferel Williams, along with Duffbunk, Nile Rodgers, and special guest Stevie Wonder, performed a medley of "Get Lucky" and Stevie Wonder's "Another Star" at the 2014 Grammy Awards at the Staples Center. "Draft Pump, Ferel, Nile Rogers and Special guest Stevie Wonder performed a medley of Get Lucky and Wonders Classic, another star."

## songs
**Get Lucky (2013)** : Co-written, performed, and co-credited to Duffbunk featuring Ferel Williams and Nile Rodgers, this monster hit single from "Random Access Memories" shot up the charts to number two on the Hot 100 in the summer of 2013.
