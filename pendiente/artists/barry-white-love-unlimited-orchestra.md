# artist - Barry White & Love Unlimited Orchestra

## genres
- Philly Soul
- Proto-Disco

## songs
**Love's Theme (1973)** : This Philly Soul instrumental, featuring proto-disco strings, was a significant hit that topped Billboard's Hot 100. It signaled an early breakthrough for what would become disco music, preceding Donna Summer's "Love to Love You Baby."
