## songs
**Amazed (2000)** : A ballad by the Nashville-based country group Lonestar that topped the Hot 100 in 2000. This marked the first country song to reach number one on the main pop chart since Kenny Rogers and Dolly Parton's "Islands in the Stream" 17 years earlier.
