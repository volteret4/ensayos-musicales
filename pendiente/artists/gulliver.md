## albums
**Gulliver (1970)** : This self-titled studio album was released in 1970 by singer-songwriter Tim Moore's band, Gulliver, which Darryl Hall briefly joined. Signed to Elektra Records, the album was characterized by "groovy psychedelic pomp" but ultimately failed to sell.
