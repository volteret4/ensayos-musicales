# artist - Chris Stapleton

## songs
**Blow (2019)** : Country star Chris Stapleton teamed up with Bruno Mars and pop singer-songwriter Ed Sheeran on the 2019 single "Blow." This genre-crossing collaboration resulted in Mars's first appearance on Billboard's Rock charts.
