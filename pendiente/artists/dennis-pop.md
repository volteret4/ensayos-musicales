# artist - Dennis Pop

## curiosities
**Heart and Soul of Cheron Studios** : Dennis Pop was regarded as the "heart and soul" of Cheron Studios. His death in 1998 had a lasting impact, with Max Martin and his team never fully getting over it, ultimately contributing to the decision to close Cheron Studios at the end of 2000.
