# artist - Dottie West

## curiosities
**Grammy Female Country Vocalist Nomination (1974)** : Dottie West was nominated for a Grammy for Female Country Vocalist of the Year in 1974, alongside Olivia Newton-John, Maria Osmond, Barbara Fairchild, and Tammy Wynett. **Country Music Icon** : Dottie West was a veteran female country vocalist nominated for Best Country Vocal Performance Female at the 1974 Grammys, which she lost to Olivia Newton-John. She also attended the protest meeting against Newton-John's perceived pop incursion into country.
