## songs
**Voices that Care (1991)** : This single was produced by David Foster in the run-up to the first Persian Gulf War, featuring prominent vocals from Michael Bolton and a Kenny G sax solo. It aimed to be pro-troops and was designed not to offend, featuring non-singing luminaries like Sally Field, Wuppie Goldberg, and Wayne Gretzky. The song peaked at number 11 on the charts, just shy of the top 10, and went gold, staying on the Hot 100 for just under four months. ## curiosities
**Vague Politics and Diverse Participants (1991)** : The politics of "Voices that Care" were deliberately vague to avoid offending, allowing famous lefties like Sally Field and Wuppie Goldberg to vocalize alongside conservatives like Wayne Gretzky without harming their public profiles. The single also featured more non-singing luminaries than usual, and its lyrics were later critiqued by a Simpsons showrunner as "apparent meaninglessness."
