# artist - Hillary Lindsay

## curiosities
**Co-writer of "Million Reasons"** : Hillary Lindsay, a Nashville songwriter, co-wrote Lady Gaga's song "Million Reasons." She later collaborated with Gaga again on "A Star Is Born."
