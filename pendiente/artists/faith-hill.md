## songs
**Breathe (2000)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 2000, notably a "number two hot 100 hit" that achieved the year-end top spot. It was a "stealth blockbuster," debuting on the Hot 100 in November 1999 and remaining on the chart for over a year, heavily played on country, Top 40, and adult contemporary radio. This power ballad triumphed over two massive Santana singles, "Smooth" and "Maria Maria," each of which spent over 10 weeks at number one. ## curiosities
**Country Megastar and Power Couple** : Faith Hill is identified as a country megastar. She and her husband, Tim McGraw, are referred to as "Nashville's ultimate recording power couple." Taylor Swift was inspired to move to Nashville after watching a VH1 "Behind the Music" special about Hill. **Concert Guest Appearance in Nashville** : Faith Hill made a surprise appearance at Taylor Swift's last concert in Nashville, joining her onstage with Tim McGraw to sing "Tim McGraw." This guest spot was part of Swift's longstanding concert tradition of bringing out starry visitors from the cities she tours in.
