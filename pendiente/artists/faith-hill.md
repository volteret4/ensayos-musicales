## songs
**Breathe (2000)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 2000, notably a "number two hot 100 hit" that achieved the year-end top spot. It was a "stealth blockbuster," debuting on the Hot 100 in November 1999 and remaining on the chart for over a year, heavily played on country, Top 40, and adult contemporary radio. This power ballad triumphed over two massive Santana singles, "Smooth" and "Maria Maria," each of which spent over 10 weeks at number one.
