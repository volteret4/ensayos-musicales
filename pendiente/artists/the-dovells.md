**Bristol Twist in Annie** : This record by The Devils, released on Cameo Parkway, was a multi-layered sequel. It served as a follow-up not only to "The Twist" craze but also to their own previous hit "Bristol Stomp" and Hank Ballard's earlier "Annie recordings." "The devils, a group on cameo parkrey who'd had a hit with the Bristol stump, recorded Bristol Twist in Annie, which managed to be a sequel not only to the twist, but to their own the Bristol stump, and to Hank Ballard's earlier Annie recordings." ← Episode 91： ＂The Twist＂ by Chubby Checker | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LzsS8EXdsm8

## curiosities
**The Bristol Stop (1961) Origin** : The Dovells' 1961 number two smash, "The Bristol Stop," was another dance craze single that originated in Philadelphia. Released on Cameo Parkway, it was named after The Bristol suburb of Philadelphia. "One year later, so did the Devel's number two smash The Bristol Stop. Another dance craze single on Camille Parkway named for The Bristol Subur of Philadelphia." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Rock ’n Soul, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Coexistence and Competition on Charts** : As a white doo-wop group, The Dovells' success with "The Bristol Stop" in Philadelphia demonstrated how white groups coexisted and competed on the charts with Black artists like Chubby Checker during the 1950s and 60s, influencing young Darryl Hall and John Oates. "where the black Chubby Checker and the white duop group The Devel's not only coexisted but competed on the charts." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Rock ’n Soul, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
