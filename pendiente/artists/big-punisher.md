## songs
**I'm Not A Player (1998)** : This song by Big Punisher was a number 19 R&B hit in 1998. Billboard's practice at the time was to track remixed hits separately only if they had a different title, setting a precedent for similar but distinct tracks. **Still Not a Player (1998)** : This deeply melodic track, featuring singer Joe (Joseph Thomas), represented Big Punisher's biggest rap-to-pop crossover, reaching number 24 on the pop charts and number six on the R&B charts. "Their deeply melodic still not a player was a number 24 pop, number six R&B hit in 1998." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say My Name, Say My Name, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Still Not a Player (1998)** : Following "I'm Not A Player," Big Punisher released this similarly titled but "completely separate hit" in 1998. Although referred to as a "remix," it was an "all new track." It reached number 6 on the R&B chart and crossed over to the pop charts, reaching number 24 on the Hot 100 in a completely separate chart run. ## curiosities
**Featured on "Feelin' So Good"** : Big Punisher was one of the MCs who contributed to Jennifer Lopez's track "Feelin' So Good," which was produced by Sean Combs. "There were rappers on Feelin' So Good, the MC's Fat Joe and Big Punisher, but this was an Amuse Boosh for what Lopez would do just a couple of years later." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Invented the Remix Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
