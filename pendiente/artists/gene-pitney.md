## curiosities
**Youthful Icon of Torch Ballads** : Gene Pitney was one of the "teen idols and youthful icons" whose "torch ballads" were popular on the airwaves in the early 1960s. His music contributed to the easy listening genre, which appealed to a broad audience, including adults.
