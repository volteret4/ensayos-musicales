# artist - MC Shan

## songs
**The Bridge (1985)** : Released in late 1985, this single by MC Shan and producer Marley Marl sparked the Bridge Wars. The song "extolled their neighborhood of Queen's Bridge as a mecca for rap," a claim that ignited offense from KRS-One and Boogie Down Productions.
