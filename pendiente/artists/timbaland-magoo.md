## albums
**Welcome to Our World (1997)** : This joint album by Timbaland and his long-time Virginia Beach friend Magoo achieved platinum status. It produced a significant hit, "Up Jumps Da Boogie," which reached number 12 on the pop charts and number 1 on the rap charts, featuring vocals from both Missy Elliott and Aaliyah. ## songs
**Up Jumps Da Boogie (1997)** : This hit single from Timbaland & Magoo's "Welcome to Our World" album reached number 12 on the pop charts and number 1 on the rap charts. It featured guest vocals from both Missy Elliott and Aaliyah, showcasing Timbaland's ability to find "hot sounds in unlikely places," including a sample from the 80s NBC TV hit "Night Rider."
