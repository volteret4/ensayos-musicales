# artist - Freddie Bell and the Bell Boys

## songs
**Houndog (1955)** : Freddie Bell and the Bell Boys, a white Las Vegas lounge act, performed an in-between cover of "Houndog" in 1955. They transformed the song by replacing the original's "racy" lyrics with "ridiculous" ones, turning the metaphors into a literal complaint to a canine, even adding "arth-arth sounds." This goofy version is what Elvis Presley saw and began performing live on stage in 1956.
