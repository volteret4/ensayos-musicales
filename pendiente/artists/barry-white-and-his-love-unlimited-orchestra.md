# artist - Barry White and his Love Unlimited Orchestra

## songs
**Love's Theme (1973)** : A Philly Soul instrumental with proto-disco strings that topped Billboard's Hot 100, one year before "Love to Love You Baby." This single, along with "Rock the Boat," signaled disco's early breakthrough on the charts.
