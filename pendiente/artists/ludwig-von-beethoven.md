# artist - Ludwig von Beethoven

## curiosities
**Influence on Billy Joel's "This Night"** : Billy Joel had previously incorporated classical melodies into his songs, notably borrowing the second movement of Ludwig von Beethoven's "Pathetique Sonata" for the album cut "This Night" on his "An Innocent Man" album, and credited Beethoven in the liner notes.
