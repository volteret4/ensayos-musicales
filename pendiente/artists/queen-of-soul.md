# artist - Queen of Soul

## curiosities
**Implied Artist - Hit Parade Command** : The transcript alludes to "the Queen of Soul" as an artist who, in an alternate memory of the narrator's birth week, commanded the hit parade. This title is widely recognized as a reference to Aretha Franklin, indicating her significant cultural and musical impact.
