## songs
**That's What Friends Are For (1986)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1986, an all-star rendition of a previously obscure soundtrack song that became one of the most famous charity mega singles. It was a benefit for AIDS research at an early stage of the epidemic, featuring fiery vocals from Dionne Warwick, Stevie Wonder, Elton John, and Gladys Knight. Like USA for Africa's "We Are the World," it spent four weeks atop the Hot 100, but unlike it, "That's What Friends Are For" took the year-end Billboard title.
