# artist - Tommy Metola

## curiosities
**Insistence on "Kiss on My List"** : Tommy Metola insisted that Darryl Hall keep "Kiss on My List" for Hall and Oates, rather than letting Janna Allen re-record it as a demo for her own career. He also encouraged Hall and Oates to make music videos as far back as the 70s.
