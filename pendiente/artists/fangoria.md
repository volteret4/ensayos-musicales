## songs
**Macarena (1993)** : Fangoria, a synthpop duo, collaborated with DJ Big Toxic to produce the "River Famix" remix of Los Del Rio's "Macarena" in 1993. This remix was considered a "weird idea" at the time, replacing the song's traditional flamenco sound with a "pulsing electronic beat" in an effort to broaden its appeal to a younger audience beyond Los Del Rio's typical fanbase.
