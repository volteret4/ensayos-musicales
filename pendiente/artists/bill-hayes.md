# artist - Bill Hayes

## songs
**The Ballad of Davy Crockett (1955)** : Bill Hayes's quickly recorded cover version of this song capitalized on the "Davy Crockett craze" in late 1954 and 1955. The song was written for the five-part "Disneyland" TV miniseries, and while Hayes's version was not heard on the show (the televised version was by The Wellingtons), his cover became an early chart-topping example of a show's titular theme song being made into an actual hit. It was released around the dawn of television, when TVs were in two-thirds of American homes by 1955. "Bill Hase's theme from the five-part Disney Land TV miniseries, Davey Crocket.
