## songs
**Steal Away (1980)** : This song, released in July 1980, was a No. 6 hit that featured the "Doobie Bounts" style, a sprightly syncopated chord progression typically played on piano or synthesizer. It exemplifies how the musical template from "What a Fool Believes" recurred on other hit records.
