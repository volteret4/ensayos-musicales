# artist - Mike Lieber

## curiosities
**Co-writer of "Stand By Me"** : Mike Lieber was a songwriting partner who collaborated with Jerry Stoller and Ben E. King to transform Sam Cook's interpolation of the gospel hymn "Stand By Me" into Ben E. King's iconic 1961 solo hit, "Stand By Me."
