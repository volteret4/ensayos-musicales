## curiosities
**First Meeting with Phil Spector** : Her initial encounter with Phil Spector was fraught with tension; Spector, displaying erratic behavior, repeatedly left her office, looked at himself in a mirror, and adjusted his appearance while she attempted to play a song intended for The Paris Sisters, leading to her blunt confrontation and the loss of the sale. "Eventually she said to him, listen to me you little prick, did you come to look at yourself or to hear my songs? **Prominent Demo Singer** : Before becoming a successful songwriter, Ellie Greenwich was recognized as one of the most important demo singers in New York, lending her voice to many potential hit songs. **Second Meeting with Phil Spector** : Despite Spector arriving four hours late, this second meeting, during which Spector was seemingly unaware he was meeting someone he had already made a poor impression on, led to a successful collaboration. Spector had become interested in a song she had sold to Aaron Schroeder and set up the meeting to discuss her writing for him. **Contribution to "Girls Just Want to Have Fun"** : Ellie Greenwich, a renowned singer-songwriter and co-writer of 1960s classics like "Be My Baby" and "Leader of the Pack," was invited into the studio by Rick Chertoff to assist with backing vocals for Cyndi Lauper's "Girls Just Want to Have Fun." She both sang and helped arrange the vocals, ultimately convincing Lauper to adopt a chant format, reminiscent of "the way the Shangra-Law's might sing it."
