## curiosities
**Innovative Rearrangement of "Proud Mary"** : As the drummer for Ike and Tina Turner, Soko Richardson developed an innovative rearrangement of "Proud Mary." In this version, Ike and Tina allowed the song to build for more than a minute before transforming it into a rave-up.
