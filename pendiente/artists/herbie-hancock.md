## songs
**Rocket (1983)** : This track, part of Herbie Hancock's "Breakbeat Experiment," was an innovative instrumental and an MTV favorite in the 1980s. It peaked at number 71 on the Hot 100 but achieved greater success on the R&B chart, reaching number 6. "Jasmine Herbie Hancock's Breakbeat Experiment and MTV favorite rocket... ## curiosities
**Jazz Artists Branching into Funk and R&B** : By the mid-1970s, Herbie Hancock was recognized as one of the leading jazz artists, admired by Nile Rogers, who successfully branched out into funk and R&B. His exploration of these genres influenced Sheik's incorporation of "jazzy intricacy" into their dance-oriented music.
