**Morning (1983)** : This song reached number 21 on the charts in May 1983 and is considered essential by the creators of the term Yacht Rock. In the song, the crooner "bids good morning to everything from his radio to his Cheerios to, in the second verse, The Golden Gate Bridge." It was produced by Journeyman J. Graden and co-written with David Foster, reaching the top 40 fairly late in the Yacht Rock era. ## curiosities
**Career Launch via Yacht Soul** : Al Jarreau, described as a jazzy soul singer, benefited from the Yacht Soul sound, which helped launch his career at both R&B and pop radio. This highlights the genre's role in providing crossover opportunities for Black artists.
