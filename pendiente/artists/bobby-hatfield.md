# artist - Bobby Hatfield

## member of
- The Righteous Brothers

## curiosities
**Solo Performance on "Unchained Melody" (1965)** : Bobby Hatfield was the sole performer on The Righteous Brothers' 1965 definitive version of "Unchained Melody," with bandmate Bill Medley not participating in the recording. Hatfield produced the orchestral arrangement and innovatively added explosive high notes, amplifying the song's drama. **Re-recording "Unchained Melody" for Curb Records (1990)** : At age 50, Bobby Hatfield went back into the studio to record a new 1990 version of "Unchained Melody" for Curb Records. This decision was driven by less favorable royalties on the original version, allowing him to benefit more from the song's renewed popularity following its feature in the movie "Ghost."
