# artist - Jean Pitney

## songs
**I'm Gonna Be Strong** : This song was originally by Jean Pitney and was famously covered by Cyndi Lauper's band Blue Angel. Their slow dance cover achieved international recognition by reaching number 37 in the Netherlands, becoming Blue Angel's only charting song.
