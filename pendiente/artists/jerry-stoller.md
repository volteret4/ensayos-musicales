# artist - Jerry Stoller

## curiosities
**Co-writer of "Stand By Me"** : Jerry Stoller was a songwriting partner who collaborated with Mike Lieber and Ben E. King to adapt Sam Cook's unique refrain from a gospel hymn into Ben E. King's enduring 1961 solo hit, "Stand By Me."
