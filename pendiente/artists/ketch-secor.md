## curiosities
**Completing Bob Dylan's "Wagon Wheel" Fragment (2003)** : In 2003, Ketch Secor took Bob Dylan's unfinished 1973 fragment "Rock Me Mama," added new verses to the original chorus, and re-arranged it with a two-step tempo, releasing it in 2004 as "Wagon Wheel" with his band Old Crow Medicine Show.
