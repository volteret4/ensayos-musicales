# artist - Bernie Taupin

## songs
**Philadelphia Freedom (1975)** : Bernie Taupin co-wrote this song with Elton John, inspired by Billie Jean King and her Philadelphia Freedoms tennis team. The song is a tribute to the city of Philadelphia, adopting the "Philadelphia sound" with its R&B feel and lush strings, and Elton John intended it to sound like a "funky black record." ## curiosities
**Original Lyrics for "Candle in the Wind" (1973)** : Bernie Taupin originally conceived the lyrics for Elton John's 1973 song "Candle in the Wind" as a heartfelt tribute to the "Fallen Hollywood legend Marilyn Monroe." **Rewriting "Candle in the Wind" Lyrics (1997)** : At Elton John's specific request, Bernie Taupin re-wrote the lyrics for "Candle in the Wind" in 1997, famously changing the opening line from "Goodby Norma Jean" to "Goodby England's Rose" to honor Diana, Princess of Wales.
