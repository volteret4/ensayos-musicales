## curiosities
**American Pop Producer** : Lucas Gottwald, known as Dr. Luke, is an American pop producer and a "prodigy" of Max Martin. He co-wrote and produced Kelly Clarkson's "Since U Been Gone" with Martin. **Inspiration for "Since U Been Gone"** : Gottwald recounted in John C. Brooks' book "The Song Machine" that he and Max Martin were listening to the Yeah Yeah Yeahs' "Maps" and were frustrated it didn't have a strong pop chorus, which directly inspired their creation of "Since U Been Gone." "As Gottwald would later recount in John C. Brooks' book The Song Machine, quote, We were listening to alternative and indie music and talking about this song. I said, ah, I love this song, and Maps was like, if they would just write a damn pop chorus on it." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The Silver Medalists Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Dominant Pop Craftsman** : Known as Dr. Luke, he was a prolific and dominant pop craftsman who produced and co-wrote numerous hits, including Kesha's "Tik Tok" and Flo Rida's "Right Round." He also produced Katy Perry's early breakthrough hits like "I Kissed a Girl" and "Hot N Cold," and later collaborated with Max Martin on Perry's number one smashes "Roar" and "Dark Horse."
