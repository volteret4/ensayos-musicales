## albums
**Cricklewood Green (1970)** : Released in 1970, this album further propelled Ten Years After's success, making the Top 15 on Billboard's album chart. Its release came after the "Woodstock" movie showcased the band, contributing to their increased popularity. **Ssssh (1969)** : This studio album was released in the same month Ten Years After played Woodstock. Despite its "rather ironic title" given Alvin Lee's loud and fierce rock style, it became an "instant smash," breaking into the Billboard Top 20 by September 1969 and establishing the band as a staple on American radio. "They had the good fortune to be issuing a brand new studio album that same month. And given Alvin Lee's pension for loud fierce rock, the album sported a rather ironic title. **Stonehenge (1969)** : Released in early 1969, this studio album was a significant success in the UK, breaking into the top ten on the UK album chart. However, its performance in the US was more modest, peaking at number 61 on the Billboard album chart just before the band's Woodstock appearance. "Their early 1969 studio album, Stonehenge, was a sizable British hit, breaking into the top ten on the UK album chart. Their album "Stonehenge," a UK Top 10 hit, only reached number 61 on the Billboard album chart in the US.
