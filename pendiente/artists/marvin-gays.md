# artist - Marvin Gays

## songs
**What's Going On** : Marvin Gays' original "What's Going On" was covered in 2001 by an all-star cast, produced by Jermaine Dupri and executive produced by Bonne. The cover was initially meant to benefit AIDS research but was later shared with the Red Cross in the wake of the 9-11 attacks.
