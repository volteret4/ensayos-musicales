# artist - Stan Kenton

## curiosities
**Battle of the Bands Poster** : In 1950, Barry Gordy Jr. saw a poster advertising a battle of the bands between Stan Kenton and Duke Ellington, which, juxtaposed with a boxing fight poster, influenced his decision to pursue music over boxing. "One of them, on top, was advertising a battle of the bands between Stan Kenton and Duke Ellington." ← Episode Sixty-Four： ＂Reet Petite＂ by Jackie Wilson | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYU-H4NvnmE
**UK Tour as Union Exchange (1955)** : Stan Kenton's band toured the UK as part of the "one-in, one-out" exchange agreement established between American and British musicians' unions in 1955. This tour facilitated a reciprocal arrangement where the Ted Heath Orchestra toured the US. "Stan Kenton's band toured the UK, while the Ted Heath Orchestra, which was not, as a point out, led by the prime minister of the same name, toured the US." ← REUPLOAD Episode 71： ＂Willie and the Hand Jive＂ by Johnny Otis | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ez6BCUlI6Rg
