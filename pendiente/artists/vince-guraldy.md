## curiosities
**Serious Jazz Player Ambitions** : Vince Guraldy never intended to be a holiday or children's recording artist; he was a serious jazz player inspired by legends like Oscar Peterson and Bill Evans, and at one point around the turn of the 1950s, he warmed up crowds for Art Tatum. "Vince Guraldy never meant to be either a holiday, nor a children's recording artist. He was a serious jazz player, inspired by the work of such prior piano legends as Oscar Peterson and Bill Evans." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Chestnut Roasters, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Pop Audience Connection** : Guraldy's exceptional quality was his ability to connect with pop audiences, even after rock and roll had replaced jazz as America's primary youth music, as demonstrated by his Top 40 pop hit "Cast Your Fate to the Wind."
