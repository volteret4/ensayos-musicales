# artist - Alison Krauss

## albums
**O Brother, Where Art Thou? Soundtrack (2000)** : Alison Krauss provided her "sterling pipes" to the soundtrack of "O Brother, Where Art Thou?", which won the 2002 Album of the Year Grammy. This was during a decade when "Starbucks albums" were a Grammy fixture. **Raising Sand (2007)** : This Americana folk album was a big winner at the 2009 Grammys, where Alison Krauss teamed with veteran Led Zeppelin vocalist Robert Plant. The album swept Album of the Year as well as Record of the Year for the song "Please Read The Letter."
