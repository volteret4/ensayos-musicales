## songs
**Somebody's Watching You (1971)** : Little Sister's version of a deep cut from Sly and the Family Stone's *Stand* album, produced by Sly Stone himself. Built out of an early drum machine (then called a rhythm box), this single peaked at number 32 on the Hot 100 and number 8 on the R&B chart in early 1971, making it "by some estimations the first popular recording to derive its rhythm from a drum machine." **You're the One** : A hit single by the vocal group Little Sister, which reached number 22 on the pop charts and number 4 on the R&B charts. It sounded like a "would-be funk sequel" to Sly and the Family Stone's "Thank You (Falettinme Be Mice Elf Agin)." ## curiosities
**Sly Stewart's Sister** : The vocal group Little Sister was named because member Vet Stone was Sly Stewart's (Sly Stone's) little sister. The group released two singles that cracked the Top 40 on Sly's vanity label, Stone Flower.
