## curiosities
**Leading Crossover Artist** : Akon was a prominent Senegalese-American R&B singer, known for his "loping pop rap hits" and "high-painting voice," making him one of the leading artists in crossover pop and hip-hop during the mid to late 2000s. He scored a dozen top 10 hits, including two number ones as a lead artist. ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Gaga is Born Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Key Role in Lady Gaga's Breakthrough** : Akon provided the final pre-fame boost for Lady Gaga. After hearing a reference vocal in the studio, he was profoundly impressed by her talent and insisted on signing her to his new vanity label, Con Live, which was affiliated with Interscope. His involvement began with Gaga's very first single, "Just Dance," which he co-helmed with producer RedOne and brought in Colby O'Donis for. ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Gaga is Born Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
