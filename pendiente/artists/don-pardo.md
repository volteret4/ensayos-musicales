# artist - Don Pardo

## curiosities
**Jeopardy! Announcer** : Don Pardo was the original announcer for the NBC version of the game show "Jeopardy!" He recorded a hilarious spoken-word interlude for "Weird Al" Yankovic's "I Lost on Jeopardy", calling Al a "complete loser", and also appeared in the song's music video.
