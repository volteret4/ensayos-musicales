# artist - Chill Rob G

## curiosities
**Sampler of Ashley's Roach clip** : Chill Rob G was a Golden Age rapper who used the Ashley's Roach clip drum break from The Soul Searchers' 1974 jam in his hip hop classics. This beat was formative in rap history and sampled on dozens of tracks. "It's the beat that powered hip hop classics by Golden Age rappers T. LaRoc.
