## curiosities
**"Bad Boys of R&B" Persona (Mid 1990s)** : Jodeci was a vocal troupe known as the "self-styled bad boys of R&B," contrasting with more clean-cut groups like Boyz II Men. Signed to Sean "Puff Daddy" Combs' Uptown Records, they carried themselves like rappers while also achieving hits with sweet soul ballads. "The vocal troupe, Jodicy, were the self-styled bad boys of R&B. The opposite of more clean cut groups like boys to men." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Flip It and Reverse It Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Devanté Swing's Production House (Mid 1990s)** : Jodeci's main songwriter, Donald DeGrate, also known as Devanté Swing, formed his own production house called Debasement. Devanté Swing would later sign Missy Elliott's girl group, Phase (renamed Sista), and Tim Mosley (Timbaland) to this venture. "Jodicy's main songwriter, Donald DeGrate, aka Devante Swing, was forming his own production house called Debacement." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Flip It and Reverse It Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Neglect of Protégés (Mid 1990s)** : While Timbaland and Missy Elliott were working on Jodeci's material in Debasement, Devanté Swing would often neglect them for months at a time while Jodeci was touring, shifting focus away from Missy's group Sista. "Devante would forget about Tim and Missy for months at a time while Jodicy toured and his protege's toiled away in debasement." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Flip It and Reverse It Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
