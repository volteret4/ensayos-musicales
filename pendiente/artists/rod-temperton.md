## curiosities
**Heatwave Keyboardist and Songwriter** : Rod Temperton was the keyboardist and primary songwriter for Heatwave. Despite being white, "weedy, and nerdy," he possessed an "uncanny knack for writing sleek American-style R&B," making him an improbable soul man. He became Quincy Jones's "secret weapon," composing three songs for Michael Jackson's "Off the Wall" solo album, including the title track. "Rod Temperton was the keyboardist and songwriter of Heat Wave, a multi-racial British R&B group that had scored dance floor hits with boogie nights, the groove line, and always and forever. An improbable soul man, Temperton was white, weedy, and nerdy, no one's idea of a front man. He nonetheless had an uncanny knack for writing sleek American style R&B. Temperton's refined grooves were ideal for the Quincy Jones sound, and he became Q's secret weapon, penning three songs for the Michael Jackson solo album. One of which gave the LP its title off the wall." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Wanna Rock with Q. Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Pivotal in Yacht Soul** : Rod Temperton, possessing an "extreme pop mentality," teamed up with Quincy Jones around 1979-1980. Their collaboration, mixing Jones' soulful and jazzy sound with Temperton's pop sensibilities, was crucial in initiating the "yacht soul strain" of music that became prominent in the era.
