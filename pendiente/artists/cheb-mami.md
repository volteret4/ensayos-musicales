# artist - Cheb Mami

## genres
- Arabic folk music

## songs
**Desert Rose (1999)** : Algerian singer Cheb Mami, a vocalist of the Arabic folk music known as Rai, collaborated with Sting on the song "Desert Rose" for Sting's 1999 album "Brand New Day." Sting co-wrote the song specifically to showcase Mami's vocals. Mami sings in Arabic at the beginning, and Sting sings in a mix of English and Arabic alongside electronic production.
