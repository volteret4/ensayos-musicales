## curiosities
**Member of SBI (1991)** : Melvin Barcliff, who would later adopt the rap name Magoo, was a high school friend of Pharrell Williams and a member of the Virginia Beach rap crew/production team SBI (Surrounded by Idiots). **Introduction to Missy Elliott (Early 1990s)** : Magoo played a pivotal role in Timbaland's career by introducing him to Missy Elliott, an ambitious vocalist from Portsmith, Virginia, who was singing in a girl group called Phase. This introduction led to the formation of a highly influential professional partnership.
