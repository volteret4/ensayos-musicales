## songs
**Conga (1985)** : This song marked a significant moment for Miami Sound Machine, as it helped them find a remarkably authentic crossover sound. "Conga" successfully generated hits on Top 40 radio, following Gloria Estefan's decision to begin singing in English in 1984 after several Spanish-language albums since the late 1970s. ## curiosities
**Evolution to Gloria Estefan's Solo Career** : After the Miami Sound Machine achieved pop radio crossover success with hits like "Conga," Gloria and Emilio Estefan made a strategic decision to shift the band's direction towards adult contemporary ballads, aiming to spotlight Gloria's vocals. This pivot led to the gradual replacement of all original "Machine" members and the subsequent featuring of Gloria's name alone on albums and singles, effectively transitioning her into a solo artist while still including up-tempo Latin cuts on future releases.
