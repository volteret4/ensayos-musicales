## genres

## curiosities
**Early Invokers of Country Tropes** : Nappy Roots, alongside OutKast, was noted for invoking country tropes at the turn of the millennium. Their approach was less overt compared to black artists who, by the end of the 2010s, began to more explicitly incorporate country elements into their rap, contributing to the development of the country trap genre.
