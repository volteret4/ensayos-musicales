## songs
**Harlem Shake (2013)** : This trap jam by Bauer debuted at number 1 on the Billboard chart in February 2013, ending a drought for instrumentals at the top of the Hot 100 that had lasted since 1985. It contained a few instructional words ("Do the Harlem Shake"). Its success was overwhelmingly powered by viral YouTube videos and benefited from Billboard's new rule counting YouTube music plays, accumulating over 100 million streams in its first week.
