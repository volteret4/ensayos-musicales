## curiosities
**Early Career as DJ** : Developed his keen ear for hits as a DJ in Stockholm, demonstrating a natural instinct for the songs and sounds that would pack a dance floor. **Founder of Cheiron Studios** : Opened Cheiron Studios in Stockholm in 1992, conceiving it not only as his personal studio and sonic playground but also as a producer collective where "savviest Swedish song-smiths" could hone their craft. The studio was named after Chiron, the centaur from Greek mythology. "He called his new facility, Sharon Studios, named after Sharon, the centaur in Greek mythology, who teaches chants and dances to Dionysus." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Give Me a Sign Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
