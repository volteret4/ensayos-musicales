## songs
**Bad Day (2006)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 2006 for Canadian singer-songwriter Daniel Powter, receiving a significant boost by becoming the weekly soundtrack for contestants being voted off "American Idol" during its fifth season. This massive exposure propelled it up the charts, where it topped the Hot 100 for five weeks.
