# artist - Eddie Cantor

## songs
**I've Got the Yes, We Have No Bananas Blues (1923)** : This 1923 single is noted as one of the earliest hit beef recordings and qualifies as a parody, an answer record, and a diss record. Cantor recorded it to express frustration with the widespread popularity of the novelty song "Yes, We Have No Bananas," which had dominated the hit parade multiple times that year. He even theatrically pauses the song to tell his backing vocalists to stop singing the original tune, conveying believable annoyance. Lewis's career subsequently entered a period of decline until the commercial success of Fats Domino's "Blueberry Hill" led to its revival.
