## curiosities
**Awkward Transition in Late 70s** : In the late 1970s, The Jacksons navigated an "awkward transition" into both adulthood and chart relevance. They left Motown, signed with CBS Records, and eventually landed at its Epic Records imprint, rebranding from "The Jackson 5" to simply "The Jacksons." "By the late 70s, the Jackson's were undergoing an awkward transition, both into adulthood and on the charts.
