## songs
**To Be With You (1992)** : This "soft rock diddy" became a number one hit for the glam metal band Mr. Big in the spring of 1992, just weeks after Nirvana's "Nevermind" topped the charts. Its success demonstrated that metal bands capable of sounding "sufficiently mellow" could continue to thrive commercially in the post-grunge era, reinforcing the power of ballads on pop radio.
