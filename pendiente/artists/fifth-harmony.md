## curiosities
**Formed on The X Factor** : Fifth Harmony was formed by Simon Cowell on the American edition of "The X Factor" in 2012, bringing together solo vocalists Normani Kordei, Lauren Jauregui, Ally Brooke, Dinah Jane, and Camila Cabello after a joint audition. The quintet's name was given by a viewer vote. "In 2012, Simon brought together solo vocalists Normani Corday, Lauren Hareghi, Ali Broke, Dina Jane and Camila Cabello for a joint audition that impressed the X Factor judges. **Eliminated but Signed** : Like One Direction before them, Fifth Harmony competed on "The X Factor" but came in third, failing to make the final round. Despite this, Simon Cowell signed them to a recording contract. "Fifth Harmony competed on the show as a quintet and came in third, failing to make the final round.
