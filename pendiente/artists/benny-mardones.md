# artist - Benny Mardones

## labels
- Polygram Records

## songs
**Into the Night (1980)** : An impassioned 1980 ode to a jail bait, which was a number 11 hit in its first run. During the "would have been, should have been" fad in 1989, it was re-released but only made it as high as number 20. Despite a lower chart peak the second time, this second run improved the song's long-term fortunes, making it a permanent oldies rotation staple.
