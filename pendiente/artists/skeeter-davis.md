## songs
**The End of the World (1963)** : At 21 years old, Skeeter Davis took this "melodramatic classic" to number one on the easy listening chart and number two on the Hot 100 in 1963. The song was prominently featured in an early episode of Mad Men, highlighting its period relevance and adult appeal.
