# artist - Ne-Yo

## songs
**Hate That I Love You (2007)** : Ne-Yo was the singer-songwriter who duetted with Rihanna on the percolating acoustic ballad "Hate That I Love You," featured on her "Good Girl Gone Bad" album. "A percolating acoustic ballad duet with singer songwriter Nioh. Hate that I love you. **Irreplaceable (2006)** : Ne-Yo was one of the songwriters for Beyoncé's longest-lasting number one hit, "Irreplaceable." "This semi-acoustic classic from songwriters Neo and Stargate." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hits of the Year Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f078e6dd12efb02ce81
**Russian Roulette (2009)** : As a frequent collaborator with Rihanna, Ne-Yo co-wrote this song, the first single from "Rated R." The track was designed to channel Rihanna's backstory and notably climaxed with the sound of gunshots, peaking at number nine. "Russian Roulette, which was co-written by frequent collaborator Niyo to channel Rihanna's backstory." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Only Girl in the World Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Take a Bow (2008)** : Ne-Yo co-wrote "Take a Bow," a contemptuous ballad from "Good Girl Gone Bad Reloaded" that demonstrated Rihanna's vocal growth and became her first hit to top both the Hot 100 and Billboard's R&B chart. "Take a bow, addressing down of a faithless soon-to-be-X written by Nio and Norwegian production team Stargate, who'd produced several of her prior hits, showed how far Rihanna had come as a vocalist in just a couple of years." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Only Girl in the World Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
