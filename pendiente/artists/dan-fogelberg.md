## songs
**Same Old Lang Syne (1980)** : This vaguely New Year's themed story song became a number nine hit, breaking during the holiday season of 1980 but not reaching its top 10 peak until February 1981. Its success showed that Christmas music could compete if it didn't sound overtly Christmasy. "Dan Fogelberg's vaguely new years themed story song same old land song. ## curiosities
**Soft Rock Influence on Garth Brooks** : Dan Fogelberg, a soft rock singer-songwriter, inspired Garth Brooks during his youth. Brooks's song "The Dance" was specifically noted for its resemblance to Fogelberg's style, suggesting a direct influence on Brooks's fusion of soft rock and country. "Growing up in Yukon, Oklahoma, Garth Brooks, by his own admission, listened more to rock than country music. In particular, he was inspired by the soft rock, profored by singer songwriters like James Taylor and Dan Fogelberg." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Friends in Low Places, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
