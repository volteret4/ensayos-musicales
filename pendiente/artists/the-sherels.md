# artist - The Sherels

## songs
**Will You Love Me Tomorrow? (1961)** : This song became one of the earliest number one hits of the girl group wave in 1961. Written by Carol King and Jerry Goffin, it was a pioneering track for its overt discussion of sex and female agency, asking whether intimacy would lead to heartbreak.
