# artist - The Nicholas Brothers

## curiosities
**Cameo in "Alright" Music Video** : The Nicholas Brothers, renowned pre-rock legends, made a cameo in the music video for Janet Jackson's "Alright." Their appearance, alongside other iconic figures, contributed to the video's recreation of a Busby Berkeley musical look, reinforcing its nostalgic and elaborate visual style.
