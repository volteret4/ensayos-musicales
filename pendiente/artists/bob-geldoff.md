# artist - Bob Geldoff

## curiosities
**Producer of Do They Know It's Christmas? Remakes** : Bob Geldoff produced three remakes of "Do They Know It's Christmas?" in future years, all of which briefly topped the British charts but none are regularly played on the radio. This highlights the difficulty of matching the original's sticky persistence.
