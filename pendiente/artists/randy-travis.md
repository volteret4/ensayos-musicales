# artist - Randy Travis

## genres
- Country

## albums
**Multi-Platinum Albums** : Randy Travis generated a string of multi-platinum albums during the late 1980s, a rare feat at a time when country hitmakers were fortunate to achieve gold certification. This success underscored his status as the mega star of new traditionalism. ## songs
**Forever and Ever Amen** : Randy Travis's biggest hit, the gentle two-step "Forever and Ever Amen," achieved significant mainstream exposure. It secured airplay on heartland pop stations and even on the video channel VH1, making him the closest any late 80s country star came to fully crossing into the mainstream.
