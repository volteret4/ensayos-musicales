## songs
**Bailamos (1999)** : Enrique Iglesias's first number one hit, an intentionally bilingual track where "Bailamos" was the only Spanish word in the title. The song was written by Englishmen Paul Barry and Mark Taylor, with Barry later admitting that the emphasis in the title was on the wrong syllable due to his non-fluent Spanish. Its success on the Hot 100 demonstrated that a broad "Latin flavor," rather than rigorous authenticity, was enough for an English-language breakthrough. "By Lamos was like Martin's live in La Vida Loka, and intentionally bilingual hit." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > La Vida Loca Edición | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Bailamos (1999)** : This song was featured in Anne Powers' 1999 New York Times article, which framed the summer's music as a series of one-on-one battles for the "Song of the Summer." It was explicitly pitted against Ricky Martin's "Livin' La Vida Loca." **Be With You (2000)** : Enrique Iglesias scored his second number one hit with "Be With You" in the summer of 2000. While danceable, this song notably "bore little relationship to Latin music at all," signifying a shift in the perceived authenticity of the Latin pop boom as it progressed. ## curiosities
**Independent Career from Father (1990s)** : Enrique Iglesias, son of global superstar Julio Iglesias, intentionally launched his musical career in the 1990s completely independent of his famous father. His early Spanish-language albums for FonoVisa achieved massive success, selling 14 million copies. "Enrique Iglesias, son of Julio Iglesias, had launched a musical career in the 90s, totally independent of his megastar father." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > La Vida Loca Edición | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Signing with Interscope Records (June 1999)** : The breakthrough success of Ricky Martin and Jennifer Lopez in 1999 accelerated Enrique Iglesias's long-held plans to move into English-language pop. He signed a new deal with Interscope Records in June 1999, right as the Latin crossover explosion was in full force, and quickly scored his first number one hit within months.
