## songs
**Caribbean Queen (1984)** : A number one pop and R&B smash hit for Billy Ocean, which was helmed by R&B producer Keith Diamond. This success prompted the Drive team to seek a single that could follow up on its chart performance. **Get Out of My Dreams, Get Into My Car (1988)** : A danceable, exuberant, and kitschy song produced and co-written by Mut Lang. It was a comeback hit for Billy Ocean after a string of mid-80s hits, rising to number five on the pop chart and just weeks away from scoring another number one. The sound was characterized as "Clattery Dance Pop with Husky Soul Vocals." **Loverboy (1984)** : This R&B song broke onto the Hot 100 in December 1984 and ultimately peaked at number two. Despite being by an R&B artist, it was described as sounding like "Moody New Wave guitar rock," highlighting the widespread genre crossing of 1984. The track was notably co-written and co-produced by famed rock producer Robert John "Mutt" Lang. "It's R&B singer Billy Ocean with Love A Boy. **Loverboy (1985)** : Produced by Mut Lang, teaming with Keith Diamond and Ocean, this single took Billy Ocean in an entirely different direction with a sleek, space-age sound. It fused the dance pop Billy Ocean had been doing with the synth rock Mut Lang had developed for other artists like Def Leppard and The Cars. The accompanying video fittingly looked like an extension of the George Lucas's Star Wars universe, and the song reached number two on the Hot 100 in early 1985. **When the Going Gets Tough, the Tough Get Going (1985)** : Mut Lang teamed with Billy Ocean again for this bouncy pop song, which was contributed to the soundtrack of the 1985 Kathleen Turner and Michael Douglas film "Jewel of the Nile." It became another number two hit for Billy Ocean.
