## curiosities
**Early 80s Top 40 Radio Preference** : In the early 1980s, Top 40 radio predominantly played the new romantic synth pop of bands like Kajagoogoo, making Billy Idol's "too punk" sound less compatible with mainstream radio at the time. "At a moment when Top 40 radio was playing new wave but mostly the new romantic synth pop of Durand Durand, Kaja Gugu or Culture Club, Billy Idol still sounded a little too punk for Top 40." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > At Last, My Legacy Has Come Along Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
