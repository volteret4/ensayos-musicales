**1999 (1982)** : This double album, released in late 1982 by the collective now calling itself Prince and the Revolution, definitively affirmed that Prince could not be confined to any one sound. It featured the smash hit "Little Red Corvette," showcasing a blend of pulsating synthesizers, classic rock choruses, and searing guitar solos. **Purple Rain (1984)** : The title track of Prince's 1984 debut movie and accompanying magnum opus album, "Purple Rain" was inspired by power ballads from rock titans like Bob Seger and borrowed chord changes from Journey's "Faithfully." It reached number two on the pop chart, number four on the R&B chart, and number 18 on the Rock Chart, becoming Prince's third straight single to hit all three charts.
