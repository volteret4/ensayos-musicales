## curiosities
**Formation (1965)** : This group was formed by renaming the Chad Mitchell trio at the end of 1965, after founder Chad Mitchell's departure, with John Denver taking on lead roles in recordings such as their cover of Pete Seeger's "The Bells of Romney."
