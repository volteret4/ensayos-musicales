## curiosities
**Production of "Hot in Here"** : Production wizards Pharrell Williams and Chad Hugo, known as The Neptunes, were responsible for producing Nelly's 2002 Song of the Summer, "Hot in Here." They achieved this by taking an old Chuck Brown go-go record, amplifying its heat "high enough to make you quote take off all your clothes," and integrating St. Louis rapper Nelly's "Dirty South Style." "In 2002, production wizards The Neptune's, Ferel Williams and Chad Hugo, took an old Chuck Brown gogo record, turned up the heat high enough to make you quote take off all your clothes and added St. Louis rapper Nellie who spelled the word here with two Rs, Dirty South Style." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Song(s) of the Summer Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef50c30a1408dc292e4
**Production of Gwen Stefani's "Hollaback Girl" (2005)** : The hip hop production duo The Neptunes, consisting of Pharrell Williams and Chad Hugo, co-wrote and produced Gwen Stefani's massive hit "Hollaback Girl." Their work contributed to the song's "hybridization of hip hop beef with pop rock sass," demonstrating their ability to bridge genres for mainstream success.
