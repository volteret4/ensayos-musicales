## albums
**We Invented the Remix (2002)** : Following Billboard's new remix rules, P Diddy (then Sean Combs) titled his 2002 compilation album "We Invented the Remix" as a "total flex," seemingly embracing the new remix world order. This album included two separate songs, both called "I Need A Girl," which under the new rules charted separately. He contributed a " bespoke rap" to the song, which also featured a sample from Enya. **I Need A Girl Part One (2002) (feat. Usher & Loon)** : This song, featured on P Diddy's "We Invented the Remix" compilation, reached number two on the Hot 100 in May 2002. Under Billboard's new rules, it charted as a separate hit from its counterpart, "I Need A Girl Part Two." **I Need A Girl Part Two (2002) (feat. Loon, Ginuwine & Mario Winans)** : A "completely different" song from "I Need A Girl Part One," this track also featured on P Diddy's "We Invented the Remix" compilation. It reached number four on the Hot 100 a couple of months after "Part One" in 2002. Under the old chart system, both "I Need A Girl" tracks might have merged into a single number one hit, but under the new rules, they charted separately as top five hits.
