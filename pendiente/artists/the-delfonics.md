## curiosities
**Influence on Silk Sonic** : The Delfonics were one of the 70s-style soul groups whose musical tropes and aesthetic were intentionally woven into the fabric of Silk Sonic's debut album, "An Evening with Silk Sonic," as they "play acted the roles of 70s style soulman." "songs bathed in the tropes of Barry White, the Delphanix, Teddy Pendergrass and Aritha Franklin." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Lenny on Mars Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f54d1ba84fb8f0463bf
