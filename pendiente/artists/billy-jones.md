# artist - Billy Jones

## songs
**Yes, We Have No Bananas (1923)** : This version of the mega-hit novelty song was performed by Billy Jones, a tenor and early radio star. The song was so ubiquitous in 1923 that it inspired an answer record from Eddie Cantor. Billy Jones himself even recorded covers of both the original "Yes, We Have No Bananas" and the beef record "I've Got the Yes, We Have No Bananas Blues."
