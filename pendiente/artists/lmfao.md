## songs
**Party Rock Anthem (2011)** : This song was the winner of the 2011 Billboard Song of the Summer. Described as a "very silly song," it became a "pretty serious hit" with six weeks at number one in July and August. The "goof ball duo" released a "hot weather banger" that included its own dance ("every day I'm shuffling") and represented early 2010s electronic dance music reaching a "new pop apex." They even audaciously titled it an "anthem." "Goof Ball Duo LMF AO dropped a hot weather banger that came with its own dance. Remember every day I'm shuffling? ## curiosities
**Not an Imperial Act** : Despite scoring back-to-back number one hits with "Party Rock Anthem" and "Sexy and I Know It" around 2011-2012, LMFAO was not considered an imperial pop act. Their success was short-lived, with no further top 40 hits, and their second hit sounded very similar to the first, indicating a lack of artistic stretch during their peak. "Unlike pet shop boys at no point were LMFAO an imperial pop act." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Material Girl in an Imperial World Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f03154465cd600c5379
