# artist - Keith Forsey

## songs
**Hot Stuff (1979)** : Co-written by Pete Bellotte, Keith Forsey (future Billy Idol collaborator), and Harold Faltermeyer, this song was described as "the ultimate hybrid single," combining rock and disco elements, and won the first Grammy for Best Female Rock Vocal Performance.
