## albums
**Bayside Boys Mix (1996)** : This version of "Macarena" achieved significant chart success, hitting number one on Billboard's Hot 100 in August 1996. By the close of 1996, it had sold over 4 million copies. Its reign at number one lasted for 14 weeks before being replaced by Blackstreet's "No Diggity" in November 1996, marking "still one of the longest runs in Billboard history." : "In August 1996, the bass-eyed boys mix hit number one on Billboard's Hot 100." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > One Year - Hey Macarena! | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
