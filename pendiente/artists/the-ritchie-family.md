## songs
**The Best Disco in Town (1976)** : This song by the Philadelphia trio The Ritchie Family reached the top 20 in 1976. It was a popular example of a 12-inch dance single that strung together snippets of half a dozen prior hits in under three minutes, a format that inspired later bootleg disco mega mixes.
