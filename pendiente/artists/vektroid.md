# artist - Vektroid

## albums
**Floral Shoppe (2011)** : Released under the Macintosh Plus project by producer Vektroid, this album is arguably the most remembered and genre-defining work within vaporwave. It played a crucial role in establishing both the musical and visual identity of the genre. "especialmente Floral Shope, de Macintosh Plus.
