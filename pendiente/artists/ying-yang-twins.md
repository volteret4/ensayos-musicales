# artist - Ying Yang Twins

## curiosities
**Guest Refrain on Bubba Sparxxx's "Ms. New Booty" (2006)** : The Atlanta Crunk Kings, Ying Yang Twins, contributed a guest refrain to Bubba Sparxxx's "dirty South Jam," "Ms. New Booty." Their participation helped this catchy single reach number 7 on both the pop and R&B charts.
