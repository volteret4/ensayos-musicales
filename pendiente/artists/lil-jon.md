## songs
**Snap Yo Fingers : Crunk Godfather's Snap Music Hit** : As the "Crunk Godfather," Lil Jon contributed this hit to the Snap Music mini-genre. Snap Music, an offshoot of Crunk Music, is known for its reduced digital essence, featuring 808-thumps, high-hats, snapping sounds, and simple chanting vocals, making it ideal for mobile phone ringtones. **Pioneer of Crunk** : Lil Jon was identified as the pioneer of Crunk, a Southern Rap subgenre. His sound was introduced to many Americans through Usher's 2004 number one smash hit "Yeah!"
