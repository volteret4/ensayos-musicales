# artist - Jeanne Green

## curiosities
**Uncredited Backing Vocalist on "Suspicious Minds"** : Jeanne Green, a Muscle Shoals vocalist, performed very audible backing vocals on Elvis Presley's 1969 hit "Suspicious Minds." Like Ronnie Milsap, she received fine-print credit in the liner notes but was not listed on the single's label.
