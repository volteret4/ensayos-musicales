# artist - Barry DeVorzon

## songs
**Nadia's Theme (Cotton's Dream) (1976)** : This composition by Barry DeVorzon was originally titled "Cotton's Dream" and served as the long-running theme to the CBS soap opera "The Young and the Restless." In 1976, it was adopted by star Summer Olympics gymnast Nadia Comaneci, leading to its renaming as "Nadia's Theme." The instrumental cracked the top 10 in the fall of 1976, peaking at number 8, and continues to be the theme for "The Young and the Restless" today.
