## songs
**California (2002)** : This song, which became *The O.C.*'s theme, was a minor hit on the modern rock chart in 2002. Its premiere with the show coincided with the iTunes music store launch, leading to it becoming a best-selling 99-cent download by 2005 and Phantom Planet's only gold-certified single, thanks to digital downloads.
