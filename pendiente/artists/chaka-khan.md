## songs
**Do You Love What You Feel (1979)** : The lead single from Rufus's LP *Master Jam*, featuring Chaka Khan's vocals. This "Yacht Soul adjacent" track brought Rufus and Chaka to number one on the R&B chart and number 30 on the Hot 100 as 1979 transitioned into 1980. **I Feel for You (1984)** : This R&B veteran's cover of a Prince song became her biggest pop hit ever, reaching number three on the Hot 100 and number one on the R&B chart in November 1984. The track featured guest harmonica by Stevie Wonder, samples from Wonder's early hit "Fingertips," rapping from Melle Mel, and up-to-the-minute scratching and hip-hop style production, serving as a "mainstream ambassador to hip-hop culture." **Stuff Like That (1978)** : Chaka Khan provided vocal work on this buttery smooth track by Quincy Jones, which he recorded with Nick Ashford and Valerie Simpson. It topped the R&B chart in the summer of 1978 and peaked at number 21 pop, helping to establish Jones as a pivotal figure in "Yacht Soul."
