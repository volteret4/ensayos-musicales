## curiosities
**"Since U Been Gone" Medley** : Indie rock luminary Ted Leo, frontman for the Washington DC group Ted Leo and the Pharmacists, created a 2005 medley of both Kelly Clarkson's "Since U Been Gone" and Yeah Yeah Yeahs' "Maps." Coming from a punk and indie scene, Leo was an unabashed admirer of Clarkson's hit, praising its hooks and advanced pop pastiche.
