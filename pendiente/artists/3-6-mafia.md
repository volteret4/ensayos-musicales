# artist - 3-6 Mafia

## songs
**It's Hard Out Here for a Pimp (2005)** : This song from the film *Hustle and Flow* earned the Memphis rap troupe 3-6 Mafia the Best Original Song Academy Award in 2008. Isaac Hayes lived to see this achievement, marking the official passing down of his Oscar's legacy to the hip hop generation.
