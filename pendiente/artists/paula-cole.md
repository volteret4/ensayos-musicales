## songs
**I Don't Want to Wait (1998)** : This single reached its peak of number 11 on the Hot 100 on January 20, 1998, the very week *Dawson's Creek* premiered and chose it as its theme song. Its association with the show significantly extended its chart life, keeping it in the top 20 for months and on the Hot 100 for 56 weeks. "By the time the show premiered on the WB network on January 20, 1998, I Don't Want to Wait had already, that very week reached its peak of number 11 on the hot 100, independently of the TV show." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > A Deal with the TV God Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Me (1998)** : Issued as the third radio single from Paula Cole's 1996 album "This Fire," this song features lyrics about crossing a bridge into self-acceptance and self-love. Cole performed "Me" as a main stage act at Lilith Fair in both 1997 and 1998, with the festival helping to elevate her profile as a hitmaker. The single reached the top 40 on Billboard's Radio Songs Chart in the spring of 1998. ## curiosities
**Emerging Artist and Peter Gabriel Vocalist** : Paula Cole was an emerging artist who broke as a vocalist on a Peter Gabriel tour before starting her own recording career. She was part of Sarah McLachlan's initial multi-artist lineup in 1996, which was a precursor to Lilith Fair. "One artist McLaughlin was already comfortable with and had played alongside was Paul Acole, a just emerging artist who had broken as a vocalist on a Peter Gabriel tour and had started recording on her own." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Building a Herstory Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Performed with Sarah McLachlan (1996)** : In the summer of 1996, Paula Cole joined Sarah McLachlan, Suzanne Vega, and Lisa Loeb for a series of multi-artist shows. These performances, including a debut in Clarkston, Michigan, were instrumental in testing the concept that would become Lilith Fair. "McLaughlin's team paired her and Paul Acole with the now veteran Suzanne Vega who was touring behind her own latest album." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Building a Herstory Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Lilith Fair Beta Test Participant** : Paula Cole participated in the 1996 Lilith beta test gigs with Sarah McLaughlin, Susanne Vega, and Lisa Loeb, which demonstrated the commercial viability of all-female concerts. She subsequently performed at the official 1997 Lilith Fair. "The small series of gigs Sarah McLaughlin played in 1996 with Paul Nicole, Susanne Vega, and Lisa Loeb gave the lie to this industry premise." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Building a Herstory Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
