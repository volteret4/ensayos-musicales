## curiosities
**Budgetary Innovation (1974)** : As a young film student, Carpenter faced budget limitations for his first film, *Dark Star*, which forced him to use an unconventional tool: a synthesizer. This decision not only saved his film but also inadvertently defined the sound of 1980s horror cinema, and by extension, contemporary horror. "la solución este problema, aparte de salvar a su película, va a definir el sonido del cine de terror de los años 80 y, por extensión, el actual." ← Por qué el cine de terror de los 80 suena así？ Horror Synth | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hd23tCTRk8E
**Renowned Soundtrack Composer** : John Carpenter is highly respected for his work on numerous film soundtracks, which have achieved strong sales. "es una persona muy consolidada por todos los trabajos que hizo con las bandas sonoras, se vendieron muy bien." ← Music Radar Clan > Lanzamientos de Octubre 2017 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q1_bxi16osQ&list=PLBFijugu0fhAAIR-Ibh59aaU8VWg-SkLc
**Signature Sound Forged** : After his initial experiment with *Dark Star*, Carpenter truly forged his signature style in *Assault on Precinct 13*. This style, characterized by synthetic and dark minimalism, would become a hallmark of his entire body of work and be widely imitated by almost all videoclippish horror cinema of the 1980s and many current works. "donde Carpenter forjó su firma, un minimalismo sintético y oscuro que caracterizará toda su obra." ← Por qué el cine de terror de los 80 suena así？ Horror Synth | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hd23tCTRk8E
**Assault on Precinct 13 Score - Dark, Eerie, and Minimalist** : For *Assalto a la comisaria del distrito 13*, un carpeto composed a score characterized by its dark, eerie, and minimalist qualities. This music was built fundamentally on synthesizers and drum machines, a choice that established his distinctive electronic style. "Con asalto a la comisaria del distrito 13, compuso una música oscura, espeluznante y minimalista, base de sentitizador y caja de ritmos." ← Las Bandas Sonoras Más Atípicas y Revolucionarias del Cine | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fiSrdUP3YXY
**Early Film Scores - Budget-Driven Electronic and Minimalist Approach** : Due to a lack of budget, un carpeto (John Carpenter) composed the soundtracks for his early films himself. For *Assalto a la comisaria del distrito 13* (*Assault on Precinct 13*), he created a dark, eerie, and minimalist score primarily using synthesizers and drum machines. This resourcefulness defined his signature sound. "También por falta de presupuesto, de un carpeto en realizó la banda sonora de sus primeras películas." ← Las Bandas Sonoras Más Atípicas y Revolucionarias del Cine | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fiSrdUP3YXY
**Genre Blending - Rosin, Progressive Electronic, Minimal Synth** : un carpeto's musical style is distinguished by its ability to blend genres like Rosin, progressive electronic music, and minimal synth. This fusion creates a unique "sign of identity" in his compositions, noted for its "escarpente de Diana" quality. "mezcla generos como la Rosin, la música electrónica progresiva y el sin voz más minimal." ← Las Bandas Sonoras Más Atípicas y Revolucionarias del Cine | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fiSrdUP3YXY
**Halloween Theme - Iconic Modern Horror Piece** : In his subsequent film after *Assalto a la comisaria del distrito 13*, un carpeto created the most iconic song of his career, which became one of the most recognizable pieces in modern horror. His music is considered a "sign of identity," celebrated for its "escarpente de Diana" quality when blending genres such as Rosin, progressive electronic music, and minimal synth. "Pero fue en la siguiente película donde realizó la canción más icónica de su carrera y una de las piezas más reconocibles del terror moderno." ← Las Bandas Sonoras Más Atípicas y Revolucionarias del Cine | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fiSrdUP3YXY
**The Thing (1982) - Morricone Collaboration and Thematic Resemblance** : For *The Thing* (1982), un carpeto had a larger budget and hired reconio Morricone to compose the score. Despite this, un carpeto was not entirely pleased with the final result. Only the film's main theme, which closely resembled un carpeto's own synthesized music, was predominantly used from Morricone's composition. "Para la cosa, Carpenter dispuso de más presupuesto y se permitió el lujo de contratar a Eniomorricone, aunque el resultado no fue del todo de la grado del director." ← Las Bandas Sonoras Más Atípicas y Revolucionarias del Cine | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fiSrdUP3YXY
