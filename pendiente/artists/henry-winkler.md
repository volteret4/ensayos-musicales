# artist - Henry Winkler

## curiosities
**"Get High on Yourself" Participant (1981)** : Henry Winkler, known from "Happy Days," was among the over 50 famous pals rounded up by Robert Evans to record the anti-drug song for the "Get High on Yourself" TV special in 1981. He was chosen for his celebrity profile rather than singing ability.
