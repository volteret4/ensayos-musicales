# artist - Da Brat

## curiosities
**Platinum Level Rapper** : Da Brat is identified as one of the "platinum level women rappers" who exemplified the strength of female hip hop throughout the 1990s. Her presence, alongside other successful female rappers, underscored the need for Lilith Fair to diversify its early lineup.
