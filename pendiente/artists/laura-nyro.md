**Stoney End (1967)** : This song, penned by Nyro in 1967, was covered by Barbara Streisand in 1970 at the encouragement of producer Richard Perry. Streisand's soulful, gospel-like rendition became her first top 40 hit in half a decade, climbing to number 6 on the Hot 100 by early 1971.
