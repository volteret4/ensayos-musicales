# artist - John Carter

## curiosities
**"Incense and Peppermints" Lyricist (1967)** : John Carter, a college student, provided lyrics to an instrumental track for The Sixpence (who later became Strawberry Alarm Clock) as a freelance gig. He was not a member of the band, and his contribution was initially met with resentment by the band members.
