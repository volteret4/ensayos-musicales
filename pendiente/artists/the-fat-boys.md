**Rising Rap Stars in 1986** : The Fat Boys were identified as one of the "rising rap stars" in the summer of 1986, alongside acts like Run DMC and Houdini. They were considered "primed to break big" as part of rap's second wave, poised for a major breakthrough to platinum status.
