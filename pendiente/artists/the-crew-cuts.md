# artist - The Crew Cuts

## songs
**Earth Angel (1955)** : The Crew Cuts' cover of "Earth Angel," performed by the white vocalists, achieved greater success on Billboard's pop charts than The Penguins' original version. Their rendition reached number three in 1955, charting concurrently with The Penguins' recording. ## curiosities
**Formula for White Doo-Wop Covers** : The Crew Cuts established the successful formula for white artists creating remakes of doo-wop songs, a strategy later copied by groups like The Diamonds.
