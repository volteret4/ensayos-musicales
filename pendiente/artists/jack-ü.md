**Where Are Ü Now (2015)** : This acclaimed single, released in late 2015, was led by the superstar DJ duo Jack Ü (Skrillex and Diplo) and featured Justin Bieber. Bieber's participation helped resuscitate his career, and the track is notable for a "dolphin-like trill" which is actually Bieber's voice processed by Skrillex.
