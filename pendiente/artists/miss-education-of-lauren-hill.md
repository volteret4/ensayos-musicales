# artist - Miss Education of Lauren Hill

## genres
- Hip Hop

## albums
**The Miss Education of Lauren Hill (1998)** : This album, which won the Grammy for Album of the Year five years prior to Outcast's *Speaker Box, Slash, The Love Below*, was the only other hip-hop album to have won the National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences' top prize at the time. It is noted for featuring Lauren Hill singing as much as she raps.
