# artist - Bob Hope

## curiosities
**"Get High on Yourself" Participant (1981)** : Bob Hope was among the over 50 famous pals rounded up by Robert Evans to record the anti-drug song for the "Get High on Yourself" TV special in 1981. He participated despite not being primarily a singer.
