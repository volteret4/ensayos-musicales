**Flying Without Wings (2003)** : As the actual winner of "American Idol's" second season, Ruben Studdard's coronation song, "Flying Without Wings," debuted at number two. This happened in contrast to runner-up Clay Aiken's song, "This Is The Night," which debuted at number one in the same year.
