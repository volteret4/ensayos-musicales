## curiosities
**Following Hawkins' Musical Path** : Canned Heat is identified as one of the 1960s white rock acts that followed in Dale Hawkins' musical footsteps. Their sound incorporated elements such as electric bass, Chess-style guitar riffs, and country-inflected vocals, which were pioneered and featured in Hawkins' "Suzy Q." "Acts like the Rolling Stones or the Animals or Cand Heat were all following in Hawkins' footsteps" ← Episode 63： ＂Susie Q＂, by Dale Hawkins | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4vqj1DcXOfw
**Band Legacy** : Canned Heat is described as a "funny, liminal late 60s band" that performed at two of the most vital concerts of the late 1960s: Monterey Pop and Woodstock. Despite their presence at these iconic festivals, their legacy is considered "quite limited," and they have notably never been included on the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame ballot, unlike many other prominent 1960s acts.
