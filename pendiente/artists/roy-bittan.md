## curiosities
**Pianist on Bat Out of Hell** : Roy Bittan, the keyboard player from Bruce Springsteen's E Street Band, was hired by Jim Steinman for *Bat Out of Hell*. Steinman regarded him as "the best pianist I had ever seen in my life," describing his talent as "without parallel" for the kind of music he created. "I thought Roy was the best pianist I had ever seen in my life. Steinman told Todd Rungren biographer Paul Myers.
