# artist - Lou Pearlman

## curiosities
**Boy Band "Svengali"** : Lou Pearlman is characterized as a "shadowy, svengali slash con man" who was the architect behind the creation of two of the best-selling boy bands, the Backstreet Boys and NSYNC. **Inspiration from New Kids on the Block** : Pearlman, who previously ran a business chartering blimps and private jets, was inspired to enter the boy band industry in 1989 when he observed the immense lucrativeness of the genre after manager Mori Star chartered a jet for New Kids on the Block. **Move to Orlando, Florida** : He moved his business to Orlando, Florida, to leverage the city's wealth of talent, which he referred to as "Disney town's wealth of talent," to form his boy bands. **Formation of Backstreet Boys** : Pearlman formed the Backstreet Boys first, naming them after an Orlando flea market, the Backstreet Market, where teenagers would congregate. "Pearlman formed the Backstreet Boys first. **Creation of NSYNC as a Rival** : In 1995, while awaiting the Backstreet Boys to gain an audience, Pearlman intentionally formed NSYNC, strategizing that if his first group succeeded, he should create their competition himself. "Pearlman figured that if Backstreet were successful, they'd have competition, so why not create a Backstreet rival himself." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Want It That Way Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Fraud and Exploitation of Artists** : Pearlman was notorious for financially exploiting the groups he created, as evidenced by the lawsuits filed against him by both the Backstreet Boys and NSYNC. The Backstreet Boys sued him for fraud in 1998, alleging he kept most of their money and treated them as "indentured servants." NSYNC members followed suit in 1999, revealing he inserted himself as a sixth member in their contracts and took their earnings, leaving them on $35 a day.
