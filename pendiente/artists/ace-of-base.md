## songs
**The Sign (1994)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1994, staging a "coup" for Swedish pop at the peak of grunge and gangsta rap. Both the single and the album "The Sign" earned Billboard year-end titles. While the Swedish foursome's career was short-lived, their 1994 conquest paved the way for the later takeover of Western pop by Swedish songwriters, notably Max Martin.
