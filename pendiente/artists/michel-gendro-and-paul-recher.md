## genres

## albums
**Let's Do It in the 80s Great Hits** : This was a bootleg 12-inch dance single credited to the artist Passion on the Alta record label, produced by French Canadian DJs Michel Gendro and Paul Recher from Montreal. It blended current disco tracks with songs that were at least a decade old, mixed over a clapping beat, notably including original, unauthorized recordings of The Shocking Blue's "Venus" and a long string of Beatles songs. ## curiosities
**Bootleg Disco Mega Mix Creation** : Michel Gendro and Paul Recher, French Canadian DJs from Montreal, created an unauthorized 12-inch dance single called "Let's Do It in the 80s Great Hits." This mix illegally sampled various songs, including original Beatles recordings and "Venus" by The Shocking Blue, effectively splicing together music from different periods and genres over a clapping beat. "It was the handy work of Michel Gendro and Paul Recher, French Canadian DJs from Montreal, who liked splicing together bits of music from different periods and genres." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Without The Beatles | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Unauthorized Use of Original Recordings** : In their bootleg 12-inch, "Let's Do It in the 80s Great Hits," Gendro and Recher used original recordings of The Shocking Blue's "Venus" and a long string of Beatles songs without authorization or clearance, which later inspired Willem Van Kuten to create a legitimate version.
