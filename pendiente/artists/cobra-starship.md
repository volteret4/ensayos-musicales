## songs
**Good Girls Go Bad (2009)** : This dance pop song was a single by Cobra Starship, featuring TV actress Leighton Meester from "Gossip Girl." It proved to be the biggest hit for both Decadence and Fueled by Ramen labels, reaching number seven on the Hot 100 in 2009.
