# artist - Glee

## songs
**Baby Got Back (1992)** : In 2013, the Fox TV show "Glee" covered Jonathan Coulton's acoustic version of "Baby Got Back" "practically note for note." Because Coulton's version was considered a new arrangement but not a new composition, only Sir Mix-A-Lot earned formal credit from Glee.
