## curiosities
**Duet with Bon Jovi on "Who Says You Can't Go Home"** : Jennifer Nettles, lead singer of Sugarland, partnered with Jon Bon Jovi for a rerecording of "Who Says You Can't Go Home," which successfully topped Billboard's Hot Country Songs in 2006. This was a notable instance of a country star helping a pop artist achieve country chart success through a duet.
