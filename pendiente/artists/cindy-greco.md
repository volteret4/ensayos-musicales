# artist - Cindy Greco

## songs
**Laverne & Shirley (1976)** : Cindy Greco performed the theme to "Happy Days" spin-off "Laverne and Shirley." This theme song reached number 25 in 1976, and notably, it rode the top 40 charts concurrently with the "Happy Days" theme.
