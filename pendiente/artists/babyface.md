# artist - Babyface

## albums
**Waiting to Exhale Soundtrack (1995)** : This acclaimed soundtrack, produced by Babyface, was a multi-platinum album and strong competition for Album of the Year at the 1997 Grammys. It was cited as one of the albums that "sounded more like the mid-90s" than Celine Dion's "Falling Into You," which ultimately won.
