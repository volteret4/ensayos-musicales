**Waiting to Exhale Soundtrack (1995)** : This acclaimed soundtrack, produced by Babyface, was a multi-platinum album and strong competition for Album of the Year at the 1997 Grammys. It was cited as one of the albums that "sounded more like the mid-90s" than Celine Dion's "Falling Into You," which ultimately won. ## songs
**I Honestly Love You (1998)** : Babyface, an R&B singer-songwriter, provided backing vocals for Olivia Newton-John's R&B-flavored remake of her 1974 chart-topper "I Honestly Love You." The reimagined slow jam reached number 67 on the Hot 100.
