## songs
**The Flying Saucer (1956)** : This hit was created by producer Dickie Goodman and his partner Bill Buchanan. It reinvented Orson Welles' "War of the Worlds" as a spoken-word news broadcast, punctuated by snippets of current rock and roll hits, for which Goodman failed to get permission. It is arguably the "Rock era's first mixtape mashup."
