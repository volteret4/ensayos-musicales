## curiosities
**Motown Backing Track Recording** : Under Mickey Stevenson's new multitracking system at Motown, the Funk Brothers would record backing tracks whenever a writer or producer had material for them. Singers would then overdub their vocals later. **Motown House Band (1960s)** : The Funk Brothers were the Motown house band whose "unstoppable playing" contributed to the distinctive "Motown sound." Their contribution included a driving snare on every beat, not just the backbeat, making Motown hits irresistible to both white and black audiences.
