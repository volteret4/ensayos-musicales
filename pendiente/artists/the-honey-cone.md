## songs
**Want Ads (1971)** : This "sturdy" song topped the Hot 100 in 1971. It was notable as one of the few girl group number ones during the first half of the 1970s, and the trio was stylistically similar to The Supremes, though not on Motown. **Want Ads (1971)** : This "delectable Funk Pop jam" reached number one and featured guitar licks by a teenage Rae Parker Jr. Its pivotal verse line, "Wanted Young Man Single and Free," makes this one-off smash a minor early 70s soul classic. It was the last Girl Group number one on the Hot 100 until the emergence of disco a half decade later.
