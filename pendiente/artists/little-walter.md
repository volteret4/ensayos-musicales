# artist - Little Walter

## curiosities
**Influence on Bob Dylan's Sound** : Bob Dylan, in preparing for his 1997 album "Time Out of Mind" with producer Daniel Lanois, expressed a specific desire to sound like an old bluesman, drawing inspiration from figures like Little Walter. This aim to emulate the raw, traditional blues style deeply influenced the album's sonic direction.
