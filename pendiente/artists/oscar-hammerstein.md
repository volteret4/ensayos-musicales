**The Sound of Music (1960)** : In 1959, Richard Rogers and Oscar Hammerstein's stage musical, "The Sound of Music," debuted on Broadway. By January 1960, its original cast recording became the number one album in America and remains the best charting cast album of a stage musical in Billboard history, having spent 16 weeks atop the album charts. ## songs
**My Favorite Things (1959)** : This song is one of the indelible standards from "The Sound of Music," so well known that pop megastar Ariana Grande built her number one smash "Seven Rings" out of it, reportedly leading the estate of Rogers and Hammerstein to take the majority of the publishing rights for Grande's song. **Some Enchanted Evening (1949)** : From the Broadway smash "South Pacific," this song was added to the Great American Songbook and became a significant hit, making Billboard's top ten four more times in 1949 alone through covers by major singers like Frank Sinatra, Bing Crosby, Jo Stafford, and Perry Como.
