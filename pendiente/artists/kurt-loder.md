# artist - Kurt Loder

## curiosities
**MTV News Special Report** : Kurt Loder delivered an MTV News Special Report on April 8th, 1994, announcing the death of Kurt Cobain. He reported that Cobain's body was found in Seattle, dead of an "apparently self-inflicted shotgun blast to the head," with a suicide note at the scene.
