## songs
**When a Man Loves a Woman (1966)** : Percy Sledge's performance, recorded in Muscle Shoals, Alabama, sounded like a standard from the moment it emerged and topped the Hot 100 in 1966. The power of this country soul pioneer's rendition is its air of "abject desperation," described as a plea more than a plea to love. ## curiosities
**Muscle Shoals Connection** : Percy Sledge was among the artists who recorded with the Muscle Shoals Rhythm Section for Atlantic Records in the mid-1960s, contributing to the label's legacy of popularizing Rhythm and Blues.
