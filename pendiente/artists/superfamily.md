## curiosities
**Hall and Oates Emulation** : The Norwegian pop group Superfamily, with lead singer Steven Ray Wilson whose voice was a "dead ringer" for Darryl Hall's, wrote an original song called "I Could Be a Real Winner" that sounded like a "would-be *Private Eyes* bonus track," paying homage to the Hall and Oates sound.
