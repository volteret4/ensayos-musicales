## curiosities
**Collaboration with Chris Cunningham** : Chris Cunningham, known for his unique visual style that merges the organic with the mechanical and creates unsettling dystopian worlds, directed videoclips for Squarepusher. "A partir de ahí empezaría una colaboración que abarcaría artistas como Square Pysher o a Fex-Twyl, llevando a dirigir artistas de la talla de Bjork o Madonna." ← La Magia Perdida de los Videoclips de los 90s | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=awdE-N1bnLY
**Second Wave IDM Artist** : Was among the "second wave" of IDM artists, with their music being edited and released by key labels such as Me le plato, Planet Moo, Mor, and El Scam. During this period, percussion became more central, influenced by jungle and drum and bass. "Me le plato, Planet Moo, Mor, o El Scam, se convirtieron en espacios clave para editar a músicos como Yucic, Bola, Alvanoto, Fortead, Squid Pusher." ← Qué fue el IDM y por qué cambió la música electrónica | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RTwn3hP1XTg
