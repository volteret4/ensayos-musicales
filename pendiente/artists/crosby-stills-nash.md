# artist - Crosby, Stills & Nash

## concerts
- Woodstock 1969

## albums
**Crosby, Stills & Nash (1969)** : This self-titled debut album was the highest-ranking album by a Woodstock act on the Billboard Top LPs chart during the week ending August 16, 1969, where it reached number 7. The album eventually achieved its peak position of number 6.
