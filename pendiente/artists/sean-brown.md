**Rappenduke (1984)** : An "earliest undisputed popular mashup of rap with cowboy culture," this novelty single featured Sean Brown doing an "intentionally wooden John Wayne impression" and a refrain (Dah-ah-dah-ha) parodying Wayne's stiff laughter, set to a simple 808 beat. Its video depicted a cowpoke with a boombox, and the rap lyrics were filled with Western tropes. It reached number 73 on Billboard's R&B chart in spring 1985.
