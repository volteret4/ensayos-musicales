# artist - Pandora's Box

## members
- Elaine Caswell

## albums
**Original Album with "It's All Coming Back to Me Now"** : This short-lived 1989 all-female vocal group, assembled by Jim Steinman, first recorded "It's All Coming Back to Me Now." Group member Elaine Caswell, a power vocalist who frequently worked with Steinman, took the lead on this track. ## songs
**Good Girls Go to Heaven (Bad Girls Go Everywhere) (1989)** : This song, named after a quote by early 20th-century sex symbol Mae West, was a "classic Jim Steinman" track: anthemic, muscular rock with a "coy wink and big pop hooks." It eventually formed the "sea" of Meat Loaf's *Bat Out of Hell II*. "Good girls go to heaven. **Original Recording of "It's All Coming Back to Me Now"** : This was the first recorded version of the mega power ballad, with Elaine Caswell on lead vocals. Steinman considered it his attempt to write the "most passionate romantic song" he ever could, inspired by Emily Bronte's *Wuthering Heights*. This version did not reach its full potential until Celine Dion later recorded it.
