**Bette Davis Eyes (1981)** : This song became the first major synth rock chart topper of the 80s, an improbable smash that topped the Hot 100 for nine weeks in the spring and summer of 1981. Originally written by Jackie DeShannon as Big Band Pop in the 70s, Carnes and her band transformed it with her gravelly voice into a shimmering, synth-heavy, kinetic clapping jam. The song seemed to anticipate the launch of MTV in August 1981, as it was designed for the music video age.
