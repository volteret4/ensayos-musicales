**Bette Davis Eyes (1981)** : Kim Carnes' electrified cover of Jackie DeShannon's 1974 deep cut, which successfully transformed the original "slap-happy piano pop ditty" into a "shimmering new wave" sound. Carnes' husky-voiced rendition topped the Hot 100 for nine weeks and was crowned the number one song of 1981. **Bette Davis Eyes (1981)** : This song became the first major synth rock chart topper of the 80s, an improbable smash that topped the Hot 100 for nine weeks in the spring and summer of 1981. Originally written by Jackie DeShannon as Big Band Pop in the 70s, Carnes and her band transformed it with her gravelly voice into a shimmering, synth-heavy, kinetic clapping jam. The song seemed to anticipate the launch of MTV in August 1981, as it was designed for the music video age.
