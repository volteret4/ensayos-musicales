## songs
**Brand New (1994)** : This hip hop soul jam, sung by Missy Elliott and co-produced by Devanté Swing and Timbaland, was in the style of Mary J. Blige. It was a minor hit for Sista, reaching a modest number 84 on the R&B chart in late summer 1994. ## curiosities
**Renaming and Signing (1994)** : The girl group Phase, featuring Missy Elliott, was renamed Sista by Devanté Swing after he signed them to his production house, Debasement. They were then signed to Elektra Records in 1994. "Phase he renamed Sista. And he gave Tim the nickname Timbaland after the hiking shoe that had taken off in the hood in the early 90s." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Flip It and Reverse It Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Album Shelving (1994)** : Due to the poor chart performance of their single "Brand New" (reaching only number 84 on the R&B chart), Elektra Records ultimately shelved Sista's debut album.
