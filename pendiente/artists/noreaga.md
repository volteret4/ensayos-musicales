## songs
**SuperThug (1998)** : This track, produced by The Neptunes, definitively established their signature sound. It was built around a melody line from a Corg synthesizer programmed to emulate a medieval clavichord, creating a "nagging, proudly synthetic sound" that sharply cut through radio broadcasts. Although it only reached number 15 on the R&B chart and number 36 on the Hot 100, "SuperThug" was deeply influential, leading many artists to seek out The Neptunes' unique production style.
