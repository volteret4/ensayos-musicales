# artist - James Ferraro

## albums
**Far Side Virtual (2011)** : This album was another important early release in the vaporwave scene. It differentiated itself by using fewer samplers and having a sound closer to Muzak and predefined digital timbres often associated with MIDI. The album was seen as visionary for evoking a world where technology and marketing had infiltrated and colonized personal space. **Far Side Virtual (2011)** : This album is cited as an example demonstrating that new sounds could be found in unexpected places and through different techniques within Dungeon Synth, utilizing old video game software and outdated MIDI samples to evoke an immersive journey.
