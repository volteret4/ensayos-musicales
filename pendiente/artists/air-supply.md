**Making Love Out of Nothing at All (1983)** : This bombastic "ultra-power ballad" was written by Jim Steinman, who developed it from a melody he had been tinkering with for years, originally the main title theme from the 1980 film *A Small Circle of Friends*. Steinman produced the single, featuring his usual team including Roy Bittan, Max Weinberg, and Rory Dodd, resulting in it sounding like a "twin" to Bonnie Tyler's "Total Eclipse of the Heart." It rose to number 2 on the Hot 100 in the week ending October 8, 1983, while Tyler's song was at number one, giving Steinman a "hammerlock" on the top of the charts.
