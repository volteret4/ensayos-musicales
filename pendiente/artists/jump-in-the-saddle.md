# artist - Jump in the Saddle

## songs
**The Curly Shuffle (1984)** : This smash hit from the Chicago band sold well enough to reach number 15 in early 1984. However, its radio spins were largely limited to morning and drive-time hours, and Jump in the Saddle never cracked the Hot 100 again.
