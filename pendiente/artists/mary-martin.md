# artist - Mary Martin

## albums
**The Sound of Music (1960)** : Broadway star Mary Martin led the original cast recording of "The Sound of Music," which debuted in January 1960 and became the number one album in America, holding the top spot for 16 weeks and establishing itself as the best-charting cast album of a stage musical in Billboard history.
