**Cheerleader (2015)** : This "globe-trotting EDM dancehall hit" by Jamaican pop singer Omi made a slow, steady climb to number one on the Hot 100, topping the chart in August and holding that position for a month. In a notable "chart upset," it ousted Wiz Khalifa and Charlie Puth's "See You Again" just after Labor Day to claim the 2015 Song of the Summer title, highlighting the importance of late-season momentum.
