# artist - Joe Jonas

## curiosities
**Inspiration for Taylor Swift's "Fearless" Lyrics (2008)** : Joe Jonas, lead singer for The Jonas Brothers, was the inspiration for "numerous heartbreak lyrics" on Taylor Swift's 2008 sophomore album, "Fearless," including the hit "Forever & Always." This marked an early instance of Swift's high-profile romantic relationships influencing her songwriting.
