# artist - Lucinda Williams

## albums
**Car Wheels on a Gravel Road (1998)** : This album, released in 1998, was declared that year's "most critically acclaimed album" and was a winner of the "Paz and Joppole" (likely Pazz & Jop Poll). Despite its high critical acclaim, Lucinda Williams accepted a second stage slot at Lilith Fair that year. ## curiosities
**Critically Acclaimed Artist on Second Stage** : Despite her 1998 album "Car Wheels on a Gravel Road" being highly critically acclaimed and winning the "Paz and Joppole," Lucinda Williams accepted a second stage slot at Lilith Fair in 1998. This situation was described as "a good problem to have" for the festival, indicating an abundance of major artists.
