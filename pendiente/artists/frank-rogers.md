# artist - Frank Rogers

## curiosities
**Producer for Darius Rucker's "Learn to Live"** : Frank Rogers, an established producer within the Nashville system who had worked with country hitmakers like Brad Paisley, Trace Adkins, and Josh Turner, produced Darius Rucker's country debut album, "Learn to Live."
