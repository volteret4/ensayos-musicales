# artist - The-Dream

## songs
**Shorty Is This Sh*t (2007)** : This was The-Dream's first single and is characterized by a "twinkling," "bass-heavy," and "plunky piano" style, which is also evident in his and Tricky Stewart's production work for Mariah Carey. **Umbrella (2007)** : The-Dream, born Terius Nash, led the trio of producers who wrote "Umbrella," a demo originally intended for Britney Spears, and he sang the demo himself before it was passed to Rihanna.
