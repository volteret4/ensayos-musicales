# artist - Chicory Tip

## genres
- British Rock

## instruments
- Moog synthesizer

## songs
**Son of My Father (1972)** : This British rock band covered Giorgio Moroder's glam pop song "Son of My Father," which Moroder wrote on a synthesizer. Chicory Tip's version featured a "fat-bottomed Moog synthesizer" and successfully topped the UK chart, also scraping the bottom rungs of the American Hot 100 in the winter of 1972.
