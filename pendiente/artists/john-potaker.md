# artist - John Potaker

## curiosities
**"Blue Monday 88" Remix Collaborator** : John Potaker, a fellow producer, collaborated with Quincy Jones to remix New Order's classic single "Blue Monday." This collaboration resulted in "Blue Monday 88," a version that for the first time brought the pioneering song onto the Hot 100 chart.
