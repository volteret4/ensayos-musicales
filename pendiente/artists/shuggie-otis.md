## albums
**Cold Shot** : At the age of 15, Sugar played all the guitar and bass parts on his father Johnny Otis's "Cold Shot" album. This record, credited to "the new Johnny Otis show," was a core band effort with Johnny Otis on piano and drums, and Delmar "Mighty Mouth" Evans on vocals. "Cold Shot, by the new Johnny Otis show, featured a core band of just three people. ## curiosities
**Early Start to Music Career** : Sugar began playing with his father Johnny Otis's band at the exceptionally young age of 11. To circumvent venues serving alcohol, he often wore false moustaches and sunglasses during performances.
