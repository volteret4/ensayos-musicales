# artist - Stevie B

## songs
**Because I Love You (The Postman Song)** : A full-on ballad, this song went all the way to number 1 on the Hot 100, where it spent four weeks, making it the longest-running chart-topper by any freestyle act. Stereogum Number Ones columnist Tom Bryant described it as "a chinsy slow jam, freestyle's biggest, blandest crossover hit of all." **I Wanna Be the One (1989)** : This firmly freestyle track marked Stevie B's breakthrough beyond Miami and New York, making it to number 32 on the national charts. **In My Eyes (1989)** : Following "I Wanna Be the One," this track reached number 37, continuing his steady rise on the national charts. **Love and Emotion (1990)** : In an effort to broaden his sound, Stevie B slowed the tempo of this track, moving closer to a New Jack Swing rhythm, which resulted in a number 15 hit. "On 1990's Love and Emotion, Stevie B took the tempo down a couple of steps, closer to a new Jack swing rhythm and he was rewarded with a number 15 hit." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Party Your Body** : This track became a staple in clubs and mixed shows in Miami and New York, but surprisingly did not chart on the Hot 100, indicating its strong regional and club-based popularity. **Spring Love (2013)** : Pitbull collaborated with Stevie B for a remake of his 1988 classic "Spring Love," with Pitbull's version leaning closer to modern EDM, though Stevie B reportedly did not mind the revival. ## curiosities
**"King of Freestyle" Self-Proclamation** : Stevie B referred to himself as the "King of Freestyle," highlighting his prominence in the genre. "You could see this most clearly with the man who called himself the King of Freestyle, the Stevie B." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Regional Success to National Breakthrough** : Initially a Fort Lauderdale native, he built a reputation as a club and mixed show staple in Miami and New York with tracks like "Party Your Body," but was slow to achieve national chart success until early 1988. "Fort Lauderdale native Stephen Bernard Hill, who recorded simply as Stevie B, became a club and mixed show staple in Miami and New York with tracks like Party Your Body, which didn't even make the hot 100." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Never Cracked Top 10 Again** : After his four-week number one hit "Because I Love You (The Postman Song)" at the end of 1990, Stevie B never achieved a spot in the pop top 10 again. "Stevie B never cracked the pop top 10 again." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Current Activity** : Stevie B remains active, participating in successful concert tours since the mid-2010s, often packaged with other vintage freestyle acts.
