# artist - The Force M.D.'s

## songs
**Tender Love (1985)** : This song, which became a hit in 1986, is an early example of the "wet sound" template developed by producers Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis. It features a chord progression very close to that found in "Human" by The Human League, "Can You Stand The Rain" by New Edition, and "Come Back to Me" by Janet Jackson. **Tender Love (1986)** : This song, produced by Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis, brought their "wet sound" production into the Billboard Top 10 in 1986. It represented an early version of their keyboard-driven sound that evoked the feeling of pouring rain in a pop song.
