## curiosities
**First Public Performance of "Me and Bobby McGee" (1969)** : Janis Joplin first performed "Me and Bobby McGee" with The Cosmic Blues Band in 1969 at the Fairgrounds in Nashville, where the crowd reacted enthusiastically. She later performed it again in Austin, Texas. "she was still in the Cosmic Blues Band at that time.
