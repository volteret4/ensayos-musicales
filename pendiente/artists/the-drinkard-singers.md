## curiosities
**Evolution to R&B Vocal Troupe** : Originating as a gospel troupe in the 1950s, The Drinkard Singers evolved into an in-demand R&B vocal troupe by the 1960s, showcasing their adaptability and influence across genres. **Legacy and Transformation** : A former vocalist from the group, Doris Troy, achieved a top 10 hit with "Just One Look" in August 1963, the same week Whitney Houston was born. By 1967, the group transformed itself into The Sweet Inspirations, maintaining a strong presence in R&B music.
