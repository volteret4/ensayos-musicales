**Love's Theme (1974)** : This "plush Philly Soul Dancefloor Hybrid" achieved number one status in February 1974, notably without featuring any of Barry White's legendary bass vocals. Some music historians have posited it as the first disco number one on the Hot 100, marking disco's emergence as a genre significantly benefiting from instrumental hits in the 1970s.
