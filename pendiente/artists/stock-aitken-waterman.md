**Hit Production Team (1980s)** : This British trio, whose name "sounded like a brokerage firm," produced hits for numerous acts ranging from Dead or Alive to Bananarama to Kylie Minogue for roughly three years. Their sound, particularly as heard on Rick Astley's "Never Gonna Give You Up," was notably indebted to vintage 70s club music, making them "proudly square, populist, and indebted to disco."
