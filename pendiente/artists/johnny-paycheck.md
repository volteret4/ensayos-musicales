# artist - Johnny Paycheck

## curiosities
**Country Veteran and Protestor** : Country veteran Johnny Paycheck attended the protest meeting against Olivia Newton-John's CMA win and was quoted as saying, "we don't want somebody out of another field coming in here and taking away what we've worked so hard for," reflecting the sentiment of traditional country artists.
