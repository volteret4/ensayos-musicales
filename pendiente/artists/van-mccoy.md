**The Hustle (1975)** : This instructional record, composed and recorded by Van McCoy "on a lark" in under an hour while concluding a session with his Soul City Symphony, became a widespread phenomenon. It capitalized on an intricate form of partner dancing gaining traction in New York City nightclubs and, despite spending only one week at number one in the summer of 1975, "announced once and for all that disco was happening." ## curiosities
**Producer of LaDiva (1979)** : Van McCoy, known as the artist behind the disco hit "The Hustle," produced Aretha Franklin's 1979 album *LaDiva* and its lead single "Ladies Only." "produced by Van McCoy, the artist behind the disco hit The Hustle." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say a Little Prayer Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eec154465cd600c4d67
**Songwriter, Producer, A&R Man** : Prior to composing "The Hustle," Van McCoy had established a career spanning over a decade as a songwriter, producer, and A&R man, working with notable artists such as Gladys Knight, The Shirelles, and Aretha Franklin. His appreciation for classical and ballroom music influenced his admiration for the "old school steps" of the dance that inspired his hit.
