## curiosities
**Virginia Beach High School Crew (1991)** : SBI was a rap crew and production team formed in 1991 by Virginia Beach high schoolers Pharrell Williams, his cousin Timothy Mosley (Timbaland), friend Melvin Barcliff (Magoo), and Filipino American friend Chad Hugo. Their demo tape, described as raw but promising with catchy beats, was eventually discovered by Teddy Riley. "This is a 1991 demo tape by SBI.
