## curiosities
**Performer at Alan Freed Show** : The Moonglows were one of the R&B stars Carole King saw perform at an Alan Freed show at the Paramount in Brooklyn, which was a pivotal experience for King in deciding her career path. "Zwick took her to an Alan Fried show at the Paramount in Brooklyn, where she saw Le Verne Baker, BB King, Mickey Baker, The Moon Glows, and several other R&B stars of the period." ← Episode 89： ＂Will You Love Me Tomorrow？＂ by the Shirelles | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Kns2HGcuSg
