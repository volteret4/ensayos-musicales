# artist - The Arrows

## songs
**I Love Rock 'n Roll (1975)** : The Arrows' 1975 "barn burner," a single by the British band that had failed to chart in either the UK or America. This song was later famously rebooted by Joan Jett & the Blackhearts, becoming their signature song and a number one hit. "this 1975 barn burner by British band The Arrows.
