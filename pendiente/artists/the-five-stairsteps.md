# artist - The Five Stairsteps

## curiosities
**Performance at Adelphi Ballroom (1967)** : The Five Stairsteps, an act from Chicago, were among the various Philadelphia label acts present to perform at the 1967 multi-act radio showcase at West Philly's Adelphi Ballroom, where Darryl Hall and John Oates first met.
