## songs
**The Fox (What Does The Fox Say) (2013)** : Recorded by Norwegian brothers Bard and Vegard Ylvisåker, a comedy duo known as Ylvis, this song was a parody of EDM created for their Norwegian TV talk show. It was an "oddly legitimate sounding dance pop song" accompanied by a "hilarious video of the brothers in Fox costumes, cavorting in a forest." Never intended for the U.S. market, it became a viral sensation and, under Billboard's new Hot 100 rules (including YouTube views), became an actual Top 10 hit, peaking at number six.
