# artist - Melly Mel

## curiosities
**Sampling Stevie Wonder (1984)** : Melly Mel sampled Stevie Wonder's 1963 hit "Fingertips" on Chaka-Kon's 1984 pop and hip-hop smash "I Feel for You", introducing the song to a new generation of listeners in the 1980s.
