# artist - Luke Combs

## genres
- Country

## songs
**Fast Car (2023)** : Luke Combs' "contrafied" country cover of Tracy Chapman's classic single, which became a smash hit in 2023. Combs' version not only reached number one on the country chart but also crossed over to the Hot 100 pop chart, peaking at number two—higher than Chapman's original. "Combs's version not only reached number one on the country chart, it also crossed over to the Hot 100 pop chart, and actually peaked higher than Chapman's original did, climbing all the way to number two." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Gotcha Covered Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f1d64fe6d21276e0fab

## curiosities
**Unicorn Cover (Mid-2020s)** : By mid-2020s standards, Luke Combs' cover of "Fast Car" is considered a "unicorn" because it is a very traditional cover that also achieved significant mainstream pop success, reaching number two on the Hot 100, which is rare in an era dominated by interpolations.
