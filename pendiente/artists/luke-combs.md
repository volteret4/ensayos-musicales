## songs
**Beautiful Crazy** : This song was at the top of the Hot Country Songs chart in March 2019, marking Luke Combs's fifth consecutive country number one. It would have faced competition from Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road" for the number one spot on the country charts had "Old Town Road" not been removed by Billboard, allowing "Beautiful Crazy" to remain atop the chart for several months. **Fast Car (2023)** : Luke Combs' "contrafied" country cover of Tracy Chapman's classic single, which became a smash hit in 2023. Combs' version not only reached number one on the country chart but also crossed over to the Hot 100 pop chart, peaking at number two—higher than Chapman's original. **Fast Car (2023)** : This country cover of Tracy Chapman's folk rock classic spent an impressive eight weeks at number two on the Hot 100 in the summer of 2023, one of the longest runs ever for a number two hit. Despite its sustained popularity, it ultimately finished in second place on the Songs of the Summer tally for that year. ## curiosities
**Unicorn Cover (Mid-2020s)** : By mid-2020s standards, Luke Combs' cover of "Fast Car" is considered a "unicorn" because it is a very traditional cover that also achieved significant mainstream pop success, reaching number two on the Hot 100, which is rare in an era dominated by interpolations.
