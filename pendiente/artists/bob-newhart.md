# artist - Bob Newhart

## genres
- Comedy

## albums
**The Button-Down Mind of Bob Newhart (1960)** : This comedy album achieved considerable commercial success, spending "14 weeks at number one on the LP chart in 1960, more than any other album that year," and remained at number one just weeks before Bob Newhart won Album of the Year in 1961. It marked the first time a comedy album took home the big prize at the Grammys.
