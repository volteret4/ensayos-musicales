# artist - 95 South

## songs
**Hoot There It Is (1993)** : This single, released in 1993, featured Miami Bass style production and went head-to-head on the Hot 100 with Tag Team's "Hoomp There It Is." Despite their Jacksonville origin, 95 South's track peaked at number 11.
