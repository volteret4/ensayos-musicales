**Recollection of Tom Petty & The Heartbreakers (1977)** : Glenn Tilbrook, leader of the British post-punk power pop band Squeeze, reminisced about seeing Tom Petty and The Heartbreakers on a double bill with The Ramones in London in 1977. He described them as "absolutely fantastic," noting their "punk energy" combined with "shoving shit and had it a real sort of craft to the band and a real driving energy."
