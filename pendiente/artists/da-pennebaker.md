# artist - D.A. Pennebaker

## curiosities
**Pioneering Music Documentarian** : A legendary documentarian who pioneered the music documentary genre, having directed films such as "Don't Look Back" (about Bob Dylan), "Monterey Pop," and "Ziggy Stardust and The Spiders from Mars" (about David Bowie). In 1988, he directed "101," which chronicled the finale of Depeche Mode's "Music for the Masses" tour. "legendary documentarian D.A. Pennebaker, who, a generation earlier, had pioneered the music documentary with Bob Dylan's Don't Look Back, Monterey Pop and David Bowie's Ziggy Stardust and The Spiders from Mars.
