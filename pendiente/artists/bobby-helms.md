**Jingle Bell Rock (1957)** : Country crossover star Bobby Helms released this easy-going rockabilly single in 1957, reaching number six on the charts a couple of weeks after Christmas. The song, which only charted for six weeks total, peaked in early January 1958, after the holiday season had ended. It functioned as both a holiday song and, to some extent, a novelty record, which was a popular genre in 1958.
