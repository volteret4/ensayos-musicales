## songs
**Tears Are Not Enough (1985)** : This Canadian famine relief offering was recorded in Toronto just weeks after USA for Africa's "We Are the World." The song was co-written and arranged by David Foster and Bryan Adams and featured a 50-member Canadian supergroup. Described as having a "remarkably sturdy melody" and "extra cheesy" production, it topped the charts in Canada. Although it did not chart in America, it was included as an album cut on "The We Are the World" album. ## curiosities
**Canadian Supergroup** : Northern Lights was a 50-member Canadian supergroup formed to produce a famine relief single. Their record, "Tears Are Not Enough," was created in Toronto shortly after the "We Are the World" sessions. **Unique Chart Success in Canada** : Canada was the only country where all three major national pride charity singles—"Do They Know It's Christmas?" by Band-Aid, "We Are the World" by USA for Africa, and "Tears Are Not Enough" by Northern Lights—hit number one on the charts. This phenomenon was attributed to strategic timing, as "Tears Are Not Enough" was released alongside "We Are the World" when global attention for the Ethiopian famine was at its peak.
