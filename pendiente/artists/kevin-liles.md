## curiosities
**Future Def Jam Executive** : Kevin Liles was a member of the Baltimore DJ crew Newmarks, who wrote "Girl You Know It's True" in 1986. He later became a prominent executive at Def Jam Records, where in the late 1990s, he signed rapper DMX and was responsible for generating all of DMX's big hits.
