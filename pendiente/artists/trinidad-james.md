# artist - Trinidad James

## songs
**All Gold Everything (2013)** : Atlanta rapper Trinidad James's 2013 hit, from which Bruno Mars "nicked the line, don't believe me, just watch" for "Uptown Funk." James was subsequently given a songwriting credit for this interpolation, highlighting the precise nature of musical sampling and borrowing in modern music.
