## songs
**Back In Your Door (1984)** : This was a "forgotten track" by the British new wave rock band, featured on the soundtrack to the 1984 movie "Teachers." Its pre-chorus hook, containing the lyric "I'm your non-stop miracle, I'm your man," was later re-purposed by Mutt Lang for Billy Ocean's "Get Out Of My Dreams, Get Into My Car."
