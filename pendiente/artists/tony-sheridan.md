# artist - Tony Sheridan

## genres
- Rock and Roll

## songs
**My Bonnie (1961)** : Tony Sheridan, a Hamburg, Germany-based rock and roll singer, recorded this traditional Scottish folk song in 1961 with the Beatles as his backing group. After the Beatles' explosion in America, the MGM label released this single, capitalizing on the Beatles' name despite Sheridan's German-accented English lead vocal. **My Bonnie Lies over the Ocean** : This song was a hit that featured The Beatles credited as a "back-end band," marking a rare instance where they shared credit on a record prior to their unprecedented full credit to Billy Preston on "Get Back."
