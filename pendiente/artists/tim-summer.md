# artist - Tim Summer

## labels
- Atlantic Records

## curiosities
**A&R Who Signed Hootie & the Blowfish** : Tim Summer was a musician and A&R man who ultimately signed Hootie & the Blowfish to Atlantic Records. He authored the memoir *Only Want to Be With You, the inside story of Hootie and the Blowfish*, which provides an inside perspective on the band's journey. **Indie Credibility** : Tim Summer brought significant indie credibility to Atlantic Records. He was a rock critic-turned bassist for the 80s Art Rock band Hugo Largo, traveled in New York hipster circles, wrote for *Altrock Bible* and *Trouser Press*, and played with no-wave pioneer Glenn Branca. His band Hugo Largo's debut EP was even produced by R.E.M.'s Michael Stipe. **Host of Postmodern MTV** : Tim Summer had a brief stint as a host for MTV's late-night alternative video show *Postmodern MTV*. During his time there, he co-hosted with figures like Michael Stipe, showcasing his connections and immersion in the alternative music scene of the era. **Hired by Danny Goldberg to Boost Alternative Roster** : Danny Goldberg recruited Tim Summer to Atlantic Records specifically to "beef up Atlantic's alternative roster." Summer initially championed Seattle-based punk bands like The Gits and Seven-Year Bitch, demonstrating his early focus on the more aggressive sounds of the alternative scene before discovering Hootie & the Blowfish. **Recognized Hootie's Potential Through Comparison** : Upon seeing Hootie & the Blowfish perform in 1993, Tim Summer was able to articulate their appeal through a combination of contemporary and antecedent influences. He noted that their songwriting and production evoked Heartland rocker John Mellencamp, their jangly guitars and Southern college rock sensibility were reminiscent of R.E.M., and Darius Rucker's "throaty, growly voice" brought to mind Eddie Vedder of Pearl Jam.
