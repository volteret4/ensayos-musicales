# artist - Sean O'Noel-Lennon

## member of
- The Peace Choir

## songs
**Give Peace a Chance (1991)** : Sean O'Noel-Lennon, John Lennon's actual child, co-led an all-star version of his father's anthem, "Give Peace a Chance," alongside Lenny Kravitz. This rendition, credited to The Peace Choir, was released in the run-up to the first Persian Gulf War and featured more than 40 eclectic acts.
