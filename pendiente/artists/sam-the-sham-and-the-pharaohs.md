## songs
**Wooly Bully (1965)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1965, pulling off a significant chart upset against artists like The Rolling Stones, The Beatles, The Supremes, and The Four Tops. It succeeded due to its nature as an "archetypal 60s party record" with a groove that "transcends generations." Its enduring cultural relevance is highlighted by its appearance in the 1982 teen movie "Fast Times at Ridgemont High."
