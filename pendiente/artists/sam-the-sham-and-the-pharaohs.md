## songs
**Wooly Bully (1965)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1965, pulling off a significant chart upset against artists like The Rolling Stones, The Beatles, The Supremes, and The Four Tops. It succeeded due to its nature as an "archetypal 60s party record" with a groove that "transcends generations." Its enduring cultural relevance is highlighted by its appearance in the 1982 teen movie "Fast Times at Ridgemont High." **Wooly Bully (1965)** : This "ebullient party record" only reached number two on the Hot 100 in the summer of 1965, but it was surprisingly named the top song of the year by Billboard for 1965. It achieved this distinction through its remarkable chart longevity, staying on the chart for 18 weeks, which was considered an "eternity" during an era when big hits typically cycled off in as little as 10 weeks. It was the first hit in Hot 100 history to claim the year-end top spot despite peaking in the runner-up position.
