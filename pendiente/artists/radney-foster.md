## curiosities
**90s Country Hitmaker and Rucker's Inspiration** : Radney Foster, an "Adele Rio Texas singer and 90s country hitmaker," served as Darius Rucker's primary inspiration to pursue a country music career. Rucker stated that ever since hearing Foster, he would say, "I'm going to make a country record someday," which became a "mantra" in his head.
