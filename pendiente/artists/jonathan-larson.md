# artist - Jonathan Larson

## curiosities
**Creator of Rent** : Jonathan Larson created "Rent," a musical reimagining of Puccini's opera "La Bohème" as a story of New York Bohemians living in the shadow of HIV AIDS, which became the first major Generation X musical. "Rent was a reinvention of the Puccini opera La Boheme as a story of New York Bohemians living in the shadow of HIV AIDS. And it emerged as the first major generation X musical. Its creator, Jonathan Larson, quite literally gave his life for the show." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Lullaby of Broadway Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Tragic Death Before Premiere** : Jonathan Larson tragically died unexpectedly from an aortic tear, misdiagnosed as stress, on the morning of "Rent's" first off-Broadway preview performance, literally giving his life for the show.
