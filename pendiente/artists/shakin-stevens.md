# artist - Shakin' Stevens

## curiosities
**Quintessential British Hitmaker** : Shakin' Stevens was described as a "goofy, quintessentially British hitmaker" known for holiday-centric novelty records. In 1985, his song "Merry Christmas Everyone" became a twinkly and twee Christmas number one. "the goofy quintessentially British hitmaker shaken Stevens with 1985's twinkly and tweed Mary Christmas everyone." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Christmas is All Around Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Quirky Christmas #1 Example** : Shakin' Stevens is presented as an example of the kind of "cheese, the naff, the absurd" artist that embodies the unique spirit of the British Christmas number one tradition. His presence on the Christmas chart is implicitly contrasted with the dominance of "established platinum superstars" in the streaming era. "It shouldn't be about established platinum superstars like Sheeran or Grande, whatever you think of them. It should be about the cheese, the naff, the absurd, the cliff-richards, shaken Stevens's, hell, even the Mr. Blobby's." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Christmas is All Around Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
