**When the Heart Rules the Mind (1986)** : This "prog-on-pop" single was a sizable hit from GTR's self-titled album. It debuted on the Hot 100 in late Spring 1986 and by June, was two positions ahead of Genesis's "Invisible Touch" at number 35. It eventually reached a respectable number 14. ## curiosities
**Fantasy League Supergroup (1986)** : GTR was described as a "fantasy league" for readers of *Guitar World* magazine, teaming up guitarists Steve Howe (formerly of Yes and Asia) and Steve Hackett (formerly of Genesis).
