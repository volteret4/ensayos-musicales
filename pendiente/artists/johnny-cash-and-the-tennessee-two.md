## curiosities
**Development of the "Boom-Chick-a-Boom" Rhythm** : Since the group did not have a drummer, Johnny Cash innovated by placing paper between the strings and the fretboard of his acoustic guitar. This deadened the sound, approximating a snare drum, and resulted in the "boom chick" sound, which became a signature of Cash's recordings for decades. This unique country music take on the two-beat rhythm was largely born out of instrumental necessity. "As the Tennessee 2 didn't have a drummer, Cash put paper between the strings and the fretboard of his acoustic guitar to deaden the sound and turn it into something that approximated the sound of the snare drum." ← Episode 37： ＂I Walk The Line＂ by Johnny Cash | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1uhw6KjgSME
**Name Change from The Tennessee 3** : When "Hey Porter" was released, Sam Phillips suggested a slight name change from The Tennessee 3, and the single came out under the name Johnny Cash and the Tennessee 2.
