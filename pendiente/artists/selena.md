## albums
**Amor Prohibido (1994)** : Selena's last all-Spanish album, released in 1994, showcased a wide array of genres. It not only featured her signature Tejano sound but also incorporated R&B and hip-hop influences, demonstrating her versatility. **Dreaming of You (1995)** : Selena's first partial English album was mostly finished at the time of her tragic death in March 1995. Following her passing, *Dreaming of You* made history as the first predominantly Spanish-language album to debut at number one on the Billboard 200 album chart. The label released a half-dozen singles from the album, and it was ultimately certified Triple Platinum. **Industry Recognition and Crossover Plan (1989)** : Years before the breakthroughs of Ricky Martin, Jennifer Lopez, or Shakira, Selena was recognized for her immense potential by the recording industry. She was signed to EMI's Latin division in 1989 with a long-term strategy to introduce her to the Anglo market.
