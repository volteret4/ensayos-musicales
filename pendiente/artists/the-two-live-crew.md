## songs
**Banned in the USA (1990)** : The Two Live Crew were featured on Luther Campbell's (Luke) 1990 top 20 hit "Banned in the USA." The credit "Luke featuring the two live crew" was considered strange because Luke himself was a member of the group. ## curiosities
**Pioneers of Miami Bass and Censorship Controversy** : This unabashedly lewd rap troupe was among the first to turn the bodacious Miami Bass style into platinum in the late 1980s. They became a "lightning rod for criticism and censorship of rap" due to their explicit content.
