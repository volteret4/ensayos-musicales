# artist - Mark Hoyle

## songs
**We Built This City on Sausage Rolls (2018)** : "We Built This City on Sausage Rolls" is Mark Hoyle (Ladbaby)'s 2018 cover of Starship's "We Built This City," featuring new lyrics as an homage to his favorite food. Despite its "barely competent" backing track and lyrics not as good as Weird Al's, the song was a campaign to bring back the "cheese, the naff, the absurd" to the UK Christmas number one contest, contrasting with the polished global hits dominating charts due to streaming. "Yes, this is a cover of We Built This City, the 1985 Starship Smash, with new lyrics and a new title. ## curiosities
**2018 UK Christmas Number One Campaign - "We Built This City on Sausage Rolls"** : Mark Hoyle, known online as Ladbaby, initiated a campaign in late 2018 to get his wife, Roxanne Hoyle (LadbabyMum), a unique Christmas present: a UK Christmas number one. Despite Hoyle having "essentially no musical talent," he released a cover of Starship's "We Built This City" with new, sausage roll-themed lyrics. This whimsical attempt, aiming for an "absurd" and "naff" hit, was seen as a delightful throwback to the traditional British Christmas number one spirit, offering a small glimmer of hope against the growing dominance of global streaming hits.
