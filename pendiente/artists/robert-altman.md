# artist - Robert Altman

## curiosities
**"MASH" Film (1970)** : Robert Altman directed the 1970 film "MASH," which was set in Korea but served as a clear statement about the Vietnam War. Altman had previously worked with Timothy Brown, who played Spear-chucker Jones in "MASH," before casting him as Tommy Brown in "Nashville." **"Nashville" Film (1975)** : Robert Altman directed the 1975 film "Nashville," a satirical look at the 1970s country music industry and American politics. The film included the character Tommy Brown, widely believed to be based on Charlie Pride, although Pride denied this. Altman's methodology for the film involved actors singing their own songs, which were then integrated into the screenplay. **Actor-Driven Music in "Nashville"** : For his film "Nashville," Robert Altman gave actors the opportunity to sing their own songs, often compositions they had already written outside the film's production. The screenplay was then specifically written to incorporate these songs, allowing for a unique integration of character and music.
