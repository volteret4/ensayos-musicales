## concerts
- **Live at the Acropolis (1994)** : A landmark concert special filmed at the Acropolis in Athens, Greece, with the Parthenon as a backdrop. Directed by George Varis and backed by the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra, this spectacle was entirely self-funded by Yani and Linda Evans (approximately $2 million) with the specific goal of licensing it to PBS Pledge Drives. The production was designed to be visually grand, employing techniques like lighting the Parthenon, using wide-angle lenses, and "power shots" to create an "Olympic opening ceremony" feel, despite modest attendance. "The 1994 TV Special of Yani's concert at the Acropolis has all the trappings of a musician doing a victory lap after really hitting the big time."
