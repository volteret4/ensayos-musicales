# artist - Pretty Tony

## genres
- Electro
- Freestyle

## curiosities
**Electro Simplification & Freestyle Origin** : Miami producer Tony Butler, known as Pretty Tony, simplified the Electro sound for records produced on limited budgets, notably on tracks like "Don't Stop the Rock." The group name "Freestyle" associated with this track is widely credited as the likely origin of the genre name. **Shift to Miami Freestyle** : Pretty Tony played a pivotal role in shifting the Electro sound towards what would become Miami freestyle, particularly through his production work for club singer Deborah Kowalski (Debbie Deb).
