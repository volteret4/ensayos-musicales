# artist - Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde

## songs
**Genius Rapp (1981)** : This duo covered Tom Tom Club's "Genius of Love" almost immediately in 1981, directly converting Tina Weymouth's distinctive singing into straight-up rapping. This early adaptation demonstrated how melodic hooks from other genres could be reinterpreted and integrated into a pure rap style.
