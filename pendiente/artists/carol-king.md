## albums
**Music (1971)** : Carol King's immediate follow-up to *Tapestry*, released in the same year. Less remembered today, this album was rumored to have sold a massive 1.3 million copies on its first day. Due to data lag on the Billboard charts, it reached number one just after Christmas on a chart dated January 1st, 1972, concluding the musical year of 1971 with Carol King at number one. **Tapestry (1971)** : This album featured songs that were both distinctly Carol King's and adaptable to other artists. Its expectations were initially modest due to the poor sales of her previous album, *Writer*, but it benefited from a market opened by Joni Mitchell and James Taylor's successful solo singer-songwriter albums. It went on to spend 15 consecutive weeks at #1 on the Billboard Top LPs chart, a record for the longest run since The Beatles' *Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band* in 1967. "This was the thing about the album, Carol King, would ultimately name tapestry. Its songs were both distinctly Carol King's and adaptable to others." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Spirit of ’71, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Writer (1970)** : Carol King's largely ignored debut album as a recording artist, released in 1970. It failed to chart, despite employing similar soulful singing, warm piano, and reinterpretation of 60s songs for the 70s, which would later make her a legend. "Upon its release in 1970, the writer LP didn't chart even though it uses some of the same approaches as the album that would make King a legend just one year later." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Spirit of ’71, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

**Go Away Little Girl (1962)** : A song written by Carol King and Jerry Goffin in 1962, originally recorded by King as a demo for Bobby Vee, whose version was not a hit. ## curiosities
**Birth Name Change** : Carol King was born Carol Klein in Manhattan, later adopting her more well-known professional name. "King, born Carol Klein in Manhattan, was the melody writer and Goffin, the lyricist, for a string of singles from a range of artists." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Spirit of ’71, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Brill Building Songwriter** : Carol King, alongside her then-husband and lyricist Jerry Goffin, worked as a songwriting team from the famed Brill Building at 1650 Broadway in New York City. They ranked among the building's most successful "two-person hit factories," channeling youthful emotions into sophisticated pop singles for various artists. "The songwriting team and then married couple wrote four and from the Fiend Brill building at 1650 Broadway in New York City." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Spirit of ’71, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Early Solo Career Struggles** : In the early 1960s, Carol King's attempts to establish herself as a front-line recording artist largely fizzled. Her voice was considered an "odd fit" for the Girl Group and Teen Idol era, with a follow-up single "He's a Bad Boy" stalling at #94 after the perceived fluke success of "It Might as Well Rain Until September." "King's voice was an odd fit for the Girl Group and Teen Idol era of the early 60s, and attempts to promote her as a front-line act quickly fizzled." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Spirit of ’71, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Marriage Disintegration and West Coast Move** : By 1967, Carol King's marriage to Jerry Goffin was disintegrating, prompting her to move out west to Los Angeles. This move introduced her to the vibrant Laurel Canyon scene, a hotbed of 60s rock counterculture and a key instigator of the singer-songwriter movement, where she befriended influential artists like Joni Mitchell and James Taylor. **Aged 29: New Artist, Veteran Songwriter** : At 29 years old, Carol King presented a unique profile as she gained commercial success: she was a relatively new artist as a singer, yet a seasoned veteran as a songwriter with a decade of experience, which equipped her with an innate ability to deliver hits.
