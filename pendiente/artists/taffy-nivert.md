# artist - Taffy Nivert

## member of
- Fat City
- Starland Vocal Band

## songs
**Afternoon Delight (1976)** : Taffy Nivert, along with Bill Danoff, John Carroll, and Margot Chapman, formed the four-part harmony for "Afternoon Delight." She recognized that while she and Bill were good singers, they weren't great, so they brought in other talented vocalists. ## curiosities
**"Kiss of Death" Remark for Best New Artist Grammy** : As a vocalist for the Starland Vocal Band, Taffy Nivert famously described the Best New Artist Grammy, which her band won in 1977, as "the kiss of death," after which the band never charted again.
