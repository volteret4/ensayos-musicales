# artist - Jane Lynch

## songs
**Physical (2010)** : Jane Lynch, a star of the musical TV series "Glee," sang an EDM-style club remake of Olivia Newton-John's 1981 smash "Physical" with Newton-John herself. This collaboration marked Newton-John's last Hot 100 hit, reaching number 89.
