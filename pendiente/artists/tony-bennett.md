# artist - Tony Bennett

## albums
**Cheek to Cheek (2014)** : This album featured 11 tracks of duets exclusively with Lady Gaga, following discussions between them after the success of their 2011 duet, "The Lady Is a Tramp." They performed standards from composers such as Cole Porter, Irving Berlin, George Gershwin, and Jerome Kern. The album debuted at number one in October 2014, marking Bennett's second straight chart-topping album. **Duets II (2011)** : A chart-topping album featuring Tony Bennett collaborating with 17 singing partners. Lady Gaga was one of these luminaries, contributing to the track "The Lady Is a Tramp." Other collaborators included Aretha Franklin, Natalie Cole, and Willie Nelson. Critics noted the strength of Gaga's vocals and her rapport with Bennett, a collaboration that would later prove significant for her career. ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Gaga is Born Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**MTV Unplugged (1994)** : This collection of Great American Songbook standards, performed on the MTV Unplugged stage, won Album of the Year two years after Eric Clapton's "Unplugged" album. Despite its Grammy win, the album had barely scraped the top 70 on the Billboard 200 during its 1994 chart run, making it the lowest charting Album of the Year winner in Grammy history to date. It won over competition from Green Day, TLC, Snoop Dogg, an all-blues album by Clapton, and a live album by The Three Tenors. ## curiosities
**Performer of Johnny Mercer Standards** : Tony Bennett was one of the esteemed artists who performed numerous standards composed by Johnny Mercer, the individual who founded Capitol Records. **Target of Coasters' Market Transition** : Tony Bennett was identified as an artist whose records were purchased by the "adult white market," which The Coasters aimed to transition into as their teenage pop fan base declined. "The kind of people who were buying records by Tony Bennett or Andy Williams." ← Episode 68： ＂Yakety Yak＂ by the Coasters | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtMHDD0-oOo
**Vocal Style Avoiding Malisma** : Tony Bennett is identified as one of the notable vocalists from the first half of the 20th century who adhered to the practice of singing one note per syllable, hitting each note precisely without excessive ornamentation, consistent with the prevailing definition of good singing at the time. "If you listen to any of the great voices of the first part of the 20th century, Sinatra, Bing Crosby, Tony Bennett, they will almost without exception hit the note dead arm, one note per syllable." ← Episode 32： ＂I Got A Woman＂ by Ray Charles | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0oeI9f2x_fo
