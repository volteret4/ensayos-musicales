# artist - Tony Bennett

## albums
**MTV Unplugged (1994)** : This collection of Great American Songbook standards, performed on the MTV Unplugged stage, won Album of the Year two years after Eric Clapton's "Unplugged" album. Despite its Grammy win, the album had barely scraped the top 70 on the Billboard 200 during its 1994 chart run, making it the lowest charting Album of the Year winner in Grammy history to date. It won over competition from Green Day, TLC, Snoop Dogg, an all-blues album by Clapton, and a live album by The Three Tenors. ## songs
**The Shadow of Your Smile (1965)** : This jazzy pop standard, penned for the Elizabeth Taylor film "The Sandpiper" (which flopped), was covered by dozens of artists. Tony Bennett's version reached number 95 on the Hot 100 in 1965. It famously won the 1966 Grammy for Song of the Year, beating The Beatles' "Yesterday." ## curiosities
**Performer of Johnny Mercer Standards** : Tony Bennett was one of the esteemed artists who performed numerous standards composed by Johnny Mercer, the individual who founded Capitol Records. **Target of Coasters' Market Transition** : Tony Bennett was identified as an artist whose records were purchased by the "adult white market," which The Coasters aimed to transition into as their teenage pop fan base declined. "The kind of people who were buying records by Tony Bennett or Andy Williams." ← Episode 68： ＂Yakety Yak＂ by the Coasters | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtMHDD0-oOo
**Vocal Style Avoiding Malisma** : Tony Bennett is identified as one of the notable vocalists from the first half of the 20th century who adhered to the practice of singing one note per syllable, hitting each note precisely without excessive ornamentation, consistent with the prevailing definition of good singing at the time.
