# artist - Jabrieth

## curiosities
**Out, Non-Chart-Topping Performer** : Jabrieth is cited as an artist who was out of the closet but did not achieve number one status on the Hot 100 or the pop album chart. This highlights the challenges openly queer artists faced in topping mainstream charts in earlier periods.
