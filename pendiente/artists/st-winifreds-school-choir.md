**There's No One Quite Like Grandma (1980)** : This "hokey holiday chestnut" by a primary school singing group of kids aged around seven took the UK Christmas number one crown in 1980, staying at the top for a fortnight. Its success highlighted the unpredictable nature of the UK Christmas chart competition, as such a one-off act would be unlikely to top the charts without the unique holiday context.
