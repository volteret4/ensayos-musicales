## concerts
- **2014 Grammy Awards Performance** : Duffbunk, along with Ferel Williams, Nile Rodgers, and special guest Stevie Wonder, performed a medley of "Get Lucky" and Stevie Wonder's "Another Star" at the 2014 Grammy Awards at the Staples Center. "Draft Pump, Ferel, Nile Rogers and Special guest Stevie Wonder performed a medley of Get Lucky and Wonders Classic, another star."

## curiosities
**Carrying the Chic Sound Torch** : French EDM duo Duffbunk was recognized for most firmly carrying the torch of the Chic sound, which flowed through a new wave of electronic dance music in the 21st century after they built a large audience for their robotic throwback future funk.
