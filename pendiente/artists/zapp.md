## curiosities
**Dayton, Ohio Funk Originators** : Zapp is identified as one of several funk bands that originated from Dayton, Ohio, a city credited as the "birthplace of funk." Their music, along with that of other Ohio bands, contributed to defining the sound of the 1970s and 1980s. **Vocoder Sound Beloved in Hip-Hop** : The distinctive vocoder sound pioneered by Zapp became highly influential and "beloved in hip-hop circles," leading to collaborations such as Roger Troutman's involvement in Tupac and Dr. Dre's "California Love" in 1995.
