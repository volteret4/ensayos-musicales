## curiosities
**"Jive Talkin'" Synthesizer Hook** : A Welsh keyboardist brought in by producer Arif Mardin, Blue Weaver played a distinctive synthesizer hook on The Bee Gees' "Jive Talkin'," which was noted for recalling the Synclavier playing style of Stevie Wonder.
