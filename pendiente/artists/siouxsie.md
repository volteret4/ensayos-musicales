## curiosities
**Dislike of "Goth" Label** : Siouxsie, described as a "fantastic art pop slash art rock performer," reportedly disassociates herself from the "goth" label. During an interview, Ned Raggett deliberately avoided mentioning the word "goth," for which Siouxsie expressed deep gratitude, indicating her weariness of the association, a common sentiment among artists who often reject genre tags applied by audiences.
