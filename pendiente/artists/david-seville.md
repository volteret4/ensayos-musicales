## curiosities
**Sped-up Recording Technique Pioneer** : David Seville developed the clever trick of singing songs slowly and then speeding up the tape to pitch his voice high, a technique that was "novel" at the time. He applied this to his smash hit "Witch Doctor" in April 1958, and it became a "wacky sped up vocal fad" that year, influencing other artists like Sheb Wooley.
