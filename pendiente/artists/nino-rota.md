# artist - Nino Rota

## songs
**Love Theme from Romeo and Juliet (1968)** : This was Nino Rota's signature melody, composed for Franco Zeffirelli's 1968 film adaptation of Shakespeare's romantic tragedy. Henry Mancini later covered and orchestrated this theme, which became a surprise number one hit in 1969.
