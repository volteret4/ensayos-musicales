## songs
**Mambo No. 5 (1999)** : A dance pop interpretation of Perez Prado's 1950s classic, this song became a global hit for German-born Latin pop musician Lou Bega in the summer of 1999. Despite its popularity, this "gleefully junky record" was intentionally unreleased on CD single.
