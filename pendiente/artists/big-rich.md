## songs

## curiosities
**Mentorship of Cowboy Troy (2005)** : Big & Rich mentored and launched the career of Cowboy Troy, who was dubbed the "Hick Hop hype of 2005." Their "bonafides" were instrumental in Cowboy Troy's album debuting at number two on Billboard's country albums chart.
