# artist - Fela Kuti

## albums
**Compilation 1978-2016** : A new compilation spanning the years 1978 to 2016 is being released, available in both three-disc and seven-disc editions. It is noted that a truly comprehensive and good compilation of this "great mythical band" had not been available until now, making this a timely release.
