# artist - Crusty the Clown

## curiosities
**Simpsons Cameo (1992)** : Crusty the Clown cameoed alongside Sting on the imagined benefit single "We're Sending Our Love Down the Well" in "Radio Bart," a January 1992 episode of The Simpsons that parodied charity mega-singles. His "yowling counterpoint vocals" were highlighted as a "savage touch" in the well-executed parody.
