**La Beyesa (2025)** : This album is a symphonic journey that incorporates Afro-Colombian rhythms, recorded with the Medellín Philharmonic. It has been noted to sound orchestrally similar to Rosalía's work, but only on paper, and is considered quite epic in scope.
