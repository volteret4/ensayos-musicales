## curiosities
**Genre-defining Artist** : T.I. was one of the artists who defined the trap genre in Atlanta, with lyrics that chronicled street dealings and daily life, rather than focusing on moral or political lessons. "Bieneros como T.A., John G.C., o Guchimane, marcaron al género con letras que no buscaban de elecciones morales ni políticas, sino que eran crónicas del trapicheo y el día a día en la calle." ← La salvaje historia del TRAP ESPAÑOL (2013-2026) | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hBhiU5jj6s
**Attested to Andre 3000's Source Awards Speech** : Atlanta's own T.I., in a VH1 documentary clip, attested to how vital Andre 3000's "The South Got Something To Say" speech at the 1995 Source Awards was for Southern rap. "including Killer Mike, Ludacris, and in this clip from a VH1 documentary, Atlanta's own T.I." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Shake It Like a Polaroid Picture Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Dominance of 2008 Radio** : T.I. was a prominent artist dominating radio in 2008, a period noted for its Southern rap influence, which contrasted with the sound of Lady Gaga's "Just Dance." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Gaga is Born Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
