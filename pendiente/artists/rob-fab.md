# artist - Rob & Fab

## members

## albums
**Rob and Fab (1993)** : After moving to Los Angeles, Rob Pilatus and Fabrice Morvan recorded this album, with many songs co-written by Morvan. It was released in 1993 only in America on the very small Joss Entertainment label and reportedly sold only 2000 copies, performing even worse than The Real Milli Vanilli's album. ## curiosities
**Attempt to Prove Singing Ability** : Following the Millie Vanilley scandal, Rob Pilatus and Fabrice Morvan formed "Rob and Fab" with the explicit goal of proving they could genuinely sing. They moved to Los Angeles and recorded an album. **Commercial Failure** : Despite their efforts, the "Rob and Fab" album was a commercial failure, reportedly selling only 2000 copies in total, which was an even poorer performance than The Real Milli Vanilli's album. This outcome supported Frank Farian's earlier assessment that the combination of his production, Rob and Fab's stage presence, and studio musicians' chops was essential for success.
