## curiosities
**Early Career and Genre Foundation** : Daddy Yankee was involved in the "Underground" scene, the precursor to reggaeton, from its very beginnings, making recordings as early as 1991 when he was approximately 13 years old. He participated in some of the most seminal pre-reggaeton recordings with DJ Play, demonstrating a rare longevity where an artist helped found a genre and remains at its forefront. "you have Daddy Yankee there from 1991, making recordings when he was I think 13 years old. And he's on some of the first, the most seminal reggaeton or pre-reggaeton recordings with DJ play." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Hits Don’t Lie | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Sustained Relevance** : Daddy Yankee has maintained his prominence in the reggaeton genre for decades, routinely topping the Latin charts even in the mid-2010s. His enduring success is exemplified by Luis Fonsi reaching out to him for the song "Despacito" in 2016-2017, highlighting his continuous influence and status as a top artist. "the fact that it's 20, this is a good transition to our next topic, it's 2016-17 and he's still enough on top of the game that this is the guy that Luis Fonsey reaches out to for Despacito." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Hits Don’t Lie | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
