## curiosities
**Popularity in the 1980s** : Alaska y Dinarama became one of the most popular bands of the 1980s in Spain. Their combination of talent and their established individual reputations contributed to their widespread success. **Evolution into Alaska y Dinarama** : Dinarama later saw Alaska and Nacho Canut join its ranks, leading to the band being renamed Alaska y Dinarama. This new configuration became one of the most popular bands of the 1980s. "Al poco tiempo se les unirán a Alaska y Nacho Canut, pasando a llamarse a Alaska y Dinarama, quienes se convertirán en una de las bandas más populares de la década de los 80." ← La España Siniestra ｜ La edad de oro del Postpunk y el Dark wave | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VN_buvQC69c
**Formation by Carlos Berlanga (Undated)** : Dinarama was formed by Carlos Berlanga after his departure from Alaska y los Pegamoides, prompted by his disenchantment with the band's darker musical direction. He sought a new creative outlet with this project.
