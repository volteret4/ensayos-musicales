# artist - Sugar Babes

## songs
**Get Sexy (2009)** : A song co-written by Bruno Mars and Philip Lawrence for the British girl group Sugar Babes, which reached number two in the UK in 2009. The track was explicitly noted as an "unabashed interpolation" of Right Said Fred's 1991 hit, "I'm Too Sexy," leveraging its familiarity for pop success.
