## curiosities
**The Social Network (2010) - Best Original Score Oscar** : In 2010, the film *The Social Network* won the Oscar for Best Original Score, composed by Tren, Rez, Noriáticos, Ros (Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross). Their work was instrumental in popularizing the use of ambient and textural music in modern dramas and thrillers, expanding the application of electronic music beyond its traditional use in science fiction. "En 2010, la red social ganó el dos cara mejor banda sonora, obra de tren, rez, noriáticos, ros y puso de modo al uso de la música ambiental y de texturas en los dramas y cilinders modernos." ← Las Bandas Sonoras Más Atípicas y Revolucionarias del Cine | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fiSrdUP3YXY
