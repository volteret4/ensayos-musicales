## songs
**Waiting for a Star to Fall (1988)** : This song, written by George Merrill and Shannon Rubikam (who recorded as Boy Meets Girl), was initially offered to Whitney Houston but rejected by Arista as "not on brand" due to its overly poppy sound. Issued a year later as a single for Boy Meets Girl, it became a top five pop hit, riding on the success of their earlier compositions for Houston.
