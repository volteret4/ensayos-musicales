# artist - Native Tongues

## curiosities
**Loose Collective of Hip-Hop Crews** : The Native Tongues was a loose collective of hip-hop groups, primarily revolving around three New York crews: De La Soul, the Jungle Brothers, and A Tribe Called Quest.
