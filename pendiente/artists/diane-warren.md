# artist - Diane Warren

## songs

## curiosities
**Superstar American Songwriter** : Diane Warren is described as a "superstar American songwriter" who had already generated multiple chart-topping hits by the late 1980s. She was on a hot streak, having scored number ones for "rebooted 60s band Starship" and "rebooted 70s hitmakers Chicago" in the two years prior to Millie Vanille's success. "Warren was herself on a hot streak, having scored number one's just in the last two years for rebooted 60s band Starship. **Prolific Songwriter** : Diane Warren is described as a "journeywoman songwriter" renowned for her uncanny ability to transform "hallmark sentiments into songs," consistently authoring numerous hit records throughout her career. **Highly Successful Year (1989)** : The year 1989 ranked as one of Warren's most successful, with her late 1988 hit "Look Away" with Chicago becoming Billboard's number one song of 1989. In September 1989, she had two songs in the Top 3 of the Hot 100: Millie Vanilley's "Girl I'm Gonna Miss You" at number one and Cher's "If I Could Turn Back Time" at number three. **Biggest Chart Coup (November 1989)** : Diane Warren achieved her biggest chart coup in November 1989 by having two consecutive number one hits on the Hot 100. First, Bad English's power ballad "When I See You Smile" held the top spot for two weeks, followed immediately by Millie Vanilley's "Blame It On The Rain" for another two weeks, totaling four straight weeks with a Warren-penned song at number one.
