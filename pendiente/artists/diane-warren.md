# artist - Diane Warren

## songs
**I Don't Want to Miss a Thing (1998)** : Diane Warren penned this "monster ballad" for Aerosmith, which debuted at number one in late 1998. The song was featured in the summer blockbuster movie "Armageddon" and represented a major chart success for both the band and the esteemed songwriter.
