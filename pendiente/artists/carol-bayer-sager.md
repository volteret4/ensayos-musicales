# artist - Carol Bayer Sager

## curiosities
**Initiating Warwick-Bacharach Reunion (1985)** : Carol Bayer Sager played a key role in reuniting Dionne Warwick and Burt Bacharach, who had not recorded together since 1971, by inviting them to contribute to Warwick's album around 1985. She also proposed that their song "That's What Friends Are For" become a benefit for AIDS research. **Proposing AIDS Benefit (1985)** : Carol Bayer Sager proposed that Dionne Warwick and Stevie Wonder's recording of "That's What Friends Are For" serve as a benefit for actress Elizabeth Taylor's charity Amphar, an early advocate for AIDS research. This initiative transformed the song into a major charity single. **"On My Own" Collaboration Process (1986)** : As a co-writer and producer of "On My Own" with Burt Bacharach, Carol Bayer Sager felt the song needed a male vocalist after Patti LaBelle's solo recording. This led to the successful remote collaboration with Michael McDonald.
