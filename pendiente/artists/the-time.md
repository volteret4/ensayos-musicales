**Evolution and Prince's Mentorship (Early 1980s)** : The band evolved from the 70s Minneapolis funk band Flight Time in the early 1980s. Under the guidance of Prince, "King of the Minneapolis Music Scene," The Time was refashioned into his side project and protégés, producing a string of cheeky, funky R&B hits. "By the start of the 80s, Flight Time evolved into the time. **Prominent Role in "Purple Rain" (1984)** : The Time was featured prominently in Prince's smash 1984 movie "Purple Rain," which significantly boosted their profile. However, by the time of the movie's release, key members Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis had already been fired by Prince.
