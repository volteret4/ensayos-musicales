## curiosities
**Rejection of "...Baby One More Time"** : Max Martin's demo for "...Baby One More Time" was offered to Deborah Cox, a rising R&B hitmaker and Clive Davis's latest protégé in the mold of Whitney Houston, but the track "wasn't right for her even."
