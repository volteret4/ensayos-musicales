# artist - Zelma Davis

## member of
- C+C Music Factory

## songs
**Gonna Make You Sweat (Everybody Dance Now) (1990)** : Zelma Davis, a model and singer, was featured in the music video for C+C Music Factory's "Gonna Make You Sweat (Everybody Dance Now)," lip-syncing the iconic melodic hooks, including "everybody dance now." These vocals were actually sung by Martha Wash, who was uncredited. **Here We Go (1991)** : Zelma Davis was credited on this follow-up single by C+C Music Factory, and she did vocalize on the track. This contrasts with her uncredited lip-sync performance on the previous hit.
