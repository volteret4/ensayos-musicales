## songs
**Tossin' and Turnin' (1961)** : This uptempo song took the 1961 Billboard Song of the Summer title. Its title is considered a fitting description for the experience of trying to fall asleep on a hot night, particularly when aroused. **Tossin' and Turnin' (1961)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1961, described as the "In Da Club of its era." In 1961, when "straight up R&B was often segregated on Top 40 radio," Bobby Lewis's hit proved undeniable, holding the top of the Hot 100 for seven weeks. It defeated early 60s classics like Patsy Cline's "I Fall to Pieces" and Roy Orbison's "Crying."
