**Early Beastie Boys Support** : The Dead Kennedies were among the "punk luminaries" that the Beastie Boys supported at New York clubs such as Maxis Kansas City and CBGBs in 1982. This was during the Beastie Boys' formative years as a hardcore punk trio.
