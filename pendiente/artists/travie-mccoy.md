# artist - Travie McCoy

## songs
**Billionaire (2010)** : Travie McCoy's solo single, co-written by The Smeezingtons, prominently featuring Bruno Mars singing the chorus hook. The song achieved a peak position of number four in 2010, contributing to Mars's burgeoning reputation as a versatile collaborator and hook singer.
