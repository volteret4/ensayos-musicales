**Musical Evolution** : This Merseyside quartet, formed around the same time as Lush, began with a Shoegaze-y dream pop style in the early 90s. In 1995, they shifted to a "punchier take on 60s rock," which allowed their music to achieve chart success, sending a single into the top 10 and their album "Wake Up" to number 1, unlike their earlier work.
