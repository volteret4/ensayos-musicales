# artist - Charles Shaw

## member of

## curiosities
**Session Vocalist for Frank Farian** : Charles Shaw was an African-American former military man stationed in Germany who worked as a session vocalist. Frank Farian hired him to add a rap to the 1987 remix of Boney M's "Dreadlock Holiday," and he later performed the rapid-fire rap vocals on Millie Vanille's "Girl You Know It's True," though he was uncredited and lip-dubbed by Fabrice Morvan. **Rap Vocalist for Millie Vanille's "Girl You Know It's True"** : Charles Shaw provided the "rapid-fire rap" vocals for Millie Vanille's hit "Girl You Know It's True." Despite being the actual vocalist, he was not deemed "hip enough" by Frank Farian to be the public face of the project and was lip-dubbed by Fabrice Morvan in the video. "Fab takes the harder vocal part, lip-dubbing Charles Shaw's rapid-fire rap." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Studio Rapper for Millie Vanilley** : Charles Shaw was the studio rapper for Millie Vanilley and, at various times between 1989 and 1990, threatened to expose the vocal deception. He even gave an interview claiming to be the actual vocalist, though he later rescinded his comments under pressure from Frank Farian.
