## songs
**Self Destruction (1989)** : This All-Star single was released in early 1989 by KRS-One's collective, featuring around a dozen East Coast rappers, including Heavy D and Flavor Flav. It was the first All-Star single from the "Stop the Violence Movement," founded by KRS-One in 1987 after a young fan was killed at a show headlined by his crew, Boogie Down Productions, and Public Enemy. The song reached number 30 on Billboard's Hot Black Singles chart and was certified gold.
