## songs
**Dolce Vita (1983)** : This song was a number one hit in half a dozen European countries in early 1983 and reached the top five in the UK. It blended the Italo disco style with English pop vocals, achieving a combination of "romantic lushness and synth pop angularity." "Ryan Paris's Dolce Vita.
