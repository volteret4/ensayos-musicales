## songs
**Jump (1992)** : A chart-topping rap single from the early 1990s, "Jump" was constructed around a prominent sample derived from an earlier hit by The Jackson Five. This track illustrated the prevalent practice of integrating well-known melodic or rhythmic elements from previous successes into new rap compositions. ## curiosities
**Backwards Clothing and Discovery (Early 90s)** : Kris Kross was a duo of 13-year-old rappers who famously wore their clothing backwards. They were discovered in an Atlanta mall by producer Jermaine Dupri, who signed them.
