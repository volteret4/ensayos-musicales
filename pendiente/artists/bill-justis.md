# artist - Bill Justis

## songs
**Raunchy (1957)** : This instrumental achieved bestseller status at number two in 1957, with Bill Justis playing a key role in pioneering the distinctive "Twangy guitar sound." It became widely recognized as a rite of passage for young rock fans learning to play guitar, famously influencing George Harrison during his audition for The Beatles.
