## songs
**You've Got to Hide Your Love Away (1965)** : This English folk group took The Beatles' "You've Got to Hide Your Love Away" to number 10 on the charts in 1965. It is noted as one of the Beatles' less prominent tracks ("closer to Deep Cuts") that found success as a cover by another act.
