# artist - Bill Nighy

## songs
**Christmas Is All Around (2003)** : As a character in the film *Love Actually*, Bill Nighy performs a "terrible" (by his own admission) cover of "Love Is All Around," rebranded as "Christmas Is All Around." This fictional endeavor is a "naked bid to score a Christmas number one" in the UK, highlighting a central plot point of the film.
