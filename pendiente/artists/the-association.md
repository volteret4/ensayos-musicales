## curiosities
**Chart Dominance in Summer of Love** : The Association's song "Windy" spent four weeks atop the Hot 100 in July 1967, effectively dominating the Summer of Love charts. This achievement surpassed the chart performance of many songs from that period that are now more widely remembered. "Their perky cheeky hit, not their first number one by the way, dominated the Summer of Love, over songs that are far better remembered from that period." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Singing Nuns and Green Tambourines Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
