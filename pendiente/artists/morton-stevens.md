# artist - Morton Stevens

## songs
**Theme from Hawaii Five-O (1968)** : Morton Stevens composed this theme for the CBS show "Hawaii Five-O." It was designed to be catchy and impactful within the show's credits, bursting from TV speakers. The established surf rock group The Ventures later adapted and extended this theme, turning it into a legitimate pop hit.
