# artist - Bell Biv DeVoe

## genres
- Slamming Hip-Hop Club

## curiosities
**Influence on Bruno Mars** : Bruno Mars's hit "Finesse" directly emulated the "slamming hip-hop club sound" characteristic of Bell Biv DeVoe, a prominent spin-off group from New Edition. This stylistic borrowing contributed to the "24K Magic" album's overarching tribute to 80s R&B.
