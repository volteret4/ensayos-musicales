## albums
**All Or Nothing (1988)** : This album was released in November 1988 on the German label Hansa, with UK distribution handled by the acid house label Cool Tempo. It was filled with tracks that emulated the sound of the duo's smash first single, "Girl You Know It's True," and performed less successfully in Europe than its American counterpart. "A full Millie Vanille album titled All Or Nothing was released in November on the German label Hansa." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Girl You Know It's True (1989)** : This American version of Millie Vanille's debut album was titled after its lead single and debuted on the charts in late March 1989. Within a month, it soared into the top 20 and achieved gold status, later going platinum. The album eventually reached number one on the album chart the same week "Girl, I'm Gonna Miss You" topped the Hot 100, outperforming the European "All or Nothing" album in Europe. Arista Records, under Clive Davis, insisted on swapping out some tracks for this release, including covers of The Isley Brothers' "It's Your Thing" and a song by Diane Warren. "The American version of the Millie Vanille album would be titled after its lead single Girl You Know It's True." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## songs
**Baby Don't Forget My Number (1989)** : This second single from Millie Vanille was a "near carbon copy" of "Girl You Know It's True," featuring the exact same Ashley's Roach clip beat on the chorus and the same rap verses/sung chorus formula. It quickly entered the top 10 on both the R&B and pop charts within two months, leading to the "Girl You Know It's True" album going platinum. For the week ending July 1st, 1989, it became Millie Vanille's first chart-topper in America on the Hot 100. "Baby, don't forget my number flew into the top 10 on both the R&B and pop charts in just two months." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Blame It On The Rain (1989)** : This single reached number one on the Hot 100 on the week ending November 25, 1989. It marked the third straight chart-topper for Millie Vanille, the fourth of their five top-five hits, and affirmed their status as one of the top pop acts of the year. The song was also the second consecutive number one for its celebrated, Oscar-nominated songwriter, Diane Warren. "The week ending November 25, 1989, when Blame It On The Rain by Millie Vanille reached number one on the Hot 100." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Girl, I'm Gonna Miss You (1989)** : A ballad written by Frank Farian's team, this song was issued as a single by the American label Arista as a strategic "sonic changeup" after two uptempo hits. It was the Hot 100's highest new entry in the first week of August 1989 and quickly rose, becoming Millie Vanille's second straight number one and third straight top two hit by mid-September. The accompanying black-and-white video featured Rob and Fab brooding in muscle t-shirts. "By mid-September, Millie Vanille had its second straight number one and third straight top two hit." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Girl You Know It's True (1989)** : Originally written by the Baltimore DJ crew Newmarks in 1986, Frank Farian transformed this minor club hit into a smash by adding the "Ashley's Roach clip" beat and session vocalists Charles Shaw, John Davis, and Brad Howe. Millie Vanille's version broke in Europe first in 1988, reaching number one in Germany for six weeks and number three in the UK. The Arista single debuted on the Hot 100 in January 1989, peaking at number two in early April, blocked by The Bangles and Roxette. It notably appeared on Billboard's first Hot Rap Singles chart in March 1989 and peaked at number three on the R&B chart, establishing itself as a "legit crossover smash." **It's Your Thing (1969)** : An R&B classic by The Isley Brothers from 1969, this song was recorded by Millie Vanille as a cover at the insistence of Clive Davis for the American version of their album, "Girl You Know It's True." Davis believed Americans would recognize it well. "Davis proposed that Millie Vanille record an R&B classic Americans would know well. They were chosen for their good looks, athleticism, and energetic performance style, rather than their limited musical skill.
