## curiosities
**Real Singer for Millie Vanilley** : Brad Howe was the uncredited main verse vocalist for Millie Vanilley's "Blame It On The Rain" and later appeared on camera for the first time in the video for The Real Milli Vanilli's "Keep On Running."
