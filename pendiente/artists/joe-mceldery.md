# artist - Joe McEldery

## songs
**The Climb (2009)** : "The Climb" was the inspirational track released by X-Factor winner Joe McEldery in 2009. It was expected to become the UK Christmas number one, continuing Simon Cowell's streak, but was famously thwarted by Rage Against the Machine's "Killing In The Name" due to a public campaign.
