## albums
**Debut CD** : Atlantic Records found significant success with Matchbox 20, selling 12 million copies of their debut CD. The band's "rustic jangle" sound was noted for basically picking up where Hootie & the Blowfish left off, although Matchbox 20 did not possess Darius Rucker's distinctive signature voice. **Yourself or Someone Like You (1996)** : This debut album became a "stealth blockbuster" of the late 90s, selling a "gobsmacking 12 million copies" despite never topping the album chart. It quietly remained on the chart for over two years, spinning off multiple hits. ## curiosities
**"Slick New Post-Grunge" Band** : Matchbox 20 was described as a "slick new post-grunge band" from Orlando, Florida, blending "old-school 70s-style arena rock with 90s grunge iris sonics." They were seen as the "tip of a very glossy iceberg" of similar bands flooding the charts. "Even more Dizzying was what happened to a slick new post-grunge band from Orlando, Florida, who called themselves Matchbox 20." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > All Apologies Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Dominance of US Pop (1997)** : By 1997, the sound of US pop was dominated by "frilly Altpop" acts like Matchbox 20, which minimized Britpop's chances of breaking through.
