# artist - Paul Simon and Art Garfunkel

## songs
**Punky's Dilemma (1969)** : After the duo dominated the charts in 1968, Barbara Streisand attempted to cover their song "Punky's Dilemma" for her 1969 album "What About Today." However, her version of the song did not chart.
