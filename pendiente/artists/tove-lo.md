## curiosities
**Imitator of Lady Gaga** : Tove Lo is listed as one of the artists whose electropop and Euro dance beats from the early 2010s showed emulation of Lady Gaga's sound during her imperial phase. "picture all of the electropop and Euro dance beats from that period, from iconopop to Kesha, Charlie XCX to Toe Vlo." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Material Girl in an Imperial World Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f03154465cd600c5379
**Part of Female Dominated Hot 100 (2014)** : Tove Lo was among the artists, many involved in collaborations, who joined Meghan Trainor in the "winner's circle" during a period in fall 2014 when women occupied the entire top five of the Hot 100 for seven consecutive weeks. "Joining Trainor in the winner's circle were hits, many of them collaborations, from the likes of not only Iggy Azalea, but Rita Ora, Tove Lowe, Jesse J, Ariana Grande, and Nicki Minaj." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Rolling in God’s Royal Uptown Road Edition. | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
