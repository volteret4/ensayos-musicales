# artist - Clarence Clemens

## instruments
- Saxophone

## curiosities
**Collaboration on "Edge of Glory"** : Legendary saxophonist Clarence Clemens, a member of Bruce Springsteen's E Street Band, collaborated with Lady Gaga on her song "Edge of Glory" (2011), providing an "80s style saxola." He filmed the video for the song with Gaga in June 2011, just six days before his death at age 69.
