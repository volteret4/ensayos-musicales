# artist - Bobcat Goldthwait

## curiosities
**Bono Parody at MTV VMAs (1987)** : At the 1987 MTV Video Music Awards, comedian Bobcat Goldthwait parodied Bono by appearing in a leather vest similar to Bono's video attire and performing a vocal impression, taking "the piss out of Bono."
