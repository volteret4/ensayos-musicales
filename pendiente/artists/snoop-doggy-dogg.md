## curiosities
**Career Launch (1992)** : Snoop Doggy Dogg's rapping career was launched through his appearance on the soundtrack to 1992's "Deep Cover," an album that also featured reggae fusion artists like Shabba Ranks and showcased the modern blend of dancehall and gangsta rap.
