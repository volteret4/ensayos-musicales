## songs
**Only You (1983)** : This acapella cover of Yaz's synth-pop classic became the UK Christmas number one in 1983. The band members, being committed socialists, were reportedly "mortified" that then-Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher publicly expressed her affection for their version of the song. "The flying pickets were committed socialists and thus mortified that Thatcher attested to her love of their acapella cover of Yaz's synth pop classic. **Only You (1983)** : This acapella, hymn-like cover of the synth-pop classic by Yazoo (known as Yaz in America) was Britain's improbable Christmas number one of 1983. The song was later named by British Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher as her favorite single of the 1980s, which was an embarrassment to the socialist Flying Pickets, who loathed Thatcher. ## curiosities
**Political Identity and Chart Success** : The Flying Pickets were a formerly hitless acapella group with socialist leanings. Their unexpected Christmas number one in 1983 with "Only You" became ironically linked to Conservative Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher, who declared it her favorite single of the 1980s, much to the embarrassment of the band who "loathed Thatcher."
