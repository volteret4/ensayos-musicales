# artist - Kiki Dee

## songs
**Don't Go Breaking My Heart (1976)** : Kiki Dee performed this cheeky male-female duet with Elton John, which topped the charts in the summer of 1976. This single was particularly notable for being Elton John's first-ever British number one hit. ## curiosities
**Duet with Elton John (pre-1991)** : Elton John's credited number one hit with Kiki Dee, occurring nearly 16 years prior to 1991, was his last chart-topper before his duet with George Michael on "Don't Let The Sun Go Down On Me."
