# artist - Jason Derulo

## songs
**Savage Love (Laxed** : Jason Derulo, described as a "meme-jacking pop star," remixed Josh 685's viral TikTok instrumental "Laxed – Siren Beat" by turning its hook into his song "Savage Love." This initial remix made the track a hit. "first in a remix by meme-jacking pop star Jason D'Rulo, who turned Josh's hook into the song Savage Love." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Invented the Remix Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Whatcha Say (2009)** : This 2009 smash hit prominently sampled Imogen Heap's "Hide and Seek," becoming a Hot 100 number one hit, a success that likely would not have occurred without *The O.C.*'s prior popularization of the original song.
