## songs
**How Do You Do It (1963)** : Recorded as their debut single after The Beatles rejected it, this song holds a special distinction for reaching number one on the United Kingdom's "Record Retailer" chart (the predecessor to the UK's modern official charts company chart) before any song by The Beatles did, technically making them the first Liverpool group with a UK number one on that specific chart. ## curiosities
**Liverpool Competition** : Jerry and the Pace Makers were considered one of The Searchers' only real competitors among Liverpool groups. "Their only real competition being the Beatles and Jerry and the Pace Makers." ← Episode 113： ＂Needles and Pins＂ by The Searchers | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=izC9ZBI0UHM
**Peaked Career (1963)** : At the time The Searchers were achieving their first number one hit, Jerry and the Pace Makers' career had already peaked, though this was not known at the time. "Nobody could have known it at the time, the Pace Makers career had already peaked at this point." ← Episode 113： ＂Needles and Pins＂ by The Searchers | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=izC9ZBI0UHM
**Mercybeat Stars** : Jerry and the Pacemakers were a prominent band of the new Mercybeat sound, having achieved three number-one records in the UK during the year of Tharp's UK performances. Despite their secular sound, they showed a direct connection to gospel through Tharp's influence. "Jerry and the Pacemakers, one of the biggest bands of the new Mercybeat sound, who'd had three number one records that year in the UK." ← Episode 5： ＂This Train＂ by Sister Rosetta Tharpe | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=II-hDnwUgT8
**First Liverpool UK #1 (Record Retailer)** : Jerry and the Pacemakers technically became the first Liverpool group to have a UK number one on the "Record Retailer" chart with "How Do You Do It," which reached the top spot before any Beatles song on that specific chart. "Making Jerry and the pacemakers technically the first Liverpool group to have a UK number one." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The British Are Charting Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Shared Management and Production** : Jerry and the Pacemakers were signed with the same manager, Brian Epstein, and producer, George Martin, as The Beatles.
