**Mercybeat Stars** : Jerry and the Pacemakers were a prominent band of the new Mercybeat sound, having achieved three number-one records in the UK during the year of Tharp's UK performances. Despite their secular sound, they showed a direct connection to gospel through Tharp's influence.
