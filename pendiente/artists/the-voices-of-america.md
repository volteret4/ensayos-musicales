# artist - The Voices of America

## songs
**Hands Across America (1986)** : This titular single was part of a grand-scale hunger and homelessness effort organized by Ken Kragen, the manager who helped create USA for Africa. The song itself was described as "utterly dreadful" and "Erzatz John Melon Camp, HAC Americana." It was released on a wave of hype surrounding a nationwide human chain event, "Hands Across America," which aimed to link people across the country but suffered from large, humanless gaps in the West. The single managed to peak at number 65 on the Hot 100. "The titular single is utterly dreadful.
