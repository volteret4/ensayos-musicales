## genres

## songs
**I Believe (2004)** : Recording under her mononym, Fantasia's "gospel-inflected" coronation song, "I Believe," debuted at number one in 2004. This occurred after her victory in "American Idol's" third season and followed the pattern of Idol finalists achieving instant number one debuts with massive first-week sales despite little radio airplay. ## curiosities
**Debut Single Interrupts Usher's Chart Run (2004)** : As the winner of American Idol in 2004, Fantasia's debut single briefly interrupted Usher's streak of three back-to-back number one hits. This one-week interruption prevented Usher from tying The Beatles' long-standing record for three consecutive chart-topping singles. "If his run hadn't been interrupted for just one week by the debut single from that year's American Idol winner, Fantasia, he'd have tied the Beatles record." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The Fab Four Sweep Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
