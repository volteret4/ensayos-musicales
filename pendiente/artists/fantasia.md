## songs
**I Believe (2004)** : Recording under her mononym, Fantasia's "gospel-inflected" coronation song, "I Believe," debuted at number one in 2004. This occurred after her victory in "American Idol's" third season and followed the pattern of Idol finalists achieving instant number one debuts with massive first-week sales despite little radio airplay.
