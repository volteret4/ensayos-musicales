## songs
**Thrift Shop (2013)** : This song featuring Wanz was the Billboard year-end number one for 2013, topping the Hot 100 for six weeks. It is debated whether it's a novelty record or a witty satire of hip hop's label-obsessed culture. As "white rappers," their status carried "its own baggage." It accumulated enough chart data early in 2013 to win the year-end title, despite Robin Thicke's "Blurred Lines" spending twice as many weeks at number one.
