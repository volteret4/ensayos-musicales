# artist - John Michael Montgomery

## genres
- Country

## albums
**Kickin' It Up (1994)** : This was the singer's second album, which reached number one on the Billboard 200. It featured "I Swear," which became the biggest country hit of 1994. ## songs
**I Swear (1994)** : This song was the biggest country hit of 1994. While it topped the country charts, it also became a pop hit, but not in Montgomery's version. The boy band All-4-One covered it and took their version to the top of the Hot 100, illustrating the concept of "parallel platinum worlds" where country and pop could succeed in their own arenas without direct crossover of the same recording.
