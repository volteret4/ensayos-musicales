# artist - Roy Wood

## member of
- Electric Light Orchestra
- Wizzard

## curiosities
**Leadership of Wizzard** : Roy Wood was the leader and founder of Wizzard, a glam rock band that was a major chart competitor in the UK in 1973. He was also an early member and co-founder of the Electric Light Orchestra. His band Wizzard's Christmas single, "I Wish It Could Be Christmas Every Day," was released as part of a famous head-to-head battle for the 1973 UK Christmas No. 1, which they ultimately lost to Slade. **Personal Relationship with Aisha Hague** : In December 1973, Roy Wood was in a relationship with Aisha Hague, who hosted the television program "Lift Off with Aisha." Both Wizzard and Slade were booked to appear on this show, which is how the bands became aware of their competing Christmas singles.
