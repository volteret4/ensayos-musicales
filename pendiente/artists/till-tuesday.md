## albums
**Everything's Different Now (1988)** : The third and final album for Till Tuesday, it represented a turning point for Amy Mann, with much of the album reflecting on her relationship with Jules Sheer. Despite critical acclaim for Mann's songwriting growth, Epic Records did not heavily promote it, leading to a dismal peak at number 124 on the album chart and a failure to go gold. **Voices Carry (1985)** : The debut album, which cracked the top 20, peaking at number 18, and went gold. It established their ethereal, moody pop sound and earned a VMA for its captivating music video. **Welcome Home (1986)** : The band's second album, recorded throughout 1986, continued the ethereal moody pop sound of their debut. Its lead single, "What About Love," returned them to the top 40. ## songs

It was a minor hit, reaching number 95 on the Hot 100, and made the top 40 on both the Adult Contemporary Chart and Billboard's new Modern Rock Chart.
