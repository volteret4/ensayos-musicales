**Pony (1996)** : Static Major co-produced this "weird lurching belching club jam" for Ginuwine alongside Timbaland, while they were both working in Devanté Swing's Debasement. The song became a significant hit, reaching number 6 on the Hot 100 and number 1 on the R&B chart.
