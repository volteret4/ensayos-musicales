# artist - Leona Williams

## songs
**The Bull and The Beaver** : This duet, performed with Merle Haggard, described two truckers connecting via their CB radio handles. It was released amidst a wave of CB-themed songs that capitalized on the popularity sparked by CW McCall's "Convoy."
