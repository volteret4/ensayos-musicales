## albums
**U2 (1991)** : This 1991 single by the experimental music group Negative Land sampled Casey Kasem's "Dead Dog Tape" rant and mixed it with an instrumental version of U2's 1987 hit "I Still Haven't Found What I'm Looking For." Negative Land was subsequently sued by U2 (not Kasem's production company), leading to the CD being pulled from the market, although many bootleg copies circulated.
