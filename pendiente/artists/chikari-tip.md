# artist - Chikari Tip

## songs
**Son of my father (1972)** : A glam pop song written by Giorgio Moroder on synthesizer, which became a UK number one hit when covered by this British rock band. Their version famously sported a "fat-bottomed moge synthesizer" and even briefly appeared on the bottom rungs of the American Hot 100 in the winter of 1972.
