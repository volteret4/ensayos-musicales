## songs
**Good Vibrations (1991)** : This song was a number 1 smash hit, featuring vocals by Lolita Holloway. Its success was immediately followed by another top 10 hit, securing the group's status beyond a one-hit wonder. **Good Vibrations (1991)** : This 1991 pop-rap hit featured Loleatta Holloway, who was given a full "above-the-line" featured credit for a sample from her 1980 club smash "Love Sensation" used for the song's chorus. This made Holloway the first act to receive full artist credit for a sample on a number one hit and the first chart-topping hook singer, after she successfully pressured the label. **Wildside (1991)** : This follow-up hit reached number 10 in 1991 and notably sampled Lou Reed's "Walk on the Wild Side." The song peaked higher than Lou Reed's original and contributed to Marky Mark's escape from one-hit wonder status. ## curiosities
**Lou Reed Sample in "Wildside"** : Marky Mark Wahlberg's 1991 hit "Wildside" notably sampled Lou Reed's "Walk on the Wild Side." Wahlberg's version not only peaked higher on the charts but also helped him avoid the one-hit wonder label, a status Reed himself retained.
