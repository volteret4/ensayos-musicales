## albums
**New Beginning (1996)** : This album was powered by the pop smash "Give Me One Reason" in the summer of 1996, selling an immediate three million copies. The success of the single and album marked a significant return for Chapman after not attempting to match her debut's unexpected chart performance for several years. **Tracy Chapman (1988)** : Released in 1988, Tracy Chapman's self-titled debut album topped the album chart in August of that year, the same week her single "Fast Car" reached its peak. According to Billboard, it was the first folk album to top the chart since Peter, Paul and Mary in 1963, and it eventually achieved sex-touple platinum certification.
