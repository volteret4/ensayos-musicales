## curiosities
**Best New Artist Win (1989)** : Tracy Chapman was awarded the Best New Artist prize at the 1989 Grammys, which was seen as a "wise" decision by the Academy. Despite this, her enduring song "Fast Car" lost out in the Record and Song of the Year categories to Bobby McFerrin's "Don't Worry Be Happy." "But in the two big song categories, recording Academy voters went instead with Ocapella Master, Bobby McFerren's Fluke No. 1 hit Don't Worry Be Happy." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > And the Grammy Goes to… Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef0154465cd600c4e3a
**First TV Performance in a Decade (2024)** : Tracy Chapman made her first television appearance in nearly a decade at the 2024 Grammy Awards, where she teamed up with country star Luke Combs to perform a duet of her classic single "Fast Car."
