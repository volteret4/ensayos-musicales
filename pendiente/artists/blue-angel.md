## curiosities
**Formation and Sound** : Blue Angel formed in 1978 when saxophonist John Tury met Cynthia Ann Stephanie Lauper at a Greenwich Village nightclub. The band cultivated a new wave retro rockabilly sound, incorporating kitsch and Lauper's vintage clothes, influenced by New York rock. While not punk, they primarily played uptown clubs and Great Guildersleeves rather than the legendary CBGB. "Blue Angel was finally the band to win Lauper a recording contract at the fairly advanced age of 26. She, John Tury, and the other band members settled on a kind of new wave retro rockability sound, which they showcased on the band's one and only self-titled album on Polydor Records in 1980." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be the One to Walk in the Sun, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Cyndi Lauper's Loyalty** : Cyndi Lauper demonstrated strong loyalty to John Tury and her Blue Angel bandmates by repeatedly declining offers to leave the group and pursue a solo career. She also resisted being typecast as a balladeer, even though her torch songs were the most commercially successful for the band. "Cindy turned down multiple offers to ditch the band and go solo. She was loyal to John and the Blue Angel bandmates, and she didn't want to be pigeonholed as a baladier." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be the One to Walk in the Sun, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Band's Dissolution** : The band experienced deteriorating relations with both Polydor Records and their manager. Ultimately, Lauper declared bankruptcy to extricate herself from her contract, which effectively led to the end of Blue Angel. "Relations soured with both Polydor Records and the band's manager, and Lauper ultimately declared bankruptcy to get out from under her contract to pay the bills, Cindy wound up working at screaming memes, a vintage clothing store famed for its ahead of the curve looks and informative to Lauper's own look." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be the One to Walk in the Sun, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
