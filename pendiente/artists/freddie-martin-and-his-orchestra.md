# artist - Freddie Martin and His Orchestra

## songs
**To Each His Own (1946)** : Freddie Martin and His Orchestra's rendition of "To Each His Own" replaced The Eddy Howard Orchestra's version at number one on Billboard's pop charts in 1946, exemplifying the era's trend of multiple recordings of a single song achieving chart success.
