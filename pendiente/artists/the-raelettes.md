# artist - The Raelettes

## curiosities
**Girl Group Joining Ray Charles's Band** : The Raelettes were a girl group, primarily composed of former members of another girl band, who were added to Ray Charles's band to complement his musical arrangements. "And he added a girl group called the Ray Lets, who were mostly former members of a girl band." ← Episode 32： ＂I Got A Woman＂ by Ray Charles | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0oeI9f2x_fo
**Inspiration for The Primettes** : The Ray Lettes, known as Ray Charles's backing vocalists, served as the inspiration for Milton Jenkins to create a female backing group for The Primes, which would eventually lead to the formation of The Primettes.
