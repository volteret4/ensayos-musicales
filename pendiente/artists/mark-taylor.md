# artist - Mark Taylor

## curiosities
**Co-Writer of "Bailamos"** : Mark Taylor, an Englishman, co-wrote Enrique Iglesias's number one hit "Bailamos." His involvement in writing the song, which had a Spanish title but was primarily English, demonstrates the collaborative and sometimes inauthentic approach to creating Latin-flavored pop hits for Anglophone audiences in 1999.
