## curiosities
**Higher Love Remix (2019)** : The Norwegian DJ and record producer resurrected Whitney Houston's 1990 cover of Steve Winwood's "Higher Love" in 2019, expertly remixing it into a contemporary tropical house style. Billed as a duet between Kygo and Whitney Houston, this version became a global hit, topping the Dance Club Songs chart, reaching number 29 on the Mainstream Top 40, number 12 on the Adult Top 40, and appearing in the top 10 of the Adult Contemporary chart, with a brief appearance on the Hot 100 and charting in the top 20 in over two dozen countries.
