## songs
**Green Tambourine (1968)** : Described as a "toothless version of Psychedelia," this song became a number one hit in 1968. The "five-man Ohio band" behind it "never came close to the top 40 again," marking them as a fleeting blockbuster in the "hippie hits by trendhoppers" category.
