## songs
**Slow Hand (1981)** : Del Reeves was the first male artist to record a country version of "Slow Hand," adapting the lyrics by switching pronouns (e.g., "I want a man with a Slow Hand" to "you want a man with a Slow Hand") and adding a country twang, reaching number 53 on the Hot Country chart.
