## curiosities
**Transition from Keyboardist to Singer** : James Ingram, initially a keyboardist, was reluctant to sing until Quincy Jones "discovered" him and convinced him otherwise. Jones admired Ingram's "gritty plain spoken voice," and subsequently jump-started his career as a vocalist, establishing Ingram as his protégé. "Prior to The Dude, James Ingram worked mostly as a keyboardist. He was not convinced he should sing, but Q had a way of changing others destiny. He loved Ingram's gritty plain spoken voice." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Wanna Rock with Q. Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
