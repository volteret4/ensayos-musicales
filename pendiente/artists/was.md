# artist - Was

## curiosities
**Don Was's Pop Collective** : Was (Not Was) was a pop collective co-led by Don Was, who was also a successful producer. The group was just scoring its first ever top ten hit with "Walk the Dinosaur" around the same time Don Was was producing half of the B-52s' *Cosmic Thing* album and about to win a Grammy for his production work.
