# artist - Charles Fox

## songs
**Killing Me Softly With His Song** : Charles Fox was one of the songwriters who, along with Norman Gimbel, collaborated to create the song "Killing Me Softly With His Song," based on Laurie Lieberman's experience watching Don McLean perform.
