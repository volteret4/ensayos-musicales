## curiosities
**Nile Rogers' First Band** : This was Nile Rogers' first band, which he characterized as a "jazz blues rock fusion electrified band." He compared their sound to jazz-inflected acts of the era such as Blood, Sweat & Tears and Elephant's Memory. The band was "unfortunately never recorded."
