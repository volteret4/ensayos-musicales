## curiosities
**Bro Country Era Chart Topper** : Jake Owen was cited as an artist producing "testosterone-fueled hits" during the "Bro Country Era" of the early 2010s. His music featured "cookie-cutter lyrics about trucks, boozy lake parties, and pretty women in cutoff shorts," which became interchangeable with other artists of the genre. "Whether the chart toppers were by Jason Aldean or Jake Owen, or by the so-called King of the Bros. Luke Bryan.
