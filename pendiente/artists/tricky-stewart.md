# artist - Tricky Stewart

## songs
**Umbrella (2007)** : Tricky Stewart was part of the production trio, along with The-Dream and Kuk Harrell, who wrote "Umbrella," creating the track from a drum sample found in Apple's GarageBand software. ## curiosities
**Production Style** : Tricky Stewart is known for a distinctive production style that features "twinkling," "bass-heavy" elements, and "plunky piano" sounds, as exemplified in his collaboration with The-Dream and his work on Mariah Carey's "Touch My Body."
