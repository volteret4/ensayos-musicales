## curiosities
**Pure Mood Artist - Incomprehensible Lyrics** : Described as a "Celtic New Age Queen," Enya is considered a "pure mood" artist, drawing parallels to Kenny G. Although she performs vocally, many of her fans often do not fully comprehend her lyrics, focusing instead on the atmospheric and emotive qualities of her music. "Enya, like Kenny G, is what you might call a pure mood." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Insert Lyrics Here Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Pre-Race Relaxation Music for Jan Mardenborough** : Professional video gamer turned racer Jan Mardenborough incorporates Enya's music into his pre-race routine for relaxation. His preference for Enya, alongside Kenny G, highlights his use of music for its mood-enhancing properties, despite Enya being a vocalist. "The film also reveals that his other favorite pre-race music is Celtic New Age Queen, Enia, who does sing." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Insert Lyrics Here Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Highest Charting Pop Hit via Sample Credit** : Enya's 2004 featured credit on Mario Winans' "I Don't Wanna Know" for a prominent sample of her 1987 song "Boadicea" technically made it her highest-charting pop hit ever, despite her not singing a new note on the track. This marked a "remarkable continuation, even an inversion of the Loliyada Holloway featured sample credit." "And yet the hit is credited to Mario Winons featuring Enia and P Diddy, making it technically the multi-platinum new-age stars highest charting pop hit ever." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Feat. **Fugees Sample Controversy** : Enya's song "Boadicea" was used as the backbone of the Fugees' 1996 track "Ready or Not" without her authorization. She considered suing the Fugees for the unauthorized sample usage and eventually settled out of court.
