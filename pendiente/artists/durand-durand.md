**The Reflex (1984)** : The remix of "The Reflex" became the definitive version of the song for Durand Durand listeners, largely overshadowing the original LP version. This remix, which secured a number one hit for Durand, is still performed live by the band and includes the "Flet-Flet flex part" that Nile Rogers added in 1984. Described as an "act of commerce" and "the work of many cooks," it is also considered a "work of fun," analogous to art by Renoir or Banksy. "By and large, Durand Durand listeners don't think about the original LP version of the Reflex anymore.
