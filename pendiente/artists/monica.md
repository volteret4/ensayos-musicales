# artist - Monica

## genres
- R&B

## songs
**The Boy Is Mine (1998)** : This duet with Brandy was the winner of the 1998 Billboard Song of the Summer and was the most dominant Song of the Summer of the 90s, achieving 13 weeks at number one between June and August 1998. The song "maxed out on Sass" and, with production by Rodney Jerkins, "lilted and fluttered like a butterfly circling over a hammock." "With 13 big weeks at number one, all of them racked up between June and August of 1998, the most dominant song of the summer of the 90s was Brandy and Monica's The Boy Is Mine." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Song(s) of the Summer Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef50c30a1408dc292e4
**The Boy Is Mine (1998)** : This duet with Brandy commanded the summer of 1998 by spending 13 weeks at number one on the Hot 100. However, it ultimately "couldn't seal the deal at year end" and did not become the year's top song. **Everything to Me (2010)** : An R&B chart-topping hit for Monica, produced by Missy Elliott. This marked Monica's first number one on the R&B chart in seven years, highlighting Elliott's continued support for artists who had also emerged in the 90s. ## curiosities
**Peak of Teen R&B** : Monica is mentioned as part of the "peak of teen R&B" in 1998 on the music charts. "It wasn't just a good time in 1998 for R&B on the charts. It was the peak of teen R&B. Pink, Destiny's Child, the early years, or Brandy and Monica. Or Alia." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Raise Your Glass Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f7364fe6d21276e441d
**Peer of Missy Elliott from the 90s** : Monica was a peer of Missy Elliott, both having emerged in the music scene in the 1990s, fostering a connection that led to Elliott producing Monica's R&B chart-topping hit "Everything to Me." "For Monica, appear to Missy, who, like her, had come up in the 90s, Elliott produced the R&B chart topping Everything To Me, Monica's first number one on that chart in seven years." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Flip It and Reverse It Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Collaboration with Rodney Jerkins** : Monica was among the artists for whom R&B hitmaker Rodney Jerkins produced music. Jerkins also produced and co-wrote Jennifer Lopez's "If You Had My Love," underscoring the common production threads in late 90s pop and R&B. "The song could just as easily have been recorded by the other artists Jerkins was producing at the time, such as Brandy, Monica, or Destiny's Child." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > La Vida Loca Edición | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
