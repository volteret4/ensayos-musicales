## curiosities
**Band Name Evolution** : This garage-style band, led by Michael Quercio, was initially known as The Salvation Army before eventually changing its name to The Three O'Clock. "Their contemporary Michael Kursio formed a garage style band, first called the Salvation Army and then eventually the three o'clock." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be the One to Walk in the Sun, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Paisley Underground Naming** : Michael Quercio of The Three O'Clock coined the term "Paisley Underground" in an interview, and the name subsequently stuck to describe the scene of retro LA bands that included The Bangles, Rain Parade, and The Dream Syndicate.
