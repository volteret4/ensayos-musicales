**Lay Down (Candles in the Rain) (1970)** : This 1970 single was not only inspired by Woodstock but "was literally about Woodstock," as Melanie wrote it after seeing thousands of concert-goers light matches and lighters during her nighttime set, resembling candles, which she perceived as a "religious experience." Featuring a strong gospel flow and backing vocals by The Edwin Hawkins Singers, the song reached number 6 in July 1970, breaking Melanie in America and spreading the "gospel of Woodstock worldwide" by reaching the Top 10 across Europe, Canada, and Australia. **Woodstock Performer (1969)** : Melanie was one of only five women or acts including women among the 32 performers at the Woodstock Music and Arts Festival in 1969. Her presence at the festival contributed to the small contingent of female artists featured at the iconic event. "Out of the 32 acts who played Woodstock, only five were women or even included women.
