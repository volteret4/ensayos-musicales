## songs
**The Chipmunks Song (1958)** : This novelty hit, performed by David Seville with Alvin and the Chipmunks, holds a unique place in US chart history. It reached the top of the Billboard Hot 100 in December 1958, less than six months after Billboard launched the chart. It is notable as the last Christmas song, specifically a song *about* Christmas, to hit number one on the Hot 100 since its inception.
