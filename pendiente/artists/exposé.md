**Formation and Early Success** : The Miami girl group Expose was formed by producer Louis A. Martenay. Their 1985 club hit "Point of No Return" led to their signing with Arista Records, a label run by Clive Davis, which was soon to see major success with Whitney Houston. "Expose, a girl group from Miami, formed by producer Louis A. Martenay, topped the Billboard Dance Disco Chart with a frenetic Latin freestyle jam called Point of No Return.
