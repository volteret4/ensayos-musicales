# artist - John Landis

## curiosities
**The Blues Brothers Director (1980)** : The film director invited Aretha Franklin to make a cameo as a waitress in his 1980 musical film *The Blues Brothers*. He was reportedly "almost shocked" when she accepted, noting "she didn't have anything else going on" at the time, highlighting her career low point prior to the film.
