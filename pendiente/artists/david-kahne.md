# artist - David Kahne

## labels
- Columbia Records

## albums
**All Over the Place (1984)** : Kahne produced The Bangles' debut LP. **Different Light (1986)** : As producer for The Bangles' second album, Kahne was "determined to make different light the Bangles pop breakthrough" at the behest of Columbia Records, focusing heavily on vocals and employing studio sidemen for instrumental parts. "David Khan, a producer who'd made his bones producing punk acts and had produced the Bangles debut LP, he went into the second album with other ideas." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be the One to Walk in the Sun, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**When You're a Boy (1991)** : Kahne produced Susanna Hoffs's first solo album, which Hoffs later stated resulted in a sound where Kahne "went out of control, pop" without the band to push back.
