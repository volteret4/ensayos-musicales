# artist - Millie Vanilli

## curiosities
**1990 Grammy Scandal** : Millie Vanilli, a hip-pop vocal duo, had their Best New Artist Grammy prize rescinded in 1990 due to fraudulent practices, marking the biggest scandal in the history of the National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences. This incident called the very existence of the Grammys into question and prompted NARAS to re-evaluate its processes.
