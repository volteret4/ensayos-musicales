# artist - West Coast Rap All-Stars

## members
- Dr. Dre
- Eazy-E
- Hammer

## songs
**We Are All in the Same Gang (1990)** : Produced by N.W.A.'s Dr. Dre, this anti-violence record was released one year after the East Coast's "Self Destruction." It featured an even larger array of West Coast luminaries, including pop rappers like Hammer and early gangsters like Eazy-E. In the East Coast vs. West Coast charity single competition, this Western release "won" by making the black singles top 10 and scraping the pop top 40, achieving higher positions on both charts than the Stop the Violence Movement's single. The song was certified gold. "One year later, N.W.A.'s Dr. Dre produced his own anti-violence record. ## curiosities
**East Coast vs. West Coast Competition** : This single was part of an informal East Coast vs. West Coast competition in the charity mega-single format. The West Coast Rap All-Stars' "We Are All in the Same Gang" outperformed the East Coast's "Self Destruction" on both the black singles chart (top 10) and the pop top 40.
