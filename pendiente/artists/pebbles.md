## curiosities
**Pop Star to Artist Management** : Pebbles transitioned from a pop star with hits like "Mercedes Boy" to artist management. Around the time she married songwriter and record label chieftain LA Reid, she was instrumental in forming the girl group TLC. "Pivoting from pop star to artist management around the time she married songwriter and record label, Chieftain LA Reed, Pebbles was introduced to a pair of aspiring singer-wrappers living in Atlanta, named Tion Watkins and Lisa Lopez." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be My Baby-Baby-Baby Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eed8e6dd12efb02c340
**Discovery of TLC and LaFace Family Affairs** : Pebbles, also known as Perry Reed and married to L.A. Reed, discovered the trio TLC, who became LaFace's biggest seller of the decade. She was deeply involved in the "LaFace family affair," even singing backup on some of her own propulsive hits.
