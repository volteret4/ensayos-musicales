## curiosities
**Founding of Lisa Lisa and Cult Jam** : Full Force, a Brooklyn rap crew, were the architects behind forming Lisa Lisa and Cult Jam as a "multi-cultural black and Latin group," actively auditioning and hiring its members, including Lisa Velis and the musicians for Cult Jam. "Full Force were a Brooklyn rap crew, and they were the ones who formed Lisa Lisa and Colt Jam as a multi-cultural black and Latin group." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Motown Influence in Production** : The members of Full Force openly admitted their intention to draw inspiration from "the old Motown days," specifically mentioning Diana Ross and the Supremes, when writing and producing "Head to Toe." "We went back to the old Motown days, back to Diana Ross and the Supremes." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Signature Sound in Production** : Their production style, as heard in "Head to Toe," incorporates distinctive "hard driving drones" underneath the Motown-inspired melodies. "Underneath it you've got some hard driving drones, which is the full force sound." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Strategic Use of Freestyle Tools** : Full Force cleverly utilized the tools of freestyle, such as synthesizers run through zylafeng and kongas, even when the resulting sound, as with "Lost in Emotion," didn't strictly adhere to the traditional freestyle genre. "Full force were using the tools of freestyle even when they weren't sounding like freestyle." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Maximizing Pop Potential** : They successfully strategized to maximize Lisa Lisa's pop potential by hinting at freestyle elements without producing full-on freestyle jams, a method that resonated with the musical trends of 1987. "Full force had figured out how to maximize Lisa Lisa's pop potential, allude to freestyle without creating full on freestyle jams." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
