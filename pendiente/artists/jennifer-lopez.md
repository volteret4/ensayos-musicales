## albums
**J-Lo (2001)** : Released in 2001, this disc permanently established the well-known nickname "J-Lo" for Jennifer Lopez. Her management aimed to affirm her as a "queen of all media," so Sony Music released the CD the same week Sony Pictures released her romantic comedy *The Wedding Planner*. It debuted at number 1 on the Billboard 200 in early February, making Lopez the first artist to have the top album and top movie at the box office in the same week. The album's lead single, "Love Don't Cost the Thing," was already in the top 5 on the Hot 100. The album began to tumble on the charts after the second single "Play" failed to perform well, even briefly falling out of the top 100.
