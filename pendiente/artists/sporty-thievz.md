# artist - Sporty Thievz

## songs
**No Pigeons (1999)** : This song was a 1999 hit, reaching number 12 on the charts. It served as a "clapback" or answer record to TLC's number one hit "No Scrubs" from the same year, notably copying the melody of the original song.
