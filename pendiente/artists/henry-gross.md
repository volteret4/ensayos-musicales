# artist - Henry Gross

## songs
**Shannon (1976)** : This 1976 hit about a dog was dedicated to a deceased pooch named Snuggles in a "Long-Distance Dedication" on American Top 40. The placement of this dedication after an up-tempo song led to Casey Kasem's "Dead Dog Tape" outburst.
