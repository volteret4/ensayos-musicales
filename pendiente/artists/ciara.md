**Western Wear Photoshoot (September 2018)** : In September 2018, Ciara, an R&B hitmaker, promoted new music with a photoshoot of herself in Western wear for the fall cover of King Kong fashion magazine. These photos were retweeted by Brian Mollandro with the caption "the yeehaw agenda is in full effect," helping to ignite the #yeehawagenda viral meme.
