## albums
**In Search Of... (2002)** : The debut album from Pharrell Williams and Chad Hugo's rock-leaning band side project, which they called N.E.R.D (reportedly standing for "No One Ever Really Dies"). It was a sleeper hit, peaking at No. 56 on the album chart but quietly going gold, and producing minor hits like "Lapdance" and "Rock Star." ## curiosities
**Origin of Name** : N.E.R.D was a side project band that Pharrell Williams and Chad Hugo had been "tinkering with" since 2001, supposedly an acronym for "No One Ever Really Dies," though most fans simply called them "Nerd."
