# artist - The Dixie Cups

## genres

## songs
**Chapel of Love (1964)** : Released in late May 1964, this song was a number one hit on Billboard's Hot 100, notably knocking The Beatles out of the top spot. It was penned by professional New York songwriters and articulated the romantic desires and dreams of young women, specifically a wish for a white wedding. The Dixie Cups were the sole American group to achieve a number one hit in the entire first half of 1964. **Iko Iko** : This song, a New Orleans standard, became The Dixie Cups' first hit version, reaching number 20 on the charts. It served as a mid-charting follow-up to their number one hit "Chapel of Love" and possessed an energy reminiscent of The Bobbettes' "Mr. Lee."
