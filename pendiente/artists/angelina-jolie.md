# artist - Angelina Jolie

## curiosities
**Lara Croft Tomb Raider Film (2001)** : Angelina Jolie starred in the first "Lara Croft Tomb Raider" film, released in 2001. U2 remixed their song "Elevation" specifically for the soundtrack to this movie, resulting in a top 10 alternative rock hit that summer.
