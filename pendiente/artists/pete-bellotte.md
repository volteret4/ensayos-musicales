# artist - Pete Bellotte

## genres

## curiosities
**Lyricist and Concept Creator** : Pete Bellotte was a lyricist, teaming with Giorgio Moroder to write the UK number one hit "Son of My Father." He also conceived the overarching concept for Donna Summer's album *I Remember Yesterday*.
