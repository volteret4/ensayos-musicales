# artist - Stonebridge

## genres
- House Music

## songs
**Show Me Love (1993)** : Stonebridge produced the definitive hit remix of Robin S's "Show Me Love," notably adding the "signature bubbly keyboard sound" and "cool atmosphere" that made the song a hit three years after its original 1990 release. Beyoncé later referenced this remix on "Break My Soul" by utilizing the same Korg keyboard preset. "Now, that's the definitive hit version of Show Me Love, but you may not be aware that that Show Me Love, with its signature bubbly keyboard sound, was actually a remix by Swedish DJ and house music producer." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > If You Love Sting, Set Him Free Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef88e6dd12efb02c671

## curiosities
**Lack of Copyright Recognition for "Show Me Love" Impact** : Despite his remix's "bubbly keyboards" being the key element that made "Show Me Love" a hit and influenced Beyoncé's "Break My Soul," copyright law did not mandate Beyoncé to credit Stonebridge. Instead, credit was awarded to the writers of the original, non-hit song, illustrating a perceived inequity in copyright. "If anyone should benefit financially from the Beyoncé hits Allusion to Show Me Love, it's surely DJ Stonebridge.
