## curiosities
**Formation** : Last Exit was formed by Sting and keyboardist Jerry Richardson, both of whom were members of the Newcastle Big Band. They were frustrated by the Big Band's conservative repertoire and sought to play more modern rock-era jazz, drawing inspiration from groups like Weather Report or Return to Forever. "This is Whispering Voices, the first single by Last Exit, a band Sting formed with keyboardist Jerry Richardson from the Newcastle Big Band.
