# artist - Kim Karnes

## curiosities
**Summer 1981 Hit** : Kim Karnes had a smash hit in the summer of 1981, which contributed to keeping Hall and Oates' "You Make My Dreams" from reaching number one, as it was "stuck for three weeks behind summer 81 smashes" by multiple artists.
