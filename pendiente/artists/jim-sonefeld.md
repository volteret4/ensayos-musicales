# artist - Jim Sonefeld

## member of
- Hootie & the Blowfish

## curiosities
**Drummer and Songwriter for Hootie** : Jim Sonefeld joined Hootie & the Blowfish in the fall of 1989, replacing original drummer Brantley Smith. A slightly older University of South Carolina graduate, he brought with him experience playing in goth-rock and classic rock bands, and notably, he was a "budding songwriter." His contribution of the song "Hold My Hand" was a crucial turning point for the band.
