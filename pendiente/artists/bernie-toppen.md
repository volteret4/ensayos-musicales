# artist - Bernie Toppen

## curiosities
**Unique Lyrics-First Songwriting System** : Bernie Toppen developed an unusual songwriting system with Elton John, where he would send fully penned lyrics to John, who would then set them to music, often without further consultation. This Tin Pan Alley-style format, adapted for rock, generated intensely personal-sounding songs that reflected both men's sensibilities.
