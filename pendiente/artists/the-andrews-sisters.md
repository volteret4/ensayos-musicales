## curiosities
**Pop Dominance (Early Domino Career)** : The Andrew Sisters were among the biggest artists in pop music when Fats Domino first began achieving hits, marking a distinct era of popular music that preceded the widespread dominance of rock and roll. "When he started having hits, the biggest artist in pop music were Perry Como and the Andrew Sisters." ← Episode 45： ＂Blueberry Hill＂, by Fats Domino | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VqIwZGJHgBg
**Pre-Rock and Roll Influence** : As actual siblings, The Andrews Sisters toured extensively with jazz bands, becoming one of America's leading live acts by the early 1940s. While not the first female vocal group, they were heavily influenced by The Boswell Sisters and significantly impacted the era preceding rock and roll, with their close harmonies, exemplified in "Boogie Woogie Bugle Boy," anticipating the characteristic "masked voices" of the later girl group era. "Although they weren't the first female vocal group, taking cues from earlier jazz trio, the Boswell sisters, the Andrews sisters had the greatest impact in the years leading up to rock and roll." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be My Baby-Baby-Baby Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Influence on The Pointer Sisters** : The Andrews Sisters' close harmonies served as a primary influence on The Pointer Sisters, a fact noted in a previous Hit Parade episode.
