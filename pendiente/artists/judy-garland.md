## songs
**Have Yourself a Merry Little Christmas (1944)** : This holiday classic originated in the 1944 movie *Meet Me in St. Louis*, starring Judy Garland. Despite its enduring popularity, it was not the biggest hit from the soundtrack upon its initial release, with "The Trolley Song" charting higher. However, more than 80 years later, Garland's original version still charts annually on Billboard's Holiday 100, currently at number 75, alongside covers by Michael Bublé and Frank Sinatra. "It entered the world via a movie. **The Trolley Song (1944)** : This song was the biggest hit from the *Meet Me in St. Louis* soundtrack at the time of its release in 1944, reaching number four on Billboard's Best Sellers Chart. It outperformed "Have Yourself a Merry Little Christmas" in initial chart performance, though the latter later became a holiday standard.
