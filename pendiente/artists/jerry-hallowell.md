## curiosities
**Presenting Award to Idol** : At the 1998 MTV Video Music Awards, then 27-year-old Jerry Hallowell of the Spice Girls, calling 40-year-old Madonna "My Idol Growing Up," poignantly presented Madonna and Jonas Auckland with the Video of the Year award for "Ray of Light."
