## curiosities
**Inspiration for "Killing Me Softly With His Song"** : Folk rocker Don McLean, known for "American Pie," gave a performance at the Troubadour in Los Angeles that so impressed singer Laurie Lieberman that her impressions of his performance became the genesis of the song "Killing Me Softly With His Song." "rising folk rocker Don McLean of American Pie fame, Lieberman shared with her songwriting partners how knocked out she was by McLean's performance." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Killing Me Softly Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
