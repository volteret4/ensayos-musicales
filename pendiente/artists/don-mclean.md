# artist - Don McLean

## songs
**American Pie (1972)** : This 1972 number one smash contains coy poetic references to other famous musicians, such as Elvis Presley (referred to as "the King"), Bob Dylan ("the Jester"), and The Beatles ("Sargents"). However, the song is described as a "hero worship record" rather than a diss record, as no one is being disparaged.
