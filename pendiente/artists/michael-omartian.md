# artist - Michael Omartian

## songs
**She Works Hard for the Money (1983)** : Omartian worked with Donna Summer as a producer and songwriter on this title track, which became Summer's biggest pop hit of the decade and her biggest R&B hit ever, reaching number three pop and number one R&B in the summer of 1983. ## curiosities
**Production and Session Work** : Michael Omartian produced Christopher Cross's entire debut album and also played on numerous Steely Dan songs, establishing a "direct line" between these seemingly disparate artists within the Yacht Rock universe. His connections illustrate the intricate network of musicians and producers in the genre.
