**Athens Rock Lineage** : Oh-OK was an Athens band that contributed to the "jangly" American indie rock sound of the mid-1980s. The band was led by Michael Stipe's sister, Linda Stipe, and featured guitar work from a young Matthew Sweet, further cementing its ties to the burgeoning Athens music scene.
