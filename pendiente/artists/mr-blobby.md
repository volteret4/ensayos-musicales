**Mr. Blobby (1993)** : This novelty single took the UK Christmas number one title in 1993. The character, Mr. Blobby, was a figure in a pink rubber costume from a BBC TV variety show, although he was actually a satire of children's television featured on an adult evening program. The song included a children's chorus. By 1994, UK newspapers reported that the British public had already grown tired of the character.
