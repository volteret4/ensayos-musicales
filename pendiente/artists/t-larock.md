## songs
**It's Yours (1983)** : This breakbeat and scratch-heavy single was produced by Rick Rubin in his NYU dorm room, featuring lyrics by Clarence Ron Keaton (T. LaRocke) that were "even more meta and self-aware than your average hip hop joint." It became Russell Simmons' favorite rap record and served as Rubin's pivotal "entree into the center stage of rap." "In 1983, at the age of 20, Rubin, who had been hanging around clubs soaking up the emerging hip hop scene, teamed with John Bias, whose nom du ex was DJ Jazee J on It's Yours, a breakbeat and scratch-heavy single Rubin produced out of his NYU dorm room, wrapped by Clarence Ron Keaton, aka T.
