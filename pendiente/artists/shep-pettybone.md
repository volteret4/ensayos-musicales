# artist - Shep Pettybone

## genres
- House music

## curiosities
**Radical Remixer and Collaborator** : Shep Pettybone's radical remix of Madonna's "Express Yourself" caught on at Top 40 Radio and MTV, becoming the widely accepted hit version of the song and signaling Madonna's growing allegiance to house music and club culture. He teamed with Madonna again for "Vogue," an original single that borrowed the remix's structure.
