# artist - Allen Toussaint

## curiosities
**Prolific Songwriter and Producer** : A "New Orleanian legend," Allen Toussaint wrote "Yes We Can," originally recorded by Lee Dorsey in 1970, and produced LaBelle's smash hit "Lady Marmalade." He also wrote "Happiness" for The Pointer Sisters, showcasing his diverse contributions to music.
