# artist - Myron Cohen

## curiosities
**Copa Cabana Comedian (1958)** : Myron Cohen was a stand-up comedian who preceded Sam Cooke's infamous performance at the Copa Cabana in March 1958. He reportedly set up Cooke for failure with the largely white audience. The film "One Night in Miami" recreates this scene.
