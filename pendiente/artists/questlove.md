## curiosities
**Rock and Roll Hall of Fame Advocate and Inductor** : Questlove, drummer for The Roots and a soul and hip-hop historian, passionately advocated for Hall and Oates' candidacy for the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame. As a member of the Nominating Committee, he reportedly put them forward for the ballot. When they were voted in on their very first ballot appearance in 2014, Questlove did the honors at the induction ceremony, emphasizing the duo's importance to the black community and their genre-defying music.
