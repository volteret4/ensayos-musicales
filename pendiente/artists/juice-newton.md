# artist - Juice Newton

## genres
- Country Pop Crossover

## songs
**Break It to Me Gently (1982)** : Juice Newton, a "then-hot country pop crossover act," remade Brenda Lee's hit "Break It to Me Gently" in the 1980s, which then returned to the Hot 100 as a number 11 hit in 1982. "That hit, by the way, returned to the Hot 100 in the 80s in a remake by then-hot country pop crossover act, Juice Newton, a number 11 hit in 1982." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Chestnut Roasters, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Queen of Hearts** : Juice Newton's "Queen of Hearts" became an eventual number two pop hit in the summer of 1981, sharing space in the top 10 with The Oak Ridge Boys' "Elvira." This demonstrated the prevalence of upbeat, crossover country-pop during that era.
