## curiosities
**Presented MTV VMA to Madonna (1998)** : At the 1998 MTV Video Music Awards, Geri Halliwell, then 27 years old and a member of the Spice Girls, presented the "Moon Man" award for Video of the Year to Madonna, then 40, whom she called "My Idol Growing Up."
