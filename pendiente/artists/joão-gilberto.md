# artist - João Gilberto

## genres
- Bossa Nova

## albums
**Getz/Gilberto (1964)** : This collaborative LP, featuring jazz saxophonist Stan Getz and Bossa Nova king João Gilberto, reached number two on the album chart and won the top Grammy prize a year after Barbra Streisand's win. Its success was significantly aided by "the top five easy listening cocktail lounge classic The Girl from Ipanema." ## songs
**The Girl from Ipanema (1964)** : As a Bossa Nova pioneer, João Gilberto recorded "The Girl from Ipanema" with American jazz saxophonist Stan Getz. Gilberto sang the Portuguese lyrics for this global smash, which also featured English lyrics by his wife Astrud Gilberto and reached number five on the Hot 100 in 1964.
