# artist - David Mason

## instruments
- Piccolo trumpet

## songs
**Penny Lane (1967)** : David Mason, a renowned Bach soloist from the English Chamber Orchestra, performed a sizable piccolo trumpet solo in the middle of The Beatles' 1967 number one hit "Penny Lane." Despite his prominent contribution, he went uncredited on the single.
