# artist - Big Toxic

## curiosities
**River Famix Producer** : Big Toxic was a DJ who, along with synthpop duo Fangoria, produced the "River Famix" remix of Los Del Rio's "Macarena" in 1993 for their Spanish label. This remix replaced the traditional flamenco sound with a pulsing electronic beat, aiming to appeal to a younger audience.
