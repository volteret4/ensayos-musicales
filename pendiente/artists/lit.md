# artist - Lit

## songs
**My Own Worst Enemy (1999)** : This song was included in Anne Powers' 1999 New York Times article, which framed the summer's music as a series of one-on-one battles for the "Song of the Summer." It was specifically pitted against Blink-182's "What's My Age Again." ## curiosities
**Relatively Less Aggressive Altrock with "Party Bro Lyrics"** : Lit was part of a "relatively less aggressive wave of Altrock bands" in 1999. However, their songs, like those of Sugar Ray and Third Eye Blind, were noted for being filled with "party bro lyrics," marking a departure from earlier alternative music.
