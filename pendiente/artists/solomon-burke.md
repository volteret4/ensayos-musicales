## songs
**Proud Mary (1969)** : Solomon Burke, a Soul Icon, released the first hit cover of "Proud Mary," which debuted on the Hot 100 just weeks after Credence's original version began falling down the chart. His version led off with a spoken-word section and peaked just outside the Top 40 in the spring of 1969. **Proud Mary (1969)** : Solomon Burke's cover version of "Proud Mary" was the first to chart, reaching number 45. This occurred before Tina Turner's much bigger hit version, marking it as the initial charting cover. ## curiosities
**Rock Hall Induction (Tenth Ballot)** : Solomon Burke was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame on his tenth ballot, a record that was later surpassed by Chic's 11 nominations without induction.
