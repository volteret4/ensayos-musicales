## curiosities
**Signing to LaFace Records** : In 1995, the teen trio Choice, including Pink, was flown to Atlanta, Georgia, to meet with L.A. Reed, president of LaFace Records, on the strength of their song "Key to My Heart." Reed signed Choice, with the trio's parents co-signing the contract. **One Single on Kazaam Soundtrack** : Choice's one and only single was issued on the 1996 soundtrack to the family film "Kazaam," starring basketball player Shaquille O'Neal. This was the only official release by the group before their eventual breakup.
