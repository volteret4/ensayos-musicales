# artist - Eagle-Eye Cherry

## albums
**Desireless (1997)** : This album achieved platinum status, with sales more than twice that of his half-sister Neneh Cherry's 1989 album, "Raw Like Sushi." The album's success was driven by the hit song "Save Tonight," which was not released as a single. ## songs
**Save Tonight (1997)** : This was Eagle-Eye Cherry's sole hit, referred to as his "four minutes of fame." Unlike his half-sister, Neneh Cherry, who released "Buffalo Stance" as a retail single, Eagle-Eye Cherry did not release "Save Tonight" as a single. It became one of the biggest radio hits of the winter of 1998 and 1999.
