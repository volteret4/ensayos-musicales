# artist - Kim Basinger

## curiosities
**"Mary Jane's Last Dance" Video Appearance (1993)** : Kim Basinger, described as Prince's one-time paramour, starred in the high-concept music video for Tom Petty and The Heartbreakers' "Mary Jane's Last Dance." In the video, she portrayed a "Weekend at Bernie's like reanimated corpse."
