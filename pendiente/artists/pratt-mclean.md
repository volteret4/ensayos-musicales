# artist - Pratt & McLean

## songs
**Happy Days (1976)** : This pop duo performed the "50s Kitsch" theme song to the TV show "Happy Days," which became a number five hit in 1976. This theme, along with the "Laverne & Shirley" theme, rode the top 40 at the same time.
