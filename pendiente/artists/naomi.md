**Please Don't Go (1984)** : This late 1984 single by Brooklyn-born Afro-Cuban singer Naomi (Naomi Godelina Gomez) is credited as the "first universally recognized Latin freestyle hit." It featured "pinging rhythms that approximated the sound of salsa music" and later re-recorded in Spanish as "Nutevias." It reached number 23 on Billboard's Dance Disco Chart in early 1985.
