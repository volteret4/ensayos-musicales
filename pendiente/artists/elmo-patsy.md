# artist - Elmo & Patsy

## genres

## songs
**Grandma Got Run Over by a Reindeer (1983)** : This novelty single by the husband and wife duo from Kentucky crossed over from country radio to top the Billboard Christmas chart in 1983. Its success was aided by Billboard's reinstatement of the Christmas singles chart, which gave a leg up to songs that would not have posed serious Hot 100 competition.
