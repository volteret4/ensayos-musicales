# artist - Jake Gyllenhaal

## curiosities
**Inspiration for Taylor Swift's "We Are Never Ever Getting Back Together" and "All Too Well" (2012)** : Actor Jake Gyllenhaal's relationship with Taylor Swift inspired "not one but two Hot 100 number ones," specifically "We Are Never Ever Getting Back Together" and "All Too Well." In the former, Swift "called her former bow 'exhausting' and made fun of his love of Indy Rock," making their breakup a public lyrical narrative.
