# artist - Gary Lewis and The Playboys

## members
- Gary Lewis

## songs
**This Diamond Ring (1965)** : This "schmaltsy cover" of an R&B song reached number one with Lewis's group. Its co-writer, Al Kooper, notably complained that the group "made a teenage milkshake out of it."
