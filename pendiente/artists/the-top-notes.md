## songs
**Twist and Shout (1961)** : The Top Notes' original recording of "Twist and Shout" in 1961 by the R&B troop. Despite being the first recorded version, it was not a hit, though the song later achieved widespread fame through covers by The Isley Brothers and The Beatles.
