# artist - Charlie Patton

## curiosities
**Influence on Bob Dylan's Sound** : In 1996, while planning his album "Time Out of Mind" with Daniel Lanois, Bob Dylan explicitly stated his aspiration to sound like an old bluesman, specifically citing Charlie Patton as an influence. This desire to capture the essence of early blues pioneers guided the production and recording techniques used for the album.
