# artist - Jimmy Wayne

## songs
**Sara Smile** : Country singer Jimmy Wayne recorded a cover of Hall and Oates' "Sara Smile," which gave Darryl Hall and John Oates their first songwriting credits on Billboard's Hot Country Songs Chart. Wayne's cover reached number 31 on the Country Chart, bridging country and pop charts for the duo.
