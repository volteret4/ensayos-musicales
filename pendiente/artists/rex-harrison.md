# artist - Rex Harrison

## albums
**My Fair Lady (1956)** : This cast album, starring Rex Harrison and Julie Andrews, knocked Elvis Presley's debut LP out of the number one spot in 1956 and achieved remarkable longevity on the Billboard album chart, spending nearly 500 weeks charting and revisiting the number one spot multiple times between 1957 and 1959.
