**Lean Wit It, Rock Wit It : Atlanta Snap Music Hit** : This song was a hit by the Atlanta troupe Dem Franchise Boys, exemplifying the Snap Music subgenre. Snap Music is an Atlanta-based offshoot of Crunk Music, distinguished by its minimalist digital sound, including 808-thumps, high-hats, snapping, and simple chanting vocals.
