# artist - Share

## curiosities
**Featured in MTV Network Pitch (1981)** : Share was among the diverse music acts whose videos were included in the carefully curated VHS presentation that John Lack used in January 1981 to pitch the concept of a 24-hour music video network. This presentation was made to Steve Ross, the head of Warner, and Jim Robinson, the CEO of American Express, whose companies had recently merged.
