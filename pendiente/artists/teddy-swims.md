## songs
**Lose Control (2023)** : Released in late 2023, this song became the number one record of 2024, an "impossible to categorize" track blending R&B, pop, and rock. Despite spending only one week at number one, its sustained presence in the top 10 throughout the entire year led to Billboard declaring it the top record of the year.
