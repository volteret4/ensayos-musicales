## curiosities
**Bluebird Cafe Connection** : Kent Blazy was one of several songwriters Garth Brooks met at the legendary Bluebird Cafe in 1987. This meeting proved to be a "vital connection" in Nashville, leading to Blazy co-writing Brooks's first number one hit, "If Tomorrow Never Comes."
