## songs
**Cupid's Chokehold (2007)** : This song was recorded by Gym Class Heroes, a rap rock band, and featured Patrick Stump on guest vocals. It was notable for borrowing its chorus from an old Supertramp song. The single was released in March 2007 and achieved significant commercial success, reaching number four on the Hot 100. ## curiosities
**Guest on Live from Darryl's House and Collaboration** : The rap rock band Gym Class Heroes, fronted by Travie McCoy, not only played at "Live from Darryl's House" but also invited Darryl Hall to guest on their single "Live Forever." Travie McCoy showed his "obeisance" to Hall and Oates by getting tattoos of their faces, taken from the cover of their 1981 album *Private Eyes*, on the tops of his hands.
