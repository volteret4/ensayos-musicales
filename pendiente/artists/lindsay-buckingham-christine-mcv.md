# artist - Lindsay Buckingham Christine McV

## albums
**Lindsay Buckingham Christine McV (2017)** : This one-off album was recorded by Lindsey Buckingham and Christine McVie as a duo in 2017. Their collaboration was marked by "easy chemistry and no baggage" due to the absence of a romantic history, unlike Buckingham's dynamic with Stevie Nicks. The album earned strong reviews and cracked the top 20 on the Billboard album chart.
