**Will You Love Me Tomorrow (1961)** : Co-written by Carole King and Gerry Goffin, this song became the first girl group number one in Hot 100 history in January 1961. It articulated teen angst surrounding a legitimate quandary at the dawn of the sexual revolution, presented with a plush orchestral arrangement and a classic Carole King melody. Its immense success initiated a wave of female vocal harmony groups and established The Cherelles as crossover mega stars, preceding Motown's consistent success in launching black artists to pop stardom.
