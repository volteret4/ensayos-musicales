# artist - Jimmy Iovine

## curiosities
**Producer for Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers** : Jimmy Iovine produced "Damned The Torpedoes," an album Petty and The Heartbreakers had been working on, which MCA Records anticipated to be a smash. He also produced three albums for Petty and The Heartbreakers during a prolific period of Petty's songwriting, including tough-minded tracks like "Refugee" and irresistible ones like "Don't Do Me Like That." **Producing for Bruce Springsteen and Patti Smith** : Jimmy Iovine is described as a "hotshot producer" who had worked with Bruce Springsteen and Patti Smith before producing Tom Petty and The Heartbreakers' "Damned The Torpedoes." **Facilitating "Stop Draggin' My Heart Around" (1981)** : While producing The Heartbreakers' "Hard Promises" and Stevie Nicks' solo debut "Bella Donna," Jimmy Iovine quietly passed a shelved song by Petty and Mike Campbell to Nicks. He then presented it as a demo to Petty, proposing it become a duet, leading to the smash hit "Stop Draggin' My Heart Around." "The way Iovine heard the song, it expressed a female point of view and it sounded like a hit.
