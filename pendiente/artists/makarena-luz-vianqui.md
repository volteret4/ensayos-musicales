# artist - Makarena Luz Vianqui

## curiosities
**Name Origin and Nicknames** : Makarena Luz Vianqui was born in Chile, but her name originates from Spain, specifically a district in Seville known for the Basilica de la Makarena and its statue of the Weeping Virgin Mary, La Vieta de la Makarena, the patron saint of bullfighting. Her parents chose the name because they knew someone independent and classy with it, and to avoid the family tradition of using "Mariah" (e.g., Mariah Carey'sa, Mariah Angelica, Mariah de la Luz). As a child in Chile, she went by "Maka," but after moving to Miami in 1982, people often mispronounced it as "Maserina," leading her to default to "Mac" or endure schoolyard taunts like "Macaroni" and "Big Mac." **Nightlife Scene in Miami Beach (1995)** : In 1995, in her early 20s, Makarena Luz Vianqui was a prominent figure in the Miami Beach nightlife scene. She worked as a door person at exclusive clubs, controlling who gained entry. She was dressed by designers, recalls being the first to wear "plethora pants," and connected with a vibrant community through house music, disliking "cheesy pop music." **Horrified Reaction to the Song** : Makarena Luz Vianqui's first encounter with the "Macarena" song was through a DJ friend in Miami, and her reaction was immediate horror and tears. She felt the song was "ripping something from me," taking away her precious, rare name and potentially destroying its uniqueness. She actively tried to avoid hearing it, describing the experience as "mortified" and the song itself as "disgusting" and "gross," especially finding the lyrics about a "freaking fluzy" upsetting and a "hijack" of her name.
