## albums
**Getz/Gilberto (1964)** : This collaborative LP, featuring jazz saxophonist Stan Getz and Bossa Nova king João Gilberto, reached number two on the album chart and won the top Grammy prize a year after Barbra Streisand's win. Its success was significantly aided by "the top five easy listening cocktail lounge classic The Girl from Ipanema." ## songs
**The Girl from Ipanema (1964)** : American jazz saxophonist Stan Getz recorded "The Girl from Ipanema" alongside Bossa Nova pioneer João Gilberto. The song, written by Antonio Carlos Jobim, featured Portuguese lyrics sung by João Gilberto and English lyrics by his wife Astrud Gilberto. This collaboration resulted in a global smash and a number five hit on the Hot 100 in 1964.
