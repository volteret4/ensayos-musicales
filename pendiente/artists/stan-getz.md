## albums
**Getz/Gilberto (1964)** : This collaborative LP, featuring jazz saxophonist Stan Getz and Bossa Nova king João Gilberto, reached number two on the album chart and won the top Grammy prize a year after Barbra Streisand's win. Its success was significantly aided by "the top five easy listening cocktail lounge classic The Girl from Ipanema."
