## songs
**The First Time Ever I Saw Your Face** : The Chad Mitchell Trio was among the folk artists who covered Ewan MacColl's song "The First Time Ever I Saw Your Face" before Roberta Flack's definitive version. ## curiosities
**John Denver's Membership (1965)** : John Denver joined this folk group in 1965 as its founder, Chad Mitchell, was departing, coinciding with the shift from folk to rock and roll.
