## songs
**I Can Feel It (Recent)** : Kane Brown's recent hit that ascended to number one on Billboard's Country Airplay Chart, garnering the biggest audience on country stations across the US. This song is an interpolation of Phil Collins' 1981 rock classic "In the Air Tonight," featuring an opening drum break, a similar title, and a bridge that directly references Collins' original. ## curiosities
**Songwriting Credit for Interpolation** : Due to common songwriting law, Phil Collins received a co-writing credit for Kane Brown's "I Can Feel It," alongside Kane Brown and his Nashville co-writers, acknowledging the interpolation of Collins's "In the Air Tonight" in the new composition.
