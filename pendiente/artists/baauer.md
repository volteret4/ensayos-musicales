# artist - Baauer

## songs
**Harlem Shake (2013)** : This track became an instant smash largely due to Billboard's new fan video rule, which factored in fan-created YouTube videos. What made it a hit was not an official video from Baauer—who only had a bare-bones clip of the single's artwork—but rather the widespread fan participation. The week it hit number one, the track was heard in YouTube videos 103 million times, setting a one-week streaming record that would later be broken by Lil Nas X. This phenomenon established an important precedent for "Old Town Road" as the first internet meme to directly create a number one hit.
