## curiosities
**Songwriter and Bandleader** : Bobby Womack was the leader and soon-to-be legendary vocalist of The Womack Brothers, who later renamed themselves The Valentinos. He famously wrote "It's All Over Now," the original version of which was recorded by The Valentinos, leading to a complex relationship with its success after The Rolling Stones covered it. "Led by the soon-to-be legendary vocalist Bobby Womack, The Womack's renamed themselves the Valentinos and scored a quick hit on Sar with Looking for a Love, which hit number eight on the R&B chart in 1962." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Don’t Know Much About History, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
