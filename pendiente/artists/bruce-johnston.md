## curiosities
**Accused of Credit Theft** : Richie Polador accused Bruce Johnston, along with Kim Fowley and Sandy Nelson, of having "no scruples whatsoever" and taking credit for songs like "Geronimo" and "Charge" that Polador claimed he wrote and recorded in his own studio. "There are three guys in the business who really have no scruples whatsoever.
