## curiosities
**Out as Bisexual in 1992** : Richard Fairbrass, the lead singer of Right Said Fred, was publicly out as bisexual by February 1992, when the band's single "I'm Too Sexy" hit number one. The song and its accompanying video were openly "gay-coded and fabulous," marking a significant moment for an out performer to top the charts.
