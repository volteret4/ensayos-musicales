# artist - Jump 'n the Saddle

## songs
**The Curly Shuffle (1984)** : This smash hit from Chicago's Jump 'n the Saddle sold well enough to reach number 15 in early 1984. However, its radio spins were largely confined to morning and drive time hours, and the band never cracked the Hot 100 again.
