**My Maria (1996)** : Brooks & Dunn covered B.W. Stevenson's "My Maria" in 1996. Ronnie Dunn initially hesitated to cover the song, viewing it as a rock song and feeling "leery about this falsetto," highlighting the genre-bending nature of the original track. "Even when Ronnie Dunn was asked about covering my Maria, which of course Brooks and Dunn did in 1996, he was not so sure that he wanted to do it because A, he viewed it as a rock song and B, he was leery about this falsetto." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 49 - Austin City Limits and the 70s Country Music Revolution | http://ftr70.com
**My Maria (1996)** : The top country song of 1996 by the multiplatinum duo Brooks & Dunn. It was a soaring cover of a 1973 Top 10 pop hit originally by B.W. Stevens.
