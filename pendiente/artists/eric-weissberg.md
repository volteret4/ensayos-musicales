## songs
**Dueling Banjos (1973)** : Performed by bluegrass musicians Eric Weissberg and Steve Mandel, this "fleet-fingered song," famously featured in a memorable scene in the John Voight and Burt Reynolds film "Deliverance," evolved into a significant cultural phenomenon, reaching number two on the Hot 100. The "Deliverance" soundtrack LP also climbed to number one, marking a high point for instrumentals where "wordless hits from seemingly any genre could come out of left field and climb the charts." ## curiosities
**Banjoist on Dueling Banjos (1972)** : Eric Weissberg was one of the banjoists credited on the soundtrack to the 1972 film *Deliverance*, which was commercially released as *Dueling Banjos*. His work on the title track, a "dizzying banjo workout," helped it reach number 2 on the Hot 100 in February 1973 and served as an ambassador for Bluegrass music.
