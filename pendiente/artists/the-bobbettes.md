## songs
**Mr. Lee (1957)** : This banging doo-wop style ode to an eccentric high school teacher reached number six in 1957. Written by the teenage Bobbettes themselves, the single exuded a distinctive schoolyard energy. ## curiosities
**Early Girl Group** : The Bobbettes are mentioned as one of the early girl groups, with their phenomenon largely based in New York. "But if you remember the episodes on the bobbets and the shantels, girl groups in the early years were largely phenomenon based in New York." ← Episode 89： ＂Will You Love Me Tomorrow？＂ by the Shirelles | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Kns2HGcuSg
**Origin and Self-Authored Hit** : This quintet from New York's Spanish Harlem, comprised of teenagers, wrote their own songs, contributing to the authentic "school yard energy" of their hit "Mr. Lee."
