# artist - Long John Baldry and the Hoochie Coochie Men

## curiosities
**Rod Stewart's First Vocal Feature** : A single by this group was the first recording to feature a 19-year-old Rod Stewart on vocals, introducing his distinctive raspy husk that would become his trademark.
