# artist - The Real Milli Vanilli

## members
- Brad Howell
- John Davis

## albums
**The Moment of Truth (1991)** : This album was recorded by Brad Howell and John Davis, the actual singers behind Millie Vanilley, and was produced by Frank Farian. Released in 1991 under the group name The Real Milli Vanilli, it was only issued in a few countries outside of the US and charted solely in Germany before the group disbanded. ## curiosities
**Attempt to Gain Recognition** : After the scandal, the actual singers of Millie Vanilley, Brad Howell and John Davis, formed The Real Milli Vanilli as a chance to finally put themselves in the spotlight. However, the group soon disbanded after their album only charted in Germany.
