## songs
**No Getting Over It** : Ronnie Milsap's "No Getting Over It" was a borderline yacht rock song that became a number five pop hit. This track exemplified how country-pop singles functioned as straight-up adult contemporary pop during the disco backlash era. "Ronnie Millsap's borderline yacht rock, no getting over it. ## curiosities
**1974 CMA Male Vocalist of the Year Win** : Ronnie Milsap won the Male Vocalist of the Year award in 1974, an award that did not make country music purists happy. "Country music purists were not really that happy about Rich getting the award in 74 or Ronnie Mills up winning male vocalist of the year that same year." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 2 - Countryish Music of the 1970s (Encore) | https://www.ftr70.com/
**Rhythm and Blues Background** : Milsap was considered an "outsider" by some purists because he started his career trying to make it in rhythm and blues. "Ronnie was a newcomer who started his career trying to make it in rhythm and blues so he was considered an outsider." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 2 - Countryish Music of the 1970s (Encore) | https://www.ftr70.com/
**Uncredited Backing Vocalist on "Suspicious Minds"** : Ronnie Milsap provided very audible backing vocals on Elvis Presley's 1969 comeback single "Suspicious Minds." Despite his prominent contribution and his own rising country star status, he was only mentioned in the liner notes, not on the single's label. "On Suspicious Minds, Millsap and Green were liner notes fine print, nowhere to be found on the label of the vinyl 45 single." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Feat.
