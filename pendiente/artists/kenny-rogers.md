## curiosities
**1980 Solo Male Superstar** : In 1980, Kenny Rogers became America's biggest solo male singer. This was driven by his Lionel Richie-penned ballad "Lady" topping both the country and pop charts, and his "Greatest Hits" LP achieving massive success on the pop album chart with 12 million in sales. "But in 1980, Kenny became America's biggest solo male singer." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Friends in Low Places, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Last Country Album to Top Billboard 200** : Kenny Rogers' "Greatest Hits" album was the last country title to top Billboard's flagship all-genre album chart (the Billboard 200) until Garth Brooks' "Ropin' the Wind" achieved the feat more than ten years later in 1991. "It was not only the first country album ever to debut at number one, it was also the first country title to top Billboard's Flags. On top, Billboard's Flags, album chart, at all, since Kenny Rogers' greatest hits more than ten years earlier." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Friends in Low Places, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Hot 100 Topper (Early 1980s)** : In the early 1980s, Kenny Rogers was among the artists topping the Hot 100, highlighting a period when "screeching metal" faced a disadvantage on the pop charts.
