# artist - Edgar Winter

## songs
**Frankenstein (1973)** : This "riff heavy beast of a single" by multi-instrumentalist Edgar Winter surged to number one in the spring of 1973. It earned its title "Frankenstein" because it was assembled from "lots of different parts fused together into one monster song."
