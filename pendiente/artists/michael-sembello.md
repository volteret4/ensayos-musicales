## curiosities
**US Chart Absence After 1983** : After 1983, Michael Sembello never again appeared on the Hot 100, despite having a Top 40 follow-up hit. "after 1983 Zembello never touched the hot 100 again." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > One and Done, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Cultural Perception as One-Hit Wonder** : Although his immediate follow-up single "Automatic Man" cracked the Top 40, culturally Michael Sembello is perceived as a one-hit wonder because "Automatic Man" is largely forgotten compared to his massive hit "Maniac." "culturally Michael Zembello reads as a one hit wonder but my fellow chart nerds and maybe even Michael Zembello himself might well object to this ignominious chart." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > One and Done, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**"Co-tails" Hit Phenomenon** : "Automatic Man" is described as a "co-tails hit" because its chart performance was primarily driven by the afterburn of its much bigger predecessor, "Maniac." "Automatic man was what I call a co-tails hit riding the chart on the afterburn of its much bigger predecessor maniac." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > One and Done, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
