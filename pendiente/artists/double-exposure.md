## songs
**Ten Percent** : This track, recorded with an orchestra providing disco backing, had more jamming than could fit on a 7-inch single. Producer Walter Gibbons took this extended jam and rebuilt it specifically as a 12-inch, making it the first commercially available 12-inch vinyl single.
