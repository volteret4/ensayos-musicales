# artist - Norman Gimbel

## songs
**Killing Me Softly With His Song** : Norman Gimbel was one of the songwriters who, along with Charles Fox, transformed Laurie Lieberman's impressions of a Don McLean performance into the song "Killing Me Softly With His Song."
