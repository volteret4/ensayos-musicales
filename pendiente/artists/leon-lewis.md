# artist - Leon Lewis

## curiosities
**X-Factor Season 3 Winner (2006)** : Leon Lewis was the winner of the third season of the British reality competition show, The X-Factor, in 2006. His win continued the pattern established by Simon Cowell where the show's winner claimed the UK Christmas number one spot. He is also noted as a "future American hitmaker."
