# artist - Lostel Mar

## curiosities
**Canadian Cover Version Controversy** : A Canadian producer assembled a group called Lostel Mar ("those from the sea") to create a cover version of "Macarena" that was almost identical, or "zero-oxed," to the original. This "shrewd business move" exploited Canadian media rules requiring a certain percentage of radio songs to be Canadian-made, giving Lostel Mar's version a significant advantage in airplay over Los Del Rio's Spanish original. "There's a law in Canada that says a certain percentage of songs played on the radio have to be Canadian made. While Lostel Rio came from Spain, Lostel Mar counted as Canadian." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > One Year - Hey Macarena! | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Canadian Chart Success** : The Lostel Mar version of "Macarena" achieved considerable success in Canada, hitting number one on the Canadian singles chart in July 1995 and holding that position for nine weeks. This success was a source of frustration for BMG Canada, who were simultaneously promoting the original Los Del Rio version, perceiving the cover as a "windfall" for a group that copied the "heart and soul" of the original composition.
