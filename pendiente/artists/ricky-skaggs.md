**New Traditionalism Artist** : Ricky Skaggs, a country bluegrass multi-instrumentalist, was one of the artists who went back to country music's roots during the "new traditionalism" era of the mid-1980s. This movement aimed to differentiate itself from the earlier country pop sound.
