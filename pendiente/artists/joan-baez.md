## curiosities
**Woodstock Performer (1969)** : Joan Baez was one of only five women or acts including women among the 32 performers at the Woodstock Music and Arts Festival in 1969. Her performance helped represent the minimal female presence at one of the most significant music events of the century. "Out of the 32 acts who played Woodstock, only five were women or even included women. Janice Joplin, Melanie, members of Sly in the Family Stone and Jefferson Airplane and Joan Byeth." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Building a Herstory Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
