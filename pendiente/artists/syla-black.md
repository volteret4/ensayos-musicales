# artist - Syla Black

## songs
**Alfie (1966)** : British singer Syla Black was the first to sing and release the title tune for the 1966 British film "Alfie," written by Bert Bacharach and Hal David. Her version was subsequently outcharted by Dion Warwick's cover.
