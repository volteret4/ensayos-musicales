## albums
**Pop Goes the Movies (1982)** : This 1982 album was a medley of various film themes, ranging from "The Magnificent 7" to "Goldfinger." It marked Meco's comeback during the medley craze, successfully cracking the top 40 on the Hot 100, reaching number 35. ## songs
**Star Wars Theme / Cantina Band (1977)** : This medley, a disco arrangement of the "Star Wars Theme" by Domenico Monardo, known as Meco, reached number one in late 1977. Its triumph exemplified the pervasive cultural impact of "Star Wars" that year, where "anything affiliated with Star Wars couldn't fail." **Post-Craze Decline (1982)** : By the summer of 1982, follow-up medleys released by Meco, along with those by Stars on 45, failed to chart on the Hot 100. This signaled the definitive end of the medley craze, as public interest in such compilations waned.
