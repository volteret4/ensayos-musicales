## curiosities
**Homage in 24K Magic** : Bruno Mars's album "24K Magic" was broadly considered "one long homage" to the 80s black boy band New Edition. Specific singles from the album echoed the distinctive New Jack Swing sound of Bobby Brown and the "slamming hip-hop club sound" of the spin-off group Bell Biv DeVoe.
