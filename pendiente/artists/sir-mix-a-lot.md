**Baby Got Back (1992)** : Sir Mix-A-Lot's first and last top 40 pop hit, this song had a profound cultural impact. Written by Anthony Ray as an admirer of "hourglass-shaped women," particularly Black women like his then-girlfriend Amelia Dorsey, who recorded the humorous spoken word intro. The song was Sir Mix-A-Lot's version of body positivity, candid and progressive about body types years before Jennifer Lopez's influence. It debuted on the Hot 100 the same week as Arrested Development's "Tennessee," reaching number one by July 4, 1992, staying there for five weeks, and going double platinum. It was the second best-selling single of 1992, behind only Whitney Houston's "I Will Always Love You," and Billboard named it the year's number two song, behind Boyz II Men's "End of the Road." The song's publishing belongs entirely to Anthony Ray due to its lack of samples, making it highly lucrative, with claims of grossing over $100 million. It remains ubiquitous, having been sampled, covered, and referenced extensively across media. "Baby Got Back was Sir Mixelot's first ever, and as it turns out, last top 40 pop hit." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Oh. My. God.
