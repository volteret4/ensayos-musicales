# artist - Tony Arata

## songs
**The Dance** : Tony Arata, a Bluebird Cafe alumnus, penned "The Dance," which became Garth Brooks' signature ballad and the biggest hit from his debut album. The song's composition was so evocative that it could easily be imagined being sung by Dan Fogelberg. ## curiosities
**Bluebird Cafe Alumnus** : Tony Arata was identified as a "Bluebird alumnus," indicating he was part of the songwriting community connected to the legendary Nashville venue where Garth Brooks also played. Arata went on to pen "The Dance," Brooks' signature ballad and biggest hit from his debut album.
