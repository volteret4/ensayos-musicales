# artist - Chris Kristofferson

## curiosities
**Co-starred in Streisand's "A Star Is Born" (1976)** : Chris Kristofferson co-starred with Barbara Streisand in her 1976 remake of "A Star Is Born." Streisand transformed the story into a music business fable and used it as a vehicle to project the swagger of a contemporary rock star.
