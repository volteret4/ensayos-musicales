## songs
**In the Summertime (1970)** : This song reached number three on the Hot 100 by Labor Day in 1970. In Billboard's retroactive 1970 Song of the Summer tally, it ranked 10th overall, indicating it was a significant summer hit but not the dominant one of that year.
