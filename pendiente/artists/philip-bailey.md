## songs
**Easy Lover (1984)** : This duet between Earth Wind & Fire singer Philip Bailey and Phil Collins was a major crossover success, reaching number two on the Hot 100, number three on the R&B chart, and number five on the Album Rock Chart. The song showcased "Bailey's fluttery soul vocals" alongside Collins's booming rock drums, solidifying a powerful collaboration.
