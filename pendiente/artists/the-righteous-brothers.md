## songs
**Unchained Melody (1965)** : The Righteous Brothers issued what is considered the definitive version of "Unchained Melody" in 1965. This recording, primarily featuring Bobby Hatfield, reached number four on the Hot 100 and was a very big hit. "In the summer of 1965, the righteous brothers Unchained Melody single reached number four on the Hot 100, a very big hit." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Second-Chance Hits Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef18e6dd12efb02c42e
**Unchained Melody (1990)** : Due to the song's prominent feature in the 1990 film "Ghost," "Unchained Melody" surged back onto the charts, but with a catch. The Verve label reissued the original 1965 version on vinyl 45 (reaching number 13, fueled by radio), while Bobby Hatfield re-recorded a new version for Curb Records (reaching number 19, selling a million copies on cassette and CD) due to royalty issues. This resulted in The Righteous Brothers competing against themselves with two versions in the Top 20 simultaneously.
