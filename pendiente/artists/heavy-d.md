**Jam (1992)** : Heavy D was featured as the bridge rapper on Michael Jackson's 1992 hit "Jam." This exemplifies the "featured bridge rapper" model of pop-rap crossover prevalent in the 1990s and 2000s, where a rapper adds a hip-hop edge to a pop song.
