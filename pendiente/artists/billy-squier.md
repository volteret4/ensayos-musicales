# artist - Billy Squier

## albums
**Signs of Life (1984)** : Jim Steinman was brought in to produce this album. While the album showed fewer of Steinman's signature sonic touches and Squier wrote all his own material, Steinman's pop hook sensibility contributed to its success. ## songs
**Rock Me Tonight (1984)** : This song became Billy Squier's biggest ever top 40 hit, peaking at number 15 in the summer of 1984, attributed to Jim Steinman's production and his "way with a pop hook."
