**Lady Marmalade (1975)** : Produced by Allen Toussaint for their 1974 album *Nightbirds*, this song was a genre hybrid combining the "gut bucket funk" of Dr. John with the "danceable soul" of The Pointer Sisters. Written by Bob Gaudio and Kenny Nolan, who sought to capture the lasciviousness of Bourbon Street, its initial recording by studio disco group The 11th Hour was unpromising, but Toussaint's production made it sound authentically New Orleans. After a three-month climb in late 1974 and early 1975, it topped both the R&B and pop charts in February and March 1975. The song featured a sexy French refrain, "Voulez-vous coucher avec moi ce soir?", which became a schoolyard titter and a radio in-joke among mid-70s pop fans.
