## songs
**Lady Marmalade (1974)** : Released in 1974, this song about a hooker was written by men, Bob Crew and Kenny Nolan, and Patti LaBelle stated she didn't even know what the song was about. Despite this, the song is significant for empowering the character of "Creole Lady Marmalade," who boldly asks "Voulet Vou Coucher avec moi, c'est soi," presenting a woman in a position of power in a sexual context, marking progress in the portrayal of female sexuality in music. ## curiosities
**Groundbreaking Style and Confidence** : LaBelle was recognized as a groundbreaking group that pushed boundaries with their innovative costumes, dynamic stage shows, and assertive, confident presence. Their style represented a significant departure from earlier, more constrained female groups like Diana Ross and The Supremes.
