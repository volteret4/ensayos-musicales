## curiosities
**Bakersfield Sound Revivalist** : Dwight Yoakam was recognized as a Bakersfield Sound Revivalist, contributing to the "new traditionalism" movement of the mid-1980s. His biggest hit involved a pairing with country legend Buck Owens, further cementing his traditional country bona fides.
