# artist - Boys to Men

## curiosities
**Precedent for Self-Replacement at Number One** : Boys to Men were one of the four acts (Elvis Presley, The Beatles, and Nelly) that Outcast followed in replacing themselves at number one on the Hot 100, a rare achievement in chart history.
