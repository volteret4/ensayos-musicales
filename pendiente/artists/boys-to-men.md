# artist - Boys to Men

## songs

## curiosities
**Precedent for Self-Replacement at Number One** : Boys to Men were one of the four acts (Elvis Presley, The Beatles, and Nelly) that Outcast followed in replacing themselves at number one on the Hot 100, a rare achievement in chart history. **Airplay on Cube 93 FM** : Boys to Men were among the typical artists played on Cube 93 FM in Seattle, a Top 40 station in the mid-1990s.
