# artist - Susanne Vega

## curiosities
**Rise in the 1980s** : Susanne Vega's career rise in the 1980s is noted as a part of the "prehistory" leading up to the Lilith Fair, alongside Tracy Chapman. She represented an early wave of successful women in music.
