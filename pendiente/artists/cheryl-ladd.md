# artist - Cheryl Ladd

## songs
**Think It Over (1979)** : This song was the subject of a bold "Long-Distance Dedication" in April 1979, when a high school junior named Barry dedicated it to Cheryl Ladd, a pop singer and actress from "Charlie's Angels," in an attempt to ask her to his prom. "In April 1979, a high school junior from Minnesota named Barry asked Kaseyme to play Think It Over by Cheryl Ladd, who was both a pop singer and one of the stars of TV's Charlie's Angels.
