## curiosities
**Defiant Advice to Jerry Leiber (June 1958)** : When Jerry Leiber was incapacitated by pneumonia and Colonel Parker insisted on his presence at an Elvis session, Mike Stoller advised Leiber to defiantly tell Parker to "do something to himself," using strong language. This act of defiance was a contributing factor to the final severing of Leiber and Stoller's direct professional relationship with Elvis. **Houndog Songwriting (1952)** : Mike Stoller co-wrote the celebrated song "Houndog" with Jerry Leiber in 1952. Their original lyrics for Big Mama Thornton's 1953 version were described as "thinly veiled metaphorical lyrics."
