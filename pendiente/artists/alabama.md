# artist - Alabama

## albums
**Mountain Music** : Alabama's album "Mountain Music" achieved quadruple platinum status in the early 1980s, reflecting a period when the band consistently hit the pop Top 20 on both the album chart and the Hot 100. However, by 1984, their albums began missing the Top 40, and singles missed the Hot 100, indicating a significant decline in their pop crossover success. ## curiosities
**Mid-80s Pop Decline** : Alabama, once one of the biggest bands of their era, experienced a significant decline in pop crossover success by 1984. They went from consistently hitting the pop Top 20 with albums like the quadruple platinum "Mountain Music" and singles on the Hot 100 in the early 80s, to their albums missing the Top 40 and singles missing the Hot 100.
