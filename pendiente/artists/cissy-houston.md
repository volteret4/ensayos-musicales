## curiosities
**Aretha Franklin's Backup Singer** : Cissy Houston, Whitney Houston's mother, sang backup for Aretha Franklin for many years, establishing a deep musical connection between the families. "daughter of Sissy Houston, who'd sang backup for Aritha for years." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say a Little Prayer Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eec154465cd600c4d67
**Matriarch of a Musical Family** : Born Emily Drinkard, she changed her first name to Sissy after marrying John Houston and is the mother of Whitney Elizabeth Houston, born in August 1963. She is also the aunt of Dionne and D.D. Warwick, establishing her as a key figure in a prominent musical lineage. "Dating back to the 50s, Sissy, who was born Emily Drinkard, before marrying John Houston and changing her first name to Sissy, sang in a gospel troupe called The Drinkard Singers, with her niece's Dion and D.D. Warwick." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I'm Your Whitney Tonight Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Backing Vocalist for Aretha Franklin** : Sissy Houston served as a prominent backing vocalist on Aretha Franklin's 1967 Atlantic debut album, a role that contributed to the Sweet Inspirations signing their own contract with the label. "Sissy was a prominent backing vocalist on Aretha Franklin's Atlantic debut album." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I'm Your Whitney Tonight Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Solo Career in the 1970s** : In 1970, following the success of The Sweet Inspirations, Sissy Houston permanently broke away to pursue a solo career, though her chart success did not reach the heights of Aretha Franklin or her niece Dionne Warwick. **Backing Vocals on "How Will I Know"** : Sissy Houston contributed multi-tracked background vocals to her daughter Whitney Houston's hit song "How Will I Know," having been invited into the studio by producer Narada Michael Walden to participate in the recording. **Family Connection** : Sissy Houston led The Drinkard Singers, a gospel vocal troupe, and is the mother of Whitney Houston and aunt of Dion Warwick. She also provided backup vocals on The Drifters' "Mexican Divorce" alongside Dion Warwick and others.
