# artist - Alexandra Burke

## songs
**Hallelujah (2008)** : Alexandra Burke's 2008 cover of Leonard Cohen's "Hallelujah" was her winning single from The X-Factor, and it became a massively popular Christmas number one. The single sold an astonishing 576,000 copies in its first week alone in the UK, a significant sales number for the country's size. This made it the fastest selling single released by a woman in UK chart history at the time. ## curiosities
**X-Factor Winner (2008)** : Alexandra Burke won The X-Factor in 2008, extending the show's unbroken streak of Christmas number ones. Her winning single broke new ground by being the first recording artist to take a version of Leonard Cohen's "Hallelujah" to the top of the charts.
