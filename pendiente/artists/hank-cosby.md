# artist - Hank Cosby

## curiosities
**Co-writer of "The Tears of a Clown" (1967)** : Hank Cosby, a Motown co-writer, collaborated with Stevie Wonder on "The Tears of a Clown" in 1967, contributing to the song before Smokey Robinson completed the lyrics for The Miracles.
