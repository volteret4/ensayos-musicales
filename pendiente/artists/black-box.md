## curiosities
**Mystery Title** : The very title of the Italian House Music group, Black Box, suggested a kind of mystery regarding the identities of the actual performers. "Italian House Music Group Black Box, an act whose very title suggested a kind of mystery about who exactly was performing." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Vocalist Deception** : Black Box engaged in a layered deception regarding its vocalists for "Ride On Time," starting with a sample of Loleatta Holloway, then having Heather Small re-record the part, and finally employing model Catrin Cranall to lip-sync in the video, without either of the actual singers performing for the cameras. This pattern continued with "Everybody, Everybody," where Martha Wash sang but Quenall was the group's public face. "So a vocal that had started as Loliada Holloway's and had been re-recorded by Heather Small was performed without either of them for the cameras." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Martha Wash Lawsuit** : Famed vocalist Martha Wash, who sang on "Everybody, Everybody" and two subsequent Top 40 hits for Black Box, ended up suing the producers and the label due to her uncredited contributions. She won a large settlement, even as the group continued to score hits with her "invisible vocals."
