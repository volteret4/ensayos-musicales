## curiosities
**Not an Imperial Act** : Despite their initial massive success, including back-to-back number one hits and an album that topped the charts for 15 weeks, Men at Work were not considered an imperial act. Their hits quickly dried up, they never achieved a fifth top 10 hit, and their sound neither influenced other acts nor consistently connected on the airwaves, indicating they were "a momentarily big act that were never imperial."
