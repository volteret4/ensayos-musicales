## albums
**Minute by Minute (1978)** : This studio album was the Dubie Brothers' first chart-topping LP and became their all-time biggest seller. It affirmed Michael McDonald's conversion of the group into a smooth pop and R&B crossover act, featuring "What a Fool Believes" as its centerpiece. **Taking It to the Streets (1976)** : This LP marked Michael McDonald's rapid emergence as the group's most successful songwriter, as he penned the album's only Top 40 hits. The title track, a number 13 hit in the summer of 1976, foreshadowed the sleek R&B direction McDonald would soon take the band.
