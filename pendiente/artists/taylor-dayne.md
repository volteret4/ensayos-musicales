# artist - Taylor Dayne

## songs
**I'll Always Love You (1988)** : After releasing only a couple of singles in freestyle mode, Dayne switched to mainstream balladry with this 1988 single, which became a number 3 hit. ## curiosities
**Pop Influence (Late 1980s)** : Taylor Dayne, described as a "Dance-Diva," was among the pure pop artists who absorbed the "power-balled metallic pop drama" that was rubbing off on pop music during the late 1980s. "Some of this same power-balled metallic pop drama started rubbing off on other pure pop of the period, Weatherfun Share, or even Dance-Diva Taylor Dane." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The You Give Rock a Bad Name Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Freestyle as Gateway to Mainstream** : Taylor Dayne effectively leveraged freestyle as her entry point into mainstream fame, before transitioning predominantly to balladry and adult contemporary pop by her sophomore album. "Freestyle was her gateway to the mainstream." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Shift to Balladry** : By her sophomore album, she had mostly shifted her musical focus to balladry and adult contemporary pop, moving away from her initial freestyle sound.
