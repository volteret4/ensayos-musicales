# artist - Blue Swede

## songs
**Hooked on a Feeling (1974)** : This song was a number one smash hit on the Hot 100 in 1974. It later gained renewed cultural recognition in a 1998 episode of the Fox TV show "Ally McBeal," where the title character imagined a computer-generated baby dancing to the song, which also became a hit meme online. However, despite this cultural moment, "Hooked on a Feeling" did not re-chart due to its "Ally McBeal" appearance or the web meme.
