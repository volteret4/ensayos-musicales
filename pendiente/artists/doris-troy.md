## songs
**Just One Look (1963)** : This classic hit, released in August 1963, reached the top 10 on both the pop and R&B charts. Its success occurred in the same week that Whitney Elizabeth Houston was born, highlighting its significance in the 60s rhythm and blues sound. "In fact, the week in August 1963 that Sissy Houston gave birth to that child, Whitney Elizabeth, a former vocalist from The Drinkard Singers, Doris Troy, was in the top 10 of both the pop and R&B charts with the classic hit Just One Look." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I'm Your Whitney Tonight Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Backup Vocalist** : Doris Troy was a friend of Sissy Houston and an affiliate of The Drinkard Singers. She provided backup vocals on The Drifters' B-side "Mexican Divorce" along with Sissy Houston, Dee Dee Warwick, and Dion Warwick. "The backup vocals on Mexican divorce were by several affiliates of the drinker singers, Sissy Houston, her friend Doris Troy, Sissy's nieces DD Warwick and Dion Warwick, then 21 years old." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Killing Me Softly Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
