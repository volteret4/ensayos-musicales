# artist - Janis Ian

## albums
**Between the Lines (1975)** : Janis Ian's album "Between the Lines" hit number one on the album chart one week after her single "At Seventeen" reached number three in September 1975. This album chart victory occurred nearly two decades before she publicly came out as a lesbian. **At Seventeen (1975)** : "At Seventeen" was a "tender hit" about feeling like a teenage outcast that reached number three on the charts in September 1975, briefly making Janis Ian a chart-topping star. Her album "Between the Lines" hit number one a week later, but Ian would not come out as a lesbian until nearly two decades after this success. ## curiosities
**Closeted During Album Chart Victory** : Janis Ian's album chart victory with "Between the Lines" in 1975, which followed her hit single "At Seventeen," came "nearly two decades before she came out publicly as a lesbian." This makes her another example of an LGBTQ artist achieving major success while still closeted.
