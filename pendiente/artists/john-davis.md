# artist - John Davis

- Millie Vanille

## curiosities
**Real Singer for Millie Vanilley** : John Davis was Frank Farian's "other ghost vocalist" for Millie Vanilley, joining Brad Howe on the chorus for "Blame It On The Rain" and later appearing on camera in the video for The Real Milli Vanilli's "Keep On Running."
