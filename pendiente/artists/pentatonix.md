## albums
**Pentatonix (2015)** : In 2015, the "best-selling acapella singing troupe" Pentatonix hit number one with their self-titled fourth album. Two of its members, Mitch Grassi and Scott Hoying, are gay, marking their success as part of the trend of openly queer artists topping the Billboard 200 post-Adam Lambert's "Trespassing" milestone.
