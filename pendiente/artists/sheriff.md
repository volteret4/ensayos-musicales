# artist - Sheriff

## labels
- Capitol Records

## songs
**When I'm With You (1983)** : Sheriff's one and only hit, a "gooey power ballad," originally peaked at number 61 in 1983. Inspired by the success of UB40's "Red Red Wine" in late 1988, Minneapolis radio programmer Brian Phillips plucked it from an old album and put it back into rotation. Capitol Records reissued the single, and it rapidly climbed the Hot 100, reaching number one by February 1989. ## curiosities
**Band Disbandment and Reissue (1984, 1988)** : The band Sheriff had broken up four years before their song "When I'm With You" was reissued and went to number one in 1988-1989. Capitol Records, their label, was annoyed by this, as they felt they were promoting a "mirage" with no future to build on. "Unlike UB40, a band that at least still existed, Sheriffs, the band behind When I'm With You, had broken up four years before." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - Red, Red Wine Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Lead Singer's New Group (1990s)** : Sheriff's lead singer and guitarist capitalized on the song's renewed popularity by quickly forming a new group called Alias, which then recorded another power ballad, "More Than Words Can Say," sounding like a sequel to "When I'm With You," reaching number two in 1990.
