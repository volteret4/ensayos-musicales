## curiosities
**Debut Hit Crediting Discrepancy** : The Blue Bells' first hit, "I Sold My Heart to the Junk Man" in 1962, was a remake of a 1940s doo-wop song. Although credited to The Blue Bells, the lead vocals were actually performed by another group, The Starlets, due to the label owner's decision to attach The Blue Bells' name to the record. **Cindy Birdsong's Departure to The Supremes** : In 1967, after their #36 R&B hit "Take Me For A Little While," founding group member Cindy Birdsong was poached by Motown Records to replace Flo Ballard in their top act, The Supremes. This departure significantly impacted The Blue Bells, leading the remaining three members to regroup and eventually rename themselves simply LaBelle.
