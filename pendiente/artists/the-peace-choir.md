## songs
**Give Peace a Chance (1991)** : This all-star rendition of John Lennon's anthem was released in March 1991, co-organized by Yoko Onas and featuring over 40 eclectic acts like MC Hammer, Axel Rose, Peter Gabriel, Iggy Pop, and Michael McDonald. **Give Peace a Chance (1991)** : This remake of John Lennon's anti-war anthem was the brainchild of Lenny Kravitz, who teamed with Yoko Ono and their 15-year-old son, Sean Ono Lennon. Released in 1991 as a benefit for Gulf War victims and an implied protest against the war, the video opened with Lenny and Sean playing guitar side-by-side. The recording featured more than 40 eclectic acts and peaked at No. 54 on the Hot 100, solidifying Kravitz's reputation as Gen X's premier Boomer Rock Nostalgia. "Credited to The Peace Choir, the new Give Peace a Chance featured more than 40 eclectic acts from MC Hammer to Axel Rose to Peter Gabriel to Iggy Pop to Michael McDonald." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Lenny on Mars Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
