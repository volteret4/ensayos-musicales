# artist - Keith Sweat

## genres
- New Jack Swing

## songs
**I Want Her (1987)** : This smash hit, released in late 1987, inaugurated the sultry, percolating, and rap-derived trend that the media dubbed New Jack Swing. Its success, along with contributions from producers like Teddy Riley, played a key role in defining the sound of popular African American music in the late 80s.
