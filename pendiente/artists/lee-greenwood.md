# artist - Lee Greenwood

## songs
**God Bless the USA (1984)** : This song, released in 1984, is described as an "unabashedly jingoistic" track. The Reagan campaign's misinterpretation of Bruce Springsteen's "Born in the USA" assumed it was similar to this song due to its patriotic title.
