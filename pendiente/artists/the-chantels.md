## songs
**Maybe (1958)** : Released approximately six months after The Bobbettes' "Mr. Lee," this song introduced a layer of heartache to the burgeoning girl group sound. It became a number two R&B hit and a number 15 pop hit in early 1958. Widely regarded as a slow dance classic, it featured a soulful, yearning vocal performance from Bronx-born singer Arlene Smith, which became an aspiration for subsequent girl group vocalists.
