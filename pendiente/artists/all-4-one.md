## songs
**I Swear (1994)** : This song was the winner of the 1994 Billboard Song of the Summer. It is a ballad performed by the "Boyband Vocal Troop" All-4-One. **I Swear (1994)** : This boy band recorded a cover of John Michael Montgomery's country hit "I Swear" and successfully took their version to the top of the Hot 100 pop chart. This demonstrated how a song could be a hit in both country and pop genres simultaneously, but through different recordings. **I Swear (1994)** : This R&B cover of a country ballad spent 11 weeks at number one in 1994, momentarily making the multiracial Los Angeles quartet chart commanders. ## curiosities
**"Boyish" Rather Than "Boy Band"** : Although they scored a handful of hits, All-4-One were described as more "boyish" than genuinely boy band-like, failing to inspire the intense "pin-up poster fandom" associated with groups like New Edition or New Kids on the Block.
