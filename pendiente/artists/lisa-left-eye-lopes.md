# artist - Lisa "Left Eye" Lopes

## curiosities
**Platinum Level Rapper** : Lisa "Left Eye" Lopes is identified as one of the "platinum level women rappers" who contributed significantly to the strong female hip hop scene of the 1990s. Her success was part of the context for Lilith Fair's initial "glaring omission" of hip hop artists.
