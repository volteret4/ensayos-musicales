## curiosities
**Phonk Collective** : This collective, led by Space Goshpar in Miami, was instrumental in developing the early Phonk sound in 2011. They integrated the "dirty and lo-fi techniques" of Memphis rap with the "narcotic slowness" of Houston, adding a modern and digital feel, heavily influenced by West Coast G-Funk. "el productor y rapero de Miami Space Goshpar y su colectivo Raider Clan cogieron las téticas sucia y lofa y de Memphis, la mezclaron con la lentitud narcótica de Houston, y aportaron una sensibilidad moderna y digital." ← ¿Qué pasó con el PHONK？ (La historia real) | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ehfWyUHFFT8
**Phonk Popularizers** : SpaceGhostPurrp and his collective Raider Klan were instrumental in popularizing the term and sound of "Phonk" in the early 2010s, notably with songs like "Filled Up Funk 1990."
