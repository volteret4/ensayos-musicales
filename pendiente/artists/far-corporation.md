## songs
**Stairway to Heaven (1986)** : Far Corporation recorded a version of this classic rock song in 1986, originally by Led Zeppelin. Their cover reached number 89 on the US Hot 100, making them the only act ever to chart with the song, as Led Zeppelin famously did not issue it as a retail single. It was also briefly a top 10 hit in England, a first for any version of the song in the UK. ## curiosities
**Supergroup Formation** : Far Corporation was a short-lived supergroup formed by Frank Farian in the mid-1980s. The lineup included Farian himself, three players from the 80s American hitmakers Toto, and other musicians "borrowed" from a couple of UK bands. The group's name, originally "Frank Farian Corporation," was shortened for the LP sleeve. "Farion hired three players from Toto to record with some others he borrowed from a couple of UK bands to form a short-lived supergroup named after himself, Frank Farion Corporation." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Only Act to Chart "Stairway to Heaven"** : Far Corporation holds a unique footnote in chart history as the only act ever to crack the Hot 100 with a version of Led Zeppelin's "Stairway to Heaven." This is notable because Led Zeppelin themselves famously never released the 1971 track as a retail single.
