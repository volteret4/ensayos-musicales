## curiosities
**Blondie's Predecessor** : The Stilettos were a band that opened for Television in 1974, fronted by Deborah Harry (a former waitress and Playboy bunny from New Jersey) and guitarist Chris Stein (from Brooklyn). After lineup changes and name changes, they eventually became Blondie.
