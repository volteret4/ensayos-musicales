# artist - Bizz Markie

## songs
**Just a Friend (1990)** : His one and only pop hit, spending its 22nd and final week on the Hot 100 in June 1990. Bizz Markie served as his own featured act, delivering both the rapped verse and a sung chorus that millions still drunkenly sing along with to this day. It was a number nine pop and number 37 R&B single, uniquely blending humor and melody. ## curiosities
**One Hit Wonder Status** : "Just a Friend" was Bizz Markie's only Hot 100 hit or major R&B hit of any kind, categorizing him as a one-hit wonder. His career was defined by this single, despite its lasting cultural impact as a sing-along anthem.
