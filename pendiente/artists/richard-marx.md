## curiosities
**Number One Ballads (Summer 1989)** : Richard Marx was one of the artists who achieved "syrupy number ones" with ballads during the summer of 1989. This period saw a proliferation of ballads on the pop charts, influencing label strategies for acts like Millie Vanille.
