## albums
**Titanic (1997)** : James Horner composed the score for James Cameron's *Titanic*, which constituted over 90% of the film's soundtrack. The album became the top-selling CD of 1998 and, with the inclusion of Celine Dion's theme song, topped the Billboard 200 for 16 weeks and went diamond, selling 10 million copies in just 6 months.
