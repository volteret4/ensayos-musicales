## songs
**When Boy Meets Girl (1996)** : The Neptunes wrote and produced this track for Total, a girl group protégé of Sean Combs. The song's beat was constructed using a B.G. sample, and its production featured dry, brittle drums and a stripped-down sound, hinting at The Neptunes' future sonic direction. ## curiosities
**Biggie Collaboration** : The Notorious B.I.G. was featured on a song by Total, which was part of a strategic effort by him and Sean "Puff Daddy" Combs to keep Biggie in the mix without overexposure before the release of "Hypnotize."
