# artist - Tom Bryan

## curiosities
**"Go Away Little Girl" Critical Reception** : Tom Bryan, a chart columnist who writes the number one series for StereoGum, gave both Steve Lawrence's and Donnie Osmond's versions of "Go Away Little Girl" a rare one on his one-to-ten scale, making it the only song he graded that low twice. This critical assessment highlights the song's polarizing reception despite its chart success.
