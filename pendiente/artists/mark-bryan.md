# artist - Mark Bryan

## member of

## curiosities
**Discovery of Darius Rucker** : As a freshman and budding guitarist at the University of South Carolina in the mid-1980s, Mark Bryan first discovered Darius Rucker's vocal talent. He overheard Rucker singing Billy Joel's "Honesty" in a dorm shower, an encounter that led to their musical collaboration and the eventual formation of Hootie & the Blowfish.
