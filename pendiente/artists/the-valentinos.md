# artist - The Valentinos

## labels
- SAR Records

## songs
**It's All Over Now** : The original version of this song was written and recorded by Bobby Womack, leading his brothers in The Valentinos. While the song reached number 94 on Billboard's Hot 100 and number 21 on the R&B chart in early September 1964, it was overshadowed by The Rolling Stones' cover, a fictionalized account in "One Night in Miami" suggesting it disappeared due to the Stones' No. 1 success. **Looking for a Love (1962)** : The Valentinos' quick hit on SAR Records, reaching number eight on the R&B chart in 1962. This success demonstrated the benefits artists on SAR were receiving from Sam Cook's production savvy.
