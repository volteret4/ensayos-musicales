**Roxanne's Revenge (1985)** : This seven-minute freestyle was recorded by Lolita Shantay Gooden, who renamed herself Roxanne Shanté, as an answer record to UTFO's "Roxanne Roxanne." She was a teenager from Queens who "regarded UTFO's song as an affront to women," and her track "dissing the trifling dudes for their whack comos" is widely considered the first major rap beef single. It sold a quarter million copies in the New York City area alone and reached number 22 on Billboard's R&B chart, bubbling under the Hot 100.
