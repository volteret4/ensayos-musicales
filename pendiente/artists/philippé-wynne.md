## songs
**Then Came You (1974)** : Philippe Wynne, as the lead singer of The Spinners, was paired with Dion Warwick on this quintessential Tom Bell Philly Soul production. The song was specifically conceived to showcase their combined "fiery vocals" and became a #3 Pop, #1 R&B smash.
