# artist - Fat City

## members
- Bill Danoff
- Taffy Nivert

## curiosities
**Collaboration with John Denver** : This husband-and-wife duo, consisting of Bill Danoff and Taffy Nivert, opened for John Denver in Washington, D.C. in December 1970. They co-wrote "Take Me Home, Country Roads" with Denver and provided backing vocals on his 1971 recording of the song.
