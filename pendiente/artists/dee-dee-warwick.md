# artist - Dee Dee Warwick

## member of
- The Drinkard Singers

## songs
**You're No Good (First Recording)** : This song, originating from the Brill Building era, was first recorded by Dee Dee Warwick, who is Dionne Warwick's sister. Linda Ronstadt later achieved significant success with her cover version of the track. "it was first recorded by Dion Warwick's sister, Dee Dee." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 47 - Dick Clark's Entertainment Empire | https://www.ftr70.com/

## curiosities
**Backup Vocalist** : Dee Dee Warwick, niece of Sissy Houston and cousin of Dion Warwick, provided backup vocals on The Drifters' B-side "Mexican Divorce" along with Sissy Houston, Doris Troy, and Dion Warwick. "The backup vocals on Mexican divorce were by several affiliates of the drinker singers, Sissy Houston, her friend Doris Troy, Sissy's nieces DD Warwick and Dion Warwick, then 21 years old." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Killing Me Softly Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
