# artist - John Lennon & Yoko Ono

## songs
**Happy Xmas (War Is Over) (1971)** : Issued in 1971 by John Lennon and Yoko Ono, this peaceful protest song was initially only allowed on Billboard's Christmas singles chart, where it peaked at a respectable number three. Its chart performance was limited by the policy of segregating holiday songs, contrasting with later rule changes for other former Beatles' holiday releases.
