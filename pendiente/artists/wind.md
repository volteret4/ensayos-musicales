# artist - Wind

## songs
**Let The Sun Into Your Heart (Las DeZone in Dine Huts) (1987)** : This was Germany's entry in the 1987 Eurovision Song Contest, with Rob Pilatus (later of Millie Vanille) appearing as a backing vocalist. The song placed second in the competition, making Germany that year's runner-up behind Ireland.
