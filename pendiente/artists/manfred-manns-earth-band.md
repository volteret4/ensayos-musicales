**Blinded by the Light (1977)** : A 1976 cover of Bruce Springsteen's R&B-flavored boogie rock jam. Released in the closing weeks of 1976, it took 14 weeks to reach number one on the Hot 100 for the week ending February 19, 1977, becoming Springsteen's only #1 hit credit as a songwriter. The version was drenched in Mann's "frothy 70s AOR keyboards" and tweaked Springsteen's original lyric "cut loose like a deuce" to "revved up like a deuce," which was widely misheard as "wrapped up like a douche." This misheard lyric is considered one of the most famous in pop history and possibly contributed to the song's massive success, a claim even Springsteen acknowledged. ## curiosities
**Covering Dylan and Newman** : The Earth Band's first two singles were covers of songs by Bob Dylan and Randy Newman, indicating a pattern of covering established singer-songwriters before their success with Springsteen's material. "By the way, the Earth Band's first two singles were covers of songs by Bob Dylan and Randy Newman." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Tramps Like Us, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Misheard Lyric Success** : The band's version of "Blinded by the Light" featured a tweaked lyric ("revved up like a deuce") that was famously misheard as "wrapped up like a douche." This gross but memorable mishearing is believed to have played a role in the song becoming a number one smash. "Instead, toked up 70s listeners heard wrapped up like a douche. **"Blinded by the Light" Number One Hit** : Bruce Springsteen is noted as "the man who accidentally wrote a number one for Manfred Man," referring to Manfred Mann's Earth Band's cover of his song "Blinded by the Light," even though Springsteen himself had never topped the chart at that point.
