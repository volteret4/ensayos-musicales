## curiosities
**Famous Baptist Minister** : Reverend C.L. Franklin was one of the most famous Baptist ministers in the American Midwest, known for his compelling sermons and powerful voice. **"Million Dollar Voice" and Recording Star** : C.L. Franklin was renowned for his "million dollar voice" and was a recording star in his own right, with his sermons captured for posterity. His sermon, "The Eagle Stireth Her Nest," is even preserved in the U.S. Library of Congress. **Family Life and Infidelities** : Due to Reverend Franklin's infidelities, he and his wife, Barbara Franklin, separated in 1948 when Aretha was six. His frequent travels also meant that Aretha and her four siblings were often raised by extended family and friends, including gospel legend Mahalia Jackson. **Manager and Music Mentor to Aretha** : Reverend Franklin began managing his daughter Aretha in 1954 when she was 12, taking her along on his gospel caravan tours where she would perform songs like "Jesus Be a Fence Around Me" or "Precious Lord." He also arranged for her to record tracks for a small independent label in Detroit in 1956 and gave his blessing for her to pursue pop music around her 18th birthday.
