# artist - The Rapping Duke

## curiosities
**Pioneer in Genre Mixing** : The Rapping Duke was among the artists who, decades prior to Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road," were mixing country music with R&B and Rap, contributing to a cross-genre tradition.
