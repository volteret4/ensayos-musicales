# artist - Steve Luger

## curiosities
**Initiation of Yacht Rock Book** : Steve Luger, a book agent, approached the Yacht Rock creators at one of their live events with the idea of writing a book on the genre. He subsequently helped them secure a book deal with Little Brown UK for "the ultimate, all-in-all book on Yacht Rock."
