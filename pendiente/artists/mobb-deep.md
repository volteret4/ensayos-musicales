# artist - Mobb Deep

## songs
**Out of Control (2005)** : The rap duo Mobb Deep was featured on the remixed/remade version of 50 Cent's song "Out of Control" in 2005. This remix reached number 6 on the Hot 100, charting separately from the original album version, in compliance with Billboard's post-2002 rules.
