# artist - Sarah Allen

## curiosities
**Co-Writer for Hall and Oates** : Sarah Allen was a companion and frequent co-writer for Darryl Hall and John Oates, contributing to songs like "I Can't Go for That (No Can Do)" and "Maneater." She also co-wrote the title track "Swept Away" for Diana Ross's 1984 album with Darryl Hall.
