**Pony (1996)** : This song was Ginuwine's first single after signing with Epic Records in 1996. Devised by Timbaland while working in Devanté Swing's Debasement and co-produced with Static Major, it was described as a "weird lurching belching club jam" and a "cheeky metaphor for sex." "Pony" soared up the charts, reaching number 6 on the Hot 100 and spending two weeks at number 1 on the R&B chart, becoming "ground zero for the quirky Virginia Beach sound." ## curiosities
**Meeting Timbaland (Mid 1990s)** : Elgin Baylor Lumpkin, known professionally as Ginuwine, met Timbaland while both were working in Devanté Swing's Debasement. This encounter led to Timbaland producing Ginuwine's breakthrough hit "Pony." "For Timbaland, the breakthrough came via an equally quirky production he devised for an R&B singer he met during his years with Devante Swing, a man from Washington DC born Elgin Baylor Lumpkin who went by the name Ginuon." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Flip It and Reverse It Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
