**Hot Lanta (1971)** : An instrumental jam from The Almond Brothers Band's 1971 live album "At Fillmore East." Though the band was from Jacksonville, Florida, this track helped popularize and institutionalize the "Hot Lanta" nickname, contributing to the idea of Atlanta as a music capital.
