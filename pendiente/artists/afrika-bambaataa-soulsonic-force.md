**Planet Rock (1982)** : Considered Electro's "signature track," it reached number four on the R&B chart in 1982. The song was built from an interpolation of Kraftwerk's "Trans-Europe Express," featuring Bambaataa's vocoder-filtered "behind the beat" rapping, "explosive synth stabs," and a "dizzying array of electronic effects." Critics agree it "changed the course of R&B, rap, and dance production for the rest of the 1980s." "Planet Rock by Africa Bombata and Solsonic Force is considered Electro's signature track.
