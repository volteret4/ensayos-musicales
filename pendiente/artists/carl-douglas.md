**Kung Fu Fighting (1974)** : This 1974 number one hit was an accidental smash, making Carl Douglas wealthy from its millions of sales and continuous covers, including for the *Kung Fu Panda* series of films. Douglas, of Jamaican descent, became the first Jamaican with a US number one hit. Producer Biddu Apaya initially preferred another song for the A-side. Douglas wrote "Kung Fu Fighting" after seeing kids mock-fighting to music in a Soho pinball arcade, conceptualizing the melody line and lyrics in his head. It was quickly recorded in just two takes during the last 10 minutes of a London studio session for "I Want to Give You My Everything." UK Pie Records staff recognized it as the hit despite Biddu's initial dismissal. The song benefited from the 1970s kung-fu craze, reaching number one in England in September 1974 and topping the Hot 100 just before Christmas 1974.
