## curiosities
**Producer Credit on "The Message"** : Edward "Duke Booty" Fletcher, the producer of Grandmaster Flash and the Furious Five's 1982 classic "The Message," was given explicit credit on the single's label. This was an early and somewhat unusual instance of a producer receiving such recognition.
