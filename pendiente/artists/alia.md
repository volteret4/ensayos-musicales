**Self-Titled Third Album (2001)** : Alia's third studio album was self-titled and released in July 2001, following five busy years of work. It featured several collaborations with Timbaland and debuted at number two on the Billboard 200, which was her highest album chart position to date at the time. After her tragic death on August 25, 2001, the album rose to number one on the Billboard 200, achieving this peak just days before the horrific tragedy of 9/11. ## curiosities
**Tragic Plane Crash (August 25, 2001)** : On August 25, 2001, while returning from shooting a music video for "Rock the Boat" in the Bahamas, Alia and her entourage boarded a Cessna twin engine plane. The plane became overloaded and crashed shortly after takeoff, killing Alia, the pilot, and all eight passengers on board. She was only 22 years old at the time of her death.
