## songs
**Smooth Operator (1985)** : This song was Sade's breakthrough US hit, reaching number five in May 1985. It featured "jet setting bossa nova rhythms" and is an example of the Sophisti-pop sound that blended soul, cocktail jazz, and lavish production. ## curiosities
**Ambient Tracks and Smooth Jazz** : Sade is recognized for her "languor" and "ambient tracks" within the pop genre, and her music was blended into early smooth jazz radio formats. "From the silkeness of a George Benson guitar solo to the langer of a Shade Jam smooth jazz... Smooth Jazz stations blended... The more ambient tracks from pop acts like Shade." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Insert Lyrics Here Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Representative of UK Sophisti-Pop** : Sade is mentioned as a key artist representing "UK Sophisti-Pop," a smooth, jazzy cocktail pop emerging in 1984-85, distinct from West Coast Yacht Rock but equally smooth. This new sound often had connections to New Romantic and New Wave movements. "This was the moment for UK's Sepist Pop, like Shade." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > What a Fool Believes, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
