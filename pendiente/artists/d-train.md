**You're the One For Me (1982)** : This 1982 R&B hit reached number 13 on the R&B chart. It exemplifies the Post-Disco idiom with its heavy use of synthesizers, which functioned not merely as rhythmic or ornamental elements but as an "extra voice on the track."
