# artist - The Pied Pipers

## member of
- Tommy Dorsey

## curiosities
**Small Print Vocal Credit** : The Pied Pipers, along with Frank Sinatra, received credit for the "vocal refrain" in very small print on the label of Tommy Dorsey's 1940 number one hit, "I'll Never Smile Again." This was a minor acknowledgment compared to the main artist credit.
