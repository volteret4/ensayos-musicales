**The First Time Ever I Saw Your Face (1972)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1972, a success born from "a series of lucky breaks" for Roberta Flack. This old folk standard, sung at a "smolderingly slow tempo," gained significant traction after filmmaker Clint Eastwood licensed it for his directorial debut, "Play Misty for Me." It became a "late breaking smash," spending six weeks at number one, the most of any song in 1972, and secured the year-end title over Don McLean's "American Pie." **Killing Me Softly with His Song (1973)** : This song was a significant success, spending "four week number one in 73," and it "cleaned up at the 74 Grammys," winning both Record and Song of the Year. This made Roberta Flack the first artist ever to win both categories for two consecutive years.
