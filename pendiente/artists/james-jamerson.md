**Motown Bass Legend** : James Jamerson was a Motown legend and member of The Funk Brothers, known for playing the signature baseline on The Supremes' 1966 number one classic "You Can't Hurry Love." This baseline was widely "ripped off" and was particularly popular in 1982, influencing hits by The Jam, Phil Collins, and Hall and Oates' "Maneater."
