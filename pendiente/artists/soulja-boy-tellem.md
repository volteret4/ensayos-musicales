## songs
**Crank That (Soulja Boy) (2007)** : Released in 2007, this song became a number one hit and a YouTube Dance Craze. The video's explosion in popularity was instrumental, showcasing the combination of comedy and music for virality, a blueprint later adopted by Lil Nas X. Soulja Boy refers both to De André Wei's rap name and the name of his signature dance, a "bounce back plus Superman pose maneuver." ## curiosities
**Seven Weeks at Hot 100 #1** : "Crank That (Soulja Boy)" spent seven weeks atop the Hot 100 in 2007. Its success was fueled by weekly download sales that were roughly twice the size of D4L's downloads from two years prior.
