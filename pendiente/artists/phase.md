## curiosities
**Discovery by Devanté Swing (Mid 1990s)** : Phase, a girl group featuring Missy Elliott, was discovered by Jodeci's main songwriter Donald DeGrate (Devanté Swing) after he heard them sing following a Jodeci concert in Virginia Beach. Devanté Swing not only signed Phase but, at Missy's insistence, also signed their beatmaker, Tim Mosley (Timbaland).
