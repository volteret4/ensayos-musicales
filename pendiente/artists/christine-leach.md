# artist - Christine Leach

## curiosities
**Collaboration on Zephyr in the Sky** : As a niece of David Atkins, also known as Dave Curtis of Curtis Maldunor, Christine Leach borrowed her uncle's track "Sephiron" and collaborated with William Orbit to create "Zephyr in the Sky." She sang Curtis Maldunor's lyrics over Orbit's instrumental track, leaving the first verse virtually untouched.
