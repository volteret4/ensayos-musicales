## songs
**We Built This City on Sausage Roll (2018)** : This song unexpectedly won the UK Christmas number one for 2018, narrowly beating out Ariana Grande's "Thank U, Next" and Ava Max's "Sweet But Psycho" in a tight competition that was decided in the final couple of days before the holiday. The victory was announced on December 21, 2018. It is described as "kind of terrible, but kind of awesome" and fits into a British tradition of "random left field novelty cheesy" songs topping the charts at Christmas. ## curiosities
**Internet Pseudonym and Goal** : Mark E. Hoyle is known by his internet pseudonym, Lad Baby, and is from West Bridgeford, Nottinghamshire. He aimed to achieve a UK Christmas number one spot, intending to give it as a gift for his wife, whom he refers to online as Lad Baby Mum.
