# artist - Dave Lyons

## curiosities
**Co-creator of Yacht Rock Series** : Dave Lyons co-created the "Yacht Rock" Web TV series in 2005. He played the pivotal role of Coco Goldstein, the fictional mogul, and assembled his iconic costume, which later influenced the fashion of Yacht Rock tribute bands and audience members. "JD Riznar, Dave Lyons, and Hollywood Steve Huey co-created the Yacht Rock Web TV series back in 2005." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Yacht Or Nyacht | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Co-creator of Beyond Yacht Rock Podcast** : Dave Lyons is a co-creator of the "Beyond Yacht Rock" podcast, which further explored and defined the Yacht Rock genre through the "Yachtsky scale." He also contributed to the upcoming "ultimate, all-in-all book on Yacht Rock" by Little Brown UK. "Their follow-up series, the podcast Beyond Yacht Rock, coined other would-be genres, like Triumph Raps, Hard Oregon, Divorce Core, New Whop, and My Personal Favorite, Dystopian Future in which Rock and Roll has been outlawed." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bridge - Yacht Or Nyacht | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**All Music Guide Data Entry** : For about a year after college while living in Ann Arbor, Dave Lyons worked in data entry at the All Music Guide, where his job involved typing liner notes and track listings into the database. This experience led him to "obsess over liner notes" and gain a deep understanding of the prominent studio musicians of the era.
