## curiosities
**Lounge Act Success, No Hit Records** : Prior to their collaboration with Gamble and Huff, The Three Degrees were a popular lounge and nightclub act, celebrated for their sweet harmonies, but they had not yet achieved any significant hit records. "They had such sweet harmonies that they were popular as a Lounge Act or a Nightclub Act, but they had no real hit records" ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 37 - Philly Soul and the Sound of the 70s | https://seventies.libsyn.com/ep-37-philly-soul-and-the-sound-of-the-70s
**Sheila Ferguson's Initial Reaction to "When Will I See You Again"** : Sheila Ferguson initially perceived "When Will I See You Again" as an insulting and overly simplistic song when Kenny Gamble first presented it on the piano, though she later offered a sincere apology after it achieved massive commercial success.
