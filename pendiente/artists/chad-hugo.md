## curiosities
**Member of SBI (1991)** : Chad Hugo, a Filipino American friend of Pharrell Williams, was a member of the Virginia Beach rap crew/production team SBI (Surrounded by Idiots) during high school. **Early Mentorship by Teddy Riley (Early 1990s)** : After Teddy Riley moved to Virginia Beach, he allowed Chad Hugo and Pharrell Williams to spend time at his studio. Hugo, along with Williams, learned from Riley how to craft syncopated hooks and thumping beats, a foundational skill that would shape their future success as The Neptunes.
