**"Tiptoeing" Out of the Closet** : Frank Ocean was noted for "sort of tiptoeing out of the closet" as a member of the R&B and hip hop communities. His continued success, including achieving number one albums after revealing his sexuality, was seen as an encouraging sign for younger LGBTQ+ artists like Montero (Lil Nas X).
