# artist - The Simpsons

## curiosities
**Parody of Charity Mega-Singles (1992)** : A January 1992 episode titled "Radio Bart" featured a parody of benefit singles, "We're Sending Our Love Down the Well," with cameos from Sting and Crusty the Clown. Showrunner Mike Reese later revealed in a DVD commentary that this parody was inspired not just by "We Are the World," but specifically by "Voices that Care" and its "apparent meaninglessness" of pro-troops, late-to-the-war lyrics.
