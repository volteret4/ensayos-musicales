# artist - Scott Storch

## curiosities
**Target of Timbaland's "Give It to Me" (2007)** : Scott Storch was the target of Timbaland's 2007 number one hit "Give It To Me." Storch had "demanded co-writing credit for Timberlake's hit Cry Me a River," leading Timbaland to publicly call him "just the piano man" in the lyrics, escalating a professional dispute into a chart-topping diss.
