# artist - Shane Ward

## curiosities
**X-Factor Season 2 Winner (2005)** : Shane Ward was the winner of the second season of the British reality competition show, The X-Factor, which premiered in the fall of 2004. Ward's win in 2005 marked the beginning of a four-year streak where the X-Factor winner's debut single automatically secured the UK Christmas number one spot, a pattern intended by the show's creator, Simon Cowell.
