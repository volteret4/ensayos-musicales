# artist - Leo Sayer

## genres
- Disco

## songs
**You Make Me Feel Like Dancing (1976)** : A "perky chart-topper" that Richard Perry produced in 1976, showcasing his ability to adapt his production to the emerging disco era. **You Make Me Feel Like Dancing (1976)** : This chart-topping song is cited as an example of music that was distinctly *not* Yacht Rock, due to its disco genre. ## curiosities
**Intended Recipient of "He's So Shy"** : Songwriter Tom Snow initially wrote the song "He's So Shy" (originally "She's So Shy") with Leo Sayer in mind to perform it. "Tom Snow wrote it with Leo Sayer in mind." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Yes We Can Can Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
