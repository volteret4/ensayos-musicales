# artist - Andre Crouch Choir

## curiosities
**Featured on Madonna's "Like a Prayer" (1989)** : The famed Andre Crouch Choir was hired by Madonna to sing on her gospel-conceived song "Like a Prayer." They had previously sung on Michael Jackson's 1988 number one hit, "Man in the Mirror."
