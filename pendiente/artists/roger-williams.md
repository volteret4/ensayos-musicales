# artist - Roger Williams

## songs
**Autumn Leaves (1955)** : This instrumental, a novel arrangement of a song originally written with both French and English vocals, topped Billboard's Best Sellers Chart in October 1955. It marked the first instrumental number one of the nascent rock era, characterized by cascading keyboard runs designed to evoke falling foliage, and notably did not align with the emerging rock and roll sound.
