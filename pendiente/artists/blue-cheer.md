## songs
**Summertime Blues (1968)** : Described as the "first hit generally agreed to qualify as Heavy Metal," this was Blue Cheer's "turned up to 11" cover of Eddie Cochran's classic 50s song. It peaked at number 14 on the Hot 100 in May of 1968.
