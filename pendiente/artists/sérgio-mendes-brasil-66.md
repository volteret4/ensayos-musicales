## songs
**The Look of Love (1968)** : Sérgio Mendes & Brasil '66's cover of the Burt Bacharach/Hal David "cocktail classic," featuring vocalist Janis Hansen, hit the top five in 1968. Their music, described as "bossa Nova-flavored pop," was considered "closer to jazz than rock," illustrating the eclectic nature of 60s pop radio.
