## curiosities
**Neo-Soul Standard (Turn of the Millennium)** : D'Angelo was cited as an example of neo-soul, a style of "adult R&B" that was typical around the turn of the millennium. Darius Rucker's "Back to Then" was noted as not being D'Angelo-style, contributing to its genre ambiguity in the R&B market. "It was an adult R&B record, which around the turn of the millennium typically meant so-called neo-soul, such as D'Angelo, Erika Badu or Jill Scott." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > A Little Love and Some Tenderness Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
