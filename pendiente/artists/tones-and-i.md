**Dance Monkey (2020)** : This song by Australian singer-songwriter Toni Watson (Tones and I), a former street busker, broke into the Hot 100's Top 5 in February 2020, eventually peaking at number four. Globally, it was a massive hit in late 2019, reaching number one in over 30 countries, including an astounding 11 weeks at number one in the UK and a record 24 weeks at number one in her Australian homeland. "This is Dance Monkey, and it's by the quirky Australian singer and songwriter, Tony Watson, who goes by the name, tones and eye.
