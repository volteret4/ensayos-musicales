# artist - Bertha Franklin

## curiosities
**Hacienda Motel Manager in Cook's Death** : Bertha Franklin was the manager of the Hacienda Motel in Los Angeles who claimed she shot Sam Cook in self-defense on December 11, 1964. This account led to the closure of the case by LA authorities, but Cook's family and supporters have questioned the incident's circumstances ever since.
