# artist - Alan George

## songs
**Show Me Love (1990)** : Alan George co-wrote and produced the original 1990 version of Robin S's "Show Me Love" alongside Fred McFarlane. This initial version was not a hit and sounded distinctly different from the later popular remix. ## curiosities
**Songwriting Credit for "Break My Soul"** : In 2022, Alan George, along with Fred McFarlane, was awarded songwriting co-credit for Beyoncé's "Break My Soul." This credit was given for their original 1990 version of "Show Me Love," despite it sounding nothing like "Break My Soul" and the hit-making elements of "Show Me Love" originating from Stonebridge's remix.
