# artist - Celtic Woman

## curiosities
**Group Formed for PBS Debut** : The musical group Celtic Woman was specifically conceived and assembled by a producer with the primary purpose of debuting their act on a PBS pledge drive special. This illustrates how significant the pledge drive model became as a launchpad for new artists, to the extent that groups were created solely for this platform.
