# artist - The Dells

## curiosities
**Belated Rock and Roll Hall of Fame Induction (2004)** : The Dells were among the inductees at the March 2004 Rock and Roll Hall of Fame ceremony who had been eligible for the honor for about a decade or more before being belatedly inducted.
