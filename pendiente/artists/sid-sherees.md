# artist - Sid Sherees

## curiosities
**Cameo in "Alright" Music Video** : Sid Sherees, a pre-rock legend, made a cameo appearance in the music video for Janet Jackson's "Alright." The video itself recreated the look of a Busby Berkeley musical, incorporating these historical figures to enhance its old-school aesthetic.
