# artist - Sister Hazel

## genres
- Friends Rock

## songs
**All For You (1997)** : Sister Hazel scored a top 10 hit with this song in late 1997, identified as "very Hootie-esque" alt-pop. This indicated the continued chart presence and commercial viability of a sound similar to Hootie & the Blowfish even as Hootie's own commercial momentum waned.
