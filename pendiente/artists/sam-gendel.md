# artist - Sam Gendel

## instruments

## albums
**The Room (2024)** : This album is a beautiful collaboration with Brazilian guitarist Fabiano Do Nascimento. Unlike Gendel's usual approach of using many effects and tricks with his sax, on this record, he and Fabiano simply bring their instruments into a room to produce "beautiful, spare, highly vulnerable music."
