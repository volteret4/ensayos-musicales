**The Tears of a Clown (1967)** : Co-written by Stevie Wonder and Hank Cosby, with Smokey Robinson completing the lyrics, this song was originally considered an "interesting album closer" due to its circus-like melody and was buried at the end of Side 2 of their 1967 LP "Make It Happen." Three years later, after Smokey Robinson had left the group, Motown's British division released it in the UK, where it topped the charts in September 1970. Motown's home office then released it in America in October 1970, and it became The Miracles' first and only number one pop hit by December, ironically fronted by an already departed Smokey Robinson.
