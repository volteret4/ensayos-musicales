# artist - David Fincher

## curiosities
**Director for Madonna's "Express Yourself" Video (1989)** : David Fincher, who would become a prominent Hollywood director, directed the multi-million dollar music video for Madonna's "Express Yourself," which paid homage to Fritz Lang's "Metropolis."
