**Rich Men North of Richmond (2023)** : This acoustic folk-screed, performed on video by Christopher Anthony Lunsford (Oliver Anthony Music) with a "scraggly beard and a couple of snoozing pooches," became a sensation in the summer of 2023. It is described as an "incoate rant" that complains about both billionaires and welfare recipients. The "unadorned recording" caught fire as the 2024 presidential race began to heat up, with tens of millions of Americans buying and streaming the song, pushing it onto both the pop and country charts where it instantly materialized on top. Despite its initial success, it had "short coattails," lasting 20 weeks on the Hot 100. Two years later, in early June 2025, it received only one spin on US radio, though its lifetime Spotify total of 321 million streams is higher than some Mariah Carey hits. ## curiosities
**Happenstance Hit (2023)** : "Rich Men North of Richmond" by Oliver Anthony Music, whose performer had barely recorded before, was a "happenstance of a hit" that saw the first-ever chart hit by a man with a beard and sleeping dogs enter the Hot 100 at number one in the summer of 2023. Its unexpected success may have foreshadowed the results of the 2024 election.
