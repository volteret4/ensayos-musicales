# artist - Matt Cardell

## songs
**When We Collide (2010)** : "When We Collide" was Matt Cardell's X-Factor coronation song that achieved the UK Christmas number one in 2010. This win came the year after Rage Against the Machine had broken Simon Cowell's streak, demonstrating that the "X-Factor Christmas command" was only paused, not ended.
