## curiosities
**Neo Folk Presence on Modern Rock Chart (1988)** : British folk rock guitarist Joan Armatrading was among the neo-folk artists prominently featured on the inaugural Modern Rock Tracks chart when it launched in September 1988. This indicated the chart's diverse scope beyond postpunk music. "Neo Folk was all over the chart, including 10,000 maniacs.
