# artist - Bruce Channel

## instruments
- Harmonica

## songs
**Hey Baby (1962)** : This harmonica-fueled hit was a popular light pop single in 1962. John Lennon learned its harmonica intro from Channel's side-man, legendary blues instrumentalist Delbert McClinton, when Channel toured England in 1962, and subsequently adapted it for the Beatles' song "Love Me Do."
