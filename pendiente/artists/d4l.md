## songs
**Lafay Taffy (2005)** : This quirky and arguably simple track, originating from the Bankhead projects in Atlanta, exploded in popularity during the 2005 holiday season. In the first week of January 2006, it ejected Mariah Carey's "Don't Forget About Us" from the number one spot on the Hot 100, where it topped the chart for one week. The song was a prototypical example of Snap Music, an Atlanta-based offshoot of Crunk Music characterized by an 808-thump, high-hat, snapping, and chanting vocals, with a focus on simplicity. It sold a record 175,000 downloads in one week, largely fueled by 99-cent downloads from Apple's iTunes Music Store, which began counting for Billboard charts in 2005. Despite not being among radio's most played songs that week, the ubiquity of "Lafay Taffy" as a ringtone on Motorola and Nokia phones contributed to 3 million full-length downloads. It's considered the first Hot 100 number one hit powered by mobile virality, where short ringtone bursts compelled millions to buy the full song.
