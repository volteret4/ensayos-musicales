# artist - Maya

## songs
**Ghetto Superstar (That Is What You Are) (1998)** : Maya was teamed with Pras Michel and Wu Tang Clan rapper Ol' Dirty Bastard on this "irresistible single" from the soundtrack of the movie "Bullworth." The song, a hip hop anthem based on the Gibb brothers' "Islands in the Stream," topped charts around the world.
