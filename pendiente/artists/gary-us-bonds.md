## songs
**Quarter to Three (1961)** : This classic R&B song topped the Hot 100 way back in 1961, when Bruce Springsteen was ten years old. "Gary U.S. Bones, the classic R&B singer who had topped the Hot 100 way back in 1961 with quarter to three when Bruce was all of ten, recorded this little girl." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Tramps Like Us, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**This Little Girl (1981)** : Originally a *Darkness on the Edge of Town* outtake by Springsteen, it was deliberately refashioned by him to suit Gary U.S. Bonds and served as the lead single for Bonds' 1981 album *Dedication*. It peaked at number 11 on the Hot 100 in June 1981 and was noted for its potential to have fit easily on Springsteen's *The River*. "This little girl, the lead single, peaked at number 11 on the Hot 100 in June of 81." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Tramps Like Us, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
