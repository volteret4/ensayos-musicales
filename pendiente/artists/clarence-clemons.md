## curiosities
**Freeway of Love Sax Solo (1985)** : Bruce Springsteen's E Street Band saxophonist, Clarence Clemons, was hired by Narada Michael Walden to perform the sax solo on Aretha Franklin's "Freeway of Love" in his "inimitable style," creating a significant connection to Springsteen.
