# artist - Clarence Clemons

## member of
- Bruce Springsteen
- The E Street Band

## instruments

## songs
**You're a Friend of Mine (1986)** : This good-natured and "utterly schlocky" duet with Jackson Browne saw saxophonist Clarence Clemons not only playing his horn but also singing, and it achieved a "shockingly legit" number 18 on the Hot 100 in January 1986. Its success highlighted the significant influence of Bruce Springsteen's "coattails" in the mid-80s pop landscape. ## curiosities
**Freeway of Love Sax Solo (1985)** : Bruce Springsteen's E Street Band saxophonist, Clarence Clemons, was hired by Narada Michael Walden to perform the sax solo on Aretha Franklin's "Freeway of Love" in his "inimitable style," creating a significant connection to Springsteen.
