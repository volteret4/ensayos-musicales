## curiosities
**Early Band for Tom Petty** : Tom Petty spent half a decade in Mudcrutch, a Gainesville, Florida band, before forming The Heartbreakers. The band also featured future Heartbreakers Mike Campbell and Benmont Tench, with Petty serving as its primary songwriter. **Move to Los Angeles and Breakup** : Mudcrutch moved to Los Angeles, signed to LA-based Shelter Records, and released one single before breaking up in 1975. Shelter Records retained Petty due to his role as the band's primary songwriter.
