# artist - Freedom Williams

## member of
- C+C Music Factory

## songs
**Gonna Make You Sweat (Everybody Dance Now) (1990)** : Freedom Williams was the credited rapper on C+C Music Factory's 1990 hip-house jam, "Gonna Make You Sweat (Everybody Dance Now)," which topped the Hot 100 in February 1991. He performed all the verses of the song. "The full song credit on the charts read CNC music factory featuring Freedom Williams.
