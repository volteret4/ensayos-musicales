## songs
**Ghetto Superstar (That Is What You Are) (1998)** : One year after Wyclef Jean's hit, Pras Michel borrowed the Gibb's 1980s composition "Islands in the Stream," a song that Kenny Rogers and Dolly Parton took to number one, and transformed it into a hip hop anthem. Taken from the soundtrack of the Warren Beatty movie "Bulworth," "Ghetto Superstar" teamed Pras with singer Maya and Wu Tang Clan rapper Ol' Dirty Bastard, becoming an "irresistible single." It topped charts around the world, hitting the top 20 on the Hot 100, top 10 on the R&B chart, and reaching number one or two in a dozen other countries.
