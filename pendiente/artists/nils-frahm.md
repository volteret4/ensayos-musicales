## albums

## curiosities
**German Minimal Instrumental Scene** : Nils Frahm belongs to a new generation of minimal experimental instrumental artists in Germany, a scene also featuring Ólafur Arnalds, with whom Frahm has collaborated on several projects. While Arnalds might be more famous, Frahm's work is equally noteworthy and worth exploring.
