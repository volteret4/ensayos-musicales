# artist - Lambert, Hendricks & Ross

## members
- Annie Ross
- Dave Lambert

## curiosities
**Pioneers of Vocalese and Interracial Trio** : This 1950s vocal trio not only helped pioneer the intricate jazz singing style known as vocalese but was also notable for being a co-ed and interracial group, inspiring The Pointer Sisters.
