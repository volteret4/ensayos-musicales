# artist - Dababy

## songs
**Levitating (2020)** : Rapper DaBaby was featured on a remix of Dua Lipa's "Levitating," commissioned by Warner Bros. in late 2020 to get the song onto the charts. In 2021, while the song was in the top 10, DaBaby made "stupid homophobic comments." This led to his name being removed from the Billboard chart entry as radio and streaming shifted back to the original album cut, a unique instance of a remix being effectively "unremixed" due to controversy.
