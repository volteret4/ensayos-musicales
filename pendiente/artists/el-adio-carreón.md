## curiosities
**Bad Bunny's Discoverer** : El Adio Carreón, who is on Bad Bunny's record label Rima, is credited with discovering Bad Bunny. He knew Bad Bunny from college and introduced him to the label head at Rima, initiating the process that led to Bad Bunny's career.
