## songs
**Killing Me Softly With His Song** : Laurie Lieberman was a folk and soft rock singer who, after being "knocked out" by a performance by Don McLean at the Troubadour in Los Angeles, shared her impressions with songwriters Norman Gimbel and Charles Fox. They turned her feelings into the song "Killing Me Softly With His Song." Her version was not a hit, but it was heard by Roberta Flack, who later made it famous. "Laurie Lieberman was a folk and soft rock singer who teamed with a pair of male songwriters to produce her music.
