# artist - The Rappin' Duke

## curiosities
**John Wayne Imitator** : The Rappin' Duke was an artist who imitated John Wayne. His self-titled minor hit in 1985 became legendary enough on hip-hop radio to later be name-checked by The Notorious B.I.G. "To a John Wayne imitator, who called himself The Rappin Duke.
