## songs
**I Can't Wait (1986)** : This song by the white husband-and-wife duo from Portland, Oregon, was already in the R&B top 10 in the spring of 1986, weeks before it eventually reached number two on Hot Black Singles and subsequently number three on the Hot 100. This demonstrated how some white artists could break bigger and faster at R&B radio. **I Can't Wait (1986)** : This 1986 single was an "on trend cutting edge freestyle pop and beat" track, built with "pinging synthesizers a la Shannon and vocal chops a la Lisa Lisa." It first broke on Black Radio before crossing to Top 40, eventually reaching number three on the Hot 100 and number two on the R&B chart. Many listeners initially assumed the group was not white due to its sound. "I Can't Wait ultimately reached number three on the Hot 100 and number two on the R&B chart." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Point of No Return (1986)** : A "sound alike" follow-up single to "I Can't Wait," this track reached number 28 on the pop charts and number 36 on the R&B charts, maintaining Nu Shooz's presence with their freestyle sound. "Day and Smith followed it up quickly with the sound alike single Point of No Return, which made it to number 28 pop, number 36 R&B." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Crossover Identity and MTV Reveal** : In 1986, listeners initially mistook Nu Shooz, a husband-and-wife duo (Valerie Day and John Smith) from Portland, Oregon, for being non-white based on their freestyle sound. Their video on MTV later revealed their racial identity, showcasing freestyle's ability to "cross over effortlessly" across demographics. "Only when their video broke on MTV later that year did music fans learn they were a pair of white people from Portland, Oregon, a wife and husband named Valerie Day and John Smith." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Point of No Return Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
