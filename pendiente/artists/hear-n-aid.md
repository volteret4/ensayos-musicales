## songs
**Stars (1986)** : This single was recorded in May 1985 by a hard rock mega-group formed out of both compassion for famine relief and resentment over the exclusion of metal acts from Band-Aid and USA for Africa. The track featured eight solo vocalists, nine solo guitarists, and a chorus with over 40 hard rock "screamers," including members of Quiet Riot, Judas Priest, Dokken, and Queensrÿche, as well as Michael McKean and Harry Shearer from Neville Paradist's Spinal Tap in character. Due to contractual disputes among the artists' respective labels, the single went unreleased for over half a year. When it was finally issued in early 1986, it missed the Hot 100 entirely and only reached number 39 on Billboard's Album Rock Chart, by which time the Ethiopian famine had become a more complex issue. ## curiosities
**Motivation for Formation** : Hear 'n Aid was formed out of a dual motivation: compassion for the Ethiopian famine and resentment from members of the metal band Dio who noted that both Band-Aid and USA for Africa had "pointedly excluded metal acts." The project aimed to redress this snobbery.
