# artist - Janna Allen

## curiosities
**Co-writer for Hall and Oates** : Janna Allen, sister of Darryl Hall's companion Sarah Allen, was a budding songwriter who co-wrote "Kiss on My List" with Darryl Hall, coming up with its infectious chorus. She also co-wrote "Did It in a Minute."
