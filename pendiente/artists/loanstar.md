**Amazed (1999)** : Due to its crossover appeal, Loanstar re-recorded "Amazed" with pop-leaning instrumentation, removing elements like the pedal steel and other "twangy country instruments." Because this re-recording retained the same title, airplay for both versions contributed to a single chart position, helping Loanstar climb all the way to number 1 on the Hot 100 in March 2000. This strategy was a precedent for Corey Rooney and Sony's "I'm Real" approach.
