# artist - Barry Weiss

## curiosities
**Jive Records President** : Barry Weiss, the President of Jive Records, initially resisted releasing "Everybody (Backstreet's Back)" as a single and even kept it off the US album due to its incongruous nature for a debut. He only relented and added it to a 1998 reissue after Canadian and some US radio stations started playing it independently.
