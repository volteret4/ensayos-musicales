# artist - DJ Mustard

## curiosities
**Production of Kendrick Lamar's "Not Like Us" (2024)** : West Coast hitmaker DJ Mustard produced Kendrick Lamar's "Not Like Us." His production gave the track a pop music bounce, while still featuring "some of the most blazing bars Lamar had ever recorded," allowing the controversial diss track to achieve mass appeal.
