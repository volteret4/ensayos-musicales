## songs
**Flava in Ya Ear (1994)** : This track was a 1994 hit for the Bronx rapper, produced by Sean Combs (then known as Puff Daddy). By October 1994, the single had peaked at number 16 on the Hot 100 and number eight on the R&B chart. **Flava in Ya Ear (1994)** : Released just days after the original single seemed to peak, this remix, orchestrated by Puff Daddy, featured a "hip-hop army" including The Notorious B.I.G., L.L. Cool J, Grand Page, and Busta Rhymes, with Craig Mack nominally remaining the lead artist. The remix dramatically reversed the track's decline, sending it back up to new peaks of number four on the R&B chart and number nine on the pop chart in November 1994. The remix contributed to the track achieving platinum status, partly because it was the only way fans could own the track, as it would not appear on a Bad Boy album for a decade.
