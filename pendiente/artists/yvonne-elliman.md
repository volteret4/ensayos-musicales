# artist - Yvonne Elliman

## albums
**Jesus Christ Superstar (1970)** : Yvonne Elliman, a future rock and disco singer, sang the tender ballad "I Don't Know How to Love Him" for the Mary Magdalene character on the "Jesus Christ Superstar" concept album, which was released in 1970 and became the number one album in America by February 1971. "I don't know how to love him. ## songs
**I Don't Know How to Love Him (1971)** : Yvonne Elliman's version of this tender ballad from "Jesus Christ Superstar," released as a single, peaked on the Hot 100 in June 1971, though Helen Reddy's cover achieved a higher chart position. "Before Eleman's version was even issued as a single, an up and coming hitmaker named Helen Ready took her majestic cover onto the charts. In June of 1971, both versions, Reddy's and Eleman's peaked on the Hot 100 simultaneously." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Lullaby of Broadway Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**If I Can't Have You (1977)** : This song was the only one on the "Saturday Night Fever" soundtrack not performed by the Bee Gees, yet it became one of four number one hits from the soundtrack.
