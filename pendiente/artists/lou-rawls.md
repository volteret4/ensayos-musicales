## curiosities
**Friend and Touring Companion** : Lou Rawls was a friend and fellow touring singer of Sam Cook, who later recounted that Cook wrote "Chain Gang" after witnessing an actual chain gang. Rawls was also hospitalized alongside Cook after a severe car crash in 1958 and was among those who tried to comfort Cook after his son's tragic death. "Cook's friend and fellow touring singer Lou Rawls later said that Cook wrote the song after seeing an actual chain game, toiling in the hot sun by the road as they drove past." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Don’t Know Much About History, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
