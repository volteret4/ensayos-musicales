## songs
**Just the Two of Us (1981)** : This 1981 Yacht Soul ballad, a collaboration with veteran soul singer Bill Withers, was a No. 2 Pop hit and a No. 3 R&B hit. It served as a vital crossover success, demonstrating the importance of Yacht Soul for African American artists in bypassing Top 40 radio's post-disco bias.
