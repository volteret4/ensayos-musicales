## songs
**Axel F (2005)** : This instrumental theme, originally from the "Beverly Hills Cop" soundtrack, was reinterpreted by the animated character Crazy Frog, created by Hamba. This polyphonic ringtone became the best-selling ringtone of all time, reaching number one on charts worldwide. It marked a shift in ringtones from personal expression to a commercial spectacle, with accelerated remixes and supposedly humorous audio clips dominating the market. ## curiosities
**Global Phenomenon - Crazy Frog Character** : Crazy Frog was a computer-animated character created by the company Hamba. Initially accompanying an audio track imitating a motorcycle sound, it quickly gained global recognition when it was used for remixes of popular songs. This phenomenon exemplified the hyper-commercialization of ringtones, leading to accelerated remixes and novelty sounds. "Crazy Foxy convertió en un fenómeno mundial.
