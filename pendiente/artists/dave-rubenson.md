# artist - Dave Rubenson

## curiosities
**Producer for Pointer Sisters Debut LP** : Dave Rubenson produced The Pointer Sisters' debut LP and suggested they re-record Lee Dorsey's "Yes We Can," leading to their hit "Yes We Can Can."
