# artist - India.Arie

## curiosities
**Breakthrough on Second Stage** : As an "unsigned black artist" at the time, India.Arie was among those whose careers were helped by Lilith Fair's second stage. Her inclusion, despite the festival's overall diversity issues in its first year, offered a platform for emerging black artists.
