## songs
**Pop Goes the Weasel (1991)** : Led by white rappers MC Serch and Pete Nice, Third Bass released this satirical crossover pop jam in response to Vanilla Ice's "Ice Ice Baby." The track, built around samples of Peter Gabriel and The Hooh, "eviscerated Vanilla Ice" for his "hokey pop friendly production, sampling of Queen and David Bowie, and appropriative flow." Perhaps ironically, this satire became their only top 40 pop hit, peaking at number 29 in 1991. **Pop Goes the Weasel (1991)** : Released in the late summer of 1991, this single by Third Bass was a direct satirical attack on Vanilla Ice, featuring lyrics that "eviscerated" him as a "phony entertainer and a chef who can't cook." The accompanying video depicted MC Search and Pete Nice administering a beat down to a Vanilla Ice look-alike. The song itself sampled Peter Gabriel's "Sledgehammer" in a "copyright flouting" and "stupid obvious" manner, mirroring Vanilla Ice's own controversial sampling.
