## curiosities
**Later Backing for Elvis** : This girl group provided backing vocals for Elvis Presley later in his career, having previously backed Aretha Franklin. **Aretha Franklin's Backing Vocalists** : The Sweet Inspirations provided backing vocals for Aretha Franklin, notably on her fall 1967 single "You Make Me Feel Like A Natural Woman." Cissy Houston was a member of this group before embarking on her solo career, and they contributed to Franklin's distinct gospel-infused sound.
