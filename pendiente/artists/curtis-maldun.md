# artist - Curtis Maldun

## genres

## songs
**Sephiroth** : An early 70s song by this British folk duo, which William Orbit brought to the studio and which formed the backbone of Madonna's "Ray of Light" title track. Curtis Maldun were ultimately given songwriting credit on the track.
