## songs
**Pillowtalk (2016)** : This song was the first single released by Zayn Malik (transcribed as Zane Malik) after leaving One Direction and immediately debuted at number one, achieving something that had eluded the boy band: an American number one hit. By 2025, "Pillowtalk" was only played 44 times on US radio in a week. ## curiosities
**First One Direction Member to Solo Number One** : Zayn Malik (transcribed as Zane Malik) was the first member of the British boy band One Direction to go solo and achieve an American number one hit with his first single, "Pillowtalk," a feat his former band had never accomplished.
