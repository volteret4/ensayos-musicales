# artist - Newcastle Big Band

## members
- Gordon Sumner
- Jerry Richardson

## genres

## curiosities
**Sting's Early Band** : This jazz combo, named after Newcastle-on-Tyne (the nearest city to Sting's birthplace), was where a young Gordon Sumner played bass in the early 1970s. "This is the Newcastle Big Band, a jazz combo young Gordon Sumner played with in the early 70s.
