## albums
**Infinity on High (2007)** : Released as the eagerly anticipated follow-up to "From Under the Cork Tree" (referred to as "the court tree out" in the transcript), this album kicked off with a track titled "Thriller," named after Michael Jackson's blockbuster album. It notably featured their then-label boss, rapper Jay-Z, who opened the track and the album "like a hype man." ## songs
**Centuries (2014)** : A top 10 hit for Fallout Boy, this song gained recognition for its interpolation of Suzanne Vega's "Tom's Diner." As of the transcript's context, "Centuries" held the distinction of being Fallout Boy's most popular song on Spotify. "2014's centuries a top 10 hit built out of an interpolation of susan vegas tom's diner it remains fallout boys most popular song on spotify." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Write Sins, Not Tragedies, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**My Songs Know What You Did in the Dark (Light Em Up) (2013)** : This track was one of Fallout Boy's significant radio hits in the 2010s, marking a period where the band's sound began to evolve away from purely punk influences while maintaining their status as hitmakers. "they scored big radio hits with 2013's impressively titled my songs know what you did in the dark light them up." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Write Sins, Not Tragedies, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Thanks for the Memories (2007)** : Released as a follow-up to "This Ain't a Scene, It's an Arms Race," this song proved more enduring on the charts. It spent approximately two months longer on the Hot 100 than its predecessor, even though it peaked at number eleven by 2007. "arms race was a flashy hit but not as enduring as fallout boys follow up thanks for the memories spent about two months longer on the hot one hundred even though it only peaked at number eleven by 2007." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Write Sins, Not Tragedies, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**This Ain't a Scene, It's an Arms Race (2007)** : The first single from "Infinity on High," this track was described as "equally imperial" and featured its main hook structured as a "crowd chant" to emphasize the band's perceived dominance. It debuted at number two on the Hot 100, propelled by over 160,000 first-week downloads on iTunes, but was prevented from reaching number one by Beyoncé's "Irreplaceable." "infinity on highs first single this ain't a scene it's an arms race was equally imperial fallout boy feeling their dominance by structuring the main hook as a crowd champ." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Write Sins, Not Tragedies, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Green Day Hall of Fame Induction Presenters (2015)** : Fallout Boy had the honor of presenting Green Day for their induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2015. Members Patrick Stump and Pete Wentz spoke at the podium, acknowledging Green Day as their heroes and predecessors in the punk-to-pop crossover landscape. "presenting them for induction was none other than fallout boy the band who in terms of punk to pop crossover had in essence succeeded them both." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Write Sins, Not Tragedies, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Mistaken for Green Day** : Pete Wentz shared an anecdote about the public often mistaking Fallout Boy for Green Day, stating that about 80% of the time, after fans take pictures with them in an airport, they walk off exclaiming, "holy shit I just got a picture with fucking Green Day." This highlights the perceived connection between the two bands in popular culture. "about 80% of the time when someone takes a picture with us you you hear them walk off and they're like holy shit I just got a picture with fucking green day." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > I Write Sins, Not Tragedies, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Hella Mega Festival Tour (2021)** : In 2021, following the reopening of concerts after the first wave of the COVID-19 pandemic, Fallout Boy joined Green Day and Weezer on the touring Hella Mega Festival. While the bands had not yet shared the stage during the tour, it was scheduled to continue through the following July.
