## songs
**Lazy Sunday (2005)** : This rap song about two New York City residents' plans to cure a weekend hangover and watch a Narnia movie was performed by SNL cast members Chris Parnell and Andy Samberg. It instantly went viral after airing on a pre-Christmas episode of SNL in December 2005, with dozens of bootleg copies uploaded to YouTube. The millions of views it garnered on YouTube and early social media sites proved that professionally produced, shareable video content like music videos was ideal for YouTube's platform.
