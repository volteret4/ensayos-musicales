## curiosities
**Lyrical Reference to Atlanta Killings** : In the song "Aquemini," Andre Benjamin's lyrics describe meeting on a bridge in Atlanta and envisioning the ghosts of Black children who died in the Atlanta killings of the late 1970s and early 1980s, a period that coincided with his own childhood.
