# artist - Tag Team

## songs
**Hoomp There It Is (1993)** : This single, released in 1993, featured Miami Bass style production and competed directly on the Hot 100 with 95 South's "Hoot There It Is." Although from Atlanta, their pop-friendly hit sounded distinctly like Miami Bass and reached number 2 on the Hot 100, making them one of Atlanta's best-selling rap acts at the time.
