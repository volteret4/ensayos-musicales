**"Unremixing" Chart Incident (2021)** : Dua Lipa's "Levitating" experienced a unique chart drama in 2021. After its remix with DaBaby helped it reach the top 10, DaBaby made homophobic comments, causing radio and streaming platforms to pivot back to the original album cut. This compelled Billboard to remove DaBaby's name from the chart entry, making it a rare instance of a smash remix "unremixing itself" on its way up the charts. Billboard later named "Levitating" the number one song of the year, highlighting the full circle of the remix's impact.
