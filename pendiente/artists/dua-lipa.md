# artist - Dua Lipa

## songs
**Levitating (2021)** : This song reached number two on the charts but achieved an upset win as the top song of 2021 through sheer persistence, spending 41 weeks in the Top 10, 7 of those weeks as the top song at radio, and a total of 77 weeks on the Hot 100. Its "triple time lyrics and bouncy groove" were boosted by TikTok, where video makers widely adopted the song. It defeated hits by The Weeknd, Olivia Rodrigo, and Doja Cat for the year-end title.
