# artist - Tony Tribe

## genres
- Reggae
- Rocksteady

## songs
**Red Red Wine (1969)** : Tony Tribe's version, a cover of Neil Diamond's song, was recorded and released in 1969. Tribe retained the fundamental sadness but brought it up to tempo, transforming it into a "drinking your cares away record." This rendition was a minor chart success in England, peaking at number 46 in the UK, and was the version that influenced UB40. ## curiosities
**Influence on UB40 (1960s)** : Tony Tribe's 1969 version of "Red Red Wine" was the only one known to the members of UB40 as they grew up in Birmingham, influencing their own cover. They were unaware of Neil Diamond's original.
