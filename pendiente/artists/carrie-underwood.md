## songs
**Inside Your Heaven (2005)** : As the winner of "American Idol's" fourth season in 2005, Carrie Underwood's coronation ballad, "Inside Your Heaven," debuted at number one. Despite few radio stations playing the song initially, its "huge sales" from her fanbase ensured its instant chart-topping success, following the established "American Idol" pattern. ## curiosities
**Successful Post-Idol Career** : Carrie Underwood went on to have a "hugely successful career as a frequent country hitmaker and occasional pop hitmaker" after her "American Idol" victory. Like Kelly Clarkson, her later hits had to "climb the charts the old fashioned way" after the initial Idol-driven number one debut wave.
