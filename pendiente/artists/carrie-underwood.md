## songs
**Before He Cheats (2006)** : This "sharp-tongued revenge song" was Carrie Underwood's third single and a "well-timed breakthrough." After a long run on the country charts in 2006, culminating in a month at number one, it gradually crossed to pop radio by spring 2007. It reached number eight on the Hot 100 in its original version, without any pop remix, signaling to Taylor Swift's team that pop crossover was possible without losing a country audience. **Inside Your Heaven (2005)** : As the winner of "American Idol's" fourth season in 2005, Carrie Underwood's coronation ballad, "Inside Your Heaven," debuted at number one. Despite few radio stations playing the song initially, its "huge sales" from her fanbase ensured its instant chart-topping success, following the established "American Idol" pattern.
