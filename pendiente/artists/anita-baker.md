# artist - Anita Baker

## curiosities
**Top Five R&B Hit (1989)** : Anita Baker had a top five hit on the R&B chart in March 1989, sharing the top five with Levert and Millie Vanille's "Girl You Know It's True," which peaked at number three. This demonstrates her consistent presence as a major R&B artist.
