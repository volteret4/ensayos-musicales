## songs
**Apache (1973)** : This 1973 funk version of Cliff Richard and the Shadows' 1960 instrumental "Apache" was a significant intermediary cover. It reinterpreted the rock original into a funk track before The Sugar Hill Gang adapted it into their popular party record in 1981, showcasing its cross-genre evolution.
