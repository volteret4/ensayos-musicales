## songs
**I Feel For You (1984)** : Meli Mel provided the looped rap "Shaka Khan let me rock you, let me rock you Shaka Khan" at the start of Chaka Khan's 1984 number three pop and number one R&B smash. Despite his crucial contribution, he was not name-checked in the credits, consistent with 1984 standards. **The Message (1982)** : Meli Mel was the prominent lead rapper on Grandmaster Flash and the Furious Five's classic 1982 single "The Message," and was specifically credited on the single's label. This was an early instance of a rapper receiving explicit credit. **White Lines (1983)** : Meli Mel was "coming off his classic 1983 hit White Lines" when he agreed to contribute a "bespoke rap" honoring Chaka Khan for her 1984 remake of "I Feel For You." This track established his reputation as a top rapper. ## curiosities
**Contribution to "I Feel For You" Cover (1984)** : Rapper Meli Mel, the MC side man of rap godfather Grandmaster Flash, led the reinvention of Prince's "I Feel For You" on Chaka Khan's 1984 cover, contributing to its seminal R&B hip-hop hybrid sound. "Reinvented in late 1984 by former Rufus singer, Chakakon, I feel for you was reimagined into a seminal R&B hip-hop hybrid led by rapper Meli Mel, the MC side man of rap godfather, Grandmaster Flash." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - Le Petty Prince Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
