# artist - Ofra Haza

## curiosities
**Sampled in "Paid in Full" Remix** : The Israeli singer Ofra Haza was one of the artists whose music was sampled via "needle drops" in Coldcut's legendary "Seven Minutes of Madness mix" of Eric B. and Rock him's "Paid in Full." Her inclusion showcased the diverse sonic palette being incorporated into hip-hop and dance remixes of the era.
