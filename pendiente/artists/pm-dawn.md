# artist - P.M. Dawn

## songs
**Set Adrift on Memory Bliss (1991)** : P.M. Dawn's 1991 number one smash, which effectively transformed Spandau Ballet's 1983 ballad "True" and elevated it to greater success. Spandau Ballet's lead singer, Tony Hadley, publicly endorsed this reboot by appearing in P.M. Dawn's music video.
