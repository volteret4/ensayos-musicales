## curiosities
**Melodic Rap Controversy** : PM Dawn was a melodic rap troupe that routinely sang on their hits, a style that provoked controversy among hip-hop purists like KRS-One. This led to KRS-One famously "bum rushing" PM Dawn's Prince Be at a live concert, an event portrayed by the music press as a defense against perceived "softness" or non-masculine elements in rap. "KRS1 got into his own beef with the melodic rap troupe PM Dawn, who routinely sang on their hits." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say My Name, Say My Name, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Early Sound Comparison to De La Soul** : Before signing with a label, PM Dawn (then Atrell and Jarrett Cordes) were told by executives at Tommy Boy Records that they sounded too much like the label's flagship act, De La Soul.
