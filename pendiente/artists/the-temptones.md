## songs
**Girl I Love You (1967)** : This 1967 single was sung by Darryl Hall and credited to his group, The Temp Tones. Hall explained that the group's name was a direct homage to The Temptations, whom they considered "the gods" and the best vocal group globally at the time. "This 1967 single is Girl I Love You sung by Darryl Hall.
