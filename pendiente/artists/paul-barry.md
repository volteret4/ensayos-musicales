# artist - Paul Barry

## curiosities
**Co-Writer of "Bailamos"** : Paul Barry, an Englishman, co-wrote Enrique Iglesias's number one hit "Bailamos." He later admitted that the emphasis on the Spanish title was incorrect ("Bailamos" instead of "Bailamos") because he did not speak fluent Spanish, highlighting the song's deliberate, yet not always rigorously authentic, blend of languages for crossover appeal.
