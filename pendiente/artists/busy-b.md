# artist - Busy B

## curiosities
**First Known Rap Beef Involvement (1981)** : Busy B was involved in the first known rap beef during a live performance at the Harlem World Club in 1981. He had been "taking pot shots at other rappers rhyming skills," which directly led to Cool Mo Dee of Treacherous 3 freestyling disses against him immediately after his set.
