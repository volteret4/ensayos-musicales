# artist - Buckingham Nicks

## albums
**Buckingham Nicks (1973)** : This album was recorded in Sound City Studios the year before Mick Fleetwood discovered Lindsey Buckingham and Stevie Nicks. Despite being recorded with "the cream of LA session players," the album "went nowhere," failing to chart or generate radio hits, though it contained sturdy songs like Nicks-penned "Crying in the Night."
