**Let's Hear It for the Boy (1984)** : This exuberant song became Deniece Williams's first solo number one smash and her second Hot 100 chart-topper overall. It was a last-minute addition to the "Footloose" soundtrack, written by Dean Pitchford, and soundtracked one of the movie's most popular scenes: Kevin Bacon teaching Chris Penn to dance. Despite lyrics that could be seen as "a bit insulting to the narrator's boy," Williams's joyful delivery made the song a hit. It was also one of five number one hits nominated for the Best Original Song Oscar in 1984. **Too Much, Too Little, Too Late (1978)** : This duet with Johnny Mathis became Deniece Williams's first Hot 100 chart-topper, reaching number one six years prior to her solo hit "Let's Hear It for the Boy."
