## songs
**Saturday (1978)** : An "instant smash" and a number 15 R&B hit, "Saturday" was the lead single from Norma Jean Wright's 1978 debut solo album, *Norma Jean*. Produced by Bernard Edwards and Nile Rogers, the song seamlessly extended the sophisticated musical brand associated with Sheik. ## curiosities
**Lead Vocalist on Sheik's First Recording** : Norma Jean Wright served as the lead vocalist on Sheik's very first recording, "Everybody Dance." She later departed the group to launch her solo career, leading to Alfa Anderson assuming primary vocal duties for Sheik.
