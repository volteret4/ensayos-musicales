## songs
**The Chipmunks Song (1958)** : This single was a blockbuster during the 1958 holiday season, becoming the fastest-selling vinyl 45 of the year by shifting 2.5 million copies in under a month. It spent a month at number one on the brand new Hot 100, which had launched just four months earlier. The song's success was largely due to the "sped up vocal records" fad of 1958, and it's not particularly "Christmasy" as it lacks sleigh bells or snow, focusing instead on the anticipation of receiving presents like a hula hoop.
