# artist - The Mothers of Invention

## curiosities
**Studio Collaboration with Johnny Otis** : The Mothers of Invention were conducted in the studio by Johnny Otis for a few songs, including "The One." Frank Zappa, who was in the control room, sought Otis's help to achieve a "greasy 50s sound" for these recordings. "Otis also conducted the mothers of invention in the studio and a few songs, while Zapper was in the control room, helping him get the greasy 50s sound he wanted, on songs like the one." ← REUPLOAD Episode 71： ＂Willie and the Hand Jive＂ by Johnny Otis | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ez6BCUlI6Rg
