# artist - John Phillips

## curiosities
**Writer of San Francisco (Be Sure to Wear Flowers in Your Hair)** : John Phillips, a member of The Mamas and the Poppas, wrote the song "San Francisco (Be Sure to Wear Flowers in Your Hair)." He did so at the behest of producer Lou Adler, specifically to promote the 1967 Monterey Pop Festival that they were organizing.
