**Barbara Streisand (2010)** : This single reached the top of Billboard's Club Songs chart in 2010. Its title was derived from its only intelligible lyrics, which consisted of just two words: "Barbara Streisand." The actual Barbara Streisand later expressed that she was "flattered by the club song bearing her name."
