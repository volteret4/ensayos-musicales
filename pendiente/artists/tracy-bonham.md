## songs
**Mother Mother (1996)** : This "rageful rocker" achieved an unusual chart distinction: after spending three weeks at number one on Modern Rock Tracks in the spring of 1996, it became the last alternative chart-topper by a solo woman artist for the next 17 years. This marked a significant turning point in alternative radio programming. "Mother mother held an unusual chart distinction that only became clear years later.
