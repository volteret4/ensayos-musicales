# artist - Yoko Onas

## curiosities
**Co-organizer of The Peace Choir Single (1991)** : Yoko Onas co-organized the 1991 all-star single "Give Peace a Chance," credited to The Peace Choir. This initiative brought together over 40 eclectic acts, from MC Hammer to Axel Rose, for an anti-war statement during the Persian Gulf War.
