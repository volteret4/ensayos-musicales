**You Make Me Feel (Mighty Real) (1978)** : This high-energy dance classic and queer landmark was the flip side of the "Dance (Disco Heat)" single, considered the A-side in clubs and on the Disco chart, establishing Sylvester's legend. **Unambiguous Gay Pride Icon** : Sylvester occupied an unambiguous position on the disco spectrum, proudly and openly gay in the 1970s without costume or parody. Despite growing up with a Pentecostal Church mother who did not accept gayness, he left home at 15 to live with his grandmother due to his mother's non-acceptance, refusing to change who he was. "there is nothing ambiguous about Sylvester. He was not in costume. He was not a parody of a gay man.
