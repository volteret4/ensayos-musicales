**Disco Duck (1976)** : This novelty song by Memphis radio DJ Rick Dees topped the Billboard Hot 100 for 10 weeks in October 1976. Although it was included in the film *Saturday Night Fever* (1977), Dees's management made the decision not to allow it on the movie's soundtrack, fearing it would compete with his own sales, a decision later regarded as a "mistake."
