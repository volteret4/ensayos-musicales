## curiosities
**Connection to Whitney Houston's "I Will Always Love You"** : Kevin Costner is associated with Whitney Houston's legendary cover of "I Will Always Love You" due to their co-starring roles in *The Bodyguard*. For many, his involvement is how they were first introduced to the song.
