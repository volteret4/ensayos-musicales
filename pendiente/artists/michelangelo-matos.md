**"The Underground is Massive" Book Author** : Michelangelo Matos authored "The Underground is Massive," an authoritative book on electronic music. In it, he explains that the term "EDM" appealed to scholars and critics because it lacked specific stylistic connotations like house or techno, and it avoided evoking the "late 90s major label Gold Rush" associated with "Electronica."
