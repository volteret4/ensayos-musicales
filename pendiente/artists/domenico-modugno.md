**Volare (1958)** : This song, performed by "Eurovision Crooner" Domenico Modugno, achieved significant recognition by winning both Record of the Year and Song of the Year at the inaugural Grammy Awards in 1959. It was also Billboard's number one year-end hit of 1958, demonstrating its mass popularity despite the Grammys' stated anti-popularity credo. **Volare (Nel blu dipinto di blu) (1958)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1958. As an Italian-language song by actor-singer Domenico Modugno, its popularity in America in 1958 was remarkable, especially when even British pop was considered exotic. It won the first-ever Grammys for Record of the Year and Song of the Year and placed third in that year's Eurovision Song Contest. The "hammy pop show song about flying through a sky of blue" has endured through covers and film appearances.
