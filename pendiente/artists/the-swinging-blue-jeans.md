# artist - The Swinging Blue Jeans

## songs
**Hippy Hippy Shake (1964)** : This cover of the Chan Romero rock and roll rave-up was in the top 40 and peaked at number 24 on the Hot 100, the same week The Dave Clark 5's "Glad All Over" reached its peak.
