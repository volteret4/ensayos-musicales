## songs
**Rappo Clapo (1979)** : On this one-off single, Latin Boogaloo and Salsa vocalist Joe Bataan created a "clap your hands" invocation that became a timeless rap trope. These early rap experiments were extensions of the call-and-response music he had been making for years, contributing to the nascent form of rap before its official recorded hits.
