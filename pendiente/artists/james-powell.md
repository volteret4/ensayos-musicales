## songs
**Beverly Angel (1959)** : This Do-Wop track was a minor regional hit in 1959. James Powell, a singer from Northern California, hired The Blue Velvets (CCR's precursor band) to back him on the recording session for this single, marking the first record appearance for John Fogarty and his bandmates.
