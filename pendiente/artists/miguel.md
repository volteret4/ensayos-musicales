**Sure Thing (2011)** : This song topped the R&B chart in May 2011 and briefly crossed over to the pop charts, reaching number 36. A decade later, it experienced a resurgence when TikTok users began sharing sped-up versions in their videos. This social activity led to its return to the Hot 100 in 2022 and its transformation into a pop radio smash (though played at normal speed on radio). By May 2023, "Sure Thing" reached a new Hot 100 peak of number 11 and became the most played song at Top 40 radio, hitting number 1 on Billboard's Pop Airplay chart, thus crossing Miguel over to a new audience.
