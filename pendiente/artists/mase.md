## songs
**Can't Nobody Hold Me Down (1997)** : Ma$e and Puff Daddy's 1997 hit, a composite of two earlier songs, which borrowed its title from Matthew Wilder's 1984 hit "Break My Stride" and sampled Grandmaster Flash & The Furious Five's "The Message." This "hip hop Frankenstein's monster" spent six weeks at number one on the charts. **Lookin' at Me (1997)** : This track, produced by The Neptunes, further developed their signature sound through its minor key piano and synth lines. It was the final single from Mase's multi-platinum "Harlem World" album, peaking at number 8 on both the pop and R&B charts in the summer of 1998, and marking The Neptunes' first official top 10 hit as frontline producers. "Rapper Macy's track, Looking at Me. **Mo Money Mo Problems (1997)** : Mase, a rapper from the Bad Boy label, performed the first two verses of this song alongside Sean "Puff Daddy" Combs, before The Notorious B.I.G.'s appearance.
