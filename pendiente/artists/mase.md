# artist - Mase

## songs
**Can't Nobody Hold Me Down (1997)** : Ma$e and Puff Daddy's 1997 hit, a composite of two earlier songs, which borrowed its title from Matthew Wilder's 1984 hit "Break My Stride" and sampled Grandmaster Flash & The Furious Five's "The Message." This "hip hop Frankenstein's monster" spent six weeks at number one on the charts.
