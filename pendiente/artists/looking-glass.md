# artist - Looking Glass

## songs
**Brandy (You're a Fine Girl)** : This song is cited as an example of music people mistakenly identified as Yacht Rock by simply "putting on a captain's hat and listen[ing] to Brandy by looking glass." This illustrates the literal interpretation of the genre's name that the creators aimed to correct.
