**Jam on It (1984)** : This "zany electrophunk classic" from the Brooklyn rap troupe Nucleus became a national hit in the summer of 1984, reaching number 56 on the pop chart and number 9 on the R&B chart. Its success was particularly notable as a rap single on an independent label, and it was "as huge on the radio that summer as anything by Madonna or Durandra."
