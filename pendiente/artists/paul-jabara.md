# artist - Paul Jabara

## songs
**It's Raining Men (1982)** : This song was co-composed by Paul Jabara along with Paul Schaeffer. It achieved success as a club smash and a gay anthem, performed by The Weather Girls. ## curiosities
**Oscar-Winning Songwriter** : Jabara was the primary writer of Donna Summer's hit "Last Dance," for which he won an Oscar in 1979. His recognition was largely due to the song's remarkable structure and Summer's powerful vocal performance.
