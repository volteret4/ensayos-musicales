# artist - Dallas Frazier

## songs
**Ali Oop** : Frazier, a country songwriter, wrote this goofy homage to the prehistoric caveman character from the comic strip, which later became a number one hit for The Hollywood Argyles. **Elvira** : Dallas Frazier wrote the song "Elvira," which was famously covered by The Oak Ridge Boys. Their version became a kitschy pop hit, reaching number five on the Hot 100 in the summer of 1981.
