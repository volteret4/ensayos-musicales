# artist - Queen Latifah

## songs
**Ladies First (1989)** : One of Queen Latifah's early hits, this song demonstrated her versatility as an emcee. She rapped with speed and lyricism comparable to her male peers while also deploying melodic hooks, establishing her as a multifaceted artist in rap's Golden Age. ## curiosities
**Platinum Level Rapper** : Queen Latifah is noted as one of the "platinum level women rappers" who contributed to the strength of female hip hop throughout the 1990s. Lilith organizers struggled to attract her interest in the first year, but she eventually signed on as a main stage act in Year Two (1998) of Lilith Fair, helping to diversify the festival's lineup. "Queen Latifa, Salt and Peppin, Debrat, Lisa left eye Lopez, there was a wealth of platinum level women rappers in the 90s." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Building a Herstory Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Versatile Female Emcee** : As the highest-profile solo woman emcee of rap's Golden Age, Queen Latifah was eager to showcase her versatility from very early on. On hits like "Ladies First" (1989) and "U.N.I.T.Y." (1993), she demonstrated the ability to rap with the speed and lyricism of her male peers while also adeptly deploying melodic hooks, sometimes even handling the melodies by herself. "When Latifa was the highest profile solo woman emcee of rap's Golden Age, from very early on on her hits like 1989's Ladies First Latifa was eager to show she could do it all, rapping with the speed and lyricism of her male peers, but also.
