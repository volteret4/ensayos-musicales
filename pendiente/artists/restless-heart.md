# artist - Restless Heart

## songs
**I'll Still Be Loving You (1987)** : Nashville band Restless Heart crossed over to the Hot 100 in 1987 with their stately ballad "I'll Still Be Loving You." This was a rare occurrence at the time, being the only record of its kind to make pop radio, and the industry in Nashville was stunned by its modest peak at number 33 on the Hot 100. Nashville's music industry was reportedly stunned by this event, even though the song only peaked modestly at number 33 on the Hot 100.
