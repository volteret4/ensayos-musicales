## songs
**I Heard It Through the Grapevine (1967)** : Gladys Knight and the Pips' "rip-roaring recording" of "I Heard It Through the Grapevine" was the first version to be issued as a single during their brief stint on the Motown label. It hit number two on the Hot 100 in late 1967. "It was by Gladys Knight and the Pips during their brief stint on the Motown label. The Pips rip-waring recording hit number two on the Hot 100 in late 1967." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Gotcha Covered Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**The Way We Were / Try to Remember (1975)** : In 1975, this live medley of Barbara Streisand's "The Way We Were" and the showtune "Try to Remember" was taken to number 11 on the Hot 100 and number 6 on the R&B chart by Gladys Knight and the Pips. "In 1975, the great Gladys Knight and the Pips took alive medley of the way we were plus the showtune tried to remember to number 11 on the Hot 100 and number 6 on the R&B chart." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hello, Gorgeous Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
