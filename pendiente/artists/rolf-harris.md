# artist - Rolf Harris

## songs
**Tie Me Kangaroo Down, Sport (1963)** : This song was a number three US hit in 1963 and was regarded as a culturally specific novelty hit. It was considered a "borderline offensive diddy" that used Australian stereotypes for the amusement of non-Australians.
