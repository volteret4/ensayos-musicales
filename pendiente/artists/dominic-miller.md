# artist - Dominic Miller

## member of

## instruments

## songs
**Shape of My Heart (1993)** : Dominic Miller created the "entrancing guitar riff" that powers Sting's song "Shape of My Heart." Sting only added lyrics to this riff, resulting in Miller sharing songwriting and publishing credits with Sting for the track. Given the song's extensive sampling, including by Juice Wrld for "Lucid Dreams," these samples are worth millions, potentially benefiting Miller's descendants.
