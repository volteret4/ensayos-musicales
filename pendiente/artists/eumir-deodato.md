**Also Sprach Zarathustra (1973)** : This progressive funk cover of a Richard Strauss composition, arranged by avant-garde jazz artist Eumir Deodato, reached number two in 1973. Although "Zarathustra" gained fame in the late 1960s as the theme to Stanley Kubrick's film "2001, A Space Odyssey," Deodato's successful arrangement was entirely independent of the movie.
