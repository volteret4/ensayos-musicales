## curiosities
**Example of Modern Electro-Pop** : Tio Cruz's hits were cited as examples of the modern electro-pop sound prevalent in the early 2010s, which was largely electronic rather than acoustic. His chart success contributed to the production style Taylor Swift's evolving music began to demand, with many of these hits produced or co-written by Max Martin.
