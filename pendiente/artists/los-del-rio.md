## songs
**Macarena (1996)** : This song was the winner of the 1996 Billboard Song of the Summer. It is described as a "bilingual novelty hit" that spent a staggering 14 weeks at number one, with approximately half of those weeks accumulated between late July and Labor Day. **Macarena (1996)** : This song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1996. Its existence is described as a "series of flukes," and it proved a key moment for both Spanish language pop and one-hit wonders. While the original song is fun, the Bayside Boys remix is noted for its "annoying" and "tacky English lyrics." The song's success was instrumental in getting Americans to embrace bilingual pop decades before "Despacito." ## curiosities
**Macarena's Impact on Bilingual Pop (1996)** : The "Macarena (Bayside Boys Remix)" was a "key moment for both Spanish language pop and for one hit wonders in general," introducing Americans to bilingual pop decades before "Despacito." Despite the "tacky English lyrics" of the remix, its overall effect was positive in fostering this cultural shift.
