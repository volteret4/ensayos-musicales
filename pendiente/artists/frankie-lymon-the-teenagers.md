## curiosities

**First True Boy Band of the Rock Era** : Generally considered the first true boy band as the term is understood today, forming when the word "teenager" was still new to the lexicon. They were a multi-racial group. "In the rock era, generally the first true boy band, as the term is understood today, was a do-wop group that literally called themselves the teenagers." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Want It That Way Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Frankie Lymon's Youth and Tragic Death** : Frankie Lymon was a gifted black vocalist who fronted the group and was only 13 years old when he was singing on hit records. Their hit-making days ended by 1960, and Lymon, unable to cope with aging out of his boyhood fame, died of a drug overdose in 1968 at the age of 25.
