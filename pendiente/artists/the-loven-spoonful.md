## songs
**Summer in the City (1966)** : This song, all about hot weather and urban ferment, entered Billboard's Hot 100 in July 1966 and climbed rapidly, reaching number one by August of that year. It notably evoked the national mood during a summer marked by protest and cultural upheaval. Despite its appropriate title and chart success, Billboard magazine decades later determined "Wild Thing" by The Troggs was the actual Song of the Summer for 1966.
