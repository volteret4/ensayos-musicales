# artist - Al Green

## curiosities
**Donation to Jackie Wilson's Medical Fund** : Al Green, the singer, performed at a benefit concert for Jackie Wilson and donated $10,000 towards his medical bills. "Al Green, the singer, not Wilson's ex-mayager, performed at the concert and gave $10,000." ← Episode Sixty-Four： ＂Reet Petite＂ by Jackie Wilson | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYU-H4NvnmE
**Turned Down "Pillow Talk"** : Al Green rejected "Pillow Talk," a song written for him by Sylvia Robinson, because its "blatant sexuality" conflicted with his "newfound religion." Sylvia later had a hit with her own version of the song. "Pillow Talk that she'd written for All Green, but which he turned down due to its blatant sexuality, conflicting with his newfound religion." ← Episode 49： ＂Love is Strange＂ by Mickey and Sylvia | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FkyLPVvPbcI
