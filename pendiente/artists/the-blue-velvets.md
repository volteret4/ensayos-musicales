## curiosities
**Formation and Early Work** : The band started as The Blue Velvets in El Serito, California, with John Fogarty, Doug Clifford, and Stu Cook meeting in junior high school and jamming by high school. By 1959, they were tight enough to be hired by singer James Powell to back him on his Do-Wop single "Beverly Angel," which was a minor regional hit and the first record appearance for Fogarty and his bandmates. **Diverse Early Influences** : The early single "Beverly Angel" reflected the mix of influences John Fogarty and his bandmates had absorbed growing up in El Serito, synthesizing an array of genres. The track itself was a pure R&B record sung by a black vocalist, and John Fogarty played a stand-up bass borrowed from a local country musician.
