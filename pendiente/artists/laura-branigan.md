# artist - Laura Branigan

## genres
- Pop

## songs
**Gloria (1982)** : Laura Branigan was frequently featured on the "Solid Gold" TV show, with producers sometimes adjusting chart positions to accommodate her availability for lip-syncing performances. The song was ranked number nine for the year 1983 according to American Top 40's year-end countdown and was a big family favorite. "It seemed to love Laura Branigan, for example, who was always willing to show up to lip sync her latest hit." **Self-Control (1984)** : This "ghostly Synthpop hit" reached number four on the charts, appearing a couple of months after Eurythmics' similar success. Although not a direct copy, it demonstrated that "Team Branigan was taking notes on British New Wave," showcasing how American artists were influenced by UK trends. The song is a cover of an Italian pop song.
