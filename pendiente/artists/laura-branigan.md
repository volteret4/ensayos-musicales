# artist - Laura Branigan

## genres
- Pop
- Synthpop

## songs
**Self-Control (1984)** : This "ghostly Synthpop hit" reached number four on the charts, appearing a couple of months after Eurythmics' similar success. Although not a direct copy, it demonstrated that "Team Branigan was taking notes on British New Wave," showcasing how American artists were influenced by UK trends. The song is a cover of an Italian pop song.
