# artist - Patti Page

## genres
- Traditional Pop

## songs
**(How Much Is That) Doggie in the Window (1953)** : This traditional pop song was a number one hit in 1953 and became one of Patti Page's biggest successes. The recording included distinctive barking sounds. While whimsical, it was not explicitly funny, but it fit within a long tradition of comical or quirky records. ## curiosities
**Diverse Repertoire** : Patti Page was known for recording a wide variety of songs, scoring hits with diverse tracks ranging from the classic "Tennessee Waltz" to "Allegheny Moon," in addition to her novelty-adjacent hit "Doggie in the Window."
