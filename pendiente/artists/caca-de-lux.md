## curiosities
**Founding of a Movida Madrileña Pillar (Late 1977)** : Caca de Lux was founded in late 1977 by Olvido Gara (Alaska), Carlos Berlanga, Manolo Campoamor, Nacho Canut, Enrique Sierra, and Fernando Márquez 'El Zurdo'. They became pioneers of glam punk in Spanish and a fundamental pillar of the nascent Movida Madrileña scene.
