## albums
**Sacred Songs (1977)** : Recorded in 1977 with Robert Fripp, Brian Eno, and Tony Levin, this solo album was considered by Fripp to be the first in his "pop trilogy." When Hall presented it to RCA in 1977, they "balked," refusing to release it for three years, claiming it would "unsettle the Hall and Oats fanbase" because it didn't sound like Hall and Oates. It was finally released in March 1980, performing decently by reaching number 58 and selling better than some of Hall & Oates's 1970s LPs. Its "unclustered new wavy, fripper-tronics production" inspired the sound of the 1980 Hall & Oates blockbuster "Voices." "RCA balked.
