**Star of Broadway "Dance of the Vampires" (2002-2003)** : Michael Crawford, who originated the role of The Phantom of the Opera, starred in the troubled Broadway production of Jim Steinman's *Dance of the Vampires*. His involvement represented a collaboration with a "top-tier Broadway star" for Team Steinman.
