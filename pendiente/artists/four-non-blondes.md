## songs
**What's Up (1993)** : This song was a number 14 hit for the all-female rock band Four Non Blondes in 1993. It is described as a "strummy don't worry be happy mimicking Singalong." This was their only major hit, and the band's lead singer-songwriter, Linda Perry, was "not really a big fan of my band." **Linda Perry's Discontent** : Linda Perry, the lead singer-songwriter for Four Non Blondes, expressed her dissatisfaction with the band's music, stating, "I wasn't really a big fan of my band." She left the band after their single album due to being uninspired by their follow-up recordings.
