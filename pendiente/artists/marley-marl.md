# artist - Marley Marl

## songs
**The Bridge (1985)** : Marley Marl produced this late 1985 single with MC Shan, which was instrumental in sparking the Bridge Wars. The track controversially "extolled their neighborhood of Queen's Bridge as a mecca for rap," leading to a direct lyrical challenge from the Bronx-based Boogie Down Productions.
