## songs
**TROLLZ (2020)** : This aggressively provocative hip hop track, featuring female rap legend Nicki Minaj (transcribed as Nikki Manage), debuted at number one in the spring of 2020. The rapper, described as a "professional Troll," explicitly recorded the song to score a number one debut and "prove a point about the corrupt ability of the Billboard charts," making it as "lured and leering as possible." Five years later, "TROLLZ" generates only three radio plays a week.
