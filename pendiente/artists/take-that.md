# artist - Take That

## songs
**Back for Good (1995)** : This song was a UK number one hit and reached number seven in America in 1995. Despite this US success, Take That never again appeared on the Hot 100. ## curiosities
**Multiple UK Number Ones, Limited US Impact** : Take That scored multiple British number ones, making them a significant chart-topping pop troop in the UK during the mid-90s. However, their success in America was limited to a single hit.
