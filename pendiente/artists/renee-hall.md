# artist - Renee Hall

## curiosities
**Arranger for "A Change Is Gonna Come"** : Renee Hall was the arranger for Sam Cook's masterpiece, "A Change Is Gonna Come." His orchestration of the song made the finished product sound distinctively grand, drawing comparisons to the "natively arranged classics" of Nat King Cole, rather than a folk song.
