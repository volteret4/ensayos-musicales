# artist - The Blackout All Stars

## members
- Dito Nievis
- Dito Puente
- Ray Barreto
- Sheila E

## songs
**I Like It (1994)** : A significant 90s makeover of Pete Rodriguez's "I Like It Like That," recorded by a collective of Latin music legends including Dito Puente, Ray Barreto, Dito Nievis, and Sheila E. This version, featuring a thumping club beat and hip hop conversant swing, reached number 25 on the Hot 100 and became Gen X's preferred rendition of the New Yorker's standard. ## curiosities
**Old-Fashioned Cover with Modern Elements (1994)** : Despite its innovative thumping club beat and hip hop conversant swing, The Blackout All Stars' 1994 remake of "I Like It" was fundamentally an old-fashioned cover song, with only the original 1967 songwriters, Mani Rodriguez and Tony Pabón, receiving credit for the composition.
