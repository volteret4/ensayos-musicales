## songs
**I Like It (1994)** : A significant 90s makeover of Pete Rodriguez's "I Like It Like That," recorded by a collective of Latin music legends including Dito Puente, Ray Barreto, Dito Nievis, and Sheila E. This version, featuring a thumping club beat and hip hop conversant swing, reached number 25 on the Hot 100 and became Gen X's preferred rendition of the New Yorker's standard. **I Like It Like That (1997)** : The Blackout All-Stars' cover of Pete Rodriguez's 1967 salsa classic was recorded for the soundtrack to the 1994 movie *I Like It Like That*. After taking nearly three years to gain traction, a push from New York Dance Music Station WKTU and a 1996 Burger King commercial helped it cross over, finally reaching number 25 on the Hot 100 in March 1997, making the New York standard an actual pop hit. ## curiosities
**Old-Fashioned Cover with Modern Elements (1994)** : Despite its innovative thumping club beat and hip hop conversant swing, The Blackout All Stars' 1994 remake of "I Like It" was fundamentally an old-fashioned cover song, with only the original 1967 songwriters, Mani Rodriguez and Tony Pabón, receiving credit for the composition.
