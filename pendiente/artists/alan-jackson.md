## songs
**Chattahoochee (1993)** : A wistful reminiscence about summer near Jackson's Georgia hometown, this song was the number one country song of 1993. It was issued as a retail single, which went gold, but on the Hot 100, it peaked just below the top 40 due to limited airplay beyond country stations, thus validating Garth Brooks's strategy of avoiding pop competition.
