## albums
**Alligator Bites Never Heal (2024)** : Praised as the hip-hop album of the year by one critic, this record was described as a comprehensive blend of a bars album, a pop album, and a mixtape. Doja Cat was characterized as a "hard ass Florida rap technician" who delivers "bars for days" with a conversational flow, also building characters and exhibiting "musical theater energy." The album was noted for its continuous energy and dynamism, metaphorically stating, "it just cooks." ## curiosities

The result was one of the most memorable Tiny Desk concerts ever made.
