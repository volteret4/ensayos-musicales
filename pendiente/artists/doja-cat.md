## curiosities
**Tiny Desk Performance (December 2024) - Electronic Artist with Live Band** : In December 2024, the performance of Doja Cat (referred to as "Dohe e Shin" in the transcript), a renowned electronic artist, on Tiny Desk was a notable example of an artist known for electronic sound performing with a full band, including winds, choirs, guitar, bass, and drums. The result was one of the most memorable Tiny Desk concerts ever made. "En diciembre de 2024, la actuación de Dohe e Shin el TinyDES fue un ejemplo de como un artista electrónica renomoso sonido, a salir con una banda con vientos, coros, guitarra, bajo y batería." ← De Tiny Desk a Boiler Room： El Boom de los Conciertos en YouTube | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QPTMemJ4ZUA
**Collaboration with SZA** : Doja Cat collaborated with SZA on a single, for which SZA won a prior Grammy in a genre category for a supporting role.
