# artist - Bruce Hornsby and The Range

## songs
**The Way It Is (1986)** : The narrator predicted this song would be a number one hit upon first hearing its video on MTV in the summer of 1986, even though it wasn't yet on the Hot 100 and Hornsby's previous pop hit hadn't charted higher than number 72. It debuted on the Hot 100 at number 86 in September, cracked the Top 40 a month later, and reached number one two months after that.
