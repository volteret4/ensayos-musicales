# artist - Catrin Cranall

## member of
- Black Box

## curiosities
**Model for Black Box Videos** : Catrin Cranall was hired by Black Box as a model to lip-sync "Ride On Time" in its video, despite Loleatta Holloway originally singing the part and Heather Small re-recording it. She continued to serve as the public "face of the group" for "Everybody, Everybody" and two other Top 40 hits, while Martha Wash performed the actual vocals.
