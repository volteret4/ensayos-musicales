# artist - Richard Strauss

## songs
**Also Sprach Zarathustra** : This composition gained widespread fame in the late 1960s as the iconic theme to Stanley Kubrick's film "2001, A Space Odyssey." In 1973, Eumir Deodato achieved a hit with a progressive funk cover of the piece, notable for being entirely unaffiliated with the film.
