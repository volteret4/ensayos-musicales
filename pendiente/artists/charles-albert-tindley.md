# artist - Charles Albert Tindley

## curiosities
**Author of Influential Gospel Hymns** : Charles Albert Tindley was a legendary minister and gospel composer who wrote the turn-of-the-century gospel standard "Stand By Me," which later influenced Sam Cook and Ben E. King. He is also credited with writing the song that later became the iconic civil rights anthem "We Shall Overcome."
