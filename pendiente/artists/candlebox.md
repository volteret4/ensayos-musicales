## albums
**Candlebox (1994)** : The self-titled debut album by the Seattle band Candlebox went triple platinum at the end of 1994, becoming the fledgling Maverick label's biggest hit to date. It outsold Maverick co-founder Madonna's double platinum *Erotica* album. ## curiosities
**"Nickelback of Their Era"** : Candlebox was described as "essentially an 80s hard rock band in 90s Grunge Rock clothing" and "the Nickelback of their era." They were scorned by scenesters who viewed them as bandwagon jumpers with limited connection to the punk and indie roots of Seattle music, and were pilloried by rock critics for their Top 40-friendly alt-pop, despite selling "truckloads of albums."
