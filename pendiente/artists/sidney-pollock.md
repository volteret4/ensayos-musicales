# artist - Sidney Pollock

## curiosities
**Amazing Grace Documentary Director (1972)** : The acclaimed Hollywood director filmed Aretha Franklin's *Amazing Grace* sessions in January 1972 with the intention of producing a documentary. However, a technical glitch where audio and video were unsynced, and his failure to use clapperboards, led to the film being shelved for over four decades until its 2018 release, after both he and Franklin had died, with 21st century technology making the fix possible.
