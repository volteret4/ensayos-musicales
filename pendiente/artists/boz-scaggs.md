# artist - Boz Scaggs

## songs
**Look What You've Done To Me (1980)** : This song was written by Boz Scaggs with lyrics inspired by watching the film footage of a slow dance scene between Bud and Pam in "Urban Cowboy." It became integral to the movie's narrative, playing during that specific moment. "Look what you've done to me by boss Skags who wrote the lyrics after watching the film of that scene." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 28 - Soundtracks of the 70s | https://seventies.libsyn.com/028-soundtracks-of-the-70s-12221-5-45-pm
**Lowdown (1976)** : John Travolta used this song to practice dance scenes for "Saturday Night Fever" before the Bee Gees were involved, specifically for the rehearsal dance scene later set to "More Than a Woman." "they were doing it to low down." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 1 - Disco Doesn't Suck (Encore) | https://www.ftr70.com/blog/tag/70s+podcast
