# artist - Desmond Child

## curiosities
**"Secret Sauce" Song Doctor** : Identified as the "secret sauce" behind Bon Jovi's third album, "Slippery When Wet." Child is described as a "song doctor" who was "innately plugged in to what made a pop hit work," drawing comparisons to Phil Spector, Diane Warren, and Max Martin. **Diverse Songwriting & Production Career** : Child co-wrote Kiss's crossover disco rock hit "I Was Made for Lovin' You" in 1979. He went on to write and produce hard rock anthems for 80s stars like Aerosmith, Joan Jett, and Cher, and in the late 90s, he co-wrote Ricky Martin's English-language breakthrough, "Livin' la Vida Loca." **Influential Songwriting (1987)** : Desmond Child was implied to have a connection or influence on Belinda Carlisle's chart-topping single "Heaven is a Place on Earth" due to its structural similarities to Bon Jovi's work, a band he frequently collaborated with. He is also known for the "song opening exploding pop chorus" trick, a tactic later reanimated by Max Martin.
