# artist - Danny McCaslin

## instruments
- Saxophone

## curiosities
**Collaboration with David Bowie (2014-2016)** : Danny McCaslin led a jazz combo that collaborated with David Bowie on the 2014 single "Sue (Or in a Season of Crime)" and subsequently on the entire "Blackstar" album. This marked Bowie's first significant foray into experimental jazz, with Bowie encouraging the ensemble to experiment during studio sessions, contributing to the album's unique sound.
