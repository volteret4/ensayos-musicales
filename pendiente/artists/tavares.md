## songs
**She's Gone (1974)** : This group of five brothers, signed to Capitol Records, recorded a cover of Hall and Oates' "She's Gone" in the summer of 1974. Their producer, Dennis Lambert, admired the Hall and Oates version so much that he replicated its arrangement almost exactly. By December 1974, the Tavares version reached number one on Billboard's Hot Soul Singles chart, prophetically becoming the first R&B chart topped by a song co-written by Hall & Oates.
