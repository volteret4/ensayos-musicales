# artist - J.W. Alexander

## curiosities
**Strategic Advice for Don Arden (1962)** : As Sam Cooke's manager, J.W. Alexander offered key advice to promoter Don Arden during Little Richard's problematic 1962 UK tour, assuring him that Richard's competitive nature, coupled with Sam Cooke's strong performance, would inevitably lead Richard back to rock and roll. "Alexander told Arden he had nothing to worry about. **Business and Songwriting Partner** : J.W. Alexander was Sam Cook's crucial business and songwriting partner, advising him on the launch of his publishing company and co-founding SAR Records. He was one of the few people Cook shared his profound work with, notably playing "A Change Is Gonna Come" for him shortly after its composition. "Sam and his business and songwriting partner, J.W.
