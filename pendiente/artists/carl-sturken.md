## curiosities
**Member of Rhythm Syndicate** : Carl Sturken was a member of Rhythm Syndicate, an early 1990s white funk boy band that achieved a number two hit on the Hot 100 with "P.A.S.S.I.O.N." in 1991. **Co-founder of Syndicated Rhythm** : Following the breakup of Rhythm Syndicate, Sturken and Evan Rogers established their production company, Syndicated Rhythm, dedicated to writing and producing music for other pop artists. "After Rhythm Syndicate broke up, Rogers and Sturken stayed in the music business, forming their own syndicated rhythm production company to produce and write songs for other pop acts." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Only Girl in the World Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Discovery and Management of Rihanna** : In 2003, while on vacation in Barbados, Sturken and Evan Rogers discovered Robin Fenty (Rihanna) and subsequently signed her to a management contract. They encouraged her to adopt her middle name, Rihanna, professionally, and facilitated her journey to America for demo recordings and meetings with major record label executives. **Completion of Pon de Replay** : Carl Sturken contributed to the completion of "Pon de Replay," a pop-flavored dancehall tune that became Rihanna's first single, making her "legible" to American label executives.
