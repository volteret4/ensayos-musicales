## curiosities
**Second Wave IDM Artist** : Was a part of the "second wave" of IDM artists, with their music released on influential labels like Me le plato, Planet Moo, Mor, and El Scam. This era was characterized by an increased focus on percussion, influenced by jungle and drum and bass. "Me le plato, Planet Moo, Mor, o El Scam, se convirtieron en espacios clave para editar a músicos como Yucic, Bola, Alvanoto, Fortead, Squid Pusher, Borsafcanada o Benetan Snaers." ← Qué fue el IDM y por qué cambió la música electrónica | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RTwn3hP1XTg
