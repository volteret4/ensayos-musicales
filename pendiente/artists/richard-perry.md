## curiosities
**Hit Producer of Early-Mid 70s** : Richard Perry established his reputation in the early to mid-1970s by producing culturally ubiquitous number one records for artists such as Carly Simon ("You're So Vain"), Harry Nilsson ("Without You"), and Leo Sayer ("You Make Me Feel Like Dancing"), demonstrating his ability to craft major hits. **Founder of Planet Records (1978)** : In 1978, Richard Perry founded his own label, Planet Records, intending it as a vehicle to showcase talent he was nurturing, with The Pointer Sisters becoming his first signed act and a significant "pet project." "By 1978, Richard Perry decided to start his own label, Planet Records, as a vehicle for showcasing talent he was nurturing while still producing established artists for other labels." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Yes We Can Can Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4f6364fe6d21276e3fdd
**Resuscitated Pointer Sisters' Career** : Richard Perry was instrumental in the Pointer Sisters' comeback and reinvention, making their sound "rock adjacent" while retaining soul and danceability, leading to their mainstream recognition and huge hits like "Fire." "the pointer sisters were so deserving of mainstream success long before I was able to get them there. ... **Producer's Vision for "He's So Shy"** : Producer Richard Perry recognized "He's So Shy" as a smash hit upon hearing it, particularly noting its trendy keyboard hook, which was at the peak of Yacht Rock's popularity. He changed the song's gender from "She's So Shy" to "He's So Shy" and guided The Pointer Sisters to record it, solidifying their status as pop stars. "When Richard Perry heard Snow and Wilde's song, it sounded to him like a smash." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Yes We Can Can Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Producer's Vision for "Slow Hand"** : Richard Perry perceived "Slow Hand" as a sensual and seductive song, similar to The Pointer Sisters' earlier hit "Fire," and encouraged Anita Pointer to deliver a lead vocal that infused the track with subtly cutting-edge sex positivity. "Richard Perry heard Slow Hand as a sensual seductive song in the vein of The Pointer's previous hit Fire." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Yes We Can Can Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Sale of Planet Records (1982)** : By 1982, Richard Perry sold his vanity label, Planet Records, to RCA Records, though he continued to oversee the artists on the imprint, including The Pointer Sisters, whom he credited for the label's establishment. "By 1982, with Planet Records established, thanks in no small part to The Pointer's sisters, Richard Perry sold his vanity label to RCA Records." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Yes We Can Can Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Single Selection for "Jump For My Love"** : Richard Perry and The Pointer Sisters initially wanted to release an uptempo album cut simply titled "Jump" as their next single, but changed the title to "Jump For My Love" after Van Halen had a number one hit with their own song titled "Jump." "For their next single, The Sisters and Richard Perry wanted to go with an uptempo cut that on the album was simply titled Jump." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Yes We Can Can Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Remix and Reissue Strategy for "I'm So Excited" (1984)** : Believing that the 1982 version of "I'm So Excited" hadn't reached its full potential and responding to Anita Pointer's desire for a lead vocal, Richard Perry had the single remixed and reissued the "Breakout" LP with the new version, leading to its eventual signature hit status.
