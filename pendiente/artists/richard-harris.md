## songs
**MacArthur Park (1968)** : The original version of this song, recorded by Irish actor Richard Harris, was his only top 40 hit, peaking at number two. Written by Jimmy Webb, it was described as a "florid, overly lyrical" and strange composition, originally intended for a cantata. Harris, with his classical actor's training, managed to make the song a "camping novelty hit" at a moment in the late 60s when Baroque songs were doing well on the charts. His classical actor's training allowed him to deliver a dramatic performance on "MacArthur Park."
