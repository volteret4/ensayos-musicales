Many pop fans "mistook for Prince," demonstrating the imperial artist's influence on imitators' success. ## curiosities
**Faux-British Accent** : The lead singer, Melvin Riley Jr., used a spoken-word bit with "a faux-British accent" at the start of their number one hit "Oh Sheila," which was interpreted as a "troll" on the British invasion, mirroring similar tactics by American bands in the 60s.
