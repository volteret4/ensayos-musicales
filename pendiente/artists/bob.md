## songs
**Airplanes (2010)** : This song became a number two smash hit on the Hot 100 for rapper B.o.B. in 2010. It notably featured Hayley Williams of Paramore singing the hook, a collaboration that marked Williams's biggest pop hit at the time. **Nothing On You (2010)** : An R&B ballad that B.o.B recorded with Bruno Mars as the hook singer, after Atlantic Records acquired it from its original writers, Bruno Mars and The Smeezingtons (who wrote it for Lupe Fiasco). The song reached number one on the Hot 100 in May 2010, though Mars's "sweeping crooning on the chorus" was widely considered the most memorable part, effectively showcasing him more than B.o.B.
