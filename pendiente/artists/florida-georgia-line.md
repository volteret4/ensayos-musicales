## songs
**Cruise Remix (2013)** : In 2013, the Nashville duo Florida Georgia Line collaborated with rapper Nelly on a remix of their country smash "Cruise." They announced this hip-hop remix via their social media channels, which propelled it to the top five on the Hot 100, months after the original version had already topped the Country Chart. This successful collaboration highlighted the growing cross-pollination between hip-hop and country music. **Expected Number One Album (2016)** : Billboard chart watchers had anticipated Florida Georgia Line's latest album would debut at number one in 2016, but it was unexpectedly outsold by Barbra Streisand's *Encore, Movie Partners Sing Broadway*. **Use of Trap Beats in Country** : Florida Georgia Line is cited as a group that started using trap beats on country records, helping to make this production technique "completely normal" in the genre, indicating a broader acceptance among country audiences.
