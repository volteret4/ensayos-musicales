## songs
**Cruise Remix (2013)** : In 2013, the Nashville duo Florida Georgia Line collaborated with rapper Nelly on a remix of their country smash "Cruise." They announced this hip-hop remix via their social media channels, which propelled it to the top five on the Hot 100, months after the original version had already topped the Country Chart. This successful collaboration highlighted the growing cross-pollination between hip-hop and country music.
