## songs
**(I've Had) The Time of My Life (1987)** : Bill Medley, a veteran of 60s pop and member of The Righteous Brothers, duetted with Jennifer Warnes on this theme song for the 1987 surprise hit film *Dirty Dancing*. The song reached number one and later won the Best Original Song Oscar. ## curiosities
**Absence from "Unchained Melody" (1965)** : Bill Medley, a bandmate in The Righteous Brothers, did not perform on the 1965 recording of "Unchained Melody," which was instead the work of Bobby Hatfield.
