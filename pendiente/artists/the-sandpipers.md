## songs
**Guantanamera (1966)** : The American Easy Listening vocal group The Sandpipers took "Guantanamera" to number nine on the Hot 100 in 1966. This version was notable for retaining the Spanish lyrics of the original Cuban anthem, which dated back to the 1920s and featured lyrics by Cuban poet Jose Martí.
