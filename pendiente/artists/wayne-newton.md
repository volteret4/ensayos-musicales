# artist - Wayne Newton

## curiosities
**Post-Rock Hitmaker** : Wayne Newton was identified as a "post-rock hitmaker" in the 1960s whose music did not consistently "rock." He represents a category of artists who continued to achieve chart success with non-rock sounds during a decade often solely associated with rock and roll.
