## albums
**Five Number One Albums** : After Kerb Alpert and his group scored multiple hits, they formed an actual Tijuana Brass, which went on to score five number one albums. This demonstrated the transition from a "made-up group" to a highly successful "real group." ## songs
**The Lonely Bull (1962)** : Recorded in 1962 by LA-based trumpeter and future A&M label-boss Kerb Alpert, this single became a left-field top 10 hit. Alpert recorded the song by himself, overdubbing his trumpet to create the sound of a full group of mariaches, and credited it to the "authentic sounding but totally made-up" Kerb Alpert and the Tijuana Brass. "He recorded the song by himself, overdubbing his trumpet to resemble a full group of mariaches, and he credited the single to the authentic sounding but totally made-up, Kerb Alpert and the Tijuana Brass." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/

## curiosities
**Made-Up Group to Real Group** : Kerb Alpert initially created the "authentic sounding but totally made-up" Kerb Alpert and the Tijuana Brass to credit his 1962 hit "The Lonely Bull," which he recorded alone. It was only after scoring multiple hits that he formed an actual Tijuana Brass, which subsequently achieved five number one albums. "It wasn't until Alpert and his group scored multiple hits that he formed an actual Tijuana Brass, which went on to score five number one albums." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**No Latin Heritage** : Kerb Alpert, the founder of the Tijuana Brass, has "no Latin heritage whatsoever," despite the group's mariachi-inspired sound and name. This detail highlights the manufactured nature of the group's initial image.
