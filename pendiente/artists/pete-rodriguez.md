**I Like It Like That (1967)** : Pete Rodriguez and his Latin jazz band recorded this "Brassi Piano Vamping Track" for the Bronx-based Latin jazz label Allegro. Notably sung in English, "I Like It Like That" became legendary in salsa music circles and was a regional radio smash in Rodriguez's hometown of New York, heavily rotated by DJ Symphony Sid, although it did not make the Hot 100.
