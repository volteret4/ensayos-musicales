# artist - Pete Rodriguez

## genres

## songs
**I Like It Like That (1967)** : Pete Rodriguez's 1967 boogaloo salsa jam, an enduring Latin pop classic. This song was later given a major 90s makeover by The Blackout All Stars and famously remade and interpolated by Cardi B in her 2018 number one hit "I Like It."
