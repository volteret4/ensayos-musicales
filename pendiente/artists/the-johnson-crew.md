## songs
**Space Cowboy (1983)** : This number 12 R&B hit by the Boston electro-funk band took cowboy iconography a step further than mere cosmetic style. Although entirely sung and not rapped, it foreshadowed later genre hybridizations by mixing hip hop-style breakbeats with a narrative about an outer space outlaw armed with a laser gun.
