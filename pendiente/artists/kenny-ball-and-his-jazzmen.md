# artist - Kenny Ball and His Jazzmen

## songs
**Tsukiyaki** : This instrumental cover of Kyu Sakamoto's "Ui-Oh Muite Aruku" was recorded by Kenny Ball and his Jazzmen after a British label executive brought the original 45 back from Japan. The label issued Ball's version under the nonsensical title "Tsukiyaki" to appeal to British listeners. ## curiosities
**British Cover, American Flop** : While the British label issued their instrumental cover of "Ui-Oh Muite Aruku" as "Tsukiyaki," this version was not a hit in America, unlike Kyu Sakamoto's original recording.
