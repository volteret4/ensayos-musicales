# artist - Faye Dunaway

## curiosities
**Star of "Learning to Fly" Music Video (1991)** : Faye Dunaway starred in the music video for Tom Petty and The Heartbreakers' "Learning to Fly" from the album "Into the Great Wide Open," which was directed by Julian Temple and satirized the era's teen rock idols.
