**The Girl from Ipanema (1964)** : Astrud Gilberto, wife of Bossa Nova pioneer João Gilberto, sang the English lyrics on the global smash hit "The Girl from Ipanema." This recording, which also featured American jazz saxophonist Stan Getz and Portuguese vocals by João Gilberto, became a number five hit on the Hot 100 in 1964.
