## curiosities
**Renaming by Fantasy Records** : In 1964, after signing to Fantasy Records, the band was renamed The Gollywags by label executives. They chose this "seemingly exotic, frankly offensive name" thinking it might be more marketable. **British Invasion Style Singles** : The re-christened Gollywags began playing British Invasion-style singles, aiming to jump on the bandwagon of bands like The Beatles and The Rolling Stones. They released singles for just under four years under this name. "And the re-christened Gollywags began playing British invasion style singles meant to jump on the Beatles and Stones' bandwagon." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Bad Moon on the Rise Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Evolving Sound and Leadership Change** : Over three-plus years since signing with Fantasy Records, The Gollywags' sound evolved to become more psychedelic and harder rocking, though they weren't a "Summer of Love jam band." During this period, Tom Fogarty gradually relinquished lead vocals and John Fogarty took on the majority of the songwriting.
