# artist - Al B. Sure!

## songs
**The Secret Garden (1989)** : Al B. Sure! was one of the soul singers from different generations featured on the slow jam "The Secret Garden," part of Quincy Jones's "Back on the Block" album. The song achieved number one on the R&B chart and number 31 on the pop chart.
