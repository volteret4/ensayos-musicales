## curiosities
**Presidential Inaugural Ball Performance (January 1993)** : In January 1993, a one-time supergroup named Automatic Baby, comprising members from R.E.M. (Michael Stipe, Mike Mills) and U2 (Bono, The Edge, Adam Clayton, Larry Mullin Jr.), teamed up to perform U2's song "One" at the inaugural ball for new US President Bill Clinton, one year after the song's release.
