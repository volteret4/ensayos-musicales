## albums
**Beautiful Thugger Girls (2017)** : Released in 2017, this acclaimed mixtape is considered significant for its role in widening the lane for the cross-pollination of country tropes into rap jams. Lil Nas X later credited Young Thug as the "forefather of the Country Trap sound" due to works like this. The lead-off track, "Family Don't Matter," famously begins with an "exuberant yeehaw" and continues to incorporate increasingly twangy elements. ## curiosities
**Forefather of Country Trap Sound** : Jeffrey Lamar Williams, known as Young Thug, is an eccentric Atlanta rapper recognized for his unique vocal tone. He is credited by Lil Nas X as the forefather of the Country Trap sound, having significantly broadened the acceptance and integration of country tropes into rap music in the mid-2010s. His 2017 mixtape "Beautiful Thugger Girls" is a prime example of this influence.
