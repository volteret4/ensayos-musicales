# artist - Sink

## songs
**What's Going On (2001)** : Sink was among the mega-stars featured on the 2001 Jermaine Dupri-produced cover of Marvin Gays' "What's Going On." This single was initially meant to benefit AIDS research but was later shared with the Red Cross in the wake of the 9-11 attacks.
