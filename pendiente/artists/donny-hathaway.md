**This Christmas (1970)** : Co-written with Nadine McKinnor, this song was a deliberate attempt to create the ultimate Black Christmas song, steeped in the soul tradition and entwined with 70s R&B. Producer Rick Powell recalled its musical flawlessness, from sleigh bells to brass breakdown, Hathaway's impassioned vocal, and a wonderfully casual soul piano bridge. During Hathaway's lifetime, it spent a solitary week at number 11 on Billboard's seasonal Christmas singles chart in 1972. Reissued in 1991 on an Atgo Records Soul Christmas CD, it solidified its status as a radio recurrent. In 2020, it made its Hot 100 debut, cracking the Top 40 at number 39, fueled by nearly 11,000 radio spins and 63 million streams.
