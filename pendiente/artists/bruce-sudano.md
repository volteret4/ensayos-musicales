# artist - Bruce Sudano

## member of
- Brooklyn Dreams
- Donna Summer

## curiosities
**Collaboration and Marriage with Donna Summer** : Sudano co-wrote the album's title track "Bad Girls" and "Heaven Knows" with Donna Summer, whom he later married. A song he co-wrote with Summer became a number one country hit for Dolly Parton in May 1980. **Rock and Roll Hall of Fame Acceptance** : Sudano, along with Summer's three daughters, accepted Donna Summer's posthumous induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2013, delivering gracious words on behalf of the family.
