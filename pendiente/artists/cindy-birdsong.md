# artist - Cindy Birdsong

## member of
- LaBelle
- The Blue Bells

## curiosities
**Departure from The Blue Bells** : In 1967, after The Blue Bells' #36 R&B hit "Take Me For A Little While," Cindy Birdsong was "poached" by Motown Records to replace Florence Ballard in their leading group, The Supremes. Her departure significantly disrupted The Blue Bells, leading to their regrouping and eventual renaming as LaBelle.
