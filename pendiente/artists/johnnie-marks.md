# artist - Johnnie Marks

## songs
**Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer (1949)** : Marks wrote this song to accompany a promotional holiday children's book issued by department store Montgomery Ward. He himself later called it "one of the worst songs ever written," and Gene Autry initially rejected recording it before his wife intervened. "Mark's song was played by a man named Johnnie Marks, like Erving Berlin, a Jew.
