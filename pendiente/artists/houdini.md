## curiosities
**Managed by Russell Simmons** : Houdini was among the rap acts whose careers were guided by artist-manager Russell Simmons, alongside Run DMC, Curtis Blow, and The Fearless Four. By the summer of 1986, Houdini was recognized as a "rising rap star" poised for significant breakthrough success.
