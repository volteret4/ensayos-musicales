# artist - Robbie Rosa

## curiosities
**Co-Writer for Ricky Martin** : Robbie Rosa, who was Ricky Martin's bandmate in Menudo and sang lead on "Hold Me," later became an industry veteran with Latin heritage. He co-wrote "The Cup of Life" and the blockbuster hit "Livin' La Vida Loca" for Ricky Martin, demonstrating his significant contribution to Martin's crossover success.
