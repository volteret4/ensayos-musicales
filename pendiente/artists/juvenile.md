## curiosities
**Quadruple Platinum Sales (Late 90s)** : Juvenile, a rapper on the Cash Money label, achieved quadruple platinum sales for his albums in 1998 and 1999. These sales levels affirmed the potency of Southern Hip Hop and surpassed what Outcast had achieved by that time.
