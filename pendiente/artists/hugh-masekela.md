## songs
**Grazing in the Grass (1968)** : This cowbell-inflected instrumental, performed by South African trumpeter Hugh Masekela, reached number one in the summer of 1968. Its widespread popularity led to a rare occurrence: a hit cover version by The Friends of Distinction that added lyrics, which subsequently reached number three the following summer. ## curiosities
**Festival Co-Creator Role** : Hugh Masekela, an exiled jazz musician from South Africa, co-conceived the Zaire 74 festival with Stewart Levine. They had met about 10 years prior at the Manhattan School of Music and developed a lifelong friendship, envisioning the festival as an opportunity to unite African American and African performers.
