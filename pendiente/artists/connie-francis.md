## curiosities
**Critic Robert Hull's Disparaging Remarks (1975)** : In a misogynistic assessment from 1975, critic Robert Hull dismissed Connie Francis as "two old" in a crude ranking of female singers he would like "to sit in your lap." **Chart Dominance (1960-62)** : Connie Francis was a highly successful artist in the early 1960s, topping the Hot 100 three times between 1960 and 1962. She held the title of "top female vocalist on the charts from the late 50s through the mid 60s," and chart historian Joel Whitburn ranked her eighth among all artists for the entire 1960s decade, placing her "sandwiched between the Beach Boys and James Brown." "Connie Francis was the top female vocalist on the charts from the late 50s through the mid 60s." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Singing Nuns and Green Tambourines Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Adult Appeal** : Despite being less than 30 years old when she achieved her major hits, Connie Francis's material "came to seem wise beyond her years." Her music appealed to adults in the 1960s, contributing to the easy listening genre and being enjoyed by an audience that "mostly disdained rock and roll." "though Connie Francis was less than 30 years old when she scored all of her big hits, her material came to seem wise beyond her years, to her credit and to a fault." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Singing Nuns and Green Tambourines Edition Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
