# artist - The Justice Collective

## songs
**He Ain't Heavy He's My Brother (2012)** : The Justice Collective's cover of The Hollies' "He Ain't Heavy He's My Brother" became the UK Christmas number one in 2012. This charitable single, performed by a non-professional group, was part of a series of "Do Gooder Singles" that found success against The X-Factor's typical chart command in the early to mid-2010s.
