**Venus (1970)** : This co-ed Dutch quartet, fronted by singer Mariska Veres, transformed "Venus" into a groovy dance rocker and a hippie era smash, reaching number one on the Hot 100 in February 1970. Songwriter Robbie Van Leeuwen had borrowed the melody from folk trio The Big 3's arrangement of "O'Susanna," making the song an interpolation or a cover itself. ## curiosities
**Dutch Origin and Copyright** : The Shocking Blue was a Dutch group, and the copyright for their number one hit "Venus" was held by Red Bullet Productions, a Dutch publishing company headed by Willem Van Kuten. Van Kuten's ownership of this copyright later played a crucial role in the development of the Stars on 45 medley. "The shocking blues were a Dutch group, and the copyright for Venus was owned by a Dutch publishing company called Red Bullet Productions." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Without The Beatles | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
