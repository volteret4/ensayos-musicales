## songs
**Wagon Wheel (2004)** : In 2003, Ketch Secor of Old Crow Medicine Show obtained Bob Dylan's unfinished "Rock Me Mama" fragment, added new verses to Dylan's original chorus, and gave it a two-step tempo. The band released it as a full song, "Wagon Wheel," in 2004. Their version became a standard and a barroom sing-along, reportedly leading some bars to institute "no Wagon Wheel" policies due to its widespread popularity, selling about a million copies over a decade.
