## songs
**I Don't Wanna Know (2004)** : Mario Winans took rightful credit as the lead singer on this mournful soul ballad, which was a number two hit. The supporting credits were unusual, featuring Sean Combs (P. Diddy) with a bespoke rap and New Age superstar Enya, whose sampled song "Boadicea" formed the brooding tune underneath the track.
