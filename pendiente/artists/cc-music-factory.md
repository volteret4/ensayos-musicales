## songs
**A Deeper Love** : Originally an original song by Robert Clivillés and David Cole that served as a B-side to their "Pride" cover, it topped Billboard's Club Play Chart and just missed the pop top 40. In 1994, C+C Music Factory produced a remake with new vocals by Aretha Franklin, which became the definitive version and topped the Club Play Chart again, later nominated for a Grammy. "Finally, in 1994, Clavilles and Cole produced a remake of their own A Deeper Love with new vocals by Yes, Aritha Franklin and Franklin's version became the definitive version, topping the Club Play Chart all over again." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say a Little Prayer Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eec154465cd600c4d67
**Gonna Make You Sweat (Everybody Dance Now) (1990)** : This hit song is associated with C+C Music Factory, a dance production team. "the dance production team behind hits like Gunna Make You Sweat." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say a Little Prayer Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eec154465cd600c4d67
**Gonna Make You Sweat (Everybody Dance Now) (1990)** : This hip-house jam, produced by Robert Clivillés and David Cole, topped the Hot 100 in February 1991. The chart credit read "C+C Music Factory featuring Freedom Williams," who rapped all the verses. However, the iconic melodic hooks, including "everybody dance now," were sung by Martha Wash, who was uncredited and later sued the group for fraud and misappropriation. "This hip-house jam produced by Robert Clavilles and David Cole, the CNC of the group name, topped the Hot 100 in February 1991." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Feat. **Here We Go (1991)** : This follow-up single by C+C Music Factory was credited to Zelma Davis, who did vocalize on it. This was in contrast to the group's breakthrough hit "Gonna Make You Sweat," where Davis lip-synced vocals actually sung by Martha Wash. **Pride (In the Name of Love) (1992)** : C+C Music Factory produced a dance cover of U2's 1984 anthem, which became a number six dance hit and a minor Hot 100 hit in 1992, before Aretha Franklin's involvement with their other material. ## curiosities
**Dance Production Team** : C+C Music Factory is identified as a dance production team, known for hits like "Gonna Make You Sweat." **Addendum to Pride (1990s)** : C+C Music Factory's original song "A Deeper Love" was initially conceived as an addendum to their dance cover of U2's "Pride (In the Name of Love)." "an original song meant as an addendum to Pride that Clavilles and Cole called A Deeper Love." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say a Little Prayer Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4eec154465cd600c4d67
**Similar Vocal Deception** : This American multi-platinum dance collective combined pop house hooks and prominent rapping, but also got into "hot water" by having a model lip-sync to Martha Wash's vocals, directly mirroring the type of deception employed by Millie Vanilley. "C and C music factory, the American multi-platinum dance collected, combined pop house hooks and prominent rapping. They even got into their own hot water by having a model lip sync to vocals by Martha Walsh." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Lawsuit by Martha Wash** : Martha Wash, who sang all the melodic hooks on "Gonna Make You Sweat (Everybody Dance Now)" including "everybody dance now," was uncredited on the number one hit. She later sued C+C Music Factory for "fraud, deceptive packaging, and commercial misappropriation" due to Zelma Davis lip-syncing her vocals in the music video.
