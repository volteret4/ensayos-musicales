## songs
**A Deeper Love** : Originally an original song by Robert Clivillés and David Cole that served as a B-side to their "Pride" cover, it topped Billboard's Club Play Chart and just missed the pop top 40. In 1994, C+C Music Factory produced a remake with new vocals by Aretha Franklin, which became the definitive version and topped the Club Play Chart again, later nominated for a Grammy. **Pride (In the Name of Love) (1992)** : C+C Music Factory produced a dance cover of U2's 1984 anthem, which became a number six dance hit and a minor Hot 100 hit in 1992, before Aretha Franklin's involvement with their other material.
