**Wild Wild West (1999)** : This "glossy single" from his "flop film" of the same name topped the Hot 100 in 1999. It featured Kool Mo Dee, who reprised his 1988 hook from his earlier song of the same title. Despite its connection to a cowboy movie, the track did not sound very country, being mostly built from a sample of Stevie Wonder's 1970s soul-funk classic "I Wish." ## curiosities
**Singles Withholding Impact** : As a major artist, Will Smith was among those whose singles were sometimes withheld on a case-by-case basis during the late 1990s, leading to his disappearance from the Hot 100. "by 1997, 1998 and 1999, artists as big as Janet Jackson, Will Smith and Foo Fighters were withholding singles on a case-by-case basis and disappearing from the Hot 100." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The Great War Against the Single Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
