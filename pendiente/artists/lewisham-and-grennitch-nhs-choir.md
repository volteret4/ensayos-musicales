# artist - Lewisham and Grennitch NHS choir

## songs
**A Bridge Over You (2015)** : "A Bridge Over You" was the novel single by the Lewisham and Grennitch NHS choir that achieved the UK Christmas number one in 2015. The track was a mashup of Simon and Garfunkel's "Bridge Over Troubled Water" and Coldplay's "Fix You," created by a choir of doctors and nurses from the National Health Service. The single benefited several healthcare charities and was another instance of a "charmingly non-professional charitable single" winning the Christmas title. ## curiosities
**UK Christmas Number One (2015) - NHS Charity Mashup** : The Lewisham and Grennitch NHS choir, composed of doctors and nurses from the National Health Service, secured the UK Christmas number one in 2015. Their novel single was a mashup of Simon and Garfunkel's "Bridge Over Troubled Water" and Coldplay's "Fix You," entitled "A Bridge Over You," with proceeds benefiting several healthcare charities. This success further demonstrated the viability of "charming non-professional charitable singles" to take the Christmas title.
