# artist - JD Riznar

## curiosities
**Co-creator of Yacht Rock Series** : JD Riznar co-created the "Yacht Rock" Web TV series in 2005. He was involved in developing characters, such as Coco Goldstein, for the Channel 101 show and ensured musical and historical accuracy in episodes by researching liner notes and specific eras like February 1978.
