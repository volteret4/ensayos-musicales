## songs
**The Battle of New Orleans (1959)** : This country song was the Billboard year-end number one for 1959, based on an old American folk song commemorating an event from the War of 1812 and written by a high school principal. Johnny Horton's version spent six weeks at number one and is the best-known recording, triumphing over Bobby Darin's "Mack the Knife" for the year-end title, even though "Mack the Knife" spent more weeks at number one later in the year.
