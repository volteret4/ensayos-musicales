# artist - Irene Cara

## songs
**Break Dance (1984)** : One year after her number one hit "Flashdance... What a Feeling," Irene Cara returned to the top 10 with this single. Described as "guileless," the song was credited with doing more to introduce the breaking dance style to "middle America" than most contemporary rap singles. "Actress and pop star Irene Cara, one year past her number one hit, Flash Dance, What A Feeling, returned to the top 10 with the single Break Dance." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > What’s 1984 Got to Do with It Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef5154465cd600c4fa3
**Flashdance... What a Feeling (1983)** : This song was Irene Cara's number one hit in 1983, one year prior to her single "Break Dance." Its success established her as a major pop star, preceding her role in popularizing breakdancing to a wider American audience. "one year past her number one hit, Flash Dance, What A Feeling." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > What’s 1984 Got to Do with It Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef5154465cd600c4fa3
**What a Feeling (1983)** : Irene Cara co-wrote and sang "What a Feeling" from the 1983 movie "Flashdance." This highly successful song earned her both a Grammy Award and an Academy Award. "co-wrote and sang what a feeling from the 1983 movie Flash Dance, which won her both a Grammy and an Oscar." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 47 - Dick Clark's Entertainment Empire | https://www.ftr70.com/

## curiosities
**Role in "Sparkle" and Soundtrack Controversy (1976)** : Irene Cara starred in the 1976 movie "Sparkle," portraying a singer in the film. Although she sang the songs within the movie, Aretha Franklin performed them on the official soundtrack album, a decision that reportedly disappointed Cara, who had hoped it would be her own record.
