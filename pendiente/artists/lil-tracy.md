## songs
**Like A Farmer (2018)** : Released in the spring of 2018, this "laconic backwoodsy" track is considered perhaps the most obvious antecedent to Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road," not only for its twang and sense of humor but also its lyrical hook: "I got horses in my car just like a farmer. I got some horses in this truck. Keep up." Lil Tracy explained in a Genius interview that he freestyled a deep South accent after stumbling upon a folksy beat on a beatmaker's website while "messing around with country people impersonations," a process very similar to Montero Hill's inspiration for "Old Town Road." The song amassed some two million SoundCloud streams in six months. "Like a farmer was perhaps the most obvious antecedent to Old Town Road.
