**Kathy Sledge's Zaire 74 Experience (1974)** : Kathy Sledge, then 14 or 15 years old, described Zaire 74 as "one of the most exciting points in my life" and "euphoric." Traveling on a charter plane with legends like Bill Withers, James Brown, and Johnny Pacheco, she felt a profound "sense of home" upon arriving in Africa, cherishing the experience of learning from other artists. "Once I stepped off the plane, I felt a sense of home. Wow, this is a place actually where I feel like I'm long and to be able to experience that at such a young age. **Pittsburgh Pirates Anthem** : "We Are Family" became the unexpected anthem for the 1979 Pittsburgh Pirates. The team, starting in fourth place in the National League East in early June, adopted the song after it was played during a rain delay. Led by Willie Stargell, the team embraced the "family" theme, which was printed on their dugout and became a central part of their identity, propelling them to win the World Series four months later, defeating the Baltimore Orioles. The song was played over the PA, on t-shirts, signs, and even replaced "Take Me Out to the Ball Game" during the seventh-inning stretch. "Willie Stargel said on the night that they won the World Series. That bothered me because this person didn't live with us and didn't see how much we depended on each other." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 36 - Na Na Na Na, Hey Hey Hey! 70s Music and Sports | https://seventies.libsyn.com/ep-36-na-na-na-na-hey-hey-hey-70s-music-and-sports
