## curiosities
**1974 CMA Entertainer of the Year Win** : Charlie Rich won the CMA Entertainer of the Year award in 1974, reaping rewards from his successful 1973 work. **1975 CMA Awards Incident - Card Burning** : As the reigning CMA entertainer of the year, Charlie Rich staggered onto the stage at Nashville's Grand Ole Opry in 1975, fueled by gin and tonics, and announced John Denver as the winner. He then pulled out a lighter and set the card with Denver's name on it on fire, an act widely interpreted as a statement that John Denver was not welcome in country music. Rich later claimed it was an accident caused by pain killers. "Rich reaches into his pocket, takes out a lighter and sets the card with Denver's name on it on fire." ← For the Record - The 70s > Ep. 2 - Countryish Music of the 1970s (Encore) | https://www.ftr70.com/

**1975 CMA Award Presentation Incident** : During the 1975 CMAs, Charlie Rich was scheduled to present the New Entertainer of the Year award. Upon opening the envelope and seeing John Denver's name, Rich controversially used a lighter to set the slip of paper on fire before announcing Denver as the winner. This bizarre act was later attributed to Rich being intoxicated and under the influence of pain medication, with him reportedly believing it was humorous. However, to the CMA audience, it was widely perceived as a protest against the increasing "popification" of country music, a sentiment that had been recently fueled by debates involving Olivia Newton-John. "rich, open the envelope, and when he saw John Denver's name there, took out a lighter and lit the slip of paper on fire." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Country Roads and Summer Nights Edition Part 1 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/698fd3ebd6c27a06bb92e1f2
**CMA Banning After Incident** : As a direct consequence of his controversial paper-burning incident during the 1975 CMA Awards, Charlie Rich was subsequently banned by the CMAs for an unspecified period of years.
