## curiosities
**Scheduled Grammy Performer (1998)** : Opera great Luciano Pavarotti was scheduled to perform "Nessun Dorma" at the Grammy Awards on February 25, 1998, at Radio City Music Hall in New York City. However, his doctors advised him to rest a sore throat, leading Aretha Franklin to famously step in as his last-minute replacement.
