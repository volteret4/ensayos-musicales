# artist - Two Morrow

## members
- Olivia Newton-John

## albums
**Two Morrow (1970)** : This co-ed pop group, conceived by Don Kirshner as a "transatlantic Monkees," recorded an album and starred in a sci-fi musical fantasy film. However, neither the movie nor the music connected, and its singles failed to chart.
