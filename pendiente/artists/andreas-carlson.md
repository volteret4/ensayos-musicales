# artist - Andreas Carlson

## curiosities
**Co-Writer of "I Want It That Way"** : Andreas Carlson, a Swedish co-writer, collaborated with Max Martin on the Backstreet Boys' hit "I Want It That Way." Despite critical discussion, he and Martin never revealed the meaning of the inscrutable lyrics.
