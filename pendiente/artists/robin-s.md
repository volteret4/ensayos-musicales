## songs
**Show Me Love (1993)** : This track reached the top five on the Hot 100 in 1993, becoming a club classic of the 90s house music crossover era, particularly recognized for its "bubbly keyboard sound." The definitive hit version was a remix by Swedish DJ Stonebridge; the original 1990 version, written and produced by Alan George and Fred McFarlane, was not a hit and sounded distinctly different without those keyboards. ## curiosities
**"Show Me Love" Remix Success** : The definitive hit version of "Show Me Love" was a remix by Swedish DJ and house music producer Stonebridge. The "bubbly keyboards" and "cool atmosphere" added by Stonebridge were the direct reasons the song finally became a hit three years after its original, non-hit release in 1990.
