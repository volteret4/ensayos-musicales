# artist - Nick Ashford and Valerie Simpson

## genres
- R&B

## songs
**I'm Every Woman (1978)** : Ashford & Simpson were the songwriters of Chaka Khan's definitive solo career launching single "I'm Every Woman." They had "struggled with the melody and the lyric for months" before giving it to Khan, who instinctively knew how to arrange it. **Stuff Like That (1978)** : This husband and wife team contributed vocals to Quincy Jones's single "Stuff Like That," which also featured Chaka Khan. The song reached number 1 on the R&B chart and scraped the pop top 20.
