# artist - Eternal

## genres
- R&B

## songs
**Stay** : This song helped the British R&B girl group Eternal crack the UK Top 10 and score a Top 20 hit in America. It was later used as the audition song by London managers seeking to form a new girl group, which would eventually become the Spice Girls.
