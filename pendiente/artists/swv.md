**Right Here** : New Jack Swing producer Teddy Riley had the idea to place the song's vocals over a lush sample of Michael Jackson's 1983 hit "Human Nature." Retitled "Right Here (Human Nature)," this remixed song re-entered both charts, eventually reaching number two on the pop chart and number one on the R&B chart. ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > We Invented the Remix Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Right Here Human Nature (1993)** : This song was a hybridized follow-up that remixed SWV's "Right Here" with a sample of Michael Jackson's 1983 hit "Human Nature." It reached number two in the fall of 1993.
