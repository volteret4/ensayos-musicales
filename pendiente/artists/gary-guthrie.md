# artist - Gary Guthrie

## curiosities
**WAKY Program Director - "You Don't Bring Me Flowers" Catalyst** : In 1978, as program director for Louisville Pop Radio Station WAKY, Gary Guthrie noticed that Neil Diamond and Barbra Streisand had each recorded separate album versions of "You Don't Bring Me Flowers." He took the initiative to splice these two recordings together, noting their similar arrangements and keys, to create a makeshift duet despite primitive technology. **DJ Activism - Creating a Number One Hit** : Gary Guthrie's spliced duet of "You Don't Bring Me Flowers" was tried out on WAKY and quickly inundated the station with listener requests. This phenomenon spread to other radio stations, eventually leading Neil Diamond and Barbra Streisand to officially record the duet, which then became a number one hit.
