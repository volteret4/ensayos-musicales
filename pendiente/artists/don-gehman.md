# artist - Don Gehman

## curiosities
**Producer for Hootie, Mellencamp, R.E.M.** : Don Gehman produced Hootie & the Blowfish's full-length debut album, *Cracked Rear View*. He was specifically selected by Tim Summer for this role due to his previous experience, having also produced albums for both Heartland rocker John Mellencamp and alternative rock pioneers R.E.M. in the 1980s.
