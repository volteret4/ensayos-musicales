# artist - KRS-One

## member of

## curiosities
**Initiating the Bronx Side of the Bridge Wars** : As the leader of Boogie Down Productions (BDP), KRS-One took offense at MC Shan and Marley Marl's song "The Bridge," which suggested Queens, not the Bronx, was the birthplace of rap. This led BDP to record "South Bronx" in response, igniting the "Bridge Wars" as a battle over the soul of hip hop.
