**I've Never Been to Me (1977)** : Originally released in 1977 as a "kitschy standout" dialogue-driven story song (titled in "classic seventies, me-decade-fashion"), it initially peaked at number 97. After Charlene had given up recording and moved to the UK, Tampa DJ Scott Shannon began playing it "almost as a joke" in 1982, but listener response was "unironically strong." Shannon's advocacy led Motown to relocate and re-sign Charlene, re-releasing the single which climbed to number three. Listeners at the time regarded it as an "earnest farewell to the excesses of the 70s." More than a decade later, it became a drag anthem by soundtracking the opening scene of "The Adventures of Priscilla Queen of the Desert," ultimately defining Charlene's career. "The reissued I've never been to me single climbed all the way to number three in 1982." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Second-Chance Hits Edition Part 2 | https://shows.acast.com/hit-parade/episodes/695d4ef18e6dd12efb02c42e

Its second chance success provided her with a "no joke major legacy" and affirmed her status.
