## songs
**You Can't Change That (1979)** : This single from 1979, performed by Ray Parker Jr.'s band Raydio, was a No. 9 Pop hit and a No. 3 R&B hit. It adopted the "bouncy Yacht style," showcasing how African American acts embraced this sound.
