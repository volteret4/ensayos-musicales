## curiosities
**Contractual Re-recording of "Ali Oop"** : Paxton re-recorded the song "Ali Oop" as a "beatnik proto-hippy jam" under the new act "The Hollywood Argyles" as a means to get out of a previous contract he had signed as "Skip and Flip."
