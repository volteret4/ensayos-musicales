# artist - Joe Dowell

## songs
**Wooden Heart (1961)** : This song, a cover of a German tune ("Muss I denn") that Elvis Presley recorded for a film but chose not to release as an American single, was taken to number one by Dowell in 1961, benefiting from its "Oompa rhythm."
