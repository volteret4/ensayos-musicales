## songs
**I Remember You (1962)** : This yodeling ballad was an earlier EMI single that scored a hit for VJ Records, reaching the top five on the Hot 100 in late 1962. Its success made VJ favorably disposed toward EMI's product, leading them to accept the Beatles' "Please Please Me." "VJ had already scored a hit with an earlier EMI single by a British artist.
