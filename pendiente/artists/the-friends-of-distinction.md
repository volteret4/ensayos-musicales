# artist - The Friends of Distinction

## songs
**Grazing in the Grass (1969)** : This R&B vocal group created a hit cover by writing new lyrics to Hugh Masekela's instrumental success, reaching number three the summer after Masekela's original topped the charts. This vocal rendition is familiar from its extensive use in various advertisements and the Will Ferrell comedy "Anchorman."
