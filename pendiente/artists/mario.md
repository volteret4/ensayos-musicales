# artist - Mario

## songs
**Just a Friend 2002 (2002)** : A version of Bizz Markie's "Just a Friend" released 12 years later by teen singer Mario Barrett, who went by the mononym Mario. In this rendition, Mario sang everything, featuring a completely different verse with an all new tale of unrequited love, and a chorus nearly identical to Bizz Markie's but far less comical.
