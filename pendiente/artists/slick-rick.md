## curiosities
**Sampler of Ashley's Roach clip** : Slick Rick was a Golden Age rapper who used the Ashley's Roach clip drum break from The Soul Searchers' 1974 jam in his hip hop classics. This beat was formative in rap history and sampled on dozens of tracks. "It's the beat that powered hip hop classics by Golden Age rappers T. LaRoc. Eric B. and Rock him, Slick Rick, Chill Rob G, The Getto Boys, Two Pock, and Ice Cube, among many others." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Blame It on the Feign, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Melody Interpolations (1985)** : British-born rapper Slick Rick famously slipped very famous melodies into a two-sided 1985 hit single with Doug E. Fresh and The Get Fresh Crew. On "The Show," he dropped a few bars of The Beatles, and on the flip side, "La Di Dadi," he crooned the melody and lyrics from A Taste of Honey's 1981 hit "Sukiyaki," showcasing an early and bold integration of popular melodic elements into rap. "British-born rapper Slick Rick slipped in some very famous melodies on both sides." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Say My Name, Say My Name, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**"Tsukiyaki" Interpolation** : On the seminal rap single "The Show Slash Laddidadi" in 1985, rapper Slick Rick sang a few bars of the "Tsukiyaki" melody in the middle of his rap, an illusion that later inspired Mary J.
