## songs
**7-Year Ache (1981)** : Roseanne Cash, daughter of Johnny Cash, scored her only Top 40 pop hit with the percolating 1981 single "7-Year Ache." This occurred during a period (1980-1982) when many country acts able to record crossover tracks and downplay their twang could achieve pop success.
