## curiosities
**North Carolina Jangle Pop** : Let's Active was a band from Winston Salem, North Carolina, led by Mitch Easter, who was also one of R.E.M.'s early producers. The band's sound contributed to the "jangly" American indie rock movement associated with the American South in the mid-1980s.
