**Limited Release Format for "Unchained Melody" Reissue (1990)** : When "Unchained Melody" was reissued by the Verve label after its feature in the 1990 film "Ghost," the label only held rights to issue it on vinyl 45, which was not the dominant physical single format at the turn of the 90s, where cassette singles were more popular.
