# instrument - Hawaiian shirt

## curiosities
**Yacht Rock Fashion Staple** : The Hawaiian shirt is part of the "cheap" and "accessible" wardrobe associated with Yacht Rock, along with the captain's hat. It contributed to the genre's visual style, making it easy for fans and tribute bands to adopt the look from their own closets or thrift stores.
