# instrument - Hammond B3 organ

## curiosities
**Signature 1980s Sound** : The Hammond B3 organ, particularly as featured in Billy Joel's 1986 song "The Bridge" with Steve Winnerwood playing, was noted for imbuing the track with a quintessential "1986" sound.
