# instrument - Sacks

## curiosities
**Traditional Christmas Song Element** : Old-school saxophones (referred to as "sacks") were an element used in Wizzard's 1973 Christmas single "I Wish It Could Be Christmas Every Day" to evoke a classic, Phil Spector-like sound appropriate for a holiday track.
