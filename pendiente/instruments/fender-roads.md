# instrument - Fender Roads

## curiosities
**Stevie Wonder's Sound Signature** : The "Fender Roads" (likely a transcription error for Fender Rhodes) is identified as the instrument central to Stevie Wonder's distinctive "Fender Roads Funk" sound, as heard in tracks like "Boogie on Reggae One".
