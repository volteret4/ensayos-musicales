# instrument - Broadcast Data Systems (BDS)

## curiosities
**Radio Spin Tallying** : Broadcast Data Systems (BDS) was a companion technology to SoundScan, introduced to Billboard's charts in 1991, which accurately tallied spins on radio stations. Together with SoundScan, BDS significantly improved the accuracy and speed of chart data, making the Hot 100 a more precise reflection of music consumption.
