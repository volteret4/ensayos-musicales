# instrument - Old microphones

## curiosities
**Used for "Time Out of Mind" (1997)** : For the recording of Bob Dylan's 1997 album "Time Out of Mind," producer Daniel Lanois specifically set up the studio with old microphones. This was done to help recreate the echo of old 1950s records and achieve the desired atmospheric vibe Dylan sought for the album, aiming for a sound akin to classic bluesmen and Buddy Holly.
