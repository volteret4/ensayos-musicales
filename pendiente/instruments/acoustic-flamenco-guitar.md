# instrument - Acoustic Flamenco Guitar

## curiosities
**Foundation of Rumba Flamenca** : The acoustic flamenco guitar is the core instrument for Rumba Flamenca, the genre characteristic of Los Del Rio's music. The original 1993 version of "Macarena" featured this instrument, maintaining a traditional sound before later electronic remixes.
