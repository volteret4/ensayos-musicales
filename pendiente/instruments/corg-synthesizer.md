# instrument - Corg synthesizer

## curiosities
**The Neptunes' Signature Sound (1998)** : The Neptunes used a Corg synthesizer, programmed to emulate a medieval clavichord, to create the distinctive melody line for Noreaga's "SuperThug." This "nagging, proudly synthetic sound" became a hallmark of their production style, cutting through the radio with a unique presence.
