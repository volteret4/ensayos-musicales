# instrument - Spotify

## curiosities
**Frictionless Music Consumption** : Spotify, succeeding the iTunes Music Store, offered music fans a "frictionless way to consume their music." This ease of access enabled songs to "explode out of nowhere" digitally before amassing a radio audience, helping to reestablish the number one debut phenomenon. **Streaming Added to Hot 100 Formula** : Streaming music from services like Spotify was added to the Hot 100's formula in 2012, eventually overtaking downloads in the chart's calculations by the mid-2010s. This shift had a significant impact on number one debuts, leading to more rappers and "zoomer pop stars" launching at the top of the chart.
