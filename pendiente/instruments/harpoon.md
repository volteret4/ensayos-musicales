# instrument - Harpoon

## curiosities
**Coco Goldstein's Prop** : A harpoon was a prop fashioned by Dave Lyons for his character, Coco Goldstein, in the "Yacht Rock" web series. It was made from "old crap I had laying around my house," adding to the character's unique, slightly outrageous persona. "Then I made a harpoon. You're lucky harpates. I've got my lucky harpoon.
