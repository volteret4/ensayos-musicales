# instrument - Technics Turntable

## curiosities
**Sampling in Studio** : Carlos De Arza and Mike Treyi's studio, Basside Music, was equipped with a Technics turntable, which Carlos De Arza used to find and sample sounds from old-school records, such as an isolated laugh from a Situation 12-inch, for the Basside Boys' "Macarena" remix.
