**Capturing Early Rap Beefs** : The legendary live diss performance by Cool Mo Dee against Busy B in 1981, which established the rap diss as a live medium, was "fortunately captured on tape." This preservation allowed the historical significance of the event to be recognized by rap scholars and cemented its place in hip hop history.
