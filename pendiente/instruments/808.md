## curiosities
**Core Element of Snap Music** : The 808-thump is a fundamental component of Snap Music, an Atlanta-based hip-hop subgenre. This distinctive drum machine sound contributes to the genre's minimalist "digital essence," alongside high-hats, snapping, and chanting vocals, emphasizing simplicity.
