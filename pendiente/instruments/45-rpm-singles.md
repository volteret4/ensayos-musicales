# instrument - 45 rpm singles

## curiosities
**UK Sales Basis** : For most of its history, the UK singles chart was a sales-only chart, meaning only sales of 45 rpm singles counted towards a song's position. This methodology made it easier for songs like Elvis Presley's "Jailhouse Rock" or "It's Now or Never" to debut at number one, as sufficient first-week sales could directly lead to the top spot.
