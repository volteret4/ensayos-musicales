# instrument - Claviole

## curiosities
**Proto-Synthesizer on "Telstar"** : The claviole is described as a "proto-synthesizer" used by The Tornados on their 1962 hit "Telstar." This instrument contributed to the distinct sound of the surf rock instrumental.
