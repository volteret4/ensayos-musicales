# instrument - SoundScan

## curiosities
**Enhanced Chart Accuracy and Speed** : SoundScan was a barcode scanning system that, when added to Billboard's charts in 1991, accurately tallied music sales in record stores for the first time. This technology, along with Broadcast Data Systems (BDS) for radio spins, made the charts significantly more accurate and faster, immediately reflecting huge first-week sales for albums or singles. **Genre Fortunes Boosted** : SoundScan's accurate data collection boosted the fortunes of genres like country music, hip hop, and alternative rock, which had not previously performed as well on the pop charts. Its introduction allowed these genres to achieve more accurate representation and higher positions.
