# instrument - Mosrite Electric Guitar

## curiosities
**Ricky Wilson's Unique Surf Guitar Sound** : Ricky Wilson of The B-52s played a Mosrite electric guitar to create his "gaudily original kind of post-punk surf music." He was not a virtuosic shredder but achieved a unique sound, often with broken or missing strings. Bandmate Keith Strickland claimed he would intentionally break a string on Wilson's guitar, leading both to set up the Mosrite with an open tuning. This allowed them to play low, almost bass notes, and lead notes simultaneously, creating an accidental genius riff for "Rock Lobster."
