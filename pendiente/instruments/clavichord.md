# instrument - Clavichord

## curiosities
**Emulation by Corg Synthesizer (1998)** : The Neptunes utilized a Corg synthesizer to emulate the sound of a medieval clavichord, producing a "nagging, proudly synthetic sound" that formed the basis of Noreaga's influential track "SuperThug" and became a signature element of their production.
