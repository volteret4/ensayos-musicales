# instrument - Roland Compurism

## curiosities
**Darryl Hall's Experimental Use (1982)** : One night in the studio, after most of the band had left, Darryl Hall was "fiddling with a primitive drum machine called the Roland Compurism." Liking its dry, brittle beat, he layered keyboards and had John Oates add a skeletal guitar riff, resulting in "I Can't Go for That (No Can Do)." This experimentation produced one of the biggest and most influential experimental tracks of the era.
