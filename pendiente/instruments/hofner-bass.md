# instrument - Hofner bass

## curiosities
**Paul McCartney's Trademark Instrument** : Paul McCartney played the bass line on Elvis Costello's song "Veronica" using his "trademark Hoffner bass." This iconic instrument contributed to the song's "Beatlesque touches" and highlighted McCartney's direct involvement in the track.
