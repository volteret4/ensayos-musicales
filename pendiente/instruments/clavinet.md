# instrument - Clavinet

## curiosities
**Showcased by Stevie Wonder (1968)** : Stevie Wonder's 1968 hit "Shoo-Be-Doo-Be-Doo-Da-Day" was the first hit to showcase his playing on the amplified electric keyboard known as the Clavinet. **Key to "Music of My Mind" Sound (1972)** : Stevie Wonder's album "Music of My Mind" was notably "drenched" not only in his beloved clavinet but also a room full of synthesizers, as part of his radical departure from the Motown sound. **Central to "Superstition" (1972)** : On "Superstition," Stevie Wonder played the "stabbing funk lines" on his clavinet, which, along with the syncopated drumbeat and Moog baseline, formed the core of the song's distinctive sound.
