## curiosities
**Seminal 80s Technology (Year Unspecified)** : The Lindrum drum machine was a "seminal piece of technology" in the 1980s, already appearing on major hits by artists like the Human League and Prince. Its innovative use by Rufus keyboardist David "Hawk" Wolinski on "Ain't Nobody" demonstrated how artists tinkered with new technology to create inspired singles.
