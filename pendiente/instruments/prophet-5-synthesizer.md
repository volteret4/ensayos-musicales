# instrument - Prophet 5 synthesizer

## curiosities
**Joe Jackson's Key Tool** : Joe Jackson programmed a Prophet 5 synthesizer in his small SoHo studio as one of his primary tools for creating the sounds on his album "Night and Day." This instrument played a crucial role in shaping the electronic elements of the album, particularly for the centerpiece song "Steppin' Out."
