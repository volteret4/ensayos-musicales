# instrument - Balalaikas

## curiosities
**Used in Boney M's "Rasputin"** : The rhythm guitar hook in Boney M's 1978 hit "Rasputin" is notably played on balalaikas. This instrument choice contributed to the song's pseudo-Russian sound and visual aesthetic, despite no actual Russians being involved in its creation.
