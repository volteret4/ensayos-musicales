# instrument - Guitar riff

## curiosities
**Sampled for Legacy** : Stevie Nicks' "killer guitar riff" from "Edge of Seventeen" was sampled by Destiny's Child for their 2001 number one hit "Bootylicious." This sampling contributed to "Edge of Seventeen" entering permanent classic hits rotation and becoming a clear radio staple for Nicks, despite its original lower chart performance.
