# instrument - Captain's hat

## curiosities
**Yacht Rock Fashion Icon** : The captain's hat became an iconic symbol and "fashion template" for the Yacht Rock genre, largely due to Dave Lyons' character Coco Goldstein in the "Yacht Rock" web series, who wore one. It is a "cheap," "accessible" costume piece, easily found in costume or thrift stores, which contributed to the genre's widespread visual adoption by tribute bands and fans.
