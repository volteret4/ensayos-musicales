# label - Parlophone Records

## curiosities
**Comedy Label's Major Rock Signing** : When The Beatles finally signed with Parlophone Records in June 1962, it was a subsidiary of EMI that primarily made its profits from comedy recordings by artists such as Peter Sellers. The label was led by producer George Martin, and its initial chart fortunes for the band were limited, indicating the unconventional nature of the signing for a rock group.
