# label - DB Records

## curiosities
**Atlanta-Based Independent Label** : DB Records was a "tiny independent label based in Atlanta" that released The B-52s' first single, "Rock Lobster," in 1978. Despite its small size, the single was a "sensation" by indie standards, selling approximately 20,000 copies, demonstrating the label's early success with groundbreaking new wave music.
