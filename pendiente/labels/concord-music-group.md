# label - Concord Music Group

## curiosities
**Acquisition of Fantasy Records (Mid 2000s)** : The Concord Music Group acquired Fantasy Records from Saul Zantz in the mid-2000s. This acquisition made them the new parent company, leading to a significant shift in the ownership and management of the music catalog, including that of Creedence Clearwater Revival. **Reversion of Creedence Catalog Rights (Mid 2000s)** : Following their acquisition of Fantasy Records, the Concord Music Group took a significant step in the mid-2000s by reverting the rights to Creedence Clearwater Revival's catalog back to its primary writer, John Fogerty. This gesture of peace and goodwill effectively ended decades of legal battles and acrimony.
