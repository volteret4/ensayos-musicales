# label - Keen Records

## curiosities
**First Release: Sam Cooke's Secular Material** : Keen Records launched with Sam Cooke's secular recordings, including "You Send Me" and "Summertime," which Bumps Blackwell brought to the label after his departure from Specialty Records. **Run Holden's Label in LA** : After moving to Los Angeles, Run Holden signed with Keen Records, where he proceeded to record an entire album. **Smaller Label for Sam Cooke (1957-1959)** : Sam Cooke and Bumps Blackwell signed with Keen Records after Cooke's departure from Specialty. Keen agreed to release Cooke's uptempo "Summertime" single. "So Sam and Bumps took their recordings and signed with the smaller label Keen Records, which agreed to put out the up tempo summertime single." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Don’t Know Much About History, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Overmatched to Promote Sam Cooke** : By 1959, Sam Cooke began to feel that Keen Records, as a smaller label, was "overmatched to promote a rising artist of his stature," contributing to his decision to seek a major label deal.
