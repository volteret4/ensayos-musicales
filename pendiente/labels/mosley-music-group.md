# label - Mosley Music Group

## curiosities
**Timbaland's Vanity Label (2006)** : Mosley Music Group was a vanity label launched by Timbaland in 2006 in partnership with Universal Music, playing a part in his commercial return by providing a platform for artists like Nelly Furtado.
