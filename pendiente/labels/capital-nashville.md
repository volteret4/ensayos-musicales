# label - Capital Nashville

## curiosities
**Cautious Approach to Pop Crossover** : Capital Nashville, Garth Brooks's label, was not actively promoting his song "Shameless" to Top 40 radio in 1991, as they were wary of moving beyond the country audience due to the boom and bust of the Urban Cowboy Fad a decade prior. "Garth's label wasn't actively promoting Shameless to Top 40 Radio and the crossover didn't interest them all that much." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Friends in Low Places, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Compilation Album Resistance** : Capital Nashville had urged Garth Brooks for years to release a greatest hits compilation, but Brooks resisted out of concern that it might cannibalize sales of his continuously successful studio albums. "Brooks had resisted pleas for a compilation from fans and his label, Capital Nashville, fearing it might cannibalize sales of his studio albums" ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Friends in Low Places, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Garth Brooks' Label (1995)** : Capital Nashville was the label for Garth Brooks when they announced the title and release date for his album *Fresh Horses* in 1995. "Brooks and his label, Capital Nashville, announced the album's title, Fresh Horses, and its release date, three days before Thanksgiving, 1995." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Friends in Low Places, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
