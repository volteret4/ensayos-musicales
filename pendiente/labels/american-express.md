# label - American Express

## curiosities
**Early Supporter of MTV Concept** : In January 1981, Jim Robinson, the CEO of American Express, was one of two key executives John Lack needed to convince to approve the launch of a 24-hour music video network, especially given American Express's recent merger with Warner. Despite being a banking and credit card company, Robinson surprisingly became the first to enthusiastically support the idea after Lack's pitch, focusing on the financial viability and market potential.
