# label - Bayside Boys

## curiosities
**Remix Production Team for Los Del Rio** : The Bayside Boys were a Miami production team responsible for producing the partially English remix of Los Del Rio's "Macarena." This remix became the version most familiar to Americans and topped the Hot 100 for 14 weeks in 1996.
