# label - The Gold Mind

## curiosities
**Missy Elliott's Label (Late 1990s)** : Missy Elliott, having achieved superstar status, launched her own record label, The Gold Mind, which was distributed by Elektra Records. The label quickly produced a hit with "Make It Hot" by Nicole Ray.
