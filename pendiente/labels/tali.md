# label - Tali

## curiosities
**VJ Records Subsidiary for The Beatles** : Tali was a new subsidiary label founded by VJ Records specifically to issue new Beatles singles. It was named after Vivian Carter's brother, Calvin Tali Carter. This creation allowed VJ to find "every possible loophole in their agreement with EMI to release as much Fab Four product as possible," including "Twist and Shout" in 1964.
