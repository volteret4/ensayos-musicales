# label - Electra Records

## curiosities
**Graham Parker's Label Switch and New Band** : Graham Parker switched labels from Arista Records to Electra Records. After this move, in 1985, he formed a new backing band called The Shot, which then recorded his most romantic song ever, "Wake Up Next to You."
