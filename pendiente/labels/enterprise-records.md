# label - Enterprise Records

## curiosities
**Shaft Soundtrack Release** : Enterprise Records, a subsidiary of Stax Records, was compelled to issue "Theme from Shaft" as a single after the album cut became popular in clubs. They edited the track down for its release in late September 1971.
