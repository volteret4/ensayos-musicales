# label - Alta

## curiosities
**Bootleg Label** : Alta was the record label associated with the bootleg 12-inch mix "Let's Do It in the 80s Great Hits," which was created by French Canadian DJs Michel Gendro and Paul Recher and later served as the inspiration for Stars on 45. "It was credited to the artist's passion on a record label called Alta, but it was basically a bootleg." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Without The Beatles | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
