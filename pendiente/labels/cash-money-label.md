# label - Cash Money label

## curiosities
**Platinum Success (Late 90s)** : Run by entrepreneur brothers Brian (Birdman) and Ronald (Slim) Williams, the Cash Money label minted platinum with rappers like Juvenile in the late 1990s. Its success, including quadruple platinum albums in 1998 and 1999, further affirmed the potency of Southern Hip Hop.
