# label - Rare Earth

## curiosities
**Motown Subsidiary for Stoney and Meatloaf** : Rare Earth was a subsidiary of Motown Records to which the duo Stoney and Meatloaf, featuring Marvin Lee Aday (Meat Loaf), were briefly signed, scoring a number 36 R&B hit in 1971 with "What You See is What You Get."
