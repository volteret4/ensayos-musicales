## curiosities
**Dominance in Music Business (2010)** : In 2010, Apple's iTunes store held a dominant position in the music business, facilitating "frictionless singles buying" for consumers, with individual digital copies typically priced between 99 cents and $1.29. **Peak Sales (2011)** : The iTunes store was at its zenith in 2011, making it exceptionally easy for consumers to purchase singles. This ease of access contributed to unprecedented sales quantities for pop singles, with many tracks selling five million or more downloads apiece.
