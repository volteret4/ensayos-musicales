# label - Polydor

## curiosities
**Underestimation of "I Will Survive" (1978)** : Gloria Gaynor's label, Polydor, underestimated the potential of "I Will Survive," placing it on the flip side of the late 1978 single "Substitute," a more traditional club track. They may have done so due to its "oddness and locatiousness."
