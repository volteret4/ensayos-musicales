Under Davis's leadership, the label commanded the charts with a roster of danceable diva pop artists including Taylor Dayne, Expose, and Whitney Houston, showcasing Davis's acumen for identifying hit music. **Class Action Lawsuit Settlement** : Arista Records settled a class action lawsuit filed by buyers of the Millie Vanilley album in the United States. This legal action was prompted because the liner notes of the American edition of the album, unlike its European counterpart, explicitly listed Rob Pilatus and Fabrice Morvan as vocalists, thereby making the label liable for the deception.
