# label - Death Row Records

## curiosities
**Release of Dr. Dre's "The Chronic" (1992)** : Death Row Records issued Dr. Dre's "multi-platinum 1992 album The Chronic" after he left N.W.A. over financial disputes. This album became a cornerstone of the label's early success and a seminal work in West Coast hip hop.
