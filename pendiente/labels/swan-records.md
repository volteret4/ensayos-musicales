# label - Swan Records

## curiosities
**Tiny Label's Bargain for "She Loves You"** : Swan Records, a small regional label based in Philadelphia, licensed and released The Beatles' legendary single "She Loves You" from EMI for "practically no money." Swan had previously scored only one big single ("Palisades Park" by Freddie Cannon). This deal highlights the Beatles' desperation to gain a foothold in the American market before their breakthrough, as EMI was "begging" tiny labels to distribute their music.
