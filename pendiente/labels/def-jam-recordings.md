# label - Def Jam Recordings

## curiosities
**Founding and Early Vision** : Def Jam was co-founded by Russell Simmons, a budding black entrepreneur and artist manager, and Rick Rubin, a white NYU student and self-styled hip-hop producer. It emerged as one of the first labels exclusively specializing in rap, initially formed by Rubin in his dorm room in 1984 to release T. LaRock's "It's Yours" before Simmons joined to formally launch it. "These two acts, LL Cool J, and the Beastie Boys, recorded for deaf jam, one of the first labels specializing in rap.
