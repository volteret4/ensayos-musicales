# label - SBK Records

## curiosities
**Single-Pulling Strategy (1990)** : As an EMI subsidiary, SBK Records had a "sample-heavy pop rapper" in Vanilla Ice. When his smash 1990 hit "Ice Ice Baby" was poised to top the Hot 100, SBK deliberately pulled the single from stores. This limited the song to one week at number one but drove the album *To the Extreme* to shot to number one for 16 weeks and eventually went Septuple Platinum.
