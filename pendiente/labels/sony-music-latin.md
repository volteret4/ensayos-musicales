# label - Sony Music Latin

## curiosities
**Signing Ricky Martin (Early 1990s)** : Sony Music's Latin division, Sony Discos, signed Ricky Martin at the start of the 1990s. This partnership saw Martin releasing Spanish-language pop for the Latin market, laying the groundwork for his eventual global crossover success. **Lobbying for Ricky Martin's Grammy Performance (1998)** : Recognizing the greater potential of Ricky Martin and his song "The Cup of Life," Sony Music actively lobbied Grammy producers in late 1998 to secure a performing slot for Martin on the telecast the following February. This strategic move proved instrumental in launching Martin's career into the global mainstream.
