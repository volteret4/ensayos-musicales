# label - Portrait Records

## curiosities
**Cyndi Lauper's Solo Contract** : Portrait Records, a subsidiary of Epic Records, signed Cyndi Lauper for her solo album by the end of 1982. This deal was secured by her manager Dave Wolfe and marked Lauper's return to recording after the dissolution of Blue Angel.
