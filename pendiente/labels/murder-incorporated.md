## curiosities
**Ja Rule's Hit-Making Base (2000-2001)** : Murder Incorporated was the hip hop-plus-pop label where Ja Rule achieved a string of hits in 2000 and 2001, often through collaborations with female singers and rappers. This label's team, including Ja Rule, was given "cart blanche" to remake Jennifer Lopez's "I'm Real."
