# label - Quest Records

## curiosities
**Quincy Jones's Own Imprint** : Quest Records was Quincy Jones's new record label, launched in 1980 with George Benson's "Give Me the Night" as its first release. It also secured the US distribution rights for the British Electro Rock band New Order.
