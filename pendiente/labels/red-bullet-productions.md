# label - Red Bullet Productions

## curiosities
**Copyright Ownership** : Red Bullet Productions, a Dutch publishing company run by Willem Van Kuten, owned the copyright to The Shocking Blue's number one hit "Venus." This ownership became significant when Van Kuten discovered an unauthorized DJ mix containing "Venus" and subsequently commissioned the Stars on 45 medley. "The copyright for Venus was owned by a Dutch publishing company called Red Bullet Productions.
