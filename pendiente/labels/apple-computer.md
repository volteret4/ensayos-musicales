# label - Apple Computer

## curiosities
**U2 iPod Promotion (2004)** : In 2004, Apple Computer entered a "shrewd" partnership with U2 to launch a U2-branded iPod and promote their album "How to Dismantle an Atomic Bomb." This innovative campaign featured an Apple ad that was the first to use an established band's music and its members (as silhouettes). Bono and The Edge also joined Apple CEO Steve Jobs on stage to unveil the "gorgeous black" iPod with a "beautiful red click wheel" and laser-engraved signatures, marking "the beginning of a new era of music distribution." **Songs of Innocence Giveaway Controversy (2014)** : Apple's 2014 deal with U2 to automatically deliver "Songs of Innocence" for free to all iTunes and iPhone users' music libraries turned into an "APR Disaster" for both parties. Users complained the album was an "unasked for memory hog" on their devices, and initially, Apple made the song files "impossible to delete," leading to widespread criticism. Apple ultimately had to release a "bespoke application" to allow users to remove the album, effectively providing a "vaccine" for what critics called worse than spam. **CEO Transition (2011)** : Tim Cook assumed the role of CEO of Apple after the passing of Steve Jobs in 2011. Cook was notably involved in the controversial 2014 "Songs of Innocence" giveaway, where he and Bono "touched fingers as they counted down the launch" of the free album to half a billion people.
