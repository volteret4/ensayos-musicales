# label - LaFace Records

## curiosities
**Foundation and Success** : LaFace Records was co-founded by producer L.A. Reed and his songwriting partner Babyface. In the mid-1990s, the label was "riding high" with successful R&B acts like OutKast and the girl group TLC. The label played a pivotal role in Pink's early career, signing her group Choice and later Pink as a solo artist. "L.A. Reed, the producer turned president of LaFace Records. **L.A. Reed's Role** : L.A. Reed was the producer turned president of LaFace Records. He signed Pink's first group, Choice, and later gave Pink an ultimatum to go solo, which launched her career. He was initially skeptical of Pink's rock-oriented direction for her second album, "Missundaztood," believing it would be "commercial suicide," but eventually relented, giving her the "opportunity to fail." **"Stupid Girls" Promotion** : LaFace Records was persuaded to promote "Stupid Girls" as the lead-off single for Pink's "I'm Not Dead" album, primarily due to the significant controversy generated by the song's music video.
