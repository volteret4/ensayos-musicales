# label - Super Ego

## curiosities
**Amy Mann's Independent Label (2000)** : Super Ego was launched by Amy Mann as her own independent label, specifically to release her unreleased album, "Bachelor No. 2, or The Last Remains of the Dota," after she bought it back from Interscope. This move marked her exit from the major label system.
