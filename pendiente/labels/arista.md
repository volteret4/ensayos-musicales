# label - Arista

## curiosities
**Hiring Nile Rogers to Save Thompson Twins (1985)** : Arista executive Clive Davis engaged Nile Rogers to rescue The Thompson Twins' "Here's to Future Days" album, which was faltering after its lead single missed the US charts in late 1984, showcasing Rogers' reputation as a problem-solver in the music industry. "Arista Executive, Clive Davis hired Nile Rogers to save The Thompson Twins project." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > These Are the Good Times, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Rival Label (1983)** : Arista Records was the label for Air Supply's "Making Love Out of Nothing at All" when it was rising on the charts simultaneously with Bonnie Tyler's "Total Eclipse of the Heart" on rival label Columbia. "And even though they were on rival labels, Columbia and Arista, Bonnie Tyler's and air supplies respective singles scaled the heart 100 together." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Turn Around, Bright Eyes, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
