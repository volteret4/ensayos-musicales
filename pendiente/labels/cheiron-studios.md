# label - Cheiron Studios

## curiosities
**Stockholm Song Factory** : Max Martin's home base and "song factory" in Stockholm, where he relentlessly worked to perfect "American sounding pop." "his home base, the Shea Ron Studio's song factory in Stockholm." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Give Me a Sign Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Founding and Concept** : Established by Denniz Pop in Stockholm in 1992, this facility was conceived as both his personal studio and a "producer collective" where the "savviest Swedish song-smiths" could apply their skills. It was named after Chiron, the centaur in Greek mythology who taught chants and dances to Dionysus. "Dennis conceived Sharon not only as his studio and sonic playground, but as a producer collective where the savviest Swedish song-smiths could ply their trade." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Give Me a Sign Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Dominance in Diverse Era** : Along with Denniz Pop and Ace of Base, the studio achieved "the seemingly impossible" by dominating the 1994 charts with "pure Swedish Europop," defying the prevailing trends of grunge, gangster rap, and divas. "Dennis Pop and his Cheyron Studios had done the seemingly impossible. They dominated the year of grunge, gangster, and divas with pure Swedish Europop." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Give Me a Sign Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Base for Pop Renaissance** : The studio became "the base of operations for the pop renaissance" that swept across radio in the final years of the 1990s, a trajectory significantly aided by the early success of Ace of Base. "helped make Cheyron and Sweden the base of operations for the pop renaissance that would overtake the radio in the final years of the 1990s." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > The Give Me a Sign Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
