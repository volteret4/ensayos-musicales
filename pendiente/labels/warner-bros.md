# label - Warner Bros

## curiosities
**Fine for Non-Compliance** : The Beach Boys failed to meet the May 1973 deadline imposed by Warner Bros for the release of Smile. Consequently, they had to pay a $50,000 fine for not fulfilling this contractual obligation, further adding to the legend of the lost album. **Smile Release Clause** : After signing with Warner Bros., The Beach Boys were bound by a contract clause that mandated them to publish Smile before May 1973. This condition highlighted the continued mystique and commercial interest in the unreleased album. **Elvis Costello's Debut** : Elvis Costello released his album "Spike" as his new Warner Bros. album in the winter of 1989. This marked a significant label signing for Costello, leading to his first and only US Top 20 pop hit with the single "Veronica."
