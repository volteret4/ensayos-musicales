# label - Warner Bros

## curiosities
**Fine for Non-Compliance** : The Beach Boys failed to meet the May 1973 deadline imposed by Warner Bros for the release of Smile. Consequently, they had to pay a $50,000 fine for not fulfilling this contractual obligation, further adding to the legend of the lost album. **Madonna's "Into the Groove" Strategy (1985)** : Warner Bros. decided not to issue Madonna's "Into the Groove" as a US single in 1985 to avoid diverting radio attention from other singles being promoted from her *Like a Virgin* album and the *Vision Quest* soundtrack. Instead, they placed "Groove" on the B-side of a 12-inch single, with "Angel" as the A-side, making it eligible for Billboard's dance charts but not the Hot 100.
