# label - Tamla

## curiosities
**Part of the Motown Family of Labels** : Tamla is identified as a member of the "Motown family of labels," indicating its close association with Berry Gordy's broader enterprise. **Motown's First Imprint** : Tamla was Barry Gordy's first Detroit imprint, established before he expanded it into the Motown recording empire. The Miracles were the first acts signed to it, and their breakthrough hit "Shop Around" was released on this label. "Barry Gordy's first Detroit imprint, before he expanded it into his Motown recording empire, shop around was Motown's first million-selling single, and the first smash written by the miracles lead singer and songwriter William Smoky Robinson Jr." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Hit Parade - The Silver Medalists Edition | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**First Imprint of Motown** : Tamla was the very first imprint of Barry Gordy's Motown recording company, which is where he signed young Steveland at the age of 11.
