# label - Arraster Records

## curiosities
**Strategic Pricing of *Speaker Box, Slash, The Love Below*** : As Outcast's distributing label, Arraster Records cleverly priced the two-disc set *Speaker Box, Slash, The Love Below* so that retailers could discount it to approximately the cost of a single CD. This marketing tactic successfully encouraged the public to perceive the album as an extra-long Outcast release, contributing to its commercial success.
