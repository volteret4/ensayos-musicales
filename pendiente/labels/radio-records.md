# label - Radio Records

## curiosities
**Litigation Avoidance Strategy** : As a US subsidiary of Atlantic Records, Radio Records was responsible for issuing Stars on 45 in America. To avoid issues with "litigious music publishers," they released the single with a 41-word title that listed every song covered by the Dutch team.
