# label - Shelter Records

## curiosities
**Signing Mudcrutch and Tom Petty (1970s)** : LA-based Shelter Records signed Mudcrutch, Tom Petty's early band, and released one single before the band broke up in 1975. The label retained Petty due to his role as Mudcrutch's primary songwriter, leading to his formation of The Heartbreakers under their roster. **Affiliation with ABC Records (Late 1970s)** : Shelter Records was affiliated with ABC Records in the late 1970s, which led to a complex contractual situation for Tom Petty when Shelter switched distributors, and ABC demanded to keep Petty and The Heartbreakers.
