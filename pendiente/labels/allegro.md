# label - Allegro

## curiosities
**Bronx-based Latin Jazz Label** : Allegro was a storied Bronx-based Latin jazz label that released Pete Rodriguez and his Latin jazz band's "Brassi Piano Vamping Track" "I Like It Like That" in 1967.
