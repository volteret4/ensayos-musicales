# label - Cleveland International Records

## curiosities
**Independent Label for Bat Out of Hell** : Cleveland International Records was a small imprint managed by A&R man Steve Popovich, who, despite rejections from larger labels, signed Meat Loaf and Jim Steinman and loved their *Bat Out of Hell* album after hearing only half a song.
