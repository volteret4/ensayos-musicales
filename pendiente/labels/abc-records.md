# label - ABC Records

## curiosities
**Affiliation with Shelter Records and Acquisition by MCA (Late 1970s)** : ABC Records was affiliated with Shelter Records and demanded to retain Tom Petty and The Heartbreakers when Shelter switched distributors. Soon after, ABC Records itself was sold in its entirety to major conglomerate MCA Records, triggering further contractual disputes with Petty.
