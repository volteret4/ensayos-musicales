# label - SAR Records

## curiosities
**Vision for Black Performers** : Formed by Sam Cook, J.W. Alexander, and Silas Roy Crane (S.A.R.), this label was envisioned as a haven for black performers from its inception. Cook actively used it as a platform for advocacy, supporting artists like his former group The Soul Stirrers by writing and producing for them, establishing this vision before Motown truly scored any major hits.
