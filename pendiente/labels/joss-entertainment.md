# label - Joss Entertainment

## curiosities
**Small Label for Rob & Fab Album (1993)** : Joss Entertainment was a very small record label that released the "Rob and Fab" album in 1993, exclusively in America, highlighting the diminished industry support for Rob Pilatus and Fabrice Morvan after the Millie Vanilley scandal.
