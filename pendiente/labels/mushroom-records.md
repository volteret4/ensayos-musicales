# label - Mushroom Records

## curiosities
**Scandalous Promotional Tactics** : Mushroom Records was the former label of the band Heart, and the target of their song "Barracuda." The label had controversially attempted to promote Heart's lead singer Ann Wilson and her sister Nancy Wilson as an "incestuous lesbian couple."
