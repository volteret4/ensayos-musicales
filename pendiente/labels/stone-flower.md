# label - Stone Flower

## curiosities
**Sly Stone's Vanity Label** : Stone Flower was a vanity label founded by Sly Stone, affiliated with Atlantic Records. It was not very productive, only issuing four singles in total, two of which were Top 40 hits by the vocal group Little Sister.
