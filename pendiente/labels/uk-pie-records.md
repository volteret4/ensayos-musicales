# label - UK Pie Records

## curiosities
**Recognition of "Kung Fu Fighting" (1974)** : The "anor staff" (A&R staff) of UK Pie Records heard both Carl Douglas's intended A-side "I Want to Give You My Everything" and the quickly recorded B-side "Kung Fu Fighting." They decided that "Kung Fu Fighting" was the hit, despite producer Biddu Apaya's initial dismissal of it as "just a fun thing."
