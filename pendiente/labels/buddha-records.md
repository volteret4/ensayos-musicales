# label - Buddha Records

## curiosities
**Early Pressing of "Dance, Dance, Dance"** : Buddha Records, a smaller record label, played a crucial role in Sheik's early success by issuing an early pressing of their single "Dance, Dance, Dance." This pressing generated immediate and significant "DJ and Dance Floor Buzz," which subsequently prompted Atlantic Records to belatedly sign Sheik and reissued the track.
