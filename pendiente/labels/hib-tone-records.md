# label - Hib-Tone Records

## curiosities
**Short-Lived Athens Label** : Hib-Tone Records was a "short-lived Athens label" that released R.E.M.'s debut single, "Radio Free Europe," in 1981. This regional independent label played a crucial role in the very first commercial release from the band that would become one of Athens, Georgia's most famous musical exports.
