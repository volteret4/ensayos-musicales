# label - Big Machine

## curiosities
**Country Radio Remix Strategy (2012)** : To help ensure acceptance of Taylor Swift's pop-leaning single "We Are Never, Ever Getting Back Together" by country radio, Big Machine label president Scott Borchetta arranged for a special remix. This version replaced Max Martin and Shellback's synthesizers and drum machines with traditional country instruments like "Mandolin, Steel Guitar and snare drums."
