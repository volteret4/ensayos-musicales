# label - Maverick Records

## curiosities
**Single Withholding for Album Boost (1995)** : Maverick Records initially withheld Alanis Morissette's breakthrough hit "You Oughta Know" from retail single release. This strategy successfully helped to power her *Jagged Little Pill* album to the top of the album chart, where it spent 12 weeks and became one of the best-selling albums in the SoundScan era.
