# label - Rolling Stones Records

## curiosities
**Band's Own Label with Lips & Tongue Logo** : Established by The Rolling Stones, this label became the vehicle for their albums starting in the early 1970s, featuring their iconic Lips & Tongue logo. This move allowed the band to profit more fully from their recordings and represented a new level of artistic and business control.
