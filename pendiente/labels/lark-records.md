# label - Lark Records

## curiosities
**First "Donna Summer" Spelling** : This German label released Donna Sommer's 1974 single "Denver Dream," accidentally pressing and labeling it as "Donna Summer" (S-U-M-M-E-R). This accidental spelling ultimately stuck and became her iconic stage name.
