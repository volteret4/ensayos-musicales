# label - iTunes Music Store

## curiosities
**Dominance in Digital Downloads (2000s-Early 2010s)** : The iTunes Music Store, owned by Apple, dominated digital music downloads in the 2000s ("Aughts") and early 2010s before streaming services became prevalent. "digital music meant downloads, which were dominated in the Aughts and Early Tens by Apple's iTunes Music Store." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Smells Like Christmas Spirit, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**First Holiday Season Counted for Hot 100 (2005)** : The 2005 holiday season marked the first time Apple's iTunes downloads were counted for the Hot 100 chart, significantly impacting chart dynamics. "this was the first holiday season where Apple's iTunes counted for the Hot 100." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Smells Like Christmas Spirit, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Post-Christmas Sales Records (Mid-2000s to Early 2010s)** : For roughly half a dozen Christmases, as the iTunes store grew in popularity, download sales in the week after the holiday consistently set new sales records, driven by young people filling new iPods and using gift cards. "For the next half dozen Christmas as the iTunes store kept growing in popularity, download sales the week after the holiday kept setting sales records." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Smells Like Christmas Spirit, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
