# label - Hollywood Records

## curiosities
**Disney's In-House Label** : Hollywood Records is Disney's own record label, used to cultivate both the pop and TV stardom of artists like Hillary Duff and the Jonas Brothers by releasing their top-selling albums in-house, rather than farming them out to other labels.
