# label - Decadence

## curiosities
**Pivotal to Emo Explosion** : Launched by Fall Out Boy members Pete Wentz and Patrick Stump as a subsidiary of Fueled By Ramen, the Decadence label (spelled 'decay dance') proved "pivotal to the explosion of emo later on." It played a key role in signing and promoting bands that became central to emo's commercial success, such as Panic! at the Disco. **Biggest Hit with "Good Girls Go Bad" (2009)** : In 2009, during a period dominated by EDM and Lady Gaga, Decadence, in collaboration with Fueled by Ramen, scored their biggest hit. This was with Cobra Starship's dance pop song "Good Girls Go Bad," featuring Leighton Meester, which reached number seven on the Hot 100.
