## curiosities
**Promotional Gambit for Backstreet Boys** : Jive Records placed a bonus track promoting their label needs at the end of Britney Spears' "...Baby One More Time" album, which was an effective promotional strategy for the Backstreet Boys' album "Millennium." "Jive placed a bonus track at the end of her baby one more time album promoting her label needs.
