# label - Streetwise Records

## curiosities
**Maurice Starr's Independent Imprint** : This was Maurice Starr's small independent label, where he released New Edition's debut track "Candy Girl." The song topping the Billboard R&B chart in 1983 was considered "a major coup for an independent imprint."
