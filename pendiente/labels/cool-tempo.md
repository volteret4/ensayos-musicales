# label - Cool Tempo

## curiosities
**UK Acid House Distribution for Millie Vanille** : Cool Tempo, an acid house label, handled the UK distribution for Millie Vanille's album "All Or Nothing" in 1988. This illustrates the crossover appeal of Millie Vanille's sound, even to labels associated with burgeoning electronic music genres.
