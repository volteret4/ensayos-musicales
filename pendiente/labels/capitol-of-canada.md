# label - Capitol of Canada

## curiosities
**Early Support for The Beatles** : Capitol of Canada executives showed greater foresight and less cynicism than their American counterparts regarding The Beatles' prospects. In November 1963, they issued a 45 for the Canadian market featuring "Roll Over Beethoven." This record subsequently charted in the US as an import, providing further evidence of the intense demand for Beatles music across North America.
