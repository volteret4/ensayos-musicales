**Major Success with Other Artists** : Maverick achieved its biggest hit to date with Candlebox's triple platinum self-titled debut album by the end of 1994, outselling Madonna's *Erotica*. It also profited handsomely from Alanis Morissette's diamond-selling *Jagged Little Pill*. In 1997, Maverick Records signed The Prodigy and released *Firestarter* and *The Fat of the Land*, the latter debuting at number one on the Billboard 200.
