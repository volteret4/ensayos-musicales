# label - Curb Records

## curiosities
**Bobby Hatfield's New Recording of "Unchained Melody" (1990)** : Bobby Hatfield re-recorded "Unchained Melody" for Curb Records in 1990, a decision influenced by the less favorable royalties he received from the original version. This new recording sold a million copies on cassette and CD, despite receiving little airplay, peaking at number 19.
