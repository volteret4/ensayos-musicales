# label - Planet Records

## curiosities
**Richard Perry's Vanity Label (1980s)** : Planet Records was a vanity label founded by producer Richard Perry, who sold it to RCA Records in 1982 after its establishment was significantly aided by the success of The Pointer Sisters.
