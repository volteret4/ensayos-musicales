# label - Bad Boy label

## curiosities
**Puff Daddy's Launch and First Signing (Mid-90s)** : The Bad Boy label was launched by Sean Combs (Puff Daddy) in the mid-1990s, with Brooklyn rapper Biggie Smalls (The Notorious B.I.G.) as his first major signing. The label quickly became a significant player in the East Coast hip-hop scene.
