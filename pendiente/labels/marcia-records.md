# label - Marcia Records

## curiosities
**Legal Action Against Basside Boys Remix** : Marcia Records, Los Del Rio's label, initiated legal proceedings against the Basside Boys (Carlos De Arza and Mike Treyi) after their unauthorized "Macarena" remix gained national popularity. The label's lawyer contacted Carlos De Arza, threatening cease-and-desist orders for copyright infringement due to the remix's immense popularity. "This is the lawyer in-house for Marcia Records. Who is your lawyer?" ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > One Year - Hey Macarena! | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Copyright Resolution for Basside Boys Remix** : Instead of shutting down the popular Basside Boys remix, Marcia Records struck a deal. The record company retained the rights to the remix and released it on CD, granting the Basside Boys "a small cut of the proceeds," acknowledging the remix's cultural impact and the impracticality of pulling it from the airwaves. "The label did not shut down the bass-eyed boys. Given how popular the remix was, it would have been crazy to pull it off the air. So they struck a deal." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > One Year - Hey Macarena! | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
