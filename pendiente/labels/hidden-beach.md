# label - Hidden Beach

## curiosities
**Neo-Soul Imprint (2002)** : Hidden Beach was described as a neo-soul imprint, which, distributed by Epic Records (part of the Sony family and a rival to Atlantic), agreed to release Darius Rucker's solo R&B album "Back to Then" in summer 2002 after Atlantic Records refused. It was Jill Scott's label. "Hidden Beach, a neo-soul imprint distributed by Epic Records, part of the Sony family and hence a rival label to Atlantic, ironically, agreed to put out back to then in the summer of 2002, nearly three years after Rucker had started recording it." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > A Little Love and Some Tenderness Edition Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
