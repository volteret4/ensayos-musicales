# label - Geffen Records / DGC Records

## curiosities
**Acquisition of Amy Mann's Contract (Early 1990s)** : After Immago Records went out of business, Geffen Records, specifically its DGC label (which famously hosted Nirvana), acquired Amy Mann's contract. "Amy Mann's contract was acquired by Geffen Records, specifically the DGC label, which had famously hosted Nirvana." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Be the One to Walk in the Sun, Part 2 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Distracted Promotion (1995)** : Geffen Records did "little to promote Mann's album" *I'm With Stupid*, as they were "distracted by bigger bands like Weezer and Garbage," despite the album's quality.
