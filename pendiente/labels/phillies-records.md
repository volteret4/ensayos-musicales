# label - Phillies Records

## curiosities
**Phil Spector's Label** : Phillies Records was the label founded by producer Phil Spector, which served as a showcase for his stable of acts including The Ronettes, The Crystals, Darlene Love, and Bob B. Soxx, all of whom appeared on his influential 1963 album "A Christmas Gift for You."
