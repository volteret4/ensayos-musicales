# label - FonoVisa

## curiosities
**Enrique Iglesias's Early Success (1990s)** : FonoVisa was the label responsible for releasing Enrique Iglesias's first three Spanish-language albums in the 1990s. These albums achieved remarkable success, collectively selling 14 million copies, showcasing Iglesias's early popularity before his English-language crossover.
