# label - Warner Records

## curiosities
**Release of "Letter from Elena"** : Warner Records released Casey Kasem's spoken-word single "Letter from Elena" in 1964 because of the significant listener feedback it generated for his radio show, despite only reaching number 103 on the Hot 100. **Initial Skepticism for MTV** : In January 1981, Steve Ross, the head of Warner, initially expressed skepticism regarding John Lack's ambitious proposal for a 24-hour music video network. His approval was critical due to a recent merger between Warner and American Express. Ross only granted the "green light" after his teenage daughter enthusiastically endorsed the idea, famously telling him he'd be "mad to pass on it" and that this new channel would be "the hottest thing that ever happened to TV." "Still Warner's Steve Ross is not saying much, but seems like a bad sign." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Introducing History Daily - The Launch of MTV | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Resource Contribution to MTV Launch** : Warner Communications played a crucial role in enabling the low-cost launch of MTV. John Lack highlighted that Warner Communications possessed a spare satellite transponder that was not being utilized at the time, which allowed the music channel to be launched efficiently using existing technology and resources. This availability was a key factor in convincing executives to approve the network, as it meant the transponder could start generating revenue as soon as possible.
