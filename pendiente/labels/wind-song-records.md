# label - Wind Song Records

## curiosities
**John Denver's Vanity Label (established by 1976)** : Wind Song was John Denver's vanity record label, established by 1976. The first act Denver signed to the label was the Starland Vocal Band, who went on to score a number one hit with "Afternoon Delight," powered by Denver's label and promotional prowess.
