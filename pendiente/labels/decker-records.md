# label - Decker Records

## curiosities
**Reissue of "Nights in White Satin" (1972)** : Decker Records issued a more "judicious four and a half minute edit" of The Moody Blues' "Nights in White Satin" in 1972, which capitalized on the evolving tastes of radio programmers and record buyers for progressive and psychedelic pop sounds, leading to the song's massive Top 40 success.
