# label - Casablanca Records

## curiosities
**Neil Bogart's "I Feel Love" Insight** : Neil Bogart of Casablanca Records was instrumental in the release of Donna Summer's "I Feel Love" as a single, despite neither Summer nor producer Giorgio Moroder initially believing it should be released that way. **Role in "Love to Love You Baby" Development** : Casablanca Records, founded by Neil Bogart, played a crucial role in the creation of the extended 17-minute version of Donna Summer's "Love to Love You Baby." Bogart's vision for a long-form song conducive to lovemaking led him to request the extended version from Giorgio Moroder, shaping the song's iconic status. **Demand for Extended Mix** : Neil Bogart called Giorgio Moroder in the middle of the night (Germany time) after playing "Love to Love You Baby" at a party where guests repeatedly requested the "orgasmic record," demanding Moroder and Summer make it "many times longer" to fill an entire LP side. This led to the 17-minute album version. **Donna Summer's Imperial Era Label** : Casablanca Records was Donna Summer's label during her Imperial phase, a period where everything she released turned to platinum. Her "On the Radio" greatest hits compilation spanned her entire Casablanca era. **Acquisition by Mercury Records** : After Summer left Casablanca for Geffen in 1980, she still owed Casablanca one more album. This obligation had to be fulfilled for Mercury Records, the label that had absorbed Casablanca, leading to the creation of "She Works Hard for the Money."
