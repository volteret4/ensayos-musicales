# label - MCA

## curiosities
**Limited Single Release** : MCA, as Aqua's American label, issued the "Barbie Girl" single in a limited edition in the fall of 1997. This strategy led to copies being quickly exhausted, but it significantly boosted sales of the album "Aquarium." **Patti LaBelle's New Direction (1984)** : Patti LaBelle signed with MCA in 1984, signaling her intention to move out of her "ballad heavy lane" and undergo a "high-tech sonic makeover" for the *Beverly Hills Cop* soundtrack. This label change marked a significant shift in her career strategy. **Distribution for IRS Records** : MCA was one of the major labels that provided distribution for IRS Records, the quasi-independent label that signed R.E.M. This partnership enabled IRS to maintain its independent spirit while benefiting from broader market access for its roster of new wave artists.
