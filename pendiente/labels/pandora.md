# label - Pandora

## curiosities
**Role in Hot 100 Methodology** : Pandora is specified as a form of radio, whether terrestrial or streaming, whose inclusion in the Hot 100 chart methodology helps prevent any single artist from dominating the entire top 5. This is due to radio's tendency to play only one or two current hits by an artist at a time.
