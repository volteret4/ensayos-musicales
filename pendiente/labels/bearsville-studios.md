# label - Bearsville Studios

## curiosities
**Woodstock Recording Location** : Bearsville Studios, located in Woodstock, New York, was the usual workplace of producer Todd Rundgren, and the site where Jim Steinman and Meat Loaf recorded their landmark *Bat Out of Hell* album in 1975-76. "Todd Rungren would invite Steinman's team up to his usual workplace at Bearsville Studios in Woodstock, New York." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Turn Around, Bright Eyes, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
**Financial Backing for Bat Out of Hell** : Bearsville Studios' parent company, Warner Brothers, ultimately turned down *Bat Out of Hell* even after Todd Rundgren had bankrolled the recording costs himself through Bearsville. "Todd Rungren had bankrolled the recording, backed by Bearsville Studios. But Bearsville's parent company, Warner Brothers, still turned down the project, even when it was complete." ← Hit Parade Music History and Music Trivia > Turn Around, Bright Eyes, Part 1 | https://my.slate.com/podcasts/feeds/hit-parade/
