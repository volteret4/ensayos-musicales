# label - Rocket Records

## curiosities
**Elton John's Label** : Rocket Records was Elton John's own record label. In 1975, Neil Sedaka's number one single "Bad Blood," which featured backing vocals by Elton John, was issued on this label. This meant that when Elton John's "Island Girl" subsequently topped the Hot 100, he effectively replaced a song from his own label, featuring his own vocals, at number one.
